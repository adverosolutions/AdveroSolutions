Two ways to start

Two ways to fill your calendar.

Reactivation

Win back lost accounts

The follow-up and win-back agent works every account that cancelled, stopped renewing, went quiet or never closed. The ones ready to talk land on your calendar.

  • Written and sent under your name
  • Replies answered for you
  • A weekly scorecard of what went out, who replied and what closed

What you need: a list of 60 or more lost or dormant accounts. Lost proposals and former clients count.

Proof

A commercial roofer doing $150M a year recovered about $6M in contracts within 6 months.

How you pay

$0 up front. You pay a share of what comes back, nothing if nothing closes.

Outbound

Reach new accounts as they need you

The research agent watches for the moments a business needs what you do and reaches out under your name. The reply agent books the calls.

  • Triggers picked for your market
  • A weekly scorecard of what went out, who replied and what closed

What you need: your service area and your ideal client.

Proof

The same roofer brought in about $180,000 in new contracts in 90 days.

Guarantee

A written guarantee for your market, or your money back plus a $200 steakhouse gift card.

Who it's for

Built around how you sell, not what you sell.

We work with service businesses that close new business on a call, a walkthrough or a quote. The agents learn your offer and your market before anything goes out.

It's a fit if

  • You close new business on a call, a walkthrough or a quote
  • You have past customers, lost proposals or old leads, 60 or more
