# Cold Email Framework — Angle 2: "Found Your Website" / "Was Searching For X"

**Why it works:** This exploits genuine relevance — it tells the prospect *why them, why now* in one line, satisfying the "Perceived Likelihood" half of Hormozi's Value Equation (this isn't spam, it's specific).

---

## Variables

**Prospect**
- `{{firstName}}`, `{{companyName}}`, `{{website}}`, `{{industry}}`

**Research**
- `{{icebreaker}}` — the specific thing you noticed on their site
- `{{painPoint}}` — the specific gap/problem you spotted
- `{{searchTerm}}` — what you were searching for when you found them (for variant B)

**Offer (fill in once your real business is locked in)**
- `{{leadMagnet}}` — the free resource/teardown/audit you're offering
- `{{proofPoint}}` — optional stat or example ("a few other {{industry}} teams have found useful")

**CTA**
- `{{cta}}` — the low-friction ask

---

## The 4-Part Skeleton

```
1. SUBJECT LINE — curiosity/relevance, 3–6 words, never salesy
2. THE OPENING (Hook) — one sentence proving specific homework, zero pitch
3. THE VALUE (Give) — free resource offered BEFORE any ask
4. LOW-FRICTION CTA — a yes/no question, not a calendar link
```

---

## Two Variants — Pick Based on What's True

### Variant A — "I was looking at your website"

**Subject Line Formulas**
- `quick note on {{companyName}}'s {{painPoint}}`
- `noticed something on {{website}}`

**Template**
```
SUBJECT: quick note on {{companyName}}'s {{painPoint}}

Hey {{firstName}},

Was on {{website}} earlier looking at {{icebreaker}} and noticed
{{painPoint}} — figured it was worth a quick note since it's a
pretty easy fix.

I put together a {{leadMagnet}} that shows exactly how to close
that gap (takes about 2 minutes to read, no fluff). Want me to
send it over?

{{senderName}}
```

### Variant B — "I was searching for X and found you"

**Subject Line Formulas**
- `found you while researching {{industry}}`
- `came across {{companyName}} today`

**Template**
```
SUBJECT: found you while researching {{industry}}

Hi {{firstName}},

I was searching for {{searchTerm}} and {{companyName}} came up
— {{icebreaker}} stood out.

Since I was already digging into this space, I put together
{{leadMagnet}} on {{painPoint}} that a few other {{industry}}
teams have found useful ({{proofPoint}}). Want me to shoot it
over?

{{senderName}}
```

---

## Hormozi Principles Applied

| Principle | How it shows up here |
|---|---|
| **Give before you ask** ($100M Leads) | The teardown/resource is offered before any request for a call. |
| **Specificity = believability** | `{{icebreaker}}` names the exact page/element you saw — not a vague compliment. |
| **Perceived Likelihood** (Value Equation) | Framing the resource as a byproduct of research you were *already doing* lowers perceived selling intent and raises trust. |
| **Low-friction CTA** | "Want me to send it over?" — a one-word reply, not a scheduling ask. |

## Filled Example (Variant A)

```
SUBJECT: quick note on Apex Roofing's ads

Hey Mike,

Was on apexroofingatx.com earlier checking out your service
pages and noticed your ads are sending people straight to the
homepage instead of a dedicated landing page — that's a pretty
easy fix and usually costs a few extra points of conversion
rate.

I put together a quick teardown showing exactly where the leak
is and how to patch it (takes about 2 minutes to read, no
fluff). Want me to send it over?

Jordan
```

## Tone Checklist

- [ ] Would this sound normal texted to a friend, not a sales rep?
- [ ] No "I hope this email finds you well," no "I wanted to reach out."
- [ ] Contractions used naturally.
- [ ] One idea per sentence.
- [ ] Value is *offered*, not attached — ask "want me to send it?"
- [ ] Sign-off is first name only.
- [ ] Under 120 words total.
