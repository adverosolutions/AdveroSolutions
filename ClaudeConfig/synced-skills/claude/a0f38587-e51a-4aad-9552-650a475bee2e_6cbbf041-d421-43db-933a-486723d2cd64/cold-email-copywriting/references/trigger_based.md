# Cold Email Framework — Angle 3: Trigger-Based

**Why it works:** Timing is leverage. A trigger event (funding, hiring, launch, expansion, review, PR mention) justifies the outreach and creates natural urgency — the "why now" that Hormozi's Value Equation rewards.

---

## Variables

**Prospect**
- `{{firstName}}`, `{{companyName}}`, `{{industry}}`

**Research**
- `{{triggerEvent}}` — the specific recent event (funding round, new hire, launch, expansion, award, review)
- `{{painPoint}}` — the bottleneck that typically follows this trigger

**Offer (fill in once your real business is locked in)**
- `{{leadMagnet}}` — the free resource/guide you're offering
- `{{mechanism}}` — the "how" behind your solution, briefly
- `{{proofPoint}}` — a stat or example backing the resource

**CTA**
- `{{cta}}` — the low-friction ask

---

## The 4-Part Skeleton

```
1. SUBJECT LINE — curiosity/relevance, 3–6 words, never salesy
2. THE OPENING (Hook) — one sentence proving specific homework, zero pitch
3. THE VALUE (Give) — free resource offered BEFORE any ask
4. LOW-FRICTION CTA — a yes/no question, not a calendar link
```

---

## Subject Line Formulas

- `saw the {{triggerEvent}} — quick thought`
- `congrats on {{triggerEvent}}`
- `noticed {{companyName}} is {{triggerEvent}}`

---

## Framework Template

```
SUBJECT: saw the {{triggerEvent}} — quick thought

Hey {{firstName}},

Saw that {{companyName}} just {{triggerEvent}} — congrats,
that's a big deal.

Usually when companies hit that stage, {{painPoint}} becomes
the next bottleneck. I put together a short {{leadMagnet}} on
how {{mechanism}} solves that, based on {{proofPoint}}.

Want me to send it your way before you're knee-deep in it?

{{senderName}}
```

---

## Hormozi Principles Applied

| Principle | How it shows up here |
|---|---|
| **Give before you ask** ($100M Leads) | The guide is offered before any request for time. |
| **Value Equation — Time Delay** | "Before you're knee-deep in it" frames the resource as solving the problem *ahead* of when it hits — speed is the pitch. |
| **Urgency without artificial scarcity** | The trigger event itself creates real timing pressure — nothing manufactured. |
| **Specificity = believability** | `{{triggerEvent}}` and `{{mechanism}}` are concrete, not generic congratulations. |

## Filled Example

```
SUBJECT: saw you're hiring — quick thought

Hey Mike,

Saw Apex Roofing just posted for two new crew positions —
congrats, that's a good problem to have.

Usually when a crew scales up that fast, lead volume becomes
the next bottleneck — more hands than jobs booked. I put
together a short guide on how local roofers keep the pipeline
full during a hiring push, based on what's worked for a few
other Austin contractors.

Want me to send it your way before it becomes an issue?

Jordan
```

---

## Tone Checklist

- [ ] Would this sound like a genuine "hey, saw this" note from a peer?
- [ ] No "I hope this email finds you well," no "I wanted to reach out."
- [ ] Contractions used naturally.
- [ ] One idea per sentence.
- [ ] Value is *offered*, not attached — ask "want me to send it?"
- [ ] Sign-off is first name only.
- [ ] Under 120 words total.

**Where to find triggers:** LinkedIn job posts, funding databases (Crunchbase), local news, Google Alerts, industry award announcements, new location openings, recent reviews mentioning a specific problem.
