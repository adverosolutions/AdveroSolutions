# LinkedIn simple plan (from Lorenzo, pasted 2026-10-20 11:31 CT; "get a move on")
Source: Jonatan Maker School sequence. Ref doc https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kMiei1lCW3rMdc7iX8H2Hpiry-gH7rO7Z5cozJQaTAA/edit ; research backup https://docs.google.com/document/d/1dBddy3rWtRJGCVNld4k6d2_ELFRoq6Vfu6p47Me-Q6A/edit
Sequence: clear profile -> useful posts + connections -> short personal Looms -> follow-ups -> weekly review.
1. One message. Proposed pilot: US PPC agency owners, faster inquiry-to-call follow-up (not a change to email niches). Profile answers who/what/how; real samples only, no unprovable results.
2. Sales Navigator: US PPC/paid media/Google Ads agencies, size 1-10 and 11-50 (51-200 only if confirmed <100). Owners/founders/CEOs, "Posted on LinkedIn" on, exclude 1st-degree and viewed; save prospects first. ~20s company check, skip doubtful. Blank connection requests, small daily batch, genuine, non-promotional.
3. Post 4x/week: Tue inquiry-to-call problem, Wed simple workflow demo (labelled demo), Thu real prospect question, Fri personal building lesson. One hook, one idea, one next step. Lorenzo's own words first; never invent client wins.
4. Looms only after ~200 relevant connections + several posts. Practice ~10 first. ~1 min, start on their profile: name/company/role -> problem check -> outcome -> how system helps -> genuine proof -> easy next step. Download and upload video natively in LinkedIn DM. Volume matches new accepted connections. Check contact history first.
5. Follow-ups day 2, 4, 7 after first message; stop on reply/no/opt-out. Tracker fields: person, company, niche, request date, accepted, video sent, next follow-up, reply, call booked, call held, client won, DNC/cooldown flags. Weekly review; judge acceptance after ~2 weeks.
Guardrails: manual, respect LinkedIn warnings, don't chase caps, keep 6-month cleaning recontact rule and DNC, no pay-on-results promises. All LinkedIn writes ask-first; Lorenzo sends DMs/Looms himself unless he says otherwise.
