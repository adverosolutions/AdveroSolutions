## 1. Working name (draft)
**Cold Email System** (alt labels to pick: Outbound Email Engine, Cold Outreach Build, Booked-Conversation Outbound)

Frame: Advero sells the ability to run cold email outbound as the product itself. Not only as a reactivation or trigger wrap around cleaning / bookkeeping / recruiting.

---

## 2. Mechanism (plain words)
What we run for the buyer (DFY):
1. A target list for their niche (scrape + enrich + suppress).
2. Short cold sequences in their voice (or Lorenzo-style peer copy under their name, per deal).
3. Sending setup (domains/inboxes warm, Instantly or equivalent, deliverability hygiene).
4. Personalization at send ({{personalization}} style openers from public signals).
5. Reply handling handoff so interested replies become booked conversations on their calendar.

How it works in one line: we run the outbound machine; they show up to the calls.

---

## 3. Who it's for
Owners / sales leads at B2B or high-ticket local service firms who:
- Need a steady flow of first conversations (not more "content" or ads talk).
- Do not have a full outbound team in-house.
- Can take 15-minute discovery or sales calls when leads reply.
- Have a clear ICP that is scrapable (Maps, directories, Clutch, BBB, etc.).

Fit filter (draft): they already sell something with enough margin that a booked conversation is worth buying. Not a fit: pure productized one-off freelancers with no calendar capacity, or anyone asking us to spam consumers without a B2B angle.

---

## 4. What we promise in outbound copy (no numbers)
Allowed promise shape (no prices, fees, %, retainers, or guarantee figures):
- We run a cold email outbound system for {{companyName}} (done-for-you).
- Outreach can run under their name.
- Goal: booked conversations with the prospects they want on their calendar.

Not allowed in new outbound drafts (even if live Instantly campaigns still carry these):
- Any dollar amount, %, retainer, commission figure, money-back, gift card, or "nothing upfront" pricing line.
- Invented case studies or niche-proof for "cold email as product."

Call ask: soft 15-minute ask (mirror cleaning_trigger) or soft "Let me know" (mirror cleaning_reactivation). Ranked by Instantly interested rate, not raw unique replies. Lead picks a time later; no booking link.
