## 2. How a question arrives
COO drops one file in ~/advero/state/committee/inbox/<date>_<slug>.md with: the question in one line, the proposed change, why it is proposed, what it replaces, cost (money, time, Connects, credits), how reversible it is, and evidence (real numbers only, with source paths).

## 3. The five seats (no names, no personas)
1. Simplicity: Is this needed at all? Can we delete or reuse something instead of adding?
2. Revenue impact: Does it move calls booked or $ closed toward the $20K 90 Day Sprint? What does it cost per result?
3. Risk and reversibility: What breaks, what depends on it, what is the downside case, can it be undone fast?
4. Time cost: How much of Lorenzo's time and bot usage does it take? Does it protect his time for Looms and sales calls?
5. Compounding: Does it keep paying off over the 90 days, or is it a one-off?
All seats weigh equally. The reasoning must cite the evidence in the question file.

## 4. Process
- Round 1: each seat states yes / no / not yet and its single strongest reason.
- Round 2: each seat answers the strongest opposing point once.
- Vote: each seat votes yes, no or not yet, one sentence each. Majority (3 of 5) wins.
- Tie or no majority, or missing evidence: no change. Status quo holds and it goes to Lorenzo flagged "split".

## 5. Output
Verdict card to the COO, who relays it to Lorenzo:
- Question (one line)
- Verdict: YES / NO / NOT YET, vote count (e.g. 4-1)
- Best case for, best case against (one line each)
- What would change the vote (the missing fact or test)
- One line per seat
Full debate saved to ~/advero/state/committee/verdicts/<date>_<slug>.md, one line to decisions.jsonl.
