Once a deal is agreed, the build must not add any setup cost, paid add-on or subscription the client wasn't already told about. Prefer the free route inside the tools they already pay for. Anything usage-based or unavoidable gets flagged to Lorenzo with the free alternative, never sprung on the client.

**Why:** Lorenzo, 2026-10-06 (ZIND Academy build): "if I didn't bring it up already, it's going to be really abrasive to bring it up now. So just make sure that there's no new additional setup cost." He wins clients on trust and a fixed quote.

**How to apply:** for every tool choice, check whether it is included, usage-billed or an add-on, and pick the included option (e.g. GHL workflows over paid AI plans, client's own SMTP over paid email, screen-share access over paid seats). Put a cost table in any client plan. Related: [[zind-academy-ghl-build]].
