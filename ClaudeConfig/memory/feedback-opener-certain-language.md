Personalization opener is now `Hey {first_name}, I know that you do {x}. Congrats on {specific}. Was wondering if I can run something by you.` Replaces the 2026-09-07 "saw that you do {x}. That's exciting, {y}" form. Same day he also cut "That's exciting" ("sounds really AI"), any filler reaction ("Very cool"), and the "you must" Y; {specific} is a real achievement from their site (8-rung ladder in the cold-email-personalization skill, saved 2026-10-10). Specifics "really crush". No specific: a cold read in the Congrats slot (same day): a compliment true of nearly everyone in the niche that sounds aimed at them, e.g. "Fantastic work on the content. I can tell that you really lean into being real and original." Never "Congrats on the results you've been able to get", and don't drop leads for lacking a specific.

**Why:** Lorenzo (2026-10-10, on the PPC agencies samples): "that's just really weak... saying I know that you do XYZ sounds better than saying I saw. Like we just want to say we know." He wants certain language in openers.

**How to apply:** use "I know that you do" for every niche's template-mode line. Both cold-email-personalization and lorenzo-voice skills carry the new form. Related: [[personalization-philosophy-2026]], [[ppc-agencies-personalization-2026-10-10]].
