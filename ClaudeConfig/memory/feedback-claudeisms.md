No "Claudeisms" in any copy: a sentence or tacked-on clause that restates, sums up or sells what the message just said ("Nobody in the office has to remember to ask.", "That way the big tickets don't get chased like a small repair.", "No more chasing leads."). Test: delete it; if no fact, question or next step is lost, it goes.

**Why:** Lorenzo (2026-09-23, Upwork touch walkthrough) said humans never write these; they read as weird and machine-made. He also wants normal capitalization, not all-lowercase casual.

**How to apply:** Always, in every copy task. Personal skill `~/.claude/skills/claudeisms/`, always-on line in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` ("Writing"). Runtime prompts that generate copy need the rule written into the prompt itself (the model never sees the skill).
