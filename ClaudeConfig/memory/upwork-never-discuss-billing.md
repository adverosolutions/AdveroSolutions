No billing of any kind in an Upwork proposal: no prices, rates, retainers, budgets, fees or payment terms, even when the post asks for a rate, a fixed price or a retainer, and even on full-time posts. Pricing is for the call; the private assessment keeps the numbers. The P.S. case-study facts (numbers, what they were) never change, but the framing tail and the order fit each job: "on the same kind of system" only when the build is what his agency runs on (outbound, lead gen and follow-up, AI agents); otherwise the agency bullet ends at "$10,000 a month."

**Why:** 2026-09-24 I put "I'd run this as a monthly retainer rather than full-time hours" into two proposals (HubSpot/Shopify, Money Merch). Lorenzo: "we never discuss billing. We never discuss retainers... Put this as a hard stop." Same day: "Don't change any of the proof, but just position it slightly differently" and, clarifying, "scaled my own AI agency... on the same kind of system" doesn't apply to a HubSpot sync for an e-commerce store; it does for cold email.

**How to apply:** SKILL.md proposal rules 11 and 12 (both copies); `verify_job` fails a proposal with billing words above the P.S. (`BILLING`) or a P.S. missing a case study's number (`PROOF_FACTS`). A full-time post is still built and never skipped ([[upwork-never-skip-saved-jobs]]), but the retainer is raised on the call only. See [[upwork-proposal-copy]].

Paid consultation posts (2026-09-24): he bids consultations low on purpose ($5 per 30 minutes, i.e. $10/h in the hourly rate field) to win the follow-on build. The rate goes in Upwork's bid field only; the cover letter offers "30 minutes or the full hour, whichever you prefer" and never names the price.
