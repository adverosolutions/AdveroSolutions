Lorenzo, 2026-09-28: "we need to be thinking of what's the highest quality thing that the prospect could be delivered to them, and then from there, that's how we start working." Before speccing any Upwork job, work out the best version of what they asked for that can reasonably be built within the pipeline (n8n/Make/GHL/chart), and build that. Don't stop at a minimal canvas.

**Why:** He wants every build to go above and beyond. That's the quality metric in [[upwork-quality-overhaul-2026-09]].

**How to apply:** Put this in every spec-agent prompt. Cover every pain point the post names, visibly and properly, even if the canvas goes past the usual 10-15 nodes ([[upwork-builds-minimal]] is the floor, not the ceiling).
