Build `page.html` for every job in a batch unless Lorenzo explicitly says not to. That includes posts that ask to see "a workflow you use yourself": build the page around his real workflow instead of skipping it.

**Why:** on 2026-09-29 I skipped the page for the Claude Code EU job and gave him beats for a screen-share. When he reached it to record he had nothing to open: "we just wasted our time."

**How to apply:** at batch start, spawn a page agent for every job. When a post asks for your own live workflow, the page shows his real system (e.g. the Upwork proposal pipeline) labelled live, not illustrative.
