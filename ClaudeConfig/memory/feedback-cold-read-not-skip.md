When a lead's site has no usable fact, or the site is down, the "Congrats on {specific}" slot gets a cold read. It must be true of nearly every company in the niche but read as if it were about them. Never skip the lead for lack of info, and never use the generic "the results you've been able to get". Hard skips stay: already contacted, already in Instantly, do-not-contact, duplicates, foreign, no MX, off-niche and the segment rule.

Lorenzo's model (2026-10-10): to a content creator, "fantastic work on the content. I can tell that you really lean into being real and original." Every creator believes that applies to them.

Cleaning pool, rotated so each is about 20% of the batch and colleagues at one company differ:
- "the clients who keep coming back"
- "building a crew clients trust with the keys"
- "the word of mouth you've built up"
- "building a name for showing up and doing it right"
- "being the team clients can count on"

For dead sites, X comes from the company name plus the source's commercial screen: janitorial work for local businesses / house cleaning and commercial cleaning / commercial cleaning for local businesses. See [[commercial-cleaning-personalization-2026-10-10]] and [[feedback-opener-certain-language]].

**Why:** a generic fallback or a skip wastes the lead; a cold read still feels personal.
**How to apply:** every "Congrats on {specific}" batch, every niche. Write the niche's own pool and show it to Lorenzo in the report.
