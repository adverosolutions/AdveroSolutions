Don't run `open <page.html>` when a page finishes. List it as ready with its path, and open it only when Lorenzo says he's ready.

**Why:** he records Looms while the batch builds, and an auto-open takes over his browser mid-recording and pops a new window (2026-09-29).

**How to apply:** on each finished page, report the path, the job link and the beats, then wait for him to ask. Related: [[upwork-visual-walkthrough-lane]], [[upwork-batch-mode]].
