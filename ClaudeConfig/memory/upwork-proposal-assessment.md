After building a workflow for a pasted Upwork job post, always append a short private
assessment block (for Lorenzo only, never for the proposal text):

1. **Estimated time** — realistic hours plus calendar weeks, not the happy path.
2. **Likelihood of actually delivering it** — what specifically could break, especially
   work that happens in the client's account or in a UI rather than in code.
3. **Standard market pricing** — the defensible fixed-price or hourly range for that
   scope, plus recurring software/API costs when the post asks for them.

**Why:** he is quoting fixed-price work and delivering with Claude doing most of the
build, so the risk is not whether the code can be written — it is the hours that land
on him (platform admin, client access, tuning, testing) and underquoting against them.
He usually bids below the standard range on purpose, but wants the market number as a
reference point before deciding.

**How to apply:** keep it to a few lines unless there is a real risk worth naming.
Price for his actual learning curve on unfamiliar platforms, not for a specialist's.
State plainly when a requirement depends on the client's subscription tier or account
access, and list what to ask them before quoting.
