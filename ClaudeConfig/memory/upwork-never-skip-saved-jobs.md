Every job Lorenzo saves in Upwork gets the full build and a proposal, whatever its terms say (full-time, fixed hours, entry level, a human receptionist or VA role). For those, the pitch is a retainer he runs (e.g. an AI receptionist he sets up and operates monthly) instead of full-time hours, raised on the call only: the proposal never mentions a retainer or any billing ([[upwork-never-discuss-billing]], 2026-09-24).

**Why:** 2026-09-23 I skipped a full-time virtual receptionist post under the old "skip full-time fixed-hours posts" rule. He said: "Don't you dare skip the seventh. If I added it, I want it done... I'll just put them on a retainer and pitch my retainer."

**How to apply:** In batch mode step 1, drop only jobs that are already hired or older than the batch. The skill line was rewritten the same day in both SKILL.md copies. See [[upwork-batch-mode]] and [[upwork-quality-overhaul-2026-09]].
