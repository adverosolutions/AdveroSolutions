Follow-ups sent right after a good sales call (e.g. Bollywood dance studio, GHL setup at $1,650, 2026-10-01) are extremely friendly, use exclamation marks, and spell out the full scope in depth (grouped sections, every piece discussed, including human escalation and error handling) plus the price and milestone option. They close with "I'd really love to win your business" and an offer to help with anything else.

**Why:** He wins these on personality. The no-exclamation and no-price rules in [[lorenzo-voice]] are for cold email and Upwork proposals, not a prospect who already heard the price on a call.

**How to apply:** For any "follow up after my call" request, go warm and in depth from the first draft. Still no em dashes and no Claudeisms ([[feedback-claudeisms]]), and nothing about the client that wasn't said on the call.
