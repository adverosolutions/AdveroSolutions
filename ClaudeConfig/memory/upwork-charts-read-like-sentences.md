Each chart step label makes sense when Lorenzo reads it aloud word for word, to him and to the client. Sentences are fine but not required; short readable phrases pass, jargon and compressed fragments fail. Example sentence style ("3 - They book a tasting in Square, and Zapier adds the date to their Monday item"), in one straight line from first step to last, with zero or one gate, so reading the steps in order explains the system without narration. When the post is vague, the steps are the automations Lorenzo would really put in for that kind of business, not guesses about their current setup.

**Why:** 2026-09-24, loading the wedding cake bakery chart (Title Case fragments, a "my guess at your current Zap" step, two branch gates): "Your flowcharts are extremely hard to walk through. They don't make sense. They need to almost read as if they are sentences, and it goes step by step... we'll just have to lean on experience in terms of automation that we've implemented before."

Refined the same day, while the Money Merch and GHL-consult charts were being redone: "Don't rewrite them as sentences necessarily. Just make sure that they are readable and that, if I just read the text in front of me, it would make sense to the person I'm speaking to and to me."

**How to apply:** `_AGENT_BRIEF.md` step 4 and the chart line in both SKILL.md copies. Rebuilt the bakery chart that way (11 sentence steps, no gates). See [[upwork-builds-minimal]].

Same day: when the client already has a flow the post doesn't describe, show the full end-to-end version from his experience and say in the proposal "we keep what you already have and plug in what it's missing" (SKILL.md proposal rule 4).

2026-09-25, final word (reverses the "sentences optional" refinement): full sentences only. "You have Claudeisms within your Excalidraw workflows... They're not full sentences. They are tidbits that humans don't use. We use full sentences." Rewrote the GHL follow-up chart: "6 - When they book, they get a confirmation right away and reminders before the visit." Section notes too (no "Your parts 6 and 7:" lead-ins). Alternative he offered: one big readable text block with short node names; default is full-sentence steps.
