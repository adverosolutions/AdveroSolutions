Danielle Becks, Partner Co-Founder Danielle s Bio Danielle Becks is Partner and Co-Founder of Hunter SF. Prior to founding Hunter SF, Danielle worked with a boutique recruiting firm in San Francisco, where she was a director. She has had the pleasure of working with many of the Bay Area’s leading companies and talent. Danielle has worked across a range of industries including Financial Institutions, Private Equity, Hedge Funds, Venture Capital, Start-up Technology, Consulting and Software/Internet. Previously, Danielle worked at Heidrick Struggles, a global executive search firm, where she was part of a team that specialized in the private equity, wealth management, hedge fund and investment banking industries. She also participated in general counsel and other top legal searches for Fortune 500 clients. Danielle graduated from the University of California, Berkeley, where she double majored in Mass Communications and Political Science. At UC Berkeley, she competed on the Varsity Swim team and was a four-time All-American. Danielle is an active member of the Olympic Club and competes with the open water swim team. In her spare time Danielle enjoys traveling, trying new SF restaurants and spending time with friends and family.
