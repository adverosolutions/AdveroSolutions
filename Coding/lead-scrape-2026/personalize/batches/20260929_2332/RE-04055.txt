So what is embedded talent? An embedded talent consultancy provides a business with one or more Talent Partners that become fully ’embedded’ in their client. At Scout, that means that the Talent Partner works for their client on the same contracted hours as all other full time employees and their time is dedicated entirely to their client. The Talent Partner will be given access to internal communications, attend internal meetings, use internal platforms and may even attend the Christmas party.
