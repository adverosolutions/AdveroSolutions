About Us Altitude Recruitment Skip to content Search for: Search Who we are The Altitude Story since 2016 Altitude is the brainchild of Paul Halstead and Damian Procter, two avid enthusiasts in Melbourne s Recruitment industry who worked together previously for several years. They wanted to perform recruitment in an ethical manner compared to the rest of the market and it was this vision that inspired them to establish Altitude in 2016 as a customer-centered, professional recruitment and advisory consultancy. With Paul now following his passion for building automobile masterpieces , Altitude has continued to grow from strength to strength with Damian and a dedicated team of consultants, providing personalised recruitment services across a specialised range of roles.
