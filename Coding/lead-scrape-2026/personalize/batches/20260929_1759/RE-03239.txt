Our Founder's Story Entering into a career in executive search after graduating from college in 1996, Chris Thurmond quickly learned just how critical the role of an executive recruiter is to the success of most large companies today. Starting his career with one of the largest specialty recruiting companies in the country, Kimmel Associates , gave Chris the firm foundation and professional pride that would lead him and his future companies to the upper echelon of the specialty search arena. After just f our years, Chris founded Thurmond Associates, a search firm specializing in the domestic, commercial construction market. Then, in 2005, Chris co-founded Top Tier Services, LLC , with the help of one of his former co-workers from Kimmel Associates. Top Tier Services expanded very quickly and grew to encompass two specializations with the addition of the financial services division. I n 2009 that partnership dissolved and Chris founded Thurmond Company, Inc.
