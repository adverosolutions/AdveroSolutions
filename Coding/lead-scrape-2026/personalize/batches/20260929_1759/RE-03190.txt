Rather than trying to become the biggest staffing company out there, AnDek Staffing has chosen to remain smaller by design . Why? It s Simple. It allows us take care of you better . Whether it s a job to help you begin your career, or a candidate to fill an important position, being small allows us to accelerate the process without compromising on quality or service. Our founders Mindy Decker and Karen Danielson experienced first-hand what it was like to work for a larger staffing agency where the culture started to change. In 2007, they left.
