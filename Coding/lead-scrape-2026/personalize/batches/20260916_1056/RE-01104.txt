Our goal is to become a trusted long-term staffing partner to businesses throughout our community. We take the time to understand each employer s needs and each candidate s skills, experience and goals so we can make better employment matches not simply fill positions. Our Business Culture At Pettit Staffing, relationships matter. We work to understand our clients businesses, goals and workforce needs while treating every candidate with professionalism and respect.
