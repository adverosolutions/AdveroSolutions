Privacy policy Deny All Accept All Search Our Story For more than two decades, UHC has had a successful track record of helping our Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) and community healthcare-focused clients. Our mission is simple: to recruit healthcare professionals who are mission-driven and have a passion for serving populations that are underserved and marginalized. As the premier community healthcare recruitment agency in the country, our team is very active within the National Association of Community Health Centers (NACHC) network. Our goals are your goals: building capacity while improving the health of the communities we serve. This work has never been easy, and it will be a continuing challenge for the community healthcare system in the coming years. With close to 30 million patients served today – and a goal of 50 million in the next decade – the demand for healthcare leaders, providers, and other mission-driven professionals has never been greater. That’s where we come in. UHC Solutions is your trusted community healthcare recruitment specialist.
