--> About - David Mills CPA, LLC About Our History David Mills CPA LLC provides the small and medium business market access to financial information in a way that you can understand and implement in your business. Simply getting financial statements won’t give you the information you need. We will analyze your information, interpret the results and advise you on your options and the best course of action. Our team includes six Certified Public Accountants, one Enrolled Agent, and several staff bookkeepers. Continuing education for our staff is important so we are current with the laws and trends that affect you. No matter what stage your business is in including startup, growth, or contemplating selling your business, we can assist you. Established and Expanding David Mills C.P.A. has over 30 years’ hands-on experience in roles of a controller, business owner and in upper management of (2) franchises. David specializes in tax planning, preparation and consulting for businesses and individuals. He graduated from the University of Illinois-Springfield and has a Certificate of Financial Planning from Kansas State University. He is also a member of the Illinois CPA Society and the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) as well as the National Association of Tax Professionals.
