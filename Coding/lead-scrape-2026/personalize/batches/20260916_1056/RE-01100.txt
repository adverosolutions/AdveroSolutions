About SpringHouse 0 about us a local and personalized staffing agency rooted in the pacific northwest We are SpringHouse Professionals, a local staffing agency dedicated to connecting speech-language pathologists and school psychologists to professional opportunities and offering a supportive environment to thrive and grow. Our goal is sustainable, long-term relationships with our clinicians and with the districts we support. Our dedicated team is committed to creating a space where school-based clinicians can find a professional home, avoid burnout, and receive the support they need to make a meaningful impact in their roles. We understand the unique challenges that school-based clinicians face and strive to match them with opportunities that align with their expertise and career goals.
