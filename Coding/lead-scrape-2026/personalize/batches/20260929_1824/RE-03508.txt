Partner with KIMARU Talent​ Fewer searches. More care. After more than a decade placing executives at global brands and startups, our founder Rumiko Matsumoto kept seeing the same pattern: large firms moving candidates like inventory, prioritizing speed and volume over fit, and leaving clients to deal with the fallout. KIMARU was built as the alternative. We work exclusively with companies in gaming, technology, and advertising -industries that move fast, where culture is everything, and where the wrong leadership hire can set a team back by years. We keep our client list intentionally small so we can go deep on every search. Rumiko built this firm on a straightforward premise: the best executive searches feel less like a vendor transaction and more like a trusted colleague quietly working on your behalf. That s still how we operate.
