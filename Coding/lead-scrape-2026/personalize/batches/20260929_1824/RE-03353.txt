As a VP of a Fortune 500 company, Bill engaged a number of searches in his corporate career. He identified a void in the industry which he set out to fill when he established Handler in 1977. In 2008, Bill stepped down from the firm, but his son, Eric Handler, leads the team today with the same commitment to innovation, client service and the pursuit of excellence.
