About Us - Finance Search Partners About Us Finance Recruiting by Finance Professionals How Finance Search Partners was Founded Ray and Nadica met over 20 years ago, when Ray was an aspiring CFO and Nadica a trusted finance recruiter. Nadica, with her unique ability to advise candidates on potential career options, recruited Ray for his first COO / CFO opportunity. Not long after, Ray began working with Nadica to hire top talent for a hyper-scaling organization. Right away Nadica noticed that Ray had a passion for helping others with career coaching, mentorship, and talent acquisition. A few years later, Nadica proposed to Ray that he consider a career in finance recruiting. In 2025 the time was right to co-found Finance Search Partners. Ray and Nadica are thrilled to join forces to serve the community and fuel the economic growth of select companies with the right finance talent. Together, they offer their clients more than 50 years of experience in addressing the most complex finance and accounting recruiting challenges across multiple industries. Ray Nuñez Co-Founder | Partner Ray works with boards, executive leadership, and investors to help them build winning finance teams—teams that perform at a high level and successfully partner with the CEO and functional executives to produce outstanding results. An active CPA and CGMA, Ray is also a founding board member of the Sacramento Finance Executive International (FEI) chapter and a member of the CFO Leadership Council. He received his BSC in Accounting from Santa Clara University and an MBA in Corporate Finance from the UCLA Anderson School of Management. Ray has had a distinguished career, one that includes roles in Silicon Valley, San Francisco, and Sacramento. He earned his CPA while at Deloitte and spent 16 years in various finance leadership roles at Hewlett-Packard. He has been CFO and COO for venture capital firms, early-stage startups, and professional services companies. He was named CFO of the Year by the Sacramento Business Journal in 2020. Ray thrives on setting big professional and personal goals, competing, and overcoming obstacles to find success. In his free time, Ray is a competitive USTA tennis player, NTRP National Doubles Champion, and is ranked #2 in the USA and #1 in Northern California.
