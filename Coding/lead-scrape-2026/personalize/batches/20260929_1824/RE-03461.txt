ABOUT The Workshop LA 0 Meet the Team Get to know the team of recruiters who will be supporting and guiding you through your journey. we have walked in your shoes The Workshop LA is a female founded, owned and operated fashion and lifestyle recruiting agency headquartered in Venice, CA. Our client roster consists of cutting edge, Los Angeles based fashion and lifestyle companies ranging from start-ups to some of the biggest in the business. We find exclusive jobs, both freelance and full time, for our fashion community. Our core principles lie in establishing a meaningful relationship with each client and candidate in order to provide the most individualized search and match. We treat our candidates as more than just a resume, investing countless hours, months and even years into long lasting relationships that often lead to candidates becoming our clients when the time is right. We understand time is of the essence in most job searches, and make a point to work fast, efficiently and successfully. You can put your trust in our hands. We have the success rate to prove it. Audrey Shaeps founder ceo Audrey Shaeps is the founder and owner of The Workshop LA. She started the agency in 2015 after working for 10+ years in the fashion industry both in New York and Los Angeles. She is the girl who had a million jobs when she was first starting out- from design to sales and marketing - and understands firsthand how challenging it can be to find a career fit, especially when you’re starting out. Audrey founded The Workshop LA to make peoples job searches easier and to help companies find exceptional talent. Audrey was born and raised in Santa Monica, CA and graduated with a Sociology degree from UC Santa Cruz. Prior to starting The Workshop LA, she worked for companies including Zac Posen, Aveda/Estee Lauder, The California Market Center and UBM Fashion/MAGIC Tradeshows. She understands the wholesale and manufacturing business better than most because she has worked inside it. In her free time, she balances family life with two toddlers and sample sale shopping, going to concerts and exploring new restaurants all over the Westside - she is a Venice lifer.
