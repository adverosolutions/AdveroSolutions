This is where I'm building the next phase of my career — bringing Silicon Valley recruiting expertise to early-stage startups hiring their first engineering teams. § 02 // METHODOLOGY Why technical recruiting? People are fascinating — and interviewing is complicated. A great engineer can give a bad interview answer. A polished candidate can ace every question and still be the wrong hire. There is no A plus B equals C. What thousands of interviews give you is calibration. I work to cut through how someone presents and make accurate judgments about who is actually worth investing in. That means: [✓] Telling a hiring manager when a technically strong candidate is showing real culture red flags. [✓] Telling a candidate honestly when a role doesn't match what they said they want. [✓] Eliminating assumptions so both sides make decisions they'll stand behind. "I don't sell anyone on anything." § 03 // FOUNDATIONAL_STAGE Why seed stage? The last four years of my career have been focused entirely on seed-stage teams. I've developed a sharp sense for the specific type of person who thrives at that stage — and who doesn't. I also host Your Next Tech Startup , a podcast where I speak with engineering leaders about what it actually takes to make great early hires. It keeps me sharp and it's shaped how I recruit. Early hires are foundational. They shape technical culture, product velocity, and team dynamics for years.
