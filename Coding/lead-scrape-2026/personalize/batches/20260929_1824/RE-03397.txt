Our Story BioMed’s Mission Hire people who make a significant, positive, and long-term impact at companies advancing health through technology. Why Lab Automation? Ian s recruitment journey began in San Francisco, where he placed engineers in Big Tech, Digital Health, Biotech, and Medical Device companies. Despite his success, he struggled to find a niche he was truly passionate about—until the pandemic hit in 2020. In response to the urgent need for automation in Covid-19 testing, Ian helped companies build their automation departments from the ground up. It was here he discovered the significant shortage of qualified candidates capable of building, programming, testing, and servicing automated equipment. This realization ignited his fascination with the technology and the dedicated, tight-knit community behind it. Recognizing both the incredible achievements and the vast potential for growth in lab automation, Ian has since focused exclusively on hiring automation specialists for various labs and product companies. Schedule Meeting Why Start BioMed Recruitment? In early 2021, Ian returned to his hometown in North County San Diego to start BioMed Recruitment. Reconnecting with Chris, a fellow Encinitas native and successful recruiter, the two shared a vision to offer a more progressive and specialized service than generic recruitment agencies. Common Recruitment Challenges Agencies forcing unqualified matches between hiring managers and candidates. High-volume operations taking on too many openings simultaneously. Junior recruiters tasked with sourcing and screening. Lack of industry knowledge and established candidate network. Agencies providing only resumes with basic notes. Our Commitment At BioMed Recruitment, employers are guaranteed that either Chris or Ian will personally source and screen each candidate. They partner closely with hiring teams, both remotely and in person, to ensure the best match. By engaging daily with automation specialists over the past few years, they have built an extensive network and deep knowledge, allowing them to present top-quality candidates for every opening. Through the launch of BioMed+, they provide candidate shortlists that align with each company s objectives, surpassing standard requirements.
