Legal Compliance Recruitment Experts | Kerwin Our Story How It All Began Kerwin was founded on a simple belief: the most transformative tech companies are built on the strength of their people. Since 1990, we have specialized in executive search for legal and compliance teams. We partner with legal leaders at AI, emerging technology, and established companies to design and build high-impact legal teams during moments of rapid scale and regulatory uncertainty. As the tech world has evolved, so have we. With a long-standing presence in Silicon Valley and a national reach, we are known for combining decades of legal recruitment expertise with a deep understanding of the technology sector. From guiding startups through their first in-house legal hire to helping global innovators scale complex legal and compliance functions, Kerwin has been a trusted partner at every stage of growth. Our Leadership Team Our team has dedicated itself to helping founders hire thousands of professionals in and around their legal teams. This passion and collaborative approach continues to this day and is what we ve built our reputation on.
