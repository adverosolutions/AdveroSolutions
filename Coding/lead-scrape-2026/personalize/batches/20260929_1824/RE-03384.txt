About Stephanie Shapiro has spent the last 20 years working at the intersection of design and talent. She began her career at Landor Associates in San Francisco as a production artist, working alongside some of the best designers in the world. Early on, she learned that great design isn’t just about aesthetics—it’s about solving real business problems. In 1997, Stephanie transitioned into recruiting at Adjacency, a small interactive design firm that was acquired by Sapient just nine months later. At Publicis Sapient, Stephanie went on to lead and scale the design hiring function, growing the team from three to ten recruiters, coordinators, and sourcers. She oversaw design hiring across more than nine global offices, consistently meeting aggressive hiring goals in a highly competitive market, and spent six months in India building the design team from the ground up. In 2003, Stephanie founded DesignRecruiter, bringing her deep understanding of design, teams, and business to help companies build exceptional design organizations. Services Services include contingency and retained searches, freelance staffing for both short- and long-term engagements, and customized consulting offered on an hourly or per-project basis. Our deep industry experience allows us to identify and attract top talent, while partnering closely with clients to design effective evaluation and interview processes and support offer negotiation and closing.
