After years of working at generalist CPA firms, we saw firsthand the challenges clients face when 401(k) audits are treated as low-priority work. Audits were often assigned to low-billing staff and left waiting for untimely reviews, leading to prolonged processes and frustrating delays. We knew there was a better way one that respects your time. We Respect Your Time Here s What Makes Us Different Your time is valuable; our audit process is built with that in mind. From start to finish, we want you to know that we strive to respect your time by answering our own questions whenever possible and, with your permission, working directly with your service providers (record keeper and/or TPA) to gather much of the necessary information. With most CPA firms, your interaction with the audit partner typically ends after the initial pitch. From there, you re faced with a rotating cast of recent college graduates and interns asking the same questions year after year. At Dufek Company, you work directly with the firm s owners Scott and Nancy who each bring over two decades of experience auditing employee benefit plans. With us, there s no revolving door, no repetitive questions, and no wasted time. Specialization That Saves You Time Unlike generalist CPA firms that divide their attention among tax, consulting, and other services, Dufek Company focuses exclusively on 401(k) plan audits. This specialization representing 100% of our revenue has allowed us to refine an audit process that combines unmatched efficiency, precision, and advanced technology. The shift to remote auditing , accelerated by COVID-19, has transformed the industry, and we have embraced this approach to maximize efficiency. Conducting audits remotely respects your time by allowing your staff to address requests at their convenience. Leveraging cloud-based platforms, we ensure secure request management and real-time progress tracking while AI-driven tools streamline the process, reduce redundancies, and enhance accuracy.
