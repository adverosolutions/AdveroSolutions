What you should be doing for your small business now: Third Quarter 2026 NOW is the time to find a good accountant and bookkeeper! Do not wait until the end of the year, or worse, April 1st. Ask for an education session with an accountant to review and explain financial reports to you. Entering checks and deposits into financial software does not mean your financial reports are correct. Know the difference between a Profit and Loss and a Balance Sheet. Payroll tax deposits include the employer portion AND employee withholding. Loan payments must be split to show principal and interest. Principal goes on the Balance Sheet; interest is on the Profit and Loss. If you take a draw/distribution/etc. from your business, it probably needs to go on the Balance Sheet. EVERYTHING: Bank Accounts, Credit Card Accounts, Loan Accounts, Line of Credits, etc. ALL go on the Balance Sheet and must be reconciled monthly. Estimated tax payments go on the Balance Sheet. They are not a business expense. This is the same as the taxes pulled from a paycheck if you had a W2 position. Your AI generated category assignments are probably not right. Don't let software tell you how to run your business. ( USE AI , trust but VERIFY .) RECORD ALL BUSINESS EXPENSES: Credit card, line of credit, checking, savings, petty cash, etc. This includes "no interest loans" for large purchases. Did you buy a new piece of equipment, an A/C unit, renovate your office? If you use it for your business, it needs to be recorded. Check with your state for annual reporting requirements.
