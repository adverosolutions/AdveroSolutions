His journey began at Weatherby Healthcare, where he cultivated enduring relationships within the emergency medicine community. In 2000, Shawn channeled his entrepreneurial spirit to found TeedCo., assembling a team of seasoned professionals dedicated to connecting healthcare organizations with exceptional physicians, nurse practitioners, and physician assistants. Under his guidance, TeedCo. has become a leading name in recruitment, renowned for its unwavering commitment to quality and personalized service for both clients and candidates. Beyond his professional achievements, Shawn and his wife, Deirdre, actively support A Better Chance of Westport (ABC), a program that empowers academically gifted students from underprivileged communities. In his downtime, Shawn enjoys reading, cooking, boating and spending time with his wife and kids.
