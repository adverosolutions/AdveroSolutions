About us KNM Resources KNM Resources is committed to bringing the right parties together. A job change / new hire search should not be a stressful process. By carefully listening to hiring managers we partner with and professionals who seek our assistance, KNM Resources has a proven track record of successfully matching talent with opportunity. KNM Resources is a Certified Women Owned Business Enterprise with over 40 years combined experience in full service recruitment. Our core business is recruiting professionals on a contingent, retained, RPO or interim basis. Partnering with Fortune 1000 companies, private companies and public accounting firms in the tri-state area for industries that include pharmaceutical, biotech, financial services, manufacturing, technology, and hospitality, we identify and recruit top talent in Finance, Accounting, Audit, and Tax.
