AMERI-CANS is a provider of all manner of temporary sanitation support, from portable toilets and handwash stations, fully self-contained, climate-controlled trailers and modular units, to bulk waste and water hauling. We operate across the spectrum from mission critical job sites, to special events and in support of emergency services and disaster relief. Our team brings the efficiency, planning, high standards, and execution you would expect to all aspects of the operation, enabling our customers to focus on other aspects of their event or project. As a certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business staffed by its Veteran owners, AMERI-CANS knows what it means to execute. We are committed to making your experience with our portable sanitation facilities differentiated. Whether you need a single porta potty and a handwash station, a toilet trailer, or a fleet of toilets and sinks, our teams in Northern Virginia, Richmond, VA, Southern Maryland, Fort Wayne, IN, Newark, OH, and Charleston, SC, will get it to you clean, where you need it, when you need it at a fair price.
