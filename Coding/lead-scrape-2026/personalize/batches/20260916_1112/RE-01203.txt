McKenna Founder President, Navesink Logistics, Inc. [email protected] Thomas A. McKenna is the founder and owner of Navesink Logistics, Inc. , established in 1994. With more than four decades of experience in the logistics and supply chain industry, he brings extensive executive management and consulting expertise spanning technology, project management, finance, business development, operations, and recruiting. Since founding Navesink Logistics, Mr. McKenna has partnered with a wide range of clients to execute logistics cost reduction initiatives and supply chain transformation projects. His advisory work also includes providing Internet resource management consulting, expert witness testimony, and due diligence services for investors, attorneys, banks, and financial institutions. Before establishing Navesink Logistics, Mr. McKenna served as an executive at Leaseway Transportation , where he managed business operations with revenues exceeding $125 million . His thought leadership has been featured in numerous industry publications, including Global Logistics Supply Chain Strategies , Inbound Logistics , Traffic World , and Private Carrier . He is a frequent guest speaker for organizations such as the Food Distribution Industry Group , American Trucking Association , National Private Truck Council , and National Association of Purchasing Managers . Mr. McKenna honorably served three years in the United States Army in the European Theater. He earned Bachelor of Science degrees in Management Science and Economics from Kean University .
