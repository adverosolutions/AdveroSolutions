About Spartan Strategics | Behavioral Health Executive Search Firm Skip to content ABOUT SPARTAN STRATEGICS Built for Behavioral Health. Not Retrofitted for It. Founded in 2014 by two operators who spent years inside healthcare recruiting before deciding behavioral health deserved a firm of its own. Our Story Spartan Strategics is led by founding partners William Rhody and Christopher Savery — two operators who built the firm from the ground up to serve the behavioral health industry the way operators actually run. Both Will and Chris came up through healthcare recruiting — Chris starting in 2012, Will in 2013 — after early careers that put them inside the sales, financial, and clinical realities of the industry before they ever ran a search. They founded Spartan in 2014 with a simple thesis: the best behavioral health leaders are almost never actively looking. Finding them requires named-by-name sourcing, confidential outreach, and the discipline to build relationships months before a search opens. A decade and hundreds of placements later, that thesis has scaled from South Florida addiction treatment to a national practice covering five behavioral health verticals — SUD , ABA and autism services , eating disorders , mental health platforms , and multi-vertical MSOs .
