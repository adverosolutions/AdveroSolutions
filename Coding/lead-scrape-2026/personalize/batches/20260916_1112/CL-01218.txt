As Grand Rapids residents, we share a deep love for West Michigan and its people. I have a passion for serving others and helping to provide safe living spaces for my clients. As an employer, I am committed to ensuring that my team members are paid a livable wage, are treated with respect, and have growth opportunities both personally and professionally. I have modeled my business after one of my favorite Bible verses, Matthew 5:16 - " Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven."
