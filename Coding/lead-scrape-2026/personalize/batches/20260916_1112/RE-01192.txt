About ICS and our Leadership Team ICS Our Company About ICS Information Consulting Services (ICS) provides IT staffing services to clients across North America. ICS is known for its decorated 30+ year history and 100% client repeat rate. Working directly with clients and through systems integrators, ICS focuses on four primary practice areas: Cloud, Application, Infrastructure and Security. The firm’s proprietary approach is based on the Lead Technology Consulting Model, and its founder’s belief that ICS has a responsibility to “set the standard – and raise the bar – for professional integrity”. ICS is privately-held and headquartered in the Philadelphia metro area . Leadership Team Bill Ciemny , President and CEO Bill Ciemny assumed ownership of ICS in 2016 and is responsible for the day-to-day operations and strategic direction of the company. Bill brings over thirty years of IT experience to the position and is committed to the success of the company. Bill comes to ICS from IBM where he held a variety of executive roles, including Vice President of Enterprise Application Consulting, Vice President of Business Process Outsourcing Supply Chain Management, and Managing Director, IBM Switzerland. Prior to IBM, Bill was Group Vice President, Global Services Unit for Siemens Medical Health Services Division and was a Director of Consulting for SAP America, where he helped lead the early growth of the company.
