We are a team of highly respected, experienced accounting professionals who support small businesses and nonprofit organizations in our communities. Our staff provides finance office solutions and serve as advisors to top management. Learn More Countryside Accounting Solutions provides finance, accounting and bookkeeping services to small businesses and nonprofit organizations in Southeast Pennsylvania, Delaware and New Jersey. The firm was founded in 1998 to meet a need for fractional accounting services. It was true then and still resonates that many small businesses and tax-exempt organizations need expert advise and assistance in maintaining their financial information. Stable leadership, personalized attention given to each client. Teams of long-tenured staff work with each client often for the life of the relationship. CAS prides itself on maintaining client relationships over many years. The CAS team works seamlessly with the client’s staff to organize the financial information into meaningful reporting units. Learn More Dedication to Meaningful Financial Information Through strong client relationships and deep industry experience, the CAS team helps clients gain the financial clarity needed to support informed decision making and long-term success. Advocacy The owners and team at Countryside Accounting Solutions are proud to support local nonprofits and advocate for the causes that matter most in our community. By partnering with these organizations and investing in their work, we help create lasting, positive change. Expertise in the Accounting Field Our team’s certifications, credentials, and advanced degrees —paired with the latest technology — enable us to provide efficient, client-focused accounting services. Central Location While CAS’s work takes place in many locations, the main office is located in West Chester, a historic town in Chester County that brings the best of professional and community-focused organizations and individuals.
