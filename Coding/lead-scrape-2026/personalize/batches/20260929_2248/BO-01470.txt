About Susie Q Bookkeeping 0 About Stress less, accomplish more. We empower inspired entrepreneurs with clear and timely financial data and perspectives for stress-free decisions and growth. Established in 2014 in Alexandria, Virginia, Susie Q Bookkeeping provides continuous, high-quality, cloud-based digital bookkeeping services to more than 150 businesses throughout the U.S. and internationally. Our certified QuickBooks ProAdvisors are trained to find the best solutions for your financial challenges. We love bookkeeping, and we excel at it. Bookkeeping is truly fun for our happy little team, so we make it fun and stress-free for our clients. We work with accuracy, integrity, transparency, and clear, consistent communication. Finding opportunities for hardworking clients is a thrill for us, and sparing them IRS nightmares is deeply gratifying. As an LGBTQ-owned company, we have a special love for and experience in serving non-traditional, uniquely creative, and underserved business owners. The Susie Q Bookkeeping Story Susie Clark began her career in real estate and learned bookkeeping working as an office admin and real estate agent. She began taking on freelance clients and transitioned to doing books for small businesses full time in 2014. Susie’s obvious passion for helping small business owners built us a reputation as reliable, accurate, and approachable. The business grew exponentially, and in 2017, D Schwartz joined Susie Q Bookkeeping full time to manage operations and training. Today, we have an amazing team of six fully certified full-time and part-time bookkeepers (and one very loud cat). We consider ourselves lucky to play a key role in helping businesses grow.
