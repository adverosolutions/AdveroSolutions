Contact us today About Jones and Kolb: Firm Overview Jones and Kolb is a well-respected accounting and consulting firm in Atlanta, Georgia. Formed in 1976, the Firm provides a high level of client service and professionalism to individuals and businesses in the metropolitan area. Over the past four-plus decades, Jones and Kolb has grown; however, our client service philosophy has remained strong and even today it stands as the cornerstone of our approach to serving clients. Our proactive services are tailored to match your specific circumstances and align with your goals. We believe that client service is most effective when delivered by partners and professionals who truly know you and your business inside and out. The Firm is staffed by a diverse accounting, tax, advisory, and support team. Many team members have been with Jones and Kolb for more than ten years and all have industry experience, insights and knowledge, and a strong desire to provide the very best client service. Our team approach to every engagement provides senior-level guidance, continuity, industry-specific experience, and proactive solutions to fulfill our clients’ service requirements; however, we go beyond compliance services.
