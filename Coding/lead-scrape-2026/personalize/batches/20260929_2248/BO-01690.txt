Baniakas Associates, LLC --> About Our Firm Baniakas Associates provides accounting, tax consulting, QuickBooks™ training and CPA services to small businesses across the country and around the world. Located in Pensacola, Florida, Baniakas Associates started as Charles Fairchild, CPA. We Are Baniakas Associates Our History 1992 Regina Baniakas, CPA started with Charles Fairchild, CPA as staff accountant. 1996 Regina Baniakas, CPA becomes partner. Charles Fairchild, CPA becomes Fairchild Baniakas, CPAs. 1999 Fairchild Baniakas, CPAs earn QuickBooks™ Pro Advisor status. 2007 Regina Baniakas, CPA purchases Charles Fairchild’s interest in Fairchild Baniakas, CPAs, forming Baniakas Associates, CPAs in February of 2007. Today Baniakas Associates is still located in downtown Pensacola. They continue to serve the accounting, tax consulting and QuickBooks™ training needs of small businesses.
