About Us Robert van Teeffelen is a native of the Netherlands. After a two year military service, he worked for 13 years in public auditing for PriceWaterhouseCoopers. During this time, he obtained his certification as a Dutch licensed CPA (equal to a US CPA certification). Beginning in 1988 he worked for 12 years as an international controller for sales and manufacturing with Lely Research Holding in Switzerland. From 2000 he worked for the same company as corporate controller for North America, which included substantial real estate development activities in Florida and Texas. After the North American activities were sold, he has worked as a controller for different real estate development and construction companies in Florida. Most recent positions were as international controller for World Triathlon Corporation (organizer of the Ironman Triathlons) where he was responsible for the international finance and accounting activities and as Vice President / Controller for Summit Management Group of Florida (real estate development / property management) responsible for finance, HR and business development. Being a Quickbooks Online Proadvisor he will be able to help his clients set up and maintain adequate accounting records and financial reporting. As a Florida licensed CAM he will also be able to assist condominium and homeowner associations with their financial requirements. After over 30 years in the corporate world, Robert would like to use his expertise to help small businesses with their accounting and financial needs and to assist his clients to grow their business and increase their profitability. Our professionalism and expertise will help save you time and money. We'll work with you to develop the best bookkeeping strategy for your company, no matter how large or small.
