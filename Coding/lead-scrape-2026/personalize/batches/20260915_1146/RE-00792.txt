Leadership Team - Hazeltine Executive Search Skip to content Leadership Team Leadership Team Our History Hazeltine Executive Search was founded in November 2015 by Chad Spencer with one mission: to revolutionize the executive and professional search industry. After six years of success doing search the “typical” way—sending resumes, chasing candidates, and relying on volume—Chad asked a simple but transformative question: “How can we add more value to our clients, save them more time, and deliver better candidates?” That question sparked the creation of The Hazeltine Process —a video-first, insight-driven approach that replaces inefficiency with clarity and guesswork with precision. Over the years, it’s become our greatest differentiator and the foundation of everything we do. While most recruiters are out of the business within 12 months, Hazeltine has grown year over year through one thing: results . Our firm is built on repeat clients, word-of-mouth referrals, and a reputation for delivering elite finance talent others simply can’t reach or close. We believe great recruiting is more than filling a job. It’s about helping our clients make confident decisions with speed, accuracy, and insight—and doing it in a way no one else can.
