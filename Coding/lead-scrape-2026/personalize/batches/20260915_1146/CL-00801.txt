About Ahlers Building Maintenance | Kansas City Since 2000 About Ahler’s Building Maintenance About Us Ahlers Building Maintenance have been in the cleaning business over 19 years servicing the Kansas City Area. Ahlers Building Maintenance was founded in Lenexa, Kansas with a straightforward mission: provide reliable, high-quality cleaning services that Kansas City businesses can count on, day after day. For over 19 years, we have been doing exactly that. What started as a small, family-run cleaning operation has grown into one of the Kansas City metro s most trusted commercial cleaning companies. Today we serve hundreds of buildings across Lenexa, Overland Park, Olathe, Lee s Summit, Leawood, Mission, Parkville, and downtown Kansas City. From corporate high-rises to neighborhood retail shops, our teams handle buildings of every size and type. We believe that good cleaning starts with good listening. Before we ever pick up a mop, we walk through your facility with you, learn about your priorities, and put together a cleaning plan that fits your building and your budget. That personal approach is something our clients have come to rely on, and it is a big part of why so many of them have been with us for years. Every member of our team goes through thorough training and a background check before they step into your building. We carry full liability insurance and workers compensation coverage, and our supervisors conduct regular quality inspections to make sure our standards never slip.
