Located in Greenville, NC, we have provided tax and accounting services since 2002. Our expertise ranges from tax management and accounting services to more in-depth services such as audits and financial statement preparation. Accounting and auditing services include auditing defined-contribution employee benefit plans such as 401k, audits of manufacturing entities, and preparation of compiled financial statements. Our firm memberships include the American Institute of Certified Professional Accountants (AICPA), AICPA Private Companies Practice Section (PCPS), and Employee Benefit Plan Audit Quality Center (EBPAQC). Our mission is to help clients maintain financial viability in the present while taking a proactive approach to achieve future goals. This requires open communication to reach an understanding of our clients' needs through research and sound analysis. Terry Ng, CPA, PLLC is dedicated to meeting these goals with high standards of excellence and professionalism.
