About - Edge Group Skip to content About Us Retained search since 1994. Based in Houston, Texas, serving clients nationwide. Thirty years of successful placement The Edge Group, founded in 1994, began as a retained search firm specializing in healthcare and public accounting sectors. Over the past thirty years, we have maintained a strong partnership with our first client, UT MD Anderson, successfully placing over 160 professionals with the institution. Our expertise in researching and screening candidates has allowed us to expand into a broad range of for-profit and non-profit industries on a national scale. Our commitment to understanding each client’s unique culture, specific needs, and long-term objectives has fostered enduring relationships that we deeply value. Today, The Edge Group continues to play a crucial role in shaping leadership teams for our clients, leveraging our extensive experience and dedication to excellence. With thirty years in the business, we are able to recognize, assess, and attract candidates with the experience and talent that aligns with your requirements. In carefully considering our clients’ culture and objectives, we present the candidates that are most appropriately qualified to succeed. The key to our strategic methodology is to research organizations in a manner that identifies candidates that match our client’s needs. The Edge Group performs an average of seventy-five searches a year across North America and have been ranked as one of the top fifteen retained search firms in Houston by the Houston Business Journal for the past twelve years. The attentive and objective approach we practice has allowed us to achieve continued success in a wide range of industries.
