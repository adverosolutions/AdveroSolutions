Some of his specific areas of expertise include oil gas, power, renewable energy, process industries (ie chemicals,refining), aerospace and defense, decarbonization, EPC, infrastructure, OEMs, discreet and contract manufacturing, industrial services, and industrial automation. Wrendon draws on his management experience and cross-functional background to help clients address their most pressing talent needs. A veteran of the search industry, Wrendon has a strong track-record of executive and board placements in both private equity and publicly traded companies. Prior to founding H C Search, Wrendon worked for two top-tier global search firms, most recently as the Managing Director and Sector Lead for the Industrial and Manufacturing Practice. Previously, Wrendon was a core member of the chemicals process industries, industrials, CEO, CFO, PE and CIO practices at his previous firm. Prior to executive search, Wrendon served as a director of a Houston-based financial advisory and asset management firm. Previously, he served as a Presidential appointee and White House Fellow. In this capacity, he was a special assistant and advisor to the Secretary of Labor on workforce training and corporate engagement. Earlier, Wrendon worked for Dow Chemical Company, initially as a General Manager of a global commodity chemical line and subsequently as a Commercial Director for Dow s Solar Energy Business. He began his career as a Surface Warfare Officer in the United States Navy, completing two Western Pacific deployments.
