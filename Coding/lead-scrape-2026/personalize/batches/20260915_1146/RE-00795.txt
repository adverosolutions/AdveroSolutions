Kate Bannister · Founder A talent advisor who's been on both sides of the table. For more than two decades, Kate has worked at the intersection of design leadership and organizational decision-making — partnering with companies when what got them here won't get them there, and with leaders when the market isn't reflecting what they actually bring. She brings a rare dual perspective: understanding what companies actually need from senior creative leadership, and knowing how exceptional leaders think, operate, and scale impact. That perspective shaped Hive's core belief — the best teams aren't hired, they're designed. Kate founded Hive to be the partner she always wished existed. One that brings clarity, rigor, and care to leadership decisions that shape entire organizations — and to individual leaders navigating a market that doesn't always see them clearly. She is known for seeing what others miss: when a role is mis-scoped, when a structure will fail, and when a leader is right or wrong for the moment. "The best teams aren't hired — they're designed."
