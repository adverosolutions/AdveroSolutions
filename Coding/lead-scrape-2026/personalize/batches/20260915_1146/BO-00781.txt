ABout us Meet our incredible team LEIDY GONZALEZ Leidy Gonzalez CPA (NC Certificate # 40783) was born in Colombia, South America, but moved to New York City when she was 11 years old. While serving in the New York Army National Guard post-high school, she attended and graduated from Baruch College with a Bachelor of Business Administration with a concentration in Accounting in May 2007. In 2016 she obtained her Master’s degree in Taxation from St John’s University. Leidy brings over 15 years of experience in public accounting to the firm. She started her career at Raich Ende Malter Co; now Eisner Amper. More recently she worked as a Tax Senior Manager at a top 10 international CPA firm. She has spent her career serving closely-held businesses, pass-through entities, multi-state corporations, and high net-worth individuals in a variety of industries including real estate, public relations and professional services. Leidy is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) and the North Carolina Association of Certified Public Accountants (NCACPA). In 2016 Leidy moved from New York City to Apex, NC where she lives with her husband, two daughters and Lucy, the family’s labradoodle. She enjoys traveling, reading, exercising and watching comedy shows whenever possible. JOCELYN BRUMMETT Jocelyn Brummett CPA (NC Certificate #25567) began her public accounting career in 1995 and has worked for several firms including Cherry Bekaert LLP (formerly Cherry, Bekaert amp; Holland LLP) in Charlotte, NC and Thomas Judy Tucker PA in Raleigh, NC. She enjoys working with closely held businesses and their owners, assisting them with their tax, payroll, and accounting needs. Her past experience includes the following industries: retail and service companies, real estate and construction, manufacturing and distribution, and medical offices. Jocelyn graduated Cum Laude from Wake Forest University with a Bachelor of Science degree in Business and Accounting in May 1995. She is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and the North Carolina Association of Certified Public Accountants. Jocelyn lives in Holly Springs with her husband and two sons and is a sustaining member of the Junior League of Raleigh after 15 years of active membership.
