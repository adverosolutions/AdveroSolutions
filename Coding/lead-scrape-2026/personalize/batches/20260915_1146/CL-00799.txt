About Us NEMCO, Inc. | Commercial Maintenance Since 1983 43 Elizabeth Street Hackensack, NJ 07601 (201) 883-1008 office@nemcoinc.com About Us We are a full service contract maintenance provider. PROFESSIONAL. RELIABLE. FLEXIBLE. Our Services Our customers are commercial properties throughout the metropolitan area, mostly in New Jersey. We offer a large range of services including cleaning, concierge, landscape and construction. The parent company, Nemco, Inc. , is a commercial cleaning company. We offer a full range of services including daily cleaning, day matrons/porters, carpet cleaning, floor care, window cleaning, metal restoration, marble restoration and too many other tasks to list. We also provide front desk concierge for residential buildings. Nemco Landscaping, Inc. provides a complete range of grounds services including weekly care, trimming, tree care, chemical applications, landscape design, installation, pavers, retaining walls and every other related job. Nemco Pro, Inc. provides construction related services such as interior build out, sheet rock, carpentry, tile, handy man, renovations and any kind of general maintenance chore at your building or home.
