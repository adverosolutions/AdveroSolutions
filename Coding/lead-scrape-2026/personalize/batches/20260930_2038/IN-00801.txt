First Fed Insurance Skip to content Who We Are First Fed Insurance is a subsidiary of First Federal Savings Loan Association of Central Illinois offering a broad range of insurance products for individuals and businesses. We are committed to providing unparalleled customer service and insurance expertise. With insurance offices in Shelbyville, Stewardson, and Charleston as well as agents at the Shelbyville branch of First Federal, we can serve all your insurance needs wherever it is most convenient for you. Our History 1994 Killam Englund Insurance Agency opened in Shelbyville, Illinois. Over time, due to great customer service and the Killam and Englund families’ commitment to the Shelby County community, the agency steadily grew and added more quality carriers and products. 2017 Killam Englund Insurance Agency was acquired by First Federal Savings Loan Association of Central Illinois and became First Fed Insurance Services, a subsidiary of First Federal. Together, First Fed Insurance Services and First Federal can offer their customers expanded products and services to meet more of their financial needs. 2022 First Fed Insurance purchased Richards Insurance Agency in Stewardson, Illinois, adding another convenient Shelby County location to serve Stewardson and the surrounding communities. 2025 First Fed Insurance purchased Tomco Insurance Agency in Charleston. Tomco (originally Clarence Miller Insurance) is an established, successful agency that has been serving Charleston and the surrounding areas since 1977. This purchase adds another convenient First Fed Insurance location, and the Tomco team brings decades of insurance experience and expertise to serve our customers.
