Our Story: 30+ Years of Biological Lawn Care The Nature's Select ® Story From Our Roots to Today The approach took root long before the company did. Bill Hildebolt's upbringing on an Ohio family farm and his training in food science and technology shaped the soil-first approach he brought to lawn care in 1994. What follows traces three decades of biological lawn care across North Carolina, told through the people and decisions that shaped Nature's Select ® into the company it is today. 1994: The Founding Dr. Bill Hildebolt founded Nature's Select ® Premium Turf Services in Winston-Salem, drawing on his food-technology and farming background and his interest in nature's role in soil systems. Soon after opening, Bill was joined by Gene Queen. Together, they ran a successful lawn care company that used a biologically enhanced approach, creating healthy soils on hundreds of home lawns. This was the first Nature's Select ® Operating Unit. That founding company still operates in Winston-Salem today. It runs independently of the owner-operated franchises that later carried the biological approach across the Triad, Triangle, and Sandhills. 2009: Industry Leadership and the First Book Bill served as president of the Professional Landcare Network and wrote his first book, The Professional Entrepreneur (later republished as When Cowboys Grow Up), which conveys his business insights and the stories of twelve green-industry entrepreneurs. While writing the book, Bill met Robert Hayter, a green-industry consultant and business owner. That conversation led to exploring a business development approach built on partnerships with full-service landscape companies. 2014: The Second Operating Unit The second Nature's Select ® Operating Unit was formed with Morris Newlin, then-owner of New Garden Landscaping in Greensboro. Within five years, it grew from 300 to 1,200 customers, proving the model could scale biological lawn care beyond the founding location. 2017: NS Group, Inc. Formed The NS Group, Inc. was formed to own the intellectual property and business development rights for Nature's Select ® .
