Brothers Doug and Andy McDuff started Landscape America back in 2006 with a desire to provide a place of real opportunity for landscape professionals. Their goal was to build a team that views the industry as a rewarding career and their company as a place to grow. Over the years, they have continually fostered a company culture that encourages individual input, superior customer service, and continual improvement. Today, Landscape America has a staff of more than 100 highly motivated individuals committed to delivering the best for their commercial clients in Massachusetts and Rhode Island.
