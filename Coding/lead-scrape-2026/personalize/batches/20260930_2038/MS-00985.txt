Check Availability 7+ Years in Business 2,200+ Customers Connected 44+ Towers Deployed 12+ Counties Served Upward Broadband is a fixed wireless internet provider distributing fast, reliable internet to rural areas in Pennsylvania, West Virginia, and Maryland. Some of the underserved counties we serve are Bedford, Centre, Chester, Cumberland, Dauphin, Franklin, Fulton, Huntingdon , Lebanon , Mifflin, York, and Lancaster County. We started to give rural Pennsylvania a choice. A choice for internet service without the gimmicks, the bundles, and the frustrations. We re a Lancaster County-based, family-owned company with a strong set of values that guide everything we do. For you, that means you ll be dealing with a company that does the right thing, not the easiest or most profitable. Using cutting-edge technology, you can connect to the internet without cable or DSL lines. Our quick and non-invasive installation process gives you fast access to the internet via secure radiowaves — backed by a team of local experts dedicated to serving you. Our Story We Put the Rural in Rural Internet Service Brothers Tim, Marcus, Matthew, and Jason Beiler grew up on their family farm in Lancaster County. As businessmen, they realized first-hand how important it is for rural farms, homes, and businesses to stay connected — and how challenging that can be with traditional providers. In 2009, the brothers formed Paradise Energy Solutions, a Lancaster-County-based solar energy installer dedicated to helping people be good stewards of God s natural resources. A few years later, they wanted to expand and provide a solution to another problem — the internet. That s why they formed Upward Broadband in 2019 — a simple solution that helps south-central Pennsylvania stay connected. Providing a fast, reliable, and secure way to access the internet without the bundles, gimmicks, and frustrations, Upward Broadband gives you the internet you need without the hassle.
