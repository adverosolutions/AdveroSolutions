(IES) is a Small, Woman-Owned Business Enterprise with corporate headquarters centrally located in the Dallas/Fort Worth Metroplex in beautiful McKinney, Texas. IES is a specialized environmental consulting firm that recognizes and flourishes in the opportunities available in local markets, as well as venturing into regional and larger federal markets. IES provides a client-centered approach to environmental regulatory compliance and natural and cultural resources management support - services that focus on the client, client mission/objectives, and making the client's job easy on a day-to-day basis. The services provided by IES allow its clients to fully focus on their job while IES staff focus on compliance with environmental laws and regulatory requirements. The principals and key staff alike share performance histories through projects at locations throughout the United States and Puerto Rico. Staff has experience in managing and implementing broadly scoped programs and projects throughout Texas, the southern and northeastern U.S., and the Caribbean. Efforts have ranged from short, quick turn-around projects of less than 10 acres, to large, multi-year projects encompassing entire military installations or stretching over several states. Staff have extensive working knowledge of the ever-changing federal and state laws and agency regulations that can delay implementation of a project. Knowledge of the potential roadblocks and years of field experience allows IES staff to routinely implement techniques and methods to save its clients time and money, avoiding delays and ensuring that mission/objectives get fulfilled.
