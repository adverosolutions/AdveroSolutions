Our History Owning an insurance agency was a win-win for Livingston Insurance Agency founders Robert (Bob) Livingston and Raye Livingston. He saw it as a chance to become self-employed and help the community. Along with his wife, Raye, Bob started the company on September 28, 1958. Despite taking out a second mortgage to get off the ground, Bob and Raye quickly learned the ropes. Over the years, other family members would also lend a hand. Generations have watched the insurance industry grow and evolve. So much has changed over the years. Auto and fire policies were once the sole offerings.
