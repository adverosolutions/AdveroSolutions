An ISA Certified Arborist is a tree care professional who has earned certification from the International Society of Arboriculture (ISA). This credential signifies expert knowledge in tree health, safety, and proper maintenance practices. To achieve ISA certification, an arborist must have at least three years of industry experience, successfully pass a comprehensive exam, and maintain ongoing education to stay current with best practices.
