Stokes CPA Managing Partner Our Story Stokes & Company began in 1999 as Stokes & Bales, with a simple goal: To be a trusted financial partner for local businesses. When Stephen Stokes became managing partner, the firm took on his name and a renewed vision to deliver big-firm expertise with personal, small-firm care. Today, with over 50 team members across five offices in Upstate South Carolina and Western North Carolina, our focus remains the same: serving our clients with excellence, building relationships that last, and staying close to the communities we call home. For more than 25 years, we’ve supported hundreds of small businesses with accounting, advisory, and tax services designed to help them grow with confidence.
