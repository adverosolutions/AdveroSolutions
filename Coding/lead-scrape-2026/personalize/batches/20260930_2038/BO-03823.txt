﻿ About Nelson Company, P.C. | CPA and Business Consulting Tyler Tyler is a licensed Certified Public Accountant in the state of Oregon. He studied accounting, business management, French and the arts at Brigham Young University before moving back to the Pacific Northwest where he grew up. He received his Bachelor of Arts degree in Accounting in 2007 from Portland State University. He has been preparing taxes since 2006, while working at the family business and another larger local firm. During this time he also gained six years of experience working with an international tax software company, marrying modern technological advancements with the tax world. Tyler is a current member of the Oregon Society of CPA s and the AICPA. When he’s not working, he enjoys spending time with his wife and four kids, traveling (when possible), cooking, sports (especially Trailblazers basketball, soccer, and surfing), comedy, trying new foods, and having new experiences. Becky We are very lucky to have Becky working with us. Becky is essential to our practice and keeps things moving. She is a personal finance management expert and is great at wearing many ‘hats’ at once. Becky loves spending time with her husband and 3 kids, preferably outdoors, riding her bicycle, and the Trailblazers.
