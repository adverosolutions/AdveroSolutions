About | Denmark Lawn Skip to content About Denmark Lawn Seventeen years on White Settlement Road Denmark Lawn started in 2009 as a weekend hustle — Kyle Denmark mowing yards out of a Mazda Protegé hatchback to help pay for college. Today it's seven crews, one shop at 8539 White Settlement Rd, and the same phone number from the first season. The whole fleet, 2009. How we got here Kyle and Caitlin Denmark founded the company in 2009. The equipment fit in the back of a hatchback, the customers were neighbors, and the plan was to cover tuition. It was a one-man operation for longer than the photo suggests. Mowing led to fertilization, then landscaping, aeration, and holiday lights — added one at a time, each when customers kept asking for it. We grew by staying close to home: every crew still rolls out of White Settlement Road each morning, which is why we say west of I-35 and mean it. The plan in 2009 was a car that started. Now the plan is a company that runs well whether or not the owner is on site that day — so we put a business manager, an operations manager, and a director of client experience in place before the eighth truck. Every person on the crew is trained to the same standard, and the same team sees your yard every week. Why we only work west of I-35 It's not a limit; it's the reason the schedule holds. Our shop sits on the west side, every crew starts there, and a tight service area is what lets us promise 24–48 hour scheduling on maintenance and show up the same day each week.
