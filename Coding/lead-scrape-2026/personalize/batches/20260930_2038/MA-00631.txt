Broad Museum Legacy CLIENT: MICHIGAN STATE UNIVERSITY ELI AND EDYTHE BROAD ART MUSEUM The Eli and Edythe Broad Art Museum is a wonderfully angular, reflective, and unique architectural conversation piece on the campus of Michigan State University. For this fundraising brochure we focused on the thing that was the biggest identifier for the Broad — the angular shape. We diecut the brochure in the shape of the museum, and printed a photo of all it's angles on the back, over a metallic silver sheet, to simulate the dynamic, silver, building itself. About Image Creative Group Menu ABOUT IMAGE CREATIVE GROUP Jennifer Rosseter is the force behind Image Creative Group. Her history encompasses all aspects of creative development including art direction, graphic design, and illustration. Fast forward through working as a children’s illustrator, and as an art director for large and small advertising agencies servicing national accounts in industry, automotive, restaurants, retail, insurance, architecture, higher ed and nonprofits, to running her own boutique graphic design firm with a mix of higher ed, nonprofits, business and retail accounts. The common denominator is really creative marketing solutions and happy, loyal clients in both consumer and business audiences. As a business owner and creative leader, Jennifer has overseen many high-profile accounts and has retained a legacy of happy clients for as long as she has been in business (which is a long time, but who's counting?). Throughout, Jennifer has earned the respect of her peers and clients alike, and has won hundreds of awards both nationally and regionally, including Addy, Addy Best of Show, Judge’s Choice, and Mead Top Sixty, among others. Aside from her graphic design expertise, she is also a potter, and a painter currently on a journey of discovery and ‘hundreds’.
