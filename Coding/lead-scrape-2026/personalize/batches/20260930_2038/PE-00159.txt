About Atlas | Atlas Home Inspections, LLC Skip to content About Atlas At Atlas Home Inspections, we know that you want a safe, reliable home that is worth your investment. The problem is that most people can’t properly evaluate structural and mechanical systems, which makes them feel apprehensive about the largest investment they may ever make. We understand this, because we’re a family business that’s helped thousands of people with the same concerns ensure safety for their families and longevity for their homes. Our simple method generates confidence and peace of mind during the home buying process. Here’s how we do it. First, we streamline the administrative process – the Schedule Now button on this website is the magic wand that simplifies all. Then, if you’re able to attend the home inspection, we guide you through the home step-by-step, educating you and answering your questions. Third, we provide a detailed report the day of the home inspection that includes photos, descriptions, and suggestions for maintenance. Think of it as an operating manual for your home. So – to be confident about one of the biggest decisions you’ll ever make, schedule with Atlas. When you do, you’ll stop feeling unsure, anxious, and overwhelmed. Instead, you can move forward confidently with assurance that your purchase was a good investment, and enjoy life with your family in a safe, dependable home.
