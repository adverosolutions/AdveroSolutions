About CBM | Reliable Experienced Tax Experts Advisors CBM History About CBM's Journey With years of experience and a results-focused approach, we empower businesses to navigate complexity and achieve their goals. 1981 Clayton, Byrd Meeks (CBM) was founded when Mr. Clayton, Mr. Byrd, and Mr. Meeks combined their experience from other firms to build a new practice at 720 Broadway St. 1995 Mr. Clayton retired from the firm. 2017 Mr. Yates joined CBM after several years at a public accounting firm in Paducah. 2018 Mr. Whalen and Mr. Hite joined CBM from local CPA firms. That same year, Yates, Whalen, and Hite were admitted as Partners — marking a new era of growth. Mr. Byrd and Mr. Meeks retired from the partnership but continued on as Of Counsel. May 2019 CBM merged with Neal Carroll s accounting practice, expanding our client base and expertise. October 2019 We moved to our current location at 1901 Kentucky Avenue. 2022 Mr. Carroll retired from CBM. January 2026 CBM merged with Moffit Co., continuing under the CBM name. 2018–Present Since our new partners were admitted, CBM has grown from 7 staff members to 20, and achieved over 330% revenue growth, a direct result of our commitment to clients and to each other.
