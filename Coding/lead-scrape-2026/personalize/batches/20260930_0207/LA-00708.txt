About LILIPUT INC Skip to content Liliput: Family is in our Roots Established in 2003 as a small, family-owned business, Liliput was founded with the mission of treating each and every client like a member of the family. Having been trained by their mother who is an accomplished landscape architect, Erik and Brian McMorrow have always taken a highly detailed and personalized approach to landscape construction and maintenance. Now part of the Mariani Premier Group family of companies, the Liliput family has grown to include not only additional clients, but multiple maintenance and installation crews. We still hold the original mission at the forefront, and continue to strive toward unparalleled customer service. Erik McMorrow Owner Erik McMorrow was raised in the landscaping industry, and takes a committed and hands on approach to all aspects of Liliput, Inc. (Landscape Contractor License: C-27 862822) Brian McMorrow Owner Brian McMorrow joined Liliput in 2007, bringing with him an excellent customer service philosophy and focused attention to detail that play an integral role in Liliput installations.
