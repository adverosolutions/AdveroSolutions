About | Gallaher Landscaping Gallaher Landscaping was formed in 2000 by Joshua M. Gallaher. The company grew from a teenage entrepreneur earning money for college and began with a push mower, weed trimmer, gas blower, and a 1991 pick-up truck. In 2008, Josh graduated from Penn State University Park with a Bachelor’s of Science degree in Business Marketing from the Smeal College of Business. Upon graduation Josh still maintained his part time lawn and landscape business, while working full time for the Deeter Gallaher Group in Mechanicsburg, PA as a Marketing Strategist. In 2009, Josh launched Gallaher Landscaping as a full-time operating business, right in the middle of the worst recession in recent history. Today, the Gallaher Landscaping fleet serves over 150 residential and commercial clients by serving all your landscaping needs from the ground up!
