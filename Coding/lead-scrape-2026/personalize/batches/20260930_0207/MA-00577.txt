Little Red Fern About The Fern Ryan Cheevers is the founder of Little Red Fern Productions , a storytelling powerhouse that brings your brand’s vision to life with creativity and flair. With clients across the U.S., Ryan’s expertise in content creation spans from long-form projects to punchy social media edits, ensuring your story gets heard—whether it’s a minute-long ad or a feature-length script. Ryan's storytelling roots trace back to Hollywood, where he contributed to iconic projects like Dumb and Dumber To, The Wolf of Wall Street, and Daddy’s Home. But beyond the big screen, Ryan thrives on the creative process—the magic of taking an idea and crafting it into something unforgettable. That passion extends to sports broadcasting as well, where he created and produced Friday Football Tailgate , a high-energy high school football pregame show celebrating local teams, rivalries, and community spirit. At Little Red Fern , Ryan’s mission is simple: tell stories that captivate, entertain, and leave an impact. Whether he's working with global brands, emerging businesses, or producing original content, Ryan’s passion for storytelling drives every project. If you've got a story to tell, Ryan's your guy.
