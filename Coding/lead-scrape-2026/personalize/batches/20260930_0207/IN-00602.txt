About Serhey Davidson | NYC Commercial Insurance Since 1947 | Serhey Davidson About Serhey Davidson – NYC Commercial Insurance Since 1947 Continuing a Legacy: The Serhey Davidson Story, From Phil Robbins to Lacey Riger Over 79 years ago, Phil Robbins founded Serhey Davidson Corp a block from Wall Street, starting with a single policy to serve local businesses. His vision was simple yet powerful: provide exceptional commercial insurance services with a personal touch that larger firms couldn t match. Phil s commitment to understanding each client s unique needs and building lasting relationships set the foundation for what would become a multi-generational family business. He believed that insurance wasn t just about policies—it was about protecting dreams, businesses, and livelihoods. In 1965, Phil Robbins was recognized as the #1 producer in the United States for Health Insurance Paid First Year Premiums by Berkshire Life Insurance Company—a testament to his dedication and the company s early success. His son, Herb Robbins, continued this legacy, enhancing the company s reputation in commercial insurance throughout the New York metropolitan area. Under Herb s leadership, Serhey Davidson expanded its expertise, particularly in construction and commercial property insurance, while maintaining the personalized service that had become the company s hallmark. Today, the third-generation leader, Lacey Riger, upholds these values while infusing traditional principles with modern strategies. Her expertise in Construction Risk Insurance has propelled the company into a new era of success and innovation. Lacey has expanded the company s reach while staying true to the core values established by her grandfather: integrity, expertise, and unwavering commitment to client success. Under Lacey s leadership, Serhey Davidson has embraced technology to streamline processes while maintaining the personal, consultative approach that sets us apart. We continue to specialize in construction insurance, commercial liability , workers compensation , and a full range of business insurance products.
