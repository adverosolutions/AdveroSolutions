About Rootsy Garden Design Skip to content Menu Rootsy Garden Design Rootsy Garden Design specializes in beautiful, functional and eco-conscious landscape design for residents in the Peninsula Bay Area of California. Through guided collaboration with clients, we contribute our horticulture and design expertise to help garden dreams come true. We strive to enhance the lives of our clients with thoughtful design solutions that satisfy budget, lifestyle and ongoing maintenance plans – while making ecologically sensitive decisions along the way. Our clients can take pride in choosing to conserve water, support native wildlife and foster healthy soil when choosing Rootsy Garden Design to create their custom outdoor living space.
