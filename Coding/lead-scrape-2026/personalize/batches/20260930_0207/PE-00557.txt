About Steve’s Pest Control | Mid-Missouri Pest Control Skip to content About Us Get free estimate See more videos Professional Pest Control Services Steve’s Pest Control, Inc. is a family-owned and operated company founded by Steve and Anita Hotsenpiller in 1988. Since then, Steve’s Pest Control has grown to become one of the largest independent pest control operations in Missouri. This achievement has been made by taking exceptional care of our clients through honest, dependable service. Our goal is to continue to exceed the expectations of our clients and to earn the reputation of being the best pest control company in Missouri. Steve has always believed a service company is only as good as the pest control technician doing the actual work. At Steve’s Pest Control, all of our service technicians have been personally selected by Steve based on attitude and professionalism. Additionally, all of our pest control technicians are state licensed and have gone through rigorous training. But, our education doesn’t stop there! Our pest control team is continuously participating in ongoing education to stay on top of pest control best practices. Plus, all service technicians are required to become Certified Commercial Operators once they are qualified. At Steve’s Pest Control, we are truly committed to providing you the very best service experience possible!
