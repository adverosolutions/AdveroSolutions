We have years of design and landscaping experience. We are fighting back against deforestation, habitat loss, and our wasteful food systems. Together, we can make a difference that will branch out much farther than our little pocket of the world. Founded in 2023 by Joshua Ferguson, a Bucks County PA native. Spending most of his childhood outdoors and camping, a lifelong love for plants and animals was instilled in him at a young age. He was also quite artistic, always drawing and making things. Josh has been designing professionally for over a decade on a progressive path. It started with Architecture, which is traditionally what our public school system will shoehorn creative design thinking people into. Being the ambitious type he is, he began his career as soon as possible. While working at an Architecture firm as a draftsman, Josh acquired the skills that allow him to make our detailed and thorough site plans. During his pursuit of Architecture Josh was always learning how to make things for himself. He was always crafty and had a knack for picking up the skills for building/crafting with his hands. One time when he was teaching himself how to make a backpack he discovered the discipline of Industrial Design. In the following weeks he became obsessed with learning about Industrial Design. Shortly after, he realized that it was a better fit for him than Architecture. Josh graduated from Philadelphia University with a bachelors degree in Industrial Design and began working at a Design consultancy in the city. At a consultancy you get your hands on a variety of different products from many different industries all with their own individual and specific needs. To be successful in that field you need to be versatile, adaptable, and have the skills to deliver high quality designs to your various clients.
