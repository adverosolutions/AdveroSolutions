History of Stockbridge Risk Management and Meet the Team 516-487-1700 contact@stockbridgerisk.com 60 Cutter Mill Road, Suite 500 • Great Neck, NY 11021 Our History Founded in 1980 by Dan and Robert Sterling, Stockbridge Risk Management, Inc s corporate charter was established to provide independent, unbiased counsel to a wide variety of businesses in the area of risk and insurance. For over four decades, our seasoned professionals have been providing superior customer support and successfully improving the insurance programs of our clients, while reducing or containing their cost for risk and insurance. Stockbridge’s history now spans three generations, as Robert’s son Benjamin joined the firm in December of 2016. Today, Stockbridge employs fourteen consultants with a total staff of 20, serving more than 200 clients nationwide as independent risk and insurance professionals. Because we do not sell insurance, we are able to objectively determine the solutions that are right for your company.
