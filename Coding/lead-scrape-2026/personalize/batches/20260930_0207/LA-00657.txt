About wilderline 0 Wilderline Ecological Design Group was born from a vision of reconnecting native landscapes with the urban environment. Based in Dallas, TX, our work centers around creating spaces where human and ecological health are one and the same, allowing people to ground themselves in the natural beauty of their surroundings while providing critical habitat for local wildlife. Meet Evo lineberry After spending a decade in the landscape design industry and exploring Texas’ wild places, Evo launched Wilderline Ecological Design Group. With an extensive background in landscape design, native habitat restoration, and a whole life lived in the city, she understands the unique approach needed to merge the natural environment with modern urbanism. Evo serves on various boards dedicated to this mission and is a certified Texas Master Naturalist. She lives in East Dallas.
