About - Quality Tree Care Our repeat customers love the "local tree guy" and are happy to refer new customers. We have built our business on honesty, quality work and customer satisfaction. Quality Tree Care is owned and operated by Joe Mattos, who was born and raised in the local area and started his first business by clearing hillsides for fire regulations at just 14 years old. This eventually led to tree trimming and the formation of Quality Tree Care. Joe is a licensed tree service contractor and certified arborist with the International Society of Arborculture. His entrepreneurial spirit and devotion to providing quality tree care and customer satisfaction is the driving force of his business.
