About | Discover Our Story Bronwen Consulting 0 Our Story At Bronwen Consulting, we don’t just understand healthcare — we’ve lived it, built it, and brought it to life on stages around the country. Our founder, Bronwen Huron , brings a rare combination of bedside experience, enterprise strategy, and systems thinking to everything we do. With a background that spans clinical nursing, product ownership, nonprofit advocacy, and national standards development, Bronwen has spent her career translating between what’s technically possible and what’s truly valuable. After years spent building digital health products, leading interoperability efforts, and supporting frontline change initiatives, she saw a pattern: too often, great ideas were buried in jargon, fragmented efforts, or missed opportunities to connect with the people who mattered most. Bronwen Consulting was born from a desire to change that. Today, we work at the intersection of health IT , marketing , standards , and event strategy to help our clients bring their stories to life — clearly, credibly, and with staying power. Whether it’s designing an interactive demo pavilion, crafting messaging that resonates across technical and clinical audiences, or guiding interoperability efforts from concept to execution, we show up as both strategic partners and hands-on collaborators. Because when it comes to health IT, success isn’t just about what you build. It’s about how you communicate it , implement it , and connect it to the bigger picture . I've been fortunate to work with Bronwen as she coordinated, ideated, built and ran healthcare industry events for around ten years. In that time, she has been professional, personable, cooperative, and knowledgeable. She brings a detail-oriented background in healthcare, interoperability and inter-vendor coordination that goes a long way to making those events successful. She shows a consistent ability to bring stakeholders to agreement on clear messaging and a patient-centered focus, helping all participants achieve their goals together.
