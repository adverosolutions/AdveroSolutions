Noble Vision About Noble Vision Founded by Ian Noble — a software engineer with 30+ years building enterprise platforms, now pioneering AI-driven automation that compresses weeks of work into minutes. The Story I started writing Python for network management in the early 90s — automating monitoring, alerting, and infrastructure management for organizations that couldn t afford downtime. NASA. The US Army. Fortune 500 companies. For two decades, I built the invisible plumbing that kept enterprise networks running. In 2022, I built my first application with GPT-3. It was a cybersecurity graph analytics platform called TruContext. It took three weeks and $150 in API costs. That s when I realized the rules had changed. A year later, I stopped writing code manually entirely. Everything was AI-generated. I was among the first users on the waitlists for Cursor, Windsurf, Manus AI, Augment, and Cline — because I could see where this was going before most people believed it. By late 2024, I was shipping complete MVP applications in one hour. Today, in 2026, I delegate coding to AI agents that can build and deploy production applications in 15 minutes — sometimes during a phone call with the stakeholder, before the conversation even ends. That acceleration — from three weeks to 15 minutes — is what Noble Vision brings to every engagement. Whether it s building autonomous agent marketplaces at CVS Health, developing smart city cybersecurity platforms at Visium Technologies, or creating AI-powered tools for communities like NuFounders, the mission is the same: compress what s possible.
