About KIG About Us Our History Created to serve the community by offering commercial, personal, life, health, and disability insurance products, William Korotkin founded KIG in 1913. KIG has continued to grow throughout the years and has built a strong customer base and a respected reputation in the community. Today, the agency is under the leadership of the next generation of partners, who have taken insurance and customer service to the next level in response to social and technological changes. As they have in the past, KIG will continue to meet the insurance needs of the community with a commitment, dedication and responsibility to those they serve.
