Nature is our foremost collaborator Mariposa Gardening Design Cooperative, Inc. is a women-led landscape design build cooperative that is founded on restoring our relationship with nature. Eco-Friendly We place emphasis on installing habitat gardens to attract a diverse array of butterflies, songbirds and pollinators. Conscientious We choose ethically sourced materials to ensure the smallest environmental footprint, and support local economies with our cooperative business structure. LEARN MORE Caring We are a worker-owned cooperative committed to creating a safe and inclusive work environment that celebrates diversity and inspires our communities.
