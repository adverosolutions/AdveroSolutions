Horticare Landscape Management | About our company About Horticare Landscape Management Since 2008, Horticare Landscape Management has provided professional full-service landscape management solutions throughout the Greater Houston area. What began as a commitment to delivering dependable landscape maintenance has grown into a trusted company serving large homeowners associations, commercial properties, and public infrastructure projects across the region. At Horticare, we understand that landscape management is about more than simply maintaining grass and plants — it is about creating attractive, functional, and professionally maintained environments that enhance curb appeal, protect property values, and leave lasting impressions on residents, tenants, and visitors alike. Our team specializes in managing large-scale properties with a proactive and detail-oriented approach. We work closely with HOA boards, property managers, municipalities, and commercial clients to ensure every property receives consistent service, clear communication, and long-term care tailored to its unique needs. In addition to comprehensive landscape maintenance services, Horticare provides: Landscape construction and renovations HOA entrance enhancements and installations Full irrigation system installation and repair Seasonal color programs Landscape design and enhancements Drainage improvements Tree and shrub care Property cleanups and beautification projects What truly sets Horticare apart is our commitment to responsiveness, communication, and accountability. We believe successful landscape management starts with strong partnerships, which is why our team remains proactive in identifying property needs, addressing concerns quickly, and delivering dependable results year-round. From large master-planned communities and retail centers to municipal parks and public-use spaces, Horticare Landscape Management is proud to help maintain and enhance some of the Greater Houston area’s most important properties. For over 15 years, our mission has remained the same: To provide professional landscape solutions with integrity, reliability, and a commitment to excellence in every property we serve.
