About MCS Products MCS Products, founded in 2003 in Austin, Texas, specializes in mosquito control and outdoor living solutions. From mosquito control systems to mist cooling, heating, and a wide range of outdoor living products, we're your trusted experts for creating comfortable outdoor spaces. Previous Next 7,500 + Customers Served 50 States 16 Staff Members 23 Years Locally Owned and Operated MCS Products is a locally owned family business that takes pride in designing and overseeing the installation of your outdoor living system. We don't use contract labor; instead, our MCS professionals, with an average of ten years at the company and over 100 years of combined industry experience, are dedicated to ensuring your satisfaction every step of the way. All MCS field technicians are licensed through the Texas Department of Agriculture's Structural Pest Control Service, meeting all mandatory continuing education and licensing requirements. With our management/owners boasting 16 years of experience each in the industry and our technicians averaging a decade of experience in misting systems, we are fully insured above the state-required minimums in General Liability, Workers Compensation, and Auto Insurance.
