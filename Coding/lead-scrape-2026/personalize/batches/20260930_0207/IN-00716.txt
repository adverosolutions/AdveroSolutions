The O Neal Agency, Inc., located in Andalusia, AL, has proudly served the insurance needs of our community for over 60 years. The O Neal Agency was founded in 1956 by Robert C. O Neal. In an effort to expand the business, he hired his eldest son, Bond O Neal, to join him in the agency in 1962 after attending Auburn University. Lester O Neal, the youngest son, joined the agency in 1971 after graduating from Troy University. Prior to college, Lester also served four years in the Marine Corps, including two tours in Vietnam. Bob O Neal, Bond s son, joined the agency in 1991 after graduating from Auburn University. Mike O Neal, Lester s son, a Faulkner University graduate, joined the agency in 1995. The primary mission of the O Neal Agency is to provide our clients with service that is superior to that of our competition. The O Neal Agency specializes in personal as well as commercial insurance. Because of the many companies we represent, we are able to provide our customers with quality insurance protection to fit each customers unique needs at the lowest possible cost.
