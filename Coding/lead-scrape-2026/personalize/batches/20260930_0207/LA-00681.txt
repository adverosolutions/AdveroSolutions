About YesPlease.GARDEN | Diablo Tri-Valley Landscape Design Skip to content About Us YesPlease.GARDEN Local Landscape Design Planning Helping homeowners make the right decisions before anything is built. YesPlease.GARDEN is a landscape design studio based in the Diablo and Tri-Valley areas, focused on helping homeowners make clear, confident decisions about their outdoor spaces before anything is built. We design landscapes that work—functionally, visually, and over time. Our work sits at the intersection of space planning, materials, planting, and real-world construction, with an emphasis on clarity and longevity. We work with homeowners, as well as landscape professionals, who value clear documentation that translates cleanly into construction. Our work is often sought out when projects feel complex, undefined, or stuck between design and build. Who We Work With Our clients include: Homeowners planning a significant outdoor project Homeowners phasing work over time Families balancing budget, timeline, and long-term use Clients who want clarity before hiring a contractor or installer We also work alongside: Licensed landscape contractors Masonry and hardscape specialists Irrigation and lighting professionals Native plant and ecological landscape practitioners Rooted in the Community YesPlease.GARDEN serves the Diablo and Tri-Valley communities, including Danville, Alamo, Walnut Creek, Lafayette, Pleasanton, Livermore, and nearby areas. Based locally, with deep familiarity with soils, slopes, and microclimates Over 200 residential landscape designs completed More than 60 local installations guided through construction Founding member of California Native Plant Pros Ongoing involvement with Bringing Back the Natives and regional education efforts This work is shaped by living here, working here, and building long-term relationships with homeowners and local trades.
