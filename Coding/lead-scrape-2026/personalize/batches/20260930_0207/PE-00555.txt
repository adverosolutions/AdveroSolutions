Their ample knowledge of the business and hearty aspirations to serve residents in the Pacific Northwest ushered in Natura Pest Control in 2013. No matter the challenges, Jake and Jana worked day and night to provide customers with unparalleled service and build their company up from the beginning– leading to the household name we all know and love.
