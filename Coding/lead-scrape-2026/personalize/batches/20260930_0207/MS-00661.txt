About Palm Beach Flight Training | Palm Beach Flight Training professionalism and safety driven by our passion for aviation. From the day we were founded in 1998 , Palm Beach Flight Training has been dedicated to providing quality flight instruction and exceptionally maintained aircraft. This focus on professionalism and safety is what our customers expect and deserve, and it s driven by our passion for aviation. Our students are from all walks of life, and with different motivations for wanting to learn to fly. For some, the goal is to realize the dream of becoming a licensed pilot. Others come to get the training they need for a career in the airlines or corporate aviation, and still others seek the benefit of a pilot s license or aircraft ownership to help them in their work or business. But what they all have in common is a love for flying. If you re thinking about flight training, your next phone call should be to Palm Beach Flight Training. Here s just a few reasons why: The Area s Best Flight Instructors. Our highly trained instructional staff works as a team, constantly striving to refine their own skills as teachers and pilots, so they they can provide you with the finest flight training in the industry. An Uncompromising Dedication to Safety. When it comes to flying, safety and skill go hand in hand. Great flight training emphasizes both. Customer Service is Our Highest Priority. Your experience as a customer and a flight student matters. At Palm Beach Flight Training you ll learn in a relaxed atmosphere that makes training both productive and fun, and with a flexible schedule that will fit any lifestyle. The County s Largest and Most Diverse Aircraft Fleet. We have a beautiful and well-equipped assortment of aircraft for both training and rental, which means you ve always got options.
