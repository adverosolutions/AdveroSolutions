Learn how we do it ABOUT THE SILVERCHAIR PARTNERS TEAM Chantal "Taly" Russell Chantal "Taly" Russell is the CEO and founder of SilverChair Partners. Prior to founding “SCP” in January of 2013, Taly honed her craft from 2003-2012 at a renowned global Executive Search firm in which Taly specialized in recruiting the highest caliber support for CEO's, Partners and C-Suite power players in New York City. Taly is an industry expert and has personally placed over 1750 Executive Administrative and Human Resources professionals successfully over the years. Prior to joining the most senior echelons of Executive Search in 2003, Taly was a Casting Director in both New York and Los Angeles. Born in Zurich, Switzerland and having grown up in New York City, Taly attended New York University's Stern School with a double major in Business and Political Science. Taly is actively involved in a number of different philanthropies and is an active member on the Friends of Baby Buggy board in New York, LCRF, The Robin Hood Foundation and a member of the Junior Committee of the American Museum of Natural History.
