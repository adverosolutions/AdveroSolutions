Ashley Adams President Co-Owner Ashley is the powerhouse behind Spectrum ABA Billing, a company she built to raise the standard for ABA billing and credentialing. With more than 18 years in the medical field and over a decade dedicated to ABA, she s seen it all: messy AR, credentialing headaches, payer denials, and every insurance curveball imaginable. What sets her apart is not just her ability to fix the problem, but to teach providers and teams how to stay ahead of it. ​ Her journey into ABA started when she joined a clinic specializing in autism services. There, she fell in love with the field, not just for the life-changing work providers do, but for the challenge of navigating the insurance system that stands behind it. While many shy away from the billing grind, Ashley thrives on it. Her reputation in the ABA community reflects that passion: she s known as a trusted expert, a straight-shooter, and someone who gets things done. ​ Beyond running Spectrum, Ashley provides consulting, training, and resources to help ABA providers strengthen their revenue cycle management. She s not just a biller, she s an advocate for providers, a mentor in the field, and an active participant in industry conversations that shape the future of ABA. ​ Born and raised in Florida, Ashley still calls the Sunshine State home, where she lives with her husband and two daughters.
