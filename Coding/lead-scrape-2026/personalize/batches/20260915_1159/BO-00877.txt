Skip to content About Yusuf Schedule a Free Consultation (703)724-9406 --> Home About Yusuf Yusuf Kheire, CPA Managing Director Yusuf is the Founder and Managing Director of Kheire and Associates, CPA (K A) in 2011. During his 33 year plus in public accounting, Yusuf has served a client base including healthcare, construction, professional services, nonprofits, transportation and distribution, technology, telecommunication, retail, hospitality, schools, civic organization and Federal Government agencies. Yusuf began his financial services career in auditing with US Department of Health and Human Services, Office of Inspector General (OIG), Office of Audit Services, where he rose to senior audit supervisor. After 16 years of service, Yusuf left the OIG and joined US Department of Education, Office of the Chief Financial Officer, Internal Audit Division as a managing director. Yusuf always puts his client s best interests first. His dedication to serving doesn t stop with his clients either. He is involved in the community, serving as treasurer and board member for organizations such as the Orphan Care, Adams Compassionate Healthcare Network (past treasurer).
