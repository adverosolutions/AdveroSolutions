OUR MISSION To build the leading search and venture firm focused exclusively on the Cloud, Data AI space. Established in 2017 by founder Firas Sozan, Harrison Clarke is a trailblazer in the Cloud, Data AI recruiting landscape. With a deep-rooted focus on fostering collaboration and scaling product development, our journey began by addressing a gap in the market. Initially concentrated on DevOps/SRE recruitment, our vision soon expanded. Through organic growth, we ventured into DevSecOps, Platform Engineering and MLOps. In 2020, recognizing the common threads that bind these domains, we seamlessly integrated Artificial Intelligence into our portfolio, launching our second domain expertise, Data AI, focusing on such critical roles as Data Science and Engineering, Research and Machine Learning. Harrison Clarke thrives on innovation and synergy, resulting in a transformative impact on the tech industry. Our evolution echoes a commitment to excellence and an unwavering belief in technology's potential. In 2022, Harrison Clarke launched its corporate venture capital arm of the business, Harrison Clarke Ventures. Join us as we continue to redefine possibilities, bridging the gap between human ingenuity and technological advancement. We have hired and trained the very best recruiters to focus on the unique art of engaging and bringing to the hiring table engineering talent that is highly sought after. We understand our candidate's needs and career desires. We apply the indispensable skill of emotional intelligence to open the door of opportunity for our candidates and our clients.
