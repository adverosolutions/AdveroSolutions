ABOUT OUR PRESIDENT A Legacy Of Hard Work Dutch Valley President, Jason Musch has been involved in the landscaping industry since a young age and the solar industry since 2020. He recalls spending Saturdays washing his father s landscape trucks. This left such a positive impression that when it came time to choose a career, Musch decided to pursue a degree in Horticulture. He attended Joliet Junior College and received a degree in both Turf Management and Landscape Construction. In 2012 Musch purchased his father s landscape business and took over the day to day operations. Since then, he has expanded his business by adding more clients, crews and trucks while continuing his father s legacy. As part of the Illinois landscape Contractors Association and the National Association of Landscape professionals, Musch believes continued education and training are crucial to Dutch Valley s growth. Musch and his team attend industry sponsored events each year to keep up with the new trends in the industry and are always seeking efficiency without compromising quality. Musch has a passion for the outdoors and wouldn t change his story for anything. Like him, Musch s two boys come to work with him each Saturday. We look forward to seeing what the future will hold for Dutch Valley.
