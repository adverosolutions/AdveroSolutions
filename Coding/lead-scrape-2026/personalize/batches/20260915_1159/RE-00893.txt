Meet The Executive Team Amy Cole - Founder CEO Amy Cole brings over two decades of leadership across fashion sales and executive career development. She held senior roles at global apparel manufacturers including Warnaco , Imagine Capital Corp , and Ocean Pacific , where she served as Vice President of Sales —managing operations and national accounts including Macy’s Merchandising group, Target, and Kohl’s . Amy’s entrepreneurial drive and commitment to developing strong, agile teams led her to launch Acquaint New York, a boutique executive search firm based in NYC. Recognizing that talent strategy goes beyond hiring, she expanded the business to include executive coaching , partnering with organizations and individuals to unlock leadership potential at every level. In addition to her work with clients, Amy has been invited to serve as a Career Counselor at institutions such as LIM College and Columbia University’s School of Professional Studies , where she supports professionals navigating complex career transitions. As CEO, Amy combines her industry expertise with a people-first mindset—helping clients build teams that not only perform but thrive.
