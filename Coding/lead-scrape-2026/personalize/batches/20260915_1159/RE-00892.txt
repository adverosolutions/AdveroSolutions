Our Journey: Meet Elizabeth ResultsResourcing was founded by Elizabeth Eiss, a results guru whose passion is to help people get things done well. She strived to empower business owners to live their purpose and thrive in today’s ever-changing economy leading to the creation of ResultsResourcing, a freelance platform tailored to solopreneurs and small business owners. ‍ A highly accomplished C-suite executive, Elizabeth left a successful career in the insurance industry for the excitement and challenge of leading startups back in 2000. Since then, she has tackled the same challenges many entrepreneurs face. ‍ Elizabeth often wore 13 different hats. She realized that much of her work day was spent on tasks that could be outsourced, so freelancers were brought in to provide specialized skills and support scalability. Elizabeth soon discovered two things: 1) she had an innate talent for finding the right freelance talent quickly and efficiently, and 2) that how and where you invest your time matters, a lot! Book a Free Jumpstart Call Our Vision: The Value of Time Time is finite and valuable, and the key to business scalability is leveraging your time to maximize impact. During her time in start-ups and working with freelancers, Elizabeth came to understand the nuances of investing vs. spending time. When working IN the business (spending time), you miss out on working ON the business and doing the things that help you grow (investing time). Elizabeth realized this simple, yet revolutionary concept would be game-changing for entrepreneurs looking to scale, and hiring freelancers is how they could get there. Bringing freelancers on board enables small businesses to create time by outsourcing important but non-critical tasks that allow them to focus on the core work that drives their business forward. The economic advantages are immense. But true success depends on finding the right freelancers and that is where Elizabeth could see that traditional job boards weren’t enough, and yet another way small businesses were forced to spend their time, often with poor or inconsistent outcomes. By bringing freelancers on board, small businesses can quickly expand their size the moment work demands increase, then reduce back to a smaller size when necessary.
