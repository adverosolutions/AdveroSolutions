Elizabeth Kennedy Founder Elizabeth Kennedy is the Founder of Kennedy Bond and the Practice Lead for all C-Suite and Executive Search. Originally from the UK, she moved to New York City nearly a decade ago and has since built and scaled high-performing teams, personally partnering with over 100 biotech, pharma, and VC firms. In early 2024, recognizing a gap in the market for a partnership-driven approach, she launched Kennedy Bond with a specialized focus on life sciences. With over 15 years of experience in Executive Search across the US and Europe, Elizabeth has established both retained and interim consulting practices. Before founding Kennedy Bond, she held leadership roles at Investigo and LHi Group. She has successfully placed executives from Senior Director to C-suite across the biotech and pharma landscape, from stealth startups to large pharmaceutical companies. One of her proudest achievements was leading 25 senior searches for Pfizer at the height of the pandemic—delivering on every single one.
