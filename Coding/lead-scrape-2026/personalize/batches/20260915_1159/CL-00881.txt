ALL YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED What areas do you serve? We are located in South Bend, Indiana, but serve the larger Northern Indiana and Southwest Michigan area. Not sure if you are within our travel radius? No worries, give us a call and let’s find out! What is the cost of your services? Most of our services are broken down into 2 or 4 hour blocks. These blocks are $75 an hour - making our 2 hour sessions $150 and our 4 hour sessions $300. Please note: these costs do not apply to our commercial cleaning services.
