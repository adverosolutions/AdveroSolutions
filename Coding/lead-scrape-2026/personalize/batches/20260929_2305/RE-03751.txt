About EES Headquartered in Boston, Exceptional Executive Search (EES) is a nationwide search practice dedicated to helping organizations whose missions promote the advancement of health and science, education, social equity and wellness, and arts and culture. We prioritize delivering a focused, customer-centric experience, limiting the number of concurrent searches to ensure thoroughness and attention to detail. Every search is tailor-made to meeting our clients' specific needs. Our success is evident in the multiple engagements we have with returning clients. At EES, we are committed to being a working partner in ensuring that the selected candidate is aligned with the organization's mission, culture, and core values, and leadership's vision for the future.
