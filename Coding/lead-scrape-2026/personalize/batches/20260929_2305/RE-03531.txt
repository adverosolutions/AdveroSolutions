And when you’re at an inflection point: launching, scaling, evolving, getting those hires right is everything. That’s where I come in. I’m Kasi Riley, and I founded Moxii Talent Solutions to help companies scale through their most pivotal moments: early-stage growth, major transitions, or rapid expansion, with clarity, intentionality, and the right talent in place. Whether you're hiring your first leadership hire, entering a new market, or rethinking how you build teams entirely, I make sure your talent strategy is built to meet the moment, and, fuel what’s next. I bring 15+ years of experience leading talent and recruiting teams through IPOs, hypergrowth, international expansion, and everything in between. My career has spanned the Fortune 50 to early-stage startups, with leadership roles at Block (formerly Square), Stitch Fix, Target, and Wells Fargo . I’ve built and scaled entire recruiting functions from scratch, led teams across continents, and coached hundreds of leaders through the art (and chaos) of hiring well. I started as an operator, not a recruiter and that lens has never left me. I think like a business partner first. I want to know what success looks like beyond the hire. I care about what keeps you up at night. I design searches, systems, and stories that reflect who you are, not just what the job description says. Moxii isn’t just a name, it’s how I show up.
