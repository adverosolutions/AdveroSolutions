Our Story Bryant Group 0 Our Story We’re all looking for great leaders. For great talent. For organizational success and growth. And today, the stakes are higher than ever. Today, Bryant Group has impacted more than 10,000 leaders and served more than 300 organizations during our 38-year tenure. But every great story starts with humble beginnings and a great idea. Here’s our story. We’ve witnessed how incredible leadership touches lives, emboldens bold dreams and solves the world’s most daunting problems. But we’ve also witnessed that without it, missions crumble. Cultures suffer. And the spark of an organization and its people dims. The organizations we serve are simply too important and your people too valuable to see our work as filling seats. Instead, we must protect and promote alignment and empathy-based leadership. And this is the place where Bryant Group has firmly placed its stake in the ground. You see, everything shifts with the right approach, the right leaders, and the right mission. Organizations flourish, employees thrive, performance peaks, teams rally, and a fulfilled life at the office simply makes all of life better. This is the dream we show up for every day at Bryant Group. It all started in the 1980s when two former higher ed and healthcare leaders took a look around and observed an absence of intentional recruitment in development and related fields. Armed with an idea and a curious passion to create alignment, Chris Bryant and Steven Ast co-founded one of the country’s first executive search firms specializing exclusively in philanthropy recruitment. Leveraging decades of experience and an uncanny intuition, Chris became one of the early pioneers developing the thinking around the space of alignment between the candidate and the culture of the client organization . Combining this with an understanding of the field of advancement and employing a relational model of recruiting that honors and respects candidates became a winning formula – for clients and candidates. The results were staggering: our successful candidates stay with the client organization more than twice the national average. Today, Bryant Group continues that long-running legacy of integrity, diligence, and caring deeply about people that was first established by Chris. Then our story takes an interesting turn in 2016.
