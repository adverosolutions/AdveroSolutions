Sonographer-Led Imaging Operations | North Texas | HemoPhonics Imaging ABOUT About Us HemoPhonics Imaging is a sonographer-led diagnostic imaging operations firm supporting healthcare facilities across North Texas. We offer skilled sonographer coverage, managed ultrasound services, ultrasound access solutions, and strategic business advisory services to multi-specialty clinics, imaging facilities, and other healthcare organizations seeking to expand diagnostic ultrasound access, strengthen workflows, and build reliable ultrasound service models centered around real operational needs. Founded in 2020, HemoPhonics Imaging was built on the belief that the people who understand clinical operations best are often the clinicians engaged directly in patient care. Our mission is to improve patient outcomes by reducing barriers and expanding access to high-quality, reliable diagnostic ultrasound imaging services in underserved communities across North Texas through trusted partnerships with local medical facilities and physician networks. Guided by clinical experience, HemoPhonics Imaging aims to help redefine diagnostic imaging through innovative ultrasound service models and strategic healthcare partnerships that improve patient access, support preventive care, strengthen patient-physician relationships, enhance the patient experience, maintain continuity of care, and create more sustainable career pathways for sonographers. Tina Walton RVT, RDMS(AB) Meet The Owner Tina Walton, RVT, is the founder of HemoPhonics Imaging and brings 25+ years of healthcare experience across pharmacy, diagnostic ultrasound, staffing, and outpatient clinical operations. Her healthcare career began in pharmacy in 1998 before transitioning into diagnostic ultrasound in 2010, with a specialized focus in vascular sonography. Throughout her career, Tina has worked with respected medical institutions across North Texas, where she trained alongside and was mentored by skilled vascular surgeons, sonographers, and clinical teams. Known for her tech-savvy, versatile, and hands-on approach, Tina has built a career around understanding the technical precision, hemodynamic principles, workflow discipline, communication, and operational support required to deliver quality diagnostic ultrasound services.
