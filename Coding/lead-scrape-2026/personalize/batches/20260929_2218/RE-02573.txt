About | Recruiterie Hello, I’m Jon Schneider, Founder of Recruiterie. For two decades I have focused my career on helping identify the right talent to build and grow successful teams and organizations. Early in my career, I worked for large, nationally-focused search firms helping employers recruit more efficiently. The internet was only starting to blossom back when I started in the industry, but I saw its potential to help employers and job seekers connect. My career has provided me the ability to work directly with thousands of business leaders, recruiters, and other HR professionals nationwide. The resounding feedback was the sheer importance of hiring the right individuals to continue to grow organizations and strengthen their teams. With the rapid evolution in technology and how people connect, I was able to take many of the insights I learned over the course of my career and implement highly effective strategic processes that speed up hiring for my clients and deliver high-caliber, culturally-aligned talent to meet their needs. Recruiterie is a nonprofit executive search firm focused on helping nonprofit organizations find exceptional candidates to advance their missions and strengthen their leadership teams. If you re struggling to compete for talent in today’s challenging employment space or looking to build a world-class team on a budget, we can help. Recruiterie has innovative recruiting and pricing strategies to help you connect with candidates who fit your team.
