About the Founder At eSearch Hawaii, we possess the experience, knowledge and skills required in recruiting for various industries and positions within the state. Valarie Cesar, Principal and Owner of eSearch Hawaii, brings more than 30 years of experience in human resources, including talent acquisition, staffing and employee relations. She managed an international staffing office here in Hawaii for 17 years before founding eSearch Hawaii. Valarie possesses a solid understanding of Hawaii’s unique corporate culture and operations. Valarie received her Bachelor of Business Administration with a minor in Human Resources from the University of Hawaii. Valarie is a member of the Society of Human Resources Management (SHRM). She is also a member of Mid Pacific Country Club. Valarie sits on the Advisory Board of Leeward Community College and volunteers at the Waipahu High School’s Mock Interview program.
