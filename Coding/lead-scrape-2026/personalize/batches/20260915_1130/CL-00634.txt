About Us - Cllean Facility Services, LLC MENU About Us Cllean Facility Management Services was built on a strong foundation of over 30 years of industry experience in housekeeping and laundry services. Our leadership’s deep-rooted knowledge and commitment to excellence have shaped our company’s core values of reliability, integrity, and quality service. From healthcare environments to commercial spaces, we bring a hands-on, detail-driven approach that ensures every facility we manage is not only clean but truly cared for. At Cllean, we don’t just maintain spaces—we elevate them. About Us CLLean Facility Services began humbly in 2015 as L L Cleaning Co., a small, woman-owned startup with a vision of providing exceptional, tailored cleaning services. As we grew alongside our clients, we rebranded in 2024 to CLLean Facility Management Services to reflect our expanded offerings. Guided by our core values of integrity, reliability, dependability, and personalized service, we ve steadily broadened our reach while maintaining our commitment to quality and lasting customer relationships.
