About Us - All Facility Service (AFS) Home About Us Technology Founder How We've Helped Clients Services Media and Writing AFS Blog News Contact Us About Us All Facility Service is southeast Michigan-based, with national reach. We were founded with the aim of doing facilities maintenance and management smarter. The industry has been consumed with a "race to the bottom" where you get low prices in reverse auctions but then spotty service and little accountability. Instead, we get to know our clients, continually finding new problems to solve. We're constantly piloting and implementing new technologies. And we believe that old-fashioned honesty and trustworthiness are still important in business. Trustworthy We hire and cultivate the best people: employees, vendors and partners alike. And we put them into a culture suffused with honesty, humility and a service ethic. Smart Solutions We prefer to look at what you are trying to achieve and help you get there the best and most efficient way possible, rather than just throwing out low bids. Technology Technology helps us do more with less. From GPS-enabled dongles for crew tracking to tablet-based applications, we're always trying the newest and best so we can put it out in the field. Personal Touch Our sales staff, account managers and field operatives, along with long-time vendors, seek to learn as much as they can about the clients.
