About | The T-Bag Company top of page Dallas, Texas Business Ambassador Use tab to navigate through the menu items. OUR STORY I have worked in the janitorial business for a long time. Everyone seems to have their own way of storing and unrolling trash bags while they work. I used to use plastic bags from the grocery store. This worked for me, but I found that my employees were damaging and going through their plastic bags more often than I was. I thought to myself that there has to be a better way! After lots of thinking, researching, and hard work, the T-Bag was ready. I hope you find it as useful as I do! - DL WASHINGTON, FOUNDER OWNER. 2023 ALL RIGHTS RESERVED. T-BAGCOMPANY.COM bottom of page
