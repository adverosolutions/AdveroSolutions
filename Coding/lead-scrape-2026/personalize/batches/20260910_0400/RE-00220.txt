Home - Top Shelf Human Resources Welcome to Top Shelf Human Resources We specialize in connecting top talent with leading companies across many industries. Our mission is to provide exceptional recruitment services that result in successful hires and long-term partnerships. We're confident that we can help you find the right fit for your organization or your next career move. SERVICES Our recruitment services include retained monthly recruiting, contingent on-time staffing, Career Brokering, and HR consulting. We work with companies of all sizes, in a range of industries, including Manufacturing, Professional Services, Public Service, and Skilled Trades. Whether you're looking for a C-level executive, mid-level manager, or entry-level employee or career opportunity, we can help you find the talent or career you need to achieve your goals. PROCESS Our recruitment process is tailored to meet the unique needs of each client and candidate. We start by learning about our clients and candidates, and then we work to identify the most qualified candidates for the opportunities we have active. Our process includes extensive sourcing, screening, and presenting, all on a fully automated platform. OUR TEAM Our team of career consultants are experts in their respective fields and have a proven track record of success. They bring a wealth of knowledge and experience to every search and are dedicated to finding the right match for you.
