Full Scale Solutions - About Us Contact About Us Employment Services Methods Changing the Mold Home About Us President Biography As President and Founder of Full Scale Solutions, Inc., Claire is responsible for developing strategic partnerships, guiding direction and vision of the company, business development, and for building corporate alliances. Claire has been in the Staffing/Human Resources arena for over 20 years. Formerly from Connecticut, she has lived in Raleigh since 1993 with her two dogs, Eddie and Trouble. Prior to founding Full Scale Solutions in 2001, she worked for two national staffing agencies and as Director of Business Development, and headed up Sales for their Administrative and Technology Divisions. Claire was then chartered to head up regional expansion for a national human resource outsourcing corporation and her responsibilities included regional expansion throughout the Southeast of their Professional Service Division and directed its growth. The key to her success has been the ability to establish a solid reputation for providing a comprehensive range of staffing services to fulfill the clients' needs. Her attitude and the desire to always do the right thing have allowed her to EARN the trust of her clients. Claire is a member of the CED (Council for Entrepreneurial Development), Raleigh Chamber of Commerce, Healthcare Business Women's Association, NC Medical Device Organization, NCTA, the NC BIO and Women in Bio. She has been past President and Membership Director for the International Customer Service Association of America, Board Member of the Wake County Business Advisory Council, on the Board of Advisors for EntreDot, a volunteer for the Women's Center, American Heart Association and she has actively been involved with BandTogether a non-profit which raises money for multiple charities. She is also a Big Sister in the Big Brother Big Sister program where she mentors a child.
