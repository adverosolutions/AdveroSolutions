Fulton Recruiting is a direct-hire recruitment firm that connects top-quality candidates with our partner hospitals. We take a personalized approach to understand the unique needs of each hospital, its staff, and the local community.​ Our in-depth process allows us to facilitate the identification and placement of candidates who not only possess the necessary skills but also align with the organizational culture. This fosters lasting relationships, minimizes turnover, and enhances overall organizational health. ​ We work only with organizations that prioritize quality patient care and employee development. Our partner hospital systems are primarily independent hospitals in rural and mid-size markets. This is typically more conducive to an environment where hospital leaders are free to implement what is best for their teams and patients in real-time, being acutely in touch with the needs of their communities.
