About Us - Meet the Team at All Ways Clean Located in Morro Bay, CA OUR TEAM DAVID HERGENROEDER, CEO David Hergenroeder co-founded All Ways Clean along-side Trevor Smith in 1999 after seeing an opportunity in their community to provide quality commercial cleaning services across various professional sectors. Their customized janitorial services include but are not limited to; medical, commercial, educational, and industrial. David brought his 25 year textile rental business experience (10 spent as General Manager) into building this business along with his personal standard of integrity, commitment to achieving high quality results and natural talent for sales.
