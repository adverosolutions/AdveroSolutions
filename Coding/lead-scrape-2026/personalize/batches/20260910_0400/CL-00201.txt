Company History Office Pride Commercial Cleaning Services was founded in 1992 by Todd Hopkins, who was researching the commercial cleaning industry while working on his MBA. He saw an opportunity to create a company that was just as dedicated to integrity as it was high-quality services. Today, Office Pride has grown to be a nationally acclaimed commercial cleaning franchise with nearly 150 franchises across 25 states. That means we’re almost guaranteed to be able to serve you, wherever you are. Office Pride is in Franchise Business Review’s Hall of Fame and consistently ranks among Franchise Business Review’s top franchises for its exceptional franchisee satisfaction ratings.
