William Sage Group - Insourced Strategic Recruiting Partner Let's talk About About William Sage Group is a leading provider of integrated talent acquisition consulting, insourced executive/retained search, and RPP (recruitment process partnership). Founded in 2005 - we are a boutique firm of highly seasoned recruiting, talent acquisition and strategy experts. The name? It s a combination of the founder s middle name and the name of his first child. It sounds great, and it stuck! Our managing directors have backgrounds as sales and consulting leaders in the software and consulting industries prior to executive recruiting and TA consulting. We have tremendously deep domain experience. We are hiring managers and have built teams from the ground up. We immerse ourselves in our clients organization; allowing us to create the infrastructure, process, and strategy required to hire game changing talent and we deliver. Our goal was to disrupt the recruiting industry, which is littered with low-integrity resume flippers and RPO firms that provide rent-a-recruiter services adding no real business value. In addition, too many internal TA teams are entry level and lack business acumen to influence and impact real business strategy. We saw a tremendous opportunity for a recruiting partner to lead from the front. Our team has been with WSG from the beginning, and share the same goal to change how a recruiting and TA consulting firm engages with a client. We become a part of our client s organizational leadership. We understand transformation, and how to hire and cultivate talent that executes business objectives versus filling a role . Both start-up and well established organizations leverage WSG as a competitive differentiator to realize their strategic business outcomes.
