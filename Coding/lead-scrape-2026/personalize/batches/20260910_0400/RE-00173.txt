MJensen LLC Executive Search Consultant About Page MJensen LLC Scroll My story How can I help you get to where you want to go? One of the greatest joys in my work is being a resource for others. Because of this, a fundamental question underlies every relationship that I build: How can I help you get to where you want to go? Whether you are a business in need of new talent or an individual making a career move, I will purposefully help you achieve success in your goals. Sometimes it takes the simple connection of an experienced and compassionate resource to help a candidate align their talents in a new and creative way, or to help a company conduct a candidate search from a fresh perspective. It is, however, always with the practice of applying what I’ve learned from the challenges, failures, and successes that stop at my doorstep - either from my own experiences, or from the experiences others have chosen to share with me. I’ve conducted thousands of conversations across North America, Europe, and Asia and have curated a library of insights to impart to people at any stage of their career. As a relocation specialist having moved domestic and international candidates around the country, I have been rewarded with a large and diverse network. My extensive experience in helping companies and candidates through many types of business transitions has broadened my perspective of career growth and change. Throughout the search process and beyond hire, I move from being an executive recruiter to an executive coach. I use my keen sense of listening and networking to develop long-lasting relationships, and to help my clients see the great success that is possible. Best, Mary A.
