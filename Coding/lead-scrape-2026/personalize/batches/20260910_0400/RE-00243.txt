Schedules tighten. Fall demand becomes clearer. Vacation coverage changes. Year-end goals move from “later” to “coming quickly.” This is when small workforce issues deserve attention. If overtime is already creeping up, managers are regularly covering open work, or one absence can throw off an entire shift, those aren’t just September problems. They’re early information about Q4. The goal isn’t to overstaff because you’re worried about what might happen. It’s to understand where your capacity is vulnerable before demand puts it to the test. Ask yourself: If business increased tomorrow, where would our team feel it first? That answer is a good place to start your workforce planning.
