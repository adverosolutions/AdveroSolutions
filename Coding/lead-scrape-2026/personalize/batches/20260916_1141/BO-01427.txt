Mark Bayya, EA Mark Bayya has a passion for helping small business owners and individuals to manage and grow their financial well-being. With a strong accounting and tax background, Mark provides tax consulting, planning and compliance services for family-owned businesses, as well as service providers, real estate investors, and high net worth individuals. He assists in maximizing tax savings through the latest tax laws, proactive tax planning, and tax credit utilization. He represents clients in tax matters brought before the Internal Revenue Service and in various states. Mark received his Bachelor of Science in Accounting from California State University, Long Beach and began his career in public accounting in 2012 with a midsize CPA firm. With his tax experience and dream of having a business, he decided to open his own tax practice in 2019. He is licensed by the Internal Revenue Service as an Enrolled Agent and a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants, National Associate of Enrolled Agents and California Society of Enrolled Agents.
