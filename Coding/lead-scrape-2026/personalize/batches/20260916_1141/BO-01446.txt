About Adam Z. Hand Tax Financial Consulting, Inc. 0 Adam Z. Hand, Esq., CPA Adam earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in Economics from the University of California at Los Angeles and a law degree from the University of Southern California School of Law. He is an active member of the State Bar of California, a Certified Public Accountant, and a licensed life and disability insurance agent. As an adjunct professor at California State University, Northridge, Adam taught the course Advanced Topics in Finance: Income Tax and Estate Planning. Adam opened his own practice in January 2011 after more than 16 years as a tax consultant at national and large local accounting firms. He enjoys working with individuals and closely held businesses with both complex and simple overall tax and financial situations. He is dedicated to helping each client make intelligent financial decisions and ethically minimizing their tax liability. His patience and determination ensure that every client is treated with the highest regard.
