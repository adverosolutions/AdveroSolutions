Precision Tax is your partner for stress-free tax preparation Precision Tax is your partner for stress-free tax preparation Precision Tax is here to serve all of your income tax related needs! We are not a franchise, we re a locally owned, family business. There s no big tax franchise atmosphere here. We look forward to seeing you! Owner We are owned and operated by David Ruzzo. Our roots are sunk deep in the Capital Region. David was born and raised in Schenectady and attended Mont Pleasant High School where he played football and baseball. Afterward he attended Hudson Valley Community College for engineering and business. Having done Accounting work for large companies as well as for his own corporation for over 26 years, David has focused exclusively on Income Tax Accounting for the past 21 years. As an IRS Enrolled Agent (EA) Passing both the IRS SEE Examination as well as the IRS RTRP Exam, he spent 12 years working as a Manager and Senior Tax Specialist for Accutax Income Tax Services, the largest independent tax preparation firm in the Capital Region. Starting his own Tax Practice was the next logical career move. Beyond the 9-5, David enjoys traveling with his wife Julia and their two daughters. As the grandson of Italian immigrants, David enjoys growing grapes in his own home vineyard, making wine, enjoying Italian food and speaking Italian any chance he gets! He also volunteers, helping people who speak the Italian language to get to know The Bible.
