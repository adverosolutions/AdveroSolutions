About - Canyon Oak Financial Client Login About Canyon Oak Financial Canyon Oak Financial is where business, family, and legacy come together. Offering tax-smart financial planning for business owners who know family is always part of the equation. Most firms separate personal finances from business planning. We don’t—because as a family-owned business ourselves, Canyon Oak Financial understands that they are one and the same. View services Canyon Oak Financial was born from real-life experience. Our founder, Jennifer deSpain CPA, CFP®, began her career in corporate finance and later helped scale and sell a successful family tech business all while navigating major transitions in her family s and business life. That lived experience along with raising a family, caring for aging parents, and planning for the future shaped the foundation of our firm, which Jennifer is now growing with her son, her nephews, and collaborating with her sister to bring you legal advice. We know what it’s like to juggle payroll and preschool drop-off. To sit at the kitchen table talking taxes and trust planning. To make business decisions that aren’t just about the bottom line but about the people they impact and the family that runs them. That’s why we built a firm that treats clients like people first, not just business owners. Our passion is helping family-owned businesses thrive through every season, whether you re growing, transitioning, or figuring out what comes next.
