We work extensively within the medical, healthcare and B2B industries, leveraging over two decades of recruiting expertise. Known for our personal touch, we invest the time to fully understand both client needs and candidate goals, ensuring every match is the right fit. As a proud member of First Interview, the largest sales recruiter network in the country, Synergy Sales Recruiting provides clients with access to an exclusive, nationwide network of top-tier sales talent. With over 300 recruiting firms collaborating to share candidates and opportunities, we can quickly identify highly qualified, pre-vetted professionals who are ready to make an impact. Our Founder With a background spanning over 20 years in sales recruiting and leadership, owner Gwen Payne embodies Synergy Sales Recruiting’s commitment to excellence. Her extensive experience provides clients and candidates with unmatched insights. Gwen’s dedication extends beyond recruitment—her personal touch, thorough research, and ethical approach have resulted in trusted relationships and a remarkable track record of success. Founder President Gwen Payne, CPC Gwen Payne purchased Synergy Sales Recruiting, Inc. in January 2003, rebranding the company as Synergy Sales Recruiting of Louisiana, LLC. Since then, her company has served the business community, as well as job seekers, with her emphasis on excellence. She specializes in the placement of sales and sales management professionals with local and national companies in the Medical, Pharmaceutical, and B2B markets. Based in New Orleans, Gwen works with clients and candidates across the entire country. She is an award-winning affiliate of First Interview, the largest network of recruiting firms in the United States. Prior to purchasing Synergy, Gwen had spent five years as a recruiter in Kansas City, followed by 18 years as Learning and Performance Improvement Manager and Sales / Sales Management Trainer with a major international corporation, Newark Electronics, in Chicago.
