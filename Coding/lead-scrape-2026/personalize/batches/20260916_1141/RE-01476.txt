About Us Evolve HRS 0 People are your greatest asset... and the heartbeat of your business. OUR STORY Evolve HR Solutions was born out of a vision to transform how companies approach their most important asset: their people. After 25 years in corporate HR, Jeff Lupinacci recognized a pressing need across organizations: the need to build a robust talent strategy that drives long-term success. Throughout his career, Jeff saw the same challenges repeated in companies of all sizes and industries. Organizations struggled to find the best talent, nurture and develop it, and ensure they had the right people in the most critical roles to propel their businesses forward. These gaps often held companies back, stalling growth and creating missed opportunities. Jeff knew there was a better way. Leveraging decades of experience in navigating complex landscapes, he set out to create a company that would help businesses unlock their full potential by focusing on their talent. In a bold move, he stepped away from his Chief Talent Officer role and founded Evolve HR Solutions. Evolve HR Solutions was built on a simple but powerful principle: people are your competitive advantage. The company’s mission is to partner with organizations to help them identify, develop, and retain the talent they need to achieve their goals. By bringing a strategic, forward-thinking approach to talent management, Evolve HR Solutions empowers companies to align their workforce with their vision, filling gaps and driving measurable results. Since its founding, Evolve HR Solutions has become a trusted partner to businesses seeking to build a brighter future. By focusing on finding the best talent, developing it to its fullest potential, and ensuring that critical roles are filled with the right people, the company has helped clients meet their talent challenges in a rapidly changing business landscape. At its core, Evolve HR Solutions is about serving the overworked and under-resourced Executive to create lasting people impact. Jeff’s journey from corporate HR to entrepreneur reflects the belief that when you put people at the center of your strategy, extraordinary things happen. Meet the Founder Jeff Lupinacci Jeff spent the last 25 years at some of the world's best-known companies, such as Intel Corporation and Kimberly-Clark.
