About Twin Arrows, LLC Scroll About Company Twin Arrows, LLC is a full service accounting and bookkeeping provider headquartered in Decatur, GA just outside of Atlanta. As the owner of Twin Arrows, LLC, I serve small businesses and nonprofit organizations all across America. My services include financial reporting, month end close, cash reports, treasury, accounts payable, payroll administration, 1099/1096 prep, 990 support, budgeting, forecasting, and more. Twin Arrows, LLC is a SB2SB company. I understand small businesses because I am a small business. My mission is to take the burden of financial management off of small business owners and nonprofit managers so you can focus on your organization's goals. If it's driving you crazy and has to do with managing your money, call me! Who am I? I’m Selim Ikizler and I founded Twin Arrows, LLC in 2016. Prior to launching Twin Arrows, LLC, I held a variety of positions in the accounting industry ranging from Staff Accountant roles to Director of Finance. I have served as a consultant to small businesses, nonprofits, and political organizations. I grew up in Maryland after my parents emigrated to the U.S. from Istanbul, Turkey. After graduating from the University of Maryland, I spent parts of my career in Washington, DC and Boston before relocating to Atlanta in 2016. When I’m not working, I enjoy being outside, being active, cooking, and am learning about gardening. I also love all things DC sports (with apologies to Atlanta Braves fans) and superheroes. I live in Decatur with my wife, Marie, our baby daughter, and our two rescue dogs, Moo and Daffy.
