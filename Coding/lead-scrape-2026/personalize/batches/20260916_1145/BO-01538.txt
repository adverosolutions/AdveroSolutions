About | Senter, CPA, P.C. 248-934-0550 info@sentercpa.com STAFF LOGIN CLIENT LOGIN About Matthew Senter, CPA FOUNDER Matthew Senter grew up in a small-town south of Nashville, Tennessee, where he started his career before moving to Clarkston, Michigan with his family in 2012. Prior to founding Senter, CPA, P.C., he worked as a staff accountant for several very well-respected firms in Franklin, Tennessee and Southfield, Michigan; all of whom had clients across the country and abroad. Matthew has always possessed a passion for numbers. He values working closely with his clients to service their needs with accounting, consulting, tax preparation and planning from start-up to multi-millions in revenue. Matthew’s clients range from individuals, corporations, partnerships, non-profits, trusts, and employee benefit plans. His industry experience includes medical and dental practices, manufacturing, retail, service, health care, restaurants (including franchises), construction, real estate, entertainment, and transportation, among others. Matthew also has substantial experience with IRS, State and Local audit representation. Matthew enjoys spending time with his wife Cassie and their three young children: Ben, Waylon, and Charlotte. In his spare time, you can often find him watching University of Tennessee Football, hunting, fishing with his family, or enjoying the great outdoors. Matthew is a member of the Oakland County Sportsman’s Club, Ducks Unlimited, among others.
