REDEFINING WORKFORCE SOLUTIONS Helping You Stand Out With a team of industry-seasoned employees, we offer tailor-made solutions to meet your individual needs. We work with leading organizations and have developed a reputation as a staffing expert. Starting in the early 2000s, Focus Talent Solutions has been providing professional services to job-seekers and employers. And with our longevity, we’ve learned that staffing is not a numbers game — it’s a relationship-building business. That’s why we put an emphasis on listening, guiding and matching. Operating with a proactive and frontward-facing approach is what separates Focus Talent Solutions from other recruiting companies. We interview our client first. This helps our clients identify what they need in order for their new hire to make the biggest impact. These discovery sessions help us identify the skill set, education, and culture impact just to name a few. The next step is interviewing the candidate. We start with a phone interview to learn what our candidate is looking for. This includes both long term and short term goals. What drives that candidate, whether it is money, work-life balance, culture, etc. If we feel it is a good match we set up an in-person interview or a video webinar if we are unable to connect in person.
