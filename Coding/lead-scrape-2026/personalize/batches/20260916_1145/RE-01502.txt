About Evluate - Transforming Recruitment with AI Birth of Excellence How it Began Evluate was born from the innovative minds at Techvalens, a leader in IT services for over 18 years. Since 2007, Techvalens Software Systems Pvt Ltd has been at the forefront of web and mobile app development, cloud computing, and e-commerce, with offices in Indore, India, and New York, USA. As Techvalens grew, so did the challenges in recruitment. The rise of remote work and increased hiring demands exposed the flaws in traditional hiring processes. Inconsistent interviews, longer hiring times, and rising costs began to take a toll on the quality of hires. These issues became even more pronounced during the COVID-19 pandemic, when the need for remote interviewing and bulk hiring surged. Recognizing these challenges, Techvalens envisioned a solution that would streamline recruitment, making it faster, more consistent, and cost-effective at a global scale. This vision led to the creation of Evluate—a platform designed to transform the way organizations assess and hire talent, embodying Excellence in Action. Vision To transform the hiring and assessment landscape with AI-powered insights enabling organizations, educational institutions, and industries to realize their full potential and achieve excellence through innovative and intelligent solutions. Mission To deliver innovative AI-driven assessment solutions that help organizations optimize their workflows.
