Legacy Executive Search - Expert Executive Skip to content ABOUT OUR TEAM Charlie Wilgus Founding Partner CWILGUS@LEGACYEXECUTIVESEARCH.COM Charlie Wilgus brings over 27 years of executive search experience to Legacy Executive Search, partnering with organizations across North America to recruit VP/SVP, President, and C-suite leaders. His areas of expertise include Supply Chain Operations, Sales Marketing, Human Resources, and Finance. Before founding Legacy, he was a Senior Partner at Matteson Partners, a boutique retained search firm. He previously spent 19 years with Lucas Group as an award-winning search partner and senior executive, where he helped lead the company's turnaround and growth from $25M to $130M before its sale to Korn Ferry. Passionate about solving complex hiring challenges and building lasting client relationships through a high-touch, consultative process, he grounds his approach in integrity, trust, authenticity, and work ethic—values that have earned him long-term partnerships as a trusted advisor for high-stakes executive searches. He excels at identifying leaders who can modernize supply chains, transform plant operations, elevate organizational culture, increase revenue, and drive EBITDA growth. In addition to family-owned and privately held companies, he has worked extensively with private equity firms to scale portfolio companies. Charlie earned a B.A. in Sociology from Rhodes College. He has been an active supporter and fundraiser for Children's Healthcare of Atlanta for more than 15 years. He lives outside Atlanta, Georgia, with his wife and two children, both currently in college.
