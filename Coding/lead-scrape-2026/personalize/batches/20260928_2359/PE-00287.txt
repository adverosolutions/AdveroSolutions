We serve residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional customers, including school facilities, and are currently servicing within New Jersey IPM (Integrated Pest Management) regulations. All our technicians are Certified Pesticide Applicators in 7A and 13 (General Household and School IPM), or are trainees under on-site direct supervision. Richard J. Moore, Jr. Company owner and operator Richard J. Moore, Jr., is an expert at integrated pest management (IPM). He and his staff stay current with the safest and most effective applications today. Richard also provides educational presentations on School IPM, under the direction of the New Jersey DEP and the Northwest Chapter of NJ Buildings and Grounds Association. Moore Expertise Our technicians are licensed and experienced to handle a variety of pests, including bedbugs, cockroaches, rodents, fleas, ticks, stink bugs, stinging insects, termites, and small wild animals. We are experts in IPM designed for schools, pre-schools, and day care centers. Certified Technicians Any exterminating business MUST be registered with the state of New Jersey and publicly post its business license number. Each of its technicians must be certified or work under the supervision of a certified pesticide applicator in one or more of 24 categories. We employ only certified pesticide applicators who are trained and experienced in different categories, including: CORE (Safety Procedures) 7A (General Household Insects) 7B (Termite and Wood-Destroying Insects) 7D – (Food Processing) 3B – (Turf/Lawn/Weeds) 13 – (School IPM) The above categories pertain to the home, business, commercial, and institutional facilities.
