About Horizon Line Skip to content About Us Horizon Line Lawn Care A Name You Can Trust! Horizon Line Landscaping, originally founded as Evan Duffy’s Lawn Care in 2014, was built on hard work, passion, and a commitment to quality. With nothing more than a push mower, a battery-powered weed whacker, and a broom, Evan began his journey by going door-to-door, providing lawn care with honesty and dedication. From those humble beginnings, Evan quickly grew his small venture into a trusted landscaping company—founded on the values of integrity, reliability, and top-quality service. Our mission is simple: to create outdoor spaces that bring beauty, function, and comfort to your home—spaces where you can relax, entertain, and make lasting memories. What We Do 🌿 Landscaping Lawn Care We design, redesign, and maintain beautiful landscapes that keep your home looking its best all year round. From routine lawn care to seasonal precautions, we ensure your outdoor spaces are always healthy and vibrant. 🪵 Hardscapes We specialize in creating patios, walkways, and other hardscape features that enhance your home’s beauty and functionality. Our Commitment Our crews are professionally trained in customer service and landscaping care. Every project is handled with precision, attention to detail, and respect for your property. We don’t just provide services—we deliver peace of mind, knowing your outdoor space is in expert hands.
