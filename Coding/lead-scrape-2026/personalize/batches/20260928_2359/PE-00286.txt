Why Geese Are a Problem Geese are more than just a visual disturbance. When they settle in areas frequented by people, they pose several serious risks: Health Hazards : Goose droppings can carry harmful bacteria like E. coli, salmonella, and avian influenza. Property Damage : Geese damage lawns, gardens, and landscapes by overgrazing, and their droppings leave unsightly messes that require constant cleaning.
