About BSS CPA About BSS CPA Committed to Helping You Succeed Brown, Smith Settle, LLC (BSS) is a full-service CPA firm in Evansville, Indiana. BSS is committed to each and every client, big or small, individual or business. We are committed to assisting our clients in every way possible to ensure success. We want to be your trusted and valued financial advisors. With a staff of over twenty professionals, we have the knowledge and expertise to find solutions for all of your financial needs. We offer a wide variety of services that are tailored to what you need, when you need it. BSS CPA has been serving clients for over 35 years. Our clients are primarily located in the tri-state area, but we serve clients from coast to coast. Our current client base consists of approximately 1900+ individuals, 500+ businesses and 150+ estates and trusts. Our business clients range in size from small one-owner proprietorships to multi-million dollar businesses. These businesses operate in a wide range of industries; oil and gas, construction, manufacturing, retail, wholesale distribution, medicine, and real estate just to name a few. Our mission is to develop long-lasting relationships with our clients. Brown, Smith Settle, LLC History BSS began business in 1979 with three partners and a small professional staff. The firm started as Brown, Smith and Settle CPA’s. Now, all three of the founding members are retired, but their vision and mission are still alive today.
