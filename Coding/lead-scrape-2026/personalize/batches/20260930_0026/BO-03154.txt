Frere, Jr., CPA Founder Ed Frere is a long-time CPA with national firm tax experience and is the founder of Advantage Finance and Tax based in Ellicott City, MD. The practice is focused on providing a high level of client service and value in the areas of tax compliance, tax advice, tax planning, and financial planning (tax focused). Ed started his career with Arthur Andersen Co in their Washington DC tax office where he did two tours of duty; in between those he worked at Freddie Mac managing their first ever IRS audit. Ed’s early work spanned many complex areas of taxation including real estate investing/development, foreign income taxation, and structured finance investments utilizing securitization. Ed has also held significant roles at U.S. Bank and Wells Fargo with a focus spanning topics such as: taxes, regulatory filings, financial reporting, and managing large scale litigation and conflict. Ed’s strong tax background, broad financial services experience, and his well-developed ability to both grasp and manage complex and contentious matters, provides a broad perspective and suite of abilities for clients and their businesses. Because Ed’s work has also included many sensitive and complex matters, he understands the special attention required to protect a client’s confidentiality and their sensitive information.
