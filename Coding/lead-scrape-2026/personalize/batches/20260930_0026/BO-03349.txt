Enrolled Agent / President / Accountant Sandy is a Cum Laude graduate of Ottawa University with a Bachelor's degree in Accounting and a minor in Economics. She spent 15 years working as an Accountant and Tax Accountant for the founders of Management Unlimited before branching off in 2003 and opening her own firm, A.B.S. Accounting Tax Service, Inc. In 2010, Sandy bought Management Unlimited and merged the two firms together in the Menomonee Falls location. She has been helping small businesses and individuals for over 30 years! In this time, she has earned the credential of Enrolled Agent, which gives her unrestricted rights to represent taxpayers before the Internal Revenue Service, afforded only to EA's, CPA's and attorneys. From 2017 through 2020, she was an Endorsed Local Provider of Tax Services by nationally-syndicated radio host and personal finance guru, author Dave Ramsey. Sandy has been married to Peter for 26 years and has two kids, a chocolate lab and a cat. She enjoys taxes, tax law, finance, travel, organic vegetable and herb gardening, and recharging herself by socializing with family and friends. Bonnie Snow, E.A.
