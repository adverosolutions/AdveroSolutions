Our story began in the 1950s when Buford Thurmon began preparing tax returns as a side business. His commitment to accuracy and service soon gained recognition and trust within the community. In 1983, Tim Thurmon, Buford's son, returned to Higginsville to take over the family business. Tim's enthusiasm and shared commitment to exceptional client service fostered growth, leading to Kevin Thurmon and Beth Schlotzhauer joining. Our firm is proud to continue the family legacy as the third generation, Trent and Micah Thurmon, have returned to carry on the tradition of providing exceptional client service. For over six decades, our firm has been a cornerstone in the Lafayette County area, offering a wide range of accounting and financial services to individuals and businesses alike. We pride ourselves on maintaining the same values and principles that have been the foundation of our firm's success from its inception. Our team of dedicated professionals continues to uphold the tradition of meticulous attention to detail, unparalleled expertise, and personalized care, offering services that extend far beyond simple tax preparation. Located in the heart of Higginsville, we are deeply committed to the prosperity of our community and the success of our clients. Whether you're a local business seeking financial guidance, an individual in need of tax assistance, or someone looking for comprehensive financial planning, our firm is here to serve your needs with the highest level of professionalism and integrity. With a rich history and a forward-looking approach, we remain your trusted partners in navigating the ever-evolving financial landscape.
