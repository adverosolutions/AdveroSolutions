Working with a search firm, what you may not know. Recruiters have clout with good candidates. Traditional "career" recruiters are specifically trained to approach passive candidates confidentially, pique their interest and then evaluate them for their client’s position. Recruiters are invaluable during the offer stage. Simply put, good recruiters are always closing from the beginning of the process. They are keenly aware of what it will take, on both sides, to effective a successful outcome and avoid a fall-off. The cost of unfilled positions can be more than a recruiter's fee. When compared to other low-cost hiring methods, a recruiter’s cost per hire is in many cases the same or lower, but more importantly, much more effective. You can trust a good recruiter with confidential information. Whether you are an employer conducting a confidential search or a candidate searching for your next opportunity, good recruiters are experts in anonymity, as their business is built on trust. They will never reveal who they are working for or a candidate’s identity until there is mutual interest and express authorization. Testimonials What Clients Candidates Say About Us
