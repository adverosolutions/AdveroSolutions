Leadership Profiles | The Woodmansee Group Who We Are Leadership Profiles MIKE MUMFORD Mike Mumford serves as CEO and owner of TWG and spearheads our sales and leadership efforts while nurturing relationships with existing and prospective clients. Mike leads by fusing the strengths of a strategic consulting firm with the power and depth of a best-in-class recruiting and talent acquisition organization. Mike joined TWG as Chief Executive Officer in November of 2023 and has been a member of TWG s advisory board for several years. Mike leads with an entrepreneurial spirit and a collaborative approach that challenges each person he encounters to innovate in order to find success. Mike encourages autonomy, creativity, opportunistic vigilance and independent thinking across the board. Mike will continue in his role as owner and founder of Hire Road, which he started in 2013. MISSY MCCLELLAN Missy McClellan serves as President and owner of TWG and spearheads our recruitment and leadership efforts while sustaining relationships with existing and prospective clients. Missy has over 15 years of experience in the recruiting industry and specializes in retained Executive Recruitment. Since joining TWG in 2006, Missy has been responsible for search assignments across the entire enterprise in the 29 different industry segments TWG services. Prior to joining TWG, Missy specialized in the recruitment of IT, Engineering, and Finance professionals. She has both agency and in-house corporate staffing experience that spans various industries, levels and functional areas across the business enterprise. Missy received a B.A. in Political Science from Indiana University. Contact Missy through email , or connect with her through LinkedIn .
