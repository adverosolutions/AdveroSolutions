Skip to main content Family Owned Operated for Over 30 Years Offices in Racine, Waterford, and Kenosha Home Divisions Available Jobs Locations Contact Us Welcome to Personnel Incorporated Personnel Incorporated began operations in 1986 as a Woman Owned Business, "Sole Proprietorship" located in Houston, Texas. Beginning with the recruitment of engineering candidates and quickly expanded to other disciplines. Personnel Incorporated established an excellent reputation and working relationship with many fortune 500 companies within the Refining, Petro-Chemical, and Chemical industries on a nationwide basis that is ongoing. Relocation to Racine, Wisconsin occurred in August of 1992 and Personnel Incorporated was restructured as an 'S' Corp in 2000. Personnel Incorporated has evolved into a family owned and operated Full Service Staffing firm with three (3) offices located in Racine, Kenosha, and Waterford, Wisconsin, with satellite operations in Michigan, Florida, and Texas.
