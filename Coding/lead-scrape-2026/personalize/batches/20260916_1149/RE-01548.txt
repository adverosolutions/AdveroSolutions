Lightwork | About About Lightwork, the premier talent partner for early-stage startups Lightwork was born from the wide variety of recruiting experiences of Founder, Lumen Sivitz. His background — leading Recruiting for Kleiner Perkins-backed Shyp and for global advertising technology company, Quantcast, as well as founding Mighty Spring, an early talent marketplace — made for an ideal set of experiences to share with other early-stage startups. His first consulting client was Y-Combinator s Clara Labs, where he coached the founding team on recruiting best practices and worked as an embedded recruiting resource to help the company hire after raising their Series A. This mutually positive experience led to a host of referral business and evolving scopes of work for Lumen, inspiring him to formalize the consulting practice by launching Lightwork . Today, Lightwork is the perfect partner for early-stage technology companies with challenging hiring goals. We help founders build the recruiting engine they need to identify, connect with, and close top-tier talent. Our clients benefit from nearly two decades of recruiting experience paired with the understanding of what it means to be a Founder and to have full responsibility for the quality of the team.
