About Wild Goose Chase | Bird Control Services Skip to content About Wild Goose Chase Contact Us Behind the Company Science-Based Bird Control Services Who We Are Wild Goose Chase and Migratory Bird Management (our Wisconsin Branch) are leading experts in commercial bird control, solving bird problems with proven, biology-based solutions since 1998. We offer over 30 unique solutions for managing nuisance bird species across the Midwest and beyond. Our team works with clients in agriculture, aviation, education, healthcare, residential developments, logistics, and other environments where birds create health or safety risks. Every Wild Goose Chase and Migratory Bird Management solution and recommendation is grounded in biology, legal compliance, and decades of field experience. Our clients trust us because we deliver results, not band-aid fixes. We identify the cause of the problem, address it directly, and help ensure it does not return. How We Started Wild Goose Chase and Migratory Bird Management (Wisconsin Branch) was founded in 1998 by Susan Hagberg, who combined her background in landscape management with a mission to build sustainable solutions to bird-related problems. What started as an effort to protect lawns from geese quickly expanded into something more. Susan saw an opportunity to create a company rooted in ethics, science, and long-term impact. As the company grew, Susan brought on bird biologists, field technicians, and structural technicians who helped expand the company’s solutions and reputation as a trusted resource for clients facing complex bird control challenges. The company opened operations in Indiana in 2021 and in Michigan in 2025 to further service the Midwest and beyond. What We Stand For Our mission is simple: design deploy science-based solutions to mitigate conflict between birds, people and property. We do this through hands-on expertise, innovative tools, and a deep commitment to environmental responsibility. We build every program from the ground up. No two sites are alike, so no two plans are the same. Whether a client is dealing with aggressive geese, roosting pigeons, or flocks of gulls, we bring the knowledge and tools needed to fix the problem efficiently and responsibly. What to Expect Our Process Everything starts with a thorough site evaluation. Our team reviews the landscape, identifies the species involved, and maps out the patterns of behavior.
