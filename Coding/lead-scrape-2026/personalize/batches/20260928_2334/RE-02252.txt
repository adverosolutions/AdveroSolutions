Weiser Innovations 0 A Weiser approach to Talent Acquisition. We are your talent acquisition strategists. We partner with you to develop and implement an effective talent acquisition strategy for your organization. Our Story Our Services. Talent acquisition and recruitment are often used synonymously. Although they are related, they are distinct. Recruitment is just one component of talent acquisition and is the transactional act of hiring to fill a specific vacancy. Talent Acquisition is the long term strategy behind how and why hiring happens as part of the overall business plan. We understand that organizations may need entire talent acquisition strategies or support for individual areas within. Therefore, we create customized talent acquisition solutions to meet our clients’ needs. Strategy. We develop customized talent acquisition strategies to align with your overall business strategy. We work with you to define your goals and how they will be achieved. Training. Ensuring your people have the right training and tools to be effective is critical. We provide both the tools and education that help teams succeed. Retained Projects. When specific projects arise that require extra bandwidth, we can create budget friendly solutions that successfully achieve your deliverables. On-Call Support. Sometimes you just have a question. With the Support Line, our clients are able to communicate with experts who are ready to guide them to solutions. Our Services Find what works for you. Have questions regarding our services?
