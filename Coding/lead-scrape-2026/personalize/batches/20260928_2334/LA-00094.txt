Old Mission Landscaping About | oldmissionlandscapes About Reliable Residential Commercial Landscaping Maintenance Old Mission Associates is a full-service landscape design, construction, and maintenance company in the greater Grand Traverse region striving for the highest standards in quality and customer care. Meet The Team Old Mission Associates, a full service landscape design, construction and maintenance company, was established in 2004. The company is run by a hardworking, educated, and motivated team whose common goal is to provide the highest standards in quality and customer care. We are members of Michigan Landscape and Nursery Association (MNLA), National Association of Landscape Professionals (NALP), Michigan Natural Shoreline Partnership, and Northwest Michigan Invasive Species Network. Jordan Valdmanis Owner Operator Jordan has owned and operated Old Mission Associates in Northern Michigan since 2004. With over 15 years of experience in the lawn and landscape industry, he strives for excellence within the company and throughout the community. As a graduate of Michigan State University with a degree in Landscape and Nursery Management, he has extensive knowledge of lawn care, landscape construction and business management. After graduation, Jordan continued to stay connected to the program through teaching at the MSU extension in Traverse City and welcoming interns from the program into the company every summer. The strength of OMA has been built by all the dedicated team members that have been with us over the years. When not working, Jordan enjoys spending time on the ice rink with his son Andrei, boating and golfing. Zach Wood-Sternburgh Lawn and Landscape Manager Heading up the Lawn Landscape Management division, Zach has been with Old Mission Associates since 2009. He is a certified pesticide applicator and has over a decade of experience in the landscape industry. His positive attitude and his attention to detail has made him an ideal member of the Old Mission team. Zach loves to spend time with his wife and two daughters and enjoy the beauty of Northern Michigan. Andy Clapham Lead Designer Andy is Old Mission Associates Lead Landscape Designer.
