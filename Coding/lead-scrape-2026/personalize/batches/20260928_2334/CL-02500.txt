About - Lighthouse Enterprises Skip to content About Lighthouse Enterprises Where Human Potential Becomes Operational Strength Lighthouse Enterprises is the social business division of The Chicago Lighthouse, delivering secure, scalable contact center solutions through experienced U.S.-based teams, adaptive technology, and a workforce built to stay. Talk to Our Team Why Organizations Choose Us The Chicago Lighthouse, founded 1906. Lighthouse Enterprises contact center teams, today. 1906 A Lighthouse for Human Potential The Chicago Lighthouse opened in 1906 to serve people who are blind, visually impaired, disabled, and Veterans. The work has always started in the same place: identify what a person can do, remove what stands in the way, and build the training and technology around them. Employment was never a side program. Over more than a century, the organization built vocational training, adaptive technology expertise, and job placement into a single operating discipline. That discipline is the reason the enterprise side of the house works the way it does today. Explore The Chicago Lighthouse s mission Training, accommodation, and placement are one continuous process. 2012 Purpose Became an Enterprise A social mission run to commercial service standards. In 2012, The Chicago Lighthouse turned that capability into a business. Lighthouse Enterprises was built to compete on the terms buyers actually use — service levels, security posture, staffing depth, and cost — while employing the people the labor market routinely overlooks. The model is straightforward. Clients buy contact center performance. The revenue that performance generates funds the programs that keep producing skilled, motivated people. Neither side is charity; each makes the other stronger. Today the enterprise generates more than 58% of organizational revenue, supporting more than 42 programs across the organization. The Operating Advantage People Stay. Service Gets Stronger. Contact center economics are decided by turnover. Every departure costs a hiring cycle, a training cycle, and a stretch of degraded service your customers feel.
