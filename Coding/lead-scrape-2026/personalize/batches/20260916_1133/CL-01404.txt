The Durkin Company is a family-owned business that has provided janitorial and safety products to the New England area since 2003. With the resources of a national company and the service that only a small business can provide, the Durkin Company has gained the loyalty of many major corporations, property management companies, schools, municipalities and local businesses. The company is owned and operated by Joe Durkin, Jack Reynolds and Zach Trearchis who share over 65 years of experience in the cleaning industry. Together, their focus is on continuing to build a business that emphasizes quality products and services, customer satisfaction, community support and ensuring all their employees share in their vision. The new ownership group took over at the start of 2020 as the pandemic hit and challenged the world really testing the resiliency of small business. Cleaning, Disinfecting and sanitizing became the challenge that everyone faced along with supplying the products that were needed to protect everyone. Face masks, hand sanitizer, electrostatic guns, gloves, disinfectants, wipes and air purifiers were the supplies needed and were near impossible to get. The Durkin Company delivered. We were able to take care of our loyal customers as well as gain many new ones through a simple concept: communication and service. The Durkin Company will continue their mission to provide top quality goods and services while broadening their service level in the future through online ordering, increased market segments such as restroom partitions and division 10 goods and services, e-commerce and utilizing industry relationships to enhance our customer s experience.
