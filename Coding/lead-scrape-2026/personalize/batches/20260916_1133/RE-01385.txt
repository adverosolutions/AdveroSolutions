PLBsearch is a women-led boutique executive search firm specializing in the recruitment of senior-level communications / public affairs , government relations and investor relations professionals, headquartered in Washington, DC and with a network of offices around the world. The firm was founded in 2012 by Pepper Lunsford Binner , a well-known leader and authority in the placement, retention and development of corporate affairs executives . The team at PLBsearch provides high-touch , high-caliber executive recruitment and advisory services to clients across all sectors and geographies. Our global network includes partnerships with leading recruiters in corporate affairs in Europe and Asia . We offer the same exceptional quality you would expect to find in any major search firm, but with the ability to be more creative, flexible and customized in addressing each client’s specific human capital challenge .
