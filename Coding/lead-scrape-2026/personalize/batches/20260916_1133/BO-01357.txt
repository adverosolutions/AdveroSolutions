About Waterman and Associates, LLP 0 Rich Waterman, CPA Rich, the owner of the firm, graduated from UCLA in 1983 and, in addition, has a Master's Degree in Accounting from San Jose State University. He has broad business, accounting and finance experience. His tax expertise includes partnerships, small business formations, registered domestic partnerships, and trust and estate planning. Rich served two four year terms on the Campbell City Council and served one year as its Mayor in 2014. He has also been vice-chairperson on the City of Campbell Parks and Recreation Commission, was a board member for the Campbell Chamber of Commerce , and was an appointed member of the IRS Taxpayer Advocacy Panel where he served as chairman of the forms and publications committee. Rich lives with his partner of thirty six years, Bruce; puppies named Axel and Beau; and a parrot named Max. He enjoys snowboarding, wine tasting, traveling and cooking.
