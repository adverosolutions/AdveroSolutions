We built the practice to serve owners, private equity firms, lenders, and asset managers across real estate, hospitality, and casino gaming — industries where financial performance depends as much on operational fluency as on accounting discipline. Experience that shapes the work Our approach reflects hands-on time in the seats we re now advising. Sat in the CFO chair Prior roles as CFO, corporate controller, and executive team member across hospitality, casino, and real estate operators. Ran the audit function Direct oversight of accounting, internal audit, and compliance functions — with the discipline of a CPA and the investigative lens of a CIA. Worked the operations floor Close partnership with operational and construction managers, tying timeline and budget decisions to financial outcomes.
