About Chip Capelli Provincetown Accountant About News About Chip Capelli Experience Matters Chip Capelli has been working as an accountant for over thirty years. He graduated from Stockton University (Pomona NJ) with a Bachelor of Arts degree in Accounting and from Eastern University (St. David’s PA) with a Master of Science degree in Nonprofit Management. He holds a variety of certifications in financial management, leadership, and development. He has also been certified by Intuit as a Platinum Level ProAdvisor for QuickBooks. Chip has served as the Chief Financial Officer for several non-profit agencies, worked as a financial management consultant for many organizations in the for-profit and non-profit worlds and served as board treasurer for several organizations. Currently, he sits on the Finance Committee for both the AIDS Support Group of Cape Cod (asgcc.org) and the Town of Provincetown. In addition to bookkeeping and accounting, Chip s office prepares personal and corporate tax returns for a wide variety of individuals and businesses across the country. Once a tax return is filed, Chip provides year-round consulting and best-practices education for all clients. Chip has assembled a team of professionals who are experts in payroll processing, tax return preparation, bookkeeping, small business accounting and financial management.
