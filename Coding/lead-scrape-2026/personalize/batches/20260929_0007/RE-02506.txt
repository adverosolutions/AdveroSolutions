About The PMO Squad We’re One of You. We've lead the PMO. We've run the projects. We've done the hiring. You know the moments. The strategy is clear, but the initiatives behind it keep stalling. A critical PM seat sits empty while the work piles up. The PMO produces reports, yet leadership still can’t see whether the work is delivering results. We know those moments because we’ve lived them. Our team has spent decades building, scaling, and leading PMOs from the inside. We’ve sat in the steering meetings, defended the budgets, and answered for the missed dates. Project and PMO delivery is all we do. Challenges like these rarely sit neatly in one place. People, processes, and technology all affect the outcome, so we look at how they work together. That may mean helping you set direction, supporting delivery, or finding the right project leader for a critical role. We start by listening. We’ll work to understand what’s happening in your organization, what you’ve already tried, and where support could make a difference. Then we can decide together whether we’re the right fit.
