About workflow 0 About Us Work·flow The biggest blocker to tech's full potential isn't ideas, funding, or competition — it's the ability to build organizations strong enough to execute at the level of our ambitions. Workflow partners with early-stage tech companies to build people-first organizations from the ground up. Our clients don't just raise the bar — they set it. They become the companies the rest of the industry looks to. The team behind Workflow has led People Talent functions at some of the most successful tech companies in the world — through blood, sweat, and tears. We bring that hard-won expertise directly to the next generation of startup leaders. "Workforce" is about grind, hustle, and control — a model that collapses under pressure. Workflow is built on something more durable: genuine collaboration, radical inclusivity, and the full realization of human potential. This is the future of work. Join us in building it. Our founder Jen (Kim) Serena Jen is the Founder and Lead Instructor for Workflow’s programs. She holds additional roles as Venture Partner at Symphonic Capital, and Advisor/Executive Coach for top startups. Previously, she was the Head of People at Lever, a startup known for exceptional hiring, culture, and results in Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion. She is continuously expanding her work helping startups build ambitious, inclusive workplaces.
