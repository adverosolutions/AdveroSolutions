Florida Georgia Texas Find Job Position Location Rate Employment WHO WE ARE About Us FloMed was established and is operated by healthcare clinicians with over 40 years of combined experience in client relations and medical support services. As a staffing agency, we pride ourselves in connecting healthcare professionals with the right opportunity and assists clients fulfill staffing needs. The name of the company includes the word “FLO” because it represents the company’s fluidity in the ability to adapt to the constant changes and demands within healthcare staffing. FloMed is innovative, passionate, driven and solutions oriented in meeting the needs of our clients and healthcare professionals. FloMed provides full support to our clients and healthcare professionals that we serve. Mission FloMed's mission is to connect quality healthcare clinicians to world class facilities and exceed expectations as a valued partner in our community. Core Values FloMed center its core values on ensuring that healthcare professionals get connected, feel connected and stay connected. Our unique skillset allows for an in-depth understanding of the challenges of staffing from a clinician, client, and business perspective. Our vast experience in the healthcare industry, as traveling nurses, per diem nurses and permanent staff throughout various hospital systems, have provided us with direct knowledge and real time solutions in addressing gaps in staffing needs and daily on the job challenges. Vision FloMed's vision is to enhance our position as the most respected and trusted staffing agency by ensuring that we recruit, engage, and retain top notch quality professionals.
