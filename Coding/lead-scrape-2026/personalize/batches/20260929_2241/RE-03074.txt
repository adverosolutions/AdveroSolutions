Our History Career Fair Plus started in 2012 with a simple idea: career fairs should work better for everyone in the room. We built a companion app that gave students and recruiters a smarter way to connect. No more waiting in lines, no more missed conversations. Our appointment scheduling feature let students reserve 1:1 time with employers before they ever set foot on the floor.
