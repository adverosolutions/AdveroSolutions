Candela Search | In Pursuit of Extraordinary Talent. In pursuit of extraordinary talent. Our mission is simple. We aim to be your executive search partner of choice to attract the next generation of exceptional leaders. Our founders. A fresh approach to search. From Candela s beginnings in 2013, Aaron Cowan and Henry Man have sought to create a different kind of experience. Candela s search experience is set apart by close client relationships and an approach and attitude suited to the highly competitive market for transformational leaders. Henry Man Co-founder and Managing Partner Prior Experience An executive recruiter since 2000, joined the industry straight from university in the mad world of the dotcom boom. Initially worked with VC backed tech startups to build out their management teams before settling into the functional focus of technology and digital leadership across all sectors. Personal Visited over 80 countries and an avid scuba diver, although a young family has slowed recent progress to add stamps to the passport. Born in the south of England, Henry grew up in Hampshire and calls London home. Aaron Cowan Co-founder and Managing Partner Prior Experience An executive recruiter since graduating college, Aaron first joined a London-based technology search firm. He left the UK to establish the firm’s US presence, growing it to three offices and over 30 team members. As co-founder, Aaron has built the firm’s US operation, opening offices in Washington, DC and San Francisco. Personal An avid traveler, aspiring chef and fitness enthusiast, Aaron strives to strike balance between work and play. Born in Belfast, he now lives near Washington, DC with his wife and two young, highly energetic children. He has become a coffeeholic to keep himself upright. Jamie Robinson Partner Prior Experience Jamie brings over 20 years of experience in executive search and talent acquisition, specialising in Technology, Data, Digital and Cyber. He has worked across industries, primarily in Financial Services, with additional focus on Professional Services, Pharmaceuticals, Consumer and Energy, supporting clients from start-ups to global corporates. Personal Jamie has led search practices across the UK and Europe and spent 4.5 years in Hong Kong managing assignments worldwide. Outside work, Jamie is passionate about sport and food.
