We specialize in providing comprehensive facilities management, landscaping, grounds maintenance, and water treatment services for large facilities. Our dedication to excellence and integrity drives us to deliver top-tier services to our clients. Our Mission At SCM, our mission is to serve the Department of Defense, Department of Veterans Affairs, Department of Homeland Security, and Department of Energy with unwavering commitment and excellence. We aim to honor veterans and their families by transforming their final resting places into serene and reverent landscapes. We take pride in our veteran-focused approach, which emphasizes small business advocacy, marketing excellence, and securing government contracts. Landscaping and Grounds Maintenance: Our team of certified arborists and horticulturists create and maintain beautiful, sustainable landscapes.
