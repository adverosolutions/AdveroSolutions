We evolved into a Public Facility Maintenance contractor, servicing local school districts and municipalities. Today, not only do we handle facility maintenance we are also a general contractor specializing in site work packages for the private commercial and residential sectors. At Bosco Co, we are committed to guiding our customers through every phase of their projects, from start to finish, ensuring a successful and enjoyable experience. Our core values are dependability, safety, and creativity.
