About Us CloudAvail About Us Who is CloudAvail? CloudAvail is a team of five DevOps/Engineering consultants (headquartered in the Bay Area) that pride ourselves on understanding exactly what your organization needs and delivering on exactly that. Nothing more. Nothing less. Colin Johnson, Technology Lead and Senior DevOps Engineer Colin founded CloudAvail in May of 2011, convinced that there was a need for skilled DevOps consulting in the Bay Area. His intuition and timing were spot-on: the demand for AWS consulting was there, and CloudAvail took off shortly afterward. He’s most proud of the success of CloudAvail’s clients (some are national brands now) as well as current and former staff. Outside of work, Colin can be found trying to remain fit (basketball, kayaking, running, weightlifting) or enjoying life in San Francisco: coffee shops, restaurants and jazz clubs being at the top of the list.
