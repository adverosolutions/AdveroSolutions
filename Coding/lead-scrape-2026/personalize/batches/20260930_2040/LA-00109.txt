About Sean s Yard Service | Serving Local Lawns Since 2001! Skip to content About Us Sean’s Yard Service, LLC was founded by Arlington native, Sean O’Neill in 2001. As a young boy, Sean began pushing mowers around the lawns of his friends, family and neighbors. He also took great pride in helping to maintain the grounds of an Arlington landmark, The Knights of Columbus. As the years rolled along, Sean began to realize he could parlay his love of the outdoors with a sustainable business venture; one that would help keep his hometown beautiful. With that, Sean’s Yard Service was born. Sean in the Ardennes, Belgium — Meet our C.E.O. Charlotte Erin O Neill.
