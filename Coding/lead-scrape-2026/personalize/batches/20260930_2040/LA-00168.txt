--> About | SummitView Landscape Design top of page About Us. Philip Hatfield founded SummitView Landscape Design in 2016. He started out of his family s home by focusing on small landscape projects and lawn care. Over the past eight years, the company has evolved from a one-man operation to a full-service landscape company . ​ Based out of a new location, SummitView Landscape Design offers landscape maintenance services, new landscape design and installation, specializing in designing and building beautiful custom outdoor living spaces . ​ The team has installed paver patios , retaining walls , paver walkways , full landscaping with vibrant plants, water features , small patio ponds , fireplace centerpieces, outdoor kitchens , and more. ​ If you can dream up the possibility of a new outdoor living area, SummitView Landscape Design can bring it to reality. ​ SummitView Landscape Design serves Lima, Wapak, Findlay and the surrounding areas in Northwest Ohio.
