About Us | Veteran-Owned Main Creek Pest Solutions | Fresno, CA Main Creek Pest Solutions Inc. - Fresno, CA - Veteran Owned Pest Control 0 About Main Creek Pest Solutions Welcome to Main Creek Pest Solutions, a locally owned and operated pest control company serving Fresno, CA and the surrounding areas. Our founders, Bryan and Justin, are combat veterans with over 13 years of combined military experience and a deep understanding of the pest control industry. After their service, Bryan and Justin pursued higher education and obtained degrees in Kinesiology and Sports Psychology from Long Beach State, and Accounting and Auditing from the University of California Riverside, respectively. But the idea of creating a business was always on their minds. In 2021, they decided to start their own pest control company, Main Creek Pest Solutions, based on the family farm on Bryan's side of the family. At Main Creek Pest Solutions, we offer comprehensive pest control services to residential and commercial clients. Our team is committed to providing exceptional service and sustainable solutions to ensure a pest-free environment. Our founders bring their military values of integrity, honesty, and dedication to their business, and they strive to create a culture of trust and reliability in our company. Our industry experience and network of contacts make us highly skilled and trusted professionals in the field of pest control. We understand the importance of staying up-to-date with the latest technologies and techniques, and we maintain close relationships with other businesses in the industry. Main Creek Pest Solutions is also dedicated to giving back to the community. We provide services to non-profit organizations and support various military-related organizations. As combat veterans, we recognize the sacrifices made by our fellow service members and veterans and are committed to honoring their service.
