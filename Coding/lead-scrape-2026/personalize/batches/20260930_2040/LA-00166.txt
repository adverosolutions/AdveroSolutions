About - Ohio Green Works LLC - Professional Landscape Services Supply MENU 937-550-6446 Free Estimate Portal Cart 0 Ohio Green Works was born into a business in April 2009 in the midst of the Great Recession. While some companies had booming economies to grow in, founders Drew Parramore and Todd George took on the challenge of growing their business during uncertain times. The company utilized the hard work ethics of Drew and Todd and employed friends and family offering a few new services each year earning a great reputation. With customer service and satisfaction being top priority, they were able to expand the company and its list of services. Hiring skilled employees along the way, Ohio Green Works has developed into one of the leading landscape maintenance companies in the Dayton area offering an extensive list of services covering virtually everything landscape related. Drew and Todd still work daily with several crews and take pride in being able to offer clients, some that have been there since day one, a full array of professional services. On a day-to-day basis, the company is operated with the integrity and experience necessary to consistently exceed their clients expectations while offering exceptional services, quality workmanship, and professionalism. These core values allow Ohio Green Works to continually grow and set itself apart from the industry norms. With 13 years in, the company is still expanding and developing to lead the pack of companies offering high end service and with customer satisfaction being the top priority, Ohio Green Works will continue to take the lead for years to come.
