We do not use chemical pesticides of any kind. Santa Barbara Ventura County • Est. 2017 What We Do Growing up on the coast, we appreciate the majesty of the Pacific Ocean framed by stoic mountains. Since our environment is precious and immeasurably important, we do our best to provide stewardship to our clients’ property and the natural world. The word “ pest ” is used when wildlife causes humans discomfort or danger. True, human interactions with wildlife are not always pleasant. The fact remains, though, that all life on Earth contributes to a complex ecosystem that is not fully understood by modern science. With a clearer picture of our impact on nature, we are convinced that “ pest control ” needs to adapt to this realization. Why We Do It Paradise Pest Control chooses not to use conventional chemical pesticides because of the overwhelming evidence that they are harming wildlife. Since advances in the field of pest abatement provide effective solutions to many pest management issues, we feel this model is the most ethically correct choice. Our business model is 100% green pest management. Our support from Goodnature and their team also allows us to continue following the trends of green rodent control methods in a rapidly changing world. We do not use methods that have a negative impact on the ecosystem (as many conventional methods do). Our services are focused on our community and the environment. We take pride in our professional service, client communication, and results-based methods. Our team is backed by over 20+ years of experience. As effective alternative methods are developed, we continually research and use those that resonate with our mission.
