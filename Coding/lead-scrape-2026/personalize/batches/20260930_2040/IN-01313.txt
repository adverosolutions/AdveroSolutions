Kelley, Jiggins, and Associates was established back in 1970 as Kelley, Jones, and Blue Insurance Agency under the control of Joseph Dale Kelley. In 1976, Ronald Jiggins joined the agency and the name was changed to Kelley, Jiggins, and Blue. Through the years, Kelley, Jiggins, and Associates has grown through mergers and acquisitions. Our staff has developed excellent relationships with our existing companies as well as adding new companies to offer a wide array of insurance services to our clients. Build a relationship with us. We are proud to continue providing our clients with excellence in service and developing long-lasting relationships.
