Nathan Hathorn started Silverthorne Insurance Brokers, LLC in November of 2018 after being in the insurance industry as an agent for over 8 years. He was an agent and partner at his previous Insurance Agency and is excited to step into his own. Nathan is ready to continue service for his current clients and welcomes new clients to Silverthorne Insurance Brokers, LLC daily. Prior to starting Silverthorne Insurance in 2018, Nathan was an owner and agent at another local independent insurance agency in the Big Country. He has 10 years in the insurance field and has been selling insurance since he graduated from Abilene Christian University in 2009. Nathan specializes in commercial insurance as well as working with individuals on their home auto, life, health, builders risk, bonds, etc. As a Certified Insurance Counselor (CIC), he is able to help create and manage complex insurance programs and policies that help adequately cover his clients and protect their businesses, homes, vehicles, and most prized possessions. Nathan also does a lot of work with home builders, commercial contractors, oil gas companies, trucking companies, and any type of retail operations.
