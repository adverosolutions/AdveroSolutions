Nicola Yester Company, P.C. - Accounting and Tax Planning Services Skip to content Menu My Account Make a Payment Our Story Mission Statement Having the passion to empower our clients and communities as their trusted advisors; focusing on client satisfaction, service, and success Nicola Yester Co., P.C. was founded by Timothy J. Nicola, CPA and Norman J. Yester, CPA in Glastonbury, Connecticut in 1986. Now with a staff of 25 employees and seven partners, we provide accounting, tax planning and advisory services to closely held businesses in the manufacturing and construction industries, as well as other small businesses and non-profits. For over 40 years, our clients have trusted us to advise them in achieving their financial, tax, and business goals with integrity, responsiveness, and accuracy. Our clients consider us their trusted advisors, guiding them as they make informed financial decisions. We support our clients with in-depth expertise, high standards, and personalized customer service. We bring a team approach to our work, drawing on our collective experience, knowledge, and proficiency to deliver a customized approach to financial services for individuals, large and small businesses, and other agencies. We hold ourselves to high standards, take a proactive approach to our clients’ tax and financial needs, and deliver quality service. We support our staff with opportunities for continuing professional education, improving their technical expertise and financial knowledge, and a culture that prioritizes customer service. We participate in and host a number of charity events each year as a company, and support and encourage our talented and dedicated staff in their work with non-profit organizations. Favorites include the Manchester Road Race, CTCPA Annual Drive, Midwest Food Bank, and our annual Shred Event to benefit the Peter P. Monaco Detachment League in its work supporting Veterans. Nicola Yester is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants, Connecticut Society of Certified Public Accountants, Connecticut Association of Builders and Contractors, and has been named a Hartford Business Journal “Best Place to Work – Small Business”.
