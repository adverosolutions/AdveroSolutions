Our Team Straight Line Outdoor 0 Meet your local team. Dan Bostelman Owner/Operator Dan is the owner/operator of Straight Line Lawn Care LLC. The company was founded in 1997 by his father, Larry, in Defiance, Ohio. In 2017, Dan committed to growing this business full-time, utilizing his experiences in Law Enforcement and the Military to develop a community-minded and award-winning team of professionals with diverse backgrounds. Larry passed away in 2019, and Dan and his team honor Larry’s legacy, striving for excellence and exceeding expectations in innovation and service. Dan is married to Brooke and they have three children: Kipton, Kolton Indi. Brooke Bostelman Co-Owner, Administrative Executive, + Human Resources Brooke is the wife of owner Dan Bostelman and the mother of their three children. Brooke has served in the United States Air Force for over 17 years and recently stepped away from her 10 year Active Duty tour to work alongside Dan and the Straight Line Team to help support and grow the company. From providing encouragement and motivation to helping with administrative tasks like record-keeping and billing, Brooke will continue to improve business operations and reach new customers. She is excited about serving the community and helping create outdoor living families can enjoy. Guiseppe Blanchard Director Of Operations Guiseppe joined the Straight Line Outdoor team in August 2023. Before joining Straight Line, Guiseppe was Director of Operations and Workforce Development at the Defiance Dream Center. Guiseppe brings over 27 years of organizational and supply chain management experience to Straight Line Outdoor. Prior to his time at the Defiance Dream Center, Guiseppe and his family lived in northern Cincinnati and he worked as Supply Chain Manager overseeing the purchasing, sourcing, inventory management, warehousing and logistics departments for a manufacturer of air movement products to industrial, commercial, municipal and military customers across the world. Guiseppe holds a Business Administration degree from Defiance College and a Business Management degree from Northwest State Community College. Guiseppe and his wife, Brooke, have been married 25 years and have three children Alexis, Eowyn, and Ezekiel. Their oldest daughter, Alexis, is married to the love of her life, Matthew, and in August 2023 they welcomed their daughter, Iyana, into the world.
