Built on Four Generations of Green Industry Experience Meet the Tilson Family Four Generations of Landscaping Excellence Focused on You. Tilson Outdoors is led by father-and-son team Richard and Caleb Tilson—the third and fourth generation of a family deeply rooted in the Green Industry. It all started with Bryson Tilson, Caleb’s great-grandfather, who was known for being able to grow grass on a doorknob. He managed the grounds at Mars Hill College (now University) and passed his passion down to his son, Leonard Tilson, who co-founded L.A. Reynolds Garden Center in Lewisville—a staple in the local landscaping scene for decades. Explore Our Services Tilson Outdoor A Family Business Richard Tilson grew up working in that business, helping out at L.A. Reynolds and learning the value of hard work and horticultural know-how firsthand. He later went on to launch Tri-County Landscaping, gaining over two decades of experience in construction-grade landscaping and hardscaping. Caleb joined him at a young age, earned a degree in entrepreneurship from UNC Greensboro, and together they launched Tilson Outdoors. Today, they bring over 60 years of combined experience to every project, blending time-tested techniques with modern design to create outdoor spaces that are personal, purposeful, and built to last.
