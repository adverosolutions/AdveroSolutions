Christi brings not only the marketing and account planning experience, but with her journalism degree, she also brings a strong writing background to the table. Christi has served on a number of non-profit boards including United Way of Odessa (Past Chair), Centers for Children Families (Past Chair), Midland-Odessa Symphony and Junior Achievement (Past Chair), and she is also a Past President of the American Advertising Federation Permian Basin and Women in Communication. Christi received the silver medal award from the American Advertising Federation in 2010 and the Beacon Award from the Nonprofit Management Center in 2012.
