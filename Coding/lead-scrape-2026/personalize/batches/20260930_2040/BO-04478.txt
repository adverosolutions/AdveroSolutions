Since 2020, my family has been thrilled to be living in beautiful Coeur d Alene. I recognize that this area has many such recent arrivals from California that need a CPA who is versed in both Idaho and California tax law. I maintain active licenses in both states, and would be particularly suited to assist such individuals. It would be my pleasure to serve you with all your tax needs, and I look forward to speaking with you. Julie
