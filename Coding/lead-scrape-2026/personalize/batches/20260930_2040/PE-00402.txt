At On Point Exterminating, we are dedicated to providing our clients with the highest standard of integrity and extraordinary customer service. Through years of experience, our team of professionals is prepared to take on all your pest control needs. Generations of Experience Though founded September 27, 2012, the inception of On Point Exterminating began when Owner, Andrew O’Brien, was just a kid. Growing up, Andrew’s father owned and operated a fumigation company in Orange County instilling a love for the industry early on. Spending much of his childhood in the shop, Andrew became “one of the guys,” and began to work with the crew and help his dad plan out the workload each day. With so much of his life already immersed in the industry, owning and operating a company was his destiny. Captivated by the variety of pests and the reasons they enter different types of structures, Andrew found his interest in pest control. Endlessly studying books such as the Mallis Guide, better known as, “The Pest Control Handbook,” he eventually became an expert on the subject and decided it was time to make his dreams into a reality. With what began as just one man and a truck, On Point Exterminating now specializes in Rodent Removal Pest Control, and Termite Fumigation for homes and businesses throughout Southern California.
