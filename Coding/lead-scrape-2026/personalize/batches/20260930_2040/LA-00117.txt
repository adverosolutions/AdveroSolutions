His focus at the time was to provide residential lawn care and landscape maintenance service in and around his own neighborhood. Andrew had one goal in mind - to provide a full range of superior lawn care services in the area. After a few years (and after relocating to Vancouver, Washington) Andrew expanded his portfolio by becoming a landscape contractor, and offering other landscaping service that his clients had been requesting. Mostly smaller projects, such as bark installs , top-dressing, sprinkler repairs , minor planting, and pruning . After some time, we expanded further to include landscaping, hardscaping, water features and commercial lawn maintenance . All at affordable rates to all residents and business owners. Now the company has grown to become incorporated, and from a one person operation into a full staff of professionals able to meet your every landscaping need. We are still a small company, and are dedicated to building long term relationships with our clients by providing the highest standard of quality on each and every project we do. Our reputation for customer satisfaction in the area is what has made Neighborhood Lawn Care, Inc. a household name in the industry.
