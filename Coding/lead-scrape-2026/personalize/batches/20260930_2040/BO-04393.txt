About Us Strate Accounting Skip to content About Us About Strate Accounting Payroll Sonja R. Humpfer, owner of Strate Accounting Payroll, has enjoyed accounting since high school. She went on to obtain her Bachelor of Science degree in Accounting from Mount Mercy College, with a minor in Business Administration. From 1990 – 2010, Sonja gained experience in a variety of roles; including Assistant Controller / HR Manager, bookkeeper, accounts payable manager, accounting manager for companies like Allied Glass Products, Lil’ Drug Store Products, LLC., The Ar-Jay Center, Iowa Fluid Power, Dale McGrath Construction, and the Terry Durin Company. In 2010 she started Pfabio Accounting and Payroll LLC. from her home in Alburnett before moving to a shared office space in Central City. The company, now known as Strate Accounting Payroll LLC., is located at 220 Northland Ct NE, Cedar Rapids, IA 52402. Sonja is a highly-skilled professional Accountant known for prioritizing her client’s needs. She has been featured in the “Who is Who of Professional Women”, and is also a QuickBooks Certified Pro Advisor. Today, Sonja and the team at Strate Accounting Payroll are ready to help you and your small business succeed by providing top quality accounting and payroll services. Contact us today!
