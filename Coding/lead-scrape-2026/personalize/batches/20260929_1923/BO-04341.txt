About Us Sweeney Bookkeeping, LLC Based in Wichita, Kansas, Sweeney Bookkeeping focuses on your bookkeeping to allow you to focus on your business. Offering off-site and on-site bookkeeping services. Locally and Nationwide. About Us Brittany Sweeney Brittany is a business owner and dog mom. She has an extensive background in public and private accounting, having worked in the field since 2010. Over the years, she has worked with a variety of different businesses. Brittany decided to open Sweeney Bookkeeping, LLC after seeing a need for affordable bookkeeping services for small businesses. Her clients' success is what drives her. When she is not crunching numbers for her clients, you can find her networking, hanging out with her family, friends, two rescue mutts, enjoying a nice meal, or volunteering for causes she believes in. She loves to travel and is always dreaming of her next destination. You may also find her on the Peloton Leaderboard. Chanae Krahn Chanae is the virtual assistant for Sweeney Bookkeeping, wife, mom, and dog mom. She has been with the firm since 2019. As a former STEM Middle School teacher, Chanae brings her organizational and project management skills to Sweeney Bookkeeping, LLC. She has been instrumental in implementing workflow systems, helping with social media strategies, and organizing client documents. She enjoys hanging out with family and friends, and traveling.
