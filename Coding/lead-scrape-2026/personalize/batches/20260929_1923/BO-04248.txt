About Dozier Financial Services LLC 0 About Us We truly enjoy partnering with like-minded entrepreneurs to tackle the issues that keep you up at night. Whether it is through implementing a solid bookkeeping protocol for the first time or helping you level up your business with advisory services, we are here to help! Our team has decades of combined experience in accounting and financial analysis at your disposal. Founder and CEO Lauren Dozier graduated from the Sam M. Walton College of Business at the University of Arkansas with a major in Financial Management and Investments and a minor in Accounting. After graduating, she gained six years of industry experience at a Fortune 500 company, working as a financial analyst and a revenue accountant. There, she specialized in account reconciliations and financial analysis. She has since pursued her ever-growing interests in personal finance, investing, budgeting, and bookkeeping. She is a certified QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor and has a passion for helping small business owners by providing the very best bookkeeping services in Northwest Arkansas. The Team Our team is made up of staff bookkeepers, client reviewers, and account managers. By having multiple sets of eyes on the books every month, we are able to leverage the vast knowledge and expertise of our staff to deliver excellent results.
