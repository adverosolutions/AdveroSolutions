Let s Talk Part-Time Help, But 100% Committed A CFO is a financial officer of a company who has a wide range of responsibilities including controlling cash flow, overseeing the company’s expenditures, and planning for the future. A fractional CFO is a part-time CFO for companies that don’t have a big enough budget to pay a full-time CFO. Or companies that don t yet require the services of a full-time CFO. Extensive Experience Fractional CFOs have extensive financial experience, often times across multiple companies. They typically help organizations with a variety of financial needs and are a cost-effective way for small businesses to utilize professional finance and accounting expertise without the commitment of a full-time hire. A Fraction of the Cost of a Full-Time CFO Have you ever thought about hiring a CFO, but can t afford to hire someone full-time? A fractional or outsourced CFO is just the person for the job. A fractional CFO can provide the following services: financial analysis, forecasting, cash flow management, manage bank relationships, weekly meetings, company-wide KPIs, due diligence, and more, all at a fraction of the cost of a full-time CFO. The cost of hiring a full-time CFO can range from $150,000 to over $500,000 per year, plus benefits – putting the cost of an experienced CFO out of range for start-up and small businesses.
