About Denning Bookkeeping Solutions Team Denning - how we’re living a life we love - Hi, I'm Heather. I am the owner of Denning Bookkeeping Solutions. As nerdy as it sounds, numbers have always been "my thing" . In second grade when asked what I wanted to be when I grew up, I proudly responded "a mathematician". In high school I took five math courses plus three accounting courses because I actually found them interesting . I earned my Bachelor's Degree in Accounting from Wichita State University with Cum Laude honors. After a few years of working in small business accounting roles, I learned that my true passion is entrepreneurship and working one-on-one with small business owners and entrepreneurs across the country. As a fellow entrepreneur, I know firsthand what it is like to start and grow a company. I also understand the discipline it takes to set goals and stick to them . I truly am passionate about helping other small business owners and entrepreneurs succeed and grow financially . Working virtually allows the flexibility in my schedule to provide for my family while enjoying some of our favorite activities, such as traveling with my husband and our two young sons, going for walks with our rescue pup, Alfie , grilling delicious and healthy meals, and cheering on our favorite teams ( Go Chiefs and Jayhawks! ).
