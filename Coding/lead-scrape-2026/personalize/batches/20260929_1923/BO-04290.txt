About Golden Ratio Bookkeeping | Bookkeeping for Professional Service Providers 0 owner CEO of Golden Ratio Bookkeeping Meet Abby: Your Go-to Numbers Person I have always had a deep affinity for numbers. From taking extra math classes in college to creating spreadsheets for fun, numbers have been a constant source of joy in my life! Yet for many, they are a hurdle. When I started Golden Ratio Bookkeeping, I wanted to make a meaningful difference in my client’s lives – not simply fulfill an item on their to-do list. Whether we are focusing on maximizing profits or organizing complicated books, you’ll be working with someone whose love for numbers and entrepreneurship will shine through. This passion for supporting service providers is what led me to leave my high-paying, secure engineering career of 10 years. Now I get to nerd out daily, transforming bookkeeping into an empowering tool instead of a stressor! I live in Wichita, Kansas with my husband, our three children, and Kingsley, our elderly German Shepherd.
