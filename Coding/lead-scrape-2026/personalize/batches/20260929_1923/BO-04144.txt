Your Accounting Professionals At Glenmar CPAs, we believe that the foundation of a great professional relationship is mutual trust, respect, and care. We want to get to know you on a personal level, and we want you to get to get to know us. Ultimately, our goal is to form a relationship that enables us to provide year-round, understandable, and individualized advice to supply our clients with the knowledge to make informed business and personal decisions. Megan Cooper Certified Public Accountant Megan is a Bellarmine University alum with over 20 years of experience. She is our primary tax preparer and also provides bookkeeping services. Ashton Cooper Certified Public Accountant Ashton is a University of Louisville alum. She does all of our initial client on-boarding and is our QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor. OUR STORY Glenmar CPAs was formed in 2020 with one goal in mind - to serve families and local businesses. Both of our founders began their careers at large accounting firms. Through this experience, they realized that small businesses and individuals were their passion and decided to move to firms where they could better serve these types of clients. Since then, they have spent a combined total of over 25 years providing tax and accounting services and specializing in a wide variety of topics and industries. As a family owned CPA firm, we are dedicated to providing you with the tools that you need to support your business while breaking things down in a way that s easy to understand. Whether you require assistance at the corporate or personal level, we are ready to serve as your tax planners and accounting advisors.
