ABOUT FROST DANA NEWMAN CPAs Frost | Dana | Newman CPAs Menu ABOUT FROST DANA NEWMAN CPAs Frost Dana Newman was founded in 2011 by Jared Frost, Jarin Dana and Todd Newman, three CPAs who worked well together for years at the international accounting firm PricewaterhouseCoopers. In 2011, upon recognizing the limitations of personalized client service offered by larger firms, the three determined it was time to launch their own practice, bringing their relationships and skill sets together under a new CPA firm focused on comprehensive tax planning, compliance and accounting services. Far from simply starting their own CPA practice, their premise and mission was to be the one-stop-shop CPA firm for small businesses, and in particular, for small businesses and individuals operating in highly-specialized areas: locum tenens, healthcare professionals and legal services. The firm serves clients nationwide from its two offices, with Jarin Dana in the Salt Lake City, Utah, office and Jared Frost and Todd Newman in the Las Vegas, Nevada, office. They work with a complete support team equipped to truly handle clients’ needs.
