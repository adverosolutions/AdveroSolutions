The Modern Tax-Smart Wealth Solution How do I find a CPA and RIA (Registered Investment Advisor) under one roof so my taxes and investments actually work together? If you've Googled that question, you're not alone. High income earners, business owners, and retirees are increasingly frustrated by Fragmented Advice: A CPA who prepares last year's tax return and an advisor who builds an investment portfolio without a shared plan, shared data, or shared accountability. The result often includes missed planning opportunities, duplicated work, inconsistent assumptions, and unnecessary tax inefficiencies. The real challenge isn't simply "finding a good CPA" or "finding a good Financial Advisor". It's identifying professionals who operate in a coordinated structure where decisions happen in the right order, at the right time, using consistent data. Taxes affect investing. Investing affects taxes. Both influence retirement planning, business transitions, education funding, and legacy planning. Our CPA-RIA Under One Roof model is designed to create closer coordination between tax planning and wealth building strategies. Instead of tax work being a once-a-year look back, the relationship may include forward-looking projections, entity discussions, capital gains planning, charitable strategies, retirement optimization, and ongoing communication between disciplines.
