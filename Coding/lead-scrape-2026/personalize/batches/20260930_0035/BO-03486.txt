Learn More Contact Us Staff Our Team is Your Team Shawna Armstrong Owner Lead Bookkeeper About Shawna Shawna Armstrong Shawna Armstrong is the founder of Lazy Day Bookkeeping, a virtual firm based in the heart of Shedd, Oregon. With an associate degree in accounting and certifications in cloud-based bookkeeping solutions, Shawna offers expert support tailored to small business owners who want clarity and confidence in their finances. She launched Lazy Day Bookkeeping to give fellow entrepreneurs the freedom to focus on what they do best—while she handles the books with precision, transparency, and care. Clients know her not just for clean reconciliations and timely reports, but for being responsive, reliable, and genuinely invested in their success. When Shawna isn t organizing financial chaos, she s out adventuring with her husband in their Can-Am side-by-side, reading or rebinding books by hand, or tending to her small goat dairy—where the milk is raw, whole, and always local. Her life reflects the same values she brings to her business: rooted in integrity and fueled by passion.
