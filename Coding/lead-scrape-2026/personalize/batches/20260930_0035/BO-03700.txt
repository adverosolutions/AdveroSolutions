Weinberg Partners | About (888) 503-5528 info@weinbergpartners.com Who We Are Welcome to Weinberg Partners People First. With offices in Las Vegas and Chicago, we offer high-end specialized tax compliance, consulting and CPA services for public and private companies; entrepreneurs; individuals and their families; and those with international ownership, residency, and/or operations. We also provide virtual and on-sight CFO Services. We operate in a primarily virtual environment, allowing for seamless client data integration and delivery of services, giving us the time to develop and focus on our clients and their individual needs. Weinberg Partners has clients in all 50 states ; Canada, Europe , and Asia . Our Story Weinberg Partners was founded in 1986 by Paul D. Weinberg, CPA, CA, and started offering personal tax services to relocated mid-level executives at Fortune 100 companies in Chicago. Those clients were often initially serviced by the Big 4 (then the Big 6 or 8) – but then would fall off their radar requiring personalized attention at a cost-effective price. Three decades later we continue to serve as a viable alternative to the Big 4 for many individuals and companies, providing comprehensive tax and accounting services to businesses and individuals, seeking a personalized approach to their complex issues. We respond to our client’s inquiries virtually on the spot, and believe that “getting back to someone within 24 hours” is an unacceptable business practice.
