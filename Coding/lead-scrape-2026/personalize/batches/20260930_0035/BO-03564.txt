About | Bloomfield CFO About Us Bloomfield CFO was founded with a clear mission: to help business and practice owners make smarter financial decisions by providing responsive, strategic tax and accounting advice. Explore Our Services Meet the Team How It All Started After 15 years as a CFO and six years at an international accounting firm, Richard Bloomfield saw a gap in the industry: most CPAs focused on compliance, not strategy. In 2000, he founded Bloomfield CFO with a simple but powerful idea—combine technical tax expertise with real-world business insight, and deliver it all with consistent, high-touch service. Over the past two decades, Bloomfield CFO has grown from a solo practice to a nationally trusted firm, serving clients in industries like dentistry, healthcare, real estate, and other professional services. Meet Our Founder: Richard Bloomfield, CPA Rich Bloomfield founded Bloomfield CFO in 2000 after 15 years as a CFO and six years with an international accounting firm. He has a Masters of Accountancy degree (Tax Emphasis) and graduated Magna cum Laude from Brigham Young University in 1981. Rich has significant experience planning and executing buyouts and transitions for dental practices and many other businesses. He has focused much of his professional career on gift and estate planning including setting up structures to protect client businesses and assets. Included in that planning has been a significant focus on retirement planning - particularly on planning to minimize taxes during retirement. Rich enjoys spending time with his wife, 5 children and 19 grandchildren. He also enjoys running, focusing on nutrition and fitness, and spending time outdoors gardening and at the ranch as much as possible.
