Professional Online Tax and Bookkeeping We are a small firm led by an Enrolled Agent (EA), providing professional online tax and bookkeeping services backed by more than 20 years of experience. EAs are the only federally licensed tax practitioners who specialize in taxation and also have unlimited rights to represent taxpayers before the IRS.
