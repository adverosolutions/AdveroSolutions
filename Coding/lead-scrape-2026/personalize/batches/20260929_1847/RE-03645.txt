We’re 100% owner managed and our success stems from the fact that we love what we do and we take our work personally. Our philosophy is simple. We put relationships before profits. At South Bay Nannies we have a genuine desire to be known for our excellent service levels, integrity, and dedication to the families we serve. Local, and growing. Why Choose Us Working with a small business like South Bay Nannies means you get more than a placement - you gain a partner who knows and cares for your whole family. Our boutique approach allows for personal attention, lasting relationships, and carefully matched caregivers who fit seamlessly into your home. Through our direct hire model, you’ll see higher caliber applicants, reduced long-term costs and greater longevity, all while enjoying the same exceptional quality and genuine, family-feel experience that sets South Bay Nannies apart.
