About Us RemotelyHR 0 About Us We have a combined 60 years of HR experience. That means we are HR ninjas. And yes, we love HR. We’ll take over your HR and ensure compliance, streamline HR operations - and most importantly, less headaches for you. Founder principal Consultant Jamie Urquhart, SHRM-SCP Throughout Jamie’s professional career, she wore many hats, including Office Manager and Operations Manager. Through it all, the common denominator was always people. She decided to narrow her career focus, officially head down the HR career path, and received her SHRM-SCP certification. In 2021, Jamie founded RemotelyHR and has since been dedicated to helping California business owners navigate complex compliance challenges and stay updated on the latest regulations. What Jamie enjoys the most about HR helping business owners feel confident with California’s tricky compliance rules and making sure employees have what they need to succeed at work. When she’s not fine-tuning an employee onboarding process, Jamie spends time with her husband, two kids, and two rescue dogs. She also enjoys cooking, fine wine, traveling, and getting lost in a good book. HR CONSULTANT Alina Sanchez, PHRca Alina brings over 15 years of experience in Human Resources, Recruiting and Business Operations across multiple industries. As a former business owner, she understands the nuanced challenges of scaling your business, growing your teams and staying compliant. Alina holds a Bachelor's Degree from Cal Poly San Luis Obispo in Political Science (Pre-Law), a Professional in Human Resources (PHR) and a Professional in Human Resources, California (PHRca).
