Common Growth Stages pitfalls Growth Spurt You don t workforce plan based on past trends or projections so when you need more hands on deck, you rush to hire. You don t create clear role responsibilities, ways of working and expected outcomes. You onboard the rushed hire(s) without a formalized training plan SOPs, manual/guides, on-the-job coaching, etc. The cost of a bad hire is about 30% of that hire s first year wages + 17% of supervisor s time + $4K average cost of rehiring. BAD HIRES ARE COSTLY! Fast (Chaotic) Growth You already have a team but the increase in demand requires more people that can product results, faster. You continue to use outdated systems or manual processes to hire and manage people. Your ability to hire and train people lags the current demands of size, scope and speed for producing results.
