About Our Mission | Digital Action Digital Action, Inc. 0 About Digital Action, Inc. Since 1995, Digital Action has completed more than 1000 recruiting engagements across the US, placing C-level executives, managers, and functional specialists in a variety of industries, including printing and graphic arts, biotechnology and nanotechnology, medical imaging and informatics, hospice and palliative medicine, and emerging digital technologies. Our commitment Our commitment is to provide superior service to our client companies and candidates alike. We promise to provide the following: Personalized Approach/Consultative Manner – Digital Action is small enough that every client is vital to us. And large enough to deploy the full resources needed to fill each position quickly. Our team is committed to providing exceptionally responsive service. We actively partner with our clients, grasping their goals, philosophy, organization and requirements to ensure that we match parties optimally and expeditiously. Superior Lines of Communication – Your communication with us will prove to be timely and informative. Respectfully and responsively, we’ll apprise you of progress on a regular basis throughout the search. In fact, we keep all parties informed and engaged while driving the process until a position is filled. Focus on Long Term-Relationships – We’re proud of the fact that a significant percentage of our work is repeat business with select clients. As a result, our “off limits” list remains manageable and we maintain a large recruitment base. Resources – Our proprietary database of industry professionals, coupled with research we’ve compiled on companies nationwide, both updated continuously, enable us to quickly target highly qualified executives. Our consultants utilize advanced sourcing techniques leveraging social media, sophisticated search management methodology and specific industry insights to meet our clients’ needs. Confidentiality, Integrity Ethics – Respecting the sensitive nature of your business, we will never divulge confidential information nor submit a candidate’s credentials without explicit permission to do so. Our integrity and professionalism manifest themselves throughout your relationship with Digital Action.
