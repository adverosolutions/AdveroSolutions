Howard Glenn, Managing Partner Howard Glenn has worked in the executive search and talent advisory space for over a decade and founded Heritage Executive Search in 2023. Prior to Heritage, Howard joined New Capital Partners, a top quartile private equity firm in Birmingham, AL, to build their Talent group. Howard previously worked for a boutique executive search firm where his responsibilities included managing senior level searches within the private equity practice and building the emerging PE portfolio. Howard holds an MBA from the University of South Carolina and an undergraduate degree from North Carolina State University. He and his wife live in Birmingham, Alabama with their two sons.
