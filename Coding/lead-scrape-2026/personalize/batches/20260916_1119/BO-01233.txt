About Us Zoma Books 0 About Us Our mission at Zoma Books is to provide Entrepreneurs and small business owners with meaningful insight into their financial perfomance. We know that accounting can take the attention away from focusing on the core business, which is why we are here to help. Adam Marasco, CPA Adam is a Certified Public Accountant (Pennsylvania) with 8 years of professional experience. Adam graduated from University of Dayton in May 2015 and earned his CPA certification in October 2015. After college, he worked for EY in the assurance practice performing financial statement audits for both public and private clients in a variety of industries. Since leaving EY, Adam has gained further experience as a finance leader at a startup company in the real estate industry. “During the course of my career I learned how valuable accurate books and records can be to entrepreneurs and small businesses owners. I found that I have a passion for client service and my favorite part of the job is getting to learn about new businesses and industries” . Chelsea Marasco Chelsea is a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor with 8 years of professional experience. Chelsea graduated from Clarion University in May of 2015. After college, she worked in the finance department for a large publicly traded retail company, with the majority of her experience being in payroll. She now works at a local CPA firm doing monthly bookkeeping for a number of small businesses in the Pittsburgh area. “No matter how large or small the business, having an efficient, well thought out processes in place can make a big difference. It’s been a fulfilling part of my career to help organizations save time and become more efficient, which is exactly what I hope to bring to clients in the future.”
