Our business services also include bookkeeping, payroll processing and business start up consultations. What is an Enrolled Agent? An enrolled agent (EA) is a federally-authorized tax practitioner, who is recognized across all 50 U.S. States, has technical expertise in the field of taxation, and is empowered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury to represent taxpayers before all administrative levels of the Internal Revenue Service for audits, collections, and appeals.
