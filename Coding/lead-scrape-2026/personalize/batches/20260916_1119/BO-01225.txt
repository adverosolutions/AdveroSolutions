About Ryan Langan, CPA, MTax | Oakmont Tax Oakmont Tax Consulting 0 About the firm Big-firm rigor, without the big firm. Oakmont Tax Consulting is a Philadelphia Main Line CPA firm specializing in tax preparation, planning, and notice resolution for physicians, business owners, and professionals, serving clients locally and nationwide. Founder Ryan Langan, CPA Founder, Oakmont Tax Consulting Credentials education Certified Public Accountant, Pennsylvania B.S., Accounting Finance, Saint Joseph's University Master of Taxation, Villanova University Charles Widger School of Law Certificate, State Local Taxation, Villanova University Charles Widger School of Law Experience that started at the top. Ryan Langan is a Pennsylvania CPA who worked at a multinational public accounting firm, then managed corporate tax in-house at the world's largest business software company, where his work included the resolution of more than 1,000 notices from hundreds of tax jurisdictions. He founded Oakmont Tax to bring that depth to individuals and closely held businesses. During his time in public accounting, Ryan prepared returns for every kind of client, from billionaire sports-team owners to retired W-2 earners. The work spanned partnerships, S corporations, single-member LLCs, trusts, estates, and nonprofit compliance. In-house, the scope widened: corporate tax compliance across numerous subsidiaries, international filings, and a steady stream of notices from federal, state, and local authorities. Each one was resolved, documented, and closed. Ryan started Oakmont Tax because he knew there was more he could offer businesses and individuals than a large firm ever could.
