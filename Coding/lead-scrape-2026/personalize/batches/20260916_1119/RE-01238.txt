For over 40 years we have built an excellent reputation of providing a very professional and personalized service to both our Clients and Candidates. We can do this because we understand both the intricate technical, scientific, and educational needs and requirements of our client companies, as well as the unique expertise, desires, and aspirations of our candidates, allowing us to make the ideal match between the company and the individual. We concentrate our efforts on all levels of Clinical Operations, Clinical Research, Clinical Development, Drug Safety / Pharmacovigilance, Regulatory Affairs, Program / Project Management, Quality Assurance, Patient Care, Human Resources, Legal, Sales, Marketing, Market Access, Commercial, Medical Affairs, Medical Science Liaisons, and Production / Engineering / Mechanical. The combination of extensive experience in the field, and a state-of-the-art data management system enables us to offer the most confidential and thorough professional placement services available. ​ Founded by Simon Leeder (SLI s President) in 1981, we have earned unparalleled regard with potential Candidates who often prefer hearing from someone who has a lengthy track record in the industry, as opposed to a person/firm with limited experience and/or broader focus. We have also worked to gain the trust of Client companies that are best described as industry leaders in the world of new drug/product development, approval, and manufacturing by establishing a business that is anchored on integrity, responsibility, innovation, and results. And with an unmatched corporate wellness program, we talk the talk and walk the walk. SLI has the experience that gives us the insight to connect Pharmaceutical, Biotech, Medical Device, Healthcare, and Manufacturing professionals to hiring departments avoiding oversights and missed opportunities. While the majority of our efforts are concentrated on the continental United States, we have also successfully placed individuals in Canada, Mexico, and many countries abroad.
