A Lifetime of Experience Some people know from childhood what their careers will be; some people spend their whole lives figuring it out. For Drew Barbosa, a childhood hobby grew into a rewarding career and successful Dallas plumbing, heating and cooling business. Drew was just a boy when he began learning about HVAC and plumbing. His neighbor, Ed Henniger, was in the business and did a lot of work from his home. Drew helped Ed when he could, learning how to run air conditioning refrigerant lines and ductwork—a hobby that turned out to be an apprenticeship. As he got older, Drew did plumbing and HVAC work part time while he went to school. He finished school and began working in freight and trucking, as his father and grandfather had done before him, but he didn’t give up his HVAC and plumbing work. He began building a name for himself as a plumber and HVAC technician, and eventually he was earning more work than he could do part time. In 1979, he and his wife, Janet, officially began Barbosa Plumbing Air Conditioning , basing the business out of their garage until 1984 when they moved their growing HVAC and plumbing company into a building on Denton Drive in North Dallas. Since then Barbosa Plumbing Air Conditioning has continued to grow and Drew and Janet’s children have joined the company, actively helping their parents with installation, training and business development.
