Torino Accounting Group About A modern public accounting firm built around clarity, rigor, and trust. Torino Accounting Group serves clients who need responsive guidance, sound judgment, and a level of professionalism that matches the complexity of their financial decisions. Torino Accounting Group was founded to provide a more thoughtful accounting experience for clients whose needs extend beyond routine compliance. The firm advises growth-stage and established businesses, nonprofit organizations, high-net-worth individuals, and individual decision-makers who value accuracy, perspective, and steady communication. Based in New Jersey and informed by work with internationally mobile clients, Torino Accounting Group brings together tax insight, assurance discipline, and practical advisory support. William Fritchie, CPA, spent nearly a decade in audit and advisory roles at Ernst Young, PwC, and Deloitte, with experience serving multinational organizations including Toys R Us, General Electric, ANA Airlines, and Eurobank. The result is a relationship grounded in technical depth and a clear understanding of what matters most to leadership teams.
