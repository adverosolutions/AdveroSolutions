Mission - (Why we do what we do) Having a nice green healthy lawn has many benefits. A nice lawn looks great which increases your home's curb appeal and thus your property value. Many realtors associate 15% of your property value to your lawn and landscape conditions. A nice lawn also adds to the enjoyment of your property Grass is soft under foot which is more comfortable and safer to play on. It reduces the muddy mess brought into your home by kids and pets playing in the yard. Simply being around nice healthy plants is good for us too, it's proven to make people feel happier and healthier. The average sized healthy lawn here in New England circulates and cleans enough air each year to produce enough oxygen for a family of four. Healthy turf helps the ground slowly absorb water which reduces run-off and erosion, this helps to properly recharge our aquifers and avoid surface water contamination by overflowing stormdrains and local streams. Recognizing all of these benefits it is understandable that we all would desire a nice green healthy lawn around our home and where we work. The problem is that in pursuit of nice green healthy lawns over the past 30 years most do-it-yourselfers and other lawn care companies used very high levels of pesticides and chemical fertilizers without much thought of the consequences. These products poisoned our waterways and exposed our families to dangerous pesticides. Looking at the marketplace there weren't other options.
