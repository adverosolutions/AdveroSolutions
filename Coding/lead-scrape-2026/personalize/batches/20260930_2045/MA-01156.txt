About - Runway Influence About Runway Influence Learn more Connecting Businesses WIth Target Audience Beyond Geographical Boundaries Runway Influence is a leading influencer marketing agency based in Los Angeles and operating worldwide. Founded in 2017, we have quickly risen to the forefront of the industry as the go-to partner for brands seeking impactful social media campaigns. Our innovative work has been featured in major publications – from Variety and Forbes to Harper’s Bazaar, The Los Angeles Times, GQ, Elle, WWD, and Maxim – a testament to our influence and credibility in the marketing space. We pride ourselves on not just being another agency, but the industry leader in crafting authentic, high-performance influencer campaigns that drive real results. Our Story and Vision Runway Influence was started by marketing whiz Ernest Sturm with a bold vision to redefine digital advertising through the power of influencer partnerships. Recognizing a gap in the market, Sturm set out to blend creativity, data, and social media savvy to create a unique niche in the cutthroat Los Angeles marketing landscape. From day one, our mission has been clear: connect top brands with the best model influencers and content creators to tell compelling brand stories. This focus on working with elite talent and fostering genuine relationships between influencers and brands quickly established Runway Influence as a beacon of excellence in a competitive market. We understood early on that social media was the new frontier of branding, and we built strong bonds with influencers who genuinely love the brands they represent. This ethos of authenticity and passion has fueled our rise and continues to drive our vision for the future. “What sets Runway Influence apart in the influencer marketing industry is our commitment to exclusivity and authenticity.” Ernest Sturm, CEO Staying true to that philosophy, we have differentiated ourselves by emphasizing our unique niche and delivering results that speak volumes. We focus exclusively on high-quality collaborations, leveraging influencers who truly align with a brand’s image. By doing so, we ensure that every campaign feels organic and meaningful. Our founder’s original dream – to find the best influencers and connect them with the top brands they love – remains at the core of everything we do.
