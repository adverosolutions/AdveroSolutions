How MedMetrics works: MedMetrics accepts your Workers Compensation medical billing and payment data, along with claims level data and data from various additional sources in our Workers Compensation medical data warehouse. MedMetrics imports, integrates and normalizes your data for your secure, confidential sector in our data warehouse. MedMetrics analyzes your data to put decision support information at your fingertips—vigorous, targeted information to help you manage your medical costs and monitor your programs, vendors, contracts, providers and networks. MedMetrics also compares your data anonymously with that of others to create business and competitive knowledge for you. Please see Products and Services . Who we are: The leaders of MedMetrics have many cumulative years of experience in health industry, Workers' Compensation, and information technology. Karen Wolfe, BSN, MA, MBA is MedMetrics founder and president. Prior to MedMetrics , she founded a software company, Health Management Technologies, Inc., specializing in occupational health practice management and Workers' Compensation medical case management which was sold in 1999. MedMetrics technical experts leverage extensive Workers' Compensation medical data management experience to create simple, yet powerful information apps for our clients.
