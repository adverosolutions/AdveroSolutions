About Integrated Pest Management | Fort Bend County TX Skip to content Home - About Us About Integrated Pest Management, Inc. Since 1984, Integrated Pest Management, Inc. has proudly protected homes and businesses across Greater Houston with safe, effective, and environmentally responsible pest control solutions. As a family-owned company rooted in local values, we believe pest control should protect more than just property — it should protect people, pets, and the planet. Our Mission To provide innovative, science-driven pest control that delivers long-term results while minimizing environmental impact. We combine decades of experience with modern Integrated Pest Management (IPM) strategies to keep Houston’s homes and businesses safe, clean, and pest-free. Our Story Integrated Pest Management, Inc. was founded in the early 1980s to bring a smarter, more sustainable approach to pest control. What began as a small, family-run business serving Houston neighborhoods has grown into one of the region’s most trusted pest management providers — all while maintaining our core values of honesty, reliability, and care for our community. For over 40 years, our commitment has remained the same: Use proven, eco-friendly methods. Hire skilled, certified technicians. Treat every customer like family. What “Integrated Pest Management” Really Means IPM isn’t just our name — it’s our philosophy. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) is a science-based approach that combines inspection, prevention, and targeted treatment to achieve long-term pest control with minimal pesticide use. Instead of simply reacting to problems, we address the root causes — making your environment naturally resistant to pests.
