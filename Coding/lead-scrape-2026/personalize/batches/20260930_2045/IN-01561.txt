Who We Are Our Story Heath Barnett and Randy Allen, brothers by marriage, both grew up with entrepreneurial families who inspired them to strive for success and freedom. They both found themselves in the insurance business as adults, frustrated with the limitations of being a captive agent for big insurance companies. They put their heads together to start something new—Heath bringing a deep knowledge of the insurance business, Randy bringing his generations-old business systems expertise. For two years, Randy and Heath invested everything into creating a new business strategy for those willing to take a risk and bet on themselves. They would provide the tools to help people earn unlimited income for their families, without having to give up control and freedom in their life. Now, you can find Legacy Agency Partners in multiple states, and we’re always growing. We’re a community of like-minded people empowered with the support, training, and motivating competition necessary to build a business and provide the same opportunities for others. It’s time to take ownership of your career. When you take ownership of your career, you will grow in more ways than one. It’s our goal above all to help communities thrive by offering opportunities for freedom, fulfillment, and shared purpose, along with the protection our customers need to rest easy, even when bad things happen.
