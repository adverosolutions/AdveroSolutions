About 2025 - A Plus Landscaping Skip to content About Us Creating Custom Outdoor Living Spaces A Plus Landscaping A Plus Landscaping is a family-owned company rooted in hard work, craftsmanship, and a love for the outdoors. Founded in 2006 by Marcus, our journey began with mulch and stone, literally! Marcus grew up helping his father run a local mulch and stone business, where he first discovered his passion for outdoor living space design. What started as small decorative mulch and stone installs soon grew into a full-scale landscaping company after Marcus trained with a leading hardscape firm and began taking on larger projects. Today, A Plus Landscaping is a full-service landscape and hardscape company based near Lancaster, PA. We specialize in designing and building custom outdoor living spaces. From simple patios and cozy fire pits to full backyard transformations with kitchens and pools. Our mission is to help you bring your backyard dreams to life. Whether you re envisioning a quiet retreat or a space to entertain friends and family, we have the tools, experience and creativity to make it happen.
