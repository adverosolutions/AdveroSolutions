About Kyle Anderson - Vermont Accountant - Da Vinci Tax About Us MISSION, HISTORY VISION Kyle Anderson Education: Bryant University College of Business: BS Accounting Designations: Enrolled Agent (EA) Memberships: National Association of Tax Preparers (NATP) Vermont Tax Practitioners Association (VTPA) (Board of Directors, Secretary) After graduating from Bryant University’s College of Business with a Degree in Accounting and Legal Studies Kyle spent 12 years working for large public companies in various industries including Banking, high tech manufacturing, apparel and accessory licensing, and retail grocery. He started his career with a large bank in Connecticut as a Staff Auditor focusing on Financial and Operational audit. This was followed by employment for a world leading manufacture of Carbon Fiber and composite materials overseeing the Internal and Financial controls of all US operations while also collaborating with the International auditor on joint tasks. Shortly thereafter he decided to head south to enjoy the warmer climate and join family living in North Carolina. While there he was the lead auditor for a small bank managing the financial and compliance audit teams while also working in conjunction with the FDIC, the Office of the Comptroller of Currency (OCC) and the Department of Justice helping the insolvent bank come out from under a Cease and Desist orders from the FDIC and OCC and avoid a Government takeover. After assisting the bank with an acquisition by a private equity firm he moved on to work with the largest apparel and accessory licensing company as a lead Royalty Accountant. While there Kyle dealt with clothing designers such as Calvin Klein, Guess, Michael Kors, etc. managing royalty contracts and tracking the financial performance of the Royalty department. Kyle spent his last two years in North Carolina overseeing the Financial reporting for a national grocery chain. After 7 years in North Carolina Kyle decided it was time to come home to Vermont and now resides in South Burlington. Before opening his Tax practice in Shelburne Kyle was the Director of Accounting for a regional trucking company. It was at this time he decided to pursue his goal of leaving the corporate world and staring a business of his own.
