Our Story — Avalon Landscape Construction Avalon / About / Our Story Our Story Outdoor spaces have the power to transform how people live. What began as a small local landscape service grew — through consistent improvement, craftsmanship, and communication — into one of Northern Virginia leading design-build companies. § 01 · Our Story Built on craft, not shortcuts. Tyler Johnson, Founder CEO, took over a small high-school landscaping initiative in 2020 and reimagined it into a professional, client-focused, construction-oriented operation. After graduating, he committed fully to the business and began expanding services, elevating standards, and investing in the systems, people, and equipment needed to serve clients at a higher level. As the business grew, so did the vision. The company rebranded as Avalon Landscape Construction — a name chosen to represent transformation, natural beauty, and the wisdom that comes from building something meaningful over time. Today, Avalon delivers comprehensive landscape design, hardscaping, drainage solutions, and property enhancement services for homeowners and businesses who value quality, clarity, and dependable communication. § 02 · Leadership Founder CEO Tyler Johnson Leads design, estimating operations We built Avalon to be the same team from concept through year-round care.
