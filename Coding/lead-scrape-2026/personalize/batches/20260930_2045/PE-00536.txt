We work with homeowners and business owners with one goal in mind: to provide exceptional results you can count on. Our difference is our people – the Live Oak Pest Control pest specialists and customer service specialists . Our team is highly educated, well-trained, and passionate about pest control in North Central Florida. Each undergoes a minimum of 160 hours of education and testing, product safety, and customer service training before they serve even one Live Oak Pest Control customer. Pest specialists attend monthly service, education, and training meetings to ensure the highest level of competency and customer service. We proudly serve these North Central Florida counties: Suwannee County Baker County Columbia County Dixie County Eastern Leon Gilchrist County Hamilton County Lafayette County Jefferson County Levy County Madison County North Alachua County Taylor County Union County Highly Trained Pest Control Experts Over the years, Live Oak Pest Control has developed a reputation for excellent customer satisfaction, and we’re serious about keeping it that way. Before becoming a pest specialist he or she must pass the Level 2 FDLE Background Check including a Florida Division of Driver’s License check. Live Oak Pest Control only hires individuals with a clean background and driving record. All of our pest specialists must be able to read a map, have good math and communication skills as well as be of good character and attitude. Live Oak Pest Control expects each pest specialist to be courteous and respectful to every customer. We make safety and training our top priority. The State of Florida requires only two hours of training but we require an additional one hour per month of CORE training on pesticide use, handling, and safety. Live Oak Pest Control also requires pest specialists to attend a minimum of one hour of training each month, so they will be able to provide you, our customers, with the most up-to-date information and technology in the pest control industry. Our technicians are also trained in Integrated Pest Management techniques.
