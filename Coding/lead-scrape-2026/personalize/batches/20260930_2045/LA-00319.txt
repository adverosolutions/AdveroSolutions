Tim graduated from SUNY Cobleskill with an Associates Degree in Landscape Design and Turf Management. Previous to starting Brennan Landscaping, Tim had worked locally at Faddegons Nursery, Hewitt’s Garden Centers, and in Nantucket, Mass for Albert Glowacki Landscaping. Brennan Landscaping specializes in landscape design, installation, and maintenance. Tim can sit down and consult with the customer and design a landscape plan which meets all the needs of the customer. Designs are now generally completed on computer with a visual design program.
