About - City Mom Collective Skip to content About City Mom Collective City Mom Collective (CMC) is a national parenting platform that connects moms through honest content, helpful resources, and a growing directory of hyperlocal parenting sites. Whether you're looking for support, community, or just a reminder that you're doing great, CMC is a place where all moms belong, regardless of background, location, or experience. You don’t have to be perfect to belong here. We believe motherhood should be met with connection and compassion. From our national summer camp guides to our mom-to-mom stories and local directories, we’re here to make the journey feel a little less lonely and a lot more supported. Through our parent company, City Mom Media, we also offer meaningful advertising opportunities for brands that want to show up for moms in a real and relatable way. We sell advertising both on our own behalf and as an agent for our partner sites, helping businesses reach moms where they are, with messages that matter. We’re so glad you found us. This isn’t just a website. It’s a community built by moms, for moms. No matter who you are or where you’re starting from, you are welcome here.
