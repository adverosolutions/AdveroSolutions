--> About | Bella Gardens Landscaping top of page About Bella Gardens Bella Gardens does all things plants and florals! We are a boutique landscape design, garden installation, and horticultural maintenance company in NYC that is proudly woman-owned and operated. Our design studio and workshop is located on the Upper West Side and provides fully-insured services in the greater NYC area. Bella Gardens provides fine gardening services for residences, businesses, and public spaces in the New York City area. We strive to create unique and beautiful gardens that are both inviting and environmentally sustainable. ​ Our floral designers at BG Florals create custom flower arrangements for local delivery, weddings, and events, and our landscaping team creates dream gardens on rooftops and other urban spaces all over NYC. Landscape Services Our Founder Lisa holds a graduate degree with a Master of Science in Landscape Design from Columbia University in the City of New York, and a Certificate in Horticulture from the New York Botanical Garden School of Professional Horticulture and Landscape Design where she worked with some of the world s finest horticulturists in a world renowned garden. She has been involved in horticulture from a young age by working in family vineyards in upstate NY, and is now designing rooftop gardens and other urban green spaces in New York City. She recognizes the therapeutic benefits that trees and gardens have on people, and wants to bring nature into the lives of urban dwellers in the form of high end garden design. ​ Lisa has found success through her natural ability to connect with clients and truly understand what will serve a garden space best based on her client s unique NYC landscape needs, design preferences, and personality. ​ Now, the Bella Gardens team has expanded with a group of highly skilled horticulture specialists all over the New York City area. We look forward to serving you! The Team The Bella Gardens crew is a small number of talented, highly-skilled, horticultural professionals who take pride in their work and have years of experience. The Promise Bella Gardens makes sure our landscapes are beautifully manicured, have show-stopping displays, and are healthy and happy throughout the seasons. The Mission We are passionate about providing beautiful green spaces that are both inviting and sustainable for you to enjoy and to be proud of.
