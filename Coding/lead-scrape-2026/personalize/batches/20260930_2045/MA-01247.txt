About Agency Arts 0 About Agency Arts is a Los Angeles-based management company representing an intimate group of photographers and filmmakers, founded by 25-year entertainment industry insider Kelly Norris Sarno. Norris Sarno’s ferocious lifelong support of artists grew out of an early appreciation of art in its various forms, shaping a talent not only for recognizing new artists but also for facilitating their careers and creative pursuits. Her previous endeavor, the wildly successful Production company Symphony 19, which she also founded and helmed, oversaw content for Taylor Swift, Katy Perry, My Chemical Romance, Sia, Jack White, Michael Bublé, Christina Aguilera, Seal, The Cure, Rage Against the Machine, Metallica and more (with combined online views of over 1.5 billion). David Bowie’s Dead Man Walking music video, produced by Norris Sarno and directed by Floria Sigismondi, remains part of the MOMA permanent collection in New York City. Kelly’s desire to enhance and encourage the photographers, filmmakers, and fine artists she met while helming Symphony 19 made for a natural transition to artist representation. Born and raised in Canada, her first creative community upheld the motto: No egos, no assholes, only artists supporting artists. She’s happy to say that she has yet to deviate from that sentiment.
