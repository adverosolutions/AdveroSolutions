About Prime Time Insurance Agency | Freehold NJ Skip to content Home / About We're independent, and that changes everything. Insurance with a personal touch — not as a slogan, but as the reason the agency exists. Who we are Prime Time Insurance Agency is an independent agency based in Freehold, New Jersey, licensed in New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania and Maryland. We aren't employees of an insurance company; we represent more than twenty of them. When you call, you reach someone who can look at your whole picture — the house, the cars, the business, the life policy — and tell you where the gaps are. We opened our doors in 2014. An agency earns its keep on the bad days — the storm, the totaled car, the renewal that jumps for no reason you can see — and we have handled a great many of them since. Ramsey Trusted We are a Ramsey Trusted Pro — one of the insurance agencies Ramsey Solutions refers its audience to. Ramsey only refers people to independent agents rather than to company representatives, for the same reason we built the agency that way: an agent who can move you between carriers is working on your side of the table. It is an outside endorsement rather than anything we awarded ourselves, which is the only kind worth mentioning. Where we write Our office is in Freehold and we write throughout New Jersey — shore towns, the suburbs, and everything inland. We are also licensed in New York, Pennsylvania and Maryland . That matters more often than people expect. A shore house in one state and a primary home in another, a move across the river, a business that operates over state lines, a child at college out of state — all of these are simpler when one agency can write in all four rather than handing you off. How we work Three commitments shape everything we do: We shop, you decide. We bring you real options with the differences explained, including the trade-offs that make a cheaper policy cheaper. We stay reachable. You'll deal with people who know your file. No phone tree, no starting over with a stranger each time. We review when it matters. Ask us at renewal, or any time life changes — a move, a new driver, a renovation, a new venture.
