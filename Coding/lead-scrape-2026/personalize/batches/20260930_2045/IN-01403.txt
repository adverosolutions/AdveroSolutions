About Us | Frank D. Spicer Insurance Agency Skip to the content Home > About Us About Us T hree generations of Spicers have been protecting local families and businesses since 1954. Frank Spicer Sr started in the insurance business some 61 years ago. Frank Spicer Sr worked as a life insurance agent and manager with Met Life and New England Financial. Following in his father’s footsteps, Frank Spicer Jr began as a life insurance agent in 1974. After several years Frank decided to begin his own independent insurance agency in May of 1984. With no clients, no office, and very little money, Frank started the Spicer agency out of bedroom in his Annandale, VA home. Over the past 31 years the Spicer agency has grown and now has 14 employees and thousands of loyal clients. Frank Spicer Jr’s son Chris Spicer joined the agency in 1999 and both father and son proudly carry on the Spicer tradition today. The Spicer agency has been a family owned operated insurance agency since its founding. From humble beginnings the Spicer agency has grown to one of largest insurance agencies in the state of Virginia. The Spicer agency has a great staff of licensed, experienced, customer-focused agents are ready to assist you. The Spicer agency is licensed to do business in Virginia, Maryland, DC, West Virginia North Carolina. Conveniently located just across the street from Fair Oaks Mall the Spicer Agency is open Monday-Friday, from 8am-5pm, and evenings by appointment. Please don’t hesitate to contact us!
