Tom O’Brien Founder CEO Tom is an entrepreneur at heart, passionate about solving problems and building businesses that make a difference. He has launched and grown several ventures, but his greatest passion is Oxford Lawn. Tom founded Oxford Lawn in 2008 as a lawn mowing service built on reliability and quality. In 2021, recognizing the growing need for healthier, more sustainable landscapes, he expanded into lawn remediation — focusing on compost topdressing, core aeration, dethatching, and soil rebuilding. By 2023, he sold the mowing side of the business to dedicate himself fully to lawn restoration and the development of Oxford Lawn’s proprietary, soil-driven approach. Under his leadership, Oxford Lawn has become a pioneer in sustainable turf care — proven systems that restore lawns, reduce water use, and improve soil health. As the company expands through franchising, Tom’s vision is to empower other entrepreneurs to build thriving businesses while transforming how communities care for their landscapes.
