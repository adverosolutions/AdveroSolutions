CPA Firm Tucson, AZ | Accounting Tax Solutions Arizona Who We Are Our Story Over 50 years ago, founder Harvey Jansen began practicing in Tucson with one goal: to grow lasting relationships through honest, reliable accounting. From those roots in the mid-1970s, Jansen Company CPAs has grown into a multi-generational firm that still values care, trust and personal connection. Mikala Jansen’s story is part of that growth. She joined Jansen Company CPAs in 2006 at the front desk. Driven by curiosity and a love for learning, she worked her way into accounting, earned her CPA license, and eventually purchased the firm. Today, our close-knit team blends technical tax accounting expertise with a personal touch, guiding clients (and their taxes) through every season of life.
