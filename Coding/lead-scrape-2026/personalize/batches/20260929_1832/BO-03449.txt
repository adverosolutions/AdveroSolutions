Welcome to Feuerhelm Langer, LTD. We are a Certified Public Accounting firm located in Western Wisconsin. Our three offices are located in Ellsworth, River Falls, and Baldwin, WI. Owners and CPA’s, Brent Langer and Kit Feuerhelm, lead a staff of 15, including three additional CPA’s. Our team of dedicated professionals is committed to providing exceptional accounting services tailored to meet your unique needs. Experience: With years of experience in the industry, our CPA’s, accounting staff, and payroll specialists possess the necessary skills and knowledge to guide you through the complexities of personal and business taxes and accounting. Personalized Services: We recognize that every client has distinct needs and ambitions. We take the time to listen, understand, and tailor our services to align with your specific goals. Our team works closely with you to develop strategies that reflect your unique vision and aspirations. Collaborative Partnership: At Feuerhelm Langer, we value open communication. We aim to build a strong partnership with our clients. Our team is available to answer your questions, provide guidance, and help you navigate the tax and accounting world.
