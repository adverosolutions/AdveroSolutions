Who We Are | The Pulice Group About us The Pulice Group is a tax and accounting firm that started in 2012 and is located in Herndon, Virginia. Our goal is to help investors and small businesses succeed by providing financial insight and advice tailored to your goals. We add value by providing customized tax plans to match your circumstances and execute and maintain those plans for you throughout the year. We are results-driven and always act with integrity. That means we do our best to make sure you have the best outcome but always do what’s right and within the law. Ryan D. Pulice, CPA, CTP Managing Member Ryan D. Pulice is a C.P.A. and Certified Tax Planner with over 17 years of tax and accounting experience. He graduated from West Virginia University with a Masters of Professional Accountancy. His expertise is in real estate and small business accounting and tax. Ryan lives in Great Falls, Virginia, with his wife Amanda and daughter Samantha. Matthew Armandi Managing Member Matt brings over 20 years of extensive experience, serving as both a Controller and CFO in diverse sectors such as technology companies, wealth management firms, and Fintech startups. He has a strong foundation in public accounting, notably with KPMG. Today, Matt is a Partner leading the Consulting, Outsourcing, and Advisory practice at the Pulice Group. His qualifications include a CPA designation (inactive) and a BS in Accounting and MIS from West Virginia University.
