You know you need a professional with experience, but the process of handing over your financial statements can be intimidating. And it doesn’t matter how inexpensive or convenient a service is—if there’s no trust in the relationship, you won’t have peace about your financial goals. At Hulsey, Harwood & Sheridan, we view every client relationship like a close partnership and friendship. We like to put it this way: we’re going to prepare your tax return with the same pencil we use to prepare our own. We take our service to you personally, and when our clients are happy, we’re happy. Our clients know they can count on us for anything they need, and we’re committed to going above and beyond to bring you peace of mind when it comes to your tax and accounting needs. We want to be the first people you call when you have a question about your finances, and we promise to answer when you do. We're more than just a firm that puts numbers in boxes - we think creatively to help you identify the best ways to handle your accounting needs. We’re dedicated to learning, and we’ve spent over forty years honing our craft. No matter what complex questions you have, we’re confident that one of our highly experienced team members will be able to help you find the answer. You’ll never hear us say, “We can’t help you with that.” It takes a lot of time and commitment to provide this holistic service for our clients, but we know it’s’ truly in your best interest—and to us, that makes it worth it.
