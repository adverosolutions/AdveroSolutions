Ben Anderson founded the firm in December of 2015. Ben is a licensed Certified Public Accountant in Washington State. He started Olympia CPA, PLLC to provide high quality tax, accounting, and payroll services to businesses and their owners. Before starting his own practice, he worked for some of the best boutique CPA firms in the region. His accounting work experience includes private industry, government, academia, and public accounting. In 2015, Ben was awarded the Washington Society of CPAs’ New Professionals Award for passion and commitment to the community and the local Olympia chapter. He is the former chair of the WSCPA Olympia Chapter. Additionally, Ben is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and the Washington Society of CPAs. He lives in Olympia Washington with his wife and daughter. Who we are Olympia CPA, PLLC is a team of professionals dedicated to providing the best service possible.
