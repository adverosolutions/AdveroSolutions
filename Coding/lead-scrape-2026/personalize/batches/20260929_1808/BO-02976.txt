About Samira Rahimzadeh, CPA - Diamond Accounting Hub About Home About ABOUT US Not Your Typical Bookkeeper Meet Samira Rahimzadeh, CPA Samira Rahimzadeh, CPA, is the founder and owner of Diamond Accounting Hub, located in Overland Park, KS . She provides a range of services including accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, QuickBooks cleanup, outsourced controller services, and consulting for small and mid-sized businesses. As a CPA bookkeeper, Samira leverages her problem-solving skills and passion for numbers to ensure her clients financial statements are as clean and shiny as a diamond and audit-ready. Samira works with businesses across various industries, including dental offices , law firms , professional services firms , home service businesses , daycare and pre-school centers , fund and investment companies , and home service businesses . Before establishing her own firm, Samira gained extensive experience at CPA firms, where she provided outsourced controllership services, external audit, financial statement reporting, general ledger accounting and control, bookkeeping, payroll, and consulting for clients. She holds a bachelor s degree in accounting and is a CPA in both Canada and the US. Outside of work, Samira enjoys spending time with her family and cooking.
