I m an independent accountant in Washington, MO serving clients in the surrounding area. I have a passion for small business and I love helping liberate business owners to focus on the goals and dreams they have for their companies. After working in an accounting firm for almost five years, I decided to start my own independent accounting service to be closer to home and do what I m passionate about. With that experience, I know what a business needs to stay in step with the IRS. But I found I enjoyed helping businesses thrive more than simply helping them survive a tax filing. I always try to be more than just a bookkeeper for my clients. I enjoy coming alongside to learn the details of a business and figuring out how to streamline and help the business be more efficient. I ve done everything from construction loan draws and dealing with title companies to designing and producing a direct mailing. I ve worked extensively in QuickBooks Desktop as well as being a QuickBooks Online Pro Advisor. I ve had a lot of experience converting companies to the QuickBooks platform as well as data conversions between QuickBooks Desktop and Online and vice-versa. I believe that work and business are gifts from God and that we honor Him by being the best at what He has called us to do. I would love to talk to you about how we can, together, make your accounting system hum . Why "Excelsior"? Excelsior (ec-SELL-see-or) is a Latin word defined as higher, ever upward. It s an adjective. And one that I hope always describes not only my accounting services, but my whole life. As a follower of Jesus Christ, I am called to an excelsior life that reflects His own life. It is my goal and purpose to provide my clients with services that are ever increasing in excellence, efficiency and accuracy. So my business is named by my purpose. (Plus it sounds cool!)
