Home | ABD Tax Accounting LLC ABD Tax Accounting LLC is a CPA firm in Boulder, Colorado specializing in tax preparation and tax planning for individuals and small businesses. Our Story Our owner and head CPA once pursued a PhD in accounting and left her program with all but her dissertation (ABD) complete. As a nod to her unfinished degree our firm is named ABD Tax Accounting LLC. While a PhD didn’t work for her, you can let her extensive accounting knowledge work for you in all your tax-based needs. Contact us today for a free initial consultation to see if we can help. Services Tax Return Preparation We prepare a wide range of both federal and state tax returns for individuals and small businesses. Tax Planning No one likes surprises when it comes to tax. We can help you plan the tax side of all your upcoming financial changes from selling your home to planning for retirement. Audit and IRS Representation From responding to tax notices to being audited by the IRS or state Department of Revenue, we can help you through your contact with various tax authorities.
