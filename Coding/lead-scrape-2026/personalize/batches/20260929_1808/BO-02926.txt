OUR TEAM Meet Our Specialist We Have a Team of Certified, Experienced and Trustworthy Financial Professionals Mary Mullendore A graduate of the University of Illinois – Champaign-Urbana from which she obtained a Finance degree with Honors. She also holds an MBA with a concentration in Finance from the University of Wisconsin – Madison. Mary is licensed as a Certified Public Accountant in Missouri and has also been awarded Certified Management Accountant Credential. She is currently PTIN/EFIN registered with Internal Revenue Service and has been a practicing CPA in the Greater St. Louis area since August 2006. In February 2012, she opened her own CPA office in Kirkwood. Previously, she held various accounting and finance positions, including Securities Analyst for Texas State Securities Board and Divisional Financial Analyst and General Accountant for a local manufacturer. Mary served 5 years as controller for a national non-profit organization. Mary has multiple years of tax and small business experience as a CPA with a small firm in southern Illinois Mary is continually committed to various philanthropic efforts and has served such organizations generously over the years. She served as Treasurer for the Kirkwood School District Foundation. She has been strongly involved in Sign of the Arrow, a St. Louis area philanthropic needlepoint and giftshop for over 20 years. She served seven years on the Board of Directors including five years as President and two years as Chairman. Mary is a member of the P.E.O. Sisterhood, an organization committed to advancing women in the area through their educational goals. She served on the Executive Board of the St. Louis Reciprocity Chapter for two years as well as President and Treasurer for her local chapter. Mary, originally from Wisconsin, is a resident of Kirkwood, Missouri where she raised her three children with her husband and their Labrador Retrievers. She enjoys spending weekends with her family on property near Mark Twain Lake.
