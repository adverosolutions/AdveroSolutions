PNW Financial Concierge Tax Accounting Services in Southwest Washington and Beyond Welcome! PNW Financial Concierge was founded in 2017 by Amanda Hansen and Cherri Peterson by a strong desire to offer exceptional tax and accounting services to greater Southwest Washington and beyond. We are a small tax and accounting firm and proud in our ability to provide personalized service to our clients. With over four decades of combined experience, our partners’ extensive history working with small and medium-sized businesseses means you’re in good hands. We have background working with other sectors of tax preparation and planning with an emphasis on moderately complex individual returns, real estate investment development, construction contractors , and others. Clients are strongly encouraged to stay active in our relationship by consulting us prior to making significant financial decisions . Secure - Virtual - Paperless The accounting and tax industry is changing at rapid pace and PNW Financial Concierge is moving with it! While our partners and assistants are local to Southwest Washington, our firm is virtual and paperless, which enables us to serve clients coast to coast. Current clients will enjoy access to an online hub of direct messaging with firm members, electronic document signatures, and tax document storage through our secure portal . In the interest of information safety and efficiency, all communication about tax, accounting, or other work is centralized in the portal. About Us PNW Financial Concierge About Us Who We Are Amanda Hansen, EA Born and raised in Thousand Oaks, CA, Amanda worked internally in the accounting and estimating departments of prestigious construction and real estate firms throughout her early career. These experiences fueled an interest in the investment and development segments of the industry. Following several more years of internal experience in managerial roles, she added tax preparation and planning to her skillset. After moving to Washington State in 2016, she co-founded PNW Financial Concierge with Cherri the following year. Assisting real estate investors and small businesses is a particular goal of hers, and she strives to stay up-to-date on the latest opportunities available to the firm’s clients. Cherri Peterson, CPA Cherri’s varied experience in accounting and tax began her career at the former Peat Marwick (now KPMG). Her subsequent work in internal audit departments of large corporations then led to a decade of partnerships at some of the premier CPA firms in Los Angeles. Later, she took on the role of CFO for a boutique property firm with investments and entities across multiple disciplines. When Amanda approached her about co-founding PNW Financial Concierge, she accepted happily knowing the value her experiences would bring to the local community. Cherri takes great pleasure in assisting local businesses and empowering them to grow. Client Portal Next Steps
