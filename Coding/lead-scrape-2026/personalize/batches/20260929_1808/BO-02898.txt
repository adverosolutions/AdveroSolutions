About Dunham Bookkeeping Services | Meet Sara Dunham Dunham Bookkeeping Services 0 Meet Sara – Bookkeeper, cleanup problem-solver, and the person who actually reads the details. I’m Sara Dunham, owner of Dunham Bookkeeping Services in Merriam, Kansas. I help therapy practices, small law firms, nonprofits, and service-based small businesses clean up messy books, stay organized month to month, and understand what their numbers are actually saying. I started DBS in 2020 after more than 20 years of experience in accounting, business operations, and human resources. That mix matters because bookkeeping is rarely just data entry. It’s systems, communication, deadlines, workflows, payroll questions, tax readiness, and helping business owners make sense of the moving pieces. My goal is simple: give you clean, accurate financial information without making you feel judged, overwhelmed, or talked down to. If your books are behind, messy, confusing, or just not useful right now, that doesn’t scare me. That’s the work. Who DBS is best for DBS is a strong fit for business owners who want clear communication, secure document sharing, accurate monthly bookkeeping, and honest answers. I work especially well with therapy practices, small law firms, nonprofits, and service-based businesses that need organized financials but don’t want to live inside QuickBooks. DBS may not be the right fit if you want last-minute miracles, unclear records, emailed tax documents, or unlimited support without a clear scope. I’ll always be kind, but I’ll also be honest about what can realistically be done. Tools I use: I’m comfortable working inside the systems small businesses actually use, including QuickBooks, SimplePractice, Bill.com, Gusto, Google Workspace, Microsoft Office, Asana, Calendly, and other day-to-day tools. The goal is not to make your tech stack fancier. The goal is to make your financial workflow clearer. Credentials Certifications I stay current on the tools, rules, and workflows my clients rely on, especially QuickBooks. That means ongoing training, testing, and recertification through Intuit, plus continuing education in bookkeeping, payroll, small business tax, and client data security.
