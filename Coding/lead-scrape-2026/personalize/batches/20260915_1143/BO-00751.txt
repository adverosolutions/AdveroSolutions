Tailored Accounting Solutions for Small Businesses | First Hand Accounting Solutions ABOUT US At FHAS, we believe that every business deserves personalized, transparent, and reliable financial guidance. As a full-service accounting and advisory firm, we specialize in offering customized solutions that help businesses of all sizes navigate their financial journeys with confidence. FHAS was created with a simple mission: to provide hands-on, strategic support that drives real results. We understand that no two businesses are alike, which is why we take the time to truly understand your unique needs and goals, offering solutions that are as individual as your business. Our services range from day-to-day bookkeeping and financial reporting to high-level CFO and advisory services. Whether you're a startup looking for guidance or an established company in need of expert financial oversight, FHAS is here to help you grow, thrive, and make informed decisions with clarity and peace of mind. With a commitment to integrity, excellence, and client-focused service, FHAS is your trusted partner in building a strong financial foundation for your business’s future. MEET OUR FOUNDER Kristen Hand, CPA is the founder of FHAS, bringing over 15 years of experience in accounting to her role as a trusted advisor for businesses of all sizes. Kristen’s educational foundation includes a Master of Science in Accounting from the University of North Carolina at Greensboro and a Bachelor of Business Administration in Accounting (Cum Laude) from Stetson University. Over the course of her career, she has gained experience working with small and mid-size businesses, providing everything from day-to-day bookkeeping to high-level CFO advisory services. Her work with multifamily housing and non-profit organizations has allowed her to navigate unique financial structures and offer tailored solutions that drive both operational efficiency and financial success. Outside of her professional life, Kristen and her husband are the proud parents of a 10 year old daughter. Kristen’s passion for animal rescue is a big part of her personal life—she is an active foster for Red Dog Farm. She and her family share their home with a variety of animals, reflecting her love for animals and her commitment to the rescue community.
