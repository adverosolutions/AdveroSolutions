We provide manufacturing talent solutions that actually solve problems—not create new ones. Since 1980, Ron Sunshine Associates has helped companies in automotive, industrial equipment, appliances, and heavy construction find the right people for critical roles. Our process is built on trust, real industry knowledge, and straightforward communication. Offering retained, contract, and contingency search options, Ron Sunshine Associates provides agile solutions to all of today’s hiring challenges. We specialize in identifying and recruiting middle and upper management, as well as individual contributors, across production, human resources, supply chain, quality, maintenance and facilities, plant controllers, and continuous improvement. We don’t chase every job—we take on the ones we’re confident we can fill. What you get is a search partner who knows the space and stands behind every recommendation. Born in New York City and attended of the University of Oklahoma, Ron began his recruiting career in St. Louis, quickly gaining recognition for his ability to connect top-tier talent with leading companies in the manufacturing sector. With over four decades of experience, Ron has earned national credibility in the executive recruiting field. He served on the board of the Illinois Association of Personnel Consultants and most recently on the board the Executive Search Owners Association of Dallas-Fort Worth.
