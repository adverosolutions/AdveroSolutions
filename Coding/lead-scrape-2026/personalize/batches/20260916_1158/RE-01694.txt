Founded in 1984, Gillespie Search is a highly specialized recruiting firm concentrating our search efforts exclusively in the medical/surgical device segment of the healthcare industry. We are committed to delivering high-quality recruiting services by establishing long term client relationships and maintaining a high profile executive portfolio. Our firm has grown into one of the premier healthcare recruiting organizations nationwide among our corporate clients and candidates. Our Mission Our mission is to provide our clients and candidates with the best possible recruiting services that reflect our organization's three major hallmarks: integrity, versatility, and industry knowledge. Effective Staffing Solutions We specialize in offering superior executive teams for our clients, and we work on both contingency and retainer bases. Our services include all the market research and networking necessary to identify talented executives. Search assignments are conducted on all functional levels within a company’s organization including sales, sales management, marketing management, manufacturing, engineering, clinical/regulatory affairs, and the C-Suite. Our clients are involved in many medical/surgical areas of expertise, including aesthetic surgery, biologics, cardiology, gynecology, neurology, oncology, orthopedics, spinal surgery, and urology. Our Clients We are proud to recognize the increased growth and prosperity of our clients because we provided the best executive talent available. It’s exciting to be part of that growth process and even more exciting to earn the trust and respect of industry leaders within the Healthcare industry. Our clients range in size from venture capital-backed start-up firms to Fortune 500 companies involved in the healthcare industry's medical/surgical device segment. These companies manufacture and distribute products in every medical specialty from capital equipment to medical devices, instruments and disposables. Search assignments are conducted on a national basis as well as internationally upon request. Please contact us at our website or call us directly with any questions regarding our services and your executive recruiting requirements or your confidential job search.
