About Us | Top-Notch Cleaning, LLC. Our History Ohio Valley: 740-312-9090 Top-Notch Cleaning is a business and residential cleaning company serving the Ohio Valley . It opened in February of 2013 in St. Clairsville, OH. Due to our growth Top-Notch Cleaning moved to the Bridgeport Shopping Plaza in Bridgeport, OH in August of 2014. Then in June of 2015 we finally moved into a home of our own at 887 National Road in Bridgeport, OH. After numerous requests for us to open an office in Cambridge, OH we opened our second office in the spring of 2018. This location will clean Cambridge and Zanesville areas. Then in the summer of 2019 Top-Notch Cleaning purchased a location at 5711 Fairdale Drive in Cambridge OH. We were heartbroken to close the Cambridge location in February of 2024. Top-Notch Cleaning is locally owned and operated by Austin Leanne Straub. With both of us working full-time, two very active boys (at the time ages 2 5) and a jack russell it quickly became apparent to us that we needed help with our homes upkeep. We had grown tired of arguing over who last cleaned the bathroom, dusted and vacuumed. It is at this time we began to look into our local cleaning options… needless to say we weren’t impressed.
