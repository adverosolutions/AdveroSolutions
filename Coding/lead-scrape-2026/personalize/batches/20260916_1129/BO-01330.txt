Rates Blog About Testimonials Contact (831) 316-1106 Contact Us Skip to main content About Us We’ve built a long-lasting reputation with some of the best people in the business. Candace Ebert & Co. is centrally located in Santa Cruz where we service local businesses in the bay area from San Francisco to Big Sur, CA. At Candace Ebert & Co. we offer a wide variety of services including bookkeeping solutions, professional accounting services, tax preparation and planning, business consulting, QuickBooks set-up and training, remote accounting, HR consulting and payroll. Our years of real-world experience, education, well-knowledged and ambitious staff set us apart from the competition. Candace Ebert & Co. is geared towards assisting small to midsized companies with their bookkeeping and accounting needs. Our primary objective is to help business owners reduce their time performing accounting tasks and focus on what they do best. Using our services, our clients save on average 45% compared to the cost of an in-house accounting staff. Through our services, we provide timely, cost effective and user-friendly solutions for businesses today. Here at Candace Ebert & Co. we stay on top of the latest technology in accounting software and bookkeeping solutions so that we can provide the highest level of service to our clients including iPad setup and remote connection into accounting systems, use of online QuickBooks, Swizznet, Dropbox and personalized client logins.
