About Hagman Search | Brake Industry Recruiter Skip to content The Brake Industry s Executive Search Firm. We are a retained boutique dedicated exclusively to the global automotive and commercial vehicle brake sectors — OE and aftermarket, friction and hardware, hydraulic and electronic. It s not a practice area. It s our entire business. That focus is the whole point. When clients hire us, they re not getting a generalist firm with a brake vertical. They re getting a partner who works inside this industry every day — who knows the technical language, the competitive dynamics, the people who move between companies, and the people who don t. As a retained partner, we work on an exclusive basis, which lets us commit our full attention to the search rather than racing other firms for the same shortlist. The result is a deeper market sweep, a tighter shortlist, and a process built around precision instead of speed. We ve placed leaders across every major segment of this industry: OE truck manufacturers, EV OEMs, motorcycle and powersports OEMs, global Tier 1 friction suppliers, hardware and electronics specialists, and aftermarket distributors on three continents. The technical span runs from foundation brakes and friction formulation to ABS, ESC, regenerative braking, and brake-by-wire. The companies that hire us tend to share one thing in common: the role is too important to get wrong.
