The Solution Service - Commercial Janitorial Company Welcome to The Solution Service Established in 2002, The Solution Service is the Triangle’s premier commercial cleaning and janitorial service. We are a family owned business with over 30 years experience in the industry, bringing expertise and detailed attention to every job we do. The Solution Service works primarily with dental clinics, churches, office spaces, corporate settings, labs, and schools. We specialize in commercial cleaning, custodial and janitorial work, shampooing, power washing, stripping and waxing floors, and steam cleaning. We also perform move-out clean-up.
