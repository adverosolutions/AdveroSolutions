Our Team Ghirardo CPA Tax Preparation and Business Accounting Services for Marin, Sonoma, Napa, the San Francisco Bay Area Our Team About Us - Over 30 Years of Accounting and Tax Consulting in Marin, Sonoma and the San Francisco Bay Area For more than 30 years, Ghirardo CPA has played an integral role in the lives of thousands of North Bay individuals and businesses. In 1990, Novato natives Jerry and Steve Ghirardo bought the CPA practice established by their father, Edward T. Ghirardo. Today we are one of the premier accounting , tax preparation and business consulting firms in Marin County. One of the fundamental reasons for our success is the strength of our team. Our team members draw on their expertise, energy, creativity and enthusiasm to not only meet client expectations, but to exceed them. Integrity, a commitment to continuing technical expertise and an uncompromising approach to maintaining personal relationships with our clients is the foundation of our ethic, in both business and the community.
