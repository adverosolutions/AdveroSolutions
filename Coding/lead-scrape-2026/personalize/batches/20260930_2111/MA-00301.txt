About - BC/DC Ideas Skip to content Where Creativity Meets Kindness and passion BC/DC Ideas is a nonprofit communications agency partnering with organizations to make the world a better place. We Take Sides. As champions of justice, equity and fairness, we have dedicated our entire lives to partnering with nonprofit organizations to positively impact our communities, states, and country. Whether it s for intense and short-term projects or long-term support, our unwavering commitment is that we ll always stand by your side. Founded in 2010, our nonprofit communications agency creates actionable communications and marketing that deliver results. Our team, with their solid foundation in communications experience, is passionate about developing holistic campaigns, brands, and websites that make a difference. get to know us Our Team Dawn Crawford Founder & Principal About Dawn > The engine behind BC/DC Ideas, Dawn has dedicated her career to good. Dawn brings her considerable experience and expertise to helping elevate the nonprofit sector. Our team’s lead strategist, Dawn is often seen leading our IdeaStorms, penning communications plans, or training clients. Before launching BC/DC Ideas in 2010, she earned her chops in 10+ years of communications leadership roles for public health , healthcare and youth-focused nonprofits. Working for nonprofits is Dawn’s dream job, and she loves that her 40+ hours a week make the world a better place. Things that make her happy: A glass of champagne to celebrate big wins, Basecamp, living in the South, seeing the world, and a well-formatted spreadsheet.
