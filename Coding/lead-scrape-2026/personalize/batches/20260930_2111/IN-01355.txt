Premier Benefits Plans, Inc Premier Benefit Plans, Inc . is a full service insurance broker and consulting firm located in Melville, Long Island, New York. For almost thirty years, we have served clients delivering cost effective insurance products combining exceptional service with creative plan design. At Premier, we are committed to maintaining strong and stable relationships by delivering on our promise of daily accountability. We welcome your inquiries and look forward to the opportunity to serve you. Premier Benefit Plans, Inc. was founded by Thomas DiBartolomeo in 1995. His daughter Aimee DiBartolomeo-Cody joined him in 1998 and assumed the office of President and sole owner after her father's untimely death in 2005. Presently, Premier employs a staff of 20 professionals including 15 dedicated to Employee Benefits. Ms. DiBartolomeo-Cody has almost 30 years experience in the Employee Benefits industry and has expertise in all facets of the Company including sales, marketing, client relationships, carrier relationships and negotiation, plan design and creation, claims administration and resolution, and organization philosophy and policy. Prior to joining her father at Premier, Aimee held management positions in both sales and risk assessment with Mid Atlantic Medical Services, Rockville Md. Ms. DiBartolomeo-Cody earned an M.A. in International Business from American University and a B.A. in History from Gettysburg College. Aimee is a Member of the Board of Directors of the American Red Cross and is a featured member of the National Association for Professional Women.
