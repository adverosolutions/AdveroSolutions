Let s Talk Our Company Hudson Software ® began in 1991 with a simple idea from our visionary founder, Glenn Polin: to help publishers embrace a new frontier in educational technology. What started small quickly grew into the expertise that led to our incorporation in 1993 as Hudson Software Corporation. Today, we’re a leading provider of outsourced services for the EdTech industry and beyond. For more than 30 years, we’ve been a trusted partner to software developers, delivering technical support, quality assurance testing, and customer success services. Backed by a seasoned team deeply rooted in computer technology, our agents solve complex issues with skill and insight—avoiding scripted responses in favor of real, effective solutions. Our long-standing client relationships—many spanning a decade or more—reflect our unwavering commitment to exceeding expectations. We believe in partnerships that elevate your development journey and drive lasting success. Hudson Software is a 100% U.S.-owned and operated company, fully incorporated and wholly located in the United States.
