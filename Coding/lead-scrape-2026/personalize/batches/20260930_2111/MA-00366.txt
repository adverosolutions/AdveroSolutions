About - Black Wednesday Skip to content What is a Black Wednesday? Black Wednesday specializes in showcasing the human element of brands. Through our work, we are always focused on brand personification, and making real connections with the community. We custom build marketing strategies that are tailored to fit our clients’ needs. At BW, we believe that by providing opportunities for human connection, marketing can be both truly impactful and (dare we say it) fun. And we aren’t just talk: because as a company we wholeheartedly believe in connecting our clients to the larger conversations in their community. With us as your guide, you can confidently build your member network and grow alongside other dynamic and honest brands who have found success through our services. Whether it’s social media, PR, or brand services, if you care about it, we want to help you amplify your impact. Meet Corri With a background in marketing and business, I started BW to help brands connect with their audiences in personal, meaningful and grassroots ways. I’m also out to break the mold of what a “marketing agency” is, looks and feels like. From my experience freelancing with agencies and collaborating with other agencies through various projects, I have learned so much about how we as Black Wednesday can lead, challenge, and inspire as a truly stellar client partner. Black Wednesday started as Corri. But now, it’s so much more than that. BW is an incredible team that brings creative visions and storytelling to life for our clients everyday. It’s a collective passion for bringing meaningful strategies to life. It’s a better vision for how companies and brands can engage the lives of their community members. It’s… magic. The beauty of magic is that everyone can make it.
