Brown Julie Johnson Adrian Summerour See all employees Updates Valley West Insurance reposted this Vatche Saatdjian 1mo Report this post Almost every borrower has heard there is a three day wait before signing. Almost none of them know what actually restarts it, and that gap causes real panic in the last week. The rule itself is simple. Your Closing Disclosure has to be in your hands three business days before consummation. That is federal, it is 12 CFR 1026.19(f), and nobody waives it for convenience. What people get wrong is the next part. They assume any change to the file resets the clock and pushes their closing. It does not. Only three things restart it. The disclosed cost of credit turning out inaccurate beyond the tolerance the rule allows. A change in the loan product itself, fixed becoming adjustable being the clearest case. Or a prepayment penalty added where there was none. That is the whole list. Everything else gets a corrected disclosure handed to you at or before signing, and your date holds. A fee moving, a seller credit changing, your cash to close landing on a different number, a misspelled name. Two things worth writing down: Acknowledge the disclosure the moment it lands.
