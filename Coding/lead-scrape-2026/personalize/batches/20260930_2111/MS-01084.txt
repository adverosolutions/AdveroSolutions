Alyssa Fiscus, Owner Operator About the Owner Helping businesses liase between the vastly different worlds of IT and Construction to ensure a seamless workplace build-out What we do... We take the confusion out of IT-related requirements when it comes to building out a new workplace; whether that be for a small business ready to open a local storefront, an entrepreneur who would like to purchase a franchise, or a company that is expanding current operations! We also have a great deal of experience managing rollouts for corporations in need of updated infrastructure to hundreds of locations nationwide. Why we do it... With the rate at which technology changes, even the most experienced general contractors may not be able to provide the best information when it comes to IT-related facilities, infrastructure, hardware, and utilities. They sometimes rely on third-party vendors to guide purchasing decisions and installation requirements, which could mean the business owner isn t aware of all the options. That s where we come in... there s often a more efficient, cost-effective, and quality way to complete IT Facilities work that might be lost in communication during the planning and build-out process.
