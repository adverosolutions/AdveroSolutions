In 2013, three firms were brought together under the ownership of Tom Mathison and Eric Schwenk to form Community Insurance Services, now Community Insurance Solutions, Inc. In March 2025 the agency entered its next chapter when Deborah Groff purchased the firm and reaffirmed its community-first focus. We were founded on a simple belief: people deserve personal attention, not one-size-fits-all policies or impersonal call centers.
