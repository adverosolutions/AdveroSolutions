Wathen and now led by second-generation agency owner Susan Wathen, Countryside is celebrating 50 years as an independent, family-owned insurance agency in New Albany, Indiana. Because we represent 30+ insurance carriers, our clients are not limited to one company or one type of policy. We help individuals, families and business owners compare coverage options and make informed decisions based on protection, value and their individual needs—not price alone. Our agency offers: • Auto, home, renters, condo and rental-property insurance • Personal umbrella, flood, motorcycle, boat and recreational-vehicle coverage • Business owners policies, general liability, commercial auto, workers’ compensation, cyber liability and other commercial insurance • Life insurance and additional protection for families and business owners Insurance can be confusing, especially when rates, underwriting requirements and coverage options are changing. Our role is to explain the differences clearly, identify potential gaps and help clients understand what they are purchasing before a loss occurs. We also believe service matters after the policy is issued. Our local team assists with policy changes, billing questions, coverage reviews and claims guidance. When possible, we encourage clients to call us before filing a claim so we can help them understand the process and their options.
