Bourke Accounting, with offices located in Old Louisville and New Albany, takes a friendly, low-pressure approach to personal and business financial services, combining the resources of a full-service firm with the personal attention of a local team. With more than 150 years of combined experience in tax preparation, bookkeeping and business consulting, our professionals develop practical accounting solutions tailored to each client’s individual needs, goals and stage of growth. We serve individuals, nonprofit organizations, entrepreneurs and businesses across a wide range of industries, offering year-round assistance with personal and business tax preparation and planning, bookkeeping, payroll, sales tax, financial statements, financial analysis, IRS representation, business formation and entity selection, accounting software implementation, retirement planning and management advisory services. Our team can support a business from its earliest stages and continue providing guidance as it grows, restructures, expands or changes ownership.
