Dworkin Insurance is a family owned and operated insurance agency, specializing in health care insurance and travel insurance for visitor to the U.S and U.S. citizens working and traveling abroad. Current Generation James and Stefanie Dworkin are growing the business their parents established in Palo Alto. James enjoys open-water swimming, bike rides and is an amateur triathlete, with over 300 career racing events. Stefanie splits her time between the two coasts: Palo Alto, CA and Brooklyn, NY. While always available to manage and facilitate business for Dworkin Insurance, she also has a career as a documentary filmmaker, photographer and educator. Both Stefanie and James are avid dog owners and love traveling the world. Barbara and Earl Dworkin came to California in the late 1970 s from Cleveland, Ohio and established Dworkin Insurance Services in downtown Palo Alto.
