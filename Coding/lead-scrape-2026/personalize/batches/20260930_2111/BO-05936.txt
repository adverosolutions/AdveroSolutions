Welcome Butler, Lavanceau Sober, LLC is a certified public accounting firm centrally located in Columbia, Maryland. Our seasoned accountants have over 200 years of combined expertise and are ready to meet your individual and business accounting, tax, and consulting needs. We strive to build and maintain long-lasting business relationships by providing top quality compliance with the complex rules of the tax code, as well as personalized, expert advice and planning. Our team is dedicated to quality customer service and is available evenings and weekends, in addition to normal business hours, as they serve a variety of businesses and individuals in the mid-Atlantic region. We utilize the latest in computer technology, including scanning of all tax returns and documents and electronic filing of all individual income tax returns. We provide a secure, encrypted web portal for all of our clients. With this in mind, the protection of your private data is a high priority for us. We look forward to hearing from you! Butler, Lavanceau Sober, LLC Partners Colette Lavanceau, CPA Co-Founder, Partner Colette Lavanceau, CPA Co-Founder Maryland CPA License Number: 13557 Contact: info@BLScpafirm.com 410-997-9299 Ext 112 About: Colette graduated from Towson State University and became a certified public accountant in 1987. In 1993, she started her own practice and, in 1997, co-founded the firm with Ronald Butler. In her over 35 years of practice, Colette has served clients in many industries, including contractors, physician practices, commercial real estate, retailers, and Montessori schools, to name a few. She enjoys working with small businesses from inception through their growth cycle. Her personal expertise encompasses tax planning and tax preparation services to businesses. In addition, she provides individual tax return planning and preparation. Colette is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants, the Maryland Association of Certified Public Accountants, the Maryland Society of Accountants, and the National Society of Tax Professionals. Colette lives in Howard County, MD and enjoys frequent trips to see her daughter now attending NC State University. Evan Sober, CPA Partner Evan Sober, CPA Partner Maryland CPA License Number: 22924 Contact: info@BLScpafirm.com 410-997-9299 Ext 116 About: Evan grew up a native Baltimorean; he graduated from Randallstown High School in 1990 and received his accounting degree from Towson State University in 1994. After passing the CPA exam in 1994 (on his first attempt), Evan started his accounting career as a staff accountant at a small local CPA firm. Having worked there for almost 8 years, Evan was promoted to tax manager, vice-president and eventually became a partner in 2001. Evan joined Butler, Lavanceau Sober, LLC in October of 2002 and became a partner in October of 2003. Evan was married in November of 2009 and currently lives in Howard County. Evan has extensive experience helping individuals and small businesses with a wide array of tax and financial planning issues. He has served clients in many industries, including: real estate, medical services, legal services and contracting. Evan is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants, the Maryland Association of Certified Public Accountants and the Maryland Society of Accountants.
