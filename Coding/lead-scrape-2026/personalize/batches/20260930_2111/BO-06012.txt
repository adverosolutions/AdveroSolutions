About Gregory Sechler, CPA, EA | Summit Tax and Accounting About Expertise you can rely on. Service you'll remember. Summit Tax and Accounting brings more than two decades of real-world tax experience to every client, with a focus on results you can measure. Gregory T. Sechler, CPA, EA The expert behind Summit Tax. Gregory Sechler founded Summit Tax and Accounting after more than two decades working in accounting and tax, beginning with accounting roles as a teenager and later building experience across PwC, corporate accounting, and advisory work. He holds a Master of Accountancy and a Bachelor of Science in Accounting, both from the University of Utah's David Eccles School of Business. As both a Certified Public Accountant and an Enrolled Agent, Gregory holds the highest credentials available in the tax profession. His EA designation grants him unlimited representation rights before the IRS, meaning he can advocate on your behalf in any audit, appeal, or collection matter. Summit's client base spans individuals, small businesses, and nonprofits nationwide. Gregory's multi-state experience makes him well suited to serve clients with complex filing situations that cross state lines.
