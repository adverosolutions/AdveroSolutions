Learn About Our Agency | (412) 444-4470 | McGroarty Bradburn Insurance Skip to content About Us McGroarty Bradburn Insurance, LLC was established in 1989 with the merger of McGroarty Insurance Agency, Inc., established in 1926 and the Bradburn Insurance Agency, Inc., established in 1946. In 2004 we opened our second location when we purchased Banas Insurance Agency and in 2010 we moved both offices to our new location on Steubenville Pike in Robinson Township. McGroarty Bradburn Insurance is an independent insurance agency specializing in Property and Casualty Insurance for both individuals and businesses. This includes automobiles, homeowners, automobile, businesses, life, umbrellas, liability, workers compensation and professional liability, etc. We also have agents that sell health insurance. McGroarty Bradburn Insurance is owned and operated by siblings Michael McGroarty, Jr. and Megan McGroarty who purchased the agency from it s found Mike McGroarty Sr. McGroarty Bradburn and its employees are active members of Independent Insurance Agents of America, Professional Insurance Agents, Insurance Agents Brokers, the Insurance Club of Pittsburgh, South West Communities Chamber of Commerce, and Pittsburgh Airport Area Chamber of Commerce to name a few. Our goal is to provide industry leading customers service. We help you by making sure you get the right coverage at the best price.
