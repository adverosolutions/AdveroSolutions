About us Welcome to Richards, a wayfinding and branding agency that has been a family-owned business for over 43 years. What started as an ad agency has evolved into a team of passionate individuals who are dedicated to helping people navigate complex environments with ease. Our experience At Richards, we understand the importance of finding your way. Our years of experience have shown us the true power that signage holds, and we take great pride in helping our clients create effective, memorable signage systems that make a lasting impact. Over the years, we ve had the pleasure of working with many different industries, but our specialization in healthcare has truly set us apart. We believe that everyone deserves the best possible care, and that starts with the simple act of being able to navigate the facility with ease. Our expertise Our team of experts has a wealth of experience and knowledge in all areas of wayfinding and experiential design.
