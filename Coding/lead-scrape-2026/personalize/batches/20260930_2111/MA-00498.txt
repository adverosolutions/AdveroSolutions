Our Story Called to Serve The numbers 8-9-8 represent a philosophy, not just a name. As a child, Jeff Ryznar was asked by his grandfather to choose three numbers. He chose 8-9-8. Years later, he learned his grandfather had been quietly saving lottery winnings to help him start his first business. He shared three principles: 01 God gives us gifts. 02 Those gifts are meant to serve others. 03 If you do it well enough, someone will find value in it. Four days before Jeff’s sixteenth birthday, his grandfather passed away. The business never launched, but the philosophy did. 898 Marketing was built on that foundation: use your gifts responsibly, serve intentionally, and execute well enough that it creates lasting value.
