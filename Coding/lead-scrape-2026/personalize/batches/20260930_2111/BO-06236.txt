He is a member of the South Dakota CPA Society, the Nebraska CPA Society, and the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants. Ron and his wife Vicky enjoy spending time at their lake home on Lake Poinsett and with their two sons, daughters-in-law, and six grandchildren in Chicago and Boston.
