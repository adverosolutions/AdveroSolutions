A Century-Long Legacy Rooted in Ballinger Since 1903 For more than 120 years, Willingham Insurance Agency has been woven into the fabric of Ballinger and surrounding communities. Founded in 1903 to serve local families and businesses, the agency has grown from a small-town provider to a leading independent insurance partner across Texas. Through decades of change, our team has maintained an unwavering commitment to honest advice and local service, giving generations of clients stability in uncertain times. Today, we continue that tradition by offering both personal and commercial coverage options under one roof always with a personal touch. Our Mission Our mission is to deliver steady, personal guidance and reliable protection, helping Texans confidently navigate every stage of life.
