One of the owner’s biggest complaints was timekeeping and payroll. We worked with the existing payroll company to change the timekeeping service to a time clock program that now integrates daily punches with the payroll, resulting in more a more efficient process. The only report the owner needs to review is the error report, which lists missing punches or time errors. When the report is reviewed the payroll is sent. Another service added was a package that allowed the payroll company to print the checks, sign and stuff them, and reconcile the uncashed checks on the account. This eliminated a second checking that was reconciled monthly. These solutions have saved 12 hours a week doing paperwork allowing the owner more time in the store.
