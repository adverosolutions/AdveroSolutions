SCI About Us About Us SCI has been placing technology talent across North America since 1980. What began as a specialist recruitment firm serving the enterprise software market has grown into a multi-practice firm spanning digital health and biopharma, financial technology, AI: artificial intelligence, IT consulting, and cybersecurity and infrastructure software. Today, SCI is organized into five practices, each led by a managing partner with extensive experience in their field. The result is focused, market-specific expertise you'd expect from a boutique firm – backed by the breadth and infrastructure of an established national practice. Our Approach Every engagement begins with a clear understanding of your business: your market position, your growth strategy, and the kind of person who will thrive in your organization. We assess candidates against the specific commercial challenges you're trying to solve – not just the job description. The majority of the talent we place are passive candidates: high-performing leaders and individual contributors who are not actively searching but who are open to the right opportunity. These are the people who make the difference between filling a role and transforming a team.
