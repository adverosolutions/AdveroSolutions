As new parents, they hired a Newborn Care Specialist (“Baby Nurse”) to help them transition to parenthood. They needed support with everything from holding their newborn and burping to establishing routines and assisting with breastfeeding. This challenging time made them appreciate the Newborn Care Specialist (“Baby Nurse”) importance, leading them to wonder why all new parents, if financially able, wouldn’t hire such support. The proverbial light bulb went off for Saul as he recalled the moment Doc Brown envisioned the flux capacitor in “Back to the Future.” He realized, “This is it!” A Newborn Care Specialist (“Baby Nurse”) should be a staple for anyone seeking the best newborn training and support, from breastfeeding assistance to creating schedules and guiding new parents through the first few months.” In that moment, the concept for what would become Absolute Best Care was born. Saul was extremely excited about the opportunity to outsource Newborn Care Specialists (“Baby Nurse”) and shared his idea with his former Wall Street colleague and friend, Doug Kozinn. As they conducted market research, they found the Newborn Care Specialist (“Baby Nurse”) and nanny industry to be fragmented, filled with mediocre and disorganized mom-and-pop agencies. Recognizing the importance of these hires, they concluded that a systematic approach was necessary. They aimed to build an agency with highly qualified caregivers and exceptional customer service. Their strategy proved successful, leading to Absolute Best Care becoming a national leader, nominated as the Zagat award winner for small business of the year, and consistently winning awards year after year. The Growth of Absolute Best Care Saul’s wife, Monique , who had a lengthy career in corporate training and client relations, focused on developing a plan for quality control and managing day-to-day challenges in their people-oriented business.
