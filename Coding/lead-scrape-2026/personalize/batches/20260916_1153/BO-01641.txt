About Martin Associates Normal 0 false false false EN-US JA X-NONE A Little About Us A small team with big experience. Martin Associates has been helping Los Angeles and Orange Country keep their financials in order since 1981. While our team has grown and changed over the years, we have chosen to remain small so that we can continue to give each and every customer our undivided attention. We also understand the complexities of our ever-changing world and make sure that our entire team is always up-to-date on the latest tax laws. Meet Martin Associates Martin Menichiello Partner Martin, our founding partner, has been practicing as a tax preparer for over 35 years. He has extensive knowledge in individual, corporate, partnership, estate and trust taxation. Martin formed his first accounting practice back in 1982. He then branched off from his previous partner and has been working since throughout LA and Orange county. He has grown the practice from a few clients into the 1,000+ client book he has today. When he isn’t preparing taxes, Marty loves spending time with his family and friends. He is also quite the connoisseur and collector of wine. Anthony Menichiello PARTNER Anthony is our company’s newest partner. He has been practicing as a tax preparer for close to 10 years and is also an Enrolled Agent with the IRS. He has extensive knowledge in individual, corporate, partnership, estate and trust taxation. Anthony graduated from the University of the Pacific in 2007 with a bachelors in Accounting. He then went to work for a local tax and accounting practice in Cypress, CA, Murphy, Murphy Murphy. After acquiring some experience outside of the family business, Anthony joined up with his father in 2010 and has been working here ever since. When he isn’t preparing taxes, Anthony can’t get enough fantasy football, baseball, or any fantasy sport for that matter. He also enjoys a round of golf from time to time.
