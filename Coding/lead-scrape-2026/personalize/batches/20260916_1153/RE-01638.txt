Professional Management in Motion LLC Professional PASSION EXPERIENCE DILIGENCE We're Not Like Other Recruiters Our thinking, our focus, our processes are always focused on creating long term relationships. We want to connect candidates with companies at which they can build their professional futures and we want companies to continue to work with us because we recruit outstanding employees. To increase our impact, sometimes we work with other trusted, likeminded recruiters. We have found these partnerships to be very beneficial to our candidates, enhancing the reach of our network. Our recruiting process is based on trust, transparency, confidentiality and above all, respect. We fully recognize that our work has the potential to touch and transform people’s lives in profound ways. Unlike other recruiters, who often use mass emailings, impersonal search methods or online job boards, we invest a great amount of time into our searches. We are very thorough and very selective. We take the time to get to know the candidate, assess how his/her experiences align with the position, and describe the company's culture and potential career tracks. At every step of the recruiting process, from interviews to offers, we will be there, shepherding the recruiting process to a win-win outcome. We at Management in Motion recognize what a privileged position we’re in to change people's destinies. Feel free to reach out to us at any time, to learn how we might be able to change yours.
