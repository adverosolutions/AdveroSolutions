About Ray Dauria Associates - Power Industry Recruiters --> A leading power industry executive search and recruiting firm specializing in the electric generation and transmission sectors. Ray Dauria Associates (RDA) is a leading power industry executive search and recruiting firm specializing in the electric generation and transmission sectors. We have been in business since 2000 with an impressive record of placements and personalized customer service. We recruit nationally for most generation and transmission sector job functions. Our placements span the organizational range including executive and director level, middle management, supervisory and professional positions. Our generation focus areas include origination, business development, M A, finance, financial analysis, design and engineering, project management, construction, asset management, energy management, information technology and operations involving wind, solar, energy storage, combined cycle, hydroelectric, geothermal, biomass and nuclear. Our transmission practice focus areas include functions associated with the development, planning, engineering, construction, startup and operation of high voltage transmission lines, substations and subsea cables. See placements for a list of generation and transmission job functions we support. Our clients include independent power producers, transmission and distribution companies, private equity firms and platform companies, investment advisory firms, engineering and construction companies, general contractors, construction / project management and O M services providers, consultants, technology firms and utilities. Our highly qualified principal recruiters are fully engaged in all aspects of the recruiting process including liaison with clients, development of job / compensation specifications, conducting research, screening and interviewing, due diligence, qualifying candidates for short lists, supporting client interviews and facilitating the offer process.
