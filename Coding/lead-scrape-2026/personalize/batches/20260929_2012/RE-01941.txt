About Skip to content Owning Manager About Tony Highland Search Group was founded by Tony Servidio in 2003. Before establishing Highland Search Group Tony attended and graduated from Rutgers University. He then began a career in sales with Enterprise Rent a Car where he was promoted into the management development program. Working alongside talented and influential leaders he developed a passion for helping and influencing others. That experience led him to transition to a recruiting firm specializing in sales placement within the pharmaceutical/Medical industry. Here he began his career in recruiting and progressed from Recruiter Consultant to Senior Account Director. After a successful 5 years there, Tony wanted to continue to bring his passion, dedication and integrity to his clients and build his own recruiting firm founded in these values.
