Nathan Hedrick (CFPS) , Founder and President Born and raised here in the farm lands of central Indiana, Nate grew up operating and repairing farm equipment on the family farm with his brother Matt. Which he quickly developed a mechanical skill set and a passion for the agricultural industry at an early age. While obtaining his bachelors degree in agricultural and biological sciences from Purdue University. Nate started and ran an in-home health care company that is still in operation today. After graduation, Nate attended Parker Hannifin’s accelerated sales training which led to the start of his systems engineering, and later, global account management career for the decade Throughout his time at Parker, Nate worked alongside engineering teams from companies John Deere, CNH, Oshkosh and BAE Systems. He became very familiar with the recruiting industry and, more importantly, the industrial and mobile equipment manufacturing and distribution industries. Regularly being contacted by recruiters and having colleagues who owned their own recruiting companies, Nate felt there was a lack of expertise and people skills with recruiters and recruiting companies in general. Being an entrepreneur at heart, Nate took the plunge and founded PQ Talent in late 2014 with a sole focus on recruiting engineers for the mobile hydraulics industry. Nate is still very much “hands-on” in the recruiting industry cultivating relationships with some of the most talented folks and companies that are out there today. Josh Francis , Partner Indiana born and raised, Josh grew up in central Indiana and is a graduate of Kokomo High School (Class of 2006.) Early on, Josh developed a highly desirable skill set in mechanical ability, being able to build and nurture relationships and network with a vast number of people around him. Growing up, Josh was taught from his father about hot rods, off-roading and motorcycles. Being able to tear down each nut and bolt of these machines, Josh was quickly hired for a critical engine assembly technician role with Caterpillar out of Lafayette, IN in 2007. For 8 years, Josh focused in the mining, mobile equipment, electric power generation and the oil gas industries building some of the worlds largest diesel and natural gas engines from the ground up.
