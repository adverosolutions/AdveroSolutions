What exactly does recruitment outsourcing mean? Recruitment outsourcing, also known as RPO (Recruitment Process Outsourcing), is a strategic business partnership where an organization entrusts some or all of its recruitment processes to an external service provider. In recruitment outsourcing, the external provider assumes responsibility for various aspects of the recruitment lifecycle, such as sourcing, screening, interviewing, and onboarding candidates. This arrangement allows organizations to leverage the expertise, resources, and scalability of the outsourcing partner to streamline their hiring processes, reduce costs, and improve the quality of hires.
