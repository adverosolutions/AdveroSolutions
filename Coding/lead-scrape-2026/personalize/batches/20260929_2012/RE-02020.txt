About BankerHire BankerHire is a boutique search firm dedicated exclusively to the banking, financial services, and FinTech industry. We partner with community banks, regional banks, credit unions, mortgage companies, and large national banks across the United States to identify, attract, and secure exceptional talent. Our work spans executive leadership, commercial banking, treasury management, credit, risk, compliance, finance, operations, technology, HR, and many of the specialized functions that drive successful financial institutions. We believe great recruiting begins with understanding the business, not simply understanding a job description. Every search starts with understanding your organization's culture, long-term strategy, leadership style, and hiring objectives. From there, we leverage years of industry relationships expertise to identify professionals who may never apply to a job posting but are open to the right opportunity. Whether we're helping build an entire commercial lending team or conducting a confidential executive search, our focus remains the same: finding people who create lasting value for our clients. Since launching BankerHire in 2019, we have completed 200+ searches ranging from individual contributor roles to C-suite leadership for a variety of banks. Our clients have run the spectrum from local community brands under $1 billion in assets to some of the largest financial institutions in the country. We've helped clients build commercial banking teams, strengthen risk and compliance functions, recruit operational leadership, and hire executives who shape the future of their organizations. About The Founder BankerHire was founded by Shane Smith in 2019. Before launching BankerHire, Shane spent five years building a successful New England based banking recruitment practices, earning the trust of financial institutions through market expertise, honest communication, and an unwavering commitment to long-term partnerships rather than transactional recruiting. That philosophy continues to define BankerHire today. Clients work directly with Shane throughout every engagement. There are no junior recruiters handing off searches or account managers separating clients from the recruiting process.
