Coriolis People CORIOLIS PEOPLE Recruiting and Human Capital Consulting for Today’s Technology Industry Coriolis People provides recruiting and human capital consulting services to technology companies. Our primary industry expertise includes Quantitative Finance, FinTech, and Healthcare Analytics. Our clients are diverse in their long-term goals, but share the belief that an ongoing investment in research and technology can fundamentally transform their work and keep them at the forefront of their respective industries. For small and mid-sized businesses looking to bolster their recruitment pipelines, we offer contingency recruiting to connect you with high-caliber candidates and help you close your most challenging technical searches. For start-ups who value hiring and retaining excellent people, we design recruiting and human capital strategies that help you avoid costly attrition and increase productivity and happiness during your most critical periods of growth. For large, well-established firms who want to improve existing recruitment processes or better leverage in-house resources, we consult on a variety of topics including interview training, strategic sourcing, and campus recruitment. About Coriolis People About Melanie Lahti, Founder Melanie began her recruiting career at D. E. Shaw Research, a highly selective computational biochemistry research group, where she led software recruitment and designed the firm’s first successful diversity and inclusion strategy. She then joined the admissions team at the Yale School of Management where she focused on data integrity and analysis, in addition to the recruitment, evaluation, and selection of students. Melanie holds a B.A. in music from Yale University and an M.M. in clarinet performance from the University of Cincinnati-College Conservatory of Music. Over the course of her career she has developed an affinity for people analytics and behavioral design, and enjoys exploring their potential impact on human capital processes.
