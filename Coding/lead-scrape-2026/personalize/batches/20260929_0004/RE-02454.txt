Who We Are WHO WE ARE THE PARTNERS TOM GILIDA, Partner Tom Gilida is a Partner at R.M. William Talent Solutions based in Denver. Tom specializes in conducting searches for difficult to fill positions for unique situations. Tom’s experience includes searches for executive management positions including Chief Financial Officer, Vice President of Finance, Vice President Supply Chain, Chief Operating Officer, Human Resources Leader, Vice President Sales, and Vice President Innovation. Tom has been involved with numerous management consulting firms specializing in information technology consulting and placement services. Prior to R.M. William, Tom worked as an auditor for Arthur Andersen in the Denver office and ran a National Lawson ERP practice for Modis. Tom’s industry experience includes real estate, consumer services, technology, telecommunications, healthcare, real estate, construction, mining, and oil and gas. Tom earned his CPA in the State of Colorado in 1993 and his Colorado Real Estate Broker license in 2002. STACEY WITZ, Partner Stacey Witz is a Partner at R.M.
