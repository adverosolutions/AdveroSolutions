About NY Horticulture Group About NY Horticulture Group Passionate Plant People, Rooted in NY At NY Horticulture Group , we’re all about bringing beautiful green spaces to life for commercial properties and residents. Whether it’s a cozy rooftop retreat, a lush garden terrace, or a sprawling commercial landscape, we love what we do—and it shows in our work. We got our start back in July 1992 as Let It Grow, designing and maintaining landscapes all over NYC and Long Island. Our projects have ranged from charming little 400-square-foot loft gardens to massive malls covering over 2 million square feet. In 2017, we rebranded as NY Horticulture Group, but our passion for plants and dedication to detail have stayed the same. Our team is a close-knit group of plant lovers who take pride in making outdoor spaces all throughout Manhattan and Brooklyn look their absolute best. We offer a full range of services, including: Landscape Design – Creating custom landscapes that are as beautiful as they are functional. Rooftop Terrace Design – Transforming city spaces into lush green retreats. Garden Maintenance – Keeping your plants happy, healthy, and thriving year-round. Seasonal Services – Refreshing your space with seasonal touches to keep things looking fresh. Irrigation Services – Setting up smart watering systems so your plants get exactly what they need. Synthetic Grass Installation – A low-maintenance, high-quality alternative to natural grass. Holiday Decor – Adding a festive touch to your home or business during the holidays. Outdoor Staging — Elevate your NYC listings with expert outdoor staging—plants, planters, and furniture that captivate buyers. We keep things simple: "Do the work and pay lots of attention to the details." That’s our motto, and it guides everything we do. We’re all about making sure your outdoor spaces look incredible—whether it’s a tiny backyard or a giant corporate plaza. If you’re ready to bring more greenery into your life, we’d love to help.
