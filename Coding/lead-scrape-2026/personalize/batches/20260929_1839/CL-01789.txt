Our Company History Retro Maintenance LLC is an over 100 year old, family owned and operated business serving New York City for 3 generations. John Stadnyk is the third generation and current President CEO of Retro Maintenance. Retro Maintenance LLC was founded as Stadnyk Window Cleaning Company in 1923 by John Stadnyk s grandfather who came from Ukraine in 1912 to pursue the American dream. He came here unable to speak English and had little money in his pocket. He worked hard and became an American citizen. He worked for a few different cleaning companies performing window cleaning and floor cleaning services in New York City before opening his own business. Right after World War II in the mid 1940’s, John s father and uncle entered the business which they continued to operate until their retirement. John s father performed post construction cleaning on newly constructed high-rise buildings all over New York state and in the 1960’s had a work force of over 35 personnel. Retro Maintenance John Stadnyk has worked with the company for over 45 years, starting out as a window cleaner and acquiring ownership in the 1980’s.
