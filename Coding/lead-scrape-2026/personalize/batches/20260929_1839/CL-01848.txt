The Sterling group is comprised of two companies that provide an array of services to building owners and property managers. Sterling Building Services, Inc. and Sterling Window Cleaning, Inc. are all privately held and family owned sister companies that make up the Sterling group. Sterling Building Services, Inc. offers property managers and facility owners interior and exterior cleaning and maintenance services. Sterling Building Services is a Hispanic-owned Minority Business Enterprise (MBE) through the State of Florida and the City of Orlando. We are also certified as a Local Developing Business (LDB) with the Greater Orlando Aviation Authority and we have been certified by the Small Business Administration and 8(a) firm. Sterling Window Cleaning, Inc. offers both low rise and high rise window cleaning and pressure washing in the commercial sector. Sterling Window Cleaning, Inc. currently holds a Minority Business Enterprise certification through the State of Florida, City of Orlando and the Greater Orlando Aviation Authority. We are also certified as a Local Developing Business (LDB) with the Greater Orlando Aviation Authority. Who do we service?
