About CMS 0 About Our Company Commercial Maintenance Services was founded in 2013. We have strived to be a vital part of the food manufacturing and facilities maintenance industry since our inception. As we have grown, we have always kept our core values at the center of what we do: efficiency, integrity and accountability. We pursue all of our endeavors with these three values in mind. And as we continue to look to the future, we are looking for companies that we can partner with to pursue these values and improve the world in which we live for generations to come. Our Values Efficiency Our highly trained employees work hard to keep your production and maintenance schedules on time and in place, while also keeping safety at the forefront of our work. Integrity We value our employees and customers and treat them all with the dignity and respect that they deserve. We work as a united group to uphold the high expectations of both ourselves and our customers. Accountability We believe that we all are accountable, not only to ourselves, but to our coworkers, our customers, and to the communities in which we live. Our standards of excellence reflect this belief.
