Our Story | HighPoint CPAs | Accounting + Tax + Wealth Management Integrated Financial Advisory Services Get Started Our Story Our Vision We believe that clients are best served through a relationship with their trusted advisor who knows how to manage a team of professionals with a focus on designing, building and protecting a plan for client financial success. HighPoint CPAs operates on the philosophy that integrated client financial solutions (business accounting advisory services (BAAS), tax, wealth management, estate and retirement planning) provide a better client experience through not only financial outcomes but also through relationship alpha. Ultimately what matters to our clients should matter to us … so that when our clients confide in us, they know they can trust our advice because our interests are aligned.
