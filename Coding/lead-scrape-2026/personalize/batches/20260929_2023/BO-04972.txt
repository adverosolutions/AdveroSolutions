Skip to primary navigation Skip to main content Skip to footer About Us Edna is the owner of A C Consulting Group Ltd., a local business that provides Bookkeeping, Payroll, Tax Preparation, and Financial Planning Services and Training for QuickBooks to small businesses and non profit organizations. Edna has been an educator for over 20 years and has many years of experience as a Financial Controller. She has also operated her own firm for almost 10 years. Edna is a ProAdvisor certified in QuickBooks Desktop, QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Enterprise. She has a wealth of knowledge and expertise in organizing and managing the financial health of a business and will personally work with each client to develop a plan that meets the specific needs of the client. Edna will personally supervise all services to our clients and will ensure all clients receive outstanding service.
