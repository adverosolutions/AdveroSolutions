About Angeliese Wisdom | Faith-Based Bookkeeping for Nonprofits Ministries Home About Services Blog Contact Book Your Consultation BOOKKEEPING WITH WISDOM LLC The Biblical Bookkeeper Angeliese Wisdom brings a faith-centered approach to financial management, serving ministries, nonprofits, and mission-driven businesses with precision, clarity, and absolute integrity. Work With Angeliese The Origin Driven by Wisdom My name is Angeliese — pronounced Angel-iese — and I am from a small town in southern Wyoming. I have been working since I was 15, building a background in customer service and management across a wide variety of fields long before I ever put the word "bookkeeper" on a business card. Honestly? I chose accounting out of necessity. I got pregnant right out of high school and found myself homeless and jobless — facing a season that felt impossible. Through serious reflection and the guidance of the Lord, my path began to change. I met my husband, earned my AA in Accounting, and spent a lot of years figuring out finances and learning how to be the best possible support for the people around me. After our third child, my husband and I realized that the cost of us both working (swapping shifts, paying for childcare) simply didn't make sense. So I took the leap. Bookkeeping with Wisdom was born. Since then I have served businesses in construction, transportation, ecommerce, retail, nonprofits, and more. For the first time in my life, I feel like my skills are genuinely appreciated and that what I do truly matters. More recently, I felt a growing conviction that there wasn't much humanity left in tax preparation. So I embarked on the journey of obtaining my Enrolled Agent license and have begun preparing individual tax returns — with plans to grow into more complex returns as I continue learning and serving. Everything I do is rooted in stewardship, integrity, and the belief that God equips His people with more than enough , and my role is simply to help them manage it well.
