Our Purpose Greenback takes away the anxiety and hassle of helping U.S. expats become and stay compliant with their U.S. taxes while overseas. We understand that filing U.S. taxes while living abroad can be daunting. But we know it doesn’t have to be this way. Our Promise We’re proud to help people navigate a ridiculously complex system in a way that makes sense for their individual situation. While it may sound impossible, Greenback makes the process of filing U.S. expat taxes a hassle-free one — a necessary step as part of an expat’s amazing adventure abroad. Our History At Greenback, we’re driven by our core values of fairness, transparency, and offering a personalized approach for every client. Our goal is to simplify your tax process, optimizing tax credits and refunds while ensuring compliance with IRS rules. We put your interests and ethical standards first. Our journey began in 2009 when American expats Carrie and David McKeegan experienced the frustrations and challenges of filing U.S. expat taxes while living in the UK. Determined to simplify this complex process, they founded Greenback Expat Tax Services. Their vision was a customer-centric tax preparation company, staffed with accountants possessing deep expertise in U.S. expat taxes. This vision manifested into Greenback – a company tailored to cater to the unique needs of U.S. expats worldwide. The Team Based throughout the world, we’re a fully remote team of former expats and travel lovers using our decades of experience in a range of industries to now help our customers enjoy a painless tax filing experience year over year. With years of customer service experience, our Customer Champions offers support coverage in every time zone – from initial questions, account set up, payment, and everything in between.
