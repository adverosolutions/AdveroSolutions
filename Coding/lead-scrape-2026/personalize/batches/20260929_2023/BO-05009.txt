Gordon Corporation (SGC) has the foundation of a traditional Certified Public Accounting firm coupled with the leadership and experience of being family business entrepreneurs. Often times, families that have experienced a generation or two of financial success have a distinct desire to ensure they leave a legacy based upon values and opportunity for future generations and/or community. Turning financial success into a legacy that aligns with the values of each specific family takes careful planning, experienced family advisors across multiple disciplines and the ability to facilitate a path forward which includes navigating the changing landscapes, lifestyles, multiple generations and economic environments. SGC understands that execution is a very important aspect of all legacy planning, which incorporates much more than just signing documents and most often is putting in place governance and protocols to last decades. We work closely with our families designing and implementing governance that sees beyond today into the future. This includes activities such as closely held business oversight, trust operations, foundation operations, real estate portfolio strategies and a significant focus on the income and estate tax implication of all plans. Currently, SGC has narrowed its focus on a select number of families navigating wealth and preserving legacies.
