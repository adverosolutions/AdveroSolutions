--> About | Equitable Accounting Solutions top of page About Equitable Accounting Solutions Equitable Accounting Solutions PLLC offers comprehensive accounting and advisory services for service-based small businesses. We handle everything from Accounts Payable to Payroll and Taxes, so clients can focus on growing their business. Committed to diversity and inclusion, we provide personalized support to help every client achieve financial success. Mary A. Barton, CPA, is the President of Equitable Accounting Solutions. Based in Detroit, Michigan, Equitable Accounting Solutions offers full-service accounting and advisory services to small service-based businesses. Mary has over 20 years of experience providing accounting services to clients in areas of taxation, assurance, advisory and financial reporting across various industries. Her role involves working closely with business owners, key personnel, vendors, and bankers to gain a comprehensive understanding of the business s financial situation and proactively address financial matters, including wealth management. Mary has a passion for supporting women- and minority-owned businesses. As a woman of color and small business owner herself, Mary understands that small businesses are the driving force to boost the economy and she is committed to establishing a mutual connection with her clients in order to help them build and maintain financial growth. Mary is the current Treasurer for NAWBO Greater Detroit Chapter, a membership organization that propels women entrepreneurs into economic, social, and political spheres of power, worldwide. She is also an active parent and PTA Treasurer at her son s public elementary school. Mary is a member of the MICPA and a mentor for their college student members pursuing accounting. She is also a dedicated Winning Futures mentor. Most recently, Mary joined the Board of Directors of MACRS (Minority Accounting Career Resource Society), a non-profit focused on providing pathways for high school and college students into the accounting industry. During the last few years, Mary has been utilizing her skillset to support nonprofit organizations in providing technical assistance for their small business members. In 2022, Mary was a NAWBO GDC Top 10 Michigan Business Women Award Honoree and received the Next-Gen Entrepreneur Award.
