About Us Financial Clarity for Manufacturers Distributors You can’t make smart decisions about tomorrow if you don’t trust yesterday’s numbers. Accounting Sage helps manufacturers, wholesalers, and distributors build reliable accounting, understand what is happening in the business today, and use that information to make better decisions about growth, cash flow, inventory, and profitability. Your Numbers Should Help You Run the Business For product-based companies, everything is connected. Inventory affects cash. Purchasing affects margins. Freight and landed costs affect profitability. Sales growth can look great while cash gets tighter. And if the books are wrong, late, or incomplete, it becomes very hard to answer the questions that actually matter: Can we afford to place this order? Are our margins holding up? Is growth making us more profitable? Where is our cash going? What happens if we hire, expand, or invest? That is why we start with reliable accounting and then use it to help you look forward. Built for Businesses That Make and Move Products We specialize in the financial challenges of manufacturing, wholesale, and distribution businesses . We understand that your accounting is not just about debits and credits. It is about inventory, purchasing, landed costs, margins, cash flow, and the operational decisions happening every day. Our job is to connect those pieces so you can see the bigger picture. Odoo Accounting Expertise With more than five years of hands-on Odoo experience , we understand how accounting connects with inventory, purchasing, sales, AP, AR, and operations. We help businesses get better information out of Odoo not just more reports. Because a fancy dashboard is not very helpful if the numbers behind it are wrong.
