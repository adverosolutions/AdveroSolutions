About Us Skip to content About Us Our Story Stan Kristi Morton came up with the idea for Morton Bookkeeping in 2015 at the kitchen table, while talking about life, accounting, commuting, and faith. But to understand this story, you need some context. Kristi started her adult career in the banking world at a young age, over 30 years ago. She loved the numbers, finding discrepancies, but most of all, the people. You ve never seen someone glow as much as she did, when she helped people with their banking. Kristi went on to be in management and then switched to corporate accounting. Working with several large organizations such as the Girl Scouts and Marriott Hotels, she again climbed the ladder to be a Controller for one of Marriott s boutique hotels in the heart of Atlanta, Georgia. Stan got started in real estate finance. He built a small brokerage that was growing quickly, that is, up until the financial crisis of 2008. Pivoting, Stan started a real estate closing business that grew to do closings all over the US. At the same time, he was a partner in 2 different real estate start-ups. In 2013, Stan signed on as a field specialist for a national real estate investment fund. Over the next few years he rose through the ranks to be a Senior Analyst. It was then, in 2015, that Stan Kristi birthed the idea for Morton Bookkeeping. They wanted a way to offer bookkeeping for businesses that was easy to afford, gave exceptional service, was personal, virtual, and carried the weight and accuracy of an Accounting Executive. It was also important to focus on one software, and only small businesses to be as consistent as possible. In 2025, after being asked over and over, Stan Kristi put in the hours and built the team so in 2026 they finally offer Tax Prep eFiling for businesses and owners. Stan said, It just makes sense to have the people that know the most about your business finances and handle your day-to-day account to do your business and personal returns. Today, they have a thriving business that serves over 50 different industries throughout the eastern United States and offer more value than ever to their clients. The interesting part is once someone becomes a client, they don t seem to ever leave. On average, there has been less than 10% attrition since day one.
