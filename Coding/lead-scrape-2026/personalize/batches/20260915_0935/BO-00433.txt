About Me Born in California and raised in Georgia, I grew up watching my father build a business from the ground up and I knew early on that I wanted to follow a similar path. After earning my bachelor’s degree in Accounting and Business Management, my career took me through roles that taught me the foundations of excellent customer service, the inner workings of successful operations, and the difference between the actions that support business growth and the ones that quietly stall it. Along the way, I noticed a pattern: entrepreneurs and vision‑holders rarely want to be the ones tracking every financial detail. They want to build, create, lead, and serve not categorize transactions or reconcile accounts. Eventually, every business owner realizes they need someone who can bring clarity, consistency, and calm to their financial world. That’s where I come in. While many find accounting tedious, I genuinely love the structure, the precision, and the story hidden inside the numbers. Twenty years after completing my undergraduate degree, I returned to earn my MBA with a concentration in Accounting and finally set my sights on launching the bookkeeping business I had always envisioned. Today, I help business owners transform financial overwhelm into confidence.
