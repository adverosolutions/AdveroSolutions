Home | SG2 Recruiting Recruitment Process Outsourcing The Solution for Growing Companies Optimize your talent approach by engaging our experts Why Choose RPO? SG2’s RPO services streamline your recruiting process, reduce costs and time to hire while developing a hiring strategy for your company's long-term success. We will work with your team to create an effective and consistent hiring process to ensure that only the most qualified and suitable candidates are presented to your team. Acting as an extension of your business, our entire team is dedicated to finding the best candidates for your next hire. Cost Effective Traditional staffing and contingency agencies charge 20 to 35% of the salary of the candidate hired. Flexibility Scalability RPO is recruiting on demand with the ability to instantly add or subtract additional resources to the hiring process. Pipeline Ownership Unlike staffing and contingency firms, RPO provides a pipeline of all candidates sourced for future workforce needs while assuring the best candidate is placed. Reduce Time to Hire RPO is a defined, rapid recruiting process that creates an excellent candidate and hiring manager experience. Advanced Technology Constantly evaluating and utilizing state-of-the-art recruiting tools helps find and engage all candidates in the market. Higher Candidate Quality A dedicated, specialist RPO recruiter is more effective than overloaded in-house recruiters and multi-tasking hiring managers. SG2 RPO Services Project RPO SG2’s Project RPO is a scalable, integrated and cost-effective solution that delivers a short-term recruitment process for surge hiring needs, including: Start-up growth Launching a new product Newly awarded contracts Accelerated Growth Specific, hard to fill jobs Seed funding Fractional Executives A curated network of experienced executives to provide on-demand expertise to businesses.
