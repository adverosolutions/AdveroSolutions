Green is the new black Indoor air quality is one of the most important environmental issues in our lives today. According to the American Lung Association, poor indoor air quality can cause or contribute to the development of infections, lung cancer, and chronic lung diseases such as asthma and allergies and emphysema. At all accounts, EDGE uses a High Health Performance Cleaning standard (HHPC) program that was implemented as team effort with our suppliers to improve overall employee attendance and help reduce the occurrence of these common diseases into our business and into all of our accounts. The EDGE HHPC cleaning system is designed around the products, procedures, equipment, and tools that are used every day in your buildings. Your tenants, employees, and visitors are counting on you, and you can count on EDGE and our HHPC cleaning system, to improve the cleanliness of your building and the health of everyone spending time in the buildings.
