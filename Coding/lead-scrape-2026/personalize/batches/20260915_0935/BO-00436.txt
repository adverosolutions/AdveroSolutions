Today, I partner with entrepreneurs who want more than basic bookkeeping — they want clarity, structure, and confidence in their financial decisions. As a small business owner based in Atlanta, I understand the responsibility that comes with running a company. The demands are constant, and it’s easy for financial management to become reactive or overwhelming. Your numbers should support your leadership — not compete with your time or energy. I bring both technical precision and thoughtful communication to every engagement. Numbers matter — but so does how you experience running your business. I believe strong financial systems create space: space to make confident decisions, space to focus on your clients, and space to maintain the balance that allows you to lead well long-term.
