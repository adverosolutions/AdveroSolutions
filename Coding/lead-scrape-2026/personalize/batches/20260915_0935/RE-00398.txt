Who We Are - Mattran Group Who We Are Over thirty years of Precision Recruiting From our modest beginnings as a two-person office in 1987, we’ve worked diligently from day one to distinguish ourselves as one of the top franchises in the MRINetwork of over 400 offices worldwide. Our dedication to excellence has led to our being consistently ranked in the top 10% of recruiting firms within the MRI organization. As we’ve grown, each new member to join our team has made a deliberate decision to become part of our organization, bringing with them their own unique business experiences from a variety of vertical markets. They wanted to be part of a company that is committed to doing the right thing and prides itself on quality, expertise and intuition when finding the right person for the job. Over time, this has enabled us to build a team of forward-thinking, dedicated professionals whose intelligence and creativity makes them particularly adept at filling hard-to-fill positions. We’ve seen our share of challenges over the years; Black Monday, three recessions, and a global pandemic, to name a few. Each obstacle has allowed us to become stronger and more dedicated to providing excellence in recruiting. We continue to believe that attracting exceptional talent is an essential element driving business growth today and will continue to hold that view for the many productive years to come.
