View Our Searches Upload Your Resume Recommend a Candidate Find Your Big Fish Big Fish Ideas Big Fish Story The Big Fish Story A big fish is a changemaker, an influencer, someone that possesses both tangible and intangibles required not to fill a position but to be a catalyst to take your company or organization to the next level. We all want to hire big fish; the problem is big fish are hard to catch. That is where Big Fish, comes in we help you recruit and hire Big Fish. Not every big fish is a fit for your culture; rarely are you going magically cast your line into the sea and reel in the exact right fit. At Big Fish, we not only help you reel in the rock star talent you want but we take the time to understand the skills and the values that will make them succeed in your unique fish tank (Culture). Every placement comes with our Big Fish Talent Guarantee. If our fish isn’t right for your organization we will do the search again for free. Chaz Cirame Founder Big Fish Chaz Cirame has been a senior-level Professional in the non-profit, public affairs, creative, and fundraising verticals. He is the co-host of a 5000+ plus member professional development community called Rules of Networking; he has talked to audiences across the country about the value of relationship building. Over the years, he has worked with thousands of elected officials, lobbyists, fundraisers, donors, and communicators and knows what makes the best succeed---That experience is why he founded Big Fish.
