--> OUR STORY | The Tolan Group | Healthcare Executive Search Firm | USA top of page THE TOLAN GROUP Our Founder Story In my early 20 s I started my professional sales career in healthcare software. I spent my formative professional years selling software to physician offices, hospitals, dialysis units and labs. I built a sales team and traveled extensively throughout the US visiting a total of 49 states (missing Idaho). Later I joined Healtheon just before we merged with WebMD and went public. My final stop before becoming an entrepreneur was leading sales and account management teams for one of the largest clearing houses for physician claims in the US. I learned a lot but also knew I needed a change. I m from a large Irish family (my father came to the US from Belfast) and have 7 brothers, so keeping us busy was always the goal in our crowded home (emphasis on the word crowded). My dad was a salesman and as a result I started selling at a very young age, first at college football games. Eventually at the ripe ole age of 13, and at the urging of my mother, I began selling Fuller Brush door-to-door after school and on Saturday mornings. I learned a lot about cold calling, persistence, and rejection. I thank her for that nudge. It was impactful. I was burning out quickly and becoming bored. ​ My quest to become an entrepreneur was always there but like many, I was nervous about taking the risk. Finally, one day I knew it was time. It all started after reaching a combined total of 2M air miles on Delta, USAir, United, and other regional carriers. I had enough, and following the tragic events of September 11th, travel became so much more difficult. I was done! Working for others had lost its appeal and was no longer of interest to me, and there was plenty of stress owning the revenue responsibility for multiple publicly traded companies. Finally, I decided to start my own executive search firm and began planning in the summer of 2004. I wanted to leverage my decades of contacts, maintain my income while minimizing the out-of-town travel as spending 4-5 nights a week had totally lost its appeal. So, it all began on Monday September 12, 2005, just outside of Atlanta. It was me, myself, and I. Daunting for sure. Scary without a doubt. Initially, I bought an executive search franchise because I wanted the structure and business process a franchise could give me.
