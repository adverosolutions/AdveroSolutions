About Us | Singh Hecht Executive Search | Marketing Recruiters ABOUT US Singh Hecht Executive Search is a national boutique search firm specializing in the areas of marketing, public relations, and communications. We’re not just another recruitment firm; we’ve turned the serious business of executive recruitment into an art form. With a combined experience longer than the line at a coffee shop on a Monday morning, our team has a knack for finding the perfect fit for your organization. Our goal is to drive success by placing exceptional talent who can inspire innovation, foster growth, and transform businesses – each and every time. OUR STORY Founded by Jocelyn Hecht and Shabnam Singh, Singh Hecht Executive Search was born out of a desire to make the executive search process not just effective, but also a little more enjoyable (yes, it’s possible!). With four decades of combined experience, we’ve built a reputation for our personalized approach, deep industry insights, extensive network, and a commitment to client satisfaction that’s as strong as our coffee. Our mission is simple: To continue cultivating long-lasting relationships with clients and candidates, place outstanding talent who’ll make a difference, and maybe share a few laughs along the way. PURPOSEFULLY SMALL Compact yet powerful, we deliberately keep our team small. To us, small equals personal. Your search will be managed by a Partner, whether you are a hiring manager or a candidate seeking employment. Shabnam and Jocelyn have extensive experience in the public relations and marketing industries, both as recruiters and as practitioners, offering a unique perspective to assess fit and talent. Whether through hands-on work or close collaboration, we intimately understand the roles we seek to fill.
