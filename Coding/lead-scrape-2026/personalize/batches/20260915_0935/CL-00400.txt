BrightBase LLC Parking Facilities Clients About Team Contact Pay Ticket Top of Page Pay a Ticket Where to Park 1966 Our Roots It all began in 1966 right here in Chattanooga, Tennessee, with the creation of what is now known as Republic Parking System. Britton’s grandfather, Jim Berry, began with one employee and grew to the country’s largest, privately held parking company with over 2,800 employees and operations in more than 100 US cities. He grew his company to include more than 740 parking locations and more than 290,000 parking spaces under management. Over 50 years spent in the parking and service industry gained his firm a reputation for serving clients honestly, eagerly and professionally. Britton started working for Republic in 2004 and eventually worked his way up to becoming a regional vice president for the company. Grandpa Jim Britton 2021 Forming BrightBase Britton founded BrightBase in 2021 with the purpose of serving Chattanooga and the surrounding region through parking management.
