Hiring in the Age of AI: What Shark Tank’s Barbara Corcoran Gets Right About Finding Great Candidates Skip to content Hiring in the Age of AI: What Shark Tank’s Barbara Corcoran Gets Right About Finding Great Candidates August 25, 2026 / Employee Benefits , Hiring Technology , Hiring Trends , Private Hiring , Recruiter , Virtual Hiring Shark Tank investor and entrepreneur Barbara Corcoran has hired thousands of people throughout her career, but her approach to evaluating candidates might surprise employers accustomed to starting with a stack of resumes. Corcoran has said, “I don’t look at the resume. It gets in the way of sizing up the person in front of me.” Her philosophy feels particularly timely heading into fall 2026. AI can help job seekers rewrite resumes, strengthen descriptions of their experience, tailor applications to individual job descriptions and create polished cover letters in seconds. Employers may have more information about candidates than ever before, yet determining who is actually worth hiring can still require something remarkably traditional: meeting the person. New research from ICIMS and Lighthouse Research Advisory supports that concern. Only about one third of surveyed employers are confident that a resume accurately represents a candidate’s skills, while half are concerned about exaggerated or fabricated qualifications. Sixty two percent of high volume and frontline employers say finding the best candidate is now a greater priority than simply attracting more applicants. Skills based hiring also ranks as their top priority for the next 12 to 18 months. Corcoran’s approach raises an important question for employers preparing to hire this September: Are you spending too much effort generating applications and not enough creating opportunities to meet the right people? AI Can Make Almost Anyone Look Better on Paper Corcoran’s skepticism about relying too heavily on resumes becomes especially interesting when considered alongside AI. Candidates can now ask an AI tool to analyze a job description and adjust their resume accordingly. It can identify important terminology, improve descriptions of previous responsibilities, strengthen a cover letter or help someone articulate why their experience makes them a good candidate. Used appropriately, these tools can help talented candidates communicate their experience more effectively.
