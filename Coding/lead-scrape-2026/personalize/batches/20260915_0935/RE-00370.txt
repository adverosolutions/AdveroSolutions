Deny All Accept All Skip to content About Brindisi Search Brindisi Search Our background and history Brindisi Search is an agile, boutique retained search firm with a focus on finding best-in-class human resource leaders able to drive change, transform outdated organizations and partner with business leaders to maximize the impact of human capital across their organizations. Since our beginnings in 1988, we have dedicated our efforts to the long- term success of our client firms. Founded and led by Thomas J. Brindisi, President, our firm has been an outspoken advocate for radical change and business competitiveness throughout our history. We address critical HR needs, ranging from SVP and CHRO roles to functional and business unit HR leadership roles, usually at Director and VP levels. Brindisi Search has successfully completed senior level HR searches for many well-known corporations, often calling for the ability to lead dramatic transformation or to execute complex growth strategies. Our clients range across every industry, and vary in size from Fortune 50 giants to mid-cap hard-charging firms, PE portfolio companies, and innovative start-ups that rapidly exploded into multi-billion-dollar global businesses. Brindisi Search has aggressively participated in the evolution of American business and the “coming of age” for the human resources function, from the Jack Welch era through all phases of the ongoing corporate business transformation. Our firm has delivered HR leaders adept in continuous improvement, re-engineering, and every iteration of total quality management, through today’s emphasis on agile and lean management approaches, and harnessing the latest technologies. Our deep focus on cultural fit, and our active collaboration with our clients, allow us to deliver the most powerful “human solutions” available in the marketplace. We continue to focus on identifying innovative, energetic HR leaders capable of guiding their organizations through radical change and sustained success. Results have always been, and continue to be our reputation.
