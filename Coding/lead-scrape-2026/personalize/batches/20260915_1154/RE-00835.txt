Each applicant is carefully evaluated before presentation to the client. Founded in 1991, East End Resources is certified by the State and City of New York as a WMBE, and is a member of the TechServe Alliance, a national alliance of IT staffing firms. We firmly believe in ethical and honest interactions with both clients and candidates, and have become the first choice for recruiting needs among many of our clients. East End successfully manages a broad spectrum of candidate searches, including programmer analysts, software engineers, Big Data/Cloud engineers, UX designers, project managers, program managers, business and data analysts, application/production support and quality assurance analysts. Our clients come from a variety of industries including Brokerage, Banking, Pharmaceuticals, Telecommunications and Consumer Products. Our clients benefit from our valuable network of talented technical professionals, personal referrals from clients and existing technical staff.
