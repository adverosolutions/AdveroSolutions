FAZZINA CO. COMPANY Fazzina Co. is a highly specialized executive search consultancy, with an expertise in placing talent for all areas of fashion design. ABOUT Sam Fazzina Co. team have conducted talent searches for over two decades placing Creative Directors, senior leadership and building design teams for many global brands including, but not limited to: Ralph Lauren, Jason Wu, Champion, Victoria’s Secret, Vince, Under Armour, Sonia Rykiel, Gucci, Cuyana, Abercrombie Fitch, Everlane, Levi’s, American Eagle, Loeffler Randall, Nordstrom, Theory, Fossil, Hanna Andersson, Calvin Klein, J Crew, Diane Von Furstenburg, Gap, Burberry, Hugo Boss. Areas of expertise include Creative Directors, SVP/VP/Directors Design in Women’s, Men’s, Children, Accessories, Home. Fabric R D, Product Development, Color, Forecasting, Print Graphics. Consulting on ‘Design Star Search’ and niche team building assignments. The Company has an unrivaled global perspective, providing personalized partnerships and is always committed to introducing a diverse slate of brand right talent. Sam and her team have developed long-term relationships within the design community and maintain a competitive global network of candidates.
