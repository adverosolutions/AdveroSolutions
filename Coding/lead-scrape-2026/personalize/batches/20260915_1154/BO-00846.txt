About Us | TaxTimeHQ Inc. Meet James Doty, EA Most accountants are comfortable inside one system, one country, one rulebook. That works—until your business crosses a border. I work with small businesses whose operations don’t fit neatly inside the U.S. accounting box: companies with international contractors, foreign subsidiaries, overseas clients, or owners who live between countries. These businesses don’t need more software. They need financial clarity. I provide high-touch bookkeeping, CAS, and fractional controllership services designed for internationally connected companies. That means clean books, yes—but also margin insight, cash-flow foresight, and a clear understanding of how money actually moves across borders and currencies. Before founding TaxTimeHQ, my career and life were deeply international. I’ve lived and worked abroad, navigated multiple tax systems, and spent years translating complexity into something business owners can actually use. That background matters when your books involve foreign banks, international payroll, VAT conversations, or advisors who don’t always speak the same financial language. If your business is growing internationally and you’re tired of stitching together answers from three advisors in two countries, you’re in the right place. If you’re just looking for the cheapest monthly bookkeeping package, you probably aren’t. And that’s okay.
