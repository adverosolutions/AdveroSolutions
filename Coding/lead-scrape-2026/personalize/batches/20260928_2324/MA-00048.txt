WHAT WE DO WhiteSmith Marketing Group, Inc. is a full service marketing and advertising firm. Because of the way we do business, our clients receive personalized solutions that are as unique as their businesses. Clients . . . are at the center of everything we do. In fact, we pride ourselves on listening, and really hearing you, before thinking strategy. We believe you know what you want to accomplish. We'll help get it done in ways that you might not have imagined. And together, we will be on time, on budget and on target. WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT Results. We are extremely intentional as we consider working with new clients. It is important to us that we are the right fit for your company. We want to match our skills with your needs. This way you can expect results and we know we can deliver them. Creative Partnerships. Our plan is to become much more than marketing advisors for your company or organization. We want to become members of your team. We promise – we’ll be as invested as you are in making sure things get done and get done right – the first time. Simple. We know that effective doesn’t have to be hard, or expensive. We try to strike the right balance of keeping you 100% informed and executing projects without involving you in every detail. Our customers also tell us they appreciate our easy-going approach, integrity and honesty. WhiteSmith Marketing Group was established in 1999 by Lisa Smith Odendaal and Terri White. We each bring over 20 years of experience in marketing, advertising, project management, planning, special events, writing, and public relations. And in the last few years, we’ve added social media to the mix. Our clients range in size from small businesses to national companies. We’ve worked with healthcare providers, manufacturers, non-profits, transit districts, and service organizations. What do they all have in common? They are doing good work and are ready to move to the next level.
