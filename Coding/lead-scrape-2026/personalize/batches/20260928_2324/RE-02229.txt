Book a Call Meet Lauren The Story Behind Pathway Talent A Strategic Recruiting Partner Rooted in People, Not Just Process At Pathway Talent, we believe hiring should feel like a strategic advantage not a constant struggle. Founded by Lauren Treleven, a seasoned recruiter with nearly 15 years of experience, Pathway Talent was built on the belief that great businesses are powered by great people. From Fortune 500 companies to mission-driven nonprofits and agile startups, Lauren has helped organizations across industries identify and attract high-performing talent who align with both the role and the culture. Lauren s background spans nearly every type of hire interns to executives, contractors to permanent placements. Her experience includes recruiting for roles in Human Resources, Legal, Finance, Sales, Operations, Administrative Support, Technology, Marketing, Skilled Trades, and nonprofit leadership.
