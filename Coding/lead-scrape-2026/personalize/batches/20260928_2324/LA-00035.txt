Our employees have been with our firm for many of these years and our seasoned, experienced professionals. They are supported by the best equipment money can buy. We provide a manager that is there to help the crews with any guidance and support they may need to perform their jobs efficiently and thoroughly through-out each day. Our company is directed by a college degreed horticulturist with over 40 years of commercial landscape experience. Southern Care Lawn and Landscape owns a fleet of service trucks, dump equipment, loaders, irrigation equipment and tools to perform any job needed without having to depend on rental agencies. Our equipment is the latest and most efficient machines available to our industry, which saves our customers a substantial cost in labor. Our Mission Providing our customers with the highest quality services, continually researching and developing new methods of landscape management, and recruiting and retaining the best possible team members to work in a cooperative, enlightened, diverse and a team-oriented environment. Southern Care offers experienced personnel, including licensed chemical applicators, degreed horticulturists, and crew foremen, who specialize in commercial properties while maintaining a consistent level of quality. We are fully insured and licensed. Our crews are equipped with new, state-of-the-art equipment to optimize efficiency and better service your landscaping needs. Our team currently services over 250 commercial buildings. We differentiate ourselves by taking a consultative approach in developing a tailored landscape plan to meet the goals and needs of our customers. Prior to entering an agreement, we invest a significant amount of time on the front end making sure we address any special issues or concerns. By carefully evaluating each project individually, we eliminate unnecessary delays, minimize changes in the middle of jobs and most importantly, maintain the integrity of our pricing.
