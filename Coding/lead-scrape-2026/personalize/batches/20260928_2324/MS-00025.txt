Services We are an International Original Equipment Manufacture (OEM) specializing in custom automated industrial equipment and control systems. Machine Building Machine build and design is out main specialty. Machine designs vary from customer to customer and are all built to suit the customers specs or needs. Out design services include... 3D mechanical engineering and design Electrical controls design and programming Motion control design and programming Pneumatic system Hydraulic system Robotic Handling Machine Automation We're able to supply PLC programming for many different suppliers; although we excel in Allen Bradley, Rs Logix. About Us With over 2 decades of service to the general industry, Fortune 500 companies, and research departments, out experience ranges from press controls, filling equipment, rotary and robotic welders, mass spectrometers used for leak detection, and a wide range of data acquisition and test equipment.
