GoalFinn - Digital Transformation Consulting Digital Transformation That Actually Works GoalFinn exists because of a simple observation: most technology consulting gets it backward. Our Story Twenty-five years ago, Kevin Armstrong was a technology leader watching enterprises spend millions on transformation initiatives that failed to transform anything. The pattern was always the same—start with the technology, chase industry trends, follow vendor roadmaps, hope for business results. It rarely worked. So he started doing the opposite. Start with the business outcome. Work backward to the technology. Apply first principles instead of copying what worked for someone else. Design programs so one investment creates cascading value across the organization—multiplier effects, not diminishing returns. For two decades, Kevin refined this approach across industries and company sizes. He mentored leaders in the methodology. He proved, again and again, that outcome-first beats tool-first. That strategic alignment turns $1 invested into $10 of realized value. That technology should serve business results, never the other way around. GoalFinn was founded to bring that methodology—now called the Catalyst Framework—to enterprises that are tired of transformation theater and ready for transformation that actually works.
