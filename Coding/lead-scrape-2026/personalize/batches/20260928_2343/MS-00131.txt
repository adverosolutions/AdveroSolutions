Home - A.S. Webster I've always written, whether it was stories when I was young, field logs and documentation throughout my working life, and more stories as ideas came to me over the years. Now that I have some time, I'm going to try and organize these into something that can be shared. The stories, the doodles that went with them, the world building images to set place, the maps, and the other detritus of creation. There will also be photographs. Ones I've taken for inspiration for specific content, and others that were just too good to pass by. And swamps. I always love a good swamp. There's a story in those waters. Because I travel, I want to document some of my trips. These will always be written after the fact. I want to capture some of the details down for myself more than anything else. A reminder of where I've been and what I've done. Since every trip in the foreseeable future will be a birding trip of one sort or another, I'll include the relevant birding details.
