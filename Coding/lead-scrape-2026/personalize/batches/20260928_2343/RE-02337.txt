Start Your Search Our Story Altair’s foundations were built on a legacy of excellence in the Minneapolis search market. After beginning his career in professional services and business advisory, Shawn Woessner joined Woessner Associates in 2005, partnering with his father to build one of the region’s premier boutique firms. Their reputation for delivering best-in-class C-suite leadership led to the firm’s acquisition by a Top Ten global search firm in 2010. Following the acquisition, Shawn was tapped to lead the firm s Minneapolis office, eventually spending thirteen years as a senior leader across two of the world s largest search organizations. Shawn founded Altair to integrate global sophistication with the high-touch accountability of his boutique roots. While his pedigree includes serving the Fortune 100, he focuses on partnering with small- and mid-market organizations, family-owned businesses, and private equity firms. In these environments, a single leadership hire has a direct and profound impact on enterprise value. We believe success is not just about finding elite talent; it is about meticulous alignment with your business strategy and culture. At Altair, we have eliminated the structural gap often found in larger firms, where the partner who initiates the search hands the execution off to junior associates. At Altair, your search is managed with absolute continuity. Shawn remains personally immersed in every phase of the engagement, from the initial strategic intake and original research to the final negotiations. By performing the sourcing and candidate generation directly, we ensure no nuance is lost in translation. This hands-on approach allows us to provide unfiltered market intelligence and a slate of candidates who do not just meet requirements, but embody your strategic vision.
