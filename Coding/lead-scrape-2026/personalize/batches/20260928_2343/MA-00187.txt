About Slacker Media Group 0 Who we are Slacker Media Group: Amplifying Culture, Defining Moments Slacker Media Group is a creative powerhouse dedicated to curating unforgettable experiences at the intersection of music, lifestyle, and entertainment. As the visionary force behind Slacker University, It’s A 2000s Party, Sploinky Rave, and other cutting-edge event brands, we craft high-energy, culture-defining moments that bring music lovers together. From legendary throwback parties to face-melting bass music events, we empower DJs, artists, and creatives by giving them a stage to thrive. With an uncompromising passion for sound, style, and community, Slacker Media Group isn’t just throwing parties - we’re shaping the future of live entertainment. Join the movement. Stay loud, stay legendary. Our Origins Slacker Media Group was founded by Ross Friedman, an entrepreneur who began his career in the music industry at just 16 years old. As a young DJ, Friedman sought to create a nightclub-style experience for his high school peers, leading him to rent the iconic Middle East Downstairs in Cambridge, MA, for a large-scale, all-ages spring event. His inaugural production drew over 400 attendees, marking the beginning of a career in live entertainment that has since expanded his event concepts to more than 50 cities across the United States.
