PRESS RELEASE | WHY DOEREN MAYHEW | SERVICE OFFERINGS | FAQS | MAKE A PAYMENT Press Release November 17, 2025 Doeren Mayhew, a national assurance, tax and advisory firm, has acquired McMurray, Fox Associates, PLLC (McMurray Fox), effective Nov. 10, 2025. This transaction adds offices in Hendersonville and Gallatin, Tennessee, as well as three principals and 15 professionals to Doeren Mayhew’s team, while broadening its local Nashville practice with audit capabilities. “Our commitment to strategic growth is guided by the evolving needs of our clients and the opportunities in front of us,” says Chad Anschuetz , CEO of Doeren Mayhew Advisors, LLC. “The integration of McMurray Fox expands our tax expertise and establishes a local audit presence. This move strengthens our capabilities and reinforces our promise to deliver measurable value to our clients throughout the region.” For more than 50 years, McMurray Fox has built a reputation for delivering top-tier bookkeeping, tax, audit and business advisory solutions to the businesses and individuals in and around Middle Tennessee with a focus on the construction, professional services, real estate, nonprofit and manufacturing industries. “It’s an exciting time for us here at Doeren Mayhew,” said Bob Yates , Doeren Mayhew’s greater Nashville managing principal. “The addition of the McMurray Fox team is a real turning point for us. We’re laying a strong foundation for sustainable growth in the area. Combining our technical skills, specifically in the real estate and construction sectors, will give us a competitive advantage.” “As a firm, we feel like our values and vision closely align with what Doeren Mayhew represents,” said Jamie McMurry , managing partner at McMurray Fox. “We are excited to be able to offer our clients, and our Sumner County community, a more robust suite of services that comes with a top 50 accounting firm .
