Founded in 2003 by David Baumgardner, a graduate of Ball State University’s Landscape Architecture Program, Whispering Creek was built on a love for the outdoors and a commitment to excellence in design and service. After working on high-end residential and commercial projects in Aspen and Vail, Colorado, David returned to the Miami Valley inspired to bring that same balance of beauty, quality, and precision to his own community. At Whispering Creek, we believe great landscapes start with great communication. From the first consultation to the final walkthrough, we listen closely, collaborate openly, and treat every project as a true partnership. Our team combines artistry with technical skill to create outdoor environments that reflect each client’s vision while standing the test of time. We take pride in using only premium materials from trusted partners such as Unilock, Cambridge, Techo-Bloc, Oberfields, and County, ensuring lasting strength and timeless beauty in every design. As certified installers for Aqua Technics Pools and River Pools, we also bring leading innovation and durability to our fiberglass pool installations. Our craftsmen bring over 50 years of combined experience, uniting precision construction with creativity and care. As proud members of the Builders Exchange of Dayton, the Home Builders Association of Dayton, the National Federation of Independent Business, and the Better Business Bureau, we hold ourselves to the highest standards of professionalism and integrity. At Whispering Creek, exceptional customer service, clear communication, and enduring craftsmanship are not just promises. They are the foundation of everything we do.
