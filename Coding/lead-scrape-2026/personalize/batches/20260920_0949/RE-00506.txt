Hello, I’m Quai Wentt, and I founded the Purple Squirrel Network to address the disparities in tech recruitment and help underrepresented software engineers gain the skills and opportunities they deserve. Through our Leetcode classes, I teach engineers data structures and algorithms, preparing them to excel in technical interviews. Our mission goes beyond just training; we strive to create pathways for talented engineers from diverse backgrounds to break into top tech companies. By joining our network, engineers will not only gain technical expertise but will also have access to job referrals from companies committed to improving their DEI efforts. We believe that by equipping underrepresented engineers with the right skills and connecting them to the right opportunities, we can help change the face of tech and build a more inclusive, innovative industry. Ready to join? Apply Now Become a Patron Frequently asked questions Have questions about the Purple Squirrel Network? Find answers to common queries below. Who is the group for? This group is designed for software engineers from underrepresented groups (BIPOC, women/nonbinary individuals). It’s not for those completely new to coding; it’s for those who already know how to code but need to strengthen their knowledge of data structures and algorithms. What is the format of the group? The group meets three days a week for one-hour sessions. We start by working on a Leetcode problem, then I discuss the brute-force and optimal solutions, and I walk through the code line by line. Sessions are interactive, and there’s time for questions at the end. What will I learn? We focus on identifying and solving problems based on common patterns found in data structure and algorithm interviews. You will learn how to apply these patterns effectively and implement solutions in real-world interview scenarios. What time does the group meet? The group meets three days a week: Mon/Wed/Fri at 2pm EST / 11am PST Sessions are recorded if you can’t attend live.
