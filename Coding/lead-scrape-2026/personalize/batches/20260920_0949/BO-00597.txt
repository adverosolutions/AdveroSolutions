Our Team Camellia Tax Advisors 0 Our Team Amy Nadler, the founder of Camellia Tax Advisors, is a CPA and Atlanta native. She has been a tax professional for over 25 years and is a member of the Georgia Society of CPAs (GSCPA) and the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA). In addition to her role in leading the firm, she is a board member of Savvy Cyber Kids and a former board member and treasurer of the Jewish Fertility Foundation. She lives in Dunwoody with her husband, Doug, her son, Aiden, her daughter, Hannah, and her dogs, Buster and Miles. Angela McNichol is a Certified Public Accountant with more than 20 years of experience in the tax profession. She is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) and the Florida Institute of CPAs (FICPA). Throughout her career, she has been dedicated to helping individuals and businesses navigate complex tax matters. She is passionate about building strong, lasting relationships and believes understanding each client’s unique goals is the foundation of providing excellent service. She graduated from Emory University with a Bachelor’s in Economics and lived in Atlanta for over 10 years before settling down in Orlando, Florida, with her husband and two sons. Stephanie Beecham grew up in Birmingham, AL and lived in Savannah, New Orleans, and Denver before falling in love with Atlanta's charm and settling there. She is passionate about client care and has experience in customer interaction through a small business lens. She is the mother of two sweet children, and enjoys hiking with her family, as well as practicing yoga and weightlifting. Amy chose the name “Camellia” because Camellia sinensis is a tea plant, and tea has always been an important theme in her life, from celebrating life events with friends over tea to sharing afternoon tea with co-workers. Tea also symbolizes comfort and soothing, which are the feelings that we hope to invoke in others through our work.
