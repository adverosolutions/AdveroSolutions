Premier Alliance Consulting offers a broad range of services to help our clients secure a sound financial freedom which is our number one goal. Premier Alliance Consulting, LLC was founded by Takesha M. Brown, CPA in 2017. Takesha has 25 years of accounting experience including 12 years of public accounting experience within a big 4 accounting firm and 13 years of public company controllership, SEC reporting and CFO services. Her industry experience includes healthcare, insurance, technology, telecommunications and energy. Premier Alliance Consulting, LLC provides businesses with accounting, financial reporting and finance services that help them analyze their financial position in order to make important business decisions.
