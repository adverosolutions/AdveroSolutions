About Us - Green Monster Clean About Green Monster Clean Patrick, owner and operator of Green Monster Clean, has over 25 years of hands-on experience cleaning carpet, upholstery, tile, and grout. He takes pride in his work, taking his time and personalizing the service YOU need. He doesn’t pressure you into unnecessary services and has been known to decline work when he feels the results won’t make his client happy. Licensed and bondable, Patrick is honest, trustworthy, prompt, and reliable. The Green Monster Clean Story During Patrick’s career, he noticed that the constant exposure to the fumes from conventional cleaning agents (at other companies) would cause light-headedness and weakness. After researching the chemical compounds, he realized that these same fumes may have caused him long-term damage to his lungs and liver. So, when he started his own company in 2008, he decided to use premium green cleaning agents, all which are certified by the Carpet Rug Institute and/or Green Seal. They are also considered Asthma Allergy friendly, bio-degradable, and will not leave a residue or odor. These green products are effective AND safe for you, your children, pets, and the environment.
