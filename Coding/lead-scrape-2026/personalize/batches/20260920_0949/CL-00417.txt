Meet The Spaldings Brian Spalding created S S Commercial Cleaning 17 years ago after he realized there was such a need by local businesses for cleaning services. When he founded the company, Brian was a single dad to Mya (now 25, married to Sam, and a speech therapist) and wanted to develop a second income stream and ensure a secure financial future for the both of them. A few years later, he met his wife Rachel, a nurse, and they have now been married for 15 years. Their passion for serving others through a family-owned business using the Golden Rule and some old-school values has led to significant partnerships. Brian s commitment to providing high-quality cleaning services is unparalleled, as he treats every business as if it were his own. Rachel brings business savvy as she is a successful, doctorly prepared nurse leader in the region and keeps Brian s technical side in check. This duo is committed to the values of Integrity, Service, and Excellence in all they do!
