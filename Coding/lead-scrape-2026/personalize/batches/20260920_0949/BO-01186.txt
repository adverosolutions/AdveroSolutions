About Us | Kelly, Sparber, White | Latrobe Accounting Services ABOUT US Kelly, Sparber, White & Associates, LLC is a full-service certified public accounting firm located in Latrobe, Pennsylvania. Our Firm has over 60 years of experience and success behind us, and offers a wide range of services to both businesses and individuals. Tax Accounting Professionals Ronald P. Kelly founded the Firm in 1962 primarily as a tax practice, and Edward Sparber joined the Firm in 1984. Maryann White came on board in 2001 and took over the ownership and management of the Firm in 2003. Ron and Ed have since retired, but their commitment to client service continues on. Maryann White-Helfferich CPA/ABV – Partner Maryann joined the firm in 2001 and became the managing partner in 2003. She graduated summa cum laude from the University of Pittsburgh at Johnstown with a Bachelor of Arts in Business/Economics with a dual concentration in Accounting and Finance. She is a Certified Public Accountant and is Accredited in Business Valuation. Prior to joining the Firm, she worked for the "Big-Eight" accounting firm, Price Waterhouse, and Clifton Gunderson LLP, the 11th largest public accounting firm in the country, where she specialized in business valuation. Maryann received her license as a Certified Public Accountant in 1991 and became Accredited in Business Valuation in 2000. She is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and the Pennsylvania Institute of Certified Public Accountants. Maryann specializes in the valuation of small, closely held businesses for purposes of mergers/acquisitions, estate and gift taxes, succession planning, and shareholder transactions. She has qualified as an expert witness in business valuation in Westmoreland, Indiana, Fayette, and Cambria counties. Maryann also specializes in taxes and business planning. Maryann serves as treasurer for the Greater Latrobe / Laurel Valley Community Chamber of Commerce and the Latrobe Area Historical Society. She was the founding President of the Greater Latrobe Partners in Education Foundation.
