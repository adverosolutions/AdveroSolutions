About Common Sense Solutions About Comprehensive and Compassionate Solutions Who We Are Since 2001, Common Sense Solutions has provided daily money management services, fiduciary services and life management services to those who need help managing their personal affairs. We work with seniors, high net-worth families and professionals to relieve the burdens associated with handling financial and life-planning issues that typically become more difficult with age. Common Sense Solutions was founded by Vivian Wright, a Certified Trust Financial Advisor, Registered Financial Gerontologist and Daily Money Manager. Vivian has 38 years of experience managing trusts, estates and other financial assets. The company has steadily grown over the past nearly two decades and currently manages approximately $300 million in client assets. Common Sense Solutions combines the internal controls and safeguards of a bank trust department with honesty, accountability and confidentiality. We take our role as a fiduciary seriously and always remain focused on serving the best interests of our clients. Whether you or a loved one needs assistance, you can trust the team of professionals at Common Sense Solutions to take care of whatever you require. From balancing checkbooks to paying bills to managing cash flow and coordinating taxes to coordinating every detail of a move to a new home, we tailor our services to meet each client’s specific situation.
