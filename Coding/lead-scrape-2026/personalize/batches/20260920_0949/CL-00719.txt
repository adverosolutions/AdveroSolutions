About Ichiban Cleaning 0 LET’S GET ACQUAINTED Since its founding in 2003 by Ellen and Albert Sanders, Ichiban Cleaning (pronounced "ē-chē-bän") has been providing Central Florida and the surrounding areas with top-quality cleaning services. What began as a successful residential and medical office cleaning service established by Ellen in the late 1980s has blossomed into a second-generation, family-run business. Today, Ichiban Cleaning continues to uphold its commitment to exceptional service, catering to the specific needs of clients in the medical, legal, technology, aviation industries and more. The Spirit of OMOTENASHI Omotenashi (pronounced oh-moh-te-nah-she) , a cherished Japanese principle of hospitality, centers around warmly welcoming and caring for others with genuine kindness and attentiveness. Often characterized as "wholehearted hospitality" or "selfless service," Omotenashi is guided by three core principles: meticulous attention to detail, foreseeing the needs of others, and surpassing expectations to ensure a memorable experience.
