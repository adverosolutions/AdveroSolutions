About Us – The Dingman Company Menu Skip to primary navigation Skip to main content Our Team Bruce Dingman The Dingman Company Of Counsel Emeritus bruce@dingman.com Since joining The Dingman Company in 1986, Bruce has led and completed a wide range of executive search assignments for clients across diversified industries. In 1993, he became President of The Dingman Company, where he worked extensively with organizations in financial services, consumer products, manufacturing, travel, hospitality, aviation, and the nonprofit sector—including Christian organizations and universities. After decades of leadership, Bruce has transitioned from President and now serves in an Emeritus role with The Dingman Company, which operates as a doing-business-as (DBA) of Ardent Search, Inc. Day-to-day leadership and operations are overseen by his longtime associate, Tim Bernstein, under Ardent Search, Inc. tim@ardentsearchco.com . While no longer involved in daily management, Bruce remains a valued advisor and continues to offer perspective shaped by his many years in executive search. Prior to joining The Dingman Company, Bruce spent nearly 20 years in the hotel, food service, and food distribution industries. Bruce and his wife, Janet, live in Southern California and are the parents of three adult children and seven grandchildren. Bruce is a proud veteran, having served in the United States Military. Bruce and Janet are members of Calvary Community Church in Westlake Village, California.
