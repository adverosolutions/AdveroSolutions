At Vieira & Associates, CPAs P.C. our mission is to build relationships with our clients by understanding their environment and identifying their business and financial needs. We strive to provide quality services that will assist our clients in achieving their business and personal financial goals. Core Values We believe our core values hold us accountable in fulfilling our mission, our commitment to quality, building trust in public, and problem solving. Our core values define how we conduct ourselves and interact with our colleagues and clients. We believe integrity, ethical behavior, and professionalism is key in our profession. Integrity & Ethical Behavior: We adhere to the highest standards of ethical and professional conduct. We act in a manner that is fair, honest, and proper. Client Satisfaction: We build client relationships with our satisfactory services. We believe we assist our clients in promoting their financial success. Firm, Staff, and Client Success: We strive to maintain clients, who value our services. We believe our services enhances a client’s profitability or financial success which ensures our own. Valuing People: We maintain a firm culture that attracts and retains good people. We offer a healthy balance between work and personal life. We also recognize and reward outstanding performance of our employees. About Us Vieira & Associates CPAs, P.C., a professional corporation located in Endicott, New York, was formed November 29, 2005. The firm consists of four principals, staff accountants, one bookkeeper, and one administrative assistant with offices in Endicott, New York. We are a small accounting firm in Broome County that offers a variety services at competitive rates. Because we are a smaller firm, we pride ourselves in building strong relationships with our clients while offering quality services to suit their needs.
