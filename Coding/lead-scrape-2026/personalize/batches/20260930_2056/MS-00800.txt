Cobalt Technology was founded in 2016 by two friends, Brandon and Matt, under the name Cobalt Ridge. Starting off with no money in the bank, they managed to land a few network overhaul jobs for two churches in Wichita Falls and a business in San Antonio. Not really knowing what to do next with the company, Brandon started to realize that internet service in the rural areas surrounding Wichita Falls was nearly non-existent, unlike what he was used to in The Woodlands where he grew up and graduated High School. Starting with just an idea, the two founders of the fledgling company put their heads together to see if it was possible to send high-quality internet service wirelessly over several miles, since the cost of laying cables was out of reach. It turns out, it is possible! After 8 months of planning, researching, and number-crunching, Brandon and Matt used the money they had gotten from the first few network overhaul jobs to purchase some wireless radios, antennas, cables, routers, and a 1-year lease on a 440ft communications tower in Wichita Falls. The original goal was to get just 10 customers to pay the bills so Brandon could have internet at his house, but very quickly the calls started coming in, and they didn’t stop!
