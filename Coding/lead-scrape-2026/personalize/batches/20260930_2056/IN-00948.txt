Individual Self-Insured Company Management | The Lawson Group Skip to content Self-insurance overview Who Is Self-Insurance For? Individual self-insurance isn’t a fit for every employer—and that’s okay. This is typically a good match for organizations that: Want more control and transparency than the standard insurance market provides Have the financial stability to handle claims payments and reserves Are willing to invest in strong safety and return-to-work practices Understand that self-insurance is a long-term commitment (claims can stay open for years) To delve even further, check out our Self-Insurance 101 crash course .
