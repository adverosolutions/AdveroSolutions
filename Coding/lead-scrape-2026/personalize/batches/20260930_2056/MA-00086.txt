About - Live Page Marketing Skip to content About LivePage Marketing Marketing for medspas and plastic surgeons, run like a partner. Since 2008, Live Page Marketing has helped aesthetic practices build stronger websites, improve their search presence, create better content, and turn more patient interest into booked appointments. We're an experienced team that brings strategy, design, content, search, paid media, social media, technology, and tracking together under one plan. That means fewer handoffs and one team that understands how all the pieces are supposed to work together. We Thought We Could Do Better... Live Page Marketing was founded on a simple idea: aesthetic practices deserve better than generic packages and confusing reports — they deserve a real partner who stays involved. Medspas and plastic surgery practices operate in a competitive, highly visual, trust-driven market. Patients do not make decisions based on a single ad or Google search. They research treatments, compare providers, read reviews, look at before-and-after photos, visit social profiles, and study websites before they ever contact a practice. Your marketing needs to support that entire decision—not just generate another click. We build around your practice, your market, your patients, and what you are realistically trying to accomplish. Sometimes that means a full marketing program. Sometimes it means fixing the website, strengthening search visibility, or improving the parts of the patient journey where interest is being lost. The goal is not to sell you everything we do. It is to identify what matters most and do that work well. Our marketing approach Start with the patient journey We plan marketing around what a patient wants to know, what they need to feel confident, and what they are likely to do next. Every website page, search campaign, social post, email, and advertisement should have a clear job within that journey. Build authority through useful content Aesthetics practices are often chosen because they provide the most credible answer to a question the patient is already asking. We create content that explains treatments clearly, sets sensible expectations, addresses concerns, and shows why provider evaluation matters. We do not believe in exaggerated claims or making people feel worse about themselves to sell a service.
