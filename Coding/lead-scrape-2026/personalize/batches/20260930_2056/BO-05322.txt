Our Values Communication Teamwork Ownership Family Continuous Education Trust Meet Our Team TOUCH Rolando Lopez, CPA Managing Partner CEO Rolando Lopez, CPA Managing Partner CEO A native of the Tampa Bay area since 2003, Rolando attended the University of Tampa’s Sykes College of Business. After receiving his Bachelor of Science in Accounting, he remained a UT Spartan while obtaining his Masters in Accounting. Rolando is a licensed CPA in the State of Florida and began his career as a public auditor for Mayer Hoffman McCann P.C., a national public accounting firm in the US, serving both private and public clients in a wide array of industries. He later went on to work for Jabil Circuit, Inc., one of the largest multi-national Fortune 500 companies in the US, traveling internationally to manufacturing plants where he provided effective and efficient solutions to operational and financial processes. Through these experiences, Rolando developed a strong foundation of accounting principles and operational strategies which he has transitioned into his practice and clients. He is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) and the Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA).
