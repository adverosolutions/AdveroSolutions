About Us - Rohloff Associates Financial Leadership Rooted in Authentic Partnerships We operate as a seamless extension of your executive team, delivering the proactive, year-round financial guidance you need to confidently steer your company’s future. Our Story Rewriting the Rules of Traditional Accounting Nearly two decades ago, we recognized a fundamental flaw in the standard CPA model: it prioritized looking backward over planning forward. We set out to build a genuinely relational firm. By intentionally dismantling the billable hour in favor of a fixed-price partnership, we removed the friction of traditional accounting and created a system for financial advisory that prioritizes honest, ongoing conversations with the founders and families we serve. Knowledge over formulas We discard one-size-fits-all templates and focus on building a deep, nuanced understanding of the unique operational realities of your business. Uncompromising Transparency Our predictable pricing model ensures our doors are always open, completely eliminating the anxiety of surprise invoices. Human-Centric Strategy We integrate behavioral insights to ensure your financial strategy aligns perfectly with the people actually driving your company.
