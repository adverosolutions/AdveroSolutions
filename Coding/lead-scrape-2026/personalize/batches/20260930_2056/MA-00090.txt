About Polymyth About POLYMYTH Polymyth Productions LLC is a full-service video production company based on the north side of Chicago, IL. We are an interdisciplinary team of artists and engineers, brought together by the belief that stories connect all people. Polymyth was founded in 2011 by Dan Finnen, Derek Cox, and Darik Hall. Over the years, John Sexton and Carter Lodwick served as members, each elevating Polymyth with their skills during their time with the company. Currently, Polymyth is owned and run by Derek Cox, Jonathan Broeker, Aleks Vasic, and Dan Finnen. Notice those black and white photos in the headers on all our pages? They feature many of the talented crew members we bring on for every production. Making great videos requires the talents of everyone from oil painters to 3D animators, and we have an extensive network in the Chicago area ready to make your vision a reality. Check out our work and our capabilities to see how we can help tell your story. If you’re interested in joining our team at Polymyth, contact us to join our roster. He also received an Emmy for his work on a PBS documentary.
