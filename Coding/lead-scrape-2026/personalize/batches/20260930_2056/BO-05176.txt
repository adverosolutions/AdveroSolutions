About 0 About Us Payroll Boutique is a dedicated payroll company providing comprehensive payroll processing and support services for start-up and small-to-medium-sized businesses in Sonoma County and throughout California. With every client, our goal is to simplify the payroll process and make it as easy as possible for you. By taking care of your payroll, we free up your time and allow you to focus on what you love and do best—growing your business! A sister company of Denegri Associates, Payroll Boutique was born out of our understanding of the evolving needs of our clients and our strong commitment to providing exceptional services. Since its founding in 1986, Denegri Associates has been regarded for its expertise in personalized bookkeeping, accounting, and tax services in Sonoma County. Their dedication to outstanding service and approachability has earned them a stellar reputation for consistently going above and beyond for their clients. They’re not merely accountants; they serve as trusted advisors and friends. With the establishment of Payroll Boutique, we embraced the opportunity to bring our vision to life. Just as Denegri Associates never settled for a one-size-fits-all approach, we channeled that same commitment to customization and "boutique" service into the creation of Payroll Boutique.
