Very3 Technology Consultants | About Very3 About Very3 Very3 specializes in web design, web hosting, custom web applications, search engine optimization, and Google Ads and Google Maps management services. We also provide legacy codebase management services and learning design solutions for small to medium-sized businesses, schools, and universities. Very3 Technology Consultants started in 1991 as Phoenix Graphics a diminutive two-person enterprise operating from a modest space equivalent to a walk-in closet. Between painting murals for corporate break rooms and hacking out Amiga and Tandy CoCo software, we found a niche writing assembly code for the Motorola 68B09E processor , which included drivers for printers, scanners, and graphical plotters. Over time, we began to offer custom programming for CNC machining platforms, digital signage controllers, process camera lighting systems, and photo/video camera systems. In 1996, we rebranded as antiGrafx and branched out into graphic design, custom web applications, and e-commerce solutions, floating on the dot-com bubble into the late 90s and early 2000s. The name was, well, off-putting (to be kind), so in 2006, we changed it to Very3 . Over the past few years, we have experienced significant growth, and in 2022, we moved out of our dark, brooding, post-punk-garage-band phase of being just "Very3" and formed "Very3 Technology Consultants LLC." Today, Very3 Technology Consultants designs and hosts websites for clients across the globe and provides hands-on, managed technology solutions for small to medium-sized businesses, K-12 schools, colleges, and universities. At Very3, it's our business to serve your business. We are committed to providing our clients with personalized service and high-quality technology solutions that exceed expectations.
