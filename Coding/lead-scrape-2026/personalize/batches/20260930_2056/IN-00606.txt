Expert Consulting Services - Smola Consulting Skip to content About Us What We Do Smola Consulting is a full service benefit and wellness consulting firm located in Rochester, NY. We offer review, analysis, and sound recommendations of current benefit programs, in addition to implementation lead or support for new programs. As our client, Smola Consulting will review and recommend industry leading programs as well as assist your Human Resource team with day-to-day issues, including billing, claims, membership, education, open enrollment, and fostering a culture of well-being. Our Mission Our mission is to educate and consult with our clients to ensure that they are making the best benefit decisions possible. Smola Consulting strives to be an extension of the organizations we work with by becoming part of their corporate culture and personally knowledgeable of their insurance needs. Our Philosophy Smola Consulting believes in a “Pay It Forward” philosophy. The hope is that the recipient of the good deed will pay forward a good deed to others, and that this philosophy will spread. Company founder, Steve Smola, believes that we as a company and each employee can support organizations and charities that contribute to human health, happiness and hope. Who We Are Smola Consulting is comprised of experienced healthcare professionals. Our team has over 200 years of combined experience in the insurance industry. Our experience ranges from medical plans, ancillary plans such as Life, and Disability, as well as wellness and health promotion. We have experience working with all major carriers, both local and national. We have lead projects such as, dependent audits, government opportunities for available funding (ERRP), and selecting a vendor for on-line enrollment. We are industry leaders in developing and supporting ongoing consortium solutions. Our extensive knowledge and experience enables us to coordinate and implement creative solutions for our clients. We care about the success of our clients and are also always available to assist with day-to-day issues, including billing, claims, membership, and education. We are trusted and accessible consultants complemented with a broad range of products and services.
