It Makes Cents was founded with a mission to empower the small business community by providing affordable accounting resources. Our goal is to help small businesses establish effective accounting systems that facilitate growth and success. Bianca Tellez - Accountant Bianca, a native West Texan and proud mother, is a graduate of the University of Texas of the Permian Basin (UTPB) with a strong passion for accounting. With over 14 years of experience spanning the private and public sectors, non-profits, oil gas, and healthcare industries, Bianca has developed a deep affinity for the small business community. Her expertise lies in collaborating closely with small business owners, guiding them through the complexities of accounting, and helping them turn their entrepreneurial dreams into reality. Bianca is dedicated to merging operations and accounting in a way that is comprehensible for business owners. She not only supports small businesses with their accounting needs but also emphasized the importance of bookkeeping and financial education. At It Makes Cents, we are committed to empowering small businesses with the financial tools and knowledge they need to thrive.
