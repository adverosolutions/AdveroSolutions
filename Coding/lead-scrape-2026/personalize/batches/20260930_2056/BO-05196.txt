Our Team | Resolution Accounting Skip to content Our Team Our Team reelfiredesign 2020-05-13T15:05:19-04:00 We are a team comprised of women with diverse backgrounds and educational experience that ranges from bookkeeping and accounting to senior level advisory services. There was never any intention to be an all women firm – it just evolved that way as most of our people are mothers who appreciate the work-life flexibility we offer and support. We find that our respect for their commitment to family and their appreciation for flexible work hours translates into pleasant and dedicated people who serve our clients. Resolution Accounting has been offering bookkeeping, accounting, and CFO advisory services since 1999. The firm was started by Gina Edwards, who is a native of the Philadelphia area, from Radnor. After graduating from Duke University she returned to the area and has lived here ever since. Gina’s passion is to work with small business owners to help them be more profitable and stable; freeing them up to do what they do best- interact with their customers and build sales. Business owners appreciate the high quality of services Resolution offers, giving them increased peace of mind with the “backside of their business”. Gina Edwards CEO Gina Edwards Gina is a graduate of Duke University with a degree in Economics and has served as CEO of Resolution Accounting since 1998. She has held various positions in the industry that include Accountant, Controller, and CFO. From working in the field for over 25 years, Gina offers a depth of knowledge and experience in Accounting and Financial Management for Small Businesses.
