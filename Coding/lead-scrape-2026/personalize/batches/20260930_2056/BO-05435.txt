About Davis Bookkeeping Services | Trusted Bookkeeping for Small Businesses Skip to content Bookkeeping That Pays Off Helping solopreneurs step out of overwhelm — and into growth About Our Story Davis Bookkeeping Services was established with a mission to provide solopreneurs with high-quality bookkeeping support essential for their success. After spending over 15 years in the corporate world, I observed the vast resources readily available to large companies. In contrast, solopreneurs often lack these resources, which can hinder their ability to achieve their dreams or lead to burnt out as they try to manage all aspects of their business alone. At Davis Bookkeeping Services, we are guided by our core values of integrity, accuracy, and personalized service. We pride ourselves on offering tailored solutions that meet the unique needs of each client, helping them streamline their financial management and focus on growing their business. We’re building a future where solopreneurs have access to expert bookkeeping that drives clarity, confidence, and sustainable growth. About Monica Davis As the Founder of Davis Bookkeeping Services, my mission is to positively impact the lives of small business owners and their families. My accounting journey began in high school and culminated in earning a Bachelor of Science in Accounting from UCF in 2001. After nearly 20 years working in public / private Accounting and the corporate world, I faced a pivotal moment in my career. In May 2020, I was laid off from my corporate job due to COVID-19. As a single mother of two boys at the time, reentering an unstable job market after 14 years with the same employer was frightening. This led me back to working with small businesses, where I rediscovered my passion. I found a renewed sense of purpose leveraging my experience to support small businesses, which inspired me to launch Davis Bookkeeping Services. I reside in Orlando with my husband and our blended family of four children. To relax, I enjoy a good cup of coffee with family and friends or sneaking in a refreshing nap whenever I can!
