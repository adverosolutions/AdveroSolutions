About - East Side Staffing About East Side Staffing East Side Staffing is a premier, boutique recruitment firm focused on the permanent placement of Human Resources professionals. Our goal is to ensure our clients and candidates receive personalized attention, exceptional service and a consultative approach. We recognize that you demand the best from your recruitment firm. We, too, have incredibly high standards and aim to exceed your expectations. Our goal is to make your recruitment process a positive experience. Some typical Human Resources roles we work on are: SVP/VP Human Resources Chief People Office/CHRO/Chief Talent Officer Senior HR Business Partners Director, People Culture People Leaders Business Professionals with expertise in Organizational Development, Diversity Equity Inclusion, Talent Management, Total Rewards, Compensation, Training and Development, Leadership Development, HR Data Experts, Benefits, HRIS, Campus Recruitment, Talent Acquisition and more Titles, roles and demands within HR/People/Talent are always evolving. About The Owner Laura Mazzullo is the Founder and Owner of East Side Staffing, a boutique recruitment firm focused on the placement of Human Resources professionals. Since 2003, Laura has maintained a successful career in Recruitment working in major markets such as Chicago, Illinois, Stamford, Connecticut and New York, New York. She has always embodied an energetic, entrepreneurial spirit and an extraordinary desire for achieving excellence in recruitment. Laura recognized a need in the marketplace for her HR Recruitment expertise and she decided to create a recruitment firm offering a personalized service for both candidates and clients. In 2013, East Side Staffing was born! Launching a recruitment firm has been a dream of mine for many years. I felt there was a need in the marketplace for a boutique recruitment firm focused on making the recruitment process a positive experience for both candidates and clients. Ensuring the candidates I represent feel valued, respected, helped and heard has been a main motivator for embarking on this venture. Operating in a boutique setting has allowed me to focus consultatively on a few targeted searches at a time, offering clients a personalized service resulting in deeper relationships and successful placements for their team. Working in recruitment provides me with enormous satisfaction
