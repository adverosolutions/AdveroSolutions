Chairside CNY Welcome to CHAIRSIDE CNY We are a dental staffing agency providing temporary and permanent placement services throughout Central New York. Our business is built on what we know – the dental profession, and our service is delivered where our roots are deep. Thirty years of experience gives us that advantage. Dental Professionals Job Enrollment Form Dental Practices Practices Enrollment Form At CHAIRSIDE CNY our goal is to advocate for dental practices as they seek staffing solutions and dental professionals as they seek employment. We understand that the loss of staff, whether unexpected or planned, can seriously undermine production standards and compromise patient care. We recognize too, that today’s changing economies and complex lifestyles often require innovative approaches to how, where and when we work. Whether you are a dental provider or a dental professional, we are dedicated to meeting your needs. Our service includes the placement of Dental Hygienists, Dental Assistants and Office Staff. At CHAIRSIDE, our professionals are vetted and guaranteed. Our fees to providers are structured to incorporate a working interview, assuring complete satisfaction. There is never a fee for dental professionals. We are eager to earn your trust and always happy to provide references from satisfied clients. © 2021 Chairside CNY LLC
