ABOUT US Who We Are P J Vet Staffing Services is a Service-disabled Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) and Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB) specializing in federal staffing services. We help federal agencies, contractors, consulting firms (i.e., small- to large-sized), and small businesses by supplementing their program teams with top-tier professionals skilled at working throughout the entire talent acquisition lifecycle. We design our solutions to meet each client’s operational, budgetary, security clearance, and deadline goals. Investing in talent and resources to maintain critical projects is essential for any organization s success, growth, and development. As a company with abundant resources and expertise in staff augmentation, P J is positioned to help our clients acquire the best and brightest minds in the market. We believe in longstanding relationships between ourselves and our clients – as our clients are an essential part of our professional community. We recognize that organizations attain peak performance when they have the right people with the right skills.
