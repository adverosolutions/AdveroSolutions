Hardage Group 0 Professional and personalized service for both job-seekers and employers Since 1995 Founded in 1995, Hardage Group is a nationwide executive recruiting firm that provides placement services for professional level positions in engineering, operations management, human resources, safety, quality, and accounting disciplines. Hardage Group's expertise and experience is unparalleled in the metals (melting and metal forming), printing (packaging, publications, and commercial applications), and building products (flooring, windows doors, and kitchen/bath items) industries. Additionally, we have enjoyed success based on our established relationship with automotive suppliers and have gained a unique understanding of the demands of the automotive sector. The firm's professionals have earned certifications through a rigorous testing process which is implemented by the National Association of Personnel Services (NAPS). We comply with NAPS national ethical standards. We are committed to providing uncompromised quality and value in filling positions that meet both the candidate's needs the client's requirements. Hardage Group practices strict confidentiality in its efforts to protect the privacy of all parties. Hardage Group is an active member of NPAWorldwide, an international network of professional recruiters who interact with other independent owners of small to mid-sized companies. This partnership with NPAWorldwide provides Hardage Group with nationwide reach. Hardage Group's activities are conducted so as to not discriminate against any candidate because of age, race, sex, religion, handicap, veteran status, or national origin. Importantly, candidates are never charged a fee for our services.
