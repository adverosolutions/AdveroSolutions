About | STLRecruit top of page About STLRecruit STLRecruit was founded in 2023 with a mission to connect exceptional talent with client organizations seeking high-level expertise. Jeff Seeney, Owner Founder of STLRecruit Jeff graduated from the University of Missouri-St. Louis and immediately passed the CPA exam. He spent 6 years as a practicing CPA advising clients in complex litigation matters on commercial damages and business interruption insurance claims. He also advised clients on Mergers Acquisitions, tax, etc. While Jeff loved this work, he really didn t want to be deposed for a living. In 2000, having started college with the goal of getting into sales and marketing, Jeff transformed into a Business Development / Recruiter role with a Fortune 500 Staffing and Consulting company. After 5 years he helped establish and grow two St. Louis centric Executive Search and Consulting firms. He recruited and placed hundreds of permanent, interim and project professionals, primarily Manager to C Level in finance, accounting, supply chain/operations, IT Leadership, Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), digital transformation and operational excellence. From 2015 to 2023, Jeff worked in a national business development leadership role for a Top 30 Global Accounting and Consulting firm. He was instrumental in growing their Missouri practice from 110 to over 200 employees. In the latter years, he identified and oversaw the execution of several engagements related to ERP implementations (selection, re-implementation and optimization), accounting automation, acquisition integrations, operational excellence (lean implementations), shared services optimizations and providing interim leadership resources. Jeff never stopped networking passionately and helping executives and others in career transition. Having owned the domain STLRecruit.com since 2013, in 2023 Jeff realized a lifelong dream of starting his own firm, STLRecruit.
