That which we need most will be found where we least want to look . ~Carl Jung Meet the Coach I’m Amy Lee Miller — leadership coach, advisor, and speaker with a background in organizational psychology and over a decade of experience working closely with leaders across biotech, medtech, and diagnostics. I work with leaders on how they show up in the moments that matter—high-stakes conversations, decisions under pressure, and leading others with clarity. Leadership challenges aren’t always obvious at first. They tend to show up in smaller, everyday moments: Leaving a conversation thinking, “that’s not what I meant to say” • Over-explaining, softening, or hesitating instead of being direct • Stepping in too quickly instead of observing what’s actually happening • Communication gaps across teams or functions • Misalignment that isn’t being clearly addressed • Decisions that feel unclear or harder than they should be My work is grounded in organizational psychology and shaped by years of real-world experience in high-stakes hiring and leadership environments. That combination has built strong pattern recognition into how leaders think, communicate, and make decisions—especially when the pressure is high. I focus on helping leaders and teams close the gap between how they’re currently showing up and how they want to lead. My work includes: • Coaching leaders stepping into new or expanded roles • Strengthening communication and sharper thinking in real conversations • Helping teams get clear and aligned across priorities and expectations • Leadership alignment audits and hiring diagnostics to identify where friction is slowing performance • Workshops on decision-making, effective communication, emotional intelligence, and leadership development My approach is practical and actionable. We take real situations, break them down, and build better ways to handle them—so the next time you’re in that moment, you lead it with intention.
