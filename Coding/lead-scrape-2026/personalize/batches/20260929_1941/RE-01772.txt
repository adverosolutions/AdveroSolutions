Follow Discover all 4 employees Report this company About us Amanda Mintz Creative Management (AMCM) is a boutique recruitment and management consultancy that supports the world’s A-list brands, agencies, start-ups and talent. We help you navigate digital media, influencer marketing and the freelance economy; establish in-house brand and creative teams; curate and hire talent for projects—and cultivate your career. Founded in 2010 by industry insider Amanda Mintz, AMCM’s goal is to connect and empower creatives, and the businesses that engage them, equally—so they can transform brands, cultivate careers and shape the future. Our approach? Intuition and personal relationships matter as much as strategy. We are both thought partner and curator; creative recruiter and resource; advisor and talent agent. As a result, our clients span all creative industries, and start-ups to premier brands like Calvin Klein, Milk Makeup, Sephora, Sweetgreen and beyond. Above all, we believe in being real. Because before we are candidates or companies, we’re all unique individuals, who want to be understood, heard, valued and guided.
