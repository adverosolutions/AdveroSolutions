REGISTERED NURSE LICENSED PRACTICAL NURSE CERTIFIED NURSING ASSISTANT ALLIED SERVICES Nurses Direct is a nurse-owned healthcare staffing agency built on trust, experience, and an unwavering commitment to quality. Whether stepping in during a staffing crisis, helping a new nurse find their footing, or simply answering a late-night call, Nurses Direct is there with the technology to be efficient and the heart to be human. At Nurses Direct, the promise is simple: to provide healthcare facilities and nurses with the trust, support, and reliability they need to thrive because in the end, exceptional care starts with exceptional people. SureStaff Standard Excellence in Every Shift Everything we do at Nurses Direct is backed by the SureStaff Standard a proprietary placement system developed by nurses for both nurses and facilities. From the moment a professional enters the SureStaff Standard process, they undergo comprehensive evaluations to ensure they meet the highest reliability, skill, and integrity standards. For facilities, it s not just about having a nurse on the floor it s about having the right nurse at the right time for the right job.
