About ScrumMatch | ScrumMatch About ScrumMatch ScrumMatch is the job platform for Scrum Masters by Scrum Masters. ScrumMatch solves the two biggest challenges Scrum Masters and companies have with Scrum. Challenge 1 Anyone can call themselves a Scrum Master, regardless of their real understanding, experience or track record. The true Scrum Masters who actually deliver value are rare. It is difficult for them to stand out from the crowd, and it is difficult for organizations to recognize and hire true Scrum Masters. ScrumMatch helps true Scrum Masters stand out and helps organizations find and hire true Scrum Masters. Challenge 2 Many organizations use Scrum terms to look more attractive as an employer. This makes it difficult for true Scrum Masters to identify organizations that are serious about Scrum. Likewise, organizations that are serious about Scrum have a hard time differentiating themselves. ScrumMatch helps true Scrum Masters find organizations that are serious about Scrum.
