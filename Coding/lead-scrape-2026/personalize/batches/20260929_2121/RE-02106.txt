About Us - Waveland Search Waveland International was founded in 1997 with the underlying belief that, as consultants to management, our business must be as dynamic and responsive to change initiatives as the clients and industries we serve. With that thought in mind, we approach our client relationships in the true spirit of strategic partnership. We believe that the executive search industry must transform itself from a transaction-based business to a more expansive, relationship-driven enterprise. To that end, we partner with our clients by taking the necessary time to understand their business philosophies, comprehend their needs, share their energy and enthusiasm and become advocates for their principles and objectives. We pledge to function as an extension of a clients organizational needs, as a seamless partnership with a relentless level of commitment, objectivity and accountability. Waveland International has devoted the resources, the expertise, and the perseverance to conduct broad-based executive search assignments in most business sectors as well as the ability to create thorough, value-added solutions for our clients on time, within budget and according to defined specifications. We are supported by comprehensive state-of-the-art research and retrieval capabilities, proprietary databases, and an extensive network of industry sources.
