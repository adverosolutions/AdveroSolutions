Richard Founder & CEO, Richard & Associates HR --> Richard & Associates HR Your Partner in Recruiting Talent That Drives Results Richard & Associates HR is a full-cycle recruiting firm supporting employers nationwide. We manage the entire hiring process for management and executive-level roles—from defining position requirements through sourcing, evaluation, and final placement. We deliver the talent organizations need to move forward with confidence, aligning each search with business goals, team structure, and long-term success. Founded in 2016, Richard & Associates HR serves the Houston Metropolitan Area and works with companies across a range of industries. We specialize in placements at the management and executive levels, with a consistent track record of delivering qualified, high-impact professionals. We approach each search as a structured, full-cycle recruiting process—focused on identifying candidates who align with both the technical requirements of the role and the broader goals of the organization. Our work is built on clear communication, defined processes, and a commitment to delivering results. Our Mission Statement The mission of Richard & Associates HR is to deliver full-cycle recruiting process that connect employers with qualified professionals for critical roles. We manage each stage of the hiring process through a structured approach that ensures alignment with technical requirements, leadership expectations, and organizational goals. Our process supports informed hiring decisions through consistent communication, thorough evaluation, and a focus on long-term success. Our Vision Statement Our vision is to provide a consistent and structured approach to full-cycle recruiting that delivers strong hiring outcomes across industries. We focus on aligning role requirements with candidate experience through a process that remains efficient, practical, and results-driven.
