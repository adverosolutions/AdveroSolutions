About InCommon | Building teams for the AI era About Who you hire is who the company becomes. Home / About Teams we ve built What changed The shape of a team changed A team today is people and models working together. Smaller than it would have been three years ago, and denser each person carrying more, each hire mattering more. What a team has to be able to do has changed too: build with AI, know whether it works, put it inside a real business. The models are the easy half. They arrive ready. The people don t. What didn t The way teams get built didn t Recruiting still works the way it did twenty years ago. A job description goes out, applications come in, someone who has never done the job screens for keywords, and the decision comes down to an interview a skill different from the one being hired for. Nothing carries forward. The bar lives in one person s head. The reason you passed on a good candidate in March is gone by June. Every search starts from nothing, and the next one starts from nothing again. That was survivable when teams were large and a wrong hire was one of fifty. It isn t now. How we build So we build differently We start with what the team has to deliver, not a job description, and write down the bar before anyone is sourced. We draw on a pool built over years people we ve met, judged on work we ve seen, and kept talking to with no role attached. Software reads every candidate against that bar rather than against keywords. The final call is made by someone who has done the job they re judging. And we keep what we learn. Every team we build teaches us something about your bar, and none of it leaves when a recruiter does. This is how teams in the AI era get built.
