Meet Veteran Hiring Solutions Veteran Hiring Solutions was founded to fix what s broken in hiring: the loss of human connection. Years of Combined Job Seekers Justin Henderson - Chief Executive Officer and Founder Justin grew up in a small village south of Pittsburgh, PA, where life in a close-knit community forged his strong work ethic and deep commitment to service. With military service running deep in his family lineage, he enlisted in the U.S. Marine Corps Reserves after high school, serving for six years as a Motor Transport Operator and Automatic Rifleman. While serving, he earned his degree in Economics from the University of Pittsburgh balancing academic growth with military duty. For nearly two decades, Justin has worked at the intersection of business and service, guiding organizations in how to attract, evaluate, and retain military-experienced talent. He has partnered with over 500 companies and helped more than 5,000 veterans successfully transition into meaningful civilian careers. creator of the Video Job Description , Justin brings a human-first, performance-based approach to modern hiring. He is the host of The Veteran Hiring Show, a LinkedIn Top Voice on career mobility, and a featured contributor across national and local media outlets.
