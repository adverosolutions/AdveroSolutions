Throughout the years, Prodigy Search has dedicated our business principles to grow into the largest sports-specific, boutique recruiting agency in North America with a team of six (6) full-time recruiters – and 10 staff members in total – dedicated to our business. --> Prodigy Search is a national leader in executive search with a reputation for recruiting and placing senior-level talent that inspires and grows our clients’ businesses. Our successful placements include leadership roles in Management (Commissioner; President; Chief Executive Officer), Finance (Chief Financial Officer), Business Administration (Senior Vice President, Business Operations; Vice President; Executive Director), Sales and Marketing (Chief Revenue Officer; Chief Marketing Officer; Senior Vice President, Sales; Senior Vice President, Corporate Partnerships; Vice President, Ticket Sales; Vice President, Marketing Partnerships) and Operations (Regional Vice President, Stadium Arenas; Senior Vice President/General Manager). Prodigy Search has done work throughout the ever-expanding esports industry; sports technology industry; and education and nonprofit industries. Today, our senior-partner involvement, high level of service, and transparency of process have presented opportunities to expand beyond sports and entertainment.
