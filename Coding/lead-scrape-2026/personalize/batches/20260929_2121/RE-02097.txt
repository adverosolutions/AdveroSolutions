Casey currently serves as Ripple’s CEO Co-Founder. With her background in talent acquisition and human resources, she understands what’s at the core of the client’s need and always has the customer and candidate experience at the center of all interactions. Casey has partnered with executive leaders across varying industries to understand their talent needs, specifically during transformational times. She has an uncanny ability to understand complex problems and client needs translating them into a productive talent strategy. She is based in Charlotte, North Carolina. She brings a passion and energy to all that she does with a strong desire to win, and not only for herself, but for those around her.
