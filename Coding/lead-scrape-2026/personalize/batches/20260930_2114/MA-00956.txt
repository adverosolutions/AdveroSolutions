Modern Media Company 0 WHAT WE DO Traditional filmmaking with the innovation of new media. Development Hieronyvision develops and produces original content. Its HV Incubator guides recent graduates in making their first microbudget feature. New Media HV Arts Hub is a platform for discovering and making independent film, music, and art, pioneering the microstreaming model. Distribution MMC connects distinctive projects to global audiences. PaliTV , its community-supported microstreaming platform, serves the fire-affected Pacific Palisades and Altadena communities. TV Film Award-winning Werner Film , MMC's European arm, develops and finances small-to-medium budget independent features and documentaries. Financing Werner Film and Hieronyvision provide gap, bridge, and equity financing, plus nonprofit foundation funding on select projects.
