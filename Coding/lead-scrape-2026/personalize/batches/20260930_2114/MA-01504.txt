About - Fellswood Creative Empowering Business Owners and Fostering Growth In a forest, all the things that dwell there are connected. That connection allows the forest to experience growth continuously, with the different contributors benefiting in different ways. Like those relationships, Fellswood strives to create more synergy between business owners and their marketing systems and tools. From One Small Business Owner to Another When I started making websites for other people back in 2008, I couldn’t have envisioned the winding path I would take. I learned so much along the way and found myself inspired over and over again by the driven, wise, funny and truly amazing people I have had the privilege to work with. I also learned a lot about what it takes to run a business, which included a lot of things I couldn’t have foreseen at the start of this journey. Now, my primary focus is on elevating the people I collaborate with. Whether it’s by helping them with some service I offer or connecting them with someone who can help them in some positive way, I make it my goal to bring value to every interaction. I love working with all kinds of business owners and I specifically enjoy uplifting my fellow women business owners, especially early in the journey when a partnership with someone can be an invaluable tool to launch a new endeavor. When I’m not creating, I’m usually walking in the woods with my pack, cooking amazing food, spending time with my family or reading. Inspiration is everywhere and I’m always on the lookout for more!
