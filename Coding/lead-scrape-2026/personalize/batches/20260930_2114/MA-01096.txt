About - PRADERIO CONSULTING LLC Skip to content About Us Our story We are a team that for over 15 years worked in different advertising media channels. Although we were getting good results for our clients we felt like there was something missing. That s when we decided as partners to start a company where we could apply all our knowledge but with our ideologies, our system and strategies. Thus, with very few resources but with a lot of passion, Praderio consulting LLC was born in 2016. We were in a process where advertising was going through many changes. Traditional advertising began to be quickly replaced by social networks and online marketing digital came to the forefront. As new entrepreneurs the challenges are too many. It is necessary to point out that our biggest challenge in recent years has been to help our clients believe in digital advertising, since most of them came from traditional branches of advertising. Our mission is to provide a personalized service to our client with excellent results, based on analysis, strategy and return on investment through measured campaigns. We see our clients as our partners, this is the only formula where we both receive results and thus we maintain long and transparent relationships. Praderio Consulting LLC. is at the service of its clients. Always finding the solution for the growth of your business.
