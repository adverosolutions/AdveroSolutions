You Got This... what we do Tin Shingle Is An Education Empowerment Center For Businesses Who Are Getting The Word Out Tin Shingle trains and empowers business owners, makers, artists and staff members in how to get the word out about their business. Be it a non-profit, special project, or even a major call to action, Tin Shingle’s training and community support gets people doing just that - on their own. Tin Shingle puts the power of PR, Social Media, SEO, and design theory directly into the hands of business owners, makers and artists by way of a flexible Media Kit Membership subscription that includes education, training, directories, media research lists and editorial calendars. About The Owner Tin Shingle is owned and run by Katie Hellmuth Martin, a digital media artist and storyteller who works on both sides of the media landscape - as a publisher, journalist, ecommerce designer, and shopper. Katie is the publisher of the hyper-local blog, A Little Beacon Blog , which has become one of the leading sources of news and entertainment in the Hudson Valley of New York. Businesses and government offices regularly send pitches and press releases, so she can tell you what works and doesn’t work in a pitch. Katie is a designer and producer of websites both small and large with her firm Katie James, Inc., and is an expert in organic SEO copywriting. She weaves her tech experience under-the-hood in the overall editorial guidance that comes out of Tin Shingle.
