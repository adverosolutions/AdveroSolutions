About Us THNK1994 Skip to content Free shipping on orders $45 or more. Discount automatically applied. Close Log in Create account Search Submit What is THNK1994? THNK1994 is a multi-disciplinary creative agency based in New York City. Our mission began after an impulse decision to turn the hallway of an apartment in Williamsburg into a museum brought visitors from around the world. Soon the mission became larger than the space. Since that first exhibit, THNK1994 has produced over 40 exhibitions and one-night events that fuse original art, performances, and discourse into a fabulous party. We have transformed unconventional spaces like casinos and former doctor’s offices into themed exhibits like a VHS rental store, a vintage corporate office, or a multi-gallery museum. Our world renowned Museum Gift Shop is the destination to fulfill all of your pop-culture needs. Featuring designs by Laura Collins and more, our ashtray collections have been featured in Nylon, L'Officiel, Vulture, and The Guardian.
