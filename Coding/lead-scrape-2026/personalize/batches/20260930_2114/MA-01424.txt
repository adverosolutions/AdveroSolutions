Through market research and analysis, we help elevate a brand s market position and name recognition. Since our agency launch in 2017, we have helped our clients in creating the missing elements needed to elevate their brand and story. In turn, we have a track record that proves steady growth and increased brand recognition. We have also worked to help steer our clients online marketing messages and brand to one that prioritizes inclusivity, diversity in representation, as well as community outreach and engagement to best connect with global and local consumer demographics and the priorities that guide their spending and decision-making . Brave World Media is a certified Woman-owned, Minority-owned business by the Empire State Development of New York State Carol Cain (she/her) Principal / Founder​ Carol Cain built her career on telling stories that matter. Since launching the agency in 2017, Carol has led a body of work that spans brand identity, tourism campaigns, video production, packaging design, and content strategy. Her clients have achieved measurable growth in brand recognition, audience engagement, and market position — results that reflect both strategic clarity and creative excellence. The agency's work has earned industry recognition, and Brave World Media holds certification as a Woman- and Minority-owned business by New York State. With over 25 years of experience in communications, brand strategy, and marketing, Carol brings a global perspective and a proven ability to translate complex stories into campaigns that connect and convert.
