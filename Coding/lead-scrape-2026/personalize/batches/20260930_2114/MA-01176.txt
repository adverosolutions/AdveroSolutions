Why Glassell House...? Glassell House Built by Artists. Fueled by Passion. Why Glassell House...? Because that’s where it all began, in a house in Glassell Park, Los Angeles, where creative minds from all walks of life were invited to gather and collaborate under one roof. Founded in 2015 by industry veteran Xavier Savant, Glassell House is not just another creative agency, but an art collective / agency hybrid. We’ve paired the multidisciplinary creative collaboration and flexibility of an art collective with the top notch production and centralized account management services of an agency. The result is a team that loves crafting beautiful, smart and inspired work that is focused on our client’s audience and goals. We specialize in producing highly memorable and effective creative across a variety of mediums, including Digital, Print, and AV and whatever other over-the-top creative need you may have we are ready to support you.
