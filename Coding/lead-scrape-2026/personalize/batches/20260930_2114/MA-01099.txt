THE ROAD TO SIDE STAGE Side Stage Promotions 0 AMBER DEE • OWNER/CEO THE ROAD TO SIDE STAGE If when I started my marketing career over a decade ago you told me that I would own and operate a marketing agency dedicated to helping elevate independent artists , I would have called bullshit. But yet, here we are. Side Stage Promotions wasn’t built overnight — it came from years of working behind the scenes, seeing what independent artists truly need to succeed. After starting my marketing career in 2014 and diving full-time into music and tour marketing in 2018 , I wound up where creativity, passion, and a little bit of crazy go a long way. But like I said, I didn’t think I’d end up here. I’m originally from a tiny town in the middle of nowhere California that no one has ever heard of called Twentynine Palms. I was born on the U.S.M.C. base and lived there till I turned 18 where I ran off to chase those big city dreams of mine. From there, I really had to hustle . So I worked full-time in the fast food industry while I went to Make-Up Designory in Burbank, CA where I studied full-time to be a beauty and special effects makeup artist for film, TV and print photography. I worked on my first independent feature film set shortly after finishing school and went straight into the independent film industry full-time. I worked on 25+ full-length independent film productions and print photography shoots during my career and while it was fun, I decided to leave my trade in 2014. That’s where I started learning marketing, web and graphic design . I was given the opportunity to work for a healthcare marketing firm, whose goal was to empower independent physical, occupational, and pediatric therapists. This is when I developed a solid foundation of technical skills and marketing concepts. I began freelancing in other industries and gained priceless knowledge that would come to aid in the making of Side Stage Promotions. In 2018, after moving to Texas from Orange County I was referred for a marketing position at an Austin-based artist management company and quickly fell in love with tour marketing and creating music release strategies for independent artists. Here I had the chance to work with some great talent in the independent country, Texas country and americana scene . Artists like Ward Davis, Bart Crow, Cody Jinks, Charles Wesley Godwin, Sunny Sweeney, Whitey Morgan The 78s, Alex Williams, Josh Morningstar, and The Steel Woods.
