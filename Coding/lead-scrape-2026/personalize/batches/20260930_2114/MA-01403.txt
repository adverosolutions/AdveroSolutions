About Us StoryCraft 0 Storytelling is the new marketing. Meet the team Phat X. Chiem FOUNDER Phat is the founder of StoryCraft, a boutique brand storytelling and content marketing agency. Working with technology, e-commerce and nonprofit organizations, he brings his experience in journalism, media and marketing to help brands tell their best stories. Prior to establishing StoryCraft, Phat spent years as a senior editor at Yahoo curating content for its powerful homepage. He got his start in journalism as a reporter for the Chicago Tribune. He also holds an MBA from Pepperdine's Graziadio School of Business and Management. Karley Sullivan CONTENT CREATOR PROJECT MANAGER Karley is a project manager by profession and an image specialist by trade. Karley has lived in Atlanta, Seoul, and Los Angeles, and holds an MFA in Photo/Media from CalArts.
