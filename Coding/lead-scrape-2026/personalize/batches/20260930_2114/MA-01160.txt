Each company offers unique opportunities for both advertisers as well as publishers. What s behind the name? While UPT Media was founded in Los Angeles, the name was actually derived from New Orleans, LA. The UPT is often referred to as Uptown New Orleans, which is close to the heart of our company. Who Do We Work With? UPT Media works with all types of companies, from small businesses to Fortune 500 companies. We offer an array of product and services to help our clients achieve their goals. Our Philosophy UPT Media believes in forming partnerships with our clients. While we do provide a service, it s our clients goals that we constantly strive to achieve. Tobin got his start in online marketing in 1999 where he had the opportunity to grow traffic and ecommerce for a popular television show.
