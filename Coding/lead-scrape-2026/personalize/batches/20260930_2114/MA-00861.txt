About - Strouse Growth Partners Skip to content About Strouse Growth Partners We Don't Sell Marketing. We Sell Growth. A performance-driven agency built for one purpose: helping law firms and medical practices become the obvious choice in their market, backed by numbers you can verify. Our Story Built From The Client Side First Strouse Growth Partners started with a simple frustration: too many law firms and medical practices were paying agencies that treated them like every other local business on the roster. Cookie-cutter campaigns. Vague reporting. No real accountability for whether the phone actually rang. We built this agency around the opposite idea. Legal and medical are their own worlds , with their own compliance rules, their own buyer psychology, and their own competitive pressure. Marketing for a personal injury firm has almost nothing in common with marketing for a dental practice, and neither has much in common with marketing a retail brand. Generalist playbooks don't hold up in these markets. "We don't measure success in impressions. We measure it in signed cases and booked patients." Today, Strouse Growth Partners is headquartered in Fort Lauderdale and partners with firms and practices nationwide, but the standard hasn't moved: every engagement is judged by whether it actually grew the business, not by how the dashboard looks. Jack Strouse Founder CEO Meet The Founder A Growth Partner, Not Just An Agency Jack founded Strouse Growth Partners on a simple belief: a marketing partner should feel like part of your team, not a vendor sending you a monthly PDF. That means picking up the phone, explaining the strategy in plain language, and being just as invested in your case volume or patient count as you are. Jack and the team operate out of Fort Lauderdale, working with law firms and medical practices across the country who want a partner that treats their growth like it matters, because it does.
