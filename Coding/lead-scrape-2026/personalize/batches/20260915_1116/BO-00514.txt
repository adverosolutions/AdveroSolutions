About Us Colonial Bookkeeping Tax, Inc. Laura Lech, MBA, RTRP Founder and President Laura is the proud owner of Colonial Bookkeeping Tax, Inc., which she launched in 2010. During more than a decade in public accounting, Laura has worked with a wide range of clients in the non-profit and for-profit sectors as well as high-net-worth individuals. A Certified Quickbooks ProAdvisor, Laura is passionate about mastering new technologies. She also enjoys helping others understand the challenges and rewards of the business world, including the ever-evolving tax code and fast-moving tech scene. Laura is an IRS Registered Tax Return Preparer with deep working knowledge of GAAP. Equally comfortable on both sides of the auditor's desk, she reassures anxious clients by preparing them in advance for what to expect and supporting them through each step. Laura holds a certificate in Forensic Accounting from Northeastern University and a Master's in Business Administration from Nichols College. She is a Magna Cum Laude graduate of Commonwealth Honors College at University of Massachusetts Amherst.
