About Us Lecorchick Group 0 About Us Established during the height of the pandemic out of necessity, and with a mission to redefine the landscape of financial consultancy, Lecorchick Group prides ourselves on being a women-owned and operated entity. This unique characteristic not only sets us apart in a traditionally male-dominated industry but also underscores our commitment to diversity, empowerment, and the promotion of women in the fields of finance and accounting. With offices in Ohio, Colorado, and Alabama, the Lecorchick Group stands as a beacon of innovation and excellence in the realms of CPA, accounting, bookkeeping, and tax planning services. By fostering a culture of inclusivity and leveraging the unparalleled expertise of its all-women team, we strive to deliver comprehensive and tailored financial solutions that meet the evolving needs of our diverse clientele. Holly Lecorchick, MBA, CPA With over two decades of experience, I specialize in melding the worlds of finance and accounting with operations, making complex financial concepts accessible to everyone.
