About Us Focus Private Placements specializes in sourcing top-performing investment professionals at every career level across different functionalities within an organization. As a boutique, we are deeply committed to the success of our clients’ businesses. Flexibility Hiring firms need a search partner who can deliver; our expertise in research and execution guarantees success for our clients. The flexibility that comes with being a boutique is fundamental to our mission, allowing us to adapt and tailor our services to meet the individual and unique needs of each client. Trust We put an emphasis on building long-term relationships and trust with candidates so that our clients receive access to candidates who are not necessarily looking for jobs. We understand that cultural fit is often as important as investment acumen and we work closely with both our clients and candidates to ensure the best match.
