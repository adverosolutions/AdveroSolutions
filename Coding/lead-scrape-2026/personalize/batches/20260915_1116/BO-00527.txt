About HW | Digital Accounting by Haranwatson - Welcome to Haran Watson Company As President of Haran Watson Company, I am proud to lead a CPA firm that embraces the future of accounting with innovation, precision, and integrity. Our firm operates on a 100% paperless platform with fully digital, online processing —not just as a convenience, but as a strategic alignment with the IRS’s ongoing modernization of its tax systems. Recent IRS developments—such as the emphasis on contemporaneous documentation , the preference for standardized digital records , and the increased burden of proof on taxpayer-prepared documentation —are transforming how businesses must maintain and present their financial records. These changes mean that estimates and after-the-fact reconstructions will no longer suffice; accurate, real-time, and IRS-compatible records are becoming essential. At Haran Watson Company, we have invested in advanced tax technology to ensure our clients stay ahead of these evolving requirements. By integrating secure, standardized digital solutions, we help you minimize examination friction, maintain compliance, and be prepared for any IRS review—whether or not audit rates change under new reforms. Our mission is simple: to give you peace of mind, knowing your financial records are accurate, accessible, and ready for the future of tax compliance.
