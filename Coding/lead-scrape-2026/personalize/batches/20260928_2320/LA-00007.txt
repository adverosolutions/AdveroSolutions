Horizon Roofscapes produces the LiveRoof® Hybrid modular system for Georgia, Tennessee, South Carolina, and Florida. Over 25 years as a perennial plant supplier means the plants arriving on your rooftop are guaranteed to thrive in Southeast heat, humidity, and sun. The LiveRoof® modular system combines the simplicity and speed of modular installation with the natural look and function of a traditional green roof. Its fully vegetated modules create a seamless living roof from day one, helping manage stormwater, reduce energy use, protect the roof, and support healthier plant growth. About Us Skip to content About Us THE BEST PERFORMING GREEN ROOF OFFERINGS IN THE SOUTHEAST Horizon Roofscapes is your turnkey resource for the LiveRoof® Hybrid Modular system in Georgia, South Carolina, Tennessee, and Florida. We specialize in heat/humidity-tolerant green roof projects, utilizing our experience from 25 years as a perennial plant supplier. Contact Us Meet the Team Robby Jourdan CEO/Co-Founder Robby has spent years developing, propagating, and growing perennial plants in the Southeast US. His passion for plants began during high school horticulture classes and continued through the University of Georgia Agribusiness and Horticulture programs where he cultivated a desire to find the most heat and humidity-tolerant perennials in the Southeast. Robby has been in the green roof industry for 9 years. His expertise in the entire green roof process, from design consultation through installation and maintenance, has made him an asset on the most high-profile green roof projects in Atlanta and surrounding areas. Andrew MacElroy Green Roof Designer Andrew brings a strong foundation in environmental design and landscape architecture to his role as Green Roof Designer at Horizon Roofscapes. With degrees from Auburn University and the University of Georgia, his background combines thoughtful design with an understanding of how plants, people, and the built environment work together. He applies this expertise to creating functional, sustainable green roof landscapes designed to thrive for years to come. Rachael Auld Sales Specialist Rachael is a natural people connector. She is a fellow graduate of the UGA Horticulture program and uses her extensive plant knowledge to bring our green roof designs to fruition. Rachael transitions seamlessly from sales presentations and design consultations to hands-on work in the field when our team needs an extra set of hands. Connect with Rachael when you need to bring your green roof ideas from paper to planting.
