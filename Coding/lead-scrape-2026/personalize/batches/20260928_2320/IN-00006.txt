About Final Expense Brokerage Final Expense Brokerage 0 The story of Final Expense Brokerage began in 1997, inside a modest office where handwritten notes filled manila folders, and the steady ring of a landline phone echoed against the pale-colored walls. Robert Swanson sat behind a small desk—not as a broker, but as a life insurance agent, meeting with clients, listening to their concerns, and helping them find real protection for their families. He didn’t just sell policies—he built relationships. Over time, Robert saw what many agents lacked: a partner who truly understood the day-to-day challenges of being in the field. Someone who could offer not only contracts, but guidance, tools, and support that actually moved an agent’s business forward. That vision became Final Expense Brokerage (FEB). What began at one desk has become a trusted national IMO—built by an agent who lived it, and shaped for agents ready to grow. And though the office is bigger now, our purpose is still personal: help agents serve better, sell smarter, and build a business that lasts.
