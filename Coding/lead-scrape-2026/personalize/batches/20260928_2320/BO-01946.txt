About Mitch Reed Associates About Founded in 1991, we have experienced steady growth during our 35 years in business. At Mitch Reed Associates we are proud to be one of the most trusted and experienced CPA firms in the greater Knoxville, Tennessee area. We provide a full range of accounting and tax services to help individuals and business owners make informed decisions accurately, easily and effectively. We have been fortunate to maintain lasting relationships with our clients and gain deep experience across a variety of industries. Our core values are integrity, honesty, service and hard work. We carry these values with us wherever we are – working onsite with a client, preparing documentation for clients in our office, and in every step we take. We work hard so that you have peace of mind that your finances are in good hands. Mitch Reed, CPA Mitch graduated with high honors from the University of Tennessee in 1987 with a B.S. in Business Administration and a Major in Accountancy. He worked in the audit division of a Big Five accounting firm for three years and after that was responsible for financial statement reporting, SEC reporting, and taxes for a large publicly traded manufacturing firm located in Knoxville. In 1991, Mitch began his accounting and tax practice with an emphasis on both service and support. He is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and the Better Business Bureau. Mitch is a past board member for Hope Resource Center and Medic Regional Blood Center. He enjoys fly fishing, playing guitar and spending time with his family. Mitch Reed Sheri Hagood, CPA Sheri has 29 years of experience in the accounting/auditing field.
