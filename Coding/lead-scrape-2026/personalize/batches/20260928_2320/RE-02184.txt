About Recruiting Insight — Real Estate s Recruiting Company --> About Recruiting Insight Recruiting Insight is the real estate recruiting company trusted by independent brokerages, major franchises, and high-performing teams across North America. For 25 years, we ve helped real estate, mortgage, and title firms build the systems, install the processes, and develop the human capabilities required to recruit and retain top-tier talent. With 41,236 hires and counting, we don t just teach recruiting — we engineer it. Our integrated suite of solutions, ThirdPool, CoRecruit, TalentScout, and HiringCenter, gives broker-owners every tool they need to turn talent acquisition into their competitive advantage. Real Estate's Recruiting Company Since 2005 When Recruiting Insight was founded in 2005, the real estate industry was undergoing a digital revolution — yet the way brokers found talent remained stuck in the dark ages. The standard was a chaotic mix of cold-calling unverified lists and post and pray job ads. Most growth-minded brokers weren t getting more hires; they were getting more noise. We were born from a singular, disruptive belief: recruiting is a system, not a series of random events. That belief still drives every product, every coaching engagement, and every line of code we ship today. Our Founding Story — From Sourcing to Strategy The first product to come out of that belief was ThirdPool, founded in 2006. ThirdPool revolutionized how firms engaged with new-to-industry talent — moving brokerages away from the same exhausted licensed-agent pool and toward sourcing high-potential candidates from outside the industry. It quickly became the gold standard for new agent recruitment. As the market matured, brokers asked us to solve the harder problem: the predictable recruitment of experienced, high-producing agents. That challenge gave birth to CoRecruit (elite-level coaching for broker-owners) and TalentScout (professional outbound outreach handled by US-based callers). Most recently, HiringCenter unified everything — turning recruiting workflow into a single mobile-first command center with AI-assisted screening and follow-up. Today, Recruiting Insight serves as the primary growth partner for firms that refuse to leave their headcount to chance.
