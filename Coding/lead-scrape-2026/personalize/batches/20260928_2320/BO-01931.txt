About Us | SATOSHI PACIOLI ACCOUNTING SERVICES Skip to content Founded in early 2022, Satoshi Pacioli Accounting has grown into a team of eleven professionals dedicated to serving the Bitcoin community. In 2026, Satoshi Pacioli merged with Trusted Advisors CPA — uniting two firms that share the same mission: empowering clients with clarity, integrity, and forward-thinking financial insight. With deep expertise spanning accounting, tax, client advisory, and business strategy, our combined team keeps Bitcoin at the core of everything we do. Joe Wood, CPA Partner, Head of Accounting A licensed CPA with over sixteen years of experience, Joe has done everything from auditing to financial reporting to managing a shared services accounting department. As a Bitcoiner for several years, Joe Wood has a deep understanding of the industry, having really gone down the rabbit hole in 2020. He founded Satoshi Pacioli with the goal of using his years of accounting experience to help Bitcoin companies push progress forward. Jordan Guess, CPA Partner, Head of Tax As a licensed CPA since 2019 and a Bitcoiner since 2020, Jordan has developed a unique expertise in helping small business owners navigate the world of Bitcoin. Since 2017, he has been working with entrepreneurs to lower their time preference and minimize their tax burden. Jordan is a cofounder of Bitment, a pioneering Bitcoin accounting software designed to simplify the adoption of Bitcoin for small businesses. Bitment offers a range of innovative features, including Bitcoin-native bank statements, unified accounting tools for fiat and Bitcoin activity, and streamlined tax compliance for those living on a Bitcoin standard. Additionally, Jordan advises clients on tax-efficient strategies for gaining exposure to Bitcoin through mining, such as structuring entities to acquire Bitcoin at the cost of production, which is often lower than the market price, while supporting a decentralized mining ecosystem. Michael Leonardi, CPA Head of Client Advisory Services Fractional CFO A licensed CPA and skilled CFO with over twenty-six years of experience, Michael started his career as a financial statement auditor at PwC. In 2017, he discovered Bitcoin and became a passionate advocate, regularly sharing his insights through Twitter spaces, conferences, and in-person meetups.
