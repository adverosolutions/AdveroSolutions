Commercial Landscaping and Snow Removal DVC Landscaping 0 About Us DVC Landscaping was founded by twin brothers, Matt and Jon Corcoran with the intention of serving commercial clients with quality and affordability being top priorities. Both Jon and Matt are SIMA accredited CSP and are OSHA 30 hour certified. DVC Landscaping has the experience and capabilities to service projects both large and small. We have experience serving sites such as HOA’s, medical facilities, shopping centers and large industrial/distribution centers. Since 2016 our mission has been to design, construct and maintain landscapes in the Greater Lehigh Valley Area. We believe becoming your trusted partner is key to ensuring landscaping excellence. Our dedicated team of professionals offers a full range of services to keep your property at its best. These services include major installation projects for landscaping, erosion control, and drainage systems as well as ongoing landscaping maintenance and lawn care services.
