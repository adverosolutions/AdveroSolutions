About Us - Performant Scout Skip to content ABOUT Meet the people behind Performant Scout. Our story began in 2015 and we just keep bringing it. Every day. Vanessa Garcia CO-FOUNDER and CEO Vanessa Garcia was born in New York City, where most of her family resides today. Having grown up mainly in the Adirondacks of upstate NY, Vanessa is now loving the heat of the Phoenix, AZ valley! She is a graduate of Penn State University with a Bachelor’s degree in Applied Psychology, studied abroad in Sydney, Australia and has had impactful communications training through Landmark Education. Vanessa has been featured in Phoenix Magazine, The Produce News, The Packer, The Know Book Phoenix, 100+ Women You Must Know and Do Business With 2019, City Lifestyle Magazine Paradise Valley, Voyage Magazine and was recognized as an Outstanding Woman in Business honoree through the Phoenix Business Journal in 2024. Vanessa finds great value in being a member of the International Produce Association and the IFPA Talent Advisory Committee given the connection and engagement in the food and beverage industry they each provide. Vanessa is energized by connecting people with new career paths and creating win-wins for her clients! The learning opportunities that come with missing the mark and the gratification that comes with knocking it out of the park has made her as tenacious as they come. Along with growing Performant Scout, Vanessa is passionate about engaging within her community and welcomes opportunities to improve the lives of those in need! Vanessa has committed efforts to support The American Cancer Society, Phoenix Children’s Hospital, TGen, Amanda Hope Rainbow Angels, Social Venture Partners of Arizona, Goodwill’s Excel Center, Valleywise Health Foundation, The Leukemia and Lymphoma Society, Seed Spot and Arizona Community Foundation’s Latinas in Philanthropy and Service! With two young children, Vanessa is driven to contribute to causes that can help support a brighter, healthier future for the next generation! Vanessa enjoys spending quality time with her little ones and traveling anywhere new!
