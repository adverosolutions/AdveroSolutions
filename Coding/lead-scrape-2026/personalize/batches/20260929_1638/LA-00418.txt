About - turfit™ Skip to content About turfit turfit is the new experience in outdoor living. My wife, Lindsey, and I started turfit in 2019 for one reason: to bring people’s backyard dreams to reality. We’re not a cookie-cutter company and neither are our clients; we are Tampa Bay’s premier installer of luxury, high-end outdoor spaces utilizing artificial turf, pavers, natural stone + concrete masonry, and landscape + design. We use a scientific approach to artfully install landscapes that will last you for a lifetime. Drawing from decades of combined experience, an obsession with doing things right, and a rigorous attention to detail have gained our reputation; it’s why we stand by our work with an industry leading 5-year installation warranty. All of our work is completed in-house by Lindsey, our crew and myself – nothing gets sub-contracted out to other companies. We work with residential and commercial clients and also provide an artificial turf maintenance service. To Lindsey and I, turfit is not just a company or a job – it is our life. We are here for the long-haul; we understand that by partnering with turfit for your yard, you are making more than a simple purchase decision you are making an investment in your life and future with turfit.
