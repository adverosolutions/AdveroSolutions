About PRI Resource Centers provide free information on pesticides and low-impact methods of pest management for mice and rats, ants and roaches, fleas, garden pests, and more. Our Philosophy & Approach We conduct human and ecological risk assessment, develop tools to assess the relative risks associated with use of different pesticides, provide guidance and input on pesticide regulation and policy, and develop integrated pest management (IPM) approaches that solve pest problems with minimum pesticide use. Resource Center Empower Empower people to make better decisions about pesticides Provide Seek out and provide the best available pesticide information, tools, and services to our clients Educate Make it easier to understand issues surrounding pesticide use Promote Promote the lowest-impact pest control methods and products Staff Susan E. Kegley, Principal Scientist and CEO Dr. Kegley is a PhD chemist who founded Pesticide Research Institute in 2006. Her work is focused on the scope of pesticide impacts on the environment, human health, and pollinator health. Dr. Kegley has expertise in environmental, organic, inorganic and analytical chemistry, with an emphasis on pesticides and heavy metals and their fate and transport in the environment. She has extensive experience in toxicology and risk assessment, policy analysis, legal consulting and expert witness work, sampling and analytical methods, data analysis, and data management, providing resources for both professional and non-technical audiences. She is a also a beekeeper and organic farmer. Explore more of Susan's background and past work in her Curriculum Vitae. Susan is also co-owner of Bees N Blooms , an 11-acre certified organic lavender farm and nursery operation with an emphasis on providing clean food for pollinators . The farm is managed using reduced -till regenerative organic agricultur al practices and is home to 5-10 colonies of honey bees and thousands of native bees.
