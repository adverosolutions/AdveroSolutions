About Us | His Trees Inc. Rooted in Family, Growing with Purpose Here’s a little bit about us. Original Logo Circa 1992 His Trees is a third-generation, family-owned and operated company. With over 33 years of combined experience, our family started in little ol’ Terra Ceia, Florida—growing palm tree seeds and installing everything from commercial-grade to resort-style specimen trees. We take pride in living by the mantra: no job too big or too small. At the heart of what we do, landscaping isn’t just about maintenance—it’s about creating and exploring the beauty of creation. Landscape design is one of the many ways we partner with people to bring a little piece of heaven down to earth.
