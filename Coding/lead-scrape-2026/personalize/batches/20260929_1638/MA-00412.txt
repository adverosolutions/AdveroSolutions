About Richmond Weddings - Wedding Guide - How to plan a wedding - Richmond Weddings Our Story Richmond Weddings® started in 1989 as The Wedding Guide, founded by Mickie Bowman. At the time, the Guide was the only staple wedding magazine in Richmond, which allowed local brides to easily find Richmond’s best wedding professionals. Today, Richmond Weddings® remains a local, family-owned business belonging to Mickie’s daughter Amye and her husband Scott. Their respected magazine, website and twice-annual wedding shows help local businesses connect with their perfect clientele. Scott and Amye agree that their clients are their favorite part of working in the wedding industry. They love the network of friends and connections they have made since taking over the business in 2005 and consider them an extended family.
