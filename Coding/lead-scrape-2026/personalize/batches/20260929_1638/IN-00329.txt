Insuring Roanoke and All of Virginia Hi. I’m Mary, owner of Brogan Insurance. Welcome to our agency’s website. We’re a locally owned, second-generation family business that has served the Roanoke Valley and surrounding counties for almost 40 years. My father, Dan Brogan, started in 1983 as one of the first agencies to represent Erie Insurance in Roanoke. I joined him in 1985 and we worked together for 18 years until his retirement in 2003, when I became the agency owner. We provide auto/car insurance, home insurance, business/commercial insurance, and life insurance for all of Virginia. Our mission is to bring insurance value to the individuals and businesses in our community with comprehensive plans and outstanding service. Please take a minute to visit our website and let us know how we can serve you. As a family business, we know what it means to keep your family and your business safe and secure. Our next Sounds of the Mountains is April 17th-April 18th, 2026. Camp Bethel One of the few ACA accredited camps in Southwest Virginia, Camp Bethel is open and active all year. With 470 acres of hiking trails, ponds, streams, open meadows, forests, and rolling hills, it’s a great choice for a summer camp, a retreat center, a banquet site, a high/low ropes challenge, or a day of outdoor education. Project WARM (Wood Association of Roanoke Metro) A collaboration between The Salvation Army, The City of Salem, and Church of the Brethren volunteers, this program brings firewood to families in need. Mary helped bring this program to Roanoke in 1996 and her husband, Marc, is the coordinator. Since then, Project WARM has delivered over 6,000 truckloads of free firewood to those in need in the Roanoke Valley.
