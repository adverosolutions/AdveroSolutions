About National Taxor | Tax Resolution, Bookkeeping Accounting Skip to content Menu About National Taxor Focused on tax resolution, bookkeeping and accounting support. National Taxor helps individuals and business owners deal with IRS and California tax problems, while providing small-business bookkeeping and accounting support that keeps financial records clean, current and useful. Our practice is led by an Enrolled Agent and is built around straightforward analysis, clear communication and realistic expectations. We do not promise outcomes before reviewing the facts. What we focus on now Tax Resolution: IRS and California tax debt, notices, collection problems, payment options, penalty relief and related compliance work. Bookkeeping: monthly bookkeeping, reconciliations, QuickBooks Online, cleanup and catch-up work, and financial reporting for small businesses. How We Work Understand the facts first. Tax problems are rarely solved by a slogan or a one-size-fits-all promise. We start by identifying the tax years involved, the balances, filing status, notices, deadlines, financial information and collection exposure. From there, we explain the realistic options and the tradeoffs so you can make an informed decision about what to do next. Our Direction A more specialized practice. National Taxor has moved away from marketing routine annual tax-return preparation as a primary service. Our work is now centered on tax-resolution matters and bookkeeping. That narrower focus lets us spend more time on complex tax problems, representation, cleanup work and the financial records that support better business decisions.
