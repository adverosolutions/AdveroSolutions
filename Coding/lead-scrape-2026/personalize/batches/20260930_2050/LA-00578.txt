Contact Us Our Story Get to Know Us - James Hardin, Owner JH Plantscapes has proudly served the Greater Houston area for more than 45 years. After graduating from Texas A M University in 1978 with a degree in Horticulture, James Hardin spent a summer working in Human Resources at Yellowstone National Park before moving to Houston to begin his career in the corporate world. During that time, he also attended broadcasting school, earned his radio license, and worked for several radio stations, including KSBJ here in Houston.Driven by a lifelong desire to own his own business, James began with a crape myrtle farm, which ultimately led to the founding of JH Plantscapes. What started with a small crew has grown into a respected landscaping company built on hard work, quality craftsmanship, and integrity. ​James holds licenses in irrigation and chemical/pesticide application, and under his leadership, JH Plantscapes has earned numerous awards from the Texas Nursery Landscape Association (TNLA) for outstanding landscape projects. More importantly, the company has built a reputation for doing the job right.
