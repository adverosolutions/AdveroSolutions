Accounting Independence, LLC Accounting Independence, LLC strives to provide affordable, dependable accounting services with the personal attention our clients deserve. With over 20 years of experience, we offer a variety of solutions to meet your individual and business needs, including tax preparation and planning, bookkeeping, payroll, consulting, business start-up assistance, and accounting software support. Whether you are located in Anchorage or work with us remotely, our goal is to provide practical, reliable guidance and accounting services that help you and your business succeed. Our Team Jennifer Platnico jplatnico@accountingindependence.com I have worked in accounting for more than 20 years, beginning my career in 1996 with a small car rental company in Juneau, Alaska. Since then, I have had the opportunity to work in many areas of accounting, giving me a broad range of experience and a practical understanding of the challenges faced by individuals and small business owners. I earned my Bachelor s degree in Accounting in 2007. From 2010 through 2012, I was also a Certified Postsecondary Instructor at a local college, where I taught accounting courses. That varied experience has given me an understanding of accounting that goes beyond simply preparing reports or tax returns. I enjoy helping clients understand their numbers, solve problems, and find practical solutions that work for their individual circumstances or businesses. I am a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor with experience in both QuickBooks Online and Desktop and am a Registered Tax Return Preparer with the Internal Revenue Service. At Accounting Independence, LLC, my goal is to provide clients with knowledgeable, dependable, and personalized service. Whether you need ongoing accounting assistance or simply have a question you aren t sure how to solve, I am here to help.
