Who We Are - Sarver Landscape Skip to content Who We Are About Us Our company was founded after World War II, in 1946 by Herb Sarver. Armed with a dream and a truck nicknamed “The Green Hornet,” he laid the foundation for what is now a third-generation business. Having experienced significant growth since its inception, we attribute this success in part to our commitment to our founding principles: hard work, honesty, integrity and dedication. We believe our work ethic is as strong as it was when Herb picked up his first shovel, and we are confident our work speaks for itself. We are a full-service landscape, tree and snow removal resource and work to provide long-term solutions for our customers. Partnering with Sarver means you will have access to a team that values character, quality and respect. All of which you will see through our employees, their efforts and ultimately our work.
