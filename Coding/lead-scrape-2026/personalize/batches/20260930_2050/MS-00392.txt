About Us Rock Agile Consulting 0 Our focus is building and achieving simple, elegant solutions to the problem at hand. We value simplicity of form wherever possible and use our decades of experience to steer away from the norm of overly complex solutions. Our History. Brian and John have known each other since preschool and grew up together. When John wanted to work for himself and decided to go independent with his own company, He got with Brian and asked if he wanted to join him in this adventure. Brian enthusiastically agreed and Rock Agile was born in May of 2017. Since then, Rock Agile has formed into the company it is today. Our team has an employee-focused mentality, aiming to make ourselves a company that both employees and customers want to work with and be a part of. We are intentional to surround ourselves with great team players who think of Rock Agile’s clients as their own. We like honest, straightforward, mutually beneficial relationships and know our clients and employees value the same.
