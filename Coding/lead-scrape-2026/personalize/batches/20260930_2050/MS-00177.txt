About - BCPi Skip to content About BCPI Founded in 1992, BCPI began as a company dedicated to reselling software, computer systems and computer peripherals. By 2000, we expanded our offerings and became a value-added reseller, focusing on server compute, storage, and business productivity. In 2006, BCPI turned its focus toward enhancing IT security for our business and educational institutions, solidifying our commitment to this sector by adding many key security vendors. Our growth continued in 2015 as added managed services to our offerings through our partnership with Synivate. This allows us to deliver comprehensive solutions that empower organizations to streamline their IT operations. Today, BCPI proudly serves a diverse range of clients, including over 500 customers spanning schools, government agencies, healthcare facilities, small and large businesses, and many more. With decades of experience and a dedication to innovation, BCPI is committed to delivering exceptional IT solutions and services that meet the unique needs of each customer. Why Partner with BCPI? At BCPI, we are committed to working for our customers, not our vendors. We prioritize delivering superior solutions at competitive prices, ensuring that every client receives the best value for their investment. Above all, we value our customers as individuals, never just as a number, and strive to build long-lasting relationships based on trust, service, and mutual success. Many of our clients have been with us since the founding of BCPI, a testament to the strong relationships and unwavering commitment we consistently deliver. Having the right IT solutions is essential for safeguarding organizations, and every organization should have access to cutting-edge resources. That’s why we are proud to be a part of MHEC and E-Rate, two federally funded programs that provide educational institutions with the critical security resources they need to thrive and stay protected. These programs help ensure that all organizations, regardless of their budget, can access the technology and security solutions necessary for their success.
