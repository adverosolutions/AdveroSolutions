Founded in the 1940s, in the heart of Boston s Insurance District, by Herbert L. Gove, we are a fourth generation, family-owned and operated business with an unparalleled heritage of excellence. Over the years, our steady, well-managed growth brought us to Cape Ann where Bruce s background in construction was pivotal in the design and development of Gove Feldman s new home at 124 Main Street in Rockport. Throughout the firm s history and the generations of management, our vision has remained constant: Integrity, Expertise, and Service.
