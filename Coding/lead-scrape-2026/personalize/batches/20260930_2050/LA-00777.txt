About Us Kelly's Greenscapes Request a Quote Since 1999, Kelly’s Greenscapes has been transforming outdoor spaces across Waukesha County and southeastern Wisconsin. What started as a small family business has grown into a trusted name in landscaping—known for creative design, quality craftsmanship, and dependable service. With over 30 years of hands-on experience , our founder Kelly Hilmer leads a dedicated team of professionals who share a passion for building beautiful, functional landscapes. Whether we’re designing a serene backyard retreat, installing a new patio, or lighting up your outdoor space, we treat every project like it’s our own. We’re proud to be fully licensed, insured, and certified , and we stay up to date with industry trends and techniques to ensure every job is done right—the first time. Our commitment to excellence has earned us the Angi Super Service Award , and more importantly, the lasting trust of our clients. At Kelly’s Greenscapes, we believe outdoor living should be just as comfortable, personal, and enjoyable as the inside of your home. That’s why we take the time to listen, plan with precision, and deliver results that exceed expectations—on time and on budget.
