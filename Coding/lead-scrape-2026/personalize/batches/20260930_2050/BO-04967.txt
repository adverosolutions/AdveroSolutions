Hampton Everett, P.C. | Team Skip to primary navigation Skip to main content Skip to primary sidebar Skip to footer Team We are a team of dedicated highly acclaimed professionals Hampton Everett, P.C. (H E) is a team of dedicated, highly acclaimed professionals located in Charlottesville, Virginia. H E was formed in October 1992 when J. Thomas Everett III joined William U. Henderson. In 2004 Wade Hampton joined Tommy and Bill at H E, becoming a partner in 2005. William U. Henderson retired from the firm in 2007. In May 2009, Henderson Everett, PC became Hampton Everett, PC. Tommy retired from the firm in December 2020, and Andrew Mills became a partner in 2021. Meet the H E team below. You can also click below to see photos of the H E office. See Photos Of Our Office Our Team Contact Wade via email Wade H. Hampton CPA Wade was born in Oklahoma City, and lived in Texas and Kansas until moving to California to start high school. He received his bachelor’s degree in business economics with an accounting emphasis from the University of California at Santa Barbara in 1993. After leaving Santa Barbara, Wade worked for Moore Grider and Company in Fresno, California. He moved to the Los Angeles area in 1997 to manage the tax department for Edward White & Co. LLP in Woodland Hills, California. Wade’s primary areas of expertise are in the tax planning, tax compliance and small business consulting areas. Wade moved to Charlottesville in 2004 with his wife Cassie and children, Hunter and Duke to join Hampton & Everett, P.C. where he became a shareholder in 2005.
