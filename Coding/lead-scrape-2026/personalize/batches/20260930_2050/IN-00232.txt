About Young Insurance Agency | Zanesville Ohio Team About Us Home About Us Our Story What Makes Us Different? Incorporated in 1926 by C.M. ''Brigham'' Young, The Young Insurance Agency, Inc. was purchased by Thomas J. Price in June of 1930. The agency name remained due to incorporation and the tradition of business was retained. In 1964 Thomas L. Price, the son of Thomas J., joined the company. Thomas J. Price remained with the company and an active agent until his death in October of 1997 at which time Thomas L Price became the company's President. Lynnell Price-Hankinson, the daughter of Thomas L. Price, continued the tradition of the family business, representing the third generation, when she joined the company in May of 1992. She holds the office of Vice President.
