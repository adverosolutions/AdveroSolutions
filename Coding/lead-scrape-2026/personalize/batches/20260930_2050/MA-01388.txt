OUR STORY MORGAN CULHANE CEO CO-FOUNDER Morgan Culhane is a content marketing strategist specializing in the craft beverage industry. She is the CEO and co-founder of Content on Draft based in Buffalo, New York. Since 2018, she has worked with craft breweries nationwide to help them reach more people, boost engagement, and attract customers. Morgan has shared her expertise as a speaker at the National Craft Brewers Conference , National Pink Boots Society Conference , and New York State Brewers Conference . Morgan currently works with craft breweries, bars, and restaurants as an independent contractor and produces social media content for the New York State Brewers Association . She also volunteers her time to lead the Buffalo Chapter of Pink Boots Society and serves as Marketing Social Media Officer for the WNY Brewers Guild . Morgan fell in love with the city of Buffalo while studying at Canisius University . After graduating with a Bachelor s degree in Journalism and Digital Media Arts in 2014, she started her professional career as a copywriter at a digital marketing agency in Buffalo. In 2016, Morgan became a writer and content creator for Step Out Buffalo , one of the largest media companies in Buffalo. This sparked her passion for the local food and beverage scene. In 2018, Morgan reconnected with Amy and discovered they shared a passion for the craft beverage industry and saw an opportunity to help these companies improve their social media strategies. Then Content on Draft was born. Now Morgan teaches craft beverage brands how to reach, engage, and attract more customers online.
