About FOLIAVA, Garden Design in Marin County, California 0 Vanessa LEMAIRE-WORKMAN OWNER, LEAD DESIGNER Vanessa is a Marin-based landscape designer with a background in environmental science and sustainable land management. Before founding her design practice, she worked in international water infrastructure. Her designs combine artistic vision with ecological function, emphasizing low-water, low-maintenance landscapes that reflect both the architecture and the client. Vanessa holds a Master’s in Environmental Science and an A.A. in Landscape Architecture. She is a qualified Water Efficient Landscaper Recipient of the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency’s (EPA) WaterSense® label.
