By taking the time to listen to our clients' goals, we're able to develop and maintain exceptional landscapes that enhance local homes and businesses. Although the company actually began business in 2014, its earliest conception began in 2005 when Rachel Klug and her brother, Ryan Hansberger, shared a conversation on the living room couch. Ryan wanted to run a business, and Rachel had an unnatural love for mowing grass (although in unconventional patterns). A landscaping company was the obvious choice, and the siblings managed to get the necessary documents together before life got in the way. Nearly a decade later, a similar conversation kickstarted the beginnings of Eternal Eden. Two decades later, Eternal Eden is a successful landscaping company highly praised and depended on by local home and business owners. A recent winner of the 2023 Small Business of the Year from the Helotes Area Chamber of Commerce, Eternal Eden works hard to contribute to the community in many ways. They're proud members of the Texas Master Gardener Program and the San Antonio Chapter of the Community Associations Institute. As a volunteer for Project Evergreen's Greencare for Troops, Eternal Eden helps provide free lawn care and landscape services to families of currently deployed military personnel.
