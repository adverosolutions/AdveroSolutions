About Us: Supporting Contractors Since 2009 Skip to content Understand the numbers behind your business. About Beyond Balanced Books Beyond Balanced Books Inc. was founded in 2009 to support general and specialty contractors and other service-based businesses, typically between $2M and $10M in revenue, with the financial visibility they need to run and grow with confidence. Our team brings deep experience across QuickBooks and Xero, combined with strong bookkeeping and controller-level expertise, to ensure your financials are accurate, clear, and ready to use. As our name indicates, we go beyond balancing your books. Clean books are the foundation. We step in as an outsourced controller, bringing the oversight and structure most growing businesses don’t have in-house. Your numbers are not only accurate, but organized, and used to show how your business is performing, where pressure is building, and how cash flow supports your next decision. Clear reporting, data visualization, and cash flow forecasting give you a full picture of what’s happening and what’s likely to happen next. Get to Know the Team Cindy Hovig Cindy Hovig founded Beyond Balanced Books in 2009. She is an Advanced QuickBooks ProAdvisor and Certified Xero Advisor, and has been named an Insightful Accountant Top 100 QuickBooks ProAdvisor multiple times, as well as a Xero Bookkeeping Partner of the Year. Cindy works closely with business owners as a financial partner at the decision point, helping them see what their numbers are showing, what their business can support, and what a decision will do before they commit to it. When she’s not working with clients, Cindy enjoys spending time with her two grandbabies, making jam for family and friends, and being outdoors. Jessica Berkley Jessica Berkley joined Beyond Balanced Books in 2015. She holds a degree in business from Davenport University and is a certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor and Xero Certified Advisor. Jessica focuses on translating financial data into clear, usable information so clients understand what their numbers are showing and how they impact the decisions in front of them. In her spare time, Jessica volunteers and enjoys learning new languages.
