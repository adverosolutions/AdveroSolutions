About JB s Landscaping In New Jersey Skip to content About JB's Landscaping Consultation About JB’s Landscaping Transforming Outdoor Spaces Across Northwestern New Jersey Since 2004 JB’s Landscaping was founded in 2004 by Jeff Baier, Jr., whose passion for landscape design began long before he started the company. After earning his Associate’s Degree in Landscape Management and Design in 2005, Jeff set out to build a business rooted in craftsmanship, creativity, and customer care. What started as a one-man operation has grown into a trusted, full-service landscaping company proudly serving homeowners across Morris, Sussex, and Warren Counties . Our team of experienced professionals shares a common goal: to create outdoor spaces that are beautiful, functional, and built to last. Our Values JB’s Landscaping is a family-oriented company built on three key principles: Quality Craftsmanship – Every project is approached with precision, skill, and attention to detail. Exceptional Service – We listen, communicate clearly, and treat every client like a neighbor. Custom Solutions – No cookie-cutter designs—just thoughtful, personalized landscapes tailored to your property and your goals. Award-Winning Work In 2012, JB’s Landscaping was honored with a Hardscape North America Award for a project over 4,000 square feet—recognition that reflects the passion and pride we bring to every job, big or small. Serving the Communities We Call Home We’re proud to serve the communities we live and work in, including Columbia, Allamuchy, Budd Lake, Chester, Flanders, Hackettstown, Long Valley, Newton, Rockaway, Sparta, and Succasunna .
