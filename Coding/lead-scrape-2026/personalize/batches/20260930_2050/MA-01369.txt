Her focus is and has always been on building and strengthening each brand she and her team works with, developing visibility for each company large or small, start-up or more mature, and creating opportunities for their principals. Operating from a loft on West 14th Street near the Meatpacking District, Leggett has expanded her eclectic client list over the years, growing her business, and expanding the scope of services and experiences available to her clients. We are a boutique Public Relations and Marketing company that offers personalized service. We like to call it bespoke—specially formulated for each specific entity we work with to fit their needs. This makes us able to generate consistently positive results, meeting the goals of the clients and more.” Our interaction with the media worldwide is very hands-on, specialized, carefully considered. The goal with every client is to partner with them to create a successful PR and marketing strategy, add on additional services as required to help the client grow their business; then implement it consistently for continuing accomplishment. We constantly brainstorm to come up with unexpected, creative and original ways to move our clients forward. Carol Leggett and her staff of well-trained, intelligent, creative professionals have an excellent record of placements in the global media, across all platforms, as well as valuable relationships in all arenas, which has allowed Carol Leggett Public Relations to move into new areas of service including business development, management and more. Carol Leggett is a former fashion and lifestyle writer in the US and UK. She and her son Marley appeared in “Power Media Mom” in Gotham Magazine. Carol Leggett and her husband literary agent Anthony Gardner and their son live in Manhattan.
