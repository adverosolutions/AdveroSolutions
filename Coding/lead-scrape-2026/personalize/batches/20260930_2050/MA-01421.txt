About DUTCHESS MARKETING About What we are all about It all started when a translation request found its way to our little agency in 2013. Today, Dutchess Marketing is run by a dedicated team of six, operating on all things intercultural marketing and communication , from our headquarters in Brooklyn. Flexibility is our promise to you! With satellite desks in New York , Amsterdam , Colorado and Canada, as well as a team that consists of several nationalities, that converse fluently in Dutch, English, German and Spanish, we are sure to live up to that promise. The Dutchess Mission Our mission is to ensure that (Dutch related) brands who are expanding marketing efforts to the US, as well as brands who already are executing campaigns, experience success. * To help clients achieve their intercultural marketing goals and strategic communications objectives in both the Netherlands as the United States. * To deliver the highest levels of professionalism, expertise and experience. * To provide strategic counsel, execute local marketing campaigns, provide intercultural solutions and responsive services.
