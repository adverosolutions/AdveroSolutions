Years in Business 0 + Commercial B2B Focus 0 % Commercial B2B Focus 0 % Our Story Founded in 1989 by Rick Fisher, JNL Systems began as a one-person operation focused on delivering dependable communication solutions to local businesses. Through a commitment to quality workmanship, clear communication, and customer-first service, the company steadily grew into a multi-employee organization serving clients throughout Georgia and the broader Southeast United States. From the beginning, JNL Systems focused exclusively on commercial environments, allowing the team to develop specialized expertise in business-critical infrastructure. As technology evolved, JNL Systems expanded its capabilities to include structured cabling, fiber optics, security cameras, access control, and integrated security systems. Continuous investment in training, tools, and modern equipment has allowed the company to stay aligned with industry standards and best practices. Our technicians work directly on-site with clients, gaining real-world experience and ensuring each installation is executed safely and efficiently. Today, JNL Systems continues to build long-term relationships by helping businesses implement reliable, scalable systems that support daily operations and future growth.
