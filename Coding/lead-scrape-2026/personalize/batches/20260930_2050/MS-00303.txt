About Menu Matthew Robinson Matthew is a seasoned independent media contractor and consultant with more than two decades of experience. His portfolio includes clients such as the United States Government, the State of Hawaii, Microsoft, Talon Private Capital, Amazon, USA Networks, and numerous independent projects. Aside from his career in media production, Matthew has also been a dedicated media teacher in both New York City and Maui, Hawaii. On the island, he co-wrote and produced a television series called "Golfing Thru Hawaii." The eight-episode show highlights premier golf courses and recreational activities throughout the state. Matthew's passion for his craft led him to move back to Maui and establish ProShot Solutions. His focus is on creating marketing media that brings his clients' visions to life through captivating photography, 3D media, and creative visual storytelling. He takes pride in collaborating closely with clients at every stage of the development process and thrives on the creative freedom that comes with helping them realize their unique vision. With his meticulous attention to detail, professional editing skills, and keen eye for talent, Matthew is an excellent choice for any media project that needs to be brought to life. He is enthusiastic about working with local businesses to elevate their brands with stunning media, regardless of project size.
