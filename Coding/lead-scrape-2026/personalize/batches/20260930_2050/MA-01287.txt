Integrity and incredible desire to problem solve make this business soar! While we don’t claim to have all the answers to all the questions, we do know A LOT about social media. Personally and professionally, we love every aspect of the field. It’s an ever-changing arena that keeps us on our toes. Photo by: WASIO faces Tonya D. Wertman, OWNER branding expert | marketing professional | social media manager | writer With over a decade of experience in online content creation and social media management, Tonya has capitalized on her diverse marketing, publishing, and business background to develop a savvy social presence and understanding of brand building. She started with a personal blog and later penned a column sharing her secondary infertility journey for an online parenting magazine, SheKnows . In freelance writing for SheKnows and other online publications, Tonya got to know her way around social media. Part of freelancing meant promoting her own work for the companies she worked for. Tonya developed an active presence on various platforms and discovered a knack for analyzing her own social media reach. That early knack helped translate Tonya’s social media knowledge into building strategies that help clients soar toward their own social media goals. Nine years ago, Tonya started managing the social media for the yoga studio she went to, then her hair salon, and then a small subscription box company that actually paid her instead of dyeing her hair for free or correcting her camel pose. That first freelance project gave Take Flight its wings. Tonya has had the pleasure of working with many different types of small businesses and those small businesses, and the hustlers running them, remind her to keep learning, to keep challenging the perception of this dynamic field, and to always remember social media should be prioritized as a key channel for growing.
