About MTM Bookkeeping Skip to content ABOUT Mike Mliner Owner 612-327-3937 mike@MTMbookkeeping.com Mike Mliner has been in the accounting industry since 1999 and brings over 20 years of extensive accounting and bookkeeping experience and expertise. Before starting MTM Bookkeeping Services, Mike worked as a Senior Accountant for over 20 years for a large industrial real estate firm which owned over 20 million square feet of rental space around the country. Education Mike received a Bachelors degree in Business Administration from the University of Minnesota in 2001. He obtained his Certified Bookkeeper (CB) designation from the American Institute of Professional Bookkeepers and QuickBooks Certification from the National Bookkeepers Association in 2019. Also, as a QuickBooks ProAdvisor, Mike is highly trained in both the Online and Desktop versions of this widely-used accounting program. Hobbies In his free time, Mike enjoys spending time with his wife and two sons. He stays busy coaching youth hockey, baseball, and football teams. Mike also serves on the Board of Directors for Spring Lake Park Youth Hockey Association as the Treasurer.
