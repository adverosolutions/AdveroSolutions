Services About Us Resources FAQ Contact We founded Licari, Larsen Co. in 1989 with the goal of building trust and relationships as a reliable accounting firm. Today, over 30 years later, we continue to provide businesses and individuals with the accounting support they need to find financial success. We are proud to call the North Shore home, and we enjoy serving clients in Duluth, Grand Marais and surrounding areas. The secret to our success? Our unwavering commitment to clients. From tax planning and reporting to auditing and financial statement preparation, our staff has the knowledge and resources to provide you with the support that you deserve.
