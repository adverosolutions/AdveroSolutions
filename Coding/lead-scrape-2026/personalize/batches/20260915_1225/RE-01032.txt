Specialized Financial Recruiting Welcome AB SEARCH is a St. Louis based staffing and recruiting firm specializing in accounting and finance. We provide senior level accounting resources for project/interim help, and direct hire search for all accounting and finance positions. Our entrepreneurial structure and experienced recruiting team allow us to be creative and develop effective recruiting solutions specifically for you. We want to help you fill your key accounting opening, or help you make a fulfilling job change, while enjoying the process with you. Clients: If you have either a project/interim need or full time opening, we have the accounting background to understand the technical skills required, and the recruiting background to identify the best person to fit your organization. Our team has 45+ years of hands on accounting experience and 50+ years of combined recruiting experience. Our model is to visit you in person at your workplace to learn the most we can about your opening, business and culture before suggesting possible candidates. Our goal is to help you build a very talented accounting and finance team, helping lead your business to new levels of success. Candidates: If you are considering a full time job change or interested in a consulting position, we will spend quality face-to-face time with you over a cup of coffee, a meal or in the office. We want to get to know you, where you are at this point in your career, your technical experience, career goals, personal goals, interests and individuality. We will then partner with you to design a recruiting game plan specifically for you. We want to identify opportunities you will be excited about, positions that will enrich your career, and to support you with your search. Equip Clients. Employ Candidates. Enjoy Relationships. We’d love to talk to you today! Thanks for visiting our website, and feel free to contact us anytime. Take care, and we will look forward to connecting with you soon!
