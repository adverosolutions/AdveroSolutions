I’m Andrea, the founder of May Bookkeeping, LLC. I started my accounting career in Arizona with one of the largest, family-owned dairy farms in the United States. I left the dairy in 2012 and started working on a freelance basis while raising my two small children. In 2018, when I moved back to my roots in South Florida, I was ready to start my own firm. I teamed up with some amazingly talented people so I could explore more business growth opportunities. The goal was to begin offering additional support services ( see full list here ) to the creative entrepreneurs I meet and address as many of their needs as possible. Prior to bookkeeping, I was a commercial real estate landlord agent for 9 years. In this role, I utilized my love for numbers, sharp attention to detail, and my ability to problem solve to lease a 2 million square foot Class A/B building portfolio. I quickly realized I loved working with both numbers AND creative people! I graduated with a Master’s degree from the University of Florida (Go Gators!), and in my free time I love running, rock climbing, relaxing at the beach, and hanging out with my family and children. I strive for a healthy work/life balance and encourage my team to do the same. May Bookkeeping, LLC is looking for clients interested in building long-term relationships with us; clients who are flexible, creative, and are eager to collaborate to achieve their financial goals. We hope this is you! May Bookkeeping Values Our firm provides exceptional service to our clients because of our dedication to the three underlying principles: commitment, responsiveness and quality. May Bookkeeping, LLC was established in 2018 for the purpose of helping small business owners achieve their professional milestones. Each client receives close, personal and professional attention. We are dedicated to making your business our business and, as a result, are committed to helping clients attain their financial goals. We are available and flexible for our clients. We strive to to provide insightful advice, allowing clients to make informed financial decisions. We pride ourselves on organization, efficiency, dependability and confidentiality.
