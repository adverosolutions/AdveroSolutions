About us Holloway Building Services We Sell Clean! About us Meet Brian. About Hi, I’m Brian Holloway. Let’s get to know each other. Brian Holloway has spent 21 years perfecting his craft in the commercial cleaning industry. His involvement in the cleaning industry has been far-reaching over the years. Before founding Holloway Building Services in July 2019, he worked for Sturgiss Janitorial. Starting as a custodian, he worked his way up to President of the company in just six years. While at Sturgiss, Brian learned all he could about the ins and outs of running a business. In May 2020, Brian purchased the majority of Sturgiss Janitorial and now operates as Holloway Building Services. HBS operates in the Columbus metro area, Licking County, and everything in between. When not running the business, Brian enjoys spending time with his wife, Brittany (who is also his middle-school sweetheart!), two daughters Ashlynne and Cora, and Siberian Husky King Kai. He also enjoys hunting and working every day to be the best version of himself! Brian is fully trained in protocols relating to eliminating COVID-19 threats from your business or building.
