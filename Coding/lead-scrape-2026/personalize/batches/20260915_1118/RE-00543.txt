We partner directly with founders and leadership teams to run contingent and project-based searches across technical and business functions. Our work is grounded in a clear understanding of how hiring needs evolve as companies grow, and the importance of finding people who align with a company’s stage, expectations, and culture. 4Ys Partners was built to offer a more focused and accountable alternative to traditional recruiting firms. Every search is led personally, with an emphasis on thoughtful outreach, strong candidate alignment, and long-term fit. In addition to search, we also support companies through embedded recruiting and advisory engagements when additional support is needed during periods of growth or transition. If you’re building your team or want to improve how you approach hiring, we’re always open to a conversation.
