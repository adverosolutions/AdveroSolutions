About Disston Search 0 Disston Search is a full service retained executive search firm committed to partnering with non-profit organizations to identify and recruit key leaders. We provide and execute a comprehensive search process built on a foundation of communication and transparency. We understand the importance of representing our clients authentically in the market and we believe time spent at the start of a search developing an understanding of the dynamics of the role, the existing team, governance and the culture of an organization ultimately results in a stronger search. We believe an active recruiting strategy that targets specific individuals and organizations results in a more robust and diverse candidate pool. We take pride in the relationships we forge with our clients throughout the course of a search and believe in providing transparent market and candidate feedback to help facilitate a successful completion. We believe that candidate diversity is critical to a successful search and as such are committed to identifying and presenting a diverse slate of candidates for every engagement.
