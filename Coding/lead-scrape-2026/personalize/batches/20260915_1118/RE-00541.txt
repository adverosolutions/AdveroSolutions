Our Story Wilson Workforce Solutions was founded in 2020 with one simple belief: the right hire can change everything . After years working in corporate talent acquisition, founder Katie Wilson saw firsthand how many businesses struggled to find qualified, aligned talent—and how many job seekers felt lost or overlooked in the process. Recruiting is often the first function to be cut, yet it is also one of the most essential to long-term success when being proactive. So she set out to build a different kind of recruiting firm—one that’s agile, solution-oriented, and rooted in authentic relationships. Why We re Different We’re not just here to fill roles. We’re here to strengthen teams and support people through pivotal transitions. Our boutique approach means you get personalized service from experienced talent acquisition professionals who understand the realities of hiring today. For employers, we provide the strategic lift you need to streamline hiring, reduce time-to-fill, and improve retention. For candidates, we bring empathy, expertise, and a human touch to every resume, application, and interview. We can access the vast Top Echelon Network to broaden these services. OUR SERVICES Why Us?
