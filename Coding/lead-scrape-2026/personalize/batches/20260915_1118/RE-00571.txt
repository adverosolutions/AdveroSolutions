We believe that finding the best talent is more art than science, which is why client and candidate relationships are the foundation of our business. With a deep understanding of our clients’ business goals, culture, and values we are able to create a lasting fit for candidates’ skills, personalities, and philosophies. Our high-touch approach builds trusted relationships with both candidates and clients, ensuring we consistently deliver exceptional results. Terri Boyd Terri established Boyd Consulting Partners in 2009 with a unique mission: offering hands-on, personalized service to boutique Investment and Wealth Management Firms. Setting her firm apart from traditional retained search firms, Terri focuses on tailoring her services to the specific needs of her clients, delivering top-notch talent, whether for C-level positions or Associate roles. Prior experience includes 17 years with Management Recruiters International during which time she achieved the following: Rookie of the year, Account Executive of the year, Rookie Manager of the Year, Manager of the Year and Rookie office of the year.
