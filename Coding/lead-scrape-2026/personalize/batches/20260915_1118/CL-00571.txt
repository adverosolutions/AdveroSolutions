About King For more than 50 years, King Excavating has been a trusted partner to manufacturers and businesses across the Tri-State area. Family-owned since 1967, we’ve grown from a small demolition firm into a full-service provider of waste management, hauling, recycling, industrial cleaning, water blasting, excavation, and environmental services. Our team delivers reliable, cost-effective solutions tailored to each client’s needs—whether it’s large-scale industrial work or smaller specialized projects. When you work with King, you don’t just get a service—you gain a partner who treats your business like our own.
