Taylor Willis CPA's and Advisors started in 2008 when Roy Taylor and Kevin Willis came together to create a practice due to a passion to do what they love and serve their community of the Greater New Orleans area. Prior to coming together to form Taylor Willis and Advisors, Roy Taylor was a CFO for a group of closely held companies and was highly experienced in internal reporting for large businesses. Kevin Willis took over his Dad's public accounting practice and brought the knowledge of public accounting services of tax, accounting, and consulting. Throughout the years, we had the opportunity to merge with one of the Top 20 accounting firms in the country. While there, we were able to gain insight into servicing clients while being part of a much larger firm. After about 2.5 years, we de-merged and became Taylor Willis CPA's again to continue servicing our clients as Taylor Willis always has in , New Orleans, , and the surrounding areas.
