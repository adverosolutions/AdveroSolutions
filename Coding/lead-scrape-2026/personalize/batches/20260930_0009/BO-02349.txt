OCA WORKS WITH FUND MANAGERS TO CREATE INSTITUTIONALLY VIABLE OFFERINGS - OCA-Consulting Home OCA WORKS WITH FUND MANAGERS TO CREATE INSTITUTIONALLY VIABLE OFFERINGS Private funds face a dilemma early in their growth cycle: commit time, money and resources in an attempt to generate alpha, or hire senior non-investment personnel to support the business and create an institutional infrastructure? Without the creation of alpha the business isn't viable. But without an institutional platform, fundraising will be limited. OCA Consulting provides fund managers with a solution to this problem. OCA provides access to an executive suite of experienced personnel without requiring the risk and long-term commitment of multiple senior-level hires. OCA allows managers to focus on their strategy by handling all non-investment issues that face private funds. When one operational misstep can mean the loss of an investment, it is vital to build sound practices that allow allocators to commit capital constrained only by your fund’s ability to generate favorable returns. OCA will guide your team in building operational, compliance and accounting procedures that create efficiencies and present well to potential investors.
