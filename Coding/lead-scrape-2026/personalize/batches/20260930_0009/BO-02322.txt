With over 25 years of accounting experience, her career spans across the former era of “Big 6” public accounting, corporate financial reporting, and the development of technical accounting publications and training initiatives. Like many working moms, Lori sought the elusive work-life balance. She found it by becoming a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor and quickly discovered her true passion: collaborating with the ambitious minds of small and midsize business owners. Lori's talent for building trusted relationships with business owners is a cornerstone of her success. In fact, it was under the mentorship of an established business owner that she found the inspiration to launch Blueprint Accounting Solutions. Her diverse accounting expertise is coupled with a sincere desire to help business owners succeed. She is a trusted advisor, providing invaluable insight and analytics that help busy entrepreneurs not only understand their financial statements but also strategize for long-term growth. Lori’s collaborative approach creates a partnership where clients feel confident and informed. Outside of work, Lori has been enjoying navigating the new “empty nest stage” with her kids off to college. She enjoys cooking, trying new restaurants, and traveling with her family.
