About - What is a Fiduciary CFO? Skip to content SCHEDULE AN INTRO CALL About Fiduciary CFO What's a Fiduciary CFO? A financial professional that serves business owners with the same forward thinking tenacity as a CFP® but applies it to the business finances. A financial professional who recognizes that a business is the wealth building engine of the business owner, and every financial decision made in the business impacts financial decisions at home. A financial professional that communicates and collaborates with the business owners outside advisors. A financial professional that understands and manages the business and personal tax situation. A fractional CFO on financial steroids. Brian Wright, MBA, CFP® Co-Founder Fiduciary CFO Brian is a seasoned financial professional with over 20 years of business and financial consulting experience. A financial planner by trade, he provides business consulting and planning for Fiduciary CFO clients. Brian owns Wright Planning, LLC , a lifestyle RIA practice that specializes in financial planning for business owners. In 2014 he co-founded FA BeanCounters, LLC (the first virtual accounting and bookkeeping firm to target RIAs and Independent Broker Dealers) which sold to XY Planning Network in 2017.
