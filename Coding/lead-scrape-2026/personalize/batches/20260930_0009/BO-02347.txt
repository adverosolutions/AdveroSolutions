Since 2005, CPG has been helping many companies to start, grow and prosper. CPG provides personalized CFO CRO solutions for companies and individuals, from corporate finance, financial statements, tax savings, and cash accumulation to estate and financial planning and overall wealth management. We have a philosophy: serving client professionally with all we have. Note: “CPG” and “Cindie Professional Group” are marketing brands used by Cindie LLC (NJ Registered CPA Firm) and Cindie US LLC (FL S-Corporation – Tax Advisory). All services are performed by the appropriate legal entity as applicable. Cindy Yu is a licensed CPA in Florida, New Jersey, and New York , and the managing partner of CPG. With deep experience in taxation, audit, compliance, and cross-border business consulting, Cindy leads the CPG group of firms with a commitment to professionalism, integrity, and exceptional client services. Cindy Yu is an Author, MBA MS, CPA, CFP, past Adjunct Professor with Fairleigh Dickson University, managing partner, with over 20 years of valuable experience in helping business with their audit, financial, tax, compliance and cash flow needs. Ms. Yu received a M.S. in accounting from University of Delaware and M.B.A from Regis University. Ms. Yu is a licensed CPA since 1995 and a Certified Financial Planner by the CFP board. She also earned various professional designations and licenses in computer science, management accounting, investment and insurance.
