FiscalGrit - Portfolio | FiscalGrit Guy Gaffney, MBA Prior to starting FiscalGrit, I spent many years in commercial lending as a portfolio manager, loan officer, and credit analyst. Over the years, I have worked with countless small businesses of every type, size, and industry -- everything from sole proprietorships to corporations to non-profits. Sometimes they were solopreneurs with little more than an idea, other times they were larger companies with several hundred employees and millions of dollars in sales. Some were retailers restauranteurs, others were engineering firms government contractors. And many were active in real estate as builders or investors. Safe to say, I spent a lot of time reviewing and analyzing their financial statements and tax returns. During this time, I noticed that many small businesses have a distinct need for someone to help with their financial information. Most of the time, these business owners excelled at their craft and spent their time producing whatever it was that caused them to start their own business in the first place. The financial aspect was the least of their concerns...as long as there was cash in the bank account, that is. Sometimes that was just fine because of the size of their business. But other times, that lack of good and up-to-date financial information and insight caused real problems for them. Avoiding those kinds of problems is what FiscalGrit is here for.
