About Us - O connor, Brooks Co., P.C. Skip to content About Us Since first opening its doors in 1920, O Connor, Brooks Co., P.C. has dedicated itself, through the efforts of its employees, to fulfill the firm s mission statement. The mission statement to provide quality professional services which contribute to the growth and success of our clients, our community and our firm while never losing the personal relationships and local identity that are the foundations of our practice defines the everyday operations of O Connor, Brooks Co., P.C. Although the company s formal organization type has evolved from the sole proprietor to partnership to the current professional corporation, the efforts of the firm remain constant and true to its commitment of time and expertise to each of its clients. Whether it be income tax preparation, payroll services, financial statement compilation, or management advisory services, firm personnel strive to maintain the highest standards during their performance in accordance with the mission statement. Many changes have occurred at the firm over the past ninety-five years including the opening of an office in Galena, Illinois and an expansion of the Dubuque, Iowa location. Each location has staff qualified to provide the best professional service to its clients. Company staffing includes certified public accountants licensed to practice in the states of Iowa, Illinois and Wisconsin, as well as income tax professionals, payroll processing specialists and financial statement analysis. O Connor, Brooks Co., P.C. is proud to continue the rich tradition established in 1920 by its founder M.E.
