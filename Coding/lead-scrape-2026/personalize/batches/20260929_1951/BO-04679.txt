About Polaris Tax and Business Services 0 A Balanced Approach to Business After many years working as a CFO and as an analyst, Chad and Shannon left their W-2 jobs in search of something more. They launched Polaris Tax and Business Services out of a desire to find more purpose and balance in their work and use their unique strengths to help small businesses do that too. Now, nearly ten years later, they’re more passionate than ever about empowering their clients to understand and improve their business finances. Based in Cleveland, Ohio, Polaris Tax and Business Services supports photographers, artists, makers, manufacturers, and rental property owners located all across the country. Chad Herron Enrolled Agent Growing up in the family business, Chad saw the many rewards and pressures of being a small business owner. This perspective, combined with 15 years of financial leadership experience, informs Chad’s unique ability to help individuals and small businesses tackle the pressures of bookkeeping, taxes, and business decisions so they can focus on the rewards. After earning a degree in accounting and economics from the University of Richmond, Chad launched his accounting career as the controller of his family’s business. Ready for a new challenge, he then went on to join a fast-growing product manufacturing firm, Nite Ize, where he served as Controller and CFO. Throughout his 10-year tenure, the company grew from 40 employees to more than 200. Chad helped the company adapt to this growth while preserving long-term financial stability. When Chad isn’t crunching numbers, he enjoys exercising, gardening, reading, and hiking with Shannon and their dog Chewie.
