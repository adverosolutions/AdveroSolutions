Long-term stewardship. Since 1946, Erickson & Brooks has supported clients with disciplined accounting, tax, assurance, and advisory work grounded in judgment, continuity, and care. Our Mission Our mission is to deliver accounting, assurance, and advisory work that holds up over time through disciplined execution, documented methods, and professional judgment. Our Standards Independence and quality control are governed by firm policy and external peer review. Engagements follow documented procedures, supervisory review, and confidentiality controls that protect objectivity and client trust. Continuing professional education and technical consultation are required for all client work. Independence Quality Control Confidentiality Professional Excellence PRINCIPLES OF PRACTICE Principles of Practice These principles govern how work is planned, reviewed, and delivered — protecting independence, quality, and long-term responsibility. Integrity Objective judgment, confidentiality, and accountability in every engagement. Independence Advice and conclusions are governed by standards, evidence, and professional responsibility. Ownership Ownership requires responsibility for process, follow-through, and the quality and timeliness of the work. Standards Work is expected to be clear, accurate, well-reviewed, and continuously improved. Defined methods, consistent review, and disciplined execution support reliable work and long-term client trust. Stewardship Decisions are made with a long-term view, with care for clients, firm standards, and the responsibilities entrusted to us. FIRM HISTORY Eight Decades of Practice Since 1946 Nearly eighty years of client service, professional standards, and community trust. Founded in 1946, Erickson & Brooks has served Fremont and the surrounding region through generations of client relationships, business transitions, and an evolving profession. The firm’s history reflects more than longevity. It reflects relationships earned over time, standards carried forward, and a commitment to serving clients with judgment, care, and professional responsibility.
