About Marquis Harris | Noble Edge Bookkeeping About Built Around a Simple Belief About Business Owners Noble Edge Bookkeeping was built around a simple belief: business owners deserve to understand the numbers behind their businesses. Marquis Harris Founder, Noble Edge Bookkeeping Meet the founder Marquis Harris, Founder With an accounting background and experience working across multiple industries, Marquis Harris founded Noble Edge to provide business owners with accurate bookkeeping, meaningful financial reporting, and the confidence that comes from knowing their numbers. Too often, bookkeeping is treated as data entry — transactions get categorized, reports get emailed, and the business owner is left to guess at what any of it means. Marquis built Noble Edge to work the other way around: get the records right first, then use them to explain what s actually happening in the business. That means asking questions about how your business operates, structuring your books so the reports make sense, and walking through the numbers in plain language — profitability, cash flow, expenses, and where things are trending. Noble Edge is intentionally a boutique practice. You work directly with the person handling your books, which means personal attention, consistent communication, and a real understanding of your business rather than a ticket in a queue.
