Roy Associates was founded in 1981 by Adrien (Joe) Roy, Jr., CPA. We have been successfully servicing our clients ever since. Our Mission To help our clients succeed by participating in major business decisions and planning for the future and by providing the highest quality tax, consulting, and accounting services available. Our Service Areas Although our client base is most heavily concentrated in the greater Bangor area, our service area extends throughout the country as our clients relocate and follow new opportunities abroad. Modern technology allows us to service our clients without the physical limits of geography. Our Objectives Provide high-quality accounting, federal and state tax, and management advisory services to progressive organizations. Help each client reach maximum potential and efficiency through effective accounting, financial, and management consulting services. Further develop areas of concentration within the firm such as medical and dental practices, public housing authorities and development corporations, and real estate transactions. Be actively involved in professional business, community, and civic affairs. Professional Membership We are current members of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA), the Maine Society of Certified Public Accountants, the Affordable Housing Association of Certified Public Accountants, Maine Affordable Housing Coalition, and the Bangor Region Chamber of Commerce.
