The Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction can be valuable for eligible business owners, but it isn’t as simple as taking 20% of whatever your business made. Your eligibility and deduction can depend on your taxable income, business type, W-2 wages, qualified property, and other limitations. That’s why QBI should be part of the planning conversation, not something you think about only when the tax return is being prepared.
