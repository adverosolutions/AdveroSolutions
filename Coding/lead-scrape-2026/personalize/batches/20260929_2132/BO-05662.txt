Starla Smith, CPA, CFE, NTPI Fellow Starla Smith, CPA, is the Founder and Owner of Stellar Accounting LLC, where she helps individuals and small business owners relieve the burden and stress of all things tax. She is licensed as a Certified Public Accountant in Alabama and holds several respected credentials, including Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE), NTPI®? Fellow, and QuickBooks Advanced Certified ProAdvisor. Starla also serves as President of the Alabama Chapter of the National Association of Tax Professionals. With a strong foundation as a former corporate controller—having held leadership roles in energy companies including Shell and BP—Starla brings both big-business perspective and hands-on experience to her solo practice. She provides tax preparation, planning, IRS and State of Alabama tax representation, and QuickBooks clean-up services, always tailoring her approach to each client’s unique situation. Starla is especially passionate about helping clients: Catch up on unfiled tax returns Respond to IRS and state notices Navigate audits and compliance reviews Find workable solutions for unpaid or disputed tax balances Some recent client successes include: Reconstructing ten years of business records to file overdue tax returns Obtaining equitable relief for a divorced client facing joint tax liability Recovering levied funds from a client’s bank account Assisting clients with IRS and state audits, including program audits and sales tax reviews Defending education credits under audit with supporting documentation Starla stays current on the ever-changing tax landscape through frequent professional seminars and advanced training. As an NTPI Fellow, she has completed a rigorous three-level program focused on all aspects of taxpayer representation before the IRS.
