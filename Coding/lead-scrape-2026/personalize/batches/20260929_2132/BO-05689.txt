My Accounting Department Small Business Accountants We run the numbers so you can run your business. home contact about us setup training accounting coaching & consulting Who Is My Accounting Department? We are a business dedicated to helping small and growing businesses thrive and grow. Our Principal, Jim Seymour, has been in business over 40 years and a small business owner for over 20 years, during which time he watched and experienced the positives and negatives of operating a small business. He has watched as numerous small business owners found themselves so wrapped up in the paperwork of the business that they were unable to effectively do the work that would truly make the business successful. My Accounting Department allows the business owner to offload the repetitive and focus on the meaningful. Physical Location In the real world we are in Fort Pierce Florida having relocated from Holland, Michigan in 2013. Yes, it is hard to believe it has been 4 years. In the cyber world we are located on your computer at: myaccountingdepartment.org Through the cyber world, we can communicate and share data no matter where in the world our customers may be located, utilizing secure data storage sites which include regular back-ups to protect valuable data.
