About Us - Bank History | DeWitt Bank Trust Co. About Us Home About Us About Us At DeWitt Bank Trust Co., we believe banking should be personal, simple, and built on trust. As a locally owned community bank with offices in DeWitt and Wilton, we proudly serve Clinton, Cedar, Scott, and Muscatine Counties and the surrounding area. Founded in 1893 as Farmers and Citizens Savings Bank, our institution has been rooted in the community for more than 130 years. In 1934, during the Great Depression, we became DeWitt Bank Trust Co. A Community Bank Being locally owned means decisions are made right here in our communities. We understand the needs of our communities because we live and work in them too. Our team is not just bankers; we are your neighbors, your friends, people you know and trust. We take pride in delivering both professional expertise and personal service. One-third of our employees have been with us for more than a decade, bringing experience, consistency, and commitment to our customers. Our employees take pride in being involved in our community, serving on various boards and community committees, volunteering their time. Everything You Need All In One Place Whether you re opening your first checking account, buying your first home, growing a business, operating a farm, or planning for retirement, we re here to support you every step of the way. We take the time to understand your goals and help you build a path to achieve them. We offer a full range of financial services under one roof. A one-stop shop making it easier for you to manage every aspect of your financial life. Our services include: Personal and business deposit accounts Loans for consumers, businesses, and farmers A full-service tax and accounting division, including income tax preparation, bookkeeping, and payroll services Investment services through Cetera Networks to help you plan for education, retirement, and your financial legacy Trust services, including estate administration, trust management, IRA custody, and asset management This unique combination allows us to provide one of the most comprehensive and integrated financial service offerings in the area. Why Choose Us? At DeWitt Bank Trust Co., it s not just about the products we offer; it s about the people behind them. With financial experience, local decision-making, and a commitment to service, we focus on building lasting relationships. Your goals are our priority.
