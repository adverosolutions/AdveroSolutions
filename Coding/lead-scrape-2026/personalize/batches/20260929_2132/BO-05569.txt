About Us | David Saathoff CPA --> About Us David Saathoff CPA PLLC is privately-owned accounting firm located in Vancouver, Washington. We provide a variety of tax and accounting services to individuals and businesses. Our clients are our most valuable assets. One of our goals when helping clients is to have them pay the lowest amount of tax possible. With tax laws constantly changing, our team stays on top of the changes to provide the best service possible to our clients. In addition, we have an online client portal to provide faster service to our clients. We have been proudly serving the Vancouver and Portland metro area since 1984 and continue to take pleasure in assisting our clients with their ever-growing needs. We have a talented team of accountants and staff to support our clients. Meet our team below. Our Team David Saathoff Owner, Certified Public Accountant David is a licensed Certified Public Accountant.
