Johnson Global Advisory Contact us About Johnson Global Advisory Global audit regulation has changed the public accounting industry to one laden with financial, legal, and reputational risks over non-compliance. Whether your firm audits publicly-traded companies or privately- held organizations, public accounting firms are under increasing pressure to improve audit quality and comply with a constantly changing regulatory environment. Often times, local and international audit rules and regulations are different from those in the United States, but firms are expected to comply with all applicable rules. This landscape is making the burdens of compliance difficult for domestic and foreign firms alike. ABOUT JGA Johnson Global Advisory ( JGA ) is a highly-experienced team of audit and audit regulation experts dedicated to helping public accounting firms achieve the highest level of audit quality. We work with our clients to understand their current situation, and identify and implement improvements to how audits are conducted and how a firm monitors those audits. Our vast experience conducting inspections of domestic and foreign firms in dozens of countries across six continents, allows us to provide a global perspective on audit and regulatory compliance. In addition, our public company audit experience within global network firms in several countries provides our client a unique resource to tap into who understands the complexities that Non-U.S. auditors face when auditing U.S.-listed issuers. Our advisory services to firms worldwide help them comply with today s regulatory demands while building a stronger foundation for the future. We help firms by applying a preventative and detective approach to identify, interpret, and remediate audit quality concerns. And we do this by providing insight into the performance of audit engagements and the firm s system of quality control. As a result, we reduce the risk firms face over their assurance practice. Additionally, we provide technical accounting, internal audit, and other services to public and private companies in the U.S. and abroad. Our unique background is valuable to our public and private company clients given our deep experience in internal controls over financial reporting and understanding what auditors need to get through an audit as effectively as possible.
