Chief Executive Officer Timothy Stecher Tim Stecher, the founder and CEO of 3TECH Staffing, is a veteran talent acquisition professional with more than 25 years of experience in the field. Stecher possesses broad expertise across the spectrum of staffing competencies. Tim specializes in creating sustainable strategic partnerships with clients focusing on talent acquisition, technology services, and software solutions. 3TECH marks his return to the staffing world after a successful five-year foray into the non-profit world with Easterseals DC MD VA of Silver Spring, Md. There, Tim served as Executive Vice President of their veteran employment program, the Veteran Staffing Network, which he nurtured from innovative concept to fully sustainable program, and the backbone of the organization’s veteran services. Tim’s success with the VSN, taking it from the idea stage to a firmly established social enterprise business and core competency, was integral in Easterseals being selected to host the DC area veteran’s mental health clinic for the Cohen Veterans Network. Prior to his time with Easterseals, Tim he was directly responsible for the development and start-up of his own minority-owned staffing company, after a successful tenure at Siemens Government Services, where he rose to National Director of Talent Acquisition. In the community, Tim serves on several non-profit boards, including the Board of Directors for Easter Seals DC MD VA, the Maryland Youth Lacrosse Association (MYLA), North Carroll Recreation Council and the Gonzo Lacrosse Club, a youth program in Carroll County which he also founded.
