About - Executive Levels Skip to content About Executive Levels Technical recruiting with passion because no client or candidate is “just a number.” Founder/President Robin Owens-Wright Executive Levels is based in the Washington, DC area and supports searches throughout the United States (and beyond). Founder Robin Owens-Wright started Executive Levels with a simple belief: the best hires happen when you match skills + environment fit and not just keywords on a résumé. After graduating from American University (Business degree, minor in Marketing) and beginning a program at Johns Hopkins , Robin stepped away as demand grew, and leaned all-in to build a recruiting partner for companies that need hard-to-find technical talent. Her background includes experience at large and small technology minded companies including 2-4 person startups to BlueCross, Booz Allen and Canada Dry , where she learned what great teams look like and realized the most rewarding part was understanding real career paths and using smart outreach to find “hidden gem” talent.
