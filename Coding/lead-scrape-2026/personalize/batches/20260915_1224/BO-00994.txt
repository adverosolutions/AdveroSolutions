About Ficsori Accounting 0 Rock your financial world Hi, I'm Frank, the owner and founder of Ficsori Accounting LLC. I am a highly skilled accountant and bookkeeping professional with extensive experience in taxation law. I began my career working for a law firm where I helped clients settle their tax debts with the IRS through the submission of an Offer in Compromise. My role involved cleaning up years of mess and comingled money in business and personal accounts, as well as generating accurate financial reports. This work was crucial in helping one particular client settle hundreds of thousands of dollars in tax debt to just $19. As a result of my work, I realized the value of accurate financial reporting and analysis, and saw the high cost that clients were paying just to maintain their financial records.
