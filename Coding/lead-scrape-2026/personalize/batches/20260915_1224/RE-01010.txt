Our Story Global Edge Recruiting was founded in 1997 with a focus on helping companies find talented professionals and helping individuals find meaningful career opportunities. Over the years, the medical and healthcare industries have evolved dramatically. Products and technologies have changed, healthcare organizations have transformed, and the skills required of sales and leadership professionals have continued to develop. Our recruiting expertise evolved with those changes. Today, Global Edge Recruiting specializes in medical sales, healthcare, life sciences, and executive recruiting , with experience across a broad range of specialized industries and positions. Specialized Experience Medical and healthcare recruiting is not one-size-fits-all. A successful medical device sales representative may have a very different background from a pharmaceutical representative, biotechnology professional, clinical specialist, medical sales engineer, or executive leader. Our experience across these specialized markets allows us to understand the differences between industries, positions, customers, and levels of responsibility.
