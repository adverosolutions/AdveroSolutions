Box 1511 Plainville, Ma., 02762-1511 How we started and where we re going As far back as I can remember, I've always been around business operations. My parents operated a retail flower and vegetable business, sold Christmas trees and managed several rental properties. While attending Boston University, I started and ran a successful ice cream business. In the early 80 s while spending time at our winter home in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida, I met and befriended Jim Faulhaber who was operating a successful power washing business . I immediately saw the vast potential of this wonderful industry and was lucky enough to have someone unselfish enough to fuel my interest. Without doing any real market research in my area of New England, I moved forward. There were five operating power washing companies in my direct market area when we opened for business in the mid 1980s. Within one year all five businesses had vanished. This was partly due to the economic conditions present in the tough North East market at the time and to some extent the presence of a young aggressive service oriented company- Mobile Power Wash of New England . From day one, Mobile Power Wash of New England was operated as a serious cleaning BUSINESS dedicated to customer service. Basic values were applied that were instilled growing up in our family business- treat customers the way we would want to be treated and always try to make every encounter a win-win situation.
