Since 1982 she has been guiding, managing and organizing financial resources as a Certified Public Accountant. With a background that includes working at a large international firm, practicing as a partner, and now, owning her own firm, she has polished her skills through hard work, and has the ability to spot actual and potential fiscal problems and unravel the worst financial mess with an astute evaluation of what works and how to achieve it most effectively. Whatever the needs of the client may be, MES exudes the confidence and initiative that derives from repeated success. Theirs is a formula that works.
