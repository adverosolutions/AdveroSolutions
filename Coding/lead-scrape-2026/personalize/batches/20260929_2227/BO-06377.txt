Our goal is to give you the peace of mind that your tax and accounting needs are being handled in a professional, accurate and confidential manner. What Makes Summit Accounting Group Different? When we launched Summit Accounting in 2002, we knew we wanted to provide our clients accounting services at the highest level. We have created an accounting firm whose primary focus is not only responding to our clients needs, but anticipating and preparing for the clients future tax situations. The diverse professional background of our CPA s, EA s and other professionals differentiates us. The fundamental way the firm is organized to serve clients makes us different. We work independently, or mobilize efficiently into teams, depending on our clients needs. Our Focus We maintain a sophisticated business and individual accounting practice typically associated with much larger firms. To maintain our standards of client service, we limit our practice to defined areas of professional focus in which we have developed experience and a proven track record. We do this because meeting our clients passion for quality requires our commitment to exceptional work. Our Backgrounds The professional diversity of our team makes Summit Accounting Group, Inc. a marketplace of experience. The firm s CPA s and EA's bring expertise acquired at prominent national accounting firms. We strive to educate each client on the tax process so that the client can be empowered in the day to day business decisions, confident that their business decisions are sound from a tax standpoint.
