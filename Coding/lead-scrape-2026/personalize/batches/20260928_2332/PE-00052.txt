ABOUT | Kuma Pest Control top of page Kuma Pest control (404)839-4883 About Kuma pest Control About Kuma Pest Control A Family-Owned Georgia Pest Control Company Serving Greater Atlanta Since 2014 Kuma Pest Control is a family-owned, Lawrenceville-based Georgia pest control company founded by Wayne Cooper in 2014. For more than a decade, Kuma has provided residential and commercial pest-management services throughout Gwinnett County, North Atlanta, Metro Atlanta and surrounding Greater Atlanta communities. Kuma was built around a simple idea: customers deserve professional pest control, clear communication, fair pricing and a company that stands behind its work. Today, Kuma helps homeowners, businesses, property managers, apartment communities, HOAs and other commercial customers manage common Georgia pest problems, including cockroaches, German roaches, ants, mosquitoes, bed bugs, rodents and wildlife. Meet the Owner of Kuma Pest Control Wayne Cooper Founder Owner Wayne Cooper is the founder and owner of Kuma Pest Control. Wayne founded Kuma in 2014 with the goal of creating a local Georgia pest-control company that customers could depend on for responsive service, professional technicians and straightforward communication. Rather than building Kuma around high-pressure sales or long-term customer commitments, the company was designed around earning customers continued business through quality service. That philosophy continues to influence how Kuma approaches pest control today. Kuma s Core Philosophy Identify the problem. Treat it appropriately. Communicate with the customer. Help prevent it from returning.
