--> About Dixon Landscape and Irrigation top of page About Dixon Landscape and Irrigation Hello everyone! I m Thomas Dixon, the proud founder of Dixon Landscape and Irrigation, established in 2014. For more than a decade, I dedicated myself to serving as a full-time fireman in Metro Atlanta. Like many firefighters, I initially ventured into this business as a part-time endeavor, drawing from my landscaping experiences since the age of 13, working alongside my father and others. Within a span of two years, our business blossomed, acquiring several full-time employees. Faced with the growth, a pivotal decision arose whether to scale back or bid farewell to my firefighting career, as the company demanded more of my attention. In 2017, I wholeheartedly embraced entrepreneurship, stepping away from the fire department to focus on our thriving venture. The rest, as they say, is history! ​ Let me introduce you to my wonderful wife, Joy Dixon, a dedicated Cherokee County school teacher with a decade of experience. Together, we share a deep passion for serving our community and nurturing our faith in Jesus, a cornerstone of our lives. Our family includes two cherished children, Emma and Levi.
