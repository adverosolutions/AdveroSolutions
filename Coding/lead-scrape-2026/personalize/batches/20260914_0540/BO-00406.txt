Diaz, CPA, leads a small but professional team dedicated to providing expert financial services. Housed in the historic Garner Hulsey House on Gainesville s Green Street, our company is a local business committed to serving our community. We believe in serving others by providing professional services to nonprofits throughout North Georgia. While nonprofits are our specialty, we also provide quality accounting services to small businesses and individuals including bookkeeping, payroll, compilations, QuickBooks consulting, tax planning and tax preparation. Our seasoned staff provides personalized help to each and every client. Through reliable communication and quality accounting, we create accurate financial reports that limit surprises and improve your financial decisions. Our Staff Blair C. Diaz, CPA Principal Accountant Bachelor of Business Administration, University of Georgia, Class of 1993 Blair C. Diaz is a certified public accountant with more than three decades of experience in public accounting, serving businesses, individuals, and nonprofit organizations throughout North Georgia. She began her career with Coopers Lybrand, LLP in 1992 and established her own firm, Blair C. Diaz, CPA, PC, in 2004 at the historic Garner-Hulsey House on Green Street in Gainesville, Georgia. With a particular passion for the nonprofit sector, Blair and her firm have served more than 100 nonprofit organizations in the region. A frequent speaker on nonprofit finance and management, Blair is actively engaged in both her profession and the North Georgia community. She is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and the Georgia Society of Certified Public Accountants and is a graduate of the Greater Hall Chamber of Commerce s Leadership Hall program. Blair currently serves as Treasurer of the Board of Directors of the Humane Society of Northeast Georgia and Treasurer of the Gainesville Redevelopment Authority. She also serves as a grant reviewer for the North Georgia Community Foundation and as a member of the Rotary District 6910 Financial Review Committee.
