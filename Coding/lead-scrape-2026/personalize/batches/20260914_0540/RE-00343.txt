About us - Hertner, Block & Bowser HOME About us Lawyers Clients Contact HOME About us Lawyers Clients Contact Hertner, Block & Bowser Firm Profile Hertner, Block Bowser, Inc. (HBBI), was founded in 1984 to service the legal staffing needs for partners, associates and mergers of law firms. We also specialize in placing general counsel and staff counsel for corporations. With the growth of the legal profession over the past few decades, David Block and his staff focused all of their recruiting efforts on legal search, working with premier law firms and corporations in expanding their staffs. HBBI concentrates its efforts in the state of Florida and has also developed a network of leading search firms in all major cities in the United States. The firm has earned a reputation of handling both clients and candidates on an ethical and confidential basis. HBBI is a member of the National Association of Legal Search Consultants (NALSC), a national professional organization committed to upholding the highest ethical standards in the legal search field . Consultant Profiles David J. Block, Esq. Mr. Block is a 1992 graduate of the University of Miami School of Law, where he served as Vice President of Entertainment and Sports Law Society. He practiced general civil litigation in Florida for several years. He was also a Supervising Attorney for the United States Small Business Administration, delivering agency relief to disaster victims and also worked as an athlete agent representative. Mr. Block has developed a large network of in-house clients and is comfortable in providing advice to clients and candidates as to the economics of the business of law. Contact David Danielle G.
