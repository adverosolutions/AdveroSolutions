With a rich background in locums recruitment for top healthcare providers and Investment Banking Private Equity at Selby Jennings, Sarah brings a wealth of experience to the table. Outside of work, Sarah is a passionate scratch golfer and wine enthusiast who’s recently relocated to Dallas, TX. As a mom of two under two, she knows a thing or two about juggling priorities and brings that same balance and energy to her professional life. If you’re looking for a recruitment partner who’s approachable, dedicated, and focused on your growth in finance, legal, and operations, Sarah and the Trovo Group team are here for you.
