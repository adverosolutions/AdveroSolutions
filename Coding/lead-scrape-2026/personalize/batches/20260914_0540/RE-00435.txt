Diversity, Equity Inclusion Corporate Social Responsibility Corporate Social Responsibility TMP, Inc. values diversity in our team, our clients and in our suppliers. We believe that diversity makes us a stronger organization and more compassionate participants in our communities. Diversity and inclusion are embedded within every aspect of our culture, our delivery and our business strategy. We are committed to attracting and retaining qualified talent from diverse backgrounds for our clients and our internal teams. We also vet and select our vendors with an eye toward DE I. TMP believes in fostering a culture that values every member for their distinctive skills, experiences, and points of view. We recognize that our clients value DE I in their workplaces and intentionally invest our time in understanding our clients’ cultures and goals with respect to DE I so that we can play an integral role in helping them achieve their goals. Corporate Social Responsibility Corporate Social Responsibility Corporate Social Responsibility TMP Inc. is committed to operating at the highest ethical manner and contributing to economic development and sustainability while improving the quality of life of for our employees and their families, as well as our local communities, the environment, and society. For our corporate employees we provide a 100% remote work arrangement to provide a work life balance, aid in the reduction of commuting carbon emissions and provide the opportunity to practice energy and resource conservation in the manner they choose for their workspace.
