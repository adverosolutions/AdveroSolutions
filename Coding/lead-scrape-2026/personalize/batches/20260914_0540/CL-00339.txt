About | Cherry Window Cleaning Skip to content About Cherry Window Cleaning Meet the Owner Tom Cherry Born and raised in Louisville, Kentucky, Tom is a tried and true Louisville native. After attending Georgetown College, Tom and his wife, Lauren, spent a handful of years in Colorado before being brought back to the great city of Louisville where they now raise their two children in St. Matthews. Being an entrepreneur at heart, Tom saw a need in the professional service industry. Whether the problem was the service provider not showing up, or simple unprofessionalism in a home, he saw a gap that needed to be filled in the window cleaning sector. Reliability and trust are key values for the Cherry family, and Tom wanted to implement those foundational principles within his business. And so began Cherry Window Cleaning! When he’s not taking care of business, Tom enjoys playing tennis, watching college basketball, and hiking with his dog Beau. Want Sparkling Clean Windows?
