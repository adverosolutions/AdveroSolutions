ALH Search is a boutique, woman-owned recruiting firm specializing in top Sales and Go-To-Market (GTM) talent for B2B startups and high-growth tech companies. We get how hard it is to hire in a fast-moving market—especially when every hire truly matters. Whether you're bringing on your first sales leader, building out an Account Executive team, or expanding into new markets, we bring the speed, precision, and startup-savvy to get it right. My goal is to make hiring simple and fast, without ever sacrificing quality. Our network includes top-performing AEs, BDRs, GTM leaders, and enablement pros who thrive in high-velocity environments and are ready to make an impact from day one. I’ve been recruiting Sales and GTM talent for startups since 2010, starting as one of the founding sales recruiters at LivingSocial. That experience sparked my passion for helping startups build high-impact revenue teams. I founded ALH Search to provide a flexible, high-touch recruiting experience tailored to the fast-paced, high-stakes world of startups. My approach is hands-on, relationship-driven, and grounded in a deep understanding of what it takes to scale early GTM functions.
