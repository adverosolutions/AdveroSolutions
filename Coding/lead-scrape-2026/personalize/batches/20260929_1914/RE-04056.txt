CoCreate Talent About CoCreate Talent | Our Team Story 0 Experts that Deliver Results As seasoned recruiting experts with a blend of in-house and executive recruiting experience, we understand the unique challenges and opportunities that arise during organizational growth. We offer strategic guidance to CEOs and founders, navigating them through most critical moments of scale. Our personalized approach and deep understanding of leadership dynamics set us apart. WORK WITH US Meet the Founders CoCreate was founded when three recruiting leaders discovered they had complementary expertise across the exact functions that founders and CEOs need most. Together, we have decades of experience building recruiting at Lyft, Verkada, Facebook, Airbnb, and True Search. We’re all based in the San Francisco Bay Area. CO-FOUNDER Ashley Dutra Ashley brings over a decade of experience partnering with Founders and CEOs of venture backed startups and public companies. Prior to starting CoCreate, Ashley was a Partner and early member of the People and Talent Practice at True Search, supporting the firm’s growth from 100-900+ people over seven years. Ashley also led GTM recruiting for Counsyl, a genetic testing company backed by Founders Fund and Felicis Ventures. She specializes in building the leadership functions of high-growth organizations, and providing counsel to CEOs and founders during their most critical moments in building. Beyond her professional pursuits, Ashley is dedicated to personal growth and entrepreneurship. She is deeply passionate about human design, meditation, and sustainable living. When she’s not working, you can find her hiking, at the local farmers market, or traveling. Connect on LinkedIn . CO-FOUNDER Morgan Shapiro Morgan is a seasoned recruiting professional with a proven track record of scaling high-growth tech companies. Originally from San Diego, she moved to the Bay Area in 2005. Her career journey began at Zynga, where she honed her skills in full-cycle recruiting for Business Partnerships roles. Over the next six years, she steadily rose through the ranks at Lyft, culminating in her role as Recruiting Director. At Verkada, Morgan played a pivotal role in the company's rapid expansion. As Director of Global Recruiting, she oversaw the successful growth of the company from 200 to 1800 employees.
