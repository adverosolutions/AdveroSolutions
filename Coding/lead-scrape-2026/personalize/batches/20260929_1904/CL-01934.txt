Our History Dear Earth Company is a locally owned small business in central Indiana. As our cities grow, we are thrilled to see Indiana as an epicenter in the Midwest. We recognize the unlimited possibilities to create a more sustainable and progressive living experience. Established in 2019, our core undertaking is to create jobs, boost our economy, and most importantly, move toward a cleaner and healthier earth. Together with our staff, Dear Earth Company has a significant amount of years of experience in the cleaning business. We know what the needs of our community are and we are here to fulfill them.
