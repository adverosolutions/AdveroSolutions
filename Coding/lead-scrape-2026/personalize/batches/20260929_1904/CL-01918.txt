About Hal Martin s We sell Rolex Watches Hal Martin’s Watch and Jewelry Company, which began as one collector’s impassioned hobby in 1973, has since served as the premier source for high-end, pre-owned Swiss watches. As a buyer and a seller Hal Martin’s has inevitably grown into one of Houston’s and the Southwest’s largest retailers of its type, with a fully stocked brick and mortar store to showcase an extensive selection of Swiss watches. With an insatiable penchant for pre-owned Rolex watches and the addition of a generous selection of accessories from watch winders, corporate gifts and diamond jewelry available at the 6266 Westheimer location just west of Houston’s famed Galleria, it makes Hal Martins your one stop shop for all your pre-owned Swiss watch needs. Hal Martin received his first Rolex timepiece as a graduation gift from his father in 1964, and from there grew a love of fine craftsmanship. After making his first Rolex sale for a $200 profit during his college years, Hal began collecting and selling sport, dive and Swiss watches for nearly a decade before finally deciding to make pre-owned luxury Swiss watches more than a hobby, instead making it a fulltime impassioned career. When putting together his current-day team, Hal went by this philosophy: “To be great, surround yourself with greatness.” And that he has done. Pre-owned luxury watches are an investment. Whether as a purchase for yourself, an award for your employee or a gift for a loved one, timepieces such as a Rolex, Cartier, IWC or Patek Philippe (to name just a few) appreciate while standing out as a symbol of accomplishments and the rewards it can afford one. Hal Martin’s devotion to offering the highest-quality timepieces, gifts and accessories is apparent in our personal approach. Our family is skillfully trained in luxury watches, and we are available to share our knowledge with you, about selecting, owning and storing a luxury Swiss timepiece. We invite you to enjoy the complexity and artistry of a used Rolex watch or custom jewelry piece as much as all of us at Hal Martin’s Watch and Jewelry do.
