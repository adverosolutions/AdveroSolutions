About AIR Talent Firm Founder and CEO, Ali Gates Who We Are AIR Talent Firm is a woman-owned talent recruiting firm proud to advocate for overlooked candidates, including women and individuals from diverse backgrounds. Our expertise on technology roles comes from more than a decade of direct experience in these roles. We don’t discriminate on the type of industry your company is in - if you need a technical expert, we will find you the right one! Our mission is to change the makeup of diversity in the technology industry. Instead of a 1:3 ratio of women to men in tech , we believe that ratio should reflect our general population numbers: 50/50 split. With a focus on candidates with the right experience, we believe we have the best solution to meet your hiring needs while contributing to larger change. Ali turned 15+ years of startup SaaS sales experience into a successful full-desk recruiting firm. Leveraging her relationships with a strong network along with her boots-on-the-ground experience, she helps align great candidates to great companies. Primarily focusing on roles within the Revenue Organization, she also recruits for technical roles on the engineering team. As a female founder, she is especially committed to DEI initiatives and bringing a very personal approach to recruiting. While she’s sold the NLP technology to read a resume and evaluate someone based just on paper, Ali recognizes the importance of having a human in the loop and taking the time to get to know a candidate and a company. Ali is a champion of the underdog, (this goes for candidates and companies), and loves it when everyone gets a big win! Her biggest difference? Taking the time to get to know a candidate beyond their resume and providing a holistic match to the company, team, and role. AIR Talent Firm succeeds in finding top talent in any industry for technology roles.
