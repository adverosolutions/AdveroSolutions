We’re in it for the long haul. ABOUT OUR FOUNDER Jen began recruiting in 2003, after 7 years in Account Management with advertising agencies in New York and San Francisco. Her early work in digital marketing for healthcare clients such as GSK and Johnson Johnson ultimately shaped her recruiting focus. She converted colleagues to clients based on her passion for these disciplines. Jen was the top producer and co-led the recruiting team at a successful digital search firm. In this role, she hired and trained several first-time recruiters and brought in many of the firm’s clients. In 2010, she launched Nadexa Group, named after her two daughters Nadia and Alexa. The name was insurance against having a third child, though the business quickly became one! Jen is drawn to recruiting because she loves working with business leaders to assemble the teams that help them grow. She is a marketer, interviewer and storyteller, and uses these skills to engage talent. A relentless cheerleader for entrepreneurs and side hustles, Jen regularly profiles business owners on her blog and socials and produced a 10-episode podcast called Small Business Speaks . Jen lives in Fort Collins, Colorado with her husband and daughters and enjoys running, cycling and skiing whenever she can.
