About | Discover Top Talent Today JPR HUMAN CAPITAL 0 About Josh Rubinstein A career hospitality veteran, Josh Rubinstein takes pride in representing inspired companies and talented hospitality executives across the United States. Josh began his career in boutique hotel and stand-alone restaurant operations and managed various Michelin-starred and James Beard award-winning restaurants, giving him a unique perspective on executive search. He later joined Korn Ferry as a recruiter and was quickly promoted to Managing Consultant, Executive Search for Travel, Hospitality Leisure. Wanting a more personalized and boutique experience, he joined the Elliot Group as Vice President of Executive Search. In 2021 Josh joined the Passionality Group leading Executive Search and Human Capital Management and in 2022, was appointed Partner, Head of Strategic Growth Partnerships. Simultaneously, Josh founded JPR Human Capital, an innately different search firm focusing on the relationship first.
