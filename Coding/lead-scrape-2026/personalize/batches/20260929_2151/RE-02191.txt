With an extensive background in the practice of law, legal operations, and legal recruiting, Bahareh Samanian connects top legal talent with law firms ranging in scope from AmLaw 200 to niche, boutique players. More than a legal recruiter, she is a strategic talent partner to her clients and an advocate for candidates. She creates mutually beneficial relationships between clients and candidates, which she accomplishes through partnership and collaboration. Bahareh is a member of the National Association of Legal Search Consultants (NALSC) , the only organization representing the legal search profession and committed to upholding the highest ethical standards by requiring its members to adhere to a code of ethics. She serves on the NALSC Belonging Inclusion Committee. Talent and DEI Leader. Bahareh led full-cycle talent acquisition for an AmLaw 100 firm for several years where she worked closely with the firm’s Managing Partner, Executive Committee, Diversity Executive Council, Practice Group Chairs, and office leadership to achieve major hiring initiatives, particularly at the partner level. During her time, the firm diversified, welcomed lateral partners, counsel, and associates in significant growth industries, and expanded several offices. With her team, she implemented DEI recruiting and integration strategies, pipeline programs, and recruited the firm’s first Chief Diversity Inclusion Officer. Bahareh supports local and national affinity organizations and is a member of the National Association of Women Lawyers (NAWL) . Litigator and Trial Attorney.
