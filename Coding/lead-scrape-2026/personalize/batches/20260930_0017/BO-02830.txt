Learn more Beyond Bookkeeping Start-up Guidance Business Owner 101 is all the tactical and back-end up-skilling so that you handle funds above board from day one, know how to pay yourself and develop comfort and confidence with reading financial statements. It’s being able to know enough about what you should be seeing in the books so that you can identify issues easily when you eventually hand-off the back-end management to another collaborator or employee. It’s making sure you’re registered with the proper State and Federal agencies, that you have your tax budget built into your planning from day one, and that you have basic internal controls and documents in place so that you’re protected and armored up as the business begins to scale. Cashflow Strategy Cashflow management is the number one challenge for most businesses, especially when revenue is seasonal or gets paid out in large lump-sums and contract milestones. We lean-in heavily on benchmarking Gross Profit margins against your industry's top performers to make sure the wind is in your sales and then use the Profit First Methodology to lean into monthly or weekly cashflow forecasting so that we can adapt to and/or avert tightness months before it arrives. The goal is that we help you manage cashflow months in advance so that you’re at ease, rather than scrambling or waiting for a check to clear every payroll.
