Steven G. Padgett CPA Steve Padgett has a Bachelors in Business Administration in Accounting from the University of Washington. Steve has over 15 years of experience working with small and medium-size businesses. Ray Padgett CPA Ray Padgett has over 35 years of experience in accounting and financial management.
