The right people become part of your culture, strengthen your organization, and help move your business forward. With more than 15 years of industry experience, our founders set out to create a different kind of nearshore partner. One that puts people and long-term relationships at the center of the business. We knew that building a nearshore outsourcing organization with an elevated culture would create stability and longevity in an industry known for volatility and turnover. Here’s how we do things differently: Building fulfilling careers, not just filling seats We don't believe in filling seats and moving on to the next placement. We believe in building careers, developing strong teams, and creating partnerships that grow and deepen over time. Prioritizing culture-first to find the right fit We invest in our people, support their success, and work closely with our clients to make sure their teams have what they need to thrive. This approach brings you talent that stays. Bespoke solutions for long-lasting hires Our outsourced nearshore staffers stay with their client companies for years, not weeks or days. That kind of commitment comes from knowing they are valued, providing great client onboarding and training, and setting up a growth path.
