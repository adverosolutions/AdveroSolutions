Our Approach Wendy Tibbetts, CPA, Managing Partner Wendy Tibbetts, CPA, MBA, is the Founder and Managing Partner of Apex Professionals. With over 25 years of experience in the accounting and finance industry, Wendy has held roles as a CFO, accountant, and financial analyst, in addition to successfully launching and managing two businesses of her own. Her background as both a financial executive and entrepreneur enables her to provide strategic, real-world guidance to business owners looking to grow profitably. Wendy founded Apex Professionals in February 2020 with the mission of empowering clients to make confident, year-round financial decisions—not just during tax season. She is committed to providing accurate, timely financial insights that help entrepreneurs improve profitability, strengthen operations, and reach their business goals. A licensed Certified Public Accountant, Wendy holds an MBA and is an active member of the National Association of Tax Professionals (NATP), the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA), and the Minnesota Society of CPAs (MNCPA). She is recognized for her strategic thinking, client-first mindset, and dedication to excellence in service. Under her leadership, Apex Professionals has become a trusted partner for business owners seeking proactive financial clarity and long-term success.
