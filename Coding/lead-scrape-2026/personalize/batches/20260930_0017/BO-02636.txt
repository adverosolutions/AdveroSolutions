Colorado Bookkeeping MEET US! Looking for an experienced bookkeeper? You’ve come to the right spot! Meet our owner, Ivana Petrovic, MPAcc. Ivana received her Bachelor’s in Accounting at Regis Univeristy and Master’s in Professional Accounting at CSU Global. With over 4 years of tax experience, Ivana has seen it all! Clean books and messy books have led to the creation of this business. Ivana saw a need for well communicating bookkeepers with knowledge of numbers. As a child of immigrants, it was always an American Dream to own her own business. It has been an exciting adventure creating and growing this business. Ivana is a Colorado native who loves what the Colorado outdoors have to offer along with traveling and spending time with the people she loves most!
