About Us Alpha Omega Accounting, LLC. Skip to content About Us Lighting Your Way to Financial Understanding Running a business or managing complex finances often means juggling far more than anyone ever planned for. Sales, payroll, compliance, vendors, and deadlines all compete for attention, and they rarely fit neatly into a nine to five schedule. We understand that pressure because we have spent decades guiding clients through it with clarity and steady support. Since 1998, Alpha Omega Accounting, LLC has helped business owners, investors, nonprofits, and individuals who want more than basic tax preparation. They want guidance. They want someone who can explain the “why,” not just deliver the “what.” And they want a partner who helps them avoid costly pitfalls before they happen. That is the heart of our work: giving you the tools, insight, and understanding you need to make informed decisions and move forward with certainty. In a landscape full of shifting rules and complex regulations, we aim to be a reliable source of clarity. Our goal is to help you stay grounded, cut through the noise, and move ahead with confidence no matter what financial challenges you are facing. Who We Are Alpha Omega Accounting, LLC is a woman owned and veteran owned firm led by Cynthia L. Finkenbinder , CPA, President, and Scott Knock , tax specialist and Vice President. Together, they bring decades of experience in tax strategy, compliance, and business advisory work. What began in Northern Colorado has grown into a practice serving clients across the state, throughout 38 states nationwide, and in several countries across the globe. Because of this, we are multi state, multi jurisdiction, multi business tax experts. Whether someone is nearby or halfway across the world, or if they run one business or many businesses, our approach stays the same: practical guidance, clear communication, and a commitment to helping clients understand their whole financial picture rather than simply react to it. Who We Serve Our clients come from many backgrounds, but they all share a common goal. They want reliable expertise and a partner who understands the complexities of their world. We work with business owners at every stage, from carefully selected startups to established companies navigating growth, compliance, or restructuring.
