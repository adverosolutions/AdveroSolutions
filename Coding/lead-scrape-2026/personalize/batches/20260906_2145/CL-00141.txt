OUR SERVICES notarization We are qualified to complete notarizations both in-person and online for a variety of documents. Our notaries confirm the identity of the person signing the document using state-issued identification, such as a driver s license, state ID card, or a passport. oaths and affirmations These notarial actions involve a person coming before the notary to take an oath or affirmation that the document they are signing is true. It s important to note that the notary does not state that the document is true, but the signer affirms that the content of the documents are true. The notary validates the identity of the signer and provides the oath.
