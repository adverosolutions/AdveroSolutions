About Us - ServiceMaster by Round the Clock Cleaning About Us We’ve been in the cleaning business for over 40 years. Born from a passion for cleaning doctor’s offices and operating rooms, Round the Clock Cleaning was born in March of 1978 by Randy Kissinger. In 1986, Randy purchased the local Easton-based ServiceMaster cleaning company, changing the name to ServiceMaster by Round the Clock Cleaning. Over their 40 years in operation, ServiceMaster by Round the Clock Cleaning has grown, inquiring and building its business into the quality cleaning operation it is today. The company acquired seven different cleaning services as it grew into one of the Greater Lehigh Valley’s largest commercial cleaning operations. Meet Our Leadership Team!
