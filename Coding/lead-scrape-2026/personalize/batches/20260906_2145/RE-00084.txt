CONTACT US Behind The Name TYCHE (tie-kee) ‍ In Greek, Tyche means “luck” and sometimes refers to destiny and fate. Tyche was the personification of the hidden dreams and wishes of all mortals or immortals, ancients or moderns. Going by titles such as “Goddess of Chance” and the “Blind Mistress of Fortune”, the ancient Greek goddess Tyche was a very beloved god due to the immense benefits that could accrue to those that faithfully worshiped her. Luck and Good Fortune. Hidden Dreams and Wishes. Exactly what we are here to help you find. In May of 2014, (after quickly altering the pronunciation above to match our audience) The Tyche (tek-kee) Project was born.
