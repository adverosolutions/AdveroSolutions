About Home About About Edisto Consulting Group Edisto Consulting Group is a fractional recruiting firm: flat monthly fee, unlimited hires, no placement fees. Founded in South Carolina, working with contractors nationwide. Our Story Built to be the third option Edisto Consulting Group started because small and mid-size companies had two bad options for hiring. Pay a headhunter a percentage of every salary, one fee per hire, with a guarantee that replaces the person but never refunds the money. Or use a temp agency whose business depends on you not hiring their people. Our founder spent her career inside the companies living that squeeze, running workforce and talent acquisition for construction and trades employers. She watched a headhunter present a candidate at $100,000 who had told her he was looking for $80,000, because a bigger salary meant a bigger fee. She started Edisto to be the third option: one flat monthly fee, unlimited hires, every candidate yours to keep, and you turn us off when the hiring s done. Who We Work With Owner-led companies that hire in waves Our clients are mechanical, HVAC, electrical and general contractors across the country, filling project managers, estimators, superintendents, service techs and office roles. Companies that make several hires a year without a recruiter on staff. We know those companies from the inside, and it shows in who we send you. How We Work The values, in practice Straight answers. We tell you up front when a role is one we re not confident we can fill, and we ll point you to business owners who ve hired through us instead of asking you to take our word. The work, done right. Every candidate is sourced, screened, and sits for a recorded interview before you spend a minute on them. No resume dumps, no volume game. Your people, your call. Candidates we present are yours to hire, no conversion fees, no strings. We handle the process; the decision stays with you.
