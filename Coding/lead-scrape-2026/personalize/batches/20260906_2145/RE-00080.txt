About Vetted | Vetted Skip Navigation Search for: About Vetted Let’s be honest: all recruiting firms have access to similar tools and data, and follow many of the same steps to identify, engage, and screen candidates. What sets us apart isn’t the software we use or our “proprietary screening process”. Instead, it’s our depth of knowledge – both of the industries we serve (marketing agencies and technology firms) and the nuances of the marketing, PR, and creative roles we recruit for – coupled with our ability to quickly forge strong candidate and client relationships; we excel in each of these areas. Our founder, Tim Walsh, worked in a variety of marketing and communications positions before beginning his career in recruitment. He understands what candidates look for in a role and company because he’s walked in their shoes, and can empathize with the challenges and big decisions that inevitably arise when building a career. At the same time, this first-hand experience translates to deeper insights about the specific skills and traits needed to thrive in particular marketing, PR, and creative roles. It’s a big reason why we can help you make high-quality hires more effectively. And it’s what keeps our clients coming back when they have critical marketing, PR, and creative roles to fill. Why Choose Vetted? Our values aren’t just website copy – they are what drive our daily behaviors and underpin every search we take on. We are committed to maintaining an inclusive culture within our own team, and strive to help our clients develop and maintain a similarly diverse and inclusive team. We also believe in giving back to communities we serve, donating a portion of our annual profits to charities that share our values, putting our money where our mouth is when it comes to building toward a better world for all. When it comes to your business’s bottom line, we offer a variety of pricing structures, including engaged, flat fee, and contingent pricing. And, all of our placements are backed by a 100% refund guarantee (for a hire’s first 100 days).
