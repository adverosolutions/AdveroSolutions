Meet The Team Barnes Financial Services (BFS) began with a focus on annual tax preparation, but over time, we ve evolved into a year-round financial concierge service. Founded in 1992 by Aquaila and her mother, Gail A. Barnes, CPA, BFS started as a family-run firm in Indianapolis. In 2000, Aquaila expanded the business to New York City, blending her financial expertise with her passion for the arts. This unique approach attracted a wide range of creative and professional clients looking for personalized service. Building on that success, we rebranded in 2018 as Barnes Financial Concierge, offering ongoing support to help clients stay compliant and reach their financial goals. Meet the Team No company is successful without the dedication and collaboration efforts found in a good team. At Barnes Financial Concierge we believe that success happens inside out. We work together to achieve the goals of the client and company. We hope to work with you! Aquaila Barnes CEO Founder Aquaila Barnes is the founder and CEO of Barnes Financial Concierge. She is also a Financial Advisor holding Investment (Advisory, Series 6 and 63) licenses in several states within the U.S. Her niche as a Tax Accountant supports creatives & entrepreneurs by bringing a holistic approach to tax planning, small business consultation, and investment planning. She is also a writer and poet, with love for the theater and all things artistic.
