Gail earned her Bachelor s degree from Rutgers University Business School in 1987, became a Certified Public Accountant in 1990 and a Certified Valuation Analyst in 2001. Stuart Rizick, MBA, CPA, CGMA earned his Bachelor s degree from Rutgers University Business School in 1987.
