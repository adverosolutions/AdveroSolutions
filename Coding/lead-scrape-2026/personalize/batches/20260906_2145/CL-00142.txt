Unlike national franchises, we offer the unique advantage of local ownership — giving our clients dedicated, customized service rooted in our deep connection to the community. West Michigan Janitorial is owned by Greg Brouwer, who joined the company in 2018 in sales and purchased the company from the previous owners in 2019. Greg has an extensive background in the cleaning and restoration industry, a love for operating and management structures, and a passion for caring for the people this business impacts. Started in 1969, West Michigan Janitorial began as fledgling janitorial service. Decades of relationship building expanded the company over a large portion of the West Michigan area. Previous owners John and Dana TenElshof designed WMJ to be a personal, friendly business with an open and honest approach. That structure continues to this day.
