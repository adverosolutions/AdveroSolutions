Company - Heveron Company Certified Public Accountants Rochester NY Company Profile Built on a tradition that goes back to 1959. Heveron Company CPAs is built on a tradition that goes back to 1959 when the late John F. Heveron Sr. began practice in Rochester NY. The current firm started in 1972. Currently our staff includes multiple CPAs, a certified internal auditor and a certified QuickBooks advisor serve nonprofits, affordable housing developers and operators, small businesses and individuals. We are a New York State certified woman owned business, headquartered in Rochester, New York and providing services throughout upstate New York. We are members of the AICPA Governmental Auditing Quality Assurance Center and the Employee Benefit Plan Audit Quality Center. We are a New York State Department of Education certified provider of CPA continuing education. We are also a member of the Forum of Firms an international organization of accounting firms established to develop international accounting and auditing standards. Members agree to meet requirements including voluntary independent quality review, complying with the IFAC code of ethics, maintaining training programs and maintaining quality standards.
