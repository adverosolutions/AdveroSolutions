Coffee and Consulting LLC Skip to content We Help Grow Your Business We are a boutique bookkeeping and tax service for small businesses that use Quickbooks. We can do your books for you, or help you to do your own bookkeeping. We can set up new books, clean up old books, and get you caught up so you can focus on your business. We can also train you to do most of the work yourself, and check in once in a while to make sure it is done right. Bookkeeping Taxes Payroll Our Services Keeping good books is important not only for tax season, but for monitoring the health of the business. Proper bookkeeping allows you to track all the information that is key to driving your business. It not only tells you what happened, but can help your forecast and make decisions for the future. Some of the tasks we can perform for you are: Importing and uploading transactions into the books, and categorizing them appropriately Reconciling the books to the bank statements to check for any duplicates or missing transactions Helping you understand the story of your transaction by providing custom reports, in addition to the standard financials. Help with budgeting for variable future expenses Training you on how to use your accounting software, if so desired. Explaining the how s and why s of keeping good books.
