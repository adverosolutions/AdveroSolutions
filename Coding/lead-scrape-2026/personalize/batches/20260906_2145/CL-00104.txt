About Us - Universal Service Recycling ABOUT US Our mission is to consistently provide our clients with industry-leading customer service and competitive pricing while being at the forefront of environmental stewardship. Proud Members of Universal Service Recycling Inc. has been family owned and operated since 1996. We started out as a one-yard operation here in Stockton, California. Through the building of quality individual and commercial relationships founded on honesty, integrity, and customer service, we have grown into the largest privately-held metal recycler serving California s Central Valley. At this time, we have locations in California in Stockton, Sacramento, Modesto, Merced, as well as one in Carson City, Nevada, that serve San Joaquin, Stanislaus, Merced, Sacramento, Amador, Calaveras, Mariposa, Madera, and East Alameda counties. Universal Service Recycling has built long-term relationships with domestic and international steel mills and foundries by providing quality products at competitive prices, prompt delivery, and excellent customer service. We take pride in the fact that we supply a bulk of material to local companies in the northern California region, further helping support our local economy. We are consistently at the forefront of environmental stewardship, doing all we can to help our planet. If great pricing, clean facilities, and industry best customer service are what you are looking for, then look no further.
