They brought me opportunities that I had no interest in, and at times tried to convince me to interview for the roles anyway. Many never contacted me again after being unable to convince me to interview for a role that made no sense for me. As a client , I found that after an in-depth meeting about an open position for which I was hiring, the search firm often sent me resumes that were not aligned with the search criteria.
