About Maid VIP — Our Referral Model & Team | Maid VIP Who We Are Maid VIP is a domestic referral agency. That means we are not a cleaning company that employs the people who arrive at your door — instead, we do the work of finding, screening, and introducing independent cleaning professionals, and you engage the professional directly. Our role is the trusted layer in between: vetting, matching, and supporting, so you can hire with confidence. For a deeper look at how this model works and why it protects homeowners, see our guide on the domestic referral agency model . We focus on interior residential and commercial cleaning across four Los Angeles communities — Beverly Hills, Malibu, Thousand Oaks, and Woodland Hills — matching each client with a professional suited to their home, their standards, and their schedule.
