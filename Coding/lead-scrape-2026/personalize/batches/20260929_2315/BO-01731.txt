Walt Disney Stephanie Gillenkirk I know what it feels like to carry the weight of a business — payroll, taxes, late nights, and the constant “what’s next?” I’ve been there, and that chaos nearly broke me. It’s also what inspired me to build Pittroe™: a financial partner that helps high achievers turn overwhelm into clarity. My Story Numbers always came easily to me, but life didn’t. I worked long shifts in a pizza shop, played poker to pay bills, and later spent 13 years in the casino industry — where chaos was the norm. I became the one people leaned on when things felt unmanageable. In 2020, the pandemic gave me a wake-up call. I didn’t want to watch small business owners stress over money; I wanted to help them. So I pivoted back into accounting and started building Pittroe Business Services on the side. Like many entrepreneurs, I went all-in too fast and nearly went under. That lesson is why I do this work today: to help others avoid the same pitfalls.
