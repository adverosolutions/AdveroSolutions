The firm was founded by Artie DeSisto, Jim Jones and Don Schneeberger, all whom have practiced together for over thirty five years. We concentrate primarily on personalized tax planning and advisory services for individuals, closely held businesses and foundations. Our goal is to become sufficiently knowledgeable about each of our client s tax and financial affairs so that we can offer suggestions and make recommendations that are appropriate given their particular circumstances. We accomplish this goal by building a relationship with our clients that encourages regular communications throughout the year as opposed to once a year contact at tax time and establishing continuity, whereby each of our clients work with the same individual throughout the course of the relationship. Our Mission Our mission is to help clients maintain financial viability in the present, while taking a proactive approach to achieve future goals. This requires open communication to reach an understanding of our clients needs through research and sound analysis. DeSisto Schneeberger Associates, P.C. is dedicated to meeting these goals with high standards of excellence and professionalism. Our dedication to hard work has earned the respect of the business and financial community in and around the area. We believe this to be a direct derivative of our talent and responsiveness to our client base. Whether you are a current or prospective client, be assured that all individuals and businesses will receive competent and timely advice. DeSisto Schneeberger & Associates, P.C.
