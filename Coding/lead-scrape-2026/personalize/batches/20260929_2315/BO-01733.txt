About Us - RSCM, PC Skip to content About Our Firm Rosenberg, Smith, Cooney Migliore, P.C., (RSCM), Certified Public Accountants, is a leading tax, accounting, and consulting firm serving privately owned small and medium size business enterprises for over 30 years. RSCM provides a full line of accounting, auditing, bookkeeping, business consulting, litigation, merger and acquisition, payroll and taxation services to individuals and to small and medium size businesses. We also offer a complete Payroll Service for both our current clients as well as for non-clients. Our Payroll Service allows you to pass on to us the entire task of your payroll processing from preparing the paychecks to having the payroll taxes submitted to the taxing authorities to preparing the quarterly payroll tax reporting forms to filing the year end W-2s and annual reconciliation.
