It quickly became a passion of hers to help others succeed in their personal or business goals, and was one of the factors when opening Singer Accounting, PC. ​ Lindsey is certified by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, a proud member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and the Pennsylvania Institute of Certified Public Accountants as well as several local Chamber of Commerce organizations where she enjoys being active in her community. Lindsey attended Kutztown University of Pennsylvania where she graduated with a Bachelor of Science Degree in Business Administration. She previously worked for a top 25 nationally ranked CPA firm in Reading, PA, and a regional CPA firm in western PA.
