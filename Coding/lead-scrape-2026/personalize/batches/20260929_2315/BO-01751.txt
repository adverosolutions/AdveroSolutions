About Us | Bechtel CPA Where It All Started Bechtel CPA was founded with the goal of creating a more modern and personal approach to accounting. From the beginning, the focus has been on building strong relationships, offering proactive support, and helping business owners feel more confident in their finances. Today, Bechtel CPA is a virtual accounting firm serving women entrepreneurs and small businesses across a variety of industries. Our team provides tax, accounting, and consulting services that support both the everyday needs of running a business and the bigger decisions that come with growth. We believe accounting should feel approachable and collaborative. Our team takes the time to get to know each client, understand their business, and provide guidance that actually makes sense for where they are and where they want to go. Over the years, we have worked with clients in real estate, legal services, healthcare, construction, manufacturing, retail, professional services, and many other industries. No two businesses are exactly alike, which is why we focus on providing personalized support rather than a one-size-fits-all approach. What started as a vision for a different kind of accounting firm has grown into a team that genuinely cares about the people and businesses we work with. Our goal is to make the financial side of business feel less overwhelming and help our clients move forward with more clarity and confidence.
