Deepa Ramachandran Owner Founder, CPA Growing up surrounded by financial expertise, I learned early on that numbers tell powerful stories — and that thoughtful, transparent accounting has the power to change lives. I also saw something else: many people feel overwhelmed by their finances, and too often, traditional accountants unintentionally heighten that stress with technical jargon and rigid processes. In 2009, I founded Acumen Accounting Tax Services with a simple mission: to redefine the client experience. Our work is grounded in four core values: client-centric processes, clear communication, consistent follow-through, and delivering meaningful value every day. I want you to feel comfortable reaching out, whether you have a quick question or need deeper financial guidance. No matter how complex your situation may seem, my team and I are here to bring clarity, structure, and practical solutions. Ultimately, our goal is to help you feel confident, informed, and empowered to make smart financial decisions — not just during tax season, but year-round, whenever you need support. When I’m not working, you’ll likely find me spending quality time with my husband and our two daughters — the heart and motivation behind everything I do.
