Company Founder Jason Draper, Founder and CEO Jason True Draper is the Founder and CEO of True Solutions, a leading commercial cleaning and facility management company known for its commitment to excellence, reliability, and customer satisfaction. Driven by a passion for service and cleanliness, Jason established True Solutions with the vision of transforming the commercial cleaning industry by delivering high-quality, customized solutions tailored to each client’s unique needs. With over 20 years of experience in the cleaning and facilities management sectors, Jason brings extensive expertise in operations management, client relations, and process optimization. Prior to founding True Solutions, he held leadership positions that refined his strategic approach to service, emphasizing efficiency, attention to detail, and a client-first mindset. Under his leadership, True Solutions has experienced significant growth, earning a reputation for delivering superior service, quality results, and a dedicated workforce. The company’s diverse portfolio includes clients across universities, K-12 schools, and medical facilities. Beyond his professional endeavors, Jason is deeply committed to community involvement and corporate social responsibility. He actively supports his church and various Christian ministries, reflecting his strong faith and dedication to making a positive impact. Outside of work, he enjoys playing golf, watching football, and spending quality time with his family. Jason holds a bachelor’s degree from Southern Nazarene and an MBA from the University of Phoenix and believes in lifelong learning as a key to both personal and professional growth. Through True Solutions, he remains dedicated to setting a new standard of excellence in the commercial cleaning industry
