On a walkthrough, a quick wipe-down and a thorough initial deep clean can look almost the same for about a week. Then the difference shows up, and by then you have signed. An initial deep clean sets the baseline your recurring service has to hold. A surface pass just delays the buildup. Save this as a checklist for vetting a provider, so you know which one you are actually paying for: 1. Ask what the deep clean covers that nightly service does not. A good answer names door frames, switches, baseboards, vents, and behind furniture, not just floors and trash. 2. Ask how they document the starting condition. A serious provider photographs the baseline so future inspections have something to measure against. 3. Ask about the touch points at hand height. Interior glass, partitions, and light switches are where a shortcut hides in plain sight. 4. Ask what happens behind and under movable furniture. Months of dust live where a quick pass never reaches. 5. Ask who inspects the deep clean before recurring service starts. If nobody signs off on the baseline, there is no baseline.
