That standard is something I practice in everything I do, and every day in my business. When COVID shut down the valley, I saw an immediate need. Families were at home, more aware of germs and air quality than ever before, and many of them needed their carpets cleaned but were cautious about who they let into their homes and what chemicals would be left behind once the technician left. I developed a process centered on low-residue, soap-free solutions that dry fast, leave nothing harmful behind, and are safe for kids, pets, and people with sensitivities.
