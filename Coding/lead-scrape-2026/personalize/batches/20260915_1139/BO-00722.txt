About Merendino | Lee Merendino CPA About Merendino Lee Merendino was drawn into the accounting profession by his high school Business Law teacher, who also managed his own CPA firm. Upon earning an undergraduate degree at Pace University, he became a Certified Public Accountant. After a dozen years working for regional CPA firms, Lee worked for several corporate and nonprofit organizations as a financial executive. Recognizing a need for personalized accounting services, Lee started his own CPA firm in 2003. Unlike larger CPA firms, Merendino Accounting Tax Services values the importance of building authentic client relationships to provide the best services for individuals, small businesses, and nonprofits. Lee’s commitment to you is to provide sound financial advice in a way that promotes honesty and integrity. He takes the responsibility that comes with the CPA profession very seriously and communicates this ideal clearly to his clients.
