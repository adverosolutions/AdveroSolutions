Johnson, CPA OWNER / CPA (336) 790-5245 jared@jaredgjohnsoncpa.com Jared G. Johnson is the owner of Jared G. Johnson, CPA, PLLC. Jared graduated Summa Cum Laude from the University of North Carolina at Greensboro with a B.S. in accounting. During his college tenure, Jared was a member of Phi Beta Kappa. Since graduating, Jared has accumulated 10 years of accounting expertise in a variety of positions within the industry. Jared specializes in providing financial and tax advisory services to a variety of small businesses and individuals. He concentrates in small business tax planning. Prior to founding his firm, Jared spent about five years in North Carolina firms: as an auditor for KPMG, as a tax preparer for a CPA firm in Greensboro and as the corporate controller for a mortgage brokerage firm in Winston-Salem. Jared is a member of the North Carolina Association of CPA s, the Better Business Bureau and the American Institute of CPA s. The commitment Jared makes to each of his clients is to provide them with tax and accounting services that are both sound and timely.
