Jorgenson Pace, Inc. is a national executive search and consulting firm dedicated to organizations that strengthen communities and drive economic growth. For more than 30 years, our team has recruited executives to lead state, regional, and local organizations. These leaders have contributed to billions of dollars in economic investment and the creation of millions of well-paying jobs across the country. We specialize in serving economic and community development organizations, chambers of commerce, downtown development organizations, foundations, and nonprofits whose leaders have a lasting influence on the people and places they serve. SPECIALIZED EXPERTISE We are not a general-practice search firm. Our focus on community-focused organizations gives us a deep understanding of their leadership needs, operating environments, and relationships with boards, elected officials, business leaders, and other stakeholders. This specialized knowledge allows us to identify candidates whose experience and leadership capabilities align with each organization’s goals. A PERSONALIZED APPROACH Every engagement begins with listening. We take the time to understand an organization’s priorities, culture, community, and aspirations before developing a customized search strategy. Our team provides responsive, hands-on guidance throughout the process, from stakeholder engagement and candidate recruitment through interviews, selection, and transition. NATIONAL RELATIONSHIPS Our extensive relationships include economic development professionals, nonprofit executives, business leaders, site consultants, real estate professionals, bankers, attorneys, and other private-sector partners. Supported by a national network and rigorous research, we recruit accomplished candidates, including leaders who may not be actively seeking a new opportunity.
