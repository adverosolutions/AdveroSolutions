About Us - Integrated Cleaning Services About Us From Colorado Roots to Industry Leaders Founded in 1988, Integrated Cleaning Services has grown from a small woman-owned business into a trusted name in the cleaning industry. You may know us as DCPS Cleaning, but we're now returning to our original name, Integrated Cleaning Services—and we're happy to answer to either name. Our leadership team, comprising experienced professionals, is dedicated to operational excellence and providing responsive, tech-focused customer care. Mission Statement At Integrated Cleaning Services, our mission is to create clean, healthy, and inviting spaces that enhance the lives of the people who use them. Whether serving a multi-site portfolio or a single site, we deliver consistent, professional cleaning tailored to each client’s needs—because clean spaces lead to happy tenants, productive teams, and thriving communities. With a focus on reliability, responsiveness, and long-term partnership, we help our clients maintain safe environments where people feel welcome, supported, and cared for. Vision Statement To be the most trusted partner in commercial cleaning by setting the standard for service, reliability, and innovation. We envision a workplace where our team members are empowered, supported, and proud to stay—because great service begins with people who feel valued. Through smart technology, clear communication, and a culture of care, we help our clients maintain clean, safe environments where people and businesses thrive. Core Values People First We believe our employees are the heart of our company. We invest in their growth, wellbeing, and success because retaining great people means delivering great service. Reliability We do what we say we will do. Consistent quality, proactive support, and dependable performance are the foundation of every client relationship. Communication Clear, respectful, and timely communication is how we build trust—with clients, team members, and each other. Innovation We embrace technology and continuous improvement to work smarter, faster, and more effectively—for both our clients and our team. Accountability We take ownership of our work and hold ourselves to high standards. If something needs fixing, we address it—quickly and thoroughly. Service Excellence We’re not just here to clean—we’re here to make a positive impact on the spaces we maintain and the people who use them.
