Who We Are Corliss and Solomon Our Firm Our Principals Our Staff Peer Review Our Firm Nonprofit Experience We’re a local, certified public accounting firm focused exclusively on providing auditing, and form 990 preparation to at least 75% local and independent nonprofit organizations within 50 miles of Asheville and throughout Western North Carolina. We maintain a tight, intense focus in our chosen service area – the nonprofit sector. For over 17 years we’ve worked with over 100 nonprofit organizations of all sizes in Asheville and surrounding areas. In that time, we’ve cultivated the experience and knowledge base to become a valuable resource to our clients. And that’s a resource that isn’t simply relegated to audit time – it’s one we make accessible throughout the year for assistance with accounting and financial matters, grant compliance and other organizational issues as questions arise. 30 Years Experience Our Values We believe in making a long-term commitment to each organization we have the opportunity to work with. We believe that being a smaller firm leads to bigger things, like more individual attention, better service, and easier access when you need it most, not just when it’s convenient for us. As part of our commitment to developing and maintaining these long-term client relationships, every client we serve has direct access to our principals. Whether we re preparing an audit, doing your annual form 990 or providing planning support, we bring a personal touch to the table. We value progress over profit, and working toward greater goals with clients like you who put their best foot forward every day to tackle problems and make the community and the world a better place. In a nutshell, we believe in helping good things grow.
