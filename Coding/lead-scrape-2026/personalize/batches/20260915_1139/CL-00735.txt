Our Story Conceived To Reimagine Facility Operations Ten years ago, Jeff Twardzik, a hospital facilities manager, was frustrated by the amount of time it took to locate how-to knowledge to keep his hospital operational. Spending hours combing through manuals and documentation, Jeff knew there had to be a better way. Partnering with a friend, they initially created a basic app that stored how-to videos, but the technology was very limited at the time and was too cumbersome to use. Several years later and with the advent of AI and machine learning capabilities, Jeff sought to brainstorm the possibility of creating a more robust solution. The goal remained the same: Create an easy-to-use solution that would capture tribal knowledge from senior team members and make it accessible to everyone.
