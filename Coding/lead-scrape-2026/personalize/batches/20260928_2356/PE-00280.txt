ABOUT Planticular (Plant Watering, Pruning, Pest Control, Design)) 0 Wellness is important and nature plays a key role in helping people accomplish it. Society need plants. We want to help them thrive in the spaces where people interact. We’re providing a convenient way for people to have nature in their spaces through plantscaping and plant care modeled after care services like Dog walking apps Wag! and Rover. Society Needs Plants. #biophilia #environmentalism #gardening #plantscaping #horticulture #botany Community Efforts We take healthy ecological systems seriously and know that planet stewardship is multifaceted. In addition to being a black owned business that strives to help eliminate equity gaps, we also strive to build community and environmental sustainability through our NYC Chamber of Commerce and 1% For The Planet Memberships. Tanzania Farming Our team believes that plants help create healthy ecological systems. While it started with a few house plants and an urban garden (especially given the U.N.’s projections that 68% of the world will live in cities by 2050), we’ve taken the initiative to farm around the globe too. Plants are the precursors to many of the foods, products, and medicines we use daily. Society needs plants. Our farms in Tanzania are in mountain view of the esteemed Kilimanjaro! On these farms we grow Corn, Rice, Sunflower, Millet, and Beans with which our growers can exchange in the markets and feed their families. Check out the blogs written by our farmers to learn more about their experience and process! 2. Equitable Tourism Safaris As a part of our ecological stewardship initiatives, our team is designing websites and other infrastructure for Kilimanjaro hikes and safaris. This gives local operators a chance to receive business that otherwise may be impossible given the widening tech gap experienced in Africa.
