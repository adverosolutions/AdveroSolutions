The TurfGator Story: Rooted in the Metro East TurfGator was founded in 2009 when owner Chris Schneider returned home to Illinois with a clear vision: to build a premier, service-first company that prioritizes both its customers and its employees. What began as the acquisition of a small local business has grown into an award-winning operation servicing thousands of homeowners across the region. As a proud, long-standing 10-Year Member of the Illinois Landscape Contractors Association (ILCA) , we have built a solid reputation founded on integrity, quality, and dependability. Our dedication to excellence hasn't gone unnoticed; TurfGator is honored to have won the People’s Choice Award for Best Lawn Care Services in the St. Louis area for four consecutive years. Meet Our Owner: Chris Schneider At the heart of TurfGator’s success is our founder and owner, Chris Schneider. Chris brings over 22 years of hands-on industry experience to every property we service. Chris began his career in January 2000 with TruGreen, where his dedication rapidly propelled him from a sales representative to a sales manager in just eight months. After gaining valuable industry insights managing territories in Jackson, Mississippi, Chris chose to bring his expertise back home to Illinois to launch TurfGator and its sister company, The Pest Guys. Chris is an expert in regional turf management, with extensive training in both warm-season and cool-season grasses, as well as complex lawn installations. To ensure TurfGator remains at the absolute cutting edge of the industry, Chris holds multiple state certifications, including an Illinois structural pest license and a turf and ornamental license.
