About Us - Hanna Associates Skip to content About Us admin 2024-05-09T14:35:41+00:00 Executive Recruiting Al Hanna founded Hanna Associates in 1992 and has developed a leading boutique search firm for the AEC industry. The battle for senior talent is highly competitive and our clients need quick results. We specialize in the recruitment of senior level architects, engineers, construction, business development executives with a primary focus in Healthcare Science Technology, Pharmaceutical, Research Laboratory, Higher Education, Hospitality, Commercial, Mixed-Use, Aviation, High Rise, project types. Mergers and Acquisitions Hanna Associates has identified key M/A prospects throughout the US. These companies are considering ownership succession, expanding market share or access to resources that will empower their growth. Our trusted relationships with C-Suite Executives in the A/E/C industry puts us in prime position to introduce potential buyers and sellers on a confidential basis. About Al Hanna Al Hanna was raised in Southern California prior to attending Chico State University where he earned a BA degree in Communications. Upon graduation, he was accepted into the management training program at Warner Communications. Specializing in the recording industry, Hanna would eventually become the National Director of Marketing and Merchandising at Elektra Asylum records for artists such as The Eagles, Jackson Brown, Linda Ronstadt, Joni Mitchell and others. Hanna founded his company in 1992 with the goal of becoming an industry leader to the A/E/C marketplace. Hanna lives in the Mile-High City of Denver with his wife Rhonda.
