About Technical Staffing Specialists - Technical Staffing Specialists About Technical Staffing Specialists Who We Are Over the last 25 years, Technical Staffing Specialists, Inc. has become one of the best IT staffing agencies in the Northern Virginia, Washington, DC and Baltimore metro areas. While we are a local company and most of our clients are headquartered in the area, we have filled jobs across the USA, and as far away as the Middle East. Our local and national network is vast and extensive. Our determination to excellent service is second to none. We anticipate and solve our clients’ staffing challenges by building relationships and developing time sensitive and cost-effective recruiting plans and strategies to identify highly skilled employees and contractors. Our ongoing recruitment funnel identifies and attracts the best and brightest talent making us one of the best recruiting companies in the nation. We take a consultative approach to sourcing candidates and overall hiring plans. We help clients meet their short-term and long-term staffing needs and hiring goals. In short, our recruiters get to know our client’s businesses better than any other staffing company by identifying the best candidates allowing hiring managers to find exceptional employees and consultants, and ultimately to make better hiring decisions, thereby increasing their bottom line and exceeding organizational goals. Meet The Founder Jack Crouse Jack Crouse is a staffing industry and business veteran that founded Technical Staffing Specialists, Inc. in 1999. Prior to starting TechSSI, Jack helped drive sales, processes and coordination of staffing and sales efforts for a dozen offices in the mid-Atlantic area for a $1 billion company. This experience helped lay the foundation for Jack being hands-on with the company’s clients and really understanding the intricacies of their business in order to help them reach their staffing goals. Jack serves as an advisor and investor to a few local Washington, DC area companies involved in media, healthcare, artificial intelligence and education. Jack also serves on the University of Richmond’s User Customer Experience Advisory Board for their Executive Education Program. Earlier in his career, Jack was a founding principal of an award-winning teleservices company.
