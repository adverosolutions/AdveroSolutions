About - HackerX // hackerx.about We put engineers and companies in the same room HackerX runs invite-only recruiting events and a global talent marketplace that connect the world's best developers with the companies who want to hire them without agencies, job boards, or cold outreach. Find an Event For Employers 500+ Events run worldwide 175 Cities across 35 countries 140K+ Developers in our community 6,500+ Companies hired through us Our mission Hiring engineers is broken. Job boards bury great candidates under noise, agencies charge five-figure placement fees, and the best developers rarely apply to anything at all. We started HackerX because the best hires happen when people actually meet face to face, in a room built for real conversations, not resumes in a queue. How we got here What began as a single developer meetup grew into the largest invite-only tech recruiting event series in the world. Over a decade later we've hosted more than 500 events across 175+ cities in 35 countries, from San Francisco and New York to Berlin, London, Bangalore, and S o Paulo. Along the way we built HackerX Connect a marketplace where employers source directly from 100,000+ vetted engineers and make introductions with zero placement fees. What we believe Great recruiting is about signal, not volume. Every developer who attends a HackerX event is reviewed by our team. Every employer gets face time, not a booth in a crowd. We charge a flat fee for events and never take a cut of anyone's salary because the incentive should be a great hire, not a bigger commission. What makes HackerX different A recruiting model designed around quality from the ground up. 01 Invite-only, always vetted Every attendee is screened by our sourcing team, so companies meet engineers who are genuinely worth their time. 02 No placement fees We're not a recruiting agency. Flat event pricing and subscriptions we never charge a commission on a hire. 03 Global, but local 175+ cities across 35 countries, each run by a director who knows the local tech scene inside out. 04 Built by engineers We design our events and platform the way developers actually want to be recruited directly and respectfully.
