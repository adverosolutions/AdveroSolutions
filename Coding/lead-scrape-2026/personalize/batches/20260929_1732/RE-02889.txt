Cherry Talent Group - About About The Secret Behind Cherry Talent Group Cherry Talent Group, founded in 2022, is transforming the recruiting experience by putting people and values at the heart of hiring. Unlike traditional recruiting, which often feels rushed and transactional, we focus on whether candidates truly fit your company culture and will thrive in your environment. By addressing these crucial factors upfront, we help create lasting, meaningful connections that go beyond the resume. Why CTG Exists After 20 years in recruiting, founder Suman Cherry saw the flaws in the traditional hiring model—speed, lack of transparency, and a focus on numbers over true fit. "I knew there had to be a better way," Suman says. So, Cherry Talent Group was born, with a mission to offer a thoughtful, value-driven process that works for every budget. During the COVID-19 pandemic, Suman's approach resonated with clients, offering an hourly model and discovery sessions that prioritized culture while keeping costs manageable. Our Approach We go beyond matching skills to job descriptions. By understanding your company’s culture, values, and goals, we connect you with candidates who are not just qualified but also passionate about your mission. Our consultative, personalized approach ensures a great fit, whether you're a small business or a growing enterprise.
