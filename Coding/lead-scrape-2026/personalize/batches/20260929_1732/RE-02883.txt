As a woman-owned, mission-driven team, we lead with empathy, act with precision, and deliver talent solutions that create lasting impact. WHERE DID THE NAME BLACKMERE COME FROM? The word combines two evocative ideas: Black symbolizes the hidden depths where exceptional talent resides, while Mere, an Old English term for a pool or pond, represents a place of depth and stillness, requiring focus and skill to uncover what lies beneath. Together, the name reflects the mystery, ingenuity, and expertise that define our approach to connecting extraordinary talent with extraordinary opportunities. HOW IT ALL STARTED Blackmere Consulting started with a simple idea: connect exceptional talent with the organizations that need them most. But like most great ideas, it came to life through a combination of challenges, a lot of heart, a fair amount of luck, and the belief that the right people can change everything. In 2011, I launched Blackmere from my basement with little more than a used laptop and a vision. After years in executive recruitment and building successful practices, the 2009 Great Recession wiped everything away. That setback paved the way for an opportunity with a U.S. National Laboratory, where I helped build a world-class cybersecurity team in their national security division. Immersed in the demanding world of cybersecurity recruitment, I gained expertise and trust in one of the most challenging fields. ​ While thriving in that role, I missed supporting multiple clients and candidates simultaneously. That sparked the idea for a firm rooted in trust, meaningful connections, and a deep understanding of niche technical fields like cybersecurity, AI, and nuclear engineering. By 2011, Blackmere Consulting was born.
