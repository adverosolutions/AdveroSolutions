About Peak3 Search | Pharma Medtech Executive Search Skip to content About Peak3 Search Peak3 was founded in 2020 on a conviction twenty years of searches kept proving: companies grow at the speed of their leadership. We stay deliberately small, work directly with the people who own the outcome, and treat every search as a reflection of our own name. Andy Wilke Managing Partner More than 20 years ago, I came into executive search from a trading desk and made some of my first placements in packaging, including senior Quality and Operations leaders. I loved the hunt, the deal, and the satisfaction of getting the right person into the right role. I still love the work today, but I view success very differently. What matters most now is the long-term impact of the decisions we help clients and candidates make. I consider it a privilege every time a company chooses Peak3 to represent its business in the market, whether that means helping a global organization build in North America, supporting a private equity-backed company through growth, or finding a leader who can change the trajectory of a business. We take a long-term view of every search. In many ways, a search is never really over. Success is when a client comes back because the experience and outcome were strong. It is hearing years later that someone we placed is thriving, taking on more responsibility, or becoming a key part of the company s success. The relationships we build, the trust we earn, and the impact we can have on both sides of a search are what continue to drive me and the work we do at Peak3. Eryn Vanderwende VP, Research Search Execution I ve spent most of my career finding the people others miss.
