Home | Superior Hires Founder-led recruiting for complex, high-stakes roles Superior Hires is a founder-led recruiting practice focused on complex, high-stakes roles. Every search is led personally from initial scoping through close—no delegation, no handoffs. Engagements are intentionally selective and centered on situations where precision, judgment, and discretion matter. A core part of the work is aligning client expectations with current market realities, particularly around scope, talent availability, and compensation, before going to market. Internal requirements are then translated into clear, accurate narratives that resonate with the right candidates. This creates alignment early, so interviews confirm expectations rather than redefine them. Superior Hires is engaged when the role is important enough to require direct accountability and clear thinking throughout the search. Contact
