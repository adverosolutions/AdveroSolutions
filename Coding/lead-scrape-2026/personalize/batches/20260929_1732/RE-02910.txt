About Us Skip to content Our History Hire Technology is an award-winning, woman-owned, technology focused staffing firm established in 1999. We add value to the staffing process by shortening the time required to locate the best qualified candidates for your hiring needs. Our company’s success is owed in large part to taking the time to understand our clients – their culture, benefits selling points, environment, team management styles all the way down to the specific hiring manager. Simultaneously, we try to cultivate and grow our network of excellent candidates – understanding their skills, experiences and career aspirations. Our team diligently works to make the right match between candidates and the clients’ contract and direct hiring needs. Clients who know the monetary cost of a bad hire (which can exceed 30% of the person s annual salary, according to the U.S. Department of Labor) appreciate Hire Technology’s staffing approach. As one client reflected, “Hires that last rarely happen fast!”. In the last several years, Hire Technology has experienced a great demand for contract placements. An increasing number of employers are utilizing a blended workforce model, with both direct hires and contractors as part of a long term strategy.
