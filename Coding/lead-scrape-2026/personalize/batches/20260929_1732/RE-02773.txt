About EK Recruiting 0 How Our Agency Started Our agency started four years ago by Founder/CEO - Kurt Kummerer a seasoned medical sales/sales management professional in Corporate America. Kurt always had an itch he wanted to scratch, which was to run his own business. Be his own boss. Why recruiting? Well, at the time Kurt was a single parent with the responsibility of his child 100% of the time. So, what could he start as he had virtually no capital and keep a roof over his head that gave him ultimate flexibility? Recruiting! Having been approached about getting into recruiting multiple times before and rebuffing every time now was the time he decided to give it a try. If the recruiters who Kurt dealt with over the years could make a living, then why couldn’t he? So EK Recruiting was born. EK stands for the initials of Kurt’s child as this gave Kurt the best chance to provide for his child now and in the future.
