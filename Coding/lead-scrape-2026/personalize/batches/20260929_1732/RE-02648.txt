Subscribe to our newsletter or podcast. Subscribe to one, or both! Contact Us Subscribe to the Podcast Darcy Andersen Recruiter Darcy brings a genuine understanding of rural communities. She grew up along the Vermillion River in rural Turner County, SD, and recently returned to take over her family’s 150-year-old farm. A proud SDSU grad, Darcy spent 21 years in TV news before making a career change to join PorchLight as a recruiter. Deeply rooted in small-town life, her graduating class from Hurley High had just 13 students. She’s also the president of the Turner County Fair Board, a big supporter of 4-H, and enjoys baking, growing cut flowers, and spending time with her nieces. She shares the farm with two donkeys, a Newfoundland named Oklahoma Thunder, and a few barn cats.
