Team High Performance Networks 0 Leadership Team Will Biedron President Company founder Will Biedron is a lifelong systems professional, with a relentless passion for helping companies get high impact from their networking technology. He began his career designing and building large video, audio and film production systems, and then developed database systems for managing them. He started building computer networks in 1988 and held numerous supporting IT roles within software and hardware firms. In 1994 he became one of the first technical contributors and data network executives at Continental Cablevision (now part of Comcast), leading the design and build-out of the first residential broadband network in New England. Since then, he has held business development, sales and engineering roles at network management and content delivery companies, including Akamai Technologies.
