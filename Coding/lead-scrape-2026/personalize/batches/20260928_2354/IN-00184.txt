Service Built on Trust. For more than four decades, Stroud-Miller Insurance Service has been proud to serve families, individuals, and businesses throughout our region. What began as a small local insurance agency has grown into a team of experienced professionals dedicated to helping our customers protect what matters most. Rooted in Our Community Since 1978 Our story began on May 1, 1978, when Miller General Insurance Service opened its doors in Wilkes County. In 1981, Stroud Insurance Agency was acquired and the agency became Stroud-Miller Insurance Service. Over the years, we have continued to grow while remaining committed to the local communities we serve. In 1985, we acquired the White Insurance Agency in Taylorsville, an agency with roots dating back to the 1930s. In 1986, we welcomed the Highlander Agency in Boone, which had been serving the area since 1957. Today, our Highlander office continues to serve customers in the High Country as a branch of Stroud-Miller. While our agency has grown and changed over the years, one thing has remained constant: our commitment to providing personal, dependable insurance service to our customers. Experience You Can Count On Our team brings decades of combined insurance experience and a commitment to continuing education. Several members of our staff hold professional insurance designations, including CIC, CPCU, and CISR . We believe that being knowledgeable about insurance is just as important as being available when our customers need us. That s why we invest in the continued education and professional development of our team—so we can better understand your needs and help you make informed decisions about your coverage. Independent by Choice As an independent insurance agency, we have the ability to work with multiple insurance companies rather than representing just one. This allows us to look at your individual situation and help you find coverage that fits your needs, priorities, and budget. We understand that no two customers are alike. Whether you re protecting your home, automobile, business, family, or future, our goal is to provide thoughtful advice and insurance solutions tailored to you.
