Welcome! Heman Lawson Hawks LLP was founded in 1975 and is independently owned by the Partners. The Firm continues to follow its founding principle of providing quality, timely services for reasonable fees. We are a highly progressive and technologically advanced CPA firm located in West Lafayette, Indiana. We utilize state-of-the-art software to provide the best tax and accounting services to our clients around the world. Clients of Heman Lawson Hawks LLP include manufacturers, distributors, retailers, consultants, technology firms, attorneys, agents, health professions, contractors, food service industry, agriculture, developers, not-for-profits, charities, family partnerships and individuals. Most of the Firm’s clients have been referred by bankers, attorneys, brokers, insurance professionals and other clients who provide testimony to the Firm’s capable, professional, personal and reasonably priced services.
