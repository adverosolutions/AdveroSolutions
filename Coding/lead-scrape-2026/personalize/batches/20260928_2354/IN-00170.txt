Our Philosophy As an independent insurance agency for over 40 years, we believe that with the right insurance planning, we can help you reach your financial goals. We involve you throughout the process, making sure you understand your coverage and the options available to you. We are a “one stop shop” that will professionally coordinate your personal and business insurances: life, disability, long-term care, as well as all lines of property and casualty. We work with multiple insurers to offer you the right coverage. We believe in the personal touch, interacting directly with you to develop your plan. We don’t want to be a quote machine, which just provides price and basic coverage information. That’s just telling you the minimum information. And without more specifics, a general quote may not accurately reflect what will work best for you. Even if you want us to compete against another insurer’s policy, just showing you what we can offer doesn’t help you realize your goals. We want you to be involved in the entire process, so you fully understand our recommendations for your coverage and budget. We also work with your tax and legal professionals, at no additional cost!
