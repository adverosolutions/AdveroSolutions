About Thrive Financial Recruiting Suzanne Morrisette Suzanne has worked in Accounting Finance Recruitment since 1998. She has been a top producer placing hundreds of Accounting/Finance professionals in multiple markets in Denver, CO and in Melbourne Sydney, Australia. Suzanne earned her degree from the University of Colorado in Boulder, and she then opened breweries for several years along the Colorado Front Range. She started working in recruitment for a Fortune 500 international finance and accounting recruitment firm, where she quickly found her passion and became a worldwide top producer and manager. As a result of her success, she was hand-picked to move to Australia to help develop and grow their businesses in Melbourne and Sydney. She spent 8 years there, and during her time there she developed a team placing temporary accounting professionals in Melbourne that became the #1 fastest growing division worldwide. In Sydney, she managed a team of permanent accounting placement consultants that placed 20th in growth amongst 300+ offices worldwide. She became a trainer and “turn-around” manager helping a Sydney suburb office become the fastest growing office worldwide. Suzanne returned to the Denver area with her family. She quickly became a top performer at a local Finance Accounting Recruitment firm in Denver. It has always been her passion to launch her own business in Denver, and she wants to help companies, candidates, and her own business THRIVE. Suzanne is passionate about having a genuine, personalized approach and in building lifelong relationships with clients and candidates.
