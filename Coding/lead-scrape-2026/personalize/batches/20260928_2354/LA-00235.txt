Our Story About Natural Green Lawns Natural Green Lawns is based in and operates out of Waxhaw, NC. Before founding Natural Green Lawns, Founding Partner Gordon Howald was driven by a desire to create a safer lawn in his own backyard. He wanted an outdoor living space he felt comfortable having his family and pets play on, without exposing them to an overabundance of synthetic chemicals and broadcast pesticides. Several years later, Mark Waclawik came aboard, and the rest, as they say, is history. Natural Green Lawns was founded on a solid ethical foundation. We make customer satisfaction our top priority and ensure all customers are happy with their organic-based lawn. Being honest, doing quality work, and communicating with our customers is instrumental to our core values. We are always available to our customers, always willing to explain our process, and always focused on keeping your lawn the greenest, healthiest, and safest it can be.
