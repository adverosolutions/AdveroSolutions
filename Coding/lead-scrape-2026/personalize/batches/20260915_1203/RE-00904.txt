Headquartered in Savannah, Georgia, founding brothers Aaron and Byron Atkinson bring over 40 years of combined experience in program management, engineering, law, finance, disaster recovery, and business operations. With a “never no” mentality, Atkinson Consulting curates a positive team temperament that makes more possible for prime partners. Atkinson is a preferred small business partner for federal prime contracts. Atkinson Consulting works across the U.S. and its territories, as well as with the Department of Defense, quickly adapting to changing project requirements, tight timelines, and complex needs, while providing primes with flexibility in managing high-stakes contracts. Meet the Team Aaron Atkinson President Principal Engineer Aaron holds a Bachelor s degree in Civil Engineering, a Master s in Business Administration, and a Master s in Civil Engineering with a focus on Construction Management. With over 12 years of experience in FEMA Public Assistance, he has worked on disaster recovery efforts in Ohio, Texas, Louisiana, and New Jersey. Aaron was specifically chosen by FEMA management as the sole Architect/Engineer on a special task force in New Orleans, dedicated to resolving disputes on high-profile and controversial projects.
