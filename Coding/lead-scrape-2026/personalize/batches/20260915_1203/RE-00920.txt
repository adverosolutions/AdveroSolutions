About | Commitment To Excellence at Elite Skip to content About Elite Our Story Our Founding Principles Elite started with a simple idea: do what is right and treat people well. Ron and Sonja Bennett built this company on values they learned through years of hard work in manufacturing, human resources, and management. From the start, the goal has been the same. Connect good people with good work and help businesses build strong, reliable teams. Commitment You Can Trust We know what it means to put your name on something and stand behind it. Every client, every employee, and every job matters to us. That is why our customers trust Elite to deliver quality work and dependable service year after year. Filling the Local Gap After decades of experience working in manufacturing management and staffing, Ron and Sonja saw a gap in the local market. Businesses needed more than a staffing agency . They needed a partner that understood their operations, their people, and their community. Growth and Impact In 2012, they formed Elite to meet that need. From one office in Kingsport, the company has grown to serve customers throughout East Tennessee and Virginia. Today, Elite continues to build partnerships with clients in manufacturing, construction, and professional industries while helping hundreds of employees find steady and meaningful work. Our Motto & Mission To connect skilled and hardworking people with businesses that value them. We provide dependable staffing, training, and support that help our clients succeed and give our employees steady and rewarding work. Our mission is as simple as our motto, “Do the Right Thing.” We believe everyone deserves the chance to do good work for good people in a morally sound way. Our Vision To be the trusted name in workforce solutions across Tennessee and Virginia and beyond. We want to be known not just for what we do but for how we do it, with honesty, consistency, and care. We see a future where every client partnership is built on respect and every employee has the opportunity to grow. Our Values Doing the Right Thing: We believe honesty and integrity are the foundation of everything we do. We keep our word and make every decision with fairness and respect. Focusing on People: People are at the heart of our work. We lead with respect, humility, and care for everyone we serve. Committed to Working Safely: Safety comes first.
