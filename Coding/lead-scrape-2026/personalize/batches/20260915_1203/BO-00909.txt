Your Team Sean Fraser As co-founder and CEO of Beaverbrook, Sean oversees the vision for the company, the business development, the creation and implementation of new systems within the company, and is the final reviewer for all technical work. Prior to founding Beaverbrook, Sean worked as worked as an accountant for AEW Capital Management, one of the largest private equity investment managers in the world. He started his career as auditor at Deloitte Touche, one of the Big 4 Accounting firms. His educational background includes a MBA/MS in Accounting Dual Degree from Northeastern University, a graduate certificate from Johns Hopkins University, and a BA in Chinese from University of Massachusetts Amherst. In his free time, Sean loves to spend time with his family, travel, practice Brazilian jiu-jitsu, learn foreign languages, and rap. Jiameng Fraser As co-founder and COO of Beaverbrook, Jiameng oversees all the technical and operational activities of the firm. Prior to co-founding Beaverbrook, Jiameng worked as a fund accountant at BNY Mellon and a relationship manager at Bank of America. Her educational background includes a MS in Finance from Northeastern University and a BS in Finance from Shenzhen University.
