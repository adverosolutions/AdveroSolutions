Ok so that's the company argument but does having a newly created title like this help the candidate market themselves? In the short term probably not. Until they are generally accepted by the wider population (and there is a serious time lag) new title categories are just going to make you harder to find. New titles are basically a fad until they stick. They often deliberately obfuscate the true functional alignment to create separation or differentiation. For Example "brand strategist" as a euphemism for sales to make sales people sound less salesy. So if one of these new titles is forced upon you, what do you do as a candidate to continue to market yourself on Linkedin and in your resume? Two tips 1. New Fad title followed by real functional alignment in parentheses. 2.
