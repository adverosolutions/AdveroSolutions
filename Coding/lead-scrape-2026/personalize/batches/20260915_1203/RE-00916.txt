Team Connection Questions Omada Search Team Connection Questions Forge Meaningful Relationships Team connection isn t just about work; it s about forging meaningful relationships. For companies aspiring to not just survive but to flourish, nurturing and cherishing this connection among their teams is not a choice it s a strategic imperative. The average person will spend 90,000+ hours at work. Make the most of that time by getting to know your team. Personal Growth Goals What’s one thing on your bucket list you hope to achieve in the next year? What’s a skill you’re currently working on improving? Share a recent accomplishment that made you proud. What projects or initiatives are you most excited about in the coming months? Team Culture Appreciation How can we celebrate successes, big or small, as a team? Is there someone on the team you’d like to give a shoutout for their contributions? What’s something you appreciate about the team culture here? What’s one thing the team has been doing well that we should continue? Communication Collaboration What communication or collaboration tool do you find most effective for our team? How can we enhance our communication to ensure everyone feels heard and valued? If we were to plan a team-building activity, what would you suggest? Work-Life Balance Well-Being How do you manage your work-life balance, and do you have any tips to share? What’s your favorite way to recharge outside of work? What do the first 30 minutes of your typical day look like? What activity do you enjoy so much that it makes you lose track of time? Reflection Perspective What lessons have we learned from recent obstacles, and how can we apply them moving forward? What’s something you believed earlier in your career but think about differently now? What book has made the biggest impact on your life? Personal Connection Conversation Starters If you could have dinner with any historical figure, who would it be and why? When you were younger, what did you want to be when you grew up? Who in your life inspires you to be better? Just For Fun If you were stuck in a grocery store, how long would it take you to eat everything in the store?
