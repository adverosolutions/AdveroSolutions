Ashley s Recruiting Services (ARS) is a small, minority woman-owned business specializing in permanent placement Recruiting Services. We specialize in placing top-tier candidates at mission-driven organizations. Here at ARS we take the time to truly understand your company s needs and requirements to match you with a candidate that has both the skills and passion for your organization s mission, vision, and values. Striving for Excellence At ARS, our story began with a passion to help small and mid-size organizations achieve sustainable organizational and cultural growth, while making a positive impact on society. What makes this possible? Its People - Human Capital. Finding the right fit isn t always an easy task. That s where ARS comes into play. ARS brings a unique spin to recruiting by implementing diversity and professional solutions that empower our partners and create competitive advantage. We focus our energy on cultivating value driven relationships, while striving for excellence. Our Mission and Vision ARS is on a mission to connect employees that are talented, motivated, and driven to excellence with great businesses by opening up opportunities for both. We strive to become the preferred source for permanent placement recruiting services, that empowers people s lives through the relentless pursuit of both customer and employee excellence. We promise only what we can deliver; and we deliver on every promise. Our business is built on the foundation of honesty and integrity. Our Values We treat every individual with respect, in every interaction. We are committed to providing solutions to our partners, striving for excellence. Meet Our Founder + CEO Founder and CEO, Ashley Griffith is a Tennessee native, loving mother, and military spouse who has always been passionate about helping others and giving back to the community. Since then, she has dedicated her efforts to providing organizations with the most important aspect to any company - Human Capital. Ashley holds a Masters Degree in Human Resources Management and has over 15 years of Human Resources experience, specializing in Talent Acquisition for non-profit and for-profit organizations.
