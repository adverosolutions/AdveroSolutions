About Us | EBD CPA About Edmondson, Betzler & Dame Our firm has a long-standing heritage in Middle Tennessee, with roots dating back to 1960, when Joe Edmondson joined Peat, Marwick, Mitchell & Co. (now KPMG, PLLC.), one of the original Big 8 Accounting firms. In 1976, Joe and John Marlin established Marlin & Edmondson, which for over 25 years was one of the top CPA firms in the Southeast. When Marlin left to explore other business opportunities, the firm's name changed to Edmondson, Betzler & Dame, PLLC with Jeff Betzler and Craig Dame joining with Joe to form the Board of Directors. In all services, our purpose is to help clients identify business or personal financial goals and then aid in accomplishing these goals. How We Will Help Your Business: We will give you a clear picture of the financial health of your business. We will make recommendations on how to improve your internal processes. We will provide direction and guidance on the auditing, accounting and tax issues impacting your particular industry. We scale our services to fit your unique business needs.
