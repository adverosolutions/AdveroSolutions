About Us Levin Tabon CPAs ABOUT LEVIN TABON. Levin Tabon LLP is a Hawaii CPA firm specializing in business and other taxation matters located in Kahului on the island of Maui. The firm is experienced at providing a wide variety of accounting, tax, and financial consulting services tailored to meet the needs of our clients. Our services are value driven and designed with our client’s needs in mind. We are committed to our clients in the following areas: Tax services including income tax planning and audit representation Financial management consulting Non-Profit accounting Accounting system integration and implementation Bookkeeping services including full support for accounting software Payroll services Consulting, including Business Sale and Acquisition The goal at Levin Tabon is to provide quality accounting, tax and consulting services to our clients. We do so by being committed to the development of our professional staff, offering regular in house continuing professional education, and providing a collegial atmosphere which encourages the sharing of knowledge, experience and ideas. The firm’s principals are licensed by the Accountancy Board of the State of Hawaii, are members of the Hawaii Society of Certified Public Accountants, and the Hawaii Association of Public Accountants.
