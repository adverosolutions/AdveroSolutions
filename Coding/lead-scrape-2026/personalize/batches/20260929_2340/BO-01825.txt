About Bookkeeping Financial Management for Agricultural Businesses 0 Photo credit: Kelsey Joy Photography Lily Schneider, MS-MBA Owner, Golden Poppy Bookkeeping Business Solutions LLC Having previously spent a decade as a farm business owner, I know first hand the pressures and challenges of running a small business. I started farming in Solano County, CA just out of college with a dream and no formal business training. I soon learned (the hard way!) the value of a functioning bookkeeping system, accessing credit and cash flow management, and setting long-term goals for the business. Out of my farming career grew a desire to deepen my accounting and business knowledge with the goal of helping agricultural businesses thrive financially. Since 2017 I’ve worked with over 50 farms and agricultural related businesses in the roles of advisor, consultant, and bookkeeper. I have a certificate in Accounting from UC Davis Continuing Education, and a MS-MBA in Food and Agribusiness Management from Purdue and Indiana Universities.
