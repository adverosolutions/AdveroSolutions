About Us SIDELINE ACCOUNTING SERVICES Skip to content Meet the Owner Sideline Accounting Services was founded by Jena David, CPA, with a vision to provide expert financial support to businesses from the sidelines, allowing them to focus on their core operations and growth. Jena David is a Certified Public Accountant with over two decades of diverse experience in accounting, auditing, and financial management. Her career has spanned various industries, including technology, retail, manufacturing, and professional services, giving her a broad perspective on business financial needs. Throughout her career, Jena has held roles ranging from auditor at a Big Four accounting firm to senior accounting manager and associate director at high-growth companies. Her experience includes: Financial statement preparation and analysis Fixed asset management Capital expenditure reporting and analysis Month-end closing processes Intercompany transactions and reconciliations Sales tax reporting Accounts payable and receivable oversight Audit support and preparation Jena s particular strength lies in working with early-stage, high-growth startup companies, helping them navigate the complex financial landscape as they scale.
