Testimonials Services Contact Us About Us My Bookkeeper is a software consulting and bookkeeping firm that specializes in QuickBooks software setup, clean-up, bookkeeping, training and support. Our client base consists of various types of businesses including legal, medical, non-profit organizations, residential communities, contractors, CPA firms and more. We provide our services on an as needed basis, whether it's weekly, monthly, quarterly or once a year to accurately and efficiently process all your financial information into QuickBooks for you and your tax preparer. We customize our services to fit your business needs. Jennifer Springer is the owner of My Bookkeeper, Inc., and the former owner of J.L.B. Business Services, Inc., which began operation in 1991. She is also a QuickBooks Advanced Certified ProAdvisor in the Online, Desktop, Enterprise Solutions and Point of Sale versions. Her knowledge includes more than 30 years of bookkeeping and administration experience. Much of that time was dedicated to law firm and condominium/HOA accounting. She now focuses her efforts on the accurate setup, clean-up, support and training of QuickBooks products. Cleaning up QuickBooks, eliminating business owner's accounting nightmares and providing useful accounting information is what Jennifer excels at and finds the most rewarding. Jennifer is a Florida native and lives in Jupiter Farms with her husband Tom. When shes not working, she seeks outdoor adventrues with Tom and their dogs Bo and Jango, vegetable gardening and photography. Eileen Mendez performs routine bookkeeping services and assists with the catch-up work often involved in bringing many of our new client's books up-to-date. She has a Bachelor of Science degree in Accounting from Florida Atlantic University. Not only does she have experience with both QuickBooks Desktop and QuickBooks Online, she is a QuickBooks Online Certified ProAdvisor. Eileen adds additional law firm experience to our combined wealth of knowledge. When she's not preparing reports and reconciliaing accounts Eileen enjoys spending time with her family and doing outdoor activities. Eileen has a passion for being a Super Mom.
