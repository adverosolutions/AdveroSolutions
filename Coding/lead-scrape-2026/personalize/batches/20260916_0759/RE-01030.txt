--> Our Story 50 Years Leadership | Woman-Founded Firm - DSG About Us DSG Global has led the way in executive search for five decades. Leadership, reimagined In 1974, Judith von Seldeneck recognized a gender gap in executive leadership and decided to do something about it. She invested in a small firm dedicated to finding jobs for women. That small firm evolved into Diversified Search Group, now DSG Global, a top 10 firm in the nation and the largest woman-founded executive search firm in the world. A Catalyst for Growth Since Judee transformed the landscape of executive search 50 years ago, DSG Global has grown exponentially. In 2011, we partnered with AltoPartners — a leading international alliance of retained executive search and leadership consulting firms — expanding our reach to offices in over 30 countries and 65 cities worldwide. Acquisitions across multiple arenas Since 2016, DSG Global acquired seven additional firms — Bioquest (2016), Koya Partners (2019), Grant Cooper (2019), Storbeck Search (2020), Alta Associates (2022), Executive Women's Forum (2022), Yardstick Management (2023) — further expanding our collective expertise and flexibility to collaborate across sectors and discover exceptional talent in creative ways. Ever-evolving. Always authentic. As we continue to expand across sectors and extend our global reach, DSG Global is, and always will be, a rigorous people-first, relationship-driven firm with a collaborative and inclusive approach to finding transformational and gifted leaders. Reflecting on 50 Years. A Night to Remember. This Fall, our company celebrated a milestone that was nothing short of extraordinary—50 years of DSG Global (Diversified Search Group). It was a profound honor to gather with so many remarkable individuals to reflect on where we started, celebrate how far we’ve come, and look forward to the future with excitement and purpose.
