Firm History Striegel Knobloch Company, LLC is a regional accounting firm with offices in Bloomington, Pontiac, and Rantoul, Illinois. There are seven partners and a professional support staff of over forty employees. The Bloomington office has been in existence since 1963, and the partners have over two hundred years of combined experience in providing a variety of audit, tax, accounting, and consulting services. We produce over 50,000 man-hours each year serving a variety of clientele in Central Illinois and throughout the United States. With this experience, we understand what clients expect in terms of quality and timely service. We are committed to producing both because the success of our clients is also our own.
