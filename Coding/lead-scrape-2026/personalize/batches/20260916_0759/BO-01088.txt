About - Fireside Tax About Fireside Tax Fireside Tax Starting in 2004, Fireside Tax has provided excellent Tax, Accounting and Payroll Services to Stark County, Ohio and beyond! Paralegal to Local Professional Tax Firm Fireside was started by Timothy P. Singo as a freelance paralegal business. The demand for prearing taxes to settle the estates was much higher and profitable that its grown into the Fireside you know today. What's with the fireplace? The name came from the fireplace in the basement room where Tim started out this business. The logo is actually a picture of this fireplace. Fireside Tax has grown steadily over the years, expanding its reach and services to meet the needs of the community. The firm first relocated to Canton Centre Mall, where it operated for five years, before moving across from Central Catholic High School. Founder Tim brought extensive experience in probate, focusing on the complex taxes and accounting involved in estate work. As more clients began turning to him for personal and business tax preparation, Tim discovered both a passion and a natural talent for this field. To strengthen his expertise, he pursued additional coursework and earned an accounting degree, building on his prior education. His reputation for combining fair pricing with exceptional service has fueled Fireside’s growth, even during years when the number of returns filed by paid preparers declined nationwide. In 2012, Fireside Tax evolved into a full-service accounting firm, expanding beyond tax preparation to include payroll, bookkeeping, and comprehensive accounting services. Many of Fireside’s business clients began with the firm at their founding and have since grown into million-dollar enterprises with Fireside’s continued guidance. In 2024, Fireside Tax proudly moved into its new office at 4976 Tuscarawas St W, marking another step forward in serving clients with excellence. Today, Fireside Tax is committed to providing accurate tax returns and financial documents through advanced software and personalized consultations. Our team has expanded to include multiple accountants, seasonal preparers and more, enabling us to better serve the growing needs of individuals and businesses across Ohio. At Fireside Tax, our vision is to be “Fireside Forever.” Over the years, we’ve seen what happens when local accountants retire or pass away—clients are often left in disarray, unsure where to turn for guidance.
