Meet Our Leadership Coach Skip to content About Us Leadership Coaching Services Reimagined Is Working with a Leadership Coach the Right Move? We certainly think so! At Recruit Rebels, we don’t subscribe to the idea that there’s only one right way for you to become the best leader. Just like no two people are the same, no two leadership styles are either, but we believe that at the core, emotions play a huge role in effective management practices. From music to motion to exciting experiences, we’ll encourage you to step outside of your typical 9 to 5 mindset to discover better ways to lead. Explore Our Services Meet Our President and Leadership Coach with the Most David Dekowski is not the type of leadership coach who promises to change your life—only you have that power. Instead, he provides the tools, motivation, and imaginative thinking that help business leaders like you confidently step out of the neatly packaged box society has placed you all in. As a team, David encourages clients to take a walk on the wild side by going against the traditional methods of coaching to bring out the most authentic and change-making leaders. With an occupational history as an award-winning sales management professional, David has developed a reputation for being an excellent judge of character and having a great eye for leadership strengths. In addition to serving as the president and head leadership coach at Recruit Rebels, he is also an adjunct professor at Howard Community College, teaching Entrepreneurship and Creativity, and COO of Devaney Associates, Inc., a full-service advertising, marketing, and public relations agency. Many of David’s leadership qualities stem from personal experiences, such as overcoming Perthes disease as a child, being a proud husband and father, and his current pursuit of becoming a renowned public speaker.
