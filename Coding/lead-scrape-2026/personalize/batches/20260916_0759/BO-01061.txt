Biography As an experienced accountant, Mark has helped people structure their assets, increase their profits, and minimize their tax burdens for over 20 years. Mark began his journey as a corporate controller for an airline parts brokerage in Milwaukee, WI after graduating from SIU-Carbondale. He steered the company through a multi-million dollar merger and then took the helm as CFO before embarking on consulting for small to mid-sized businesses across the nation. From managing finances to optimizing profits, Mark knows that great accountants ask great questions. The problem? Many accountants aren’t trained to do so. Setting sights beyond the status quo. Determined to help the person behind the number, Mark pursued an advanced degree at The John Marshall Law School to complement his work experience, earning both a Juris Doctor Degree and a Master of Laws in Tax Law in 2019. At the same time, Mark also completed his MBA in HR Management from Robert Morris University. Now, Mark and the AMD team confidently approach any tax situation with a holistic view of what businesses and individuals actually need out of their accountant.
