Our Story In 2006, Chris Stark was a successful sales professional working in medical equipment sales. Like most salespeople, he got calls from recruiters all the time—people claiming they had the "perfect opportunity" for him. Most of those calls were terrible. Recruiters who hadn't read his resume. Recruiters pitching roles that made no sense for his background. Recruiters who disappeared the moment he didn't immediately say yes. It was transactional, impersonal, and frustrating. Chris realized: if this is what it's like to be a candidate, it must be even worse to be a hiring manager working with these firms. So he started The CAS Group with a simple idea: What if recruiting was actually done well? What if recruiters took the time to understand both sides? What if they only submitted candidates who were qualified and truly interested? What if they treated recruiting like a long-term partnership instead of a quick transaction? With that vision, our no-compromise approach to sales and sales management recruitment was born. Our Expertise Our first goal is to determine your firm's needs. Our experience spans a variety of industries, ranging from healthcare to manufacturing. The intensity of our sales training background, coupled with our previous sales successes, gives you seasoned recruiters who know what traits and qualifications to look for in a candidate. Our Advisory Our top priorities rest with finding fit between your company and the candidates that we ultimately select and submit for you to interview. We serve as a resource and trusted advisor throughout the process to ensure that both candidate and client are compatible with each other's requirements. We work best with clients who are interested in maintaining long-term relationships. We are salespeople who understand salespeople, and know how to find and place them in a way that is cost-efficient and saves your company time in the short and long run.
