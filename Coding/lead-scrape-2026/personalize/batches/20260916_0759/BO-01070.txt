Northbrook Accounting and Tax Services The Dolins Group in Northbrook is a trusted CPA firm serving businesses and individuals throughout the North Shore and Northwest Chicago suburbs, including Northbrook, Glenview, Deerfield, and surrounding communities. With decades of real-world experience, our team provides a full range of accounting services designed to support small businesses and individuals at every stage. We work closely with businesses to provide bookkeeping, tax planning, and new business advisory services that help owners better manage their finances, reduce tax liability, and improve overall profitability. For individuals we offer reliable income tax preparation and guidance to ensure every available deduction and credit is considered so clients never pay more than they owe. Careful Strategy. Long-Term Thinking. Founded in 1993 by Leonard Dolins and his son Steve, The Dolins Group, Ltd. builds on Leonard’s accounting career that began in 1961. Originally a small family-run practice, the firm has grown steadily under Steve’s leadership since 2000. Over the years, TDG has welcomed additional partners and staff while maintaining the trusted Dolins name in the Chicagoland area. Rebranded in 2011, the firm continues to combine the personalized attention of a family firm with the depth of services of a larger practice—guided by the belief that client success drives firm success.
