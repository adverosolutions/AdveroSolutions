About Us David Winner With over forty years of a proven track record in accounting, administration and human resources, David is a professional with special expertise in improving profits and workflow, as well as strong organizational, technical and mentoring skills. Born in Chicago, a graduate of the University of Illinois Chicago BSc (Econ), his experience includes Controller of an international manufacturing concern in Toronto, working in senior management positions for large financial institutions as well as some of the top law firms in Chicago.
