Be Additive Corporation - Connecting the World s Talent with Your Opportunities About Us Meet the team behind Be Additive. We re architects of global workforce solutions. Our story is one of relentless innovation and a commitment to our clients and candidates success. Our Story Be Additive s two founders, Hank Levine and Kash Sampat, built one of the world s largest global recruitment process outsourcing (RPO) companies. Over a span of almost 18 years, working with their teams in India and the Philippines, their accomplishments included: Growing the company s employee headcount to close to 1,000 Providing RPO services to over 400 clients Placing over 28,000 professionals into high-quality jobs in thirty countries on five continents Registering a Net Promoter Score of 83.3% from the company s clients At Be Additive, we are using the knowledge and experience we gained at our predecessor company and doing it better. Be Additive is about our unwavering passion to build an ethical company committed to making the world a better place, to take an innovative approach to recruiting utilizing the latest technology, and to connect the right people with the right opportunities. Leadership Team Statement Of Purpose Leaving the World a Better Place Be Additive was born from Koinophobia, the fear of being ordinary. The thought of having an ordinary job, working with people with the same backgrounds as me, and not making a difference is terrifying. I do not come to work every day and Be Additive does not exist just to make money. Making money is important, but it needs to be kept in balance. A life dedicated to acquiring money and material goods is an empty life. And there is always somebody with more money, so you will always lose. I love being an entrepreneur because it s what I can do best to make a difference. At Be Additive I interact with innovated and talented people from around the world – people with very different life experiences than those where I was raised. Be Additive is a company that touches and transforms many lives for the better. A company that is ethical and lasting.
