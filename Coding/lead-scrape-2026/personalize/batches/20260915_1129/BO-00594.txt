Learn more about Foxx Fiscal Solutions Founder - President CEO Doug Foxx below. Doug Foxx, MBA Founder, President CEO - Foxx Fiscal Solutions 20+ Years of Professional Experience in business and non-profit organizational management and leadership Graduate of The Ohio State University and Bluffton University Doug Foxx works with clients to establish work flows that help them manage their business and personal finances to clearly understand their assets, budgets, revenue, expenses, current and projected future balances, payroll and tax liabilities, and much more.
