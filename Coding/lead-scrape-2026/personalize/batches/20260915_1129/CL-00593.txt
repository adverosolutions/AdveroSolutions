About Us | All-N-One Maintenance Facility Services AFS Group Inc. MISSION STATEMENT: Provide our customers with proactive and cost affordable maintenance solutions. We will continue to refine our quality, expand our capabilities, increase our efficiency, and elevate the superior level of customer service we now provide. OUR BEGINNING: In 1984 Glenn Russell started sweeping parking lots at night as a High School Student. By 1986 he had purchased his first parking lot sweeper and started his own sweeping business. As a hands on owner the business grew and customers began requesting many additional services. In 1995 he incorporated the name All-N-One Maintenance, Inc. to be reflective of the wide range of services the company provided. As growth continued Mr. Russell earned his Bachelors in Business from Azusa Pacific University to help him navigate the many hurdles of a growing multi-million dollar company. By 2014 the increasing demand of our specialty services and storm system maintenance business let us to re-brand. After much consideration we proudly announced our new name All-N-One Facilities Services Group, Inc. AKA AFS Group, Inc. With the name change we also were able to bring a better web presence and easier email addresses. No changes were made to management or staffing. We will continue to focus on employing skilled people with a focus on excellent service. Our growth will never out pace our skill. WHO WE ARE: We are a fully licensed and insured company that specializes in multiple phases of commercial property maintenance that hires properly trained employees to complete the jobs. WHO WE ARE NOT: Jack of all trades handyman company. I depend on many valuable employees to fulfill our mission statement.
