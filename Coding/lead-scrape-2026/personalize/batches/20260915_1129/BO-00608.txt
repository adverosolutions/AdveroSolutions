Contact Us How It All Started Founder Larry E. Robert's passion for helping local businesses succeed was the driving force behind the establishment of Payroll Pros in October of 2006. His vision was simple – to be the area’s leading payroll service provider. With a profound understanding of the financial intricacies businesses face, Larry aimed to provide a payroll solution tailored to the unique needs of small to medium-sized companies. Meet the Core Team Larry E. Roberts, CPA, CFE Larry started his career in Tax and Accounting more than 25 years ago after graduating from Youngstown State University with a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration, majoring in accounting. In 2002, Larry started his own Accounting and Tax practice. Sensing the need for a local payroll provider, he started Payroll Pros in 2006. Besides Payroll Pros, Larry is a practicing CPA and part of the team at Fluent Ricciardi CPA’s. In his free time, Larry enjoys cooking, reading, traveling and spending time with his wife of nearly 40 years and his daughters. The greatest joys however are his grandchildren.
