Quest Technical Resources, LLC was formed in 1999 to help information technology-based companies to identify and hire scarce technical sales professionals at all levels into fulltime professional careers. Our clients are software and information technology driven businesses of all sizes. Our process begins by first understanding our clients specific business needs and then carefully matching them with the best-qualified candidates that are available in the market. We carefully screen our candidates and make a good-faith effort to only present those that have the ability, motivation and skills necessary to be successful on the job. The firm is based in Cincinnati, Ohio and is supported by over sixteen years of successful recruiting and placement experience. We support sales organizations across North America. We manage both Contingency and Retained Searches, as well as contract recruiting assignments.
