Who We Are - promisesvc.com Contact Our Promise Green Cleaning Safety. That’s a Promise! Promise Total Services stands on the ethos of reliability and trust. While working in the janitorial service industry for years from the bottom up, our CEO and founder, Helen Yoo, witnessed how casually promises were made and broken. Finally, in 2003, she founded Promise as a company bereft of empty promises and excuses, certified by NCTRCA as a Minority and Women-Owned Business Enterprise (MWBE). Promise results speak for themselves! The Promise team, collectively spanning 100+ years of experience in the service industry, takes pride in Promising what we can deliver – and a lot more. Mission Promise aims to deliver the superior quality, affordability, and professionalism you deserve from a well-established commercial and industrial cleaning business. Our CEO is involved in every endeavor, giving Promise the distinct personal touch of a family business backed by the reliable communication and structure of a large corporation. Our Green Promise is to prioritize our clients’ and community’s health by cultivating safe indoor environments with cleaning methods that are eco-friendly and energy efficient. We are committed to performing customized services to ensure a seamless, sustainable, and enjoyable experience for each of our clients. Vision Promise’s vision is to elevate every building, environment, relationship, and person we encounter with an ethical and humanitarian spirit. Our core values of being Proactive, Responsive, Observant, Meticulous, Innovative, Sensible, and Ethical reflect our desire to be more than a business affiliate, but a partner you can be proud of. Our promise to our team is to continually express appreciation for the diverse cultures that constitute the rich fabric of who we are. We offer College Scholarships, Language Courses, Donation Matching, and mentorship with the hope of seeing each Promise individual flourish and succeed. Our vision is to leverage our strengths in a way that will ultimately level up the entire service platform. We work today to build a better tomorrow!
