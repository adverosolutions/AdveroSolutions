Maggie LaHaie Owner I m Maggie LaHaie, and I m passionate about unraveling the financial difficulties of the business world. With years of hands-on experience in bookkeeping and financial management, I ve had the privilege of working closely with many businesses like yours. My journey began in 2006 when I started as a temp customer service representative for one of the largest door manufacturers in the country. After 3 years, several promotions, and a move to a new state, I was overseeing the accounting department of a new location. That is when the 2008 recession hit, and accounting everywhere was outsourced. I used the opportunity to go back to school to obtain my B.B.A. Economics from Grand Valley State University as well as a B.S. degree in International Economics from Uniwersytet Ekonomiczny w Krakowie after a one-year study abroad. Through countless interactions with ambitious business owners like you, I ve witnessed firsthand the challenges and opportunities that define being a small to medium size business owner. At Legend Bookkeeping, I believe that accurate and up-to-date financial data is the foundation for making informed business decisions. With remote bookkeeping services, I aim to streamline your accounting processes, save you time and money, and provide you with the peace of mind that your financial records are in expert hands. I am proficient in various accounting software platforms and follow best practices to ensure the accuracy and confidentiality of your financial data. Legend Bookkeeping offers a range of services, including accounts payable and receivable management, bank and credit card reconciliations, payroll processing, financial reporting, CFO services and more. What sets us apart is our commitment to personalized service and attention to detail. I take the time to understand your business needs and tailor our services to meet your specific requirements. Our remote bookkeeping services allow you to focus on what you do best – running your business – while we take care of your bookkeeping needs. I pride myself on our professionalism, reliability, and integrity. I operate with the highest ethical standards, ensuring that your financial data remains secure and confidential.
