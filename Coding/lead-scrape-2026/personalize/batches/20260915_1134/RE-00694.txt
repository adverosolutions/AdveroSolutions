About Pedagog Recruiting Careers - Jeff Bockelman Skip to content About Jeff Bockelman Founder and Chief Recruiter "People aren't resumes, and jobs aren't just openings to fill. (opens in new tab) It Started with a Simple Belief Before Pedagog existed, our founder, Jeff Bockelman , spent years working in accounting, finance, recruiting, and human resources. Along the way, he discovered something that would shape the rest of his career: people are at the heart of every successful business. After nearly 30 years of helping organizations find talent and helping professionals navigate their careers, Jeff founded Pedagog in 2013 with a simple mission... to make recruiting more personal. Not more complicated. Not more transactional. Just more human. Since day one, we've focused on building real relationships with both the companies we serve and the professionals we represent. We take the time to understand what success looks like for our clients and what truly motivates the candidates we work with. Because the best placements aren't made by matching keywords on a resume. They're made by understanding people. Over the years, we've partnered with organizations of all sizes, helping them fill critical roles across industries and tackle searches that aren't always straightforward. We've also had the privilege of guiding countless professionals through career moves that changed the trajectory of their lives. Many of our client relationships span years, not because we're the biggest recruiting firm, but because we're invested in their success. When our clients grow, we grow. When our candidates thrive, we celebrate right alongside them. At Pedagog, we believe recruiting is about more than filling positions. It's about creating connections that help people, teams, and businesses reach their full potential. That's what drove us in 2013, and it's what continues to drive us today.
