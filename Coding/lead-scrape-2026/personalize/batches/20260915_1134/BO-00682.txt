It reflects our core mission: to provide comprehensive financial solutions that empower businesses to focus on what matters most and achieve their goals.​​ The name also honors Luca Pacioli who is known as the father of accounting. His seminal work Summa de Arithmetica (1494) was the first published writing describing many modern accounting principles, including double-entry bookkeeping. ​ The very day a debit is born, it has a twin credit so it is natural for them to always go together in your books.
