Team 1 Booms Accounting 0 “Having learned much about the needs of clients through the years, I was inspired to start my own firm to focus on helping individuals and business owners succeed financially and make more informed decisions with their finances.” Brad Booms, Owner and CPA Brad grew up in the Thumb of Michigan on his family farm in Harbor Beach, Michigan. Upon graduating high school in 2010, Brad attended Delta College and Northwood where he graduated Magna Cum Laude from Northwood University in May 2013 with a Bachelor of Business Administration degree with a major in Accounting. He completed the four parts of the CPA exams along with the work experience requirement and received his CPA license in April 2014. Brad is a member of the AICPA and the MICPA. When he’s not crunching numbers and building his business, he enjoys spending time with his family and friends and is an avid outdoorsman. Experience Brad worked at Yeo Yeo CPAs Business Consultants (a regional accounting firm based in Midland, Michigan for 4 and ½ years, providing tax and consulting services to small to medium-sized businesses and high net worth individuals. There, he also became a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor and assisted clients with their QuickBooks program and accounting software needs. In May of 2017, Brad moved closer to home and transitioned to Nietzke Faupel, PC (a leading Agricultural-based CPA firm in Pigeon, MI). Here, he learned about accounting and taxes related to Agribusinesses and accrual basis financial statements. Open for Business Brad had a successful history of fulfilling client needs as a CPA in both regional and local accounting firms. With quality work, reliability, and timeliness, Brad grew in knowledge and experience of accounting for a number of different industries. Brad founded the firm on October 11, 2019, upon his withdrawal from a local accounting firm. Brad is most concerned with the value he can bring to his clients. By carefully considering not only your finances but also you and your unique needs and preferences, Brad can offer a custom full-service accounting system that helps you achieve your financial goals.
