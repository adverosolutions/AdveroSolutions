Expert Trained Teams Quality Assurance Eco-Conscious Fast Response Our Story KONIG KLEAN was established with a clear purpose: to provide premium, reliable cleaning and painting services designed specifically for the demands of New York City. In NYC, property transitions move quickly. Apartments turn over with little notice. Commercial spaces operate on strict schedules. Property managers and landlords cannot afford delays, miscommunication, or inconsistent service. We recognized a recurring challenge in the market — clients were coordinating multiple vendors for cleaning, painting, and turnover preparation, often resulting in scheduling conflicts, extended vacancy periods, and unnecessary stress. KONIG KLEAN was created to simplify that process. One team. One coordinated schedule. One trusted partner.
