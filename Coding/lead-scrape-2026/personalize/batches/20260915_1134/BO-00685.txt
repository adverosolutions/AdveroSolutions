We re glad you re here. Since opening our doors in 2001, we ve been helping individuals, families, and businesses with taxes, bookkeeping, and payroll. Over the years, we ve learned numbers may be our thing, but they aren t everyone s thing—and that s okay. Our team includes an Enrolled Agent (EA), which is a federally licensed tax professional who specializes in tax matters and is authorized to represent taxpayers before the IRS. Enrolled Agents and CPAs are different credentials, but both are respected professionals in the accounting and tax field. Our focus is providing knowledgeable tax, bookkeeping, and payroll support while helping our clients understand their options and make confident decisions. We believe in continuing to learn so we can give our clients the best answers we can. Whether we re helping with tax questions, keeping the books organized, or making sure payroll gets done correctly, our goal is to make things easier. Bookkeeping, payroll, and taxes don t have to be intimidating. Some business owners love digging into the numbers, and some would much rather spend their time doing what they do best—running their business. We understand both. Our philosophy is simple: you concentrate on what you do best, and we ll concentrate on what we do best. We ll take care of the details, answer your questions, and help you understand what you need to know (without burying you in things you don t). As a locally owned business, we re proud to support our community and the many other small businesses that make it special.
