How We Work Are you struggling to attract top technical talent during critical growth phases? Over the last decade, we have helped dozens of tech startups hire hundreds of early employees, often working as early as seed funding and continuing through IPO. Our services make timely hiring accessible to startups at any stage. Services Monthly Retainer Designed for teams who need to hire now but are not quite ready to commit to a full-time internal recruiter. We offer a full desk recruiter-on-demand that easily scales up and down with your hiring needs. We will optimize your ATS (or select one), screen applicants, source passive talent, coordinate interviews, extend offers, and close candidates. Retained Search Designed for teams who need to hire Lead/Staff/Principal Software Engineers, Engineering Managers/Directors, or any other high-priority, niche roles. We offer a robust, executive-style search process without a six-figure price tag. We will identify the population of relevant candidates and work through a multi-channel messaging strategy to attract them. You’ll have real-time visibility augmented by weekly calls so you’re always up to speed on progress. View fullsize View fullsize View fullsize About Us Matt fell into recruiting over a decade ago. In his first five years at an agency, he supported NY tech startups such as Peloton, View The Space (VTS), Citymaps, WeWork, and Gemini, before they had any internal recruiters. He continued to support through their high growth stages and often post-IPO. In 2018, Matt branched out on his own. Believing that a narrower focus and commitment would result in a better search process and result, he worked with a select few teams to test this theory. He worked as a contract internal recruiter for four different startups and successfully delivered on all open reqs across software engineering, design, product management, and product marketing. Matt continues to believe that, with the appropriate focus, process, and commitment from both the hiring team and recruiting partner, it is possible to consistently make key hires on time and on budget.
