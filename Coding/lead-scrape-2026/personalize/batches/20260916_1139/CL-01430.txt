About | Omni Workspace ABOUT THE OMNI BRANDS Our Omni family of brands are united under the core principle of building long-term business relationships. As one team, we’re an end-to-end partner for our clients, bringing valuable solutions through prefabricated construction, cutting-edge furniture solutions, and comprehensive warehousing installation services. We serve leading organizations around the globe across workplace, education, healthcare, hospitality, and sports. Atmosphere Commercial Interiors is one of the largest Steelcase dealers and commercial furnishing providers in the country with locations across Minnesota, Wisconsin, Illinois, Arizona, New Mexico, and Texas. Atmosphere s purpose is to create spaces that are engaging, inspiring, and perfectly tailored to clients’ needs and goals. VISIT ATMOSPHERECI.COM Aurora Procurement Solutions is a full-service FF E procurement and project management firm dedicated to the hospitality industry. From boutique hotels to multi-family housing and senior living, Aurora brings deep expertise, trusted partnerships, and a refined process to every project, supporting exceptional guest experiences by delivering design-aligned spaces with project precision and transparency. VISIT AURORA-PS.COM Emerald Blue is a full-service office furniture installation and facility services company with locations in Minnesota, Wisconsin, Illinois, Arizona, and Washington. Established in 2023, the brand unites A M Business Interior Services, Atmosphere Services, and Emerald City Moving Storage under one name. Backed by over 40 years of experience, these exceptional teams have delivered trusted commercial facility services to leading organizations across the corporate, healthcare, education, hospitality, and sports industries. VISIT EMERALDBLUE.COM Meso Built launched in 2022 to expand Omni’s capabilities in the built environment, delivering a wide range of interior construction solutions across Minnesota, Wisconsin, Illinois, North Dakota, and Arizona. As a signature partner of Falkbuilt, Meso leverages digital component construction to provide clients with a fully digital design experience and an off-site prefabrication process; offering faster turnaround times, reduced waste, and highly accurate estimating compared to traditional drywall construction.
