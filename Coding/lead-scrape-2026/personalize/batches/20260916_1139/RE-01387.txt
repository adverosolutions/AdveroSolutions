About Us - POLIHIRE Executive Search About Us Over 20 Years Connecting People For Over 20 Years Since 2006, POLIHIRE has built a record of unparalleled success in identifying leadership with a focus on excellence, accountability, and diversity in public-serving agencies and organizations. Our Mission POLIHIRE partners with public-serving and mission-based organizations to deeply understand their leadership needs and identify the highest-caliber candidates whose skill sets, demonstrated track record, and future aspirations are aligned with our clients’ commitment to service, excellence and equity. Our Vision At POLIHIRE we believe that public-serving and mission-based organizations require leaders to be more than technical experts in their fields. They must also be champions of the idea that success is only achieved when their missions are delivered equitably, when their services are accessible to all who might benefit, when inclusion is a hallmark of their operations, and when their institutions reflect and celebrate the diversity of the communities they serve. Through our partnership and engagement with clients, we aspire to have the most critical public-serving and mission-based organizations led by visionary leaders who embody a deep commitment to service, excellence, and equity. Our Values Integrity Humanity Curiosity Humility Candor Representation Purpose Integrity At the core of everything we do is our uncompromising commitment to operate ethically and with integrity. In all aspects of our work and engagements, we act responsibly, lead with transparency and demonstrate courage. We always strive for excellence but when we fall short, we take accountability and endeavor to make it right. Humanity Executive search is a professional service, but candidates are not commodities. We recognize the vulnerability that even the most seasoned leaders experience the moment they become candidates for a new position. We respect it, we hold space for it, and we treat this relationship with the utmost care. Curiosity There is no one-size-fits-all in executive search. Solutions are individual, and the more we understand, the better we can serve. We ask, we delve, we wonder, and we probe so that we might co-develop a deep understanding of what our clients desire in ideal candidates – enabling us to identify leaders whose skills, aspirations, and values are well aligned.
