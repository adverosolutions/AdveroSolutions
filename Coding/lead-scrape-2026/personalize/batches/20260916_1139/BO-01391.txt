RACHEL HASSELL, STRATEGIC, BALANCED ETHICAL BOOKKEEPING Rachel Hassell is an expert bookkeeping strategist, offering over 30 years experience. Although effectively working with all industries, Rachel specializes in Non-profit, Construction and Retail bookkeeping. She is an efficient director of project management and human resources, and an expert handler of daily bookkeeping operations. Rachel has a strong commitment to community, and has proudly volunteered for local schools, Safe Routes to School, Animal Friends Alliance, Salvation Army, is a former foster parent, and is a proud graduate of the Chamber of Commerce Leadership Fort Collins program (2012-2013). Rachel is proud to be an Air Force brat, and is grateful to have been able to live in such locations as Spain, Panama, Wyoming, North Dakota, Germany, Texas, and Northern Virginia. Rachel moved to Fort Collins, Colorado in 2002 to attend Colorado State University. From there, Rachel served as Accounting/Marketing Manager for a local landmark bicycle shop for nine years. Rachel then realized her vision of being a small business owner in 2014. She is proud mom to 3 grown children, 3 dogs and 2 cats. 2024 brought big changes with a new granddaughter that brought Rachel to Northern California.
