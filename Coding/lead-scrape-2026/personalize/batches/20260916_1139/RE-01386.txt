Potomac Partners is a full service recruiting firm based in Bethesda, Maryland that specializes in the recruitment and placement of top financial professionals to meet the needs of our clients. Our clients include some of the world’s most prestigious and reputable Fortune 500 companies to early stage, pre-IPO companies in a variety of industries. Our team of industry experts is dedicated to selectively recruiting top professionals. Due to the personal and confidential nature in which we conduct our searches, we have established a deep and broad network of talented professionals giving us the reputation as one of the leading executive search and recruiting firms in the Washington DC and Baltimore area, primarily servicing companies in the Washington, DC, Virginia and Maryland.
