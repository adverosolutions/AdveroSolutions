About - Bookingkoala Skip to content Our Story BookingKoala is an all-in-one platform for businesses that operate in the service industry. Anything from home cleaning, mobile car detailing, lawn care, or anything related that you can come up with. We help you start and grow your businesses with the help of our technology, community groups, and content. BookingKoala was founded in 2017 and is 100% founder-owned Founder story In 2013 Filip Boksa launched King of Maids, a cleaning service in Chicago, IL, with his best friend from high school. To get ahead of their competition, they hired a company to build a software for them. The software helped them scale the business to over $5,000,000 in 3 years. In 2016, Boksa purchased the other 50% of the company and became the sole owner of King of Maids. Filip Boksa Co-founder Vivek Prajapati Co-founder During this time, Boksa closely worked with the head developer, Vivek Prajapati. They formed a close bond, and after discovering there was a need for what they built, they decided to redesign the software and let others use it. In 2017, Prajapati and Boksa formed the company, and in 2018 BookingKoala was launched to the public. Today BookingKoala is home to thousands of entrepreneurs worldwide and across many different industries. Boksa serves as the company s CEO, while Prajapati serves as the CTO of BookingKoala. Together they are focused on making BookingKoala the perfect platform to grow a service business.
