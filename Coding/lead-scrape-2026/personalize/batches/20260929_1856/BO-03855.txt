The Bookkeeping Process GATHER/IMPORT Financial transactions are gathered and imported throughout the month. CATEGORIZE Each transaction is categorized, and available receipts are attached. RECONCILE Each account is reconciled at the close of each month. REVIEW Reconciliations, accounts, and reports are reviewed for accuracy. REPORT Financial Statements are presented in order to give you an accurate understanding of your company's finances.
