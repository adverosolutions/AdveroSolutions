About Skyward Cart 0 Back Accounting Business Consulting Fractional CFO Fraud Risk Assessment and Internal Control Review School District Support Back Personal Financial Consulting Budgetary Services Scroll Daniel Haynes, CFE - Founder ceo Daniel Haynes started Skyward Financial Solutions in the fall of 2018. In efforts to serve businesses and non-profits best, Skyward offers Bookkeeping (day to day accounting services), financial reporting, internal controls set up, measuring and monitoring internal controls, risk assessments, Fractional CFO services that consists of strategic planning, forecasting, formulating and optimizing financial strategies and navigating audits. In addition to business services, Skyward aims to serve people and their families by focusing on personal financial health through budgeting, identifying financial habits, and helping formulate a plan to reach financial goals. Daniel graduated with a BBA in Accounting from Oklahoma Christian University. In 2016, he became a Certified Fraud Examiner. While being a CFE, he also a Certified Quickbooks Proadvisor. Daniel has 13 years of accounting and finance experience ranging from small 6 figure non-profits to multi-billion for profit corporate companies including experience in faith based, tech, oil and gas, and education industries. In addition to being selected as a NextGen award recipient, Daniel has also been featured in Oklahoma Magazine as a top 40 under 40 award recipient.
