About - Wolden Accounting Financial Services LLC Skip to content About Us About Wolden Accounting Wolden Accounting Financial Services, led by Robin Wolden, delivers expert accounting and financial support tailored for small to mid-sized service-based businesses. With over 25 years of experience across diverse industries, Robin offers personalized, dependable solutions—allowing clients to stay focused on running their businesses while she takes care of the numbers. Our Story After two decades in corporate accounting, we launched Wolden Accounting with a clear mission: to help small business owners bring structure and confidence to their financial lives. Robin has a deep passion for organization and thrives on turning financial chaos into clarity. Her unique blend of precision, insight, and empathy empowers business owners to make informed decisions with peace of mind. Who We Serve Our services are designed for small to mid-sized service-based businesses, particularly in the insurance, property management, and homeowners’ association (HOA) sectors. We understand the specific financial needs of these industries and provide bookkeeping and accounting support that’s both strategic and reliable. Partnership Opportunities We collaborate with CPA firms looking to outsource their bookkeeping functions, as well as with banks, insurance agencies, and other organizations that serve small businesses. Our partners trust us to deliver accurate, professional, and client-focused financial support that enhances the services they offer. About Robin Robin holds bachelor’s degrees in both accounting (BAcc)and finance (BBA-Finance) from New Mexico State University (NMSU), as well as a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Western Governors University. She moved to Las Vegas in 1991 after completing her studies at NMSU and has called it home ever since. She’s been married to her husband, Stuart, since 2006. Together, they enjoy traveling across the United.States., with a special love for Colorado, where they frequently visit their son and extended family. Outside of her professional life, Robin is actively involved in her church community and finds joy in creative hobbies, such as quilting and wool appliqué.
