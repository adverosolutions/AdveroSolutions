What is bookkeeping? Bookkeeping is the systematic recording and organizing of a company’s day-to-day financial transactions. Core focus: Accuracy and completeness of records Typical bookkeeping tasks include: Recording sales, expenses, and payments Categorizing transactions Maintaining the general ledger Reconciling bank and credit card accounts Managing accounts receivable and payable Managing payroll processing Preparing basic financial reports (e.g., transaction summaries) Bookkeeping answers the question: “What happened financially?” What is accounting? Accounting is a broader discipline that includes bookkeeping, but goes beyond it to interpret, adjust, analyze, and report financial information.
