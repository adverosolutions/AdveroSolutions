John D. Shreck CPA, MBA — Founder, JDS Financial Management PC linkedin.com/in/johnshreck My path to accounting wasn't a straight line — and I think that's what makes me better at it. I grew up in Des Moines, Iowa, and it was actually my time as an accounting specialist in the U.S. Army that first sparked my interest in the field. After my service, I earned a bachelor's degree in Economics and Finance from the University of Iowa in 2002, and my career took off from there. When my wife was accepted to medical school at Baylor in Houston, I followed her south — and honestly, it was one of the best things that could have happened for my career. I spent two years as a financial analyst before transitioning into public accounting with a prestigious local firm, where I earned my CPA license. In 2006, we moved to Salt Lake City for her residency, and Utah quickly became home. I continued growing my accounting career and earned my MBA from the University of Utah's David Eccles School of Business with an emphasis in Tax, Finance, and Operations. Living alongside a physician for more than two decades — through medical school, residency, and attending practice — has given me a direct view of what physician finances actually look like. Hospital contracts, multi-state locum work, malpractice considerations, call schedules, deferred compensation, partnership buy-ins, and PC entity structures are part of the day-to-day reality of physician families, and that perspective informs how I work with clients. After more than a decade of working inside other organizations, I founded JDS Financial Management PC in 2013 here in Salt Lake City. The reason was simple: I wanted to do things the right way. That means delivering quality advice at a fair price, working directly with clients, and treating every client's financial situation with the attention it deserves. When you work with JDS Financial Management, you work with me. I'm responsive, accessible, and committed to delivering thorough, timely work — by phone, video, or in person.
