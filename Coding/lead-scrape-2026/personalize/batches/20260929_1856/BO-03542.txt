If you use your personal vehicle for business, keeping track of your mileage is an important part of maintaining good business and tax records. Business owners often remember the obvious trips, but those smaller trips throughout the year can add up quickly. A trip to the bank, supply store, post office, client meeting, or job site may not seem like much at the time, but over an entire year those miles can become significant.
