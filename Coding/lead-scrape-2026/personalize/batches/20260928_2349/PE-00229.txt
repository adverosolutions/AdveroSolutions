Michael Ryan purchased Tempco in 1996. Then, in 1998, he hired Scott Harding, who became his business partner. Scott’s wife, Maria, joined the team in 2009 as the customer service and routing lead. In 2014, Michael’s wife, Laska, came aboard to manage the marketing and human resources aspects of the business. Michael and Laska’s son, Andrew, graduated college in 2019. He endeavors to take the helm of the family business someday – which has been his goal since middle-school when he first asked for business cards in his name!
