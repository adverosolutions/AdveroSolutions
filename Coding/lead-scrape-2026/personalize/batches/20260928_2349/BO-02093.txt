About us We re a locally owned and operated accounting firm. At Geiger, CPA, PC, we are committed to building strong, lasting relationships with our clients. Integrity and the highest ethical standards drive everything that we do. Working in a partnership with our clients, our firm s success is built solely on their success. The personal assistance that each of our clients receive comes as the result of years of advanced training, technical experience and financial insight. Our commitment is to provided every client with close, personal attention.
