About About Us Humphrey, Covill Coleman Insurance Agency, Inc. was founded in 1926 by Ernest J. Humphrey and Raymond F. Covill, both former Liberty Mutual employees. In 1948 , Philip L. Coleman Humphrey s Yale-educated nephew and Navy veteran joined the firm, giving rise to the agency s current name. The Covill family s legacy continued as Raymond A. Covill joined in 1956 , followed by his son, Pete, in 1984 , and daughter, Elizabeth (Lisa) Covill Gibson, in 1991 . Today, the fourth generation Wiley Gibson, great-grandson of one of the founders carries that legacy forward, alongside Barry Dextraze and Hayden O Brien. W e have proudly served families and businesses throughout the New Bedford area for nearly a century. Across generations, HCC has grown with the same client-focused values, providing tailored insurance solutions, flexible payment plans, and efficient service making us a trusted partner in protecting families and businesses throughout the region. The HCC Difference means a century of experience, backed by modern solutions and a promise of honest advice, multiple carrier options, and service you can trust.
