Lawn Care Services - O Mara Landscape and Lawn Care | Blands NC Skip to content Get to know the founder of O'Mara Landscaping & Lawn care, Inc. James O Mara, a nature enthusiast born and raised on the Emerald Isle, developed a deep love for nature and gardening from a young age. Inspired by the lush landscapes of County Wicklow, Ireland, he began growing vegetables and selling them at the local market. What started as a hobby soon led him to pursue a course of study in Horticulture at Warrentown College. After completing his studies, James traveled to Swaziland, Africa, where he implemented an Agriculture Studies curriculum for a high school, sharing his knowledge and skills. In the mid-1980s, he arrived in the United States and refined his expertise through hands-on work in the landscaping trade. In 2000, James and his wife, Kate, founded O Mara Landscaping Lawn Care, Inc., serving the Chapel Hill community with exceptional professional landscaping and lawn care services. What landscaping services do you offer? We provide complete landscaping services including design, planting, mulching, shrub trimming, seasonal cleanups, hardscape features, and ongoing landscape maintenance for residential and commercial properties. How do your weed & feed services work? Our weed feed services include applying targeted herbicides to control weeds and slow-release fertilizers to nourish your lawn, keeping it green, thick, and weed-free throughout the growing season. How often should I schedule lawn care services? Most lawns benefit from weekly or bi-weekly mowing during the growing season, with seasonal treatments like fertilization, aeration, and weed control scheduled as needed throughout the year.
