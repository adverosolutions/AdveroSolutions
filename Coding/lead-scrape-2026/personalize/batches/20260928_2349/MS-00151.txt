The Oberon Technologies Difference “Uncompromising integrity, vast experience, and a desire to help every customer succeed – that’s what drives our team.” Tim Allen President/CEO Our Mission Oberon Technologies ® is the leading provider of innovative interactive solutions, leveraging extended reality technologies coupled with state-of-the-art information delivery for environments where safety, security, or efficient cost of delivery is critical. History Founded in 2007, Oberon Technologies ® was formed to help our clients address their most challenging content needs to produce and deliver product and service information in multiple formats. Partnering with industry leading content technology providers, Oberon consultants expanded their deep knowledge with hands-on experience implementing a variety of tools across many industries. Our teams’ uncompromising integrity, tools-agnostic perspective, and vast experience has made Oberon the trusted content service provider for incredible organizations from startups to Fortune 500 companies. In 2012, recognizing a product gap in the market, Oberon invested in Titania ® Software to help drive the development of a dynamic information delivery solution that could meet client needs to provide information in any format, to any device, at any time. Titania ® Software solutions are now used to deliver critical business information by many Oberon clients. Current use cases span several industries including instructions for use for medical device companies, standard operating procedures for energy providers, service information for leading transportation manufacturers, and product help information for software organizations.
