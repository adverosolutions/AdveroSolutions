He learned every trick about black widow, ant, and rat behavior, and learned how to treat them conscientiously and carefully. Alex is an Associate Certified Entomologist and holds a pest control Operator s License (#13043). He has two fluffy cats and a beautiful dog! Alex was invited to consult nationwide with several pest control companies to educate them on how best to care for their customers pest control needs. In 2018 he founded Bear’s Pest Protection in Sacramento, and thanks to our wonderful customers, referring friends, and family we have expanded each year. Bear’s Pest Protection treatment program is designed to provide you with the best possible protection around your home, specifically with families that have pets and kids in mind. We use gentle, effective products that we are proud to use around our own homes and families. About a year after starting Bear’s, Alex partnered with his older brother Ben. His skill with analytics and dedicated work ethic helps keep our efforts focused and efficient. As our company has continued to grow we have partnered with another brother (seven kids in our family)! Nick is a commercial photographer and videographer, and you may have seen his work on commercials and billboards from Microsoft, Blender Bottle, Costa Vida, and many other national brands.
