About Thrasher Termite Pest Control, Inc. Skip to content Who we are About Us AWARD-WINNING PEST CONTROL SERVICES SINCE 1979 We are Thrasher Termite Pest Control Inc. Thrasher Termite Pest Control, Inc. (Lic. PR5009) was founded in 1979 by Duff and Janet Thrasher with the mission to provide value to its customers, and a fair, stable workplace for its employees. During its early years, the Thrashers operated the business out of their home, first in Santa Clara and later in Los Gatos. By 1996, the success and growth of the company led to the construction of the company’s current Los Gatos headquarters and warehouse. From this facility, the company’s territory has grown to encompass residential and business customers throughout Silicon Valley. In 2009, Thrasher Termite Pest Control opened a branch office in San Diego under the leadership of son, Garrett Thrasher. We are proud to be a QualityPro accredited company. This is the highest accreditation available in pest control. Pest control companies earn accreditation by meeting 16 standards that exceed state and federal requirements. We check the background of all employees and have a drug-free workplace policy. We are licensed, bonded, and insured. You can be confident in our company and our pest control team. commitment to exceptional service Education and Training We believe that knowledge is power, and when it comes to pest control, staying ahead of the curve is essential. That’s why we are proud to be active members of the California State Structural Pest Control Board which prioritizes continuous education and training within the structural pest management profession. Our team of experts is always up-to-date with the latest industry standards, techniques, and best practices. Regularly participating in educational programs and training sessions, our staff remains well-informed about the most effective and environmentally friendly pest control methods.
