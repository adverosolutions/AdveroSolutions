About Headfarmer – Phoenix’s Premier Boutique Recruiting Consulting Firm Skip to content Meet Headfarmer. Who We Are At headfarmer, we stand at the intersection of innovation and traditional values in the recruitment industry. We’re a Phoenix-based firm specializing in Finance, Accounting, and Technology consulting and recruitment. Our mission has always been simple to cultivate genuine relationships, create lasting partnerships, and help people and companies grow together. We believe in doing things the right way with honesty, collaboration, and a deep respect for the people behind every placement. That means listening first, acting with integrity, and keeping our focus on long-term success over short-term wins. At the end of the day, we’re here to make a difference in the lives we touch and the community we call home. The Headfarmer Story It all started when two former colleagues shared a cubicle at one of the largest national recruiting firms in the U.S. They had a passion for helping people and disliked the transactional nature of the recruiting industry. Their vision was to start a firm that would establish a consultative approach to the industry and bring a level of transparency that was missing. After downing a wheatgrass shot at the local Whole Foods, the dream began. Established in 2011, headfarmer has now grown to be the premier Finance, Accounting and Technology consulting recruiting firm in Phoenix. It is our calling and privilege to give back to the local community and make a difference in as many lives as we can.
