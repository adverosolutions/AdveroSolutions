Our Story | Allen Partners ltd Grounded in Purpose. Built on Trust. The Allen Partners Story. Get in touch Our Story Julie Allen Founder I started my career at Disney in leadership roles across Entertainment Operations, International Marketing, and Brand Management. It was a high-performing, inspiring environment—and I loved it. I had strong mentors, thrived in fast-paced, strategic roles, and found fulfillment celebrating wins with a talented team and contributing to Disney’s unforgettable magic. After relocating to Chicago for my husband’s career, I accepted a new role that seemed great during the interviews. But from the very first day, I could tell something wasn’t right. The values and culture didn’t align with how I had thrived at Disney. And while I was successful in the role, I knew early on it wasn’t where I was meant to be. It stayed with me—not as a failure, but as a turning point. I started asking different questions: How do these disconnects happen—and how can they be prevented? That’s what led me to executive search. I believed it was the place where I could go deeper—bringing intuition and business insight to help companies make better decisions and help leaders avoid the kind of misalignment I had just experienced. I saw how hiring, done well, could create lasting outcomes—for the companies and for the people brought in to lead with purpose. In 2001, I joined a top executive search firm. In 2003, I launched Allen Partners—a boutique firm built on a high-touch, service-driven model. From the start, I focused on building trusted relationships with CEOs, boards, and leadership teams—grounded in insight, alignment, and long-term success. But more than experience, it’s the approach that’s always mattered most to me—high-touch, deeply invested, thoughtful, and intentional. I’ve never had a transactional mindset, and that difference has shaped the outcomes we’ve delivered. Our success isn’t measured by volume—it’s measured by the long-term strength of the teams we’ve built, and the impact of the people we’ve placed. After losing my father to heart failure, I felt a deep personal connection to the Medtech industry. I wanted to be part of innovations that might have saved his life—and that purpose still drives our work today. In 2005, my husband Rich joined the firm, inspired by the same values and vision.
