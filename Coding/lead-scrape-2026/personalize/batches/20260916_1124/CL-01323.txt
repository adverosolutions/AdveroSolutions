U.S. Army Then Now Maria Boddy Planner/Scheduler My name is Maria, and my journey began when I made the decision to join the military at just 17 years old. I had not enrolled in college, and at the time, joining the military seemed like a good opportunity. What I didn’t know was that this decision would completely change the course of my life. I left home and headed to basic training, where I turned 18 and completed my training as an Automated Logistical Specialist. Then I received orders to my first duty station in Korea. Going to a different country at such a young age was intimidating. I knew no one, and everything around me was unfamiliar. But it didn’t take long before the soldiers in my unit became more than just people I worked with—they became my family. The winters in Korea were especially challenging. Training in the brutal cold tested my strength, determination, and ability to push through difficult situations. Looking back, however, those challenges became some of my most meaningful memories. The experience taught me how to adapt, persevere, and appreciate the people beside me. Eventually, I received orders to Texas. Once again, I found myself in a new place, surrounded by people I didn’t know. And once again, the military gave me a second family. Everywhere I went, I learned that the bonds formed through shared experiences, challenges, and sacrifice can last a lifetime.
