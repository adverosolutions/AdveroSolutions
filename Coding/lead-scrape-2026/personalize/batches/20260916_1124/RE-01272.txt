Who we are PeopleFirst Leadership Partners Who we are Who is PeopleFirst Leadership Partners? Proud to be located in scenic La Crosse, WI, PeopleFirst Leadership Partners provides customized leadership, human capital and coaching expertise to companies that believe their people are their most important asset. With our experienced leadership and a strong network of professional affiliates stretching from the Twin Cities to Milwaukee, PeopleFirst serves a diverse and broad breadth of businesses that range from small to large, start-up to long-established, across all industries. What we do… It’s Personal Your business and your team are unique. We take a personal approach in working with our clients and strive for long-term, trusting partnerships. We evaluate your business needs and customize a solution to address your challenges and grow your success. Human capital issues and opportunities span many functions and we are prepared to guide you through them all. Our suite of solutions includes compassionate career transition, expert custom coaching, creative leadership development, search recruitment, as well as strategic and tactical human resources expertise including organizational development, compliance, employee relations, and compensation.
