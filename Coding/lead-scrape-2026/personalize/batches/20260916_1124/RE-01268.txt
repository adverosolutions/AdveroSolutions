We execute leadership searches across the U.S and deliver an exceptional experience with our clients and candidates. Quinn Roberts Company is a client centric search firm that creates long term client relationships by offering a personalized, tailored approach to executive search. We started our firm in 2016 wanting to provide a different, more transparent approach to recruitment. We have a strong history in helping our clients determine the right candidate profile and implementing an effective recruitment process to attract the right candidates that will drive business results.We specialize in leadership placements in middle market, PE/VC backed organizations at all stages of the business. We focus on quality by utilizing a unique interview process to deliver the best potential candidates to our clients. We have a proven track record of success identifying and securing top level talent that massively impacts our client’s business. Quinn Roberts executes on a tight process, but each search or project is custom designed to fit our client’s needs. We are hyper focused on our client’s company culture. Her expertise spans across functional areas and several industries. To know how one functional area supports another, you need to know them all. She has placed leadership level to C-Suite, Advisory and Board talent. Allie has a proven track record of success in identifying and captivating passive talent. Her differentiator is how she evaluates talent specifically for her clients needs and culture. She builds strong relationships and is a true partner to her clients.
