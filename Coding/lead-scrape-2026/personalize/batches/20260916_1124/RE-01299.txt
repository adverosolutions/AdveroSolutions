About 1 Polaris Talent - Driving Transformation 0 We are Polaris Talent. We Transform Companies one hire at a time We believe every company , regardless of size or industry, deserves access to top talent and to provide a candidate experience that rivals the best-of-the-best Fortune 500 companies. The difference between a good team culture and a great team culture is shaped one hire at a time. We are experts in transforming culture by working with you to identify and select people who share your vision and values. We know job seekers deserve better than they've been getting. Our No Ghosting Policy is a reflection of that. We are not a recruiting agency . We are a consulting company that works in partnership and on behalf of your existing team to guide hidden and passive talent to you. We are certified diversity recruiters and experts at shaping culture, one hire at a time. We are a majority woman-owned Wisconsin Benefit Corporation . We believe in making a positive impact in everything we do. In 2019 Polaris Talent took the 1% Pledge to donate 1% of revenue or 1% of time annually to causes we care about. Since our founding, we have supported 501c3 Arts Organizations in Southern California, sponsored women's entrepreneurship events, facilitated fund-raising events to raise money for Music Cares and the Van Stephenson Memorial Cancer Research Fund at Vanderbilt University, and are members of the Racine Unified School District BMAC Advisory Board. In 2024 we pledged our 1% donations to ShiftUp Now which supports women race car drivers. Read the press release here .
