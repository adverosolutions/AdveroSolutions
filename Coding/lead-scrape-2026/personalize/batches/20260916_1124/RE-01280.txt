About PackStaff Focused experience in packaging engineering PackStaff is a recruiting firm focused exclusively on packaging engineering and closely related roles. The work is built around clarity, restraint, and long‑term perspective rather than volume or speed. We work with employers and packaging professionals on hiring decisions and career moves that tend to carry real consequences. That means slowing conversations down, defining roles carefully, and being realistic about tradeoffs before decisions are made. A family‑owned business, intentionally small PackStaff was founded as a family‑owned business and continues to operate independently today. The firm has remained intentionally small, allowing for direct involvement and consistency rather than scale. This structure makes it possible to stay focused on packaging specifically and to avoid generalist recruiting models that don t reflect how packaging roles actually function. Grounded in the packaging community Our perspective comes from long‑term exposure to the packaging engineering community across industries, career stages, and organizational types. This includes ongoing conversations with hiring managers, engineers at different points in their careers, and early‑career talent entering the field. That exposure informs how we think about role design, compensation expectations, workload realities, and long‑term fit. Start with a conversation Whether you re hiring, considering a career move, or just looking for a grounded perspective on the packaging talent market, we re always open to starting with a conversation.
