About Us | Meet the 24 25 Cleaners Team About 24 25 CLEANERS At 24 25 CLEANERS, we believe in people-powered excellence. Behind every spotless home and happy customer is a dedicated team showing up with energy, care, and a commitment to doing the job right. Our Story 24 25 Cleaners was established in 2021 in the Greater Seattle metro area of Washington State, founded by two business partners and lifelong friends, Murat Zhandaurov and Alexandr Godovnayuk. We started small — in Issaquah — with one clear goal: to bring families a higher standard of cleaning, delivered by people who genuinely care. In the middle of the pandemic, Murat and Alexandr worked day and night, seven days a week, building the company one home at a time. They cleaned alongside their wives, answered every call themselves, and learned every part of the business by doing it firsthand. When people needed it most, our purpose became clear: to help families protect their homes and keep the people inside them safe and healthy. As word spread, we moved our Washington office to Bellevue and kept growing across the Eastside and the Greater Seattle area. Soon after, we brought the same standards to Southern California, opening our first California office in Mission Viejo. There, Murat and his wife rolled up their sleeves once again — taking on the very first jobs themselves and growing the new location by hand, exactly as they had in Washington. Every part of 24 25 Cleaners was built with Murat s and Alexandr s own hands — from scrubbing floors to sales, marketing, and operations. They climbed every ladder and learned every step along the way, and that s why, to this day, we know exactly what a truly clean home should look and feel like. Today, as a fully family-owned and operated company, our mission is bigger than spotless homes. We want to make our communities happier and give people their time back — because the hours you save on cleaning are hours you can spend with family and friends, doing the things that bring you joy. That, more than anything, is why we do this work. Thank you for letting us be part of your home.
