About Emerald City Cleaning | Woman-Owned Since 2003 Call Free Quote About Emerald City Cleaning · Family-Owned · Serving Seattle Since 2003 Two decades of showing up for Seattle. Family-owned, Washington WMBE-certified, W-2 employees, no subcontractors. Run by Seattle natives who answer the phone, show up on time, and treat every property like it's their own. Owner Principal CEO Meet Guacolda Zinsmeyer. "I started this company because cleaning isn't a transaction. It's a relationship. We learn your space, your schedule, your standards — and we earn the right to come back next week." Guacolda's path into cleaning started in 2003, prepping brand-new custom homes for builder John Baccham across Bainbridge Island and Seattle — the detailed, white-glove work of getting a home spotless before its owners ever walked in. From there she built a trusted residential cleaning business, and in 2013 she founded Emerald City Cleaning to bring that same standard — W-2 employees, real training, showing up on time — to businesses and premier homes across the city. Twenty-plus years in, the bet's paid off. She still runs the business day-to-day. Every estimate, every escalation, every late-night question from a worker — they all reach her. The company is Washington WMBE certified (Women Minority Business Enterprise) — a real credential, audited annually by the state, that opens us up to public-sector and tier-one corporate work other firms can't bid on.
