Universal Hands - About Us Search this site Embedded Files Skip to main content Skip to navigation Our History Universal Hands, LLC is a multi-service company that has provided quality services in Southeast GA and its surrounding areas. Founded in 2004 by Jason and Florence Gray, Universal Hands provided services for the working professional that alleviated the stress of daily life. These Services included Residential Cleaning, Commercial Cleaning, Lawn Care Services, Mobile Massage Therapy, and Consulting Services such as Advertising Consulting, Business Consulting, Computer Consulting, Editorial Services, Insurance Consulting, Marketing Consulting, Payroll Management Services, Resume Writing Services and Tax Consulting. Due to the economic hardships spreading across the country, in 2013 Universal Hands decided to transition its vision and started providing work from home opportunities to independent contractors. In 2015, Universal Hands expanded into the Call Center industry. Universal Hands now services many Fortune 500 clients through Customer service, Sales, and Technical support. Currently, Universal Hands provides job opportunities to 1099 independent contractors. There are work opportunities for Homemakers, Veterans, Students, Retirees and Entrepreneurial-minded individuals. In 2020, due to the economic hardships caused by Covid-19, Universal Hands is doing its part for individuals and small business owners who have lost their property in tax foreclosure.
