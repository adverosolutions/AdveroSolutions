About Us | Corporate Care Building Services About CCBS Premium care for commercial spaces A local team with Southeast reach Corporate Care Building Services was founded in Georgia on a simple belief: commercial spaces deserve the same level of care as the people who work inside them. From our Atlanta home base, we now support clients throughout the entire Southeast — scaling our crews and schedules to match properties of any size. Our team delivers nightly janitorial programs, parking lot sweeping, outside porter services, and day porter staffing, along with specialty services including window cleaning, pressure washing, carpet cleaning, and floor care — each a full, professionally managed program in its own right. For over 26 years, we ve built long-term customer relationships the same way: by delivering dependable service and earning our clients trust every month. Our standard agreements are month-to-month with a simple 30-day notice, because we d rather keep your business through performance and communication than through a restrictive long-term contract.
