Our Story B the Keeper 0 Our story starts with bees and ends with A BETTER PLANET B the Keeper was born out of an intense desire to bring people and pollinators closer together by creating more beautiful neighborhoods, sequestering carbon and promoting biodiversity. MEET THE FOUNDER — Brandon Reynolds Brandon's obsession with pollinators began after leaving the advertising industry in 2017 to pursue apprenticeships in beekeeping and landscaping. While managing beehives had its perks, he was outclassed by native perennial plants and their ability to attract and sustain regional biodiversity. Partnering with native plants, he founded B the Keeper to help others create safe spaces for pollinators. PRESS LET’S DO THIS! We’re happy you made it this far.
