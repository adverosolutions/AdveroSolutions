We will protect the living investments on every property as we invest our time and expertise with our clients. This is what our clients have come to expect and we will always uphold these principles. Experts in the Field As a company, the Arbor Advisor places great importance on the continuing education required to hold and maintain Master Arborist Board Certification. This ISA Certification is one aspect that sets us apart from many other tree service companies. Continuing education is a never-ending process and it is what enables us to remain on the cutting edge employing the most recent developments in tree care and protection. We stay up to date with new diseases, changing insect populations, and most importantly developing treatments that can help the trees in our area. Teaching people about plants is a passion for me as well. When people learn about their trees they gain a new perspective for care, and that can be a tree s best chance at a long and healthy life. Our Founder Drew Stone is an ISA Board Certified Master Arborist, and founder of The Arbor Advisor. I ve been in the industry over 14 years, doing everything from specialized pruning to large removals, and from diagnostics to treatment. Instead of removing and replanting, I look for ways to save trees because I understand their value. I give trees the nutrients they need, remove the pests and problems that threaten them, and monitor them for signs of stress.
