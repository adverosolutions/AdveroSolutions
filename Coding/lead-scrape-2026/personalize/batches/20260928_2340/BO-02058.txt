We believe that our unique structure and focus, as summarized below, allows us to provide our clients with a superior product and experience to that of a conventional CPA firm. Focus - We maintain an exclusive focus on transactional tax work and are free from the competing tax compliance-related deadlines and priorities associated with traditional CPA firms. This approach allows us to dedicate our complete attention to our clients transactions and stay current on the latest M A tax planning ideas and strategies. Experience - Our clients enjoy a partnership with experienced, former Big Four deal professionals that take a hands-on approach and perform all of the work on our engagements. We provide direct access to partner-level resources and do not delegate our engagements to junior staff. Flexibility - We understand that each transaction is unique and work with our clients to collaboratively design a scope of work, timeline and deliverable that meets their specific needs. Service - First class service, with a personal touch. We understand how important a transaction is to our clients business and ensure we provide the level of attention and responsiveness that they deserve. Value - Big firm experience without the big firm rates. Because of our lean and efficient structure, we provide competitive pricing and excellent value to our clients.
