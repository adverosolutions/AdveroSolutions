And that relationship is the best policy. - Bob and Rick Gallery Agency Profile Established in 1993 by Richard and Robert Gallery, our company has grown to a full-service insurance agency, with offices in the communities of Quincy and Canton. Currently, Atlantic Insurance Group serves the insurance needs of about 2,500 individuals and families, and the insurance needs of 700 businesses in Norfolk County, South Shore, and surrounding areas in Massachusetts. As an independent insurance agency, we do not work for an insurance company, we work for you. We work on your side when you have a loss and follow through to see that you get fair, prompt payment. We represent fine insurance companies and place your business with the company offering the best coverage at a competitive price. Our goal is to maximize your coverage while minimizing your cost.
