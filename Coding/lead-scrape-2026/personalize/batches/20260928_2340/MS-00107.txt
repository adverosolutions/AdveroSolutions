--> About | DMC Unlimited Computers top of page ABOUT US Company History In 1989 David Cherba founded DMC Unlimited as a subsidiary of Digital Machine Control, Inc. The business was located in Ludington s Industrial Park on Conrad Industrial Drive between 1st Street 6th Street. Primarily focused on providing computer services to local businesses, DMC Unlimited grew quickly. By 1996 DMC Unlimited was Digital Machine Control s main focus. ​ In 1999 DMC Unlimited was sold to Kim Larson who maintained the company s focus of providing computer products and services to the local community. In late 2002, DMC Unlimited moved to a new location at 65 S. Jebavy Dr. This location provided a much more accessible store front for DMC s customers. ​ In March of 2003 DMC Unlimited was purchased by Roger Jamie Wertz. Roger had been an employee of DMC Unlimited for three years under the ownership of Kim Larson. The Wertz s greatly enjoy working in the field of computer service and look forward to serving the community for years to come. In September of 2008, DMC Unlimited purchased the Russ Miller Studio building on Tinkham avenue. This new facility provides DMC with nearly twice the square footage of the previous building. ​ In 2015, DMC Unlimited added a gaming PC brand, GameRigz , to meet increased demand for high-performance PCs and gaming accessories. ​ In June of 2024, DMC transitioned out of the residential services space to focus on commercial IT consulting and network administration. Today DMC Unlimited continues to provide much needed computer products and IT services to the local community. Supplying IT services and support to hundreds of local businesses, DMC Unlimited is here to help you and your small business succeed.
