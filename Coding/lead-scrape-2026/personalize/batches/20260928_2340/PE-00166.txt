About Plant Health Solutions About Plant Health Solutions is located in Central Bucks County, Pennsylvania and specializes in the prevention, treatment and cure of diseases and pests in trees and shrubs. Our founder is proud to offer over 30 years of experience in the plant health field, including designations as an ISA Certified Arborist and as a Pennsylvania State Certified Commercial Pesticide and Herbicide Applicator. We are also members of the American Horticultural Society. Our focus is to provide a customized, integrated plant health care system that is both environmentally responsible and promotes healthy and strong plants. We utilize the integrated pest management (IPM) approach when treating our clients' plant materials. This allows for a safer means of controlling pests and disease. Founder Message The roots of this company began with 30 years of experience in the landscape design and installation field. Many of the elements considered in that profession cross over to plant health including proper plant location, soil conditions, drainage problems and mulching issues. The consideration of these factors leads to better diagnosis and treatment for our clientele.
