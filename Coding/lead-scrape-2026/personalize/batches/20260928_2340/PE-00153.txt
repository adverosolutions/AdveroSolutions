About Us | Tomlinson Bomberger | Landscaping, Lawncare, Pest Control in Lancaster Harrisburg Our Story Generations of excellence in landscape and lawn care Who We Are At Tomlinson Bomberger, we believe your outdoor space should be beautiful, functional, and enjoyable all year long. Since 1981, we've helped Central Pennsylvania homeowners and businesses create and care for landscapes that reflect pride, comfort, and connection. Founded by Clark Tomlinson and Dave Bomberger, who met while working at the Lancaster Country Club, our company began with a simple philosophy: Honesty Trust Total Client Satisfaction From our two locations in Lancaster and Harrisburg, we serve thousands of residential and commercial properties across seven counties. Our growth reflects not expansion for its own sake, but the trust earned through consistent excellence, deep regional knowledge, and genuine care for the properties and people we serve. Our Mission Beautiful Landscapes. Amazing Experiences. Wonderful People. To enhance the beauty, health, and value of every property we touch through expert care, proven practices, and unwavering commitment to client satisfaction. Today We proudly serve clients throughout Central and Eastern Pennsylvania, including Berks County and surrounding communities.
