Why We Are Different Who We Are Built upon a strong foundation in Federal Consulting, Saraé Global is a woman-owned business established in 2018. Leveraging on our expertise and success in securing top talent for sensitive government programs, we now offer Direct Hire, Executive Search and Recruiting Services for both Public and Commercial Sectors. Our expansion strengthens our existing capabilities by building on our experience and professional network gained through 20 years as a trusted Talent Acquisition Partner for Top Tech companies. Our Team Why We’re Different When we work with a Client, we become part of your team. Whether it’s delivering results on a hard to fill role or taking on complex projects in National Security, we’ve been in your shoes. Your success is our success, and, because we’ve been where you are, we are able to understand first hand your biggest challenges and deliver results. With us, you gain an unwavering partner with instant credibility and access to over 20 years of established relationships and strategic insight in the technology and federal industries. Saraé Global is named after an incredible Afghan-American business woman and friend ‘Sarae’ who persevered over tyranny and suppression in her home country. No matter the extremism she endured, her ability to lead and remain accountable to her people and family for decades is the inspiration and values that drive us. She was unfortunately lost in a terrorist attack in 2018 in Kabul but we continue to honor her unbelievable courage through these core values. Perseverance We rise to the challenge and don’t back down. We stand by our clients and keep pushing until we reach our goals—and yours. Accountability We follow through on our commitments to our clients and our candidates from day one through every step of the process. Humility To us, humility means being true to you, confident in your strengths, and accepting your flaws, while always working to improve.
