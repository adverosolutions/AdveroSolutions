About | Join Our Vision People Architects 0 Welcome to People Architects HR Support That Grows With You As your business grows, so do HR needs. More questions, more compliance, and more complexity. That’s where we come in. People Architects provides fractional HR support and recruiting for small and mid-sized businesses that need expert help without hiring full-time. We handle the day-to-day and help plan for the future, so you can focus on growing your business. Founded by Meg Mayhugh, we’re a proudly women-founded, woman owned and women-led team with over 300 years of combined experience. People Architects is a certified Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB) and Women's Business Enterprise (WBE). From employee issues to strategic planning, we make HR simple, smart, and scalable. Let’s take HR and recruiting off your plate. Affordably and effectively. People Architects is EOS®-Aligned We proudly run on the Entrepreneurial Operating System® (EOS®) to better serve every day, and to encourage focus, discipline, and accountability in our team. EOS is a set of tools and principles that helps organizations stay aligned toward a common vision. It's a people-oriented operating system. Like the creators of the EOS Model and the 100,000+ companies who have implemented it, People Architects believes it's possible to strengthen your business by strengthening your team and surrounding yourself with good people from top to bottom.
