HR for Small Businesses - Who We Are Skip to main content Hit enter to search or ESC to close Close Search Who We Are Our Story Teresa Murphey founded Hire Ventures® in 2001 to give early-stage tech companies access to experienced HR and recruiting at the moment they need it most, in a way that is both practical and affordable so they can focus on building and growing their businesses. What began with working with tech startups has grown alongside our clients, and today we work with small and mid-sized organizations through every stage of growth, from making their first leadership hire to multi-state expansion, leadership transitions, and successful exits. As our clients’ needs have evolved, so has our approach. Through our flexible, fractional offerings, including Fractional HR and Fractional Recruiting , we provide immediate access to senior-level HR and recruiting expertise without the need to hire a full in-house team. We focus on simplifying HR, strengthening leadership, and leveraging the right technology to build scalable people solutions and healthy, high-performing cultures.
