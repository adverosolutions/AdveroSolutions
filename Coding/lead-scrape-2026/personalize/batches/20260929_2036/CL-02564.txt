Owner / Operator Mackenzie Marie Mackenzie s education in Psychology, Behavioral Neuroscience and Behavioral Economics built the foundation for her career in Experiential Marketing. For more than 10 years, she worked to establish emerging Natural + Specialty food and beverage brands in the Portland Metro area. She brings the learnings of her experience with her to Dust Avengers, as well as her own experience as a neurodivergent (ADHD + ASD1) hoarder in recovery. Her passion for this transformative process, and a love for both cleaning and organizing, set her apart from others as she has walked in the same shoes as her clients.
