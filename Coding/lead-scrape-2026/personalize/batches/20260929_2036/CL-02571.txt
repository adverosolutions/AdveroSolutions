--> About Us — Family-Owned Cleaning Company in Kensington MD | Bluebird Cleaning About Bluebird Cleaning We Work Where We Live We're a family owned business based in Kensington, MD. We strive to offer our clientele the best quality, detail-oriented, and transparent services — without the hassle. We want to bring an approachable and friendly feeling back to the service industry, and with us you will never find any fine print, hidden fees, or lengthy contracts. Bluebird was established in 2018 after receiving high demand for interior cleaning services through another of our Montgomery County based businesses. We found many of our clients were dissatisfied with their current home cleaning providers and were desperately seeking alternative options. We saw a growing need and wanted to be their new and improved option. Meet Xiomara Brett Xiomara and Brett McCurdy have been residents of the area for many years, and combined have over 20 years of small business experience in the service and hospitality industries alone. Xiomara currently handles most of the operational aspects of Bluebird Cleaning — making sure to offer the best quality service with a smile, keep the most efficient crews, and continue to build long lasting relationships with all our clients. Brett supports operations in sales and marketing mainly, but helps with all the other tasks it takes to manage a growing small business. They both hope to bring about change in the cleaning industry with unmatched client experiences, create new jobs for locals in the area whose jobs may have been recently eliminated due to automation or outsourcing, and contribute back to the community through local sponsorships, donations and events. Bluebird Cleaning's partner company, BAM'S Landscaping , works side-by-side with us on the larger cleanout jobs and is also owned and managed by Brett and Xiomara. Brett is VP of Realty Investments Corp. in Kensington, an actively licensed real estate investment agent, and Treasurer of the KBDA (Kensington Business District Association). Xiomara works in MCPS as a translator and has a passion for interior design and flip renovations. Where Did the Name Bluebird Cleaning Come From?
