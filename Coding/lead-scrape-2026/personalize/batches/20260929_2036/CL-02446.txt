Busy Brooms Commercial Cleaning, headquartered in Boca Raton, FL, was founded in 2005 by Sandra and JP Maritz. The company provides services throughout Palm Beach and Broward counties. Busy Brooms is a family owned and operated company. We are not a franchise, so we do not buy contracts nor do we sell or sub-contract to other vendors. All of our personnel are employees who go through an extensive hiring and screening process, insuring that we can consistently provide trustworthy service. Busy Brooms' main goal is to provide our clients with custom cleaning programs that fit their needs and budget with unsurpassed quality and excellence in service.
