About ACS | Veteran-Owned Commercial Cleaning | Eastern PA --> --> Skip to main content About Us Every Clean Tells a Story, and We Write Excellence Founded in 2001, Advanced Cleaning Solutions (ACS) has been at the forefront of delivering exceptional commercial cleaning services to businesses, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and warehouses across Eastern Pennsylvania, with a particular focus on the Lehigh Valley and Reading. At the heart of our operation is Nick Ward, a U.S. Army veteran whose dedication to precision, reliability, and unmatched attention to detail is evident in every project we undertake. Our journey began with a simple but powerful vision: to provide a cleaner, safer, and more productive environment for businesses and institutions of all sizes. Leveraging Nick’s military training, ACS has developed a comprehensive suite of cleaning services that meet the diverse needs of our clients, from routine office cleaning to specialized institutional sanitization and warehouse maintenance. Nick Ward is the owner and operator of Advanced Cleaning Solutions. He was born in Philadelphia and currently resides in the Lehigh Valley with his wife and two children. Nick is a proud US ARMY veteran, having served 3 combat tours in Iraq in support of the Global War on Terrorism. Using his background in the military in everyday business operations, Nick is dedicated to excellence and providing the highest standard of service. "As with any company, everyone wants to be a leader in their industry. Our mission is very simple, be productive, listen to our customers and employees, and provide the best service possible to each.
