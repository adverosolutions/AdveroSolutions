Introducing Wave1: On-Demand People Tech | Hawkwood Back to insights 29 Mar 2026 Launching Wave1; an On-Demand People Tech Team for Scale-ups Hawkwood has co-founded Wave1; an on-demand People Tech team to help scale-ups prepare for rapid scale across Europe and the USA Share this post Hawkwood has partnered with Andy Turnbull , former Head of People Services at Bolt , to launch Wave1 as a joint venture. Wave1 is an on-demand People Tech team that helps People functions scale with confidence, implementing and optimising best-in-class HR applications, automation tools, and AI-driven workflows, all underpinned by clean, reliable HR data. Andy Turnbull co-founded Wave1 following six years at Bolt; the European mobility and delivery platform, where he joined when the company had approximately 1,000 employees and was part of the People Leadership Team that scaled it to over 4,000. Prior to Bolt, Andy spent several years in HR Transformation, delivering Workday implementations for enterprise clients. The partnership with Andy and the launch of Wave1 is a natural extension of Hawkwood’s work in recruiting People Talent hires into Technology companies, as we seek to provide other high-value solutions to our existing clients. The Problem Wave1 Solves Most People functions at scaling technology companies are running on a fragmented HR technology stack. Tools have been adopted at different stages of growth, some are integrated with one another well and others less so, systems are often procured in silos with little long-term strategy. The result is a patchwork of systems that creates friction for HR teams and manual processes for people ops and employees. Employees frequently find themselves logging into multiple different HR platforms to complete basic tasks checking payslips, requesting leave, accessing policies when ideally they would do all of it within a tool they already use every day, such as Slack. The technology exists to deliver that experience, but many companies have simply not had the internal capacity or expertise to build it. These problems compound as companies grow. Organisations regularly outgrow their HR technology stack at precisely the moment when they are least able to manage a migration: when the business is scaling rapidly, headcount is increasing, and internal teams are already stretched.
