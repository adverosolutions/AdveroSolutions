Our Recruiting Method Introductory Call Firstly, we need to get to know what makes your business unique so we can design the best strategies to assist you with your hiring needs. Many businesses struggle to find the right talent to hire. We want to make sure there’s no mystery; we believe recruiting is much more of a science than a game of hide and seek. Good compensation, benefits, and engaging work, alongside a recruiter who is educated on your business, is the formula to success. Research Sourcing We pride ourselves in finding top talent for our clients. Our expectation is to provide you with three qualified candidates within the first week of beginning our search. If we aren’t able to provide you with candidates, we’ll give you a full report on the status of your search. We not only want to fill your open positions but take a truly consultative approach by providing insight on market trends. If there’s a reason you may have struggled with filling or retaining the role in the past, we want to be the ones to let you know why. Interviews Offers We believe that first impressions are important! We are happy to advise on the best practices and trends for the hiring processes. We can manage the process including scheduling interview, assigning assessments, presenting verbal offers, and offer negotiation or work closely with your internal recruiting team. Your hiring process is your first chance to impress potential new employees. So, from the first correspondence to new hire 1st day details everything should go as smoothly as possible. We’ll always ensure we are on the same page, so candidates feel supported during the process.
