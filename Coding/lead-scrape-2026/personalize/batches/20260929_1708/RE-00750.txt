Frank Gump bio Frank Gump Founder | Executive Search Leader | Career Strategist for $200K+ Talent Frank Gump is an executive search veteran, career strategist, and trusted advisor to senior corporate leaders navigating consequential career moves. Since entering executive recruiting in 1995, Frank has spent more than three decades helping accomplished professionals position themselves, access the right people, and compete for opportunities at the highest levels of business. In 2002, he founded The Frank Gump Agency to deliver a more personal, strategic, and results-driven alternative to conventional career coaching and traditional recruiting. Frank works with directors, vice presidents, presidents, C-suite executives, and other senior leaders across Fortune 500 companies, middle-market organizations, private equity-backed businesses, and high-growth enterprises. His clients turn to him during pivotal moments: an unexpected transition, a stalled search, a move into executive leadership, an industry pivot, or the pursuit of a role that cannot be reached through ordinary job-search methods. His approach combines the disciplines of executive search, career consulting, personal branding, market intelligence, and strategic outreach. Rather than relying primarily on job postings and automated application systems, Frank helps clients define their executive value, identify the organizations where that value will matter most, and take their message directly to the people capable of creating or approving an opportunity. That direct-to-decision-maker philosophy is central to The Frank Gump Agency . Frank develops targeted campaigns designed to reach CEOs, presidents, business-unit leaders, board members, investors, executive recruiters, and other influential contacts. By moving beyond the crowded job-board market, he helps clients enter higher-value conversations, uncover opportunities before they become widely visible, and compete on the strength of their leadership impact—not simply the keywords on a résumé. Known for his candor, intuition, and ability to uncover the real story behind an executive’s career, Frank does more than prepare career materials. He identifies the patterns, achievements, and differentiators that make a leader valuable, then turns them into a compelling market position and a disciplined search strategy.
