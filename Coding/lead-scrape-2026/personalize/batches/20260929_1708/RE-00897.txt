Here, the recruiter you meet is the recruiter who runs your search. 01 Discovery A real conversation about the business: the pain points, the team, the good, the bad and the ugly. Honest coaching up front, so you do not spend six weeks interviewing people who are not close. 03 Targeted search Our network plus direct, creative outreach to people who are employed and not looking. Nobody on the shortlist came from a job board. 04 The shortlist Two or three candidates who check most of the boxes, technically and culturally.
