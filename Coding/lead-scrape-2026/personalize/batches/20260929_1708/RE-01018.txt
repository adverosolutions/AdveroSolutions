The Team Behind Growth Mode | Boutique Tech Recruiting Firm Growth Mode Talent Partners 0 Meet the Team After years leading recruiting inside fast-growing tech companies, founder Sarah Greenwell noticed the same pattern repeating itself: startups kept hiring from the same small networks, and leadership teams rarely reflected the customers and employees they served. In 2019, Sarah founded Her Move to help change that. Today, as Growth Mode Talent Partners, that mission has evolved into a broader commitment to diverse, high-impact hiring. We know from experience that leaders who bring perspectives not already in the room build stronger products, stronger teams, and stronger companies. Over time, Sarah built a team that shares that same philosophy: thoughtful, human-centered hiring rooted in real operational experience. We’ve all spent years on the hiring side of the table inside fast-growing tech companies. We’ve built teams, led recruiting, and felt the pressure of every open seat ourselves. That experience shapes how we advise hiring managers, how we evaluate candidates, and why we’ve never made a mediocre hire just to close a search. Sarah Greenwell, MA, CDR Founder + Executive Talent Partner Get to know Sarah FOUNDER + EXECUTIVE TALENT PARTNER Sarah has spent two decades in talent acquisition, including 14 years leading recruiting strategy inside tech companies like Bullhorn and Redbox, and iconic brands like Saks Fifth Avenue and The Hartford. In 2019 she founded Her Move, which evolved into Growth Mode Talent Partners in 2023. Both firms were built around a commitment to stronger, more diverse leadership teams. Today, Sarah leads Growth Mode’s executive searches personally, advising clients on role scope, market alignment, and candidate motivation.
