Josh Cahan, CPA Josh Cahan, CPA, has been practicing as a small business advisor since 1995. After graduating from UNC-Charlotte with a B.S. in Accounting, he started working for a local CPA, Steve Walker, CPA. There he prepared business and individual tax returns and handled accounting work for small businesses. It was here he developed a knack for working with small businesses. After a couple of years, he moved on to work for KPMG. There he spent three years developing and implementing tax strategies for fortune 500 companies. Although the work was enjoyable, he was drawn to small businesses and decided to use the expertise he gained at the big six firm to go back to the small business arena. And after a short time with Scharf and Pera, he opened his own shop. Josh Cahan s firm focuses on businesses in the manufacturing, franchise, retail, and health fitness industries.
