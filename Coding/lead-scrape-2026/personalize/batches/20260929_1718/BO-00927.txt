About DAT Finance | Tyler Davis, CPA | Sidney, Ohio CPA Practice About DAT A CPA partnership that leads alongside you. DAT Finance was built on a simple idea: the financial leadership that separates a good year from a great one shouldn t be reserved for businesses big enough to hire a full executive bench. The story Built for the businesses doing the building. I m Tyler Davis, the CPA behind DAT Finance. I started this practice in 2022 to be the kind of finance partner I kept watching owners go without, the one who actually sits in the chair with you when the hard calls show up. Today I work with operators across Ohio and nationwide on full-service accounting, fractional controller and CFO engagements, and the tax strategy that ties it together. Why I built it I kept seeing the same gap. Owners with real momentum and real complexity, but no path to senior finance leadership that didn t involve stitching together three vendors and hoping it held. A bookkeeper here, a tax preparer there, an outside advisor who showed up twice a year, and the owner left to be the integration layer between all of them. So I built the practice I wished existed. One partner across the accounting, the leadership, and the tax work. Senior judgment in every conversation. A relationship designed to compound year over year, not a one-off engagement designed to bill. Before this, I spent years inside operational financial leadership, as a finance director and as a board treasurer, doing the operational work most CPAs only see from the outside. That experience shapes how I think about every business I sit with, mission-driven or otherwise. How I work The person you talk to on the discovery call is the person who does the work and stays accountable for it. You don t get handed off to junior staff, and you don t get a diluted version of the relationship that brought you in. You get a financial expert who sees the whole picture, your books, your margins, your cash flow, your tax position, and treats all of it as connected, because it is. I work like an operator because I sit next to operators every day. I care how your business runs, not just how it reports. When you re weighing a hire, a price change, or a move into a new market, I run the numbers and tell you straight whether it works, the same way I d want to know if it were my own company on the line.
