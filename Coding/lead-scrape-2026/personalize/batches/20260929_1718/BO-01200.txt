We handle the accounting so our clients can focus on running and growing their businesses with greater confidence. I earned a Bachelor of Science in Accounting from Florida State University and a Master of Science in Accounting from the University of Virginia. I became a Florida CPA in 1998 and began my career in audit, working with Deloitte Touche and later with Law, Redd, Crona Munroe. After leaving public accounting, I served in several finance director roles and discovered my passion for helping organizations create order, clarity, and confidence in their financial operations. As I raised my three children, I saw an opportunity to build something different: an accounting firm that would provide exceptional service to clients while creating meaningful, flexible careers for talented professionals. That vision became Lillyfield Accounting Solutions in 2016. From the beginning, Lillyfield was designed as a remote firm built on trust, accountability, and strong relationships. Today, I am proud to lead a team of creative problem-solvers who care deeply about accounting, continuous improvement, and the success of our clients. We value flexibility, collaboration, professional growth, and healthy, balanced lives. Many of our clients come to us feeling overwhelmed. Their financial records may be behind, their systems may no longer support the business, or managing the accounting themselves may be taking too much time and energy. Our team meets them without judgment, determines what needs attention first, and creates a clear path forward with better systems, stronger processes, and dependable support.
