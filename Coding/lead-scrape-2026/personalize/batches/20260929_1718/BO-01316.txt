While Alaruna works with businesses across various industries, it specializes in small-to-midsize companies with innovative and creative leadership. Two accomplished professionals comprise Alaruna – founder Mary Hackenberg, a former Certified Public Accountant with 30 years of accounting experience , and John Burns, a former IT and Financial Services Systems Specialist.
