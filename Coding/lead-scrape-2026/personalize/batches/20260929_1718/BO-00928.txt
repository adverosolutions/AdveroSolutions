About Us Bashore Reineck Stoller Waterman Inc Skip to content Bashore Reineck Stoller Waterman Inc Our firm traces its roots back to 1955 when William F. Sinn, CPA opened an office in Paulding, Ohio. Bill Laukhuf joined the firm in 1961, opened our Van Wert location in 1968, and the firm became Sinn, Laukhuf and Co in 1971. In 1985, the firm became Arend, Laukhuf, and Stoller, Inc. transitioning to Bashore Reineck Stoller Waterman Inc in 2014. A third office, in Columbus Grove, Ohio, was opened in late 2016. The current owners in the firm are Matt Reineck, Jon Stoller, Andrew Bashore, Todd Meyer, and Andy Manz.
