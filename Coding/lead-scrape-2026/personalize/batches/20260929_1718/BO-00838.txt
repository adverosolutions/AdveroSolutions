Founder President About Vincent Servello, CPA Vincent Servello is the Founder and President of Servello CPA — he has been working in the accounting field for over 18 years. Before earning his Certified Public Accountant (CPA) license, Vincent earned a Master s degree in Taxation from Fordham University s Graduate School of Business Administration in 2011, and a Bachelor of Science in Public Accounting from Fordham in 2010. Vincent specializes in individual and business taxation, providing accounting services to organizations ranging from mom-and-pop small businesses to multi-billion dollar partnerships.
