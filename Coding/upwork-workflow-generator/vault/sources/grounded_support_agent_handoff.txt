Scope for this build:

An AI agent that answers questions grounded strictly in our knowledge base (product info, FAQs, policies), so it only ever tells a customer something that's actually true for us. Every conversation captured and logged to our CRM so nothing is lost and our team can pick up where the bot left off. A knowledge base we can update ourselves without touching the workflow.
