Phase 1 categories that get a draft:

2. Clinic locations and opening times — identify which clinic/area, draft with approved address, opening days/times, booking info, booking link. If location can't be identified confidently, flag for human review.
3. Health insurance — identify insurance/reimbursement/coverage questions, draft approved wording explaining reimbursement depends on the customer's policy and cannot be guaranteed.

4. Other/uncertain — must NOT get a detailed automated draft: specific blood test requests, lists of tests, pricing/quotation requests, doctor's requisition forms, emails with medical attachments, blood result questions, clinical/medical advice requests, complaints, anything that can't be classified confidently.
