Cold Email & Lead Generation Specialist – Instantly.ai

Company: Money Merch 
Industry: Custom Apparel / Screen Printing / Embroidery
Type: Freelance / Full-Time
Location: Remote

About Us

Money Merch is a custom apparel manufacturing company specializing in screen printing, embroidery, and branded merchandise. We work with businesses and organizations that need high-quality custom apparel, including company uniforms, employee apparel, branded workwear, promotional merchandise, and ongoing apparel programs.

We are looking for an experienced B2B Cold Email & Lead Generation Specialist to help us build and manage our outbound sales campaigns using Instantly.ai.

What You'll Be Doing

Your primary responsibility will be identifying businesses that fit our target customer profile, building accurate prospect lists, and managing cold email campaigns through Instantly.ai.

We will provide guidance on the types of companies and industries we want to target. You will then be responsible for researching those markets and turning them into qualified prospect lists.

Responsibilities will include:

Research businesses that match our target customer profiles
Build targeted B2B prospect lists based on industries and criteria we provide
Identify appropriate decision-makers within each company
Find and verify business email addresses
Organize and clean prospect data before campaigns are launched
Import and manage leads inside Instantly.ai
Create and manage cold email campaigns
Manage campaign sequences and follow-ups
Monitor email deliverability, bounce rates, reply rates, and campaign performance
A/B test subject lines, messaging, offers, and sequences
Remove bad data and maintain clean prospect lists
Identify positive responses and hand qualified opportunities over to our internal sales team
Provide regular reporting on outreach volume, replies, interested leads, and campaign performance

Target Customers

Our primary focus will be businesses that purchase company uniforms, employee apparel, branded workwear, and custom merchandise.

Examples may include:

Construction companies
HVAC companies
Plumbing companies
Electrical contractors
Landscaping companies
Automotive businesses
Property management companies
Restaurants and hospitality groups
Gyms and fitness companies
Warehouses and logistics companies
Home service businesses
Multi-location businesses
Companies with large employee teams

We will provide the industries and types of companies we want to target. You will be expected to research and build high-quality lists around those requirements.

We are not looking for someone who simply sends thousands of emails.

We want someone who can build high-quality, targeted campaigns that generate conversations with businesses that actually purchase uniforms and branded apparel.

Quality of prospect lists
Valid email / bounce rate
Positive reply rate
Qualified leads generated
Meetings or quote opportunities generated
Overall campaign improvement over time
