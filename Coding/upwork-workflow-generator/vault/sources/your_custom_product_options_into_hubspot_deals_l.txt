Project Overview:

We are seeking an experienced HubSpot and Shopify integration specialist to build a bi-directional data sync and marketing automation ecosystem. We use advanced product options in Shopify (line-item properties, metafields, and custom product configurations), which means the native HubSpot-Shopify integration app will not suffice for our needs.

We need a developer/specialist who can capture custom product properties from Shopify and pass them seamlessly into HubSpot Deals, Line Items, and Contact records for personalized email workflows, abandoned checkout tracking, and custom reporting.

Key Responsibilities & Deliverables
Custom Integration & Data Architecture

Design and implement a sync solution (using Shopify Flow, Webhooks, Make/Zapier, or a custom HubSpot Private App) to bypass native integration limits.

Map Shopify custom product options, line-item properties, and metafields to custom properties on HubSpot Deals/Line Items.

Ensure clean data handling to prevent duplicate contact creation or corrupted deal pipelines.

Automated HubSpot Workflows & Personalization

Build post-purchase, upsell, and re-engagement email sequences that dynamically pull custom product option choices into email tokens.

Set up custom Abandoned Checkout / Cart sequences that reflect specific line-item options selected prior to checkout.

Email Template Setup

Configure responsive, branded drag-and-drop HTML templates with dynamic personalization tokens (product previews, custom selections, order summary details).

Analytics & Custom Reporting

Build custom dashboards in HubSpot tracking revenue, Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), workflow conversion rates, and sales performance by product option variations.

Required Experience & Qualifications
Proven experience integrating Shopify with HubSpot for brands using advanced product options / line-item properties.

Deep familiarity with Shopify Metafields, Liquid/API payloads, and HubSpot Custom Objects / Line Item APIs.

Hands-on expertise with middleware (Make, Zapier) or serverless webhook/API implementations.

HubSpot Certified (Inbound Marketing / Workflows / Reporting preferred).

Demonstrated ability to test edge cases before deploying to a live production environment.
