Job Description

We are seeking an experienced AI automation specialist or agency to help us transform a personal injury law firm into a highly efficient, AI-powered operation.

Our goal is to automate the firm's core administrative and operational workflows, from the moment a potential client contacts our office through medical records collection, case management, settlement brochure preparation, and case resolution.

We are looking for someone who can design, develop, integrate, and implement a comprehensive AI automation system that reduces manual work, improves client communication, accelerates case processing, and allows our attorneys and legal staff to focus on higher-value work.

This is not a project for a basic chatbot or simple workflow automation. We are looking for an expert who can build an integrated AI-powered operational system specifically designed for a personal injury law firm.

All client-facing AI systems must support three languages: English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole, including voice conversations, client intake, and automated communications.

Scope of Work

1. AI Receptionist & Front Desk

We want to implement an AI-powered virtual receptionist capable of managing incoming calls, answering client inquiries, and handling routine front desk operations.

Key functionalities include:

Answer incoming calls 24/7 in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole.
Automatically detect a caller's preferred language or allow callers to select their language.
Handle multiple incoming calls simultaneously.
Answer frequently asked questions about the firm and its services.
Identify whether a caller is a new prospective client, existing client, medical provider, insurance representative, or other contact.
Route calls to the appropriate attorney, case manager, or department.
Schedule, reschedule, and confirm consultations and appointments.
Capture detailed messages and automatically create follow-up tasks.
Send appointment confirmations and reminders via text and email.
Transfer urgent or sensitive calls to a live staff member.
Integrate all call information and communications with the firm's case management system.

The AI receptionist must provide a professional, natural, and compassionate experience for personal injury clients.

2. AI Client Intake & New Case Onboarding

We want to automate the entire new-client intake process, from the initial inquiry through attorney review and case onboarding.

Key functionalities include:

Conduct initial intake conversations in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole.
Collect prospective client information through phone, website, text, and email.
Identify the type of personal injury claim, including automobile accidents, slip-and-fall incidents, premises liability, and other injury-related matters.
Collect accident details, incident dates, locations, injuries, medical treatment information, insurance information, and other relevant case details.
Identify urgent matters requiring immediate staff attention.
Automatically create and organize prospective client profiles.
Route new inquiries to the appropriate attorney or intake specialist.
Automate consultation scheduling and follow-up communications.
Generate attorney-approved intake forms, engagement agreements, and onboarding documents.
Send documents for electronic signature.
Automate requests for accident reports, insurance information, photographs, medical information, and other supporting documentation.
Perform preliminary conflict-check screening using firm-approved procedures.
Transfer approved new-client information directly into the firm's case management system.

The system should be able to follow up automatically with prospective clients who have not completed their intake or submitted requested documentation.

3. Medical Records & Medical Billing Automation

Medical records management is a major component of personal injury litigation, and we want to automate as much of this process as possible.

Key functionalities include:

Identify and organize all medical providers associated with each client.
Generate and send medical records and billing requests using approved templates and authorizations.
Track outstanding medical records, invoices, and provider responses.
Automatically follow up with medical providers regarding outstanding requests.
Receive, download, classify, and organize medical records and bills.
Extract relevant information from medical records and billing statements.
Identify treatment dates, diagnoses, procedures, medical providers, and treatment history.
Create chronological medical treatment summaries.
Organize medical expenses by provider and date.
Identify missing treatment records, incomplete billing statements, and gaps in documentation.
Automatically notify the assigned case manager when records or bills are received.
Maintain an organized medical records database for each case.
Integrate medical records and billing information directly into the case management system.

We would also like AI to assist with preparing medical treatment timelines, calculating documented medical expenses, and organizing supporting records for attorney review.

All medical information must be handled securely, with appropriate confidentiality protections and compliance with applicable privacy requirements.

4. AI Settlement Brochure & Demand Package Preparation

We want to significantly reduce the manual work involved in preparing personal injury settlement brochures and demand packages.

The AI system should be capable of gathering information from the client's case file, medical records, billing statements, and supporting documentation to assist our legal team in preparing comprehensive settlement materials.

Key functionalities include:

Collect and organize relevant case information automatically.
Review and summarize accident reports, medical records, medical bills, and supporting documentation.
Generate chronological summaries of the client's medical treatment.
Prepare structured summaries of injuries, diagnoses, procedures, and treatment history.
Organize past medical expenses and other documented damages.
Identify relevant information concerning liability, injuries, treatment, and the impact on the client's daily life.
Generate draft settlement brochures and demand letters using firm-approved templates.
Incorporate supporting medical documentation, photographs, accident reports, and other relevant exhibits.
Automatically create an organized exhibit index.
Identify missing information or documentation before a settlement package is finalized.
Allow attorneys and case managers to review, edit, and approve all AI-generated materials.
Export completed settlement brochures and demand packages into professional, editable formats, including Word and PDF.

Our goal is to automate the preparation of settlement brochures and demand packages while maintaining accuracy, consistency, and attorney oversight.

The system must not invent medical findings, treatment details, expenses, legal arguments, or other case information. Drafts should be traceable to the underlying case records and supporting documentation.

5. AI Case Management & Workflow Automation

We want an integrated case management system that automates administrative tasks and helps our team manage personal injury cases from intake through resolution.

Key functionalities include:

Automatically create and organize new case files.
Assign cases and tasks to the appropriate attorneys, paralegals, and case managers.
Track each case through the firm's established workflow stages.
Monitor outstanding medical records, insurance information, documentation, and other case requirements.
Generate automated reminders and internal follow-up tasks.
Track important case dates and deadlines, with staff verification of legally significant deadlines.
Automate routine client status updates in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole.
Create automated follow-up workflows for clients, medical providers, and insurance companies.
Identify cases that have stalled or require staff attention.
Automatically update case status when approved workflow milestones are completed.
Generate internal case summaries and progress reports.
Create dashboards displaying active cases, outstanding tasks, medical records status, settlement preparation progress, and staff workload.
Integrate communications, documents, medical records, and settlement materials into a centralized case management platform.

We want to eliminate the need for staff to manually enter the same information into multiple systems.

6. AI Client Communication & Follow-Up

We want to improve the client experience through consistent, professional, and multilingual communication.

Key functionalities include:

Communicate with clients in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole.
Send automated case status updates using attorney-approved messaging.
Send appointment confirmations and reminders.
Request missing documents and information.
Follow up with clients regarding outstanding intake forms and authorizations.
Answer routine questions about the firm's processes and the status of a client's case using approved information.
Escalate legal questions, settlement discussions, complaints, and sensitive matters to the appropriate staff member.
Maintain a complete record of client communications within the case management system.

The AI should be able to communicate naturally and professionally while ensuring that legal advice and substantive case decisions remain with qualified legal professionals.

Technical Requirements

We are looking for someone with demonstrated experience in:

AI voice agents and multilingual conversational AI.
AI workflow automation using platforms such as n8n, Make, Zapier, OpenAI, or similar technologies.
Legal practice management software and CRM integrations.
Medical records processing, document extraction, and intelligent document management.
AI-assisted legal document generation.
Automated PDF and Word document creation.
API integrations and custom automation development.
Secure document storage, access controls, and confidential data management.
Building reliable, scalable automation systems with appropriate human review and approval processes.

Experience working with personal injury law firms, legal case management software, medical records workflows, or settlement demand preparation is strongly preferred.

We expect the selected candidate to:

Conduct an assessment of our existing personal injury law firm workflows and technology.
Develop a comprehensive AI automation roadmap covering reception, intake, medical records, settlement brochure preparation, and case management.
Recommend the most effective technology stack and integrations.
Build and implement the approved AI automation systems in phases.
Ensure that English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole are supported throughout all client-facing workflows.
Integrate all automation systems with our existing legal software and document management platforms.
Implement appropriate data security, confidentiality, and attorney approval controls.
Thoroughly test the systems using realistic personal injury case scenarios.
Train our attorneys and administrative team on using the new systems.
Provide ongoing technical support, maintenance, and improvements.

We prefer an integrated solution in which information flows seamlessly between reception, intake, medical records, case management, and settlement preparation.
