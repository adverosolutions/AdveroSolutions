Outbound & GTM Automation Operator (Clay + LinkedIn + Email + CRM)

Summary
We are a growing professional services/recruitment firm looking for a strong hands-on operator to take ownership of an established outbound and GTM automation system. Our current stack includes Clay, LinkedIn automation, cold email, Recruit CRM, enrichment tools and workflow automation. This is not a greenfield build. A significant amount of infrastructure, workflows and automation already exists. We need someone who can quickly understand what has been built, take ownership of the day-to-day operation, troubleshoot issues, improve the system over time, and make sure everything continues running cleanly as we scale. This is a hands-on execution role, not a high-level GTM consulting engagement.

What You'll Be Responsible For
Managing and maintaining existing Clay tables, workflows, enrichment and list-building processes. Building highly targeted prospect lists based on ICPs and campaign requirements provided by our team. Managing LinkedIn outbound campaigns through tools such as HeyReach, Dripify or similar. Managing cold email campaign infrastructure and launches. Launching and refreshing outbound plays on an ongoing basis. Managing enrichment, categorization, deduplication and data quality. Ensuring contacts, campaign activity and responses flow correctly into Recruit CRM. Maintaining automations between Clay, outbound tools, CRM and other systems. Troubleshooting broken workflows, integrations and data issues. Monitoring email deliverability, sending volumes, inbox health and campaign health. Maintaining clear documentation so our systems are not dependent on one person. Providing a simple weekly summary of campaigns, system health, issues and recommended improvements. Identifying practical opportunities to make the system more reliable, efficient and scalable.

What This Role Is NOT
You will not be expected to own overall GTM or sales strategy, brand or marketing strategy, primary copywriting/messaging, funnel strategy, or paid/performance marketing. Our team will provide the ICP, campaign direction, positioning and messaging. Your job is to turn that direction into a well-run outbound system. That said, we value someone who understands outbound well enough to spot problems, recommend improvements and challenge us when something isn't working.

Ideal Experience
Strong hands-on experience with Clay (most important requirement), LinkedIn automation platforms such as HeyReach or Dripify, cold email infrastructure and campaign management, list sourcing and enrichment tools, CRM integrations and data management, workflow automation using n8n, Make, Zapier or similar, email deliverability and inbox health, troubleshooting existing automations rather than only building new ones, documenting workflows and creating reliable operating processes. Experience with Recruit CRM, Talin, staffing/recruitment, B2B professional services or account-based outbound is a strong bonus.

Who We're Looking For
Highly organized and technically capable. Comfortable inheriting systems built by someone else. Proactive about identifying issues before they become problems. Able to work independently without needing every task spelled out. Practical rather than overly theoretical. Strong at documentation and process discipline. Interested in an ongoing relationship rather than a short one-off project. There is meaningful opportunity for this role to grow as we continue expanding outbound and automation capabilities.

Less than 30 hrs/week, Hourly, More than 6 months, Expert, $30.00-$60.00 Hourly
