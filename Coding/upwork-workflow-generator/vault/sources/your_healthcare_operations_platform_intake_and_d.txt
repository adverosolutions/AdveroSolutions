About the Project

We are building an AI platform for a healthcare services business that handles a high volume of patient-related administrative work. The goal is not to replace clinical judgment, but to reduce repetitive operational tasks, organize information faster, and help internal staff make better decisions with less manual searching.

What We Need Built

We are looking for an experienced AI developer or small team to design and build an agentic AI system that can connect to healthcare workflows, retrieve relevant internal knowledge, and perform structured actions through APIs. This should be more than a simple chatbot. The system needs to be reliable, auditable, and designed with healthcare data sensitivity in mind.

Core Features

- AI agent workflows for intake support, document triage, and internal task routing
- RAG and knowledge-base architecture for policies, SOPs, forms, and service guidelines
- OpenAI and/or Claude integrations with clear reasoning boundaries and fallback behavior
- API integration with existing internal tools or CRM-style systems
- Admin interface for reviewing AI outputs, editing knowledge sources, and managing workflows
- Logging, permissions, and basic audit trail for actions taken by the system
- Clean web interface for staff users, with practical UX rather than over-designed screens

Technical Expectations

A good fit would be comfortable with Python, API development, AI agent development, and full-stack implementation. Experience with FastAPI, Node.js, vector databases, PostgreSQL, Supabase, LangGraph, LangChain, LlamaIndex, or similar tools would be helpful. We are open to your recommended architecture, but the system should be maintainable and easy to expand.

We also care about privacy, accuracy, and cost control. The platform should be designed so new workflows can be added later without rebuilding the entire system.

What We Are Looking For

We want someone who can take ownership of the technical architecture, ask practical questions, and work independently. Healthcare experience is a plus, but strong judgment around sensitive data and production-ready AI systems is more important.
