GoHighLevel Automation Expert Needed for Marketing Agency

We are a marketing agency looking for an experienced GoHighLevel automation specialist to help us build and refine several lead management and nurture automations inside our GHL account. This is a project-based position with the potential for additional work.

We are NOT looking for a copywriter. Our team will provide the email copy, SMS copy, landing pages, forms, and overall messaging. We need someone highly skilled in GoHighLevel to build the workflows, logic, segmentation, pipelines, tags, triggers, and follow-up systems.

What We Need Built

1. Direct Appointment Booking Automation — prospects who book a discovery call directly. Appointment confirmations, reminders, follow-up actions, pipeline organization, workflow logic.

2. Discovery Call Automation — what happens with a prospect before and after the call. Contacts segmented based on status and where they are in the sales process.

3. Online Audit Automation — a marketing audit prospects complete on our website. More advanced because prospects take several paths:
- start the audit but do not complete it
- complete the audit but do not qualify
- complete the audit, qualify, but do not book a call
- complete the audit, qualify, and book a call
Each group segmented, placed into the appropriate pipeline stage, enrolled in the correct follow-up or nurture sequence. As prospects take additional actions their status and automation should update. Contacts must not receive duplicate or conflicting messages.

4. Long-Term Nurture and Segmentation — qualified vs not-currently-qualified prospects receive different messaging and newsletters.

Existing GoHighLevel Snapshots — we have snapshots from a marketing program, but they need to be reviewed, modified and customized. We are not looking for someone who simply imports a snapshot and calls it done.

Must be comfortable with: GHL workflows and automation logic, If/Else conditions and branching, contact segmentation and tagging, pipelines and opportunities, appointment automations, workflow enrollment and removal, lead nurturing, email and SMS automations, custom fields, testing and troubleshooting.

We want someone who thinks strategically and recommends better structures, not someone who just clicks through the workflow builder.

To Apply: your GoHighLevel experience, screenshots of workflows/pipelines/automation logic you have built, an example of an advanced automation involving segmentation, branching logic, lead nurturing or multiple prospect paths, and your proposed fixed price.
