# AI Agent Engineer Needed to Build an Autonomous AI Sales Agent (Lead Research, Outreach, Follow-Ups & Meeting Booking)

## Project Overview

We are looking for an experienced **AI Agent Engineer / AI Automation Developer** to design, build, test, and deploy a reliable, end-to-end autonomous AI sales agent for our business.

We are NOT looking for a salesperson, virtual assistant, or lead generation expert to perform these tasks manually. We need a technical expert who can build the actual AI agent and automation infrastructure that performs the sales workflow with minimal human intervention.

Our company provides AI solutions, AI automation, agentic AI systems, business process automation, API integrations, CRM automation, and custom AI implementation services.

The goal is to build an intelligent sales agent that can independently identify high-potential prospects, research businesses, qualify leads, initiate personalized outreach, manage follow-ups, book meetings, and report results, while requesting human approval for predefined sensitive actions.

## What We Want the AI Agent to Do

### 1. Intelligent Lead Discovery and Market Research

- Identify companies that are actively adopting AI, investing in automation, expanding their technology capabilities, or showing clear signs of needing AI solutions.
- Research potential clients across permitted sources, including company websites, public business directories, search engines, relevant professional platforms, and authorized lead databases.
- Identify high-value industries, companies, decision-makers, founders, CEOs, COOs, CTOs, department heads, and other relevant buyers.
- Detect buying signals such as business expansion, repetitive operational processes, hiring for AI-related roles, digital transformation initiatives, inefficient workflows, and publicly announced technology investments.
- Prioritize companies with a strong likelihood of purchasing high-ticket AI services instead of generating large lists of unqualified leads.

### 2. AI-Powered Lead Qualification and Scoring

- Build an intelligent lead scoring system based on company size, industry, budget indicators, business needs, technology adoption, urgency, and purchasing potential.
- Score and rank prospects according to their fit with our services and the strength of available buying signals.
- Explain why each prospect is a good potential client and recommend the most relevant service to offer.
- Distinguish between high-priority prospects, prospects requiring further research, and poor-fit leads.
- Support configurable ideal customer profiles (ICPs), target industries, company sizes, geographical markets, and deal-value thresholds.

### 3. Personalized Outreach Automation

- Generate highly personalized, context-aware outreach based on verified company research and relevant business problems.
- Create tailored email messages, follow-ups, connection requests, and other permitted outreach communications.
- Maintain a natural, professional, value-driven tone instead of sending generic AI-generated spam.
- Support approved outreach channels and integrations, such as email, CRM-connected communication tools, and professional platforms where the relevant automation is permitted.
- Use controlled sending limits, duplicate detection, suppression lists, and opt-out handling.

### 4. Automated Follow-Ups and Lead Nurturing

- Track outreach activity and follow-up schedules.
- Send appropriate follow-ups based on the prospect's response, engagement, and position in the sales sequence.
- Detect replies and classify them as interested, not interested, requesting more information, asking to schedule a meeting, or requiring human intervention.
- Pause sequences when a prospect responds, opts out, books a meeting, or requires manual review.
- Avoid duplicate messages, excessive follow-ups, and inappropriate communication.

### 5. Meeting Booking and Calendar Management

- Recognize when a prospect is interested and ready for a meeting.
- Coordinate available time slots through an approved calendar integration.
- Send scheduling links or arrange meetings according to the prospect's preferences and the rules we define.
- Confirm bookings, create calendar events, send reminders, and handle rescheduling or cancellations.
- Notify us when a qualified meeting is booked and provide the prospect's background, business needs, buying signals, and conversation history.

### 6. CRM, Reporting, and Notifications

- Integrate with an appropriate CRM and maintain an organized record of every lead, interaction, follow-up, and meeting.
- Provide a centralized dashboard or reporting system showing leads discovered, qualified prospects, outreach activity, replies, positive responses, meetings booked, and conversion rates.
- Send regular summaries and immediate notifications for high-value opportunities, positive replies, important decisions, and booked meetings.
- Maintain an auditable activity log so we can see what the agent did, why it did it, and what happened next.

### 7. Human Approval and Control System

The agent should operate autonomously within clearly defined boundaries, with approval checkpoints for sensitive or high-impact actions.

We should be able to configure which activities the agent can execute independently and which require our approval. Examples include approving a new outreach campaign, changing targeting criteria, authorizing a high-volume campaign, approving unusual messages, and handling sensitive or high-value opportunities.

The system must support pausing, resuming, reviewing, and stopping campaigns at any time. It must also include configurable spending limits, usage limits, and safeguards against unintended actions.

## Technical Requirements

The developer should have proven experience with:

- Production-ready AI agents and agentic workflows.
- LLMs such as OpenAI, Claude, or comparable models.
- Workflow automation platforms such as n8n, Make, or equivalent tools.
- API integrations, webhooks, authentication, and third-party services.
- CRM and sales automation integrations.
- Lead enrichment, research pipelines, and AI-powered lead scoring.
- Email automation, reply classification, and calendar scheduling.
- Persistent storage, reliable workflow orchestration, retries, error handling, and monitoring.
- Security, credential management, access controls, and data privacy.
- Automated testing, debugging, deployment, and ongoing observability.

You may recommend the best architecture and technology stack for the project. We are open to a combination of AI models, custom code, and automation platforms, provided the resulting system is reliable, maintainable, and cost-effective.

## Reliability and Quality Standards

We need a properly engineered system, not a quick prototype held together by fragile workflows.

The solution must include:

- End-to-end testing of the complete sales workflow.
- Reliable handling of API failures, rate limits, timeouts, duplicate records, and interrupted workflows.
- Retry logic, failure alerts, logging, and recovery mechanisms.
- Validation of lead data and protection against fabricated research or unsupported claims.
- Safe handling of credentials and customer information.
- Clear documentation, deployment instructions, and maintainable code or workflows.
- Testing of edge cases, including negative replies, opt-outs, rescheduling, duplicate leads, and unavailable integrations.

No developer can honestly guarantee a system with zero bugs under every possible condition. We therefore expect a rigorous QA process, documented acceptance tests, and a defined post-launch bug-fixing period.

## Project Deliverables

1. A documented solution architecture and implementation plan.
2. A working AI sales agent with the agreed discovery, qualification, outreach, follow-up, and booking capabilities.
3. Integrations with the agreed lead sources, outreach channels, CRM, and calendar.
4. A configurable approval system and campaign controls.
5. A reporting dashboard or equivalent reporting interface.
6. End-to-end test results and documentation of known limitations.
7. Source code, prompts, workflow configurations, and deployment documentation.
8. Deployment to our designated environment and a handover session.
9. A defined post-launch support and bug-fixing period.

All project-specific source code, workflows, documentation, and configurations must be handed over to us, subject to the contract and any agreed third-party licensing terms.
