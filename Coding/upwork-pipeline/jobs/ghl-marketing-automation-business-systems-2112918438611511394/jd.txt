GoHighLevel Expert / Marketing Automation & Business Systems Specialist

Project Type: Initial setup with potential for ongoing work
Experience Level: Intermediate to Expert
Location Preference: Mexico / Latin America
English: Strong working proficiency
Budget: Open to hourly or fixed-price proposals

Overview

We are launching a U.S.-based financial services marketing and customer acquisition business and are looking for a highly capable, hands-on technical specialist to help build and integrate our operating infrastructure.

We need someone who can take ownership of the technical setup, configure multiple applications, connect them, and ensure everything functions together.

This is not simply a GoHighLevel project. We are looking for a versatile technical professional who understands CRM systems, marketing automation, websites, business email, telecommunications, databases, and software integrations.

We prefer someone who can become a long-term technical resource as the business grows.

Scope of Work

1. GoHighLevel — CRM & Marketing Automation

* Complete initial account setup and configuration.
* Configure sales pipelines, lead management, and customer workflows.
* Import, organize, and segment large customer databases.
* Set up automated email, SMS, and follow-up campaigns.
* Configure forms, landing pages, and lead capture.
* Establish reporting dashboards.
* Integrate calling, messaging, and other applications.

2. Microsoft 365 & Business Email

* Configure business email accounts using Microsoft 365 / Outlook.
* Set up domain-based email addresses.
* Configure DNS, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
* Ensure reliable email delivery and integration with GoHighLevel.

3. Websites, Domains & Hosting

* Configure domains and DNS through DreamHost.
* Build an initial business website and landing pages using GoHighLevel.
* Connect websites, forms, tracking, and CRM functionality.
* Configure SSL and other necessary website settings.

4. Phone Systems & Communications

* Configure inbound and outbound calling through GoHighLevel and compatible providers.
* Set up business phone numbers, call routing, voicemail, and SMS.
* Establish automated responses and follow-up sequences.
* Integrate communications with CRM customer records.

5. Software Integrations & Business Infrastructure

* Integrate GoHighLevel with other business applications.
* Configure Zapier, Make, webhooks, or APIs as needed.
* Assist with QuickBooks and other operational software.
* Establish Bitwarden password management and appropriate user permissions.
* Configure basic system security, backups, and access controls.

6. Database & Marketing Support

* Import and manage large customer databases.
* Perform data cleaning, deduplication, and segmentation.
* Configure marketing campaign tracking and reporting.
* Assist with Google Ads and Meta advertising integrations.
* Establish workflows supporting both direct mail and digital marketing campaigns.

Required Experience

* Proven hands-on GoHighLevel implementation experience.
* Strong understanding of CRM and marketing automation.
* Experience with Microsoft 365, Outlook, and domain configuration.
* Experience with Twilio or similar telecommunications integrations.
* Knowledge of DNS, email authentication, and website hosting.
* Experience with Zapier, Make, APIs, or webhooks.
* Strong Excel/CSV and database management skills.
* Ability to independently troubleshoot technical problems.

Ideal Candidate

We are looking for a resourceful, technically proficient all-around systems implementer.

Someone who can:

* Take a project from concept through implementation.
* Recommend practical, cost-effective solutions.

6. Marketing automation workflows functioning.
7. Core business applications connected.
8. Systems tested and documented.
