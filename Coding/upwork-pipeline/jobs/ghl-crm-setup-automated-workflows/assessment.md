# Assessment (private, never in the proposal)

## Time
- Discovery call and mapping their stages, forms, team roles: 2-3 h.
- Forms, tags, custom fields, contact dedupe settings: 3-4 h.
- Pipeline and stage-change workflows: 3-4 h.
- Follow-up and nurture workflows with conditional logic (text + email copy included): 5-8 h.
- Task assignment and internal notification workflows: 2-3 h.
- Test leads through every path, fixes, folder naming, short Loom walkthrough for the team: 3-5 h.
- Total: 18-27 real hours, 1.5-2.5 calendar weeks (their copy approvals and texting registration set the pace).

## Likelihood
- High for the build itself: everything in the post is native GHL (forms, tags, custom fields, pipelines, workflows, If/Else, tasks, internal notifications). No outside API.
- What breaks: texting. US numbers need A2P 10DLC registration before carriers deliver GHL texts; approval can take days to weeks and blocks the follow-up section going live. Location not shown, so unverified whether it applies. On the chart (section 3 note) and in beat 6.
- Email deliverability from GHL's mail service depends on their sending domain being set up (dedicated domain, SPF/DKIM). Unverified.
- Unverified for Lorenzo: their GHL plan tier, whether they have an agency or just a sub-account, whether any workflows or pipelines already exist, how many team users. Frame as "we map what you have on the call".
- Lorenzo builds GHL in his own sub-account; import of workflows is by snapshot or by hand, pipelines by hand.

## Standard pricing
- Market for a full GHL CRM + workflow setup of this scope: $1,200-$3,000 fixed, or $40-$90/h.
- Recurring software (theirs): GHL $97-$297/mo, plus LC Phone usage (texts, calls) and LC Email usage; A2P registration one-time fees are small.
- The post's "Fixed $50" is a placeholder and roughly 25-60x under market. Their history (206 contracts, $7,784.74 spent, about $38 a contract) says they buy many small fixed jobs; expect price pressure. Billing stays for the call.

## Ask before quoting
- How many forms and where do leads come from today (website, ads, calls, walk-ins)?
- What are your sales stages today, even if on a spreadsheet?
- How many people on the team, and who owns which leads?
- Do you want texts, emails or both, and is your number registered for business texting?
- Is there an existing GHL account with anything built, and what plan?
- Weekly lead volume (sizes nurture cadence and task load).
