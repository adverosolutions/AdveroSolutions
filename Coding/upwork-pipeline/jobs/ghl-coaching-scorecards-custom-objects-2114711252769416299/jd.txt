# GoHighLevel Systems Architect — Coaching Scorecards, Custom Objects & Automation

**Project:** Build a weekly business performance and accountability system inside our existing GoHighLevel account.

**Business:** Pursley Business Wealth — business coaching and advisory for medical and other service-based practice owners.

## Objective

We need an experienced GoHighLevel specialist to build a reliable system where our coaching clients submit weekly business statistics through digital forms, preserve historical submissions, and enable our team to monitor performance, automate reminders, and connect business problems to our training library.

We want to start with one-on-one coaching clients, then expand the system to group coaching and potentially a subscription-based software product.

## Required deliverables

1. **Data architecture**
   - Design the correct structure for clients, practices, weekly scorecards, and action plans.
   - Use custom objects and associations where appropriate.
   - Preserve every weekly submission as a separate historical record.
   - Document field definitions and relationships.

2. **Digital forms**
   - Build a branded weekly business scorecard.
   - Capture revenue, collections, leads, appointments, conversions, marketing spend, payroll, challenges, and weekly commitments.
   - Associate each submission with the correct client and practice.
   - Validate fields and test record creation.

3. **Workflow automation**
   - Weekly submission reminders.
   - Follow-up for missing submissions.
   - Confirmation and internal notifications.
   - Reliable handling of repeat submissions, duplicate triggers, and workflow errors.

4. **Reporting**
   - Create a coach-facing view of weekly submissions.
   - Track historical performance and targets.
   - Identify missing data and clients who need attention.
   - Implement calculations and dashboards where natively supported.
   - Recommend an appropriate external reporting tool if required.

5. **Coaching integration**
   - Connect performance gaps to relevant training resources and action plans.
   - Keep the architecture reusable for group coaching and future membership tiers.

6. **Testing and handover**
   - Test multiple clients and multiple reporting weeks.
   - Demonstrate that historical data is preserved.
   - Record a walkthrough video.
   - Deliver written documentation, workflow maps, and a maintenance guide.
   - Keep all assets and configurations within our business-owned accounts.

## Required experience

- Advanced GoHighLevel forms and custom fields.
- Custom objects, associations, and object-based workflows.
- Complex automation logic and troubleshooting.
- Reporting, dashboards, and historical data management.
- APIs, webhooks, and Google Sheets or similar integrations when necessary.
- Experience building systems for coaches, consultants, or membership businesses.
