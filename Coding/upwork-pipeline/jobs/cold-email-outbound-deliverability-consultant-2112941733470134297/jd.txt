Full description:

We are looking for a true world-class cold email / outbound expert to act as a senior consultant for our B2B outbound operation.

This is NOT an SDR or copywriting gig. We want someone who has personally designed and run outbound email programs at massive scale — you must have sent more than 10 million outbound emails across your career/clients, and be able to prove it (case studies, dashboards, references).

What you will do (consulting):
- Audit our current outbound setup: domains, mailboxes, DNS (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), warm-up, sending volumes, rotation, sequences and tooling.
- Design a scalable sending infrastructure: domain/mailbox strategy, inbox rotation, provider mix (Google/Microsoft/SMTP), throttling and volume ramp-up.
- Deliverability: inbox placement testing, spam/blacklist monitoring, reputation recovery, bounce and complaint control, list hygiene and verification.
- Sequencing & copy at scale: personalization strategy, spintax/variants, A/B testing frameworks, reply handling.
- Recommend the right tool stack (e.g., Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist, Apollo, Clay, custom SMTP) and how to integrate it with our data.
- Define KPIs and dashboards (inbox placement, open/reply/positive reply rate, bounce, meetings booked).
- Regular calls with our founder and team, plus written recommendations.

Must-have:
- 10M+ outbound emails sent (personally managed), ideally across multiple clients/industries.
- Deep hands-on deliverability and infrastructure experience (hundreds of domains/mailboxes).
- Proven results with numbers.
- Fluent English (calls and written communication).

Nice to have:
- B2B SaaS / data company experience.
- Experience with LatAm / Brazil markets.
- Experience automating outbound with AI (Clay, LLM personalization, n8n).

Definition of done (first phase):
1. Written audit of our current setup with prioritized issues.
2. Infrastructure and deliverability plan (domains, mailboxes, volumes, warm-up, monitoring).
3. Playbook for sequences/testing and a KPI dashboard definition.
4. Ongoing advisory calls after the initial audit.

Part-time consulting engagement, flexible hours. Location: we work best with people based in the Americas (US / Brazil / South America) for timezone overlap.
