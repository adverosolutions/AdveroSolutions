## Likelihood / what breaks
- **Twilio WhatsApp sender approval** is the real timeline risk. Meta business
  verification plus sender registration is days, not hours, and it is entirely
  on the client's side. If it is not started, two weeks for all five flows is
  not achievable no matter who builds it. Sandbox demo works meanwhile.
- **IG and FB DM intake** needs a Meta app with the messaging permissions and a
  page connected. Common blocker, not a build problem.
- **Google Business Profile API** needs an approved quota request. Weeks in the
  bad case.
- **LinkedIn posting** has no official personal-profile posting API. If the
  existing flow uses an unofficial route, "overhaul" may mean "replace with
  something that will not get the account limited". Say that plainly.
- **Lovable and "UGC flow"** are the two words in this post with no agreed
  meaning. Scope them on the call or they eat the margin.
- Honest framing for Lorenzo: n8n, Supabase, webhook intake, Slack and calendar
  work is daily work. Twilio WhatsApp sender registration and Google Business
  Profile quota are adjacent, not claimed as past shipped work. The video shows
  the logic running in n8n, which is the claim that is true.

2. Is there a CRM today, or is Supabase new? What table shape exists?
3. Where do leads actually come from right now - web form, IG DM, FB DM, all three?
4. What does "high-definition UGC flow" mean in output terms - script, image, video?
5. What tool posts to LinkedIn today, and what exactly is broken about it?
6. Which milestone do you want first, and what does "done" look like for it?
