## Format
- One HTML file (`reel.html` in the job folder), with no doctype/html/head/body tags. Start with `<title>` then the `<link>` for Google Fonts, then `<style>`.
- Keep the base's engine exactly: the 1280x720 stage scaled to fit, `.shot` layers with `data-t` times, `--k`/`--d` reveals, the CUES counters, the play overlay, the bar, Replay, and Space to pause. One Play runs the whole reel.
- Run time is 40 to 55 seconds: a cold open, 5 to 6 story shots, the builder finale, and a closing card.
- Keep the dark single-theme look (a deliberate film look). Pick one accent that fits the subject and let the aurora hue shift per shot.

## Netflix-level craft (go past the base)
- **Cold open:** a title-card moment. The client's world in one striking image built from light and type, a slow push, and a 2-line title reveal. Letterbox bars may slide in and out for the open and close.
- **Every shot has foreground, midground and background depth:** glass cards in 3D (perspective and rotateY), soft focus on what isn't the subject, and one hero element per shot.
- **Transitions:** use match cuts and push-throughs, not only crossfades. For example, zoom into a card and it becomes the next shot, a light streak wipes across, or the camera tracks laterally.
- **Motion:** spring and expo easing, staggered reveals, particles along paths, numbers ticking, statuses flipping, glows pulsing on the live element. Something always moves, but calmly.
- **Type:** the display serif for the few headline words, and the product's own words on the UI. Minimal copy: a headline per shot is 6 words or fewer.
- **Detail:** real-looking records, timestamps, tiny UI states (typing dots, check ticks, hover rings), light sweeps on key cards, and subtle chromatic glow on the accent.

## Show the automation itself
- At least one shot visualises the actual automation logic as a living flow: trigger, then conditions and branches, then actions, with data pulses traveling through it and each node lighting up as it runs.

## The builder finale ("This is what it will look like in your account")
- If the post names a platform (HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Zoho CRM, Apollo, n8n, Make and so on), the second-to-last shot is a builder view in that platform's style:
  - its layout conventions: a vertical workflow canvas with the trigger card on top, "+" connectors, if/then branch splits, action cards with icon, title and one-line detail, and a side panel
  - its general colour feel
  - the sample workflow for this client, built out node by node while the camera tracks down it
- Do **not** use the vendor's logo, wordmark or trademarked marks. Label it "Your workflow in <Platform> · illustrative". The canvas shows this client's workflow, never a generic one.
- End on the closing word card.

## Honesty and copy rules
- Sample data is labelled once ("Sample data" in the HUD). Illustrative numbers carry "Illustrative".
- Nothing is invented about the client beyond the post. Use no prices for Lorenzo's work, and no health claims.
- Use only facts about Lorenzo that appear in the job's beats and proposal, if any.
