Original job description:
MapleSpark is an education technology company looking for a full-time remote AI Agent & Workflow Specialist to work with our founder and technical lead.

We use Claude Code and Codex across different tasks. We need someone who can run agents, monitor their progress, check their output, troubleshoot problems, and improve how the work gets done.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

• Run AI agent workflows and check active tasks at least hourly during your working hours.
• Catch mistakes, stalled tasks, missed instructions, and poor-quality results.
• Write and refine prompts, break larger tasks into manageable steps, and add checks between stages.
• Adjust workflows, test different approaches, and verify whether changes improve results.
• Inspect outputs yourself rather than relying only on an agent saying the work is complete.
• Document useful processes and communicate problems clearly to our team.

The work will span different projects, including educational content, research, and testing. You should be comfortable learning new tasks and helping develop the process without needing detailed instructions for every step.

REQUIRED EXPERIENCE

• Hands-on experience with BOTH Claude Code and Codex.
• Practical understanding of AI agents, effective prompting, and multi-step workflows.
• Strong written and spoken English.
• Attention to detail, initiative, and practical problem-solving.
• Ability to test results against the original requirements and explain what you checked.

Reading code or making small code changes is a bonus, not a requirement. Directing agents effectively and checking that their output works are essential.

HOURS AND RATE

This is a full-time remote role. Your working hours must overlap substantially with our team in Toronto, Canada (Eastern Time), so we can communicate and resolve issues during the workday.

Applicants worldwide are welcome. Please propose your most competitive hourly rate in USD for a full-time engagement.

Screening questions:
1. What have you personally accomplished using Claude Code and Codex? Give a specific example for each tool.
2. Describe an AI workflow you broke into smaller steps. What checks did you use between steps, and how did you verify the final result?
3. Tell us about a time an AI agent got stuck or produced an incorrect result. How did you identify the problem, fix it, and check your fix?
4. Describe a change you made that improved an AI workflow. How did you determine that the revised process worked better?
5. What is your most competitive hourly rate in USD for full-time work? Specify your available working hours in Toronto time and earliest start date.
