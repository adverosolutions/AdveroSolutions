# Assessment (private, never in the proposal)

## Time
- Setup: 12-16 hours over 2-3 calendar weeks (domains and inboxes 2 h, warm-up 2 weeks unless pre-warmed, list build and verification 4-5 h, copy, 3 follow-ups and sequences 4-5 h, Instantly campaign and unibox 2 h).
- Ongoing: 4-6 hours a week (replies, weekly tests, list top-ups, booking).
- First booked calls realistic in weeks 3-5; month one is mostly measurement.

## Likelihood
- 10 calls/month: likely once lists and deliverability are solid. 30: depends on sends per booked call, which nobody knows until month one. Unverified: his offer strength, his own close/qualification bar, whether he already owns domains or tools.
- Risks: commercial cleaning owners are a heavily pitched niche (other agencies email them), so copy and list quality carry it. UK vs US prospects changes data sources and compliance (UK PECR/GDPR on B2B email to sole traders). Burned domains if volume is pushed before warm-up.

## Standard pricing
- Market for a done-for-you cold email appointment setter: $1,500-$4,000/month retainer, or $150-$400 per qualified call on pay-per-meeting.
- Software: 5 domains ~$10-12/yr each, 15 mailboxes ~$3-4 each/month (~$45-60/month, Zapmail), Instantly ~$37-97/month, data/verification ~$50-150/month.
- Post's rate of $4-$5/hr is far below market (roughly 5-10% of it); recorded bids average $30.32. Treat the rate as a placeholder and pitch on outcome.

## Ask before quoting
- Where are your prospects (UK, US, both) and how many cleaning companies are in that area?
- What is your offer to cleaning owners and what makes a call "qualified" for you?
- Do you already have domains, inboxes, Instantly or a data tool?
- Whose calendar do calls book onto, and who runs them?
- Monthly budget for tools and sends.

## Pricing path (Lorenzo only)
- Value: assumption, not in the post. If each booked call is worth ~$300 to his agency (a fraction of a cleaning-company retainer he sells), 20 calls/month = ~$6,000/month, ~$72,000/year.
- Price range at 10-20% of value: $7,200-$14,400/year, i.e. ~$600-$1,200/month plus tools.
- Land offer (milestone 1): infrastructure plus first list and sequence for a fixed fee.
- Upsell: monthly management retainer, then pay-per-qualified-call once sends per call are known.
- Milestones:
  1. 30% - paid once domains, inboxes and warm-up are set up in Instantly and you've signed off.
  2. 25% - paid once the first verified list of commercial cleaning owners and the sequence copy are delivered and you've signed off.
  3. 25% - paid once the campaign is live with replies handled and booking to your calendar working and you've signed off.
  4. 20% - paid once the month-one report (sends per booked call, domains needed for 30) is delivered and you've signed off.
