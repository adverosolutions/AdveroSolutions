We're looking for an n8n automation developer to build a lead-management system that connects incoming enquiries, AI-powered lead qualification, and GoHighLevel (GHL) CRM. Capture each enquiry, organize the information, update the CRM, and trigger the appropriate follow-up without repeated manual work. A working business process, not a chatbot demo. Clear workflow logic, reliable integrations, error handling, and documentation.
What you'll build:
1. Lead capture and data validation: n8n workflow receiving leads from one website form or webhook. Validate required fields, standardize contact information, check for existing records before creating new contacts.
2. AI-powered lead qualification: extract the requested service, summarize the enquiry, classify leads using agreed criteria. Missing information flagged rather than invented; unclear enquiries routed for manual review.
3. GoHighLevel CRM integration: create or update contacts, populate custom fields, apply tags, create or update opportunities in the right pipeline stage. Store the enquiry summary and qualification result where sales can review.
4. Follow-up and appointment workflows: acknowledgement and follow-up sequence with approved messaging and a booking link. Follow-ups stop when a lead replies, books, opts out, or is manually paused.
5. Monitoring and failure handling: execution logs, controlled retries, alerts for failed integrations. Repeated webhook events or retries must not create duplicate contacts, opportunities, or messages.
Deliverables: working n8n automation connected to one lead source, one GHL account, one email-sending service; CRM field mappings, pipeline rules, AI qualification instructions; tested follow-up logic, duplicate prevention, error notifications; export of workflows without secrets, setup documentation, recorded handover.
