AI Automation Engineer / Consultant for Business Workflow Automation
Hourly, part-time, expert, rate not given. Client in United States: Bennett Awards, custom awards studio (high-end custom awards and deal toys). Saved 2026-09-30. Avg bid $70. First paid project is the weekly marketing reporting automation. Asks five questions including a cost estimate (never quote; answer on the call).

Bennett Awards is a custom awards studio specializing in high-end custom awards and deal toys.

We are looking for an experienced AI automation engineer / consultant to help us design and implement several practical business automations.

We are not looking for general AI strategy or prompt-writing assistance. We are looking for someone who has personally built and deployed reliable business workflows involving APIs, automation platforms and LLMs.

Initial Projects
1. Weekly Marketing Reporting Automation
We currently manually gather data each week from:
• Google Ads
• Google Analytics / GA4
• Microsoft Ads
• Copper CRM

We then enter the data into an Excel-based historical tracking/reporting system.
We would like to automate this process so that:
• Data is retrieved automatically on a scheduled basis
• The appropriate metrics are added to our existing historical reporting
• Calculations and comparisons are completed automatically
• AI provides a short weekly analysis highlighting meaningful changes, trends and anomalies

Accuracy and reliability are very important. We expect APIs or other deterministic methods to be used for retrieving and calculating data, with AI used primarily for interpretation and analysis.

2. AI-Assisted Project Success Stories / Case Studies

We complete many custom award projects that could become website case studies, success stories and marketing content.
Relevant information is currently spread across:
• QuickBooks Online
• Gmail
• Google Drive
• Project files
• Design/specification files
• Finished-product photography

We would like to create a workflow that can gather relevant project information and produce a draft success story using a standardized Bennett Awards format and brand voice.
Potential outputs could include:
• Customer/project overview
• Customer objective or challenge
• Creative/design solution
• Materials and production techniques
• Finished case study
• Recommended/select project photography
• Photo captions and alt text
• SEO title and meta description
• Related social-media content

We would require a human review/approval step before external publication.
A key requirement is that the system must remain grounded in source material and avoid inventing unsupported project details.

3. Gmail Inbox Triage and Routing
We would also like to automate the review and sorting of incoming Gmail messages.
The system would review emails, interpret their content and assign them to predefined Gmail labels/folders such as:
• To Review
• New Leads
• Strategic Leads / "Home Runs"
• Reorders
• Photos to Add
• Testimonials to Add
• Other predefined operational categories

The system should use contextual classification rather than simple keyword matching.
Messages that clearly meet defined criteria could be routed automatically. Messages that do not match a useful category and meet predefined deletion rules could eventually be deleted automatically.

We would expect appropriate safeguards around deletion, including confidence thresholds, logging and/or an initial review period before irreversible actions are enabled.
