About ZEAL

ZEAL is building an AI-powered digital travel advisor and curated marketplace that connects travelers with authentic local experiences, trusted accommodations, and meaningful communities.

We're preparing to scale our operations and are looking for an experienced Automation & Systems Architect to design, implement, and maintain the internal systems that will power our business.

This is not a basic Zapier or Make automation role.

We're looking for someone who can design an interconnected operating system, implement the technical infrastructure, anticipate dependencies, and create scalable, well-documented workflows.

You'll work directly with the founder and collaborate with our technical and operational teams.

Our Technology Stack

ClickUp — Project management and asynchronous execution

Make.com — Workflow orchestration and integrations

Airtable — Operational databases and marketplace intelligence

Pipedrive — CRM and partnership management

OpenAI API — AI-powered workflow intelligence

BigQuery — Data warehousing

Sigma and Amplitude — Analytics and reporting

Google Workspace and Slack — Documentation and communication

Milestone 1: Internal Operating System Architecture

Our first priority is establishing a connected, reliable internal operating system.

Responsibilities include:

Audit existing tools, workflows, and architecture documentation.

Design and configure ClickUp for cross-functional team execution, including task structures, custom fields, automations, dependencies, and escalation workflows.

Architect Make.com integrations between ClickUp, Airtable, Pipedrive, Google Workspace, and Slack.

Establish consistent data models, record identifiers, and source-of-truth rules.

Automate operational handoffs between business functions.

Implement error handling, monitoring, logging, and recovery processes.

Create scalable foundations for AI-powered knowledge retrieval and operational intelligence.

Document system architecture, automation scenarios, SOPs, and maintenance procedures.

Expected outcome: A functional, integrated operating infrastructure that minimizes manual coordination and supports a growing remote-first team.

Milestone 2: AI-Powered Marketplace Sourcing & Outreach

Following the internal systems implementation, you'll help build the automation infrastructure supporting ZEAL's marketplace.

This includes:

Creator and local ambassador discovery, enrichment, and scoring.

Tour operator and experience vendor sourcing.

Accommodation partner discovery and qualification.

AI-assisted research and review intelligence.

Airtable data pipelines, deduplication, and quality assurance.

Automated qualification and outreach preparation.

Personalized messaging workflows and CRM synchronization.

Performance tracking and optimization.

We have already developed detailed workflows, data structures, AI agent specifications, scoring frameworks, and implementation blueprints for these systems.

Your responsibility will be to validate the architecture, identify improvements, and implement production-ready workflows.

Required Qualifications

Advanced Make.com experience, including routers, webhooks, APIs, data transformation, and error handling.

Advanced Airtable experience, including relational database architecture.

Strong ClickUp implementation experience.

Experience integrating multiple business applications into one operational ecosystem.

OpenAI API and structured AI workflow experience.

Understanding of data integrity, idempotency, monitoring, and system reliability.

Ability to write clear technical documentation.

Strong asynchronous communication and independent problem-solving.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with Pipedrive or similar CRMs.

BigQuery, SQL, and business intelligence integrations.

AI agents, RAG, and knowledge retrieval systems.

Marketplace, travel-tech, SaaS, or platform business experience.

Experience supporting remote-first startups.

What Success Looks Like

We want someone who can simplify complexity rather than add to it.

The right candidate will be able to explain why a system should be designed a certain way, identify potential failures before they occur, and create infrastructure that another developer can maintain.

We value architectural judgment, execution quality, and ownership more than certifications alone.

Engagement Structure

We anticipate beginning with a paid architecture assessment, followed by milestone-based implementation.

The initial engagement will focus on internal operations, with additional phases covering marketplace automation and ongoing optimization.

How to Apply

Please include:

A description of the most complex multi-platform operating system you've personally architected and implemented.

A Loom video (3–5 minutes preferred) walking through a real system you've built, including its architecture and integrations.

Your experience with Make, Airtable, ClickUp, APIs, and OpenAI.

An example of how you've handled automation failures, duplicate records, or data synchronization issues.

Your hourly rate, availability, and preferred engagement structure.

Applications consisting only of generic AI-generated proposals or lists of tools will not be prioritized.

We are seeking an experienced systems architect who can also execute—not someone learning these platforms on the job.
