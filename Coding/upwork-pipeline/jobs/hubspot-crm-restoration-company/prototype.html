01  Discovery session

How you win and track work.

The summary that comes out of the first session. Every later decision in the build points back to one of these lines.

Insurance carriers

Assignments come through a regional office, not the carrier's head office. The relationship that matters is the office and the people in it.

Tracked at the regional office

Adjusters

The single most valuable relationship. An adjuster can move between carriers or work independently, so the person is tracked separately from the company.

Tracked on the contact

Property managers

One manager covers many buildings. A good one sends repeat jobs across a portfolio, which is what BD visits are meant to protect.

Tracked on the company

Plumbers

Often first on site for a water loss. Fast handoff matters more than anything else, so speed from call to inspection gets measured.

Tracked on the job

Commercial accounts

Standing relationships with facilities teams. Fewer jobs, larger tickets, and often reconstruction as well as mitigation.

Tracked on the company

What the build has to answer

  • Which referral sources send the jobs that actually close, per office?
  • Where is every open job in the lifecycle, and which one is stuck?
  • Which adjusters and property managers has BD not seen in 30 days?

Decisions confirmed in the session

  • Is reconstruction the same job as mitigation, or its own contract?
  • Who can see the other office's jobs and relationships?
  • Which system owns the job once production starts: HubSpot or RMS/Luxor?
Sample summary, confirmed with you in the first session.

10  Integration recommendations

HubSpot keeps the relationship, the rest of your stack keeps its job.

Each recommendation says what moves, in which direction and when, so nothing is entered twice.

Outlook

Native HubSpot connection
Connect in week one
  • Emails with adjusters and managers log to their contact automatically.
  • Calendar meetings become BD visits, which feed the activity dashboard.
  • Personal and internal email domains are excluded from logging.
Outlook HubSpot

QuickBooks

Native HubSpot connection
Connect after go-live
  • Invoice status and paid amount come back onto the job.
  • Referral reports count collected revenue, not estimates.
  • Accounting stays the source of truth for invoices.
QuickBooks HubSpot

RMS/Luxor

Method set after an access check
Confirm API access first
  • Job management stays in RMS/Luxor once mitigation starts.
  • The job number and production stage come back to HubSpot for reporting.
  • Direct sync if it has an API, otherwise middleware or a scheduled file.
HubSpot RMS/Luxor
Recommendations only; each connection is confirmed with you before it is switched on.
