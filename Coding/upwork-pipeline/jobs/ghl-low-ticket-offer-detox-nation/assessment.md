## Likelihood
- High that the build works: everything shown is native GoHighLevel (funnels, order forms and bumps, memberships, workflows, scoring, pipelines, calendars, Conversation AI).
- What can break or is unverified:
  - His plan tier: memberships and Conversation AI are included on most plans, but Conversation AI and SMS are usage-billed. Unverified.
  - UK SMS: two-way SMS needs a UK number through LC Phone (or Twilio) and a compliant sender; alphanumeric sender IDs cannot receive replies. The check-in replies depend on this.
  - Email deliverability: needs his domain authenticated on LC Email (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
  - Payments: Stripe must be connected to the sub-account.
  - Content: the offer itself (what the 7 days contain) is his. We do not know how far along it is; if it does not exist yet, the calendar doubles.
  - The AI assistant must never give health advice: knowledge base limited to logistics and program FAQs, health or medication questions handed to Sandro. Stated on screen.
  - We do not know what his existing automation does; the build assumes we review and keep what works.

## Standard pricing
- Market for a GoHighLevel low-ticket funnel with delivery, nurture, AI booking and pipeline: $1,800 to $4,000 fixed, plus $300 to $800 a month if he wants ongoing optimisation.
- Recurring software (his): GoHighLevel plan ($97 to $297/mo), SMS and Conversation AI usage (roughly $10 to $40/mo at low volume), Stripe fees.
- The post's rate ($7 to $25 an hour, bids averaging $20) is far below market for this scope. At $25/h, 30 h is $750. Pitch fixed scope and outcome, not hours.

## Ask before quoting
1. What is the low-ticket offer, its price, and does its content already exist (videos, PDFs, a course)?
2. What does the automation you designed in GoHighLevel do today, and what is not working?
3. Where will buyers come from: ads, social, your treatment clients? Roughly how many past treatment clients are on your list?
4. What is the coaching program (length, price range, format), and do you take the calls yourself?
5. Which GoHighLevel plan are you on, is Stripe connected, and do you have a UK number for SMS?
6. Do you want an AI assistant answering buyers, or only automated messages?

## Flags
