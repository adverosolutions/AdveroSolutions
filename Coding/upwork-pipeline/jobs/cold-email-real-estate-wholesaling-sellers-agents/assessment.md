# Private assessment - Cold email for real estate wholesaling, sellers plus agents

Hourly $5 to $15 manual budget, full-time, expert. Client in Ohio. 27 contracts, 9 active, $68k spent, 4.65 over 12 reviews, 8,964 hours. Buys cold callers, lead managers, list builders. One cold caller contract closed at 1 star, one agency contract at 3 stars. Posted 2026-09-21, 0 hires, 0 invites, 17 connects.

## Time

Build plus infrastructure, not just a workflow.

- Infrastructure: domains, mailboxes, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX, tracking domain, Instantly workspace, warmup, limits. 8 to 12 hours of real work, then 2 to 3 weeks of warmup waiting unless pre-warmed mailboxes are bought.
- Data plumbing: two sources, merge, verification, suppression and contacted-history sheet. 6 to 10 hours, and most of that is whatever shape the client's list vendors actually return.
- The n8n system as specced: 8 to 12 hours, plus a pass on the reply router once real replies exist.
- Copy: two sequences, seller and agent, three emails each, plus subject and preview variants for the A/B. 4 to 6 hours plus revisions with whoever approves copy.
- Documentation so their team can run it: 3 to 4 hours.

Roughly 30 to 45 hours to a live, documented channel, over 4 to 6 calendar weeks because warmup is wall-clock time he cannot compress. Then it is an ongoing optimization role, realistically 5 to 10 hours a week.

## Likelihood

The mechanics are home turf. He runs this exact stack at 100k+ a month on Instantly with an agent writing each message, mailboxes at $3 to $4 through the provider, 30 a day per mailbox, 3 mailboxes per domain. The risks sit elsewhere.

1. **Real estate targeting is NOT a verified fact for Lorenzo.** He has not run seller or agent lists. Never claim real estate results. Frame it honestly: the system is identical, the lists and the copy are what change. The video shows the machine; the first two weeks decide the copy.
2. **The seller campaign is B2C and that changes the rules.** Direct-to-homeowner cold email to private individuals is consumer email, not business email. CAN-SPAM still applies, but the practical problem is that Gmail, Yahoo and the consumer filters are far harsher on unsolicited mail to personal addresses than they are on B2B. Deliverability on the seller side will be materially worse than on the agent side even with identical infrastructure. Say this before they hear a number they like: the seller campaign runs at tighter volume per mailbox, on its own domain pool separate from the agent pool, with a real physical business address in the footer and a working one-click opt out, and it gets its own reputation watched separately. The agent campaign is normal B2B and can scale faster.
3. **Do not quote bounce, reply or appointment metrics for this vertical.** There are none to quote. He must either supply real numbers from his own sending, clearly labelled as his own verticals, or decline to give a number and say the first two weeks produce the baseline. The screening list asks for metrics eight ways; answer with the video and the method, not invented percentages. A wholesaler who has burned two contractors will check.
4. **Client history is the real risk.** 1 star to a cold caller, 3 stars to an agency, 9 active contracts and nearly 9,000 hours logged. This is a buyer who hires a lot and fires. Scope in writing before any hours are logged: what is in the build, what is the ongoing role, what counts as done. An hourly full-time framing with no defined deliverable is how the 1-star happened.
5. **Data quality is theirs, not his.** Probate, tax lien and pre-foreclosure lists carry stale and wrong addresses. Expect to lose a real share of the list at verification. Set that expectation before the first suppression count lands.
6. **Domain reputation is the thing they actually bought.** Their goal line is "without destroying domain reputation". Everything about the seller campaign should be framed as protecting that.

## Standard pricing

Defensible market for build plus infrastructure plus copy plus documentation: **$2,500 to $5,000 setup**, then **$1,500 to $3,000 a month** to run and optimize. Hourly equivalent **$40 to $85**.

The post's $5 to $15 an hour full time is a fraction of that. It is cold-caller and VA money for a role that is infrastructure, deliverability and copy. He can bid into it on purpose, but the reference number is above, and full-time hours at $15 is a worse deal than a fixed setup fee plus a monthly retainer. Propose the fixed shape.

Recurring on the client's card, not his: domains about $12 to $15 a year each, mailboxes $3 to $4 each a month through the provider, Instantly from $37 a month, verification roughly $2 to $5 per thousand addresses, plus whatever the probate and foreclosure data subscriptions already cost them.

## Ask before quoting

1. What data sources are they on today for probate, divorce, tax lien, foreclosure and distressed, and how many records a month does each return?
2. What CRM is the pipe landing in: Podio, REsimpli, GoHighLevel, something else? That decides whether node 14 is an API call or a whole integration.
3. Instantly or Smartlead, and do they already have an account, or does he stand one up in their name on their card?
4. What is the volume target per day, split seller versus agent? That sets the domain and mailbox count and the bill.
5. Who writes and who approves the copy? If their team approves every email the calendar doubles.
6. Hours per week actually expected, and is this a build with a retainer after, or a full-time seat?
7. Do they have a real physical business address they are willing to put in the seller footer?
