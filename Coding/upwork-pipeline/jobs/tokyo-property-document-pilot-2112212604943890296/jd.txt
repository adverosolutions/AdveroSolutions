Title: AI & Workflow Automation Specialist for a Real Estate Business — Remote, Paid Pilot, Ongoing Potential

We are a small real estate development and investment business based in Tokyo, Japan, with hotel-related projects.

We are looking for an experienced specialist to review how we work, identify opportunities for automation, and build practical systems that reduce repetitive tasks and free up the owner’s time.

This is a fully remote role. Applicants from any location are welcome, provided we can arrange some overlap with Japan business hours for meetings.

We need someone who can guide us through the process—from understanding our operations to implementation and training. We have limited time to learn the technical details or prepare lengthy specifications.

Potential workflows:
• Extracting information from property PDFs into standardized summaries and spreadsheets.
• Organizing inquiries, summarizing emails, and preparing reply drafts.
• Updating CRM records and creating follow-up reminders.
• Organizing receipts and invoices for accounting preparation.
• Tracking tasks, outstanding items, and project progress.

Our current tools:
HubSpot, Notion, Nutstore cloud storage, email, Excel, and PDFs.

We welcome recommendations, with a preference for simple, reliable systems that are easy for a small team to maintain.

Initial paid engagement:

Review our current workflows through video calls and screen sharing.

Recommend approximately three automation opportunities, ranked by impact and implementation effort.

Agree on one clearly defined workflow and build a paid pilot.

Test the system using representative documents and practical working scenarios.

Provide documentation and train our team.

If the pilot is successful, we would like to develop a longer-term working relationship for further implementation and support.

Who we are looking for:
• Experience delivering working AI and automation systems for small businesses.
• Ability to connect CRM, email, document, and cloud-storage systems.
• Experience with AI-powered document extraction, classification, summarization, and drafting.
• Ability to handle errors, duplicate records, and human approval steps.
• Clear communication and a proactive approach to understanding business needs.
• Ability to turn screen-sharing demonstrations into requirements and proposed solutions.

Experience with Make, Zapier, n8n, or relevant APIs is welcome. Please recommend the tools that best fit the work.

Language:
English communication is required. Many source documents are in Japanese, so the systems must handle Japanese content accurately. Japanese language skills are a plus, but not mandatory if you have a reliable approach to processing and validating these documents.

Security and handover:
• Begin with sample or anonymized data and limited access.
• Use company-owned accounts with separately assigned permissions.
• Explain where our data is processed and disclose recurring service costs.
• Keep outgoing emails and other external communications subject to human approval initially.
• Provide workflow files, relevant source code, setup instructions, and operating documentation.
• Be willing to sign a confidentiality agreement.

Please include in your proposal:

One or two relevant projects, your specific role, and the results.

How you would conduct the initial workflow review remotely.

A suggested first pilot based on the workflows above.

Your estimated discovery fee, pilot budget range, and timeline.

Your time zone and availability for meetings during Japan business hours.

Your approach to secure access, handover, and ongoing support.

We would like to start with a paid discovery phase, followed by a separately agreed pilot scope and price.
