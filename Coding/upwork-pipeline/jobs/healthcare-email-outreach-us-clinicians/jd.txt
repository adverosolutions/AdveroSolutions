We are looking for an experienced B2B outreach specialist to build and run targeted email campaigns to clinicians, physicians and medical practices across the United States.
The objective is to generate qualified appointments for our internal sales team and, where appropriate, drive direct online sign-ups or subscriptions for selected healthcare technology services.
The freelancer will be responsible for identifying suitable prospects, building and cleaning contact lists, segmenting audiences, creating and testing email sequences, launching campaigns, monitoring deliverability and engagement, and continuously improving conversion rates.
Our Technical Sales Executive or founders will take over qualified meetings that require a product discussion, demonstration or commercial discussion.
Responsibilities
- Build targeted databases of US physicians, clinicians, practices and relevant healthcare decision-makers
- Segment prospects by specialty, practice type, organisation size and likely use case
- Create and execute outbound email campaigns at scale
- Write and test subject lines, messaging, CTAs and follow-up sequences
- Drive prospects either to book a meeting or subscribe online, depending on the campaign
- Track replies, clicks, appointments and conversions
- Manage list hygiene, sender reputation and email deliverability
- Optimise campaigns based on performance
- Hand qualified opportunities to our internal team with relevant context
Preferred experience
- Previous experience running B2B healthcare campaigns in the US
- Experience targeting physicians, clinics, medical groups or healthcare organisations
- Strong understanding of outbound email deliverability
- Experience with prospecting, enrichment and sequencing tools
- Familiarity with CRM systems and campaign reporting
- Understanding of US email outreach rules, including CAN-SPAM and appropriate use of business contact data
