# Assessment (private)

## Time
- Setup: 15-25 hours over about 2 weeks (database build and segmentation, verification, domains and mailboxes in Zapmail, Instantly setup, first sequences). Warm-up adds 2-3 weeks before real volume unless pre-warmed mailboxes are bought.
- Ongoing: part-time as posted, roughly 5-10 hours a week (reply handling, hand-offs, weekly tests, reporting).
- First meetings realistically land 3-5 weeks after kickoff.

## Likelihood
- Lorenzo has no healthcare outreach experience and no healthcare results. Screening Q1 asks for exactly that; answer honestly (see screening.md) and lead with his own 100k+/month Instantly outbound and the consultancy +35% bullet.
- Data: clinician emails are the hard part. NPI registry (NPPES) gives names, specialty and practice address but no email; emails need an enrichment tool, and many practices only expose front-desk inboxes. Unverified for Lorenzo: hit rates for clinician emails in any tool.
- Deliverability risk: physician-owned domains and hospital systems filter hard. Verify everything; keep bounces low from day one.
- Compliance: CAN-SPAM covers B2B email (address, opt-out honoured within 10 business days, no deceptive subjects). Client is UK-based; UK/GDPR does not govern US recipients, but they may ask. No patient data is involved, so HIPAA is not in scope; say so if asked, do not claim expertise.
- Product unknown: messaging depends on what the healthcare technology service is.

## Standard pricing
- Market for a B2B outbound specialist running infrastructure plus campaigns: $35-75/hr, or $1,500-4,000/month retainer; setup one-off $1,000-2,500.
- The post's $15-25/hr is well under market; recorded bids avg $46.50.
- Recurring software (client pays): Instantly Growth/Hypersonic about $47-97/month plus leads/verification credits; Zapmail about $3-4 per mailbox a month plus domains (~$10-15/year each); an enrichment/data tool $50-300/month depending on volume.

## Ask before quoting
- Monthly volume target (sets mailboxes and domains).
- What the healthcare technology service is, and which specialties/practice types they already sell to.
- Do they have a CRM, a booking link, and an online sign-up page?
- Existing data or tools, or starting from zero?
- Who qualifies a lead before hand-off, and what context the Technical Sales Executive wants.

## Flags
- "US-based freelancers preferred (not required)": no location claims in copy.
- 5 screening questions; Q1 asks for healthcare results he does not have.
- First-time buyer, low budget.
