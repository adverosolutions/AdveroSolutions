# Screening answers (short, point to the call)

1. Describe your recent experience running outbound campaigns targeting US physicians, clinicians or medical practices. What results did you achieve?

I run 100k+ cold emails a month for my own agency and more for clients, all personalized per recipient, and I grew a software consultancy's qualified lead volume 35% with the same approach. The video shows exactly how I'd run it for US clinicians and practices; happy to walk through results on a quick call.

2. Which tools do you use for prospecting, contact enrichment, email verification, sequencing and deliverability monitoring?

Apify for prospecting, MillionVerifier for verification, Claude for research and personalization, Zapmail for mailboxes and domains, and Instantly for sequencing and deliverability monitoring.

3. How would you build and segment a database of US clinicians and medical practices for this type of campaign?

Build by specialty and practice type, verify every email, then segment by practice size and likely use case so each group gets its own message. I'd like to go through your target list on a call first.

4. How do you manage CAN-SPAM compliance, sender reputation and email deliverability when running high-volume outbound campaigns?

Separate lookalike domains with warmed mailboxes, verified lists only, a postal address and working opt-out on every email, and daily bounce and reply monitoring in Instantly. Happy to walk through the full setup on a call.

5. What metrics would you use to assess campaign performance, and what would you change if replies, appointments or conversions were low?

Reply rate, positive replies, meetings booked and sign-ups. If they're low, I test the offer and the opening line first, then the targeting. The offer is the biggest lever, so that's the first thing I'd want to cover on a call.
