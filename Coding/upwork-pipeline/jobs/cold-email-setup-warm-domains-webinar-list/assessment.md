# Private assessment - Cold email setup (10 to 20 warm domains, webinar invites to an old list)

url: https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022102019774886555675
Hourly $12 to $35, part-time, intermediate. Colombia, Bogota time. 61 contracts, $10k spent, 4.75 over 37 reviews; a design and video buyer trying cold email for the first time. 0 hires, 0 invites, 17 connects.

## Time
6 to 10 hours of real work over 2 to 3 calendar weeks. Sizing and list verification 1 to 2 h (25k addresses through a verifier is minutes, the split and the conversation about what got removed is the hour). Domains and mailboxes 1 to 2 h. DNS 1 to 2 h plus one re-check cycle. Instantly workspace, import, warmup, unibox, limits 1 to 2 h. Handover walkthrough 1 h. Then warmup is 2 to 3 weeks of waiting, or days with pre-warmed mailboxes. Bill the wait as light monitoring, 30 min a week.

## Likelihood
Home turf: this is Lorenzo's own infrastructure at 100k a month. The two risks are the client's, not his:
- The list is old. Old lists bounce, and bounces on 20 fresh domains kill them in a week. Verification and removing catch-alls is not optional, and the client should expect to lose 10 to 30 percent of the list. Say it before they see the number.
- Webinar invites are not really cold email if these people opted in once, but to a fresh domain they are cold. Frame the setup as protecting their main domain, which is exactly what they asked for.
- "A login to your software": the Instantly workspace must be in their name on their card, otherwise he owns their sending reputation and their bill. Make that the plan, not a concession.
- 10 to 20 domains is a lot for one webinar. At 15 domains and 45 mailboxes they send 1,350 a day once warm; a 25k list clears in under 3 weeks. Fewer domains and a longer send window is cheaper; ask what the date is.

## Standard pricing
Setup of this size (10 to 20 domains, 30 to 60 mailboxes, verification, DNS, Instantly, handover): $1,500 to $3,500 flat, or $50 to $85 an hour. The post's $12 to $35 band is VA money. Recurring on their card: domains about $12 to $15 a year each, mailboxes $3 to $4 each a month through the provider (so $90 to $240 a month at 30 to 60 mailboxes), Instantly from $37 a month, a verifier run about $20 to $50 for 25k addresses.

## Ask before quoting
1. How many addresses on the list, how old, and where did they come from?
2. When is the webinar? That decides pre-warmed versus warming from scratch.
3. Do they have an Instantly account, or does he create one in their name on their card?
4. Who controls the domain registrar and the card for the mailbox provider?
5. Reply handling: do they want the unibox only, or replies sorted and drafted too?

## Flags
Hourly post; he can propose a fixed setup fee instead. No location requirement. Client's timezone is Bogota (one hour behind Central).
