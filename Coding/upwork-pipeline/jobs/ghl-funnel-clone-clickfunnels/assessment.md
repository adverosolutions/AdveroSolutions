# Assessment (private) - GHL funnel clone from ClickFunnels/WordPress

## Time
- 4 to 6 real hours: landing page rebuild 2 to 3 h, thank you page 0.5 h, assets and fonts 0.5 to 1 h, form and embed 0.5 h, mobile pass 0.5 to 1 h, link QA and test opt-in 0.5 h.
- Calendar: 1 to 2 days, mostly waiting on access and one round of their feedback.

## Likelihood
- High. Two pages and one form is squarely what the GHL builder does well.
- Risks: a custom web font GHL doesn't carry (needs uploading as a custom font); WordPress page-builder effects (animations, custom CSS) that GHL can't copy one to one; images sitting behind ClickFunnels CDN links that must be downloaded, not hotlinked.
- Out of scope and unverified: what happens to a lead after the opt-in. Any autoresponder or email tool connected to ClickFunnels today does not carry over; in GHL that needs a workflow, which the post does not ask for.
- Going live needs a domain or subdomain connected in their GHL account. The post doesn't say which; unverified whether DNS access is theirs to give.

## Standard pricing
- Market for a 2-page funnel clone into GHL with a form and QA: about $150 to $400 fixed. Recurring software: their existing GHL plan, nothing added.
- The post's $15 fixed is far below market; recorded bids average $54.55, also below market.

## Ask before quoting
- The live URL of the current funnel (landing page and thank you page), and whether it's ClickFunnels or WordPress.
- GHL sub-account access (collaborator or staff login), and which funnel/domain it goes live on.
- Opt-in form fields, and whether any lead currently gets an email or tag after opting in.
- Any custom fonts, videos or tracking pixels on the original.

## Flags
- Budget $15 fixed vs about $150 to $400 market.
- "Proven experience migrating or cloning designs into Go High Level" reads like a portfolio ask.
- Australia: time-zone gap for the review round.
- 1 already interviewing: speed of the reply matters.

## Pricing path (Lorenzo only)
- Value: consolidating off ClickFunnels saves the subscription. Assumption: $97 to $297 a month, so about $1,160 to $3,560 a year. The funnel's lead value is not in the post.
- Price range: 10 to 20% of that value, about $120 to $700.
- Land offer (milestone 1): the landing page cloned in GHL, matching the original.
- Upsell path: a GHL workflow that follows up every new opt-in by email/SMS and tags the lead; then move the rest of their marketing systems into GHL.
- Milestones:
  1. 45%, paid once the landing page is rebuilt in GHL with all assets and fonts matched, delivered and you've signed off.
  2. 30%, paid once the thank you page and the opt-in form are built and embedded, delivered and you've signed off.
  3. 25%, paid once the mobile pass, link and button QA and a test opt-in are done and the funnel is live, delivered and you've signed off.
