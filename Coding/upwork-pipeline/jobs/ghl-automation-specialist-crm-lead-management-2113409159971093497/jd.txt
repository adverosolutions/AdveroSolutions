Marvellous Property Services is a growing UK plumbing, heating and property maintenance business looking for an experienced GoHighLevel specialist to improve and automate our CRM and lead-management processes.

We already use GoHighLevel alongside Tradify and QuickBooks. We are not looking for someone to redesign our entire business from scratch. We need someone who can understand our existing setup, identify gaps, and implement practical improvements and automations within GoHighLevel.

Initial areas of focus

The first phase is likely to include:

- Reviewing our current GoHighLevel setup and pipelines.
- Ensuring enquiries from website forms, email, phone calls, missed calls, WhatsApp and other lead sources are captured and tracked consistently.
- Improving lead pipelines and stages so we have a clear view of every live enquiry.
- Building automated follow-up for enquiries that have gone quiet.
- Reviewing and improving missed-call and voicemail workflows.
- Creating internal notifications so important leads or messages are not missed.
- Automating customer communications where appropriate, including confirmations, reminders and follow-ups.
- Reviewing opportunities to reduce duplicate manual data entry between GoHighLevel, Tradify and QuickBooks.
- Creating workflows for review requests, customer follow-up and repeat-business opportunities.
- Improving tagging, segmentation and reporting.
- Identifying where AI can safely assist with lead handling, customer communication or internal administration.
- Testing all workflows thoroughly before they go live.
- Documenting what has been built so the system can be maintained internally.

Longer-term objectives

We want GoHighLevel to become a reliable central hub for customer enquiries, lead management, follow-up and marketing automation.

Longer term, this may include:

- Service reminders for boilers, cylinders and other recurring maintenance.
- Customer reactivation campaigns.
- Automated review and referral journeys.
- Lead-source and conversion reporting.
- KPI dashboards.
- Email and SMS nurturing campaigns.
- AI-assisted customer communication.
- Integration with other systems using native integrations, Zapier, Make or webhooks where appropriate.

What we are looking for

You should have strong hands-on experience with:

- GoHighLevel / HighLevel.
- Workflow and automation building.
- Pipelines and opportunity management.
- SMS and email automations.
- Forms and lead capture.
- Calendars and appointment workflows.
- Triggers, tags and custom fields.
- Reporting and attribution.
- Webhooks / Zapier / Make integrations.
- Troubleshooting existing workflows.
- CRM clean-up and optimisation.

Experience supporting UK service businesses, trades, home services, property maintenance, plumbing, heating, electrical or similar businesses would be a major advantage.

Important

We already have somebody who assists with basic website and CRM edits. This role is specifically for someone with deeper GoHighLevel expertise who can build and improve the automation and operational side of the system.

We would like to start with an audit and an agreed list of priority improvements, then work through implementation in stages.
