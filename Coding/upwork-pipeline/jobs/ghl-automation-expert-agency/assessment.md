## Likelihood (what breaks)
- The If/Else "contact replied" condition: how GHL evaluates it (a reply to
  the last message vs any reply since the workflow started) is NOT tested in
  Lorenzo's account. The chart claims only the pattern; in the build, run a
  test contact through both arms first (step 8 on the chart).
- Which form starts the lead workflow is a per-client decision, set in each
  client sub-account when the workflow is loaded.
- SMS needs a LeadConnector (or Twilio) number per sub-account; US numbers need
  A2P 10DLC registration. Email needs a sending domain for good delivery. Their
  client accounts may not have these done; that is setup time, not build time.
- Snapshots: loading one workflow into many sub-accounts works through an
  agency snapshot; the custom values then need filling per account. Nothing is
  built in Lorenzo's account for this prop, and he has not been shown pushing
  agency-level snapshots for clients: do not claim he has done it before.
- Re-entry: if a lead submits the form twice, a second opportunity may open,
  depending on the account's duplicate-opportunity setting. Unverified.

  An ongoing automation partner at 5 to 10 hours a week: about $1,500 to
  $3,500 a month.
