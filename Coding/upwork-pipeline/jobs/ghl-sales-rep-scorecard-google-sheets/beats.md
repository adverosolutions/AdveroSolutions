# Loom beats - Your Sales Rep Scorecard

- you want a sales rep scorecard in sheets, numbers per rep
- one webhook takes every ghl event, normalized into the same shape
- which event? leads credit the contact owner, appointments do not
- appointments read the calendar's assigned user, the rep who took it
- rep resolved? if not, unassigned queue for a human to fix
- every event appends to the events tab, so numbers stay traceable
- eleven pm rollup, ratios, one row per rep, slack leaderboard
