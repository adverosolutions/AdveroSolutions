Project Overview

TrustInSoft is an embedded software company using Zoho CRM and Zoho Marketing Automation. Our Marketing Automation setup was partially completed, but the person leading the project became unexpectedly unavailable.

Leads are currently not syncing properly from Zoho Marketing Automation to Zoho CRM. We need an experienced Zoho specialist to review the existing configuration, diagnose the problem and ensure the two platforms are working together correctly.

Project Scope

The project will be completed in two phases:

Phase 1: Audit and Recommendations

Review the existing Zoho Marketing Automation configuration

Review forms, field mappings, lead scoring, lists, segments and journeys

Review the integration between Zoho Marketing Automation and Zoho CRM

Diagnose why leads are not syncing properly

Identify anything that is misconfigured, incomplete or creating unnecessary complexity

Provide clear recommendations and priorities before making significant changes

Phase 2: Implementation and Testing

Correct configuration and field-mapping issues

Complete or repair the integration between Marketing Automation and CRM

Ensure leads are assigned, tagged and routed correctly

Review and correct lead-scoring rules and journey triggers where needed

Test the complete lead flow from form submission through Marketing Automation and into CRM

Document the final configuration and any recommended next steps

Required Experience

Strong hands-on experience with Zoho Marketing Automation and Zoho CRM

Demonstrated experience integrating the two platforms

Experience troubleshooting lead-sync and field-mapping issues

Experience configuring forms, journeys, lead scoring, segmentation and automation

Ability to explain technical issues and recommendations clearly

Experience working with B2B marketing and sales processes is preferred

This project specifically requires Zoho Marketing Automation experience. Experience with Zoho CRM alone will not be sufficient.

Location and Availability

U.S.-based candidates are preferred. Candidates outside the U.S. must be available for meetings and collaborative troubleshooting during standard U.S. Mountain Time business hours.

We would like to begin soon. As part of the selection process, shortlisted candidates will be asked to participate in a brief video call to discuss our current challenges and relevant Zoho experience.
