# Loom beat sheet (glance, do not read aloud)

- n8n, apify, snov and openai, one cold email funnel
- daily prospect run, apify scrapes the companies you target
- snov finds the decision maker, then verifies the email is deliverable
- openai reads their site, writes the first line and fit score
- good ones enter your snov campaign, low fit gets logged instead
- replies route themselves: interested to slack, not now pauses, unsubscribe stops
- real risk is deliverability, warmed sending domains matter more than this
