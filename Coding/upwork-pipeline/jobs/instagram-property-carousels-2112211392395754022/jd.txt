Job Title: AI Automation Expert Needed – Automated Instagram Content Creation & Publishing for Real Estate

About the Project

I’m a commercial real estate broker specializing in industrial and commercial properties in South Florida. I’m looking for an experienced AI automation developer to build a system that pulls property listings from my company’s website and automatically creates, schedules, and publishes branded Instagram carousel posts.

Website: https://www.vsre.net

I want a reliable, easy-to-use system that continuously promotes my available listings with minimal manual involvement and low recurring costs. Initially, I need to approve every post before publication, with the option to switch to fully automatic publishing later.

1. Website Integration

The system must:

• Automatically retrieve active property listings, including descriptions, specifications, pricing when available, and photographs.
• Detect new listings and changes to existing listings.
• Automatically exclude properties that are no longer available.
• Keep listing information synchronized with the website.
• Recheck availability before publication to avoid posting outdated listings.

Please review the website and recommend the most reliable integration method, preferably an API or direct data feed where available.

2. Posting Schedule & Listing Rotation

I want two Instagram carousel posts per day, seven days a week—approximately 60 posts per month.

The system must:

• Rotate through all active listings, promoting each property before repeating listings.
• Automatically restart the rotation after all available properties have been promoted.
• Prioritize newly added properties.
• Avoid posting the same property consecutively when other listings are available.
• Generate fresh captions and vary carousel layouts when properties repeat.
• Continue operating without manual restarts.
• Allow me to adjust posting times and prioritize specific properties.

3. Claude AI Integration

I want to use Claude AI to:

• Generate professional Instagram captions.
• Highlight important property features.
• Create relevant hashtags and calls to action.
• Maintain a consistent commercial real estate marketing tone.
• Produce fresh content variations when properties repeat.

All property facts must come from verified website data. AI must never invent specifications, pricing, availability, or other property details. Missing information should be omitted or flagged for my review.

4. Branded Carousel Creation

The system must automatically create professional Instagram carousels using actual property photographs.

Requirements include:

• Incorporating my company’s logo and my contact information.
• Following branding guidelines that I will provide.
• Using approved colors, fonts, and design elements.
• Offering multiple carousel layouts to maintain variety.
• Including property specifications, highlights, and appropriate calls to action.
• Producing clear, readable graphics suitable for professional commercial real estate marketing.
• Preserving the accuracy of property photographs without adding misleading features.

Please provide examples of comparable automated graphics or carousel systems you have developed.

5. Content Approval Dashboard

Initially, every post must require my approval before publication.

I need a dashboard where I can:

• Preview carousel graphics and captions.
• Edit, approve, reject, or regenerate content.
• Approve multiple posts at once.
• Review up to 14 upcoming posts in advance.
• Adjust posting schedules and prioritize properties.
• View approval, scheduling, publishing, and failure statuses.
• Pause and resume the automation.

Unapproved posts must never be published while manual approval mode is enabled. Changes to approved content must require approval again.

I also need the option to switch to fully automatic publishing later without rebuilding the workflow.

6. Email & SMS Notifications

The system must:

• Notify me by email and text when posts are ready for approval.
• Include direct links to the approval dashboard.
• Send reminders for pending approvals.
• Confirm successful publication.
• Alert me to failed posts or workflow errors.
• Let me enable or disable each notification type independently.

Notification frequency should be adjustable to avoid unnecessary messages and costs.

7. Instagram Scheduling & Publishing

The system must use approved Meta APIs or a compatible publishing platform to schedule and publish two approved carousel posts daily.

Please explain any Instagram account requirements, permissions, subscriptions, or setup steps needed.

The workflow must include error handling, duplicate-post prevention, and a process for retrying failed posts. Please explain what happens when a scheduled posting time arrives and no approved content is available.

8. Technology & Deliverables

I’m open to your recommended technology stack, including:

• Claude AI API
• n8n
• Make.com
• Zapier
• Canva or another graphics-generation tool
• Airtable or another database/dashboard solution
• Meta Instagram APIs

I do not require every tool listed above. Please recommend the simplest reliable setup with the lowest practical ongoing cost.

Required deliverables:

• A fully functioning automation system.
• Website listing and photograph integration.
• Automated branded carousel and caption generation.
• Continuous listing rotation.
• Content approval dashboard.
• Instagram scheduling and publishing.
• Email and SMS notifications.
• Error handling and operational alerts.
• Testing and troubleshooting.
• Complete setup and operating documentation.
• Training and a recorded walkthrough.
• Full handover of source code, workflows, templates, configurations, and administrative access.

9. Mandatory Cost Breakdown

Full transparency about ongoing costs is essential. Every proposal must disclose ALL expenses needed to build and operate the system.

Please clearly identify:

• One-time development and setup cost.
• Estimated monthly operating cost for approximately 60 carousel posts.
• Estimated annual operating cost.
• Mandatory versus optional subscriptions.
• Usage-based charges and the assumptions behind your estimates.
• Optional maintenance and support fees.
• Any required ongoing developer involvement.

Include an itemized estimate for:

• Claude AI API usage.
• Automation platform fees.
• Carousel rendering or graphic design tools.
• Dashboard and database subscriptions.
• Hosting and storage.
• Email and SMS notifications.
• Instagram publishing or scheduling services.
• Any additional APIs, licenses, or subscriptions.

Please account for content variations, regenerations, and approval notifications in your estimates.

I strongly prefer minimal recurring expenses and a system that can operate independently after handover without a required monthly payment to the freelancer.

10. Ownership & Access

All accounts, subscriptions, workflows, code, templates, and configurations must be owned and controlled by me or my company.

The system must be built in accounts I control, and I must receive full administrative access. Any third-party licensing restrictions must be disclosed before development begins.

The system must continue operating without relying on the freelancer’s personal accounts or continued involvement.

11. Questions to Answer in Your Proposal

Please answer the following:

1. Have you built a similar automation system? Please provide examples and explain your role.
2. Which technology stack do you recommend and why?
3. After reviewing vsre.net, how would you retrieve and synchronize the property listings and photographs?
4. How would you integrate Claude AI while preventing inaccurate property information?
5. How would you generate professional, branded Instagram carousels?
6. How would you implement continuous listing rotation and prioritize new properties?
7. How would you build the approval dashboard and email/SMS notifications?
8. How would you handle unavailable properties, missed approvals, failed posts, and duplicate prevention?
9. What Instagram account setup and permissions would be required?
10. What is your estimated timeline and fixed development price?
11. What are all anticipated recurring costs? Please provide an itemized estimate.
12. Can the system operate independently after handover without ongoing payments to you?
13. Will I receive complete ownership, source files, and administrative access?

12. Project Structure & Expectations

I’m looking for a complete, turnkey solution under a fixed-price agreement, with milestone payments tied to clear deliverables.

Suggested milestones:

• Website integration and listing synchronization.
• Branded carousel and caption generation.
• Approval dashboard and notifications.
• Instagram publishing and end-to-end testing.
• Documentation, training, and final handover.

A working demonstration and successful end-to-end testing are required before final payment.

This project is for building an automation system. Ongoing social media management is outside the scope.

Please review https://www.vsre.net before submitting your proposal and include your proposed integration approach, timeline, fixed development cost, and complete recurring cost breakdown.
