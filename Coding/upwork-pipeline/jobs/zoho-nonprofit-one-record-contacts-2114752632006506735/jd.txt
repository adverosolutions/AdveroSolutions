About the project

NAMI Broward County is a mental health nonprofit seeking an experienced Zoho specialist to help configure our relationship management system.

We already use Zoho One, Google Workspace for Nonprofits, and AppSheet. Our Office Manager is developing AppSheet applications for program operations and will serve as your internal project lead.

We need you to assess our existing setup, design and implement the agreed Zoho configuration, connect priority workflows, and train our internal lead and a backup administrator.

What we need the system to support

We need one contact record per person, with the ability to hold multiple roles and maintain related activity history:

- Participants: Program connections, relevant demographics, and links to surveys, outcomes, and results.
- Volunteers: Assignments and volunteering activity.
- Facilitators: Program assignments and facilitation history.
- Employees: Basic directory information and employment type.
- Sponsors: Contributions linked to events or purposes.
- Donors: Donation history linked to the appropriate person or organization.
- Community and mental health network contacts: Organizational affiliations, relationship type, and partnership information.
- Board members: Positions and terms.

We also need organization records for agencies, schools, businesses, and community partners, linked to the relevant people.

User permissions must determine who can view, edit, export, and administer different information.

Existing systems

- Zoho One: Existing subscription; Zoho CRM and Forms are relevant to this project.
- Google Workspace: Shared documents, spreadsheets, forms, email, and collaboration.
- AppSheet: Applications being developed internally for contacts, education enrollment, attendance, support groups, and other operational processes.

Your assessment must account for these existing systems and recommend where each type of record should be maintained. We need to avoid competing contact databases and unnecessary duplicate entry.

Stage 1: Assessment and detailed design

Work with our internal lead to:

- Review the current configuration, data structures, and workflows.
- Recommend the Zoho CRM structure, including modules, fields, relationships, and permissions.
- Identify the master source for each type of record.
- Define how contacts, program activity, surveys, and other relevant records connect.
- Recommend duplicate-management rules and consistent identifiers.
- Identify priority integrations and any recurring costs.
- Produce a written implementation scope, timeline, and itemized quote.

Stage 2: Implementation, testing, and handover

Following approval of the detailed scope:

- Configure the selected CRM components.
- Implement agreed forms, automations, and integrations.
- Prepare and import agreed existing datasets.
- Test record matching, permissions, reporting, and integration failures.
- Create usable staff views and agreed reports.
- Document the configuration and routine maintenance procedures.
- Provide hands-on administrator training and recorded walkthroughs.
- Complete handover to our internal lead and a backup administrator.

Detailed enrollment and attendance workflows may remain in AppSheet. We will agree during discovery on what information Zoho needs to exchange with those systems.

Expected results

Staff should be able to find a person or organization and understand their relevant relationships with NAMI. A person who participates, volunteers, and donates should remain one contact with connected records.

Our internal team should be able to operate the agreed workflows, resolve common issues, and make routine configuration changes after handover.

Budget and contracting approach

Our target budget is up to $5,500 for consulting, implementation, documentation, and training.

Please price Stage 1 separately and explain what implementation you reasonably expect to deliver within the overall budget. Identify additional costs, exclusions, and recurring subscriptions separately.

We intend to use milestones with clear deliverables and acceptance criteria. The full implementation scope will be agreed after discovery; the requirements above describe our intended system and priorities.

Required experience

- Zoho CRM and relevant Zoho One applications.
- Data modeling, migration, deduplication, and permissions.
- Zoho automation and integration tools, including APIs, Deluge, or Zoho Flow as appropriate.
- Google Sheets or Google Workspace integrations.
- Client training and technical documentation.

AppSheet and nonprofit experience are strongly preferred.
