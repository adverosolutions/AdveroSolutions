About us
We are a multi-location plastic surgery practice in NYC with sister brands in hair restoration, longevity and medical weight loss.

Zoho One is our operating system: CRM (Leads, Contacts, Deals, custom modules), Books, Creator, Flow and Analytics, plus RingCentral, Meta Lead Ads, Netlify, Render and a set of custom apps we have built with Claude Code.

We already automate a lot. We want someone who can take over the backlog, harden what exists, and keep building with AI as the primary tool.

What you will do
- Build and maintain Zoho CRM automations: workflow rules, Blueprints, Deluge functions, custom modules, field updates, webhooks and COQL reports
- Connect Zoho to the rest of the stack through REST APIs and serverless functions (Netlify, Render) in Node or Python
- Use Claude Code as your daily driver to build internal tools: Next.js dashboards, FastAPI services, Telegram and Slack bots, and MCP servers against Zoho
- Redesign business processes with the team, then implement them: lead routing and speed-to-lead, quote-to-invoice sync, payment reconciliation, follow-up drips and do-not-contact compliance
- Build dashboards and reports (Zoho Analytics or custom) that the owner and managers actually use
- Write clear docs and runbooks for everything you ship
- Debug production issues: failed webhooks, duplicate records, stuck sync jobs and timeouts

Must have

- Real experience building software with Claude Code or Cursor. You can show a repo or a working app
