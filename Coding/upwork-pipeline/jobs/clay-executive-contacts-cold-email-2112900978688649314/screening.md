3. In one sentence each, what do SPF, DKIM, and DMARC do?
SPF lists which servers are allowed to send email for your domain.
DKIM adds a signature to each email so the receiving server can check it really came from your domain and wasn't changed.
DMARC tells receiving servers what to do with email that fails SPF or DKIM, and sends you reports about it.
