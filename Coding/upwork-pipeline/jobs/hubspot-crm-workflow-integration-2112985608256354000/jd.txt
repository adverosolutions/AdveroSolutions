About the Project:

Studio Leonelli is a boutique U.S. law firm seeking an experienced HubSpot specialist to improve our CRM data quality, lead management processes, and integration with existing business systems.

We are looking for someone who can do more than configure HubSpot. The ideal contractor understands CRM architecture, integrations, workflow design, and user adoption—and can develop practical solutions that work for a small, fast-moving professional services organization.

The project has two phases. We are initially hiring for Phase 1, with the potential to extend the engagement into Phase 2 based on demonstrated capabilities and project needs.

Phase 1: HubSpot Data Hygiene & Lead Management (Immediate Priority)

Our primary challenge involves capturing and maintaining accurate contact and lead information in HubSpot.

Many of our new business relationships originate through our founding partner's Outlook email. However, the integration between Mac, Outlook, and HubSpot has created workflow challenges, resulting in inconsistent contact creation, tagging, and activity logging.

Our firm administrator subsequently attempts to complete or correct records, but often lacks the original relationship context needed to properly classify contacts.

We need someone who can diagnose this problem and implement a sustainable solution—not simply perform a one-time data cleanup.

Phase 1 Responsibilities

- Assess the current HubSpot configuration, data structure, lead pipeline, and Outlook integration.

- Troubleshoot the Mac/Outlook/HubSpot integration and recommend practical alternatives where appropriate.

- Establish consistent contact properties, relationship classifications, lead sources, referral attribution, and tagging standards.

- Design a streamlined process for capturing new contacts and leads, particularly from email introductions and referrals.

- Ensure emails, meetings, and other relevant activities are appropriately logged and associated with CRM records.

- Identify and resolve duplicate, incomplete, or incorrectly categorized records.

- Clarify responsibilities between the founding partner and firm administrator.

- Implement appropriate workflows, automation, and quality controls to maintain clean data.

- Document the new process and provide practical training to ensure ongoing adoption.

Phase 1 Deliverables:

- Assessment of current CRM configuration and integration issues.

- Recommended and implemented lead-capture workflow.

- Standardized contact properties, tagging, and classification structure.

- Cleanup of agreed-upon priority records.

- Reliable activity logging and follow-up process.

- Documented SOP and brief user training.

- Recommendations for ongoing data governance and Phase 2 readiness.

Timeline: We would like Phase 1 substantially completed before our founding partner's international business development trip in early November.

Phase 2: Business Development Automation & Systems Integration (Potential Extension)

Following Phase 1, we plan to expand HubSpot's capabilities for relationship management, marketing, and business development.

We are also implementing Trello for project management and need to establish coordinated workflows between HubSpot, Trello, Clio, and OneDrive.

Potential Phase 2 responsibilities include:

- Building post-event and business development follow-up workflows.

- Contact segmentation and relationship nurturing.

- Email marketing, newsletters, and automated communications.

- Business development reporting and pipeline visibility.

- Designing HubSpot-to-Trello client conversion and onboarding handoffs.

- Coordinating workflow and record references between HubSpot, Trello, and Clio.

- Maintaining data integrity across integrated systems.

- Implementing automation using native integrations or appropriate third-party tools.

Our intended systems architecture is:

HubSpot: Leads, contacts, relationship management, and business development.

Trello: Project management, task assignments, workflow tracking, and accountability.

Clio: Legal matters, timekeeping, activities, and billing.

OneDrive: Authoritative client document repository.

We want these systems to work together without unnecessary data duplication. 

Experience with Clio, Trello, Microsoft 365, and cross-platform workflow automation would be particularly valuable for Phase 2.

Phase 2 is planned, but continuation with the Phase 1 contractor will depend on the individual's capabilities and mutual fit.
