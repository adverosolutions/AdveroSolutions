# Assessment (private) - GHL landing page + form

## Time
- Landing page in GHL funnel builder from their content, desktop and mobile passes: 3-4 h.
- Form (multi-step or single) with validation, consent line, success state, embed on their site: 1.5-2 h.
- Lead routing workflow (form submitted: tag by source, opportunity, internal SMS/email, confirmation): 1 h.
- Revisions round: 1-2 h. Total 6-9 h, 2-3 calendar days from content in hand.

## Likelihood
- High for the page and form; GHL builder handles all of it.
- Unverified: their main website platform (iframe embed works on any builder that accepts HTML, but some locked site builders need a paid tier for custom code). Their GHL plan and whether a domain is connected for the funnel. Whether A2P is registered (internal SMS alerts need it; email alerts work regardless).
- Risk: "we have the content" may mean rough copy; headline and CTA usually need a rewrite to convert.

## Standard pricing
- Market for a short GHL landing page + form: $300-800. With lead routing workflow: $500-1,000.
- Post is $150 fixed, matching the bid average; well under market. The workflow is the natural extra.

## Ask before quoting
- What platform is the main website on, and do we get access or do they paste the embed?
- Is a domain connected in GHL for the page?
- Where should leads go and who gets notified (SMS, email, which pipeline)?
- Do they have logo, brand colors, photos, reviews?
- What is the date for "quick turnaround"?

## Flags
- US client. Portfolio demand ("share examples"): the prototype answers it. Client record not pulled.

## Pricing path (Lorenzo only)
- Value: assumption, a local service business converting 10 extra leads a month at a $1,500 job and 25% close = about $45k a year in added revenue (all numbers assumed, none in the post).
- 10-20% of that is $4.5k-9k, far above what this buyer expects; realistic land offer is the posted $150 page, with routing as the upsell.
- Land offer (milestone 1): landing page built from their content.
- Upsell: form routing workflow, A/B headline test, extra pages, ongoing CRO retainer.
- Milestones: 1) landing page, 50%, paid once the page is delivered and you've signed off. 2) form on page and main website, 25%, paid once both embeds are delivered and you've signed off. 3) lead routing and notifications, 25%, paid once a test lead lands correctly and you've signed off.
