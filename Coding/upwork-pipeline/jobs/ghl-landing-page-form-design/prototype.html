04 Why it converts

Every section has one job.

Hover a number to see what each part of the page is doing for you.

  1. 01

    A headline that names the outcome

    It says what the visitor gets, not who the company is. They know in a few seconds whether they're in the right place.

  2. 02

    One action, no exits

    No menu to wander off into. The phone number and the button both lead to the same quote.

  3. 03

    Proof beside the ask

    Rating, license and warranty sit right next to the form, where the doubt happens.

  4. 04

    A form with almost no friction

    The easiest question first and contact details last. Three short steps feel shorter than six fields at once.

  5. 05

    A second ask for the readers

    People who read everything first get the same single offer at the bottom, with nothing new to decide.
