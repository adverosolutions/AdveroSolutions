AI Prompt Engineer Needed for Sales Platform Features (Call Review, Compliance, Chatbots)
Hourly $15-35, part-time, intermediate. Client: NexGrowth, a done-for-you high-ticket sales agency with its own platform. Saved 2026-10-02. Avg bid $32. Ongoing. Required: proposal starts with "PROMPTED"; answer three questions (a live prompt and how it was tested; approach to a call-scoring and coaching prompt; models used most). US Eastern overlap.

About NexGrowth
NexGrowth is a done-for-you high-ticket sales agency. We've built our own platform that runs our sales operation end to end, and AI powers a growing number of its features. We need someone who can make those features sharp, reliable, and consistent.

The Role
You'll write, test, and refine the prompts behind the AI features in our platform. You won't be writing code. Our development team handles implementation. You own the thinking: what the AI is told, how it reasons, what it outputs, and how we know it's working.

What You'll Work On
- Sales call review: prompts that score call transcripts, identify missed opportunities, flag objection-handling gaps, and give reps specific coaching feedback
- Data analysis: prompts that turn pipeline, performance, and campaign data into clear summaries and actionable insights
- Compliance: prompts that review calls and messages for risky claims, missing disclosures, or off-script language
- Chatbots: conversational agents for candidates, partners, and customers that stay on-brand, on-task, and within guardrails
- New features as we build them

What You'll Do
- Write production-ready prompts with clear instructions, examples, and structured outputs (e.g. JSON) our developers can plug in directly
- Build test sets of real-world examples and edge cases, and check prompt output against them
- Iterate based on results and feedback from our team, and document what changed and why
- Keep a clean, versioned library of every prompt in the platform
- Flag where a feature needs a different approach rather than a better prompt

What We're Looking For
- Proven experience writing prompts that are used in real products, not just personal ChatGPT use
- Experience with structured outputs, multi-step workflows, and handling edge cases
- A testing mindset. You don't call a prompt done until you've tried to break it
- Clear written communication and solid documentation habits
- Ability to work independently and take feedback quickly
- Bonus: understanding of sales processes, call coaching, or compliance review

Timeline & Working Style
We have several features ready for prompt work now and more on the roadmap, so this is ongoing. You'll work directly with our ops and development team. Must be fluent in English and able to overlap with US Eastern business hours.

How to Apply
Start your proposal with the word "PROMPTED" so we know you read this. Then tell us:
1. A prompt you've written that's used in a live product, what it does, and how you tested it
2. How you would approach a prompt that scores a sales call transcript and gives the rep coaching feedback
3. Which AI models you've worked with most

Generic proposals will be skipped.
