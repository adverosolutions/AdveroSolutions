## Pay-per-meeting economics

**Market, 2026, published pricing guides (agency side):**
- Pure pay-per-meeting: about $150 to $600 per booked or held meeting for SMB and mid-market B2B, with $300 to $600 the common quote for reputable vendors; enterprise meetings run higher.
- Hybrid: a base retainer (agencies quote $2,000 to $4,000 a month) plus $150 to $400 per meeting.
- Sources: [Outbound Pros](https://outboundpros.io/blog/b2b-appointment-setting-pricing-2026), [SalesHive](https://saleshive.com/blog/pay-per-meeting-models-best-practices-deals), [IntentSignal](https://intentsignal.ai/content/cold-email-agency-pricing), [LeadHaste](https://leadhaste.com/blog/cold-email-agency-pricing-2026), [Cleverly](https://www.cleverly.co/blog/pay-per-appointment-lead-generation). Agency marketing pages, so treat as the upper reference, not a survey.

**Defensible range for this deal: $150 to $350 per held, qualified meeting.** He sits at the low end of the agency band because the client already owns the infrastructure and pays the tools. Pure performance with no setup fee justifies $250 to $400, the top of the range and a bit past it, because he funds the ramp. Anything under about $100 means he is subsidizing their pipeline.

**What he would actually earn (planning assumption, not a measured benchmark):** in a crowded category with a 7-point definition, plan on roughly 1 held, qualified meeting per 1,000 to 2,500 sends once copy is tuned. At about 9,000 sends a week that is 3 to 8 a week at full volume, and 0 to 2 a week in the first month. At $200 that is $600 to $1,600 a week for 8 to 15 hours once it runs, and $0 for the 25 to 40 setup hours plus 3 to 7 weeks of ramp. That unpaid ramp is the whole argument for a setup fee.

- Upwork mechanics: the post is listed hourly. Per-meeting billing on Upwork means fixed-price milestones (for example one milestone per week of held meetings, from the report). Confirm on the call.

**No-show and cancellation policy to propose (client friendly, used in the screening draft):**

- The client flags a disputed meeting within 48 hours of the call, naming the criterion that failed; unflagged meetings count.
