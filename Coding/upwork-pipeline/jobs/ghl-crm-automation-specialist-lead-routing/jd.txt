Part-Time GoHighLevel (GHL) CRM & Automation Specialist

We are looking for a reliable GoHighLevel (GHL) CRM & Automation Specialist to help manage, build, troubleshoot, and improve our CRM systems and client automations.

This is a part-time contractor role for someone who already has hands-on experience with GoHighLevel. We do not need someone who is learning GHL from scratch.

What You'll Be Doing
Setting up and managing GoHighLevel accounts and subaccounts
Building workflows and automations
Creating lead routing systems
Setting up sales pipelines and opportunity stages
Building forms, surveys, and lead capture systems
Connecting website leads and forms into GHL
Setting up email automations
Setting up SMS automations
Creating appointment and calendar workflows
Setting up lead notifications for sales teams
Assigning leads based on location, service, salesperson, or other rules
Building follow-up and lead nurturing sequences
Creating missed-call text-back automations
Managing tags, custom fields, triggers, and contact segmentation
Troubleshooting workflows that are not working correctly
Testing automations before they go live
Connecting GHL with third-party systems when needed
Working with Zapier, Make, webhooks, APIs, or similar tools
Helping with other CRM systems when required
Cleaning up and organizing existing CRM accounts
Documenting important workflows and processes

What We Are Looking For
You should have real experience working inside GoHighLevel. You should understand: GHL Workflows, Triggers and Actions, Pipelines, Opportunities, Lead Routing, Calendars, Forms and Surveys, Email and SMS Automation, Tags and Custom Fields, Contact Management, Subaccounts, User Permissions, Webhooks, Zapier or Make, Basic API integrations, CRM troubleshooting.

Experience working with marketing agencies or lead-generation businesses is a strong plus.

Important
We are looking for someone who can receive a request such as:
"When this lead comes in, determine which service they need, assign them to the correct salesperson, add them to the correct pipeline, send a notification, and start the appropriate follow-up sequence."
You should be able to understand the request, determine the best way to build it in GHL, implement it, test it, and make sure it works correctly.
We need someone who can help solve problems, not simply follow step-by-step instructions.

Other CRM Experience
Experience with other CRM and automation platforms is helpful, including: HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Zoho, Salesforce, Zapier, Make, WordPress forms, Facebook Lead Ads, Google Ads lead forms, Webhooks and APIs. You do not need experience with every platform.
