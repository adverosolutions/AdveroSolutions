Project Background

We are a custom manufacturing company based in China, targeting manufacturing, engineering, and product development companies in Europe.

We already have an automated workflow using n8n, Google Sheets, and Smartlead to research company websites and generate personalized outreach emails.

Our current challenges:

As a China-based company, we have difficulties registering international Microsoft 365 business email accounts.
Our emails frequently land in Spam/Junk, especially when recipients use Microsoft 365 / Outlook.
Our SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication is properly configured and passing. We need to improve actual email deliverability.
Scope of Work
1. Email Infrastructure & Deliverability (Primary Requirement)
Provide or help set up reliable business email accounts, preferably Microsoft 365.
Pre-warmed mailboxes would be a plus, but the sending domains must be professional and neutral, not obviously unrelated to our manufacturing business.
Manage email warmup, deliverability optimization, bounce issues, and ongoing maintenance.
Regularly send real test emails from the actual sending accounts to Microsoft 365 / Outlook and Gmail test inboxes, and verify whether they land in Inbox, Spam/Junk, or get rejected.
We do not want to rely solely on warmup tool scores or reported inbox rates. Actual email delivery tests and results are essential.
2. Lead Generation (Optional, but Preferred for Long-Term Cooperation)
Find suitable companies based on our target industries, countries, and criteria.
Collect company websites, relevant decision-makers, job titles, and verified business email addresses.
Deliver structured, accurate data while avoiding duplicates.
3. Smartlead Access & Management

We can use our existing Smartlead account or an account provided by the service provider.

In either case, we must have our own login access and be able to independently manage campaigns, contacts, and email sending.

Long-Term Cooperation & Pricing

We are particularly interested in a long-term partner who can provide both Email Deliverability and B2B Lead Generation services.
