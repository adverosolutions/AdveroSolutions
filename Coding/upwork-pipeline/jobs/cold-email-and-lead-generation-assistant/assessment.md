# Delivery assessment (private, not for the proposal)

- **Time:** 10 to 14 hours to a working pipeline, 1 to 2 calendar weeks. Most of it is in the client's Hunter account and criteria doc, not in n8n: turning "the criteria I give you" into a written rubric Claude can score against, mapping title rules per segment, and tuning the confidence cutoff on the first 50 companies so rejects are real rejects. Ongoing after that: a few hours a week reviewing the digest and the low-confidence queue, plus occasional domain setup.
- **Likelihood:** High on the build, medium on accuracy in week one. Hunter's Leads and Campaigns API exists but the campaign-add and lead-search calls need his plan tier and API key; dedupe against existing campaign leads depends on those calls returning the full list. Decision-maker by title is the weak link for local service businesses (owners often have no Hunter record), so expect a manual-review lane. The Apollo source is a placeholder; he may want Google Maps or a CSV.
- **Standard pricing:** Posted at hourly, avg bid ~$19/hr, high $65, starting ~5 hrs/week. Market for a real lead-gen ops person with cold-email infrastructure experience is $40 to $75/hr; a pure list-builder VA is $8 to $20/hr. Recurring: Hunter plan (~$49 to $149/mo), Apollo or Maps scraper (~$50 to $100/mo), Anthropic API (a few dollars a day at this volume).
- **Ask before quoting:** which Hunter plan and whether API access is on; how the criteria are written today (doc, examples, or in his head); which source he trusts for candidates; how many segments and campaigns exist; whether new sending domains are on Google Workspace or another host (SPF/DKIM/DMARC setup differs); expected weekly volume so the confidence cutoff can be set.
