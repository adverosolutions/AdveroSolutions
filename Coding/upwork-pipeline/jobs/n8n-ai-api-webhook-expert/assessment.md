## Ask before quoting
- Which workflow hurts most right now, and what does it do?
- n8n Cloud or self-hosted, and which version?
- Which apps: CRM, chat, sheets, email, anything without an n8n node?
- How many records a day or week go through the busiest workflow?
- Do existing workflows have an error workflow or any logging today?
- Who maintains them after handover: them, a VA, or us?

- Vague post, no business named, record not pulled.
