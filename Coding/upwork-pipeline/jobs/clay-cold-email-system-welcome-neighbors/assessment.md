# Private assessment - Cold Email + Clay Expert, "Welcome Neighbors"

## Time
- Infra setup: domains, mailboxes, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, warmup, Instantly workspace, unibox. 8 to 12 hours of real work, then 2 to 3 weeks of warmup calendar time before volume.
- Clay build: tables, Google Maps or Apollo source, enrichment waterfall, owner finder, verification, AI scoring and first-line columns, push to the email platform. 15 to 25 hours, spread over 1.5 to 2 weeks because provider credits and column tests run overnight.
- Sequences, segmentation by trade and metro, reply routing into GoHighLevel, scorecard: 8 to 12 hours.
- First real sends in week 3, first honest read on reply rate in week 5 to 6. Do not promise numbers before week 6.
- Ongoing operator role after launch: 10 to 20 hours a week if they actually scale metro by metro. "Full-time" in the post is a flag; confirm what they mean.

## Likelihood
- Highest-risk item is honesty, not build risk. **Clay is unverified experience for Lorenzo.** The post asks for Clay workflow examples and Clay table screenshots by name, and he does not have them. Do not assert Clay experience, do not show a Clay screenshot he did not build. The honest frame: his own agency's system does every step on their list at 100k+ emails a month, with Clay sitting in the canvas as the enrichment layer, and the Loom shows the real pipeline end to end. If they want Clay-native and only Clay-native, he loses that one and it costs nothing.
- Second risk: a client at 382 contracts and $224k spent hires fast and cheap. Two interviews are already done and 6 invites are out. He is behind on timing, so the Loom is the only thing that jumps the queue.
- Third risk: they want reply and appointment rates in the application. **He must supply his own real numbers or decline to quote rates.** Do not invent a positive reply rate or an appointment rate. Safe, verified things he can say: 100k+ cold emails a month on Instantly for his own agency, an agent researching and writing each message, mailboxes through a provider at $3 to $4 each, 30 sends a day per mailbox, 3 inboxes per domain, one unibox, daily metrics dashboard. If pushed for rates, say the number depends on offer and list and he will report theirs weekly from the scorecard.
- Fourth risk: deliverability is on him but the offer is theirs. If "reach new movers" does not land with HVAC owners, no infrastructure saves it. Get their offer and price before quoting a monthly.
- Fifth: Clay credits, email verification credits and mailbox costs are real recurring spend on their card, not his. Say so early so a $5 to $25 hourly does not silently absorb tooling.

## Standard pricing
- Market for this role, someone who owns infra plus Clay plus copy plus optimization: $40 to $85 an hour, or $3k to $6k for the build plus $1.5k to $3k a month to run it.
- Their posted range is $5 to $25 an hour, full-time. The top of their range is roughly a third of market for the senior version of this job, and the bottom is VA pricing. They are shopping for a Clay VA at photo-editor rates. The play is to be the operator, not the VA: the Loom shows a system they cannot get from a $8 an hour Clay tinkerer, and the call decides scope and rate.
- Recurring software on their card, rough: Clay from about $150 to $800 a month depending on credits, Instantly or Smartlead $97 to $358, mailboxes $3 to $4 each times 20 to 60, domains $12 a year each, verification credits. Budget $600 to $1,500 a month in tooling at real volume.

## Ask before quoting
- Do they already have a Clay account, which plan and how many credits, and would Lorenzo get a seat or build in their workspace?
- Instantly or Smartlead, and is an account already running or starting from zero?
- How many metros and how many leads a week do they want in the pipe? That sets mailbox count and the whole cost line.
- Who owns the sending domains and who pays for mailboxes, domains and enrichment credits?
- What data sources are in play today, Google Maps, Apollo, LinkedIn, a purchased list?
- GoHighLevel or another CRM for booked calls, and who works the replies once they land?
- What exactly is the Welcome Neighbors offer to a local business, and what does it cost the business? Copy and reply rate live or die on that.
- Hours per week expected, and is this hourly through Upwork forever or does it move to a monthly retainer once it runs?
