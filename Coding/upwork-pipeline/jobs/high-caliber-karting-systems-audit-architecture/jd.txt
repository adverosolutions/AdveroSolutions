High Caliber Karting & Entertainment is looking for a hands-on Business Systems & AI Automation Engineer to help us map, integrate, and automate a large technology stack used across our entertainment venue.

We currently operate dozens of platforms across POS, online booking, website, CRM, payments, staff management, maintenance, marketing, communications, networking, security, reporting, and physical venue controls. Our systems include platforms such as BMI Leisure, Toast, HubSpot, WordPress/Elementor, Stripe, PayPal, QuickBooks, Deputy, MaintainX, Twilio, Jotform, Ubiquiti/UniFi, Google Admin, and several lighting and facility-control applications.

This is not primarily a chatbot project and not a simple Zapier setup. We need someone who can understand how an operating business works, determine how our systems should communicate, and then help build the right architecture.

The initial engagement will be a paid technology stack audit and automation architecture project, expected to take approximately 10–20 hours.

Your first responsibilities will include:

Review and validate our current software and technology inventory
Understand how each platform is used operationally
Identify APIs, webhooks, native integrations, and other programmatic access
Determine where n8n, Make, custom code, browser automation, or AI agents are appropriate
Identify duplicate data entry and repetitive manual processes
Identify systems that should remain human-controlled
Map how information should flow between systems
Identify security, permissions, logging, retry, and failure-handling requirements
Recommend which systems should be retained, integrated, automated, consolidated, replaced, or supplemented with custom software
Develop a phased implementation roadmap based on value, complexity, and risk

We want someone who can go beyond no-code tools when necessary, using technologies such as n8n, REST APIs, webhooks, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, databases, and AI integrations.

At the same time, we do not want custom software built simply because it can be built. We want the simplest, most reliable solution for each business problem.

The ideal person thinks in terms of:

What is the source of truth?
Why is this task being done manually?
Can these systems communicate directly?
What should happen when an automation fails?
Does AI actually belong in this workflow?
Should we integrate this system or eventually replace it?

If the initial architecture phase goes well, there is significant opportunity for ongoing implementation work, including POS and booking integrations, pricing synchronization, website automation, CRM workflows, reporting, kiosks, marketing systems, customer communications, internal tools, AI-assisted workflows, browser automation, and potentially custom guest-facing applications.

We are looking for a builder and systems thinker, not just an advisor.
