  - Sending domains, mailboxes, DNS, Instantly settings, warm-up started: 3 to 4 h. Warm-up takes 2 to 3 calendar weeks; pre-warmed mailboxes cut that to days at a higher mailbox price.
  - First two industry lists (research, uniform-buyer check, decision-maker, verify, clean, current customers suppressed): 8 to 12 h.

- Ongoing: 6 to 10 hours a week, about 26 to 43 a month. New lists 3 to 5 h, tests and campaign changes 1 to 2 h, daily reply check and handoff about 15 minutes a day, report 1 h.

## Sizing (method, with their input)
- The post gives no list size or volume. Their input: Your Monthly Company Target.
- Method: one decision-maker per company, 3 emails, about 21 sending weekdays a month, about 25 sends per mailbox a day, 3 mailboxes per domain.
- Sends a weekday = target x 3 / 21 = target / 7. Mailboxes = target / 175. Domains = target / 525.
- One mailbox per 175 companies a month. On the chart (step 7) and in beat 6.
- Never their main domain. On the chart (band 3 note) and in beat 5.
