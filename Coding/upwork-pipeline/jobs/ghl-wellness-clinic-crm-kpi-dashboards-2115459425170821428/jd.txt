We are a growing medical wellness clinic seeking an experienced GoHighLevel (GHL) specialist to optimize our CRM, build automated workflows, and create trackable dashboards that measure business performance.

We currently use GoHighLevel and have implemented several basic workflows, calendars, and CRM tools. We are looking for someone who can help take our system to the next level.

Our goal is to automate repetitive tasks, improve lead conversion and patient retention, and gain clear visibility into our key business metrics.

Scope of Work

1. CRM & Workflow Automation

- Review and optimize our current GoHighLevel setup.
- Build automated lead follow-up sequences and appointment-booking workflows.
- Create workflows for appointment reminders, missed appointments, patient follow-ups, and genuine patient feedback requests.
- Develop sales pipelines for different services.
- Implement patient retention, membership tracking, and reactivation workflows.

2. KPI Dashboards & Reporting

- Build dashboards to track leads, appointments, conversion rates, and revenue.
- Track marketing performance and lead sources.
- Monitor membership growth, cancellations, and retention.
- Create simple executive-level dashboards for weekly and monthly business reviews.

3. Integrations & Optimization

- Connect relevant tools and platforms where appropriate.
- Identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency.
- Ensure automations function correctly without duplication or conflicting workflows.
- Provide basic documentation and training so our team can manage the system independently.

Ideal Candidate

- Demonstrated expertise with GoHighLevel CRM, workflows, and reporting.
- Experience building CRM systems for established businesses.
- Strong understanding of sales pipelines, conversion tracking, and marketing automation.
- Ability to create accurate, actionable KPI dashboards.
- Experience working with healthcare, wellness clinics, or membership-based businesses is a plus.
- Familiarity with HIPAA and applicable patient-data privacy requirements is preferred.
- Strong communication skills and the ability to work independently.

Project Expectations

We are looking for someone who can evaluate our current setup, recommend improvements, and implement a scalable system.

We prefer to structure this as a milestone-based project, beginning with an initial audit and implementation plan, followed by automation development, dashboard creation, testing, and training.

There may be opportunities for ongoing support as our business grows.

To Apply

Please include:

- A brief description of your GoHighLevel experience.
- Examples of CRM automations and KPI dashboards you have personally built.
- Any experience working with healthcare or membership-based businesses.
- Your estimated project pricing and availability.

We are looking for a long-term technical partner who understands business operations, not simply someone who can build individual workflows.
