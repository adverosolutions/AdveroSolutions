Description:
We are seeking an experienced, hands-on AI Agent Developer to help design, build, integrate, and deploy intelligent AI agents that automate core insurance operational processes.

Our objective is to move beyond traditional workflow automation by implementing AI agents capable of independently receiving, interpreting, processing, and executing operational transactions across multiple systems, with human intervention reserved for exceptions, complex decisions, and activities requiring underwriting authority.

The developer will partner closely with our Operations, Underwriting, and Technology teams to turn existing manual processes into scalable, AI-driven workflows.

Initial Areas of Focus

1. Inspection Ordering
Automate the identification of policies requiring inspections based on established business rules.
Initiate and submit inspection orders to appropriate vendors.
Track inspection status, retrieve completed reports, and update internal systems.
Identify exceptions and escalate issues requiring human intervention.

2. Premium Audit Ordering
Identify policies requiring premium audits and determine appropriate ordering timelines.
Automatically initiate audit requests with designated vendors.
Track audit status, outstanding requirements, and completion.
Generate reminders and follow-up activities.
Manage routine exceptions and escalate when necessary.


Technical Capabilities Required

We are looking for someone with demonstrated experience in:
Developing and deploying production-ready AI agents using LLMs and agentic AI frameworks.
Building autonomous, multistep workflows capable of interacting with enterprise applications.
Integrating AI agents with internal systems, APIs, databases, email platforms, and third-party vendors.
Leveraging intelligent document processing, OCR, and unstructured data extraction.
Combining AI reasoning with deterministic business rules and workflow automation.
Implementing human-in-the-loop approvals, exception management, audit trails, and security controls.
Monitoring agent performance, accuracy, transaction completion, and operational effectiveness.
Experience with commercial insurance, policy administration systems, underwriting workbenches, and premium audit processes is strongly preferred.

Expected Outcomes
The goal is to deliver working AI agents that execute transactions from beginning to end, rather than proof-of-concept demonstrations.

Success will be measured by:
Reduction in manual processing and repetitive operational tasks.
Increased straight-through processing rates.
Improved transaction accuracy and turnaround times.
Reduction in underwriting and operational administrative workload.
Ability to scale transaction volumes without proportionate staffing increases.
Clear visibility into agent activity, exceptions, and processing results.
The ideal candidate is a hands-on builder who can assess existing processes, architect practical solutions, integrate with our technology environment, and take AI agents from concept through production deployment.
