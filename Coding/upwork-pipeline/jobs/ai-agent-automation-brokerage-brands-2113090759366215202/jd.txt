AI Agent & Automation Developer — ChatGPT, OpenAI, n8n, Make.com

Job Type: Project-based, with potential for ongoing work
Experience Level: Intermediate to Expert
Budget: Open to competitive hourly or fixed-price proposals

Job Description

We are looking for an experienced AI Agent and Automation Developer to help build an integrated AI system for several Canadian business brokerage brands.

We already use ChatGPT extensively and have developed business-specific strategies, workflows and instructions. We now need a developer who can turn these into functional AI agents connected to our business platforms.

The goal is to automate repetitive tasks, improve marketing efficiency, analyze business performance and simplify daily operations.

Key Responsibilities

1. AI Agent Development

* Build specialized AI agents using OpenAI and ChatGPT technologies.
* Configure agents for different business functions.
* Establish separate knowledge bases and instructions for each business brand.
* Connect agents to external systems where supported.

2. Marketing & SEO Automation

* Integrate Google Search Console and Google Analytics.
* Connect Google Ads for campaign analysis and reporting.
* Automate SEO monitoring and opportunity identification.
* Assist with content creation, reporting and marketing workflows.

3. Business Operations

* Integrate Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar where required.
* Develop lead management and follow-up workflows.
* Create automated reporting and task management systems.
* Implement approval processes before important actions are executed.

4. System Integration

* Use n8n, Make.com, APIs or similar tools where appropriate.
* Recommend a practical, cost-effective technical architecture.
* Ensure systems are secure, reliable and easy to maintain.
* Provide documentation and training.

Required Skills

* Proven experience developing AI agents and automated workflows.
* Strong working knowledge of OpenAI APIs and ChatGPT.
* Experience with n8n, Make.com or similar automation platforms.
* Experience integrating third-party APIs.
* Python or JavaScript development skills.
* Experience with Google Workspace and marketing platforms.
* Strong communication and problem-solving skills.

Project Structure

We will begin with a small paid pilot to demonstrate technical ability.

Successful completion may lead to implementation across multiple brokerage brands and additional projects.

We are looking for a developer who can recommend effective solutions, work independently and build reliable systems—not simply follow basic instructions.

To Apply

Please answer the following:

1. What AI agents have you previously built? Please provide examples or demonstrations.
2. Have you connected AI agents to Google Ads, Search Console, Analytics or Gmail?
3. Would you recommend ChatGPT-native tools, n8n, Make.com or a combination? Why?
4. How would you structure an AI system supporting multiple business brands?
5. What is your hourly rate, and are you available for ongoing development work?

Please include examples of actual AI automation projects you have personally developed. Generic applications will not be considered.
