# Beats - Your Custom Product Options Into HubSpot Deals, Line Items, Contacts

- This is custom product options syncing into HubSpot, for your Shopify store
- No native app: step 2 pulls line-item properties and metafields directly
- Step 3 checks the order ID, 4a matches the contact on email
- Native app installed? Its deal sync goes off, or orders can double
- Deal gets the options, contact gets tokens your sequences pull in
- Abandoned checkout: steps 9 and 10 save cart options and recovery link
- Line items carry each option; no custom objects needed for reporting
