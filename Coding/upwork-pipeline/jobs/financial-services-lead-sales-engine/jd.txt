Marketing Automation & CRM Specialist | Build Our Digital Lead-to-Sales Engine

Remote | International Candidates Welcome | Long-Term Opportunity

About Our Company

We are an established (just celebrated 25 years), privately held U.S.-based financial services company operating in a specialized consumer lending market.

We believe the future of our business will be driven by exceptional marketing, intelligent technology, and the ability to turn consumer interest into meaningful customer relationships.

Our philosophy is simple: We're building a marketing-driven company that happens to operate in financial services.

We're looking for an entrepreneurial, hands-on digital marketing and automation specialist who shares that mindset and can help us build a more effective, measurable, and scalable marketing and sales operation.

We're not looking for someone who simply maintains websites, manages social media, or runs advertising campaigns.

We're looking for someone who knows how to connect marketing, technology, automation, and sales to turn leads into customers.

This is a confidential recruitment process. Additional information about our company and industry will be shared with qualified candidates as we progress through the selection process.

The Opportunity

We currently generate consumer leads through Meta (Facebook and Instagram) advertising and have an established company website.

However, we see significant opportunity to improve how we capture, organize, engage, nurture, and convert those leads into customers.

We do not currently have a fully integrated CRM or marketing automation system. We need someone who can help us select, implement, and optimize the right technology while improving our overall digital marketing performance.

This is an opportunity to build something meaningful from the ground up, with the potential to become a long-term member of our marketing team.

What You'll Be Responsible For

1. CRM Selection & Implementation

- Evaluate and recommend the right CRM platform for our business, such as GoHighLevel, HubSpot, or a comparable solution.
- Implement and configure the CRM from the ground up.
- Integrate Meta Lead Ads, website forms, and other lead sources.
- Create sales pipelines, lead assignments, follow-up tracking, and reporting dashboards.
- Ensure our team has a simple, effective system for managing prospects from initial inquiry through completed sales.

2. Marketing Automation & Lead Nurturing

- Build automated email and SMS follow-up workflows using approved messaging and consent-based practices.
- Develop immediate lead-response systems to improve engagement.
- Create educational nurturing campaigns for prospects who are not ready to make an immediate decision.
- Implement appointment scheduling, reminders, and sales-team notifications.
- Identify opportunities to use AI and automation to improve productivity and customer engagement.

3. Website & Conversion Optimization

- Evaluate our existing website and recommend improvements to increase conversions.
- Transform our website from an informational resource into an effective marketing and lead-generation tool.
- Design and implement landing pages for specific advertising campaigns.
- Improve calls to action, user experience, mobile performance, and lead capture.
- Implement analytics, conversion tracking, and A/B testing.

4. Meta Advertising & Funnel Optimization

- Review and optimize our existing Facebook and Instagram advertising campaigns.
- Improve lead quality and campaign performance.
- Connect advertising campaigns directly to CRM and follow-up systems.
- Identify where prospects are dropping out of the sales funnel.
- Develop strategies to improve lead-to-appointment and lead-to-customer conversion rates.

5. Reporting & Continuous Improvement

- Build dashboards showing the performance of our marketing and sales funnel.
- Track cost per lead, qualified appointments, conversion rates, customer acquisition costs, and sales results.
- Recommend ongoing improvements based on actual performance data.
- Work closely with company leadership to identify new growth opportunities.

Who We're Looking For

Our ideal candidate is someone who thinks strategically but also enjoys building and implementing solutions.

You should have:

- Proven experience implementing CRM and marketing automation platforms.
- Strong knowledge of GoHighLevel, HubSpot, or similar systems.
- Experience integrating Meta Lead Ads with CRM platforms.
- Experience with landing pages, conversion optimization, and marketing funnels.
- Working knowledge of email marketing, SMS automation, and lead nurturing.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent written and spoken English.
- The ability to work independently, communicate proactively, and take ownership of results.
- A genuine interest in helping a business grow, not simply completing assigned tasks.

Experience in financial services, mortgage lending, insurance, or other high-consideration consumer industries is a plus.

Because we operate in a regulated industry, you must also be comfortable working within advertising, privacy, and consumer communication compliance requirements.

What Success Looks Like

Within the first 90 days, we want to accomplish the following:

- Implement a functioning CRM integrated with our website and Meta advertising.
- Establish a clearly defined and measurable sales pipeline.
- Create reliable lead assignment and follow-up processes.
- Launch approved automated email and SMS nurturing campaigns.
- Improve our website's ability to generate qualified leads.
- Establish reporting that connects marketing investments to actual sales opportunities.
- Identify and begin implementing measurable improvements in lead conversion.

Our ultimate goal is not simply to generate more leads.

Our goal is to generate more customers from the leads we're already paying to acquire.

Why This Opportunity Is Different

You'll have the opportunity to help build the digital marketing infrastructure of an established company with a specialized market and meaningful growth potential.

We're looking for someone who can become a trusted, long-term resource, contribute ideas, recommend solutions, and take pride in measurable results.

We welcome qualified professionals from around the world and are interested in establishing a cost-effective, ongoing working relationship.

We value initiative, creativity, accountability, clear communication, and demonstrated performance more than formal credentials.
