```
Project Type: One-time setup with potential ongoing optimisation
Experience Level: Intermediate to Expert
Hiring: Individual freelancers only. No agencies.

Project Overview

We are a Dubai-based corporate AI training company looking for an experienced freelancer to set up and optimise our GoHighLevel (GHL) lead generation funnel and Meta advertising campaigns.

All landing page HTML files and Meta ad creatives/copy are already prepared. We do not need a designer, copywriter or marketing strategist. This project is strictly about technical implementation, campaign setup, tracking, automation and optimisation.

Our target audience is B2B decision-makers, particularly HR, Learning & Development and senior management professionals.

Scope of Work

1. GoHighLevel Setup & Automation
- Implement our existing HTML landing pages in GHL, ensuring mobile responsiveness and functionality.
- Configure lead capture forms and CRM integration.
- Set up lead pipelines, tagging and lead source tracking.
- Build automated email follow-up sequences using provided content.
- Configure notifications and appointment booking workflows.
- Ensure leads flow correctly from Meta campaigns into GHL.
- Test the complete lead journey from ad click to follow-up.

2. Meta Ads Campaign Setup
- Configure campaigns in Meta Ads Manager using our existing creatives and copy.
- Set up appropriate campaign objectives, audiences, placements and budgets.
- Configure Meta Pixel, Conversions API (where applicable) and conversion events.
- Implement UTM tracking and attribution.
- Connect Meta lead forms to GHL if required.
- Verify campaign tracking and lead data accuracy.

3. Testing & Optimisation
- Test all landing pages, forms, automations and integrations.
- Identify and fix tracking or conversion issues.
- Optimise campaign settings and targeting based on initial performance.
- Set up clear reporting for leads, cost per lead and conversions.
- Provide a brief handover explaining the setup and how to manage it.

Requirements
- Proven hands-on experience with GoHighLevel and Meta Ads Manager.
- Strong technical understanding of Meta Pixel, Conversions API and CRM integrations.
- Experience setting up B2B lead generation campaigns.
- Ability to troubleshoot independently.
- Experience with corporate training, professional services or B2B education is a plus.

To Apply
Please include:

1. Examples of similar GHL + Meta Ads setups you have completed.
2. Your experience with B2B lead generation campaigns.
3. Your estimated fixed project cost and timeline.
4. What you would need from us to get started.
5. Your recommended approach to testing and initial optimisation.

Important: We are only considering individual freelancers. Agencies, teams and outsourced service providers will not be considered.

We are looking for someone who can execute efficiently, communicate clearly and deliver a fully functional, tested lead generation system.
```
