About Us

We are a growing aged care service provider. I am the founder and main driver of the business, and as we continue to grow, I am finding it increasingly difficult to keep up with emails, administrative tasks and day-to-day processes.

We currently have no AI automation in place and are looking for an experienced AI automation specialist who can help us streamline our operations, reduce repetitive work and improve productivity.

What We Are Looking For
We want someone who can understand how our business operates, identify opportunities for improvement and implement practical AI-powered workflows.

Some of the key areas we would like help with include:

1. AI Email Assistant

Create draft email responses that match my personal writing style and tone.
Use the context of incoming emails to generate relevant, personalised replies.
Help prioritise emails and identify follow-ups and outstanding actions.
Keep me in control of reviewing and approving emails before they are sent.

2. CRM and Conversation Notes

Automatically capture and summarise relevant information from phone calls and email conversations.
Save notes and actions against the appropriate client or contact in our CRM.
Identify follow-up tasks and outstanding commitments.
Reduce manual data entry and duplication.

3. Business Process Automation

Review our existing workflows and identify repetitive or inefficient processes.
Recommend and implement AI and automation solutions that save time.
Connect our existing software and systems where appropriate.
Help us build scalable processes as the business continues to grow.
Experience Required
We are looking for someone with proven experience in:

AI workflow automation and practical business applications of AI.
Gmail and Google Workspace.
CRM integrations and automated data capture.
Connecting business software using tools such as Make, Zapier, n8n or APIs.
Designing reliable workflows, including error handling and human approval steps.
Experience working with aged care, healthcare or other businesses handling sensitive client information would be highly regarded.

Privacy, confidentiality and secure handling of client information are essential.

How We Would Like to Work
We would prefer to start with a review of our current processes and a discussion about where automation could have the greatest impact.

From there, we would like to implement a small number of high-value automations, demonstrate measurable time savings and expand the project as we identify further opportunities.

We are looking for someone who can think beyond individual tasks, understand business needs and recommend practical solutions rather than simply implement tools without considering the bigger picture.

Please include examples of similar workflows you have built, the tools you recommend using, and how you would approach the first stage of this project.
