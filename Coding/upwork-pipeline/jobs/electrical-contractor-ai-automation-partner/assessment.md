# Assessment (private, Lorenzo only)

## Time
- Phase 1 discovery and roadmap: 2 to 3 calendar weeks, about 45 to 60 hours (8 to 12 interviews across service, construction, estimating, accounting, HR, fleet, admin; volume counts; spam and bulk tuning as the quick win; written roadmap).
- Phase 2 intake (3.1) plus the phone summaries feed (3.2 second bullet): 4 to 6 weeks, about 70 to 110 hours. Power Automate or Logic Apps in their tenant, shared mailbox, Azure OpenAI or Claude via a tenant-owned key for classification and extraction, SharePoint List tracker, Teams adaptive-card alerts, escalation flows, weekly summary, docs and recorded training, then 30 days of support.
- Phase 3 (rest of the roadmap): months, retainer shaped.

## Likelihood
- High for intake: every piece is standard M365 plumbing. Risks: Power Automate premium connectors and licensing per flow owner; shared-mailbox trigger reliability at volume; getting the phone platform (not yet chosen) to post summaries into the mailbox in a parseable format.
- Medium for the phone emergency ladder: depends on the platform they pick supporting press-to-confirm call-outs. Unverified until they name it.
- Copilot pilot and M365 spam tuning are low risk; Defender for Office 365 plan level is unknown.
- Unverified for Lorenzo: no named team, no US-based subcontractors named, no production M365 tenant builds in his fact list, no trades references with 6+ months in production beyond the roofing bullet. They want 2 to 3 comparable production projects with references and named people; he has one trades bullet. Say plainly on the call which areas he runs himself and which he would partner on (likely M365 security tuning and Copilot licensing advice).

## Standard pricing
- Phase 1 fixed: market $6,000 to $12,000 for a 2 to 3 week discovery at a 130-person firm.
- Phase 2 intake build: market $12,000 to $25,000 fixed with milestones, plus 30 days of support.
- Retainer after: $3,000 to $6,000 a month.
- Recurring software (their tenant): Power Automate Premium per user where needed (about $15/user/mo), Azure OpenAI or Anthropic API usage (tens of dollars a month at their volume), Copilot at list price per pilot seat, their chosen phone platform, a social scheduler if M365 has none that fits (for example $50 to $150/mo).
- Post: hourly, rate not given; recorded bid average $64/hr. An agency RFP like this lands well above that; prices go only in the Upwork fields or on the call, never in the proposal.

## Ask before quoting
- Weekly volumes: emails, calls and leads per week, per location.
- Which phone platform, and does it support press-to-confirm call-outs and webhooks?
- M365 licence tier (Business Premium vs E3/E5), Defender plan, Power Automate premium available?
- Who owns intake today per division and location, and the target first-response time.
- Is the job-management system named yet (for the later connection)?
- NDA and US-based requirement: who touches the tenant.

## Flags
- US-based team members for anything touching systems or data; NDA required.
- Demands 2 to 3 comparable production projects (6+ months) with references, named people and roles, subcontractor disclosure.
- Wants fixed price and timeline for Phase 1 and a Phase 2 range stated; Lorenzo's rule: prices in the Upwork fields or the call only.
- On-site day in MD/VA is a plus.
