Policy · draft for your review

AI acceptable-use policy

This policy covers every employee who uses AI tools for company work. It says which tools are approved and what data may and may not go into them.

May go into approved tools

  1. Drafts of emails, letters and reports
  2. Published service descriptions and public project information
  3. Internal procedures and meeting notes without personal details

Never goes into any AI tool

  1. Employee personal, payroll or HR records
  2. Customer contract terms and pricing
  3. Passwords, access codes and bank details
  4. Bid documents before they are public

Approved tools

Microsoft 365 Copilot signed in with a company account, and the automations listed in the documentation register. Personal AI accounts are not used for company work.

Section 4 · How it starts

Discovery first, then intake, then the rest of the roadmap.

Phase 1

Discovery and roadmap

About 2 to 3 weeks · fixed price
  • Interviews and workflow mapping with actual volumes: emails, calls and leads per week
  • Quick wins done, starting with spam and bulk-mail tuning
  • Written roadmap: priority, cost, licensing, hours saved and timeline per item
  • A day or two on-site in Maryland or Virginia if useful
Phase 2

First build: intake (3.1)

Fixed price with milestones · 30 days of support
  • Shared mailbox, AI sorting, Teams alerts and the tracker
  • Auto-replies, escalation timers and the weekly summary
  • Phone summaries feeding in once your platform is live
  • Tested, trained, documented, then 30 days of support
Phase 3

The rest of the roadmap

Separate fixed-price projects or a retainer
  • Phones, website assistant, leadership email and Copilot decision
  • Marketing workflows your staff operate
  • Top-ranked time-savers, checked at 60 days
  • Only if Phases 1 and 2 go well
