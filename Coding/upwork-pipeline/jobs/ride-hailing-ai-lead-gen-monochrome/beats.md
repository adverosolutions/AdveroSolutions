This is a working prototype of Monochrome's rider acquisition engine.

1. Built around one goal: about +60 active riders a month on these numbers.
2. Your numbers: leads contacted, registration rate, active rate. It recomputes live.
3. A public listing goes in, tracked active riders come out.
4. Apify pulls leads by city; duplicates, bots and dead listings removed.
5. Claude scores likely-rider fit, explains why, writes the personal message.
6. Every lead gets its own tracked link; A/B test picks the winner.
7. Dashboard: each stage's count and cost, by source, down to active.
8. The pipeline on your Claude setup: Apify in, dashboard out.
