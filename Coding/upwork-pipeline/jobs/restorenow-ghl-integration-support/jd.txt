Job Title: GoHighLevel Integration & Workflow Specialist (Ongoing, As-Needed Support)

About Us
RestoreNow is a family-owned, IICRC-certified property restoration company serving the South Shore of Massachusetts and Rhode Island. We handle water, fire, and storm damage, and we operate 24/7. We use GoHighLevel (GHL) as our CRM and marketing automation platform, and it connects to the other tools our team uses day to day, including JobTread for project management.

What We Need
We're looking for an experienced GoHighLevel specialist to be our go-to expert on an as-needed basis. You'll troubleshoot integration problems, fix broken workflows, and help us build and improve automations as our needs change. This is an ongoing relationship with a small, hands-on team, not a one-time project.

Responsibilities

Diagnose and resolve issues with GHL integrations, including API connections, webhooks, and third-party apps
Troubleshoot and repair workflows that aren't triggering, stalling, or producing unexpected results
Build and modify workflows, triggers, pipelines, and automations to match our lead intake and follow-up processes
Set up and maintain connections between GHL and other business systems (project management, phone/SMS, email, calendars, forms)
Configure Private Integrations, API keys, and custom fields securely
Audit existing workflows and recommend improvements for reliability and efficiency
Document fixes and changes so our team understands what was done and can maintain it
Respond promptly when something breaks, since missed leads cost us business

Required Skills & Experience

2+ years of hands-on GoHighLevel experience (workflows, pipelines, custom values, triggers)
Strong working knowledge of the GHL API, webhooks, and integration tools such as Zapier, Make, or similar
Proven track record of debugging complex, multi-step automations
Experience with CRM, lead nurture, and email/SMS automation
Clear written communication; you can explain technical issues in plain language to non-technical teammates
Reliable and responsive, with a good working knowledge of U.S. business hours
