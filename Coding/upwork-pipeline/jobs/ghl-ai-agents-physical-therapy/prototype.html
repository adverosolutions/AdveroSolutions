    What the agent knows, and where it stops.

    The same agent serves every clinic. What changes per clinic is its knowledge, a few guardrail details and who it hands off to.

    Knowledge

    Read from the clinic's own information. Updated when the clinic changes it, no retraining.

    Guardrails

    Checked on every message before the agent replies.

    • Never diagnoses, names a cause or says whether something is serious.always on
    • Never suggests exercises, ice, heat, rest or medication.always on
    • Screens for red-flag symptoms before booking. If any are present, it gives urgent care guidance and alerts staff.always on
    • Confirms whether the clinic is in network, never what a plan will cover. The front desk verifies benefits.always on
    • Asks only what booking needs. No medical history over chat or text.always on

    Handoff to front desk

    The agent stops and a person takes the conversation.

    When
    • The patient asks for a person
    • Any red-flag symptom
    • A current patient with a clinical question
    • A billing or coverage dispute
    • The agent isn't sure twice in a row
    How
    • Conversation assigned to Front desk, Downtown
    • Internal note with a short summary
    • Staff notified in the GoHighLevel app
    • Agent pauses on that contact until released
    • Patient told who will reply, and by when
