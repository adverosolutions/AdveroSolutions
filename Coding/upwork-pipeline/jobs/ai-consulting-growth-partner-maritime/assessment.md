# Assessment - AI Consulting Business Growth Partner (private)

Full-time, long-term post: frame it as a monthly retainer, not a project.

## Time
- Month 1 (setup): about 40 to 60 hours. Pain, pilot offer and buyer pick (1 week), outbound stack live (Zapmail inboxes, pre-warmed, Instantly, Claude research and writing: 1 to 2 weeks), sales process and same-day pilot proposal template (1 week), onboarding checklist and Monday review sheet (1 week).
- After that: about 10 to 20 hours a week running campaigns, reviewing replies, sales support, monthly review. The post says full time; 40 h/week of Lorenzo is not realistic next to his agency, so offer a fixed weekly scope.
- First booked calls realistically 3 to 5 weeks in (warm-up plus first sends).

## Likelihood
- Setup of the system itself: high. It is Lorenzo's own stack.
- Results: unverified. Maritime logistics is a niche he has not sold into. Buyer lists (forwarders, carriers, port agents, NVOCCs) are findable, but email coverage of ops heads is thinner than SMB niches; LinkedIn may be needed alongside.
- Depends on the client having a real offer and delivery capability. Unknown: what they have built or sold so far.
- Deliverability: his own estate had burned inboxes on 2026-09-25; this build needs fresh domains, not his.

## Flags
- **No maritime logistics case studies.** The post asks for "two to three previous case studies in the area of Maritime Logistics solutions". Lorenzo has none. Do not invent any. The proposal leans on the agency ($10k/mo on the same kind of system) and consultancy (+35% qualified leads) bullets, and the chart's step 13 answers the ask by producing their own maritime case studies. Expect this to cost the job with a client who screens hard on it.
- New account, no hires, no reviews, no payment history shown.
- Rate $19-$40/hr for a growth partner is well below market.
- Panama client; timezone matches US Central within an hour.

## Standard pricing
- Market for a fractional growth / outbound partner: $3,000 to $8,000 a month retainer, or $60 to $120/hr.
- Post's rate: $19-$40/hr, roughly a third to half of market.
- Recurring software (client pays): Instantly about $97/mo, Zapmail about $3 to $4 per mailbox a month (e.g. 10 to 15 mailboxes), domains about $12/yr each, Claude API usage about $20 to $60/mo, lead data $50 to $150/mo.

## Ask before quoting
- What AI offers do you sell today, at what price, and to whom?
- How many clients so far, and any in maritime logistics?
- Which maritime segment: freight forwarders, carriers, port agents, terminals?
- Monthly revenue target and hours a week you expect from a partner.
- Do you want the partner running outbound, or advising your team to run it?
