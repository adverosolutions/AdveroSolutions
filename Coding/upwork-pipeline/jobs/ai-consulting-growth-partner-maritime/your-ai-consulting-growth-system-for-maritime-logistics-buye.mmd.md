# Your AI Consulting Growth System for Maritime Logistics Buyers

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    subgraph sec_strategy["1 - GROWTH STRATEGY"]
        direction TB
        pain["1 - We start with one pain maritime logistics teams feel daily, like slow freight quotes."]
        offer["2 - The first offer is a paid pilot that automates quoting or bill of lading data entry."]
        buyers["3 - The buyers are ops heads at freight forwarders, customs brokers and ship agents."]
        target["4 - Your monthly client target decides how many forwarders we email each week."]
    end
    subgraph sec_marketing["2 - MARKETING"]
        direction TB
        lists["5 - Lead lists come from forwarder directories and LinkedIn, sorted by trade lanes."]
        research["6 - Claude reads each company's website and writes an email about their own lanes."]
        send["7 - Cold emails go out every weekday in Instantly from pre-warmed inboxes."]
    end
    subgraph sec_sales["3 - SALES PROCESS"]
        direction TB
        book["8 - Interested replies get two call times within the hour, and quiet ones get follow-ups."]
        call["9 - On the call we map their quoting or paperwork process and put a cost on it."]
        proposal["10 - The proposal goes out the same day with the pilot scope, the price and a start date."]
    end
    subgraph sec_operations["4 - DAY-TO-DAY OPERATIONS"]
        direction TB
        monday["11 - Every Monday we review emails sent, replies, calls booked, pilots sold and delivery."]
        onboard["12 - Each client gets one onboarding checklist and a weekly update until the pilot ships."]
    end
    subgraph sec_bizdev["5 - ONGOING BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT"]
        direction TB
        casestudy["13 - Every finished pilot becomes a maritime case study with its before and after."]
        next["14 - Each month we turn pilots into retainers and pick the next segment to go after."]
    end
    pain --> offer
    offer --> buyers
    buyers --> target
    target --> lists
    lists --> research
    research --> send
    send --> book
    book --> call
    call --> proposal
    proposal --> monday
    monday --> onboard
    onboard --> casestudy
    casestudy --> next

    style sec_marketing fill:#dcfce7,stroke:#94a3b8,color:#0f172a
    style sec_sales fill:#ede9fe,stroke:#94a3b8,color:#0f172a
    style sec_operations fill:#f1f5f9,stroke:#94a3b8,color:#0f172a
    style sec_bizdev fill:#fef9c3,stroke:#94a3b8,color:#0f172a
```

- **1 - GROWTH STRATEGY** - A clear growth strategy means one pain, one offer and one buyer in maritime logistics.
- **2 - MARKETING** - Marketing is outbound straight to maritime logistics companies. Each email talks about that company's own lanes and services, not AI in general.
- **3 - SALES PROCESS** - The sales process goes from reply to call to proposal without waiting days. The call puts a cost on their manual work, so the pilot price makes sense to them.
- **4 - DAY-TO-DAY OPERATIONS** - Day-to-day operations run on a weekly rhythm. Every Monday you know what was sent, who replied, what sold and what is being delivered.
- **5 - ONGOING BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT** - Ongoing business development turns each pilot into proof and into a retainer. Every month the numbers decide which new opportunity comes next.
