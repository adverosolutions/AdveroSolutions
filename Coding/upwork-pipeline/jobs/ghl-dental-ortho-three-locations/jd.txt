GoHighLevel Expert Needed — Dental/Orthodontic CRM, Automation & Patient Tracking 

We are a multi-location dental and orthodontic practice in South Florida looking for an experienced GoHighLevel (GHL) expert to properly configure and optimize our CRM, patient lead tracking, automation, attribution, and follow-up systems.

We have 3 locations:

Pembroke Pines Boynton Beach Royal Palm Beach 

We provide both general dentistry and orthodontics, including Invisalign and braces.

This is NOT simply a request to install a generic GHL snapshot. We want someone who understands how to build a complete patient acquisition and conversion system that allows us to track a lead from the original advertising source all the way through becoming an actual patient.

Primary Goal 

We want to be able to track:

Google Ads / Meta Ads / Website / Phone Call → Lead → Contacted → Appointment Scheduled → Confirmed → Showed → Became Patient → Treatment Started / Revenue

We need to know which campaigns, locations, and lead sources are actually producing patients—not just phone calls or form submissions.

Pembroke Pines Boynton Beach Royal Palm Beach General Dental Orthodontics Invisalign Braces Other major services where appropriate

Incoming calls Original advertising source Campaign Location Call outcome Whether an appointment was scheduled Whether the patient showed Whether the caller ultimately became a patient

Hygiene/recall Unscheduled treatment Orthodontic consultations Other appropriate reactivation campaigns

Leads Phone calls Appointments scheduled Show rate No-show rate Became patients Lead-to-appointment conversion rate Appointment-to-patient conversion rate Overall lead-to-patient conversion rate Advertising source/campaign Revenue attribution where technically feasible

Answering inbound calls After-hours calls Missed-call recovery Scheduling patients Outbound recall calls Reactivation campaigns Appointment confirmation
