What You'll Do

You'll own HealFastRx's GoHighLevel marketing automation: funnels, workflows, landing pages, forms, email and SMS for patient acquisition, retention, product sales and feedback.
You'll start by quality-checking our existing GoHighLevel flows, then connect them with new funnels. From there, you turn approved marketing strategies into working customer journeys and suggest a better and more scalable structure when you see one. Communication is mostly written and to the point, with meetings as needed.
Every flow must fire correctly together: one feedback reply might trigger three other journeys and add a product-interest tag for later segmentation. You keep it all mapped and documented as it scales, and you learn and follow which patient data (PHI) must stay out of which systems.

The initial build phase is busy, then settles into a steadier rhythm with spikes around launches and other strategic initiatives. You thrive on momentum, and when something breaks, you fix it swiftly and accurately.

Smart improvements and solutions are welcome; untested changes are not.

About HealFast

Founded in 2017, HealFast helps people heal, thrive, and unlock their full health potential. We began by supporting recovery after surgery, injury and childbirth. Our physician-formulated products are grounded in the best available science and backed by a simple promise: if we wouldn't recommend it to our own family, we won't sell it.
Now we're expanding with HealFastRx, a premier physician-crafted telehealth service. HealFastRx bridges the gaps between pharmaceutical, nutritional and educational coaching solutions, pairing white-glove concierge coaching with a supportive community to help each patient become their best self.

We're a remote-first team working across US time zones. We raise the bar and go the extra mile to do right by people. If you want to strive with the best and see your work help real people feel better and live better, you'll find meaning here.
