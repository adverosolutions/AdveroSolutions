Email Marketing Operations & Deliverability Specialist — List Cleanup, Platform Migration & Custom Cart Integration

We’re looking for an experienced email marketing operations specialist to audit and rebuild our email infrastructure, clean up our lists, and migrate us from an aging in-house system to a suitable email platform.

This is primarily a technical and operational role. We have less need for creative or copywriting support and more need for someone who can diagnose deliverability problems, manage complex lists, and connect email platforms to custom-built websites and shopping carts.

The situation

Our email marketing operations have not been actively managed for approximately a year. We now face significant deliverability issues, including blocks by some mailbox providers and concerns about our sending IP and domain reputation.

We need someone who can assess the damage, develop a practical recovery plan, and establish a reliable process going forward.

What you’ll do

Audit our current email system, lists, sending practices, and deliverability.

Clean and segment our lists, including managing invalid addresses, bounces, complaints, inactive contacts, and suppression lists.

Investigate sending IP and domain reputation issues, mailbox-provider blocks, and authentication configuration.

Recommend and implement an appropriate email platform, with a clear migration plan.

Coordinate integrations between the selected platform and our in-house websites and shopping carts using APIs, webhooks, and custom event tracking.

Build or repair abandoned-cart recovery flows for our custom cart.

Restructure our email automations and establish campaign schedules planned at least 90 days in advance.

Set up monitoring, reporting, and maintenance procedures to keep the system healthy.

Required experience

Hands-on management of large email lists or multiple lists.

Proven experience diagnosing and resolving deliverability problems, including blocks, bounces, and reputation issues.

Strong understanding of SPF, DKIM, DMARC, suppression management, segmentation, and responsible re-engagement practices.

Experience with platforms such as Omnisend, Klaviyo, and SendGrid, including evaluating which platform fits a business’s technical requirements.

Experience integrating email platforms with custom-built carts and websites.

Ability to translate technical findings into a clear action plan and work with our developers on implementation.

Who this role is for

You should be comfortable working beyond standard ecommerce integrations. Experience limited to Shopify and its built-in email integrations will not be sufficient. We need someone who can troubleshoot independently and work effectively with custom systems.

To apply

Please include:

An example of a deliverability or sending-reputation problem you resolved, including your role and the outcome.

The approximate size and complexity of the email lists you have managed.

Your experience integrating email platforms with custom carts, particularly abandoned-cart recovery.

How you would approach the initial audit and first 30 days of this project.

Which parts of the implementation you can handle directly and which would require our developers.
