Zoho CRM Specialist Needed — Lead Tracking, RingCentral SMS & Follow-Up Automation

We’re looking for an experienced Zoho CRM specialist to help set up a client who is switching to Zoho. We need leads from Facebook, Instagram, Google Ads, and website forms to flow into the CRM with accurate source tracking and automated follow-up.

**Scope of work:**

* Review the existing setup and confirm the Zoho plan, integrations, and any additional subscriptions needed.
* Connect lead forms and website questionnaires to Zoho, including leads generated through Facebook, Instagram, and Google Ads.
* Install and configure the RingCentral SMS extension.
* Create automated email and text follow-up sequences for new leads.
* Create follow-up sequences for leads who have not purchased.
* Automatically send a discount offer based on agreed timing and conditions.
* Stop automated follow-ups when a lead purchases, responds, or opts out.
* Track each lead’s source, campaign, location, and sales outcome.
* Confirm leads enter Zoho with the correct source information and prevent duplicate records.
* Set up lead assignment rules if they are not already configured.
* Create reports for lead volume, close rates, sales, location, and marketing source.
* Test all integrations and automations, including stop conditions, and fix any issues before launch.
* Provide a walkthrough of the completed setup and basic documentation so the client can manage it.

We’ll agree on follow-up timing, messaging, discount conditions, and lead assignment rules before implementation.

**Experience required:**

* Hands-on experience configuring Zoho CRM and workflow automation.
* Experience integrating RingCentral SMS with Zoho.
* Experience connecting Meta lead forms and website forms to Zoho.
* Understanding of campaign attribution and UTM tracking.
* Experience using Zoho Flow, Zapier, or APIs where needed.

**Please include in your proposal:**

* Examples of similar Zoho CRM projects you’ve completed.
* Your experience with RingCentral SMS and automated follow-up.
* Any limitations or additional tools you anticipate for this setup.
* Your estimated cost and turnaround time.
* Any questions you need answered to finalize the scope.

We’re looking for someone who can handle the setup, verify that everything works, and explain the finished system clearly with a loom video or other video.
