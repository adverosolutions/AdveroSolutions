Senior Clay + HubSpot GTM Systems Architect | Enrichment, CRM Governance & Signals

Project Type: Initial architecture and implementation sprint
Estimated Initial Engagement: Approximately 15–25 hours
Experience Level: Expert
Work Arrangement: Remote
Budget: $60–$85/hour; exceptional candidates with highly relevant experience may be considered above range
Primary Platforms: Clay + HubSpot

Project Overview
Bumblebee IT Services (BBITS) is seeking a hands-on Clay + HubSpot GTM Systems Architect to design and implement a reusable data, enrichment, and market-intelligence foundation for our business-development programs.

This is not a traditional lead-generation, list-building, or outbound-campaign assignment.

We need a consultant who can architect the underlying system: how companies and contacts are discovered, qualified, enriched, validated, written to HubSpot, protected from bad overwrites, monitored for meaningful business signals, and maintained after the consultant's engagement ends.
The ideal consultant has personally designed and implemented production Clay-to-HubSpot environments and can make sound architecture decisions when data sources conflict, workflows fail, enrichment costs increase, or CRM governance requirements become complex.

The initial engagement is expected to require approximately 15–25 hours. Successful completion may lead to additional implementation, optimization, or ongoing GTM systems work as future needs are identified.

What We Need Built

The initial engagement will prioritize a practical, reusable architecture that may include:
Clay + HubSpot Architecture
•	Review the existing Clay and HubSpot environment before implementing changes.
•	Establish clear source-of-truth, field-precedence, and writeback rules.
•	Protect known-good HubSpot data from inappropriate enrichment overwrites.
•	Define staging, verification, exception, and review logic where appropriate.
•	Maintain reliable company/contact associations and record matching.

Reusable Enrichment Workflows
•	Create reusable company and contact enrichment templates.
•	Build enrichment waterfalls and conditional logic.
•	Validate decision-makers and contact information before CRM activation.
•	Support enrichment of existing HubSpot records as well as discovery of new qualified accounts and contacts.
•	Develop appropriate deduplication and normalization controls.

Market & Trigger Intelligence
Help establish reusable methods for identifying and qualifying business-development signals such as:
•	leadership changes;
•	organizational expansion;
•	cybersecurity or technology events;
•	funding or investment activity;
•	technology initiatives;
•	acquisitions or organizational changes;
•	other defined account-level triggers.

Trigger workflows should include appropriate recency, source, relevance, deduplication, and CRM-governance controls.

Clay Credit & Provider Cost Management
•	Prevent unnecessary enrichment runs.
•	Use stop-on-success and conditional logic where appropriate.
•	Sequence providers intentionally.
•	Avoid paying for data already available in HubSpot.
•	Establish testing practices before large production runs.
•	Recommend cost-conscious architecture without sacrificing data quality.

QA & Failure Recovery
•	Establish QA checkpoints before production writeback.
•	Identify failed, incomplete, or conflicting records.
•	Support batch/run tracking where appropriate.
•	Design workflows so erroneous updates can be identified and corrected without blindly overwriting legitimate later changes.

Documentation & Knowledge Transfer
The engagement should leave BBITS with an architecture we can understand and maintain.
Expected handoff should include appropriate:
•	workflow documentation;
•	field/writeback map;
•	naming conventions;
•	operating guidance;
•	exception handling;
•	maintenance instructions;
•	Loom/video walkthroughs or live knowledge-transfer session.

What We Are Looking For

You should have hands-on production experience, not simply familiarity with Clay or experience generating prospect lists.

Strong candidates will have experience with several of the following:
•	advanced Clay workflows;
•	Clay + HubSpot integration;
•	CRM field governance;
•	controlled writeback;
•	enrichment waterfalls;
•	company/contact matching;
•	data normalization and deduplication;
•	HubSpot custom properties and associations;
•	CRM cleanup and bounced-contact remediation;
•	signal or trigger-based GTM workflows;
•	APIs or webhooks;
•	enrichment-provider optimization;
•	RevOps or GTM systems architecture;
•	workflow QA and error recovery;
•	documentation and handoff.

What Success Looks Like

At the end of the initial engagement, BBITS should be able to:
•	discover and qualify appropriate target organizations;
•	identify and enrich relevant decision-makers;
•	safely improve existing CRM records;
•	create qualified new HubSpot records without unnecessary duplication;
•	identify meaningful account-level business signals;
•	control enrichment costs;
•	understand why and when data is written to HubSpot;
•	identify workflow failures or exceptions;
•	duplicate and maintain the core workflows without depending on the original consultant for every change.

We value accuracy, architecture quality, maintainability, documentation, and sound technical judgment over raw volume.
