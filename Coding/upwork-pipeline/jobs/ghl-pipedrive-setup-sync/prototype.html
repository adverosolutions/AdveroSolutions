Why email is the match key
One person, never two

Every lead is looked up in Pipedrive by email before anyone is created, so a lead who fills in two forms stays one person with one rep.

Why each CRM owns one job
No fighting over the record

GHL owns follow-up and messaging, Pipedrive owns the pipeline. Stage is the one thing both share, and it syncs both ways.

Why the sync ignores its own echo
Two-way without loops

A stage change the sync made itself is dropped on the way back, so the two CRMs never bounce the same move back and forth.

Rule 1

Returning people keep their rep

If the email is already in Pipedrive, the lead stays with whoever owns that person, whatever the new source says.

Rule 2

Unmapped sources go to a default owner

A source nobody mapped yet lands with the default owner and the needs-review tag, so nothing sits unassigned.

Rule 3

GHL knows the owner too

The owner is written back to GHL, so texts and emails go out under the right rep's name.
