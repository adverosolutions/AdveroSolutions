Why 3 workflows
Each trigger has one job

Capture, replies and bookings each run in their own workflow, so one change never breaks the other two.

Why one goal
One tag stops the follow-up

A reply and a booking both add the lead-engaged tag, and the follow-up stops on that tag either way.

Why a No Response stage
Nobody goes quiet unseen

If a lead never answers, it lands in its own stage and your team gets a task to call once.

The follow-up sequence

What the lead actually receives, and when. It stops the moment they reply or book.

Stops on reply or booking Booking link in every touch
Day 0Right away
SMSFirst text

Hi Jordan, this is Alex with your team. Thanks for filling out the form, I have your details. Want to grab a quick call? Pick a time here.

Day 0Right after
EmailGot your details, Jordan

Thanks for reaching out. I have what you sent over, and the quickest next step is a short call. Here's the link to book a time that suits you.

Day 1
SMSStill want to chat?

Hi Jordan, Alex again. Still happy to help with what you asked about. Here's the link to pick a time.

Day 3
EmailTwo quick questions, Jordan

To make the call worth your time, it helps to know two things: what you want to get done, and when you'd like it done by. Reply here or book a time.

Day 6
SMSLast check-in

Hi Jordan, last note from me so I don't crowd your messages. If you still want help, grab a time here whenever you're ready.
