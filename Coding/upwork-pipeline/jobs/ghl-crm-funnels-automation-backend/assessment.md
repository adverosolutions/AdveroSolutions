## Standard pricing
- Market for competent GHL backend builders: $35 to $75/hr, or $500 to $1,500 per account build. The post's $15-$38/hr is at or below the low end; the bids average $23.34.
- Recurring software on their side: GHL plan, LC Phone/Email usage. Nothing new from Lorenzo.

- US or other countries for SMS (A2P, sender rules)?
- Is there a standard pipeline and follow-up cadence they already use, or does Lorenzo set the default?
- Hourly ongoing, or per-account fixed?
