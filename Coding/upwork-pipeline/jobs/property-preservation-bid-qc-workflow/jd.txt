AI Automation Developer for Property Preservation Bid & QC Workflow
Hourly $19-40, intermediate, <30 hrs/week, 1-3 months. Only freelancers located in the U.S. may apply.
We are a regional property preservation company working with clients such as MCS, Freddie Mac, Guardian, Altisource, NFR, and direct bank relationships. Field operations run on 1099 contractors who complete work orders (initial secures, winterizations, grass cuts, bid approvals/requests, dozens of other tasks) and upload photo/measurement documentation into Property Preservation Wizard (PPW).
Today, in-office client managers and overseas processors manually review contractor photos, build bid sheets, submit bids to clients, review after-photos once work is approved and completed, and mark orders "ready for office" for invoicing. High-volume, repetitive, photo/data-driven.
We want an AI-powered system that takes over the manual, judgment-based review work: 1. Photo review and compliance check of before-photos against work order instructions. 2. Automated bid generation from photos, measurements, and work order type, using historical pricing and geographic benchmarks. 3. Bid review/QA layer flagging inconsistent, underpriced, overpriced, or incomplete bids. 4. Post-completion photo verification against the approved bid scope. 5. Exception flagging for follow-ups, missing items, client rejection risks. 6. "Ready for office" automation where verification passes. 7. Automated bid approval pricing assigning contractor pricing from client requirements, historical costs, market conditions, and target margins.
Anticipated approach: computer vision / image analysis; LLM reasoning over work order notes, client instructions, historical data; integration with PPW (API, RPA/browser automation, or middleware, TBD based on what PPW exposes); a pricing/market-rate model on historical bid data; a workflow/orchestration layer with human-in-the-loop override.
Scope: start with a discovery/scoping phase (what PPW allows, what data exists, recommended architecture and phased build plan), then build in phases, ideally a pilot on a limited set of work order types.
What you'll need: AI/LLM integration (Claude, GPT) and computer vision; workflow automation or RPA integrating with third-party portals; messy real-world data; comfort scoping an ambiguous ops problem into a phased plan. We are not handing you a finished spec, we're handing you a business process.
How to apply: relevant experience with AI/computer vision automation (photo verification, bid/pricing automation, workflow integration); your recommended high-level approach to a discovery phase; availability and whether you work independently or as part of a team.
