Corwen is an AI consulting and implementation company based in Salt Lake City. We help small and mid-size businesses (accounting firms, restaurants, construction firms and others) use AI in their day-to-day operations. That means building custom workflows and software for them, training their staff, and supporting them after launch.

We're adding a developer to our build team. The work is project-based: you'll report to our lead engineer and take client builds from first prototype through delivery.

What you'll work on
- Custom workflows that take a manual process (client intake, document review, reporting, reconciliations, scheduling) and automate it with LLMs and plain code
- Internal tools and client-facing web apps
- Integrations with the systems our clients already use: Microsoft 365, accounting and practice-management software, POS systems, phone systems, spreadsheets and PDFs
- Private and local AI setups for clients who can't send their data to the cloud
- Tests, documentation and handoff notes so our team and our clients can maintain what you build

What we work in
- Python (Flask, pytest) and TypeScript/Node (React, Vite)
- PostgreSQL
- Claude and the Anthropic API, plus local models through Ollama
- Git and GitHub, with review on every change
- AI coding tools like Claude Code as part of the daily workflow

What we're looking for
- 2+ years building software that real people use, not just tutorials or demos
- Strong Python and/or TypeScript
- Hands-on experience building with LLM APIs (prompting, structured outputs, tool use), and a realistic sense of where they break
- Comfortable with messy real-world data: exported spreadsheets, scanned PDFs, inconsistent records
- Careful with client data. Many of our clients handle sensitive information, so confidentiality and security are part of the job
- Clear written communication, and the ability to explain technical decisions to non-technical people
- U.S.-based, with working hours that overlap Mountain Time

Nice to have

- Microsoft Graph / Microsoft 365 integrations
- Experience with automation platforms (n8n, Zapier, Make) and good judgment about when custom code is the better choice

- You'll sign a confidentiality and IP assignment agreement before starting client work.
