Q1. How do you handle sensitive information or records?

Client records stay in your own accounts (Google Workspace, Clio, Lawmatics). I don't copy them anywhere else. I build and test with made-up clients, only ask for the access each piece needs, and keep logins in the automation tool's encrypted credential store instead of docs or email. Successful runs aren't saved, so client details don't pile up in the automation's history. The AI step runs through your firm's own Anthropic account. When the work is done, you remove my access.

Yes, I built an intake and reception system for a personal injury law firm. My usual stack for office work like this is n8n, Claude or OpenAI, Google Workspace and whichever practice or CRM system the office runs on, which for you would be Clio and Lawmatics.
