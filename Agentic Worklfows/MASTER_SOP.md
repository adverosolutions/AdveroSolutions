# AdveroSolutions Master SOP v2.0

**Last Updated:** January 2025
**Classification:** Internal Use

---

## Document Overview

### Purpose
This Master SOP establishes standardized processes for all lead generation, sales, and follow-up activities within AdveroSolutions. These procedures are designed for execution by both human team members and AI systems.

### Campaign Philosophy
Our outreach uses curiosity-based, growth-focused messaging. We position ourselves as insight providers showing contractors what top performers are doing differently.

**Core principles:**
- Deep personalization showing we researched them specifically
- Curiosity gaps that make them want to know what they're missing
- Specific numbers (25-40% growth, top 15%)
- Positioning as intelligence sharing, not selling
- Early adopter vs. late adopter framing
- Scarcity through limited availability

### Sales Philosophy: One-Call Close
Every discovery call is structured as a one-call close. Price is presented on the call and a decision is requested immediately. There is no "I'll send you a proposal" or "let me follow up with pricing."

**CRITICAL:** If you don't ask for the decision on the call, leads kick the can down the road and eventually go cold. The post-call follow-up sequences exist for leads who slip through despite your close attempt - they are not a planned second step.

## SOP-001: Lead Stage Definitions

### Stage Overview

| Stage | Description | Max Duration | Exit Trigger |
|-------|-------------|--------------|--------------|
| New Lead | In system, no outreach | 24 hrs | Email sent |
| Contacted | In cold sequence | 10 days | Reply or complete |
| Engaged | Positive reply, link sent | 72 hrs | Books or Non-Booker |
| Non-Booker | Has link, hasn't booked | 14 days | Books or sequence done |
| Booked | Call scheduled | Until call | Call or no-show |
| No-Show | Missed call | 48 hrs | Reschedules or 3x no-show |
| Discovery Done | Call completed | 7 days | Closes or follow-up done |
| Closed Won | Customer | N/A | Terminal |
| Closed Lost | Declined/DQ | 90 days | Re-engagement eligible |

**RULE:** A lead exists in exactly ONE stage at a time. All transitions require a timestamp and reason.

## SOP-002: Cold Email Campaign (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical)

**Applies to:** HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical verticals ONLY. For Roofing, see SOP-002R below.

### Campaign Overview

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| Total Emails | 3 |
| Duration | 7-10 days |
| Volume | ~2,000/day |

| Hook | What top 15% are doing differently |
| Promise | 25-40% growth without longer hours |

### Email 1: Personalized Curiosity Hook (Day 1)

**Objective:** Create curiosity about what top performers are doing differently.

**Template:**
```
Subject: What top [industry] contractors are doing differently

Hi [First Name],

[Personalized opening - reference their mission, a review, community involvement, specialization, or years in business]

I've been studying what separates the fastest-growing [industry] contractors from everyone else, and I found something interesting.

The top 15% are using AI and automation in ways most contractors haven't even considered yet - not just for one thing, but for completely transforming how they grow and operate.

The difference in results is honestly stunning. We're talking 25-40% growth without working longer hours.

I've mapped out the main strategies that are working right now in your local market, and I'd love to share what I'm seeing.

Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call? I can show you exactly what's possible for [Company Name] based on your specific situation.

Best,
Lorenzo

P.S. Even if it's not a fit, you'll walk away with actionable ideas you can use immediately.
```

**PERSONALIZATION:** The opening MUST reference something specific. Generic openings significantly reduce response rates.

### Email 2: AI Transformation + Scarcity (Day 4-5)

**Objective:** Frame AI adoption as inevitable, create urgency, introduce scarcity.

**Template:**
```
Subject: AI is transforming [industry] - early vs. late adopters

Hey [First Name],

The AI automation systems I mentioned are helping [industry] contractors generate 20-35% more leads AND streamline their operations.

AI is transforming every industry, and [industry] is no exception. Every smart contractor will eventually automate their business - the question is whether you're early or late to adopt.

Most contractors are still handling [emergency calls/service requests] manually, chasing [maintenance renewals/follow-ups] by hand, converting service calls to [larger jobs] the old way, and scheduling technicians with spreadsheets - while forward-thinking competitors implement AI automation and scale rapidly.

The contractors who implement complete AI-powered lead generation and operational automation now will dominate their markets while others scramble to catch up later.

I have 2 spots remaining this month. After that, enrollment closes until [next period].

Ready to future-proof [Company Name] with AI automation? Reply and I'll send my calendar.

Best,
Lorenzo
```

### Email 3: Breakup + Feedback (Day 7-10)

**Objective:** Create loss aversion, make responding easy, gather objection intelligence.

**Template:**
```
Subject: Last one from me

Hey [First Name],

I'm going to stop reaching out since I haven't heard back, but I'm genuinely curious - what made you decide against exploring growth strategies for [Company Name]?

Was it:
- Bad timing?
- Already have systems in place?
- Skeptical about new approaches?
- Happy with current growth rate?
- Something else?

No judgment at all - just helps me understand what [industry] contractors are prioritizing these days.

If things change and you want to explore what's possible for your business, just reply to this email.

Best,
Lorenzo
```

### Sequence Exit Conditions
- Lead replies - Move to appropriate response SOP
- Email bounces - Remove, flag for cleanup
- Lead books directly - Move to Booked
- Sequence completes with no response - Closed Lost ("No Response")

| AI mentioned | Yes | NEVER — talk about outcomes only |
| Links in emails | N/A | NO links — hurts deliverability |

### Campaign Rules & Constraints

**Tone & Voice:**
- Casual, peer-to-peer. Lorenzo talking to another business owner.
- No corporate speak, no buzzwords, no "leverage" or "synergy" or "cutting-edge."
- Write like a text message that got slightly longer. Short sentences.
- First-person singular ("I" not "we").

**Subject Lines:**
- Ambiguous and short. Must look like a personal email, not a campaign.
- Must NOT reveal it's a sales email. Must NOT mention AI, websites, or services.
- Goal: maximize open rate by looking like a human-to-human message.

**Email Body Rules:**
- 100-130 words per email. Conversational length.
- No links in outreach emails — link comes AFTER reply via automated sequence.
- No pricing, packages, or service menus.
- No "I'm reaching out because..." or "I hope this email finds you well."
- No bullet points or lists — pure conversational prose.
- Every email ends with a question or soft CTA inviting a reply.
- Do NOT use the word "AI" in the emails.
- Personalization via {firstName} and {companyName} merge tags.
- Frame as a "system" never a "website."

**What We're Selling (Without Saying It):**
- A conversion system that turns website visitors into booked jobs
- Built specifically for roofing companies
- Already live and clickable — not a concept
- Free to look at — no commitment to see the prototype

1. Email 1-4 (Days 1-7) → Generate a reply
2. Reply triggers automated sequence → Delivers live prototype link
3. After prototype is seen → Book decision call

## SOP-007: Discovery Call Framework

### One-Call Close Philosophy
Every call is structured to close on that call. You present price, ask for the decision, and handle objections in real-time. There is no follow-up call to "go over the proposal."

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** Leads who leave without making a decision almost never come back. They get busy, forget, or talk themselves out of it. The call is your best chance - use it.

### Call Structure

| Phase | Time | Goal |
|-------|------|------|
| Opening | 2-3 min | Rapport, set agenda |
| Discovery | 8-10 min | Situation, pain, quantify |
| Diagnosis | 2-3 min | Reflect back, get agreement |
| Solution | 5-7 min | Present as solution to their problems |
| Close | 5-8 min | Price, ask for decision, handle objections |

### Opening
```
"Hey [First Name], thanks for taking the time. Before we jump in, I want to make sure we use these 15 minutes well."

"I'd like to ask a few questions about how things are running at [Company Name] - specifically around leads and follow-up. Then, if it sounds like we can help, I'll show you exactly what we'd do and what it would cost."

"Sound good?"
```

Wait for confirmation. This gets buy-in on the structure AND that you'll discuss price.

### Discovery Questions

**Situation:**
- "Give me a quick overview - what's the size of your operation?"
- "How many calls/leads are you getting on a typical day?"
- "Who's handling those right now?"
- "What happens when a call comes in and nobody can answer?"

**Follow-Up Process:**
- "Once someone calls in, what's your follow-up process?"
- "If someone asks for a quote and doesn't book, what happens next?"
- "How are you tracking all this?"

**Quantify the Problem:**
- "Ballpark - how many calls go unanswered or to voicemail each week?"
- "What's your average job worth?"
- "So if even a few of those went to competitors, that's $X/week in lost revenue?"

### Diagnosis
```
"Let me make sure I've got this right."

"You're running [X trucks/techs], getting [Y calls/day], and right now [their process]. The challenge is [pain point]."

"Based on your average job at [$Z], if you're missing [N calls/week], that's potentially [$X] walking out the door."

"Does that sound about right?"
```

Get their agreement before presenting the solution.

### Solution Presentation
```
"Here's how we'd solve this for [Company Name]:"

"[Problem 1]: The AI system answers every call within 2 rings, 24/7. Qualifies them, books them, or notifies your team."

"[Problem 2]: Automated follow-up sequences so no lead falls through the cracks."

"[Problem 3]: Everything tracked in one place - you see where every lead is."

"If this captured even [conservative number] of those missed opportunities, that's [$X] in revenue you're currently leaving on the table."
```

### The Close
```
"So here's how we'd move forward:"

"Setup is [$X], which covers [what's included]. Monthly after that is [$Y] for ongoing service and support."

"We can have you up and running within [timeframe]."

"Want to get started?"
```

Then STOP TALKING. Let them respond.

**CRITICAL:** Do not offer to "send over some information" or "follow up with pricing." The price is on the table. Ask for the decision.

## SOP-008: Price Objection Handling

### Understanding Price Objections

| What They Say | What They Mean | Address By |
|---------------|----------------|------------|
| "Too expensive" | Don't see enough value | Rebuild value |
| "Can't afford it" | Not priority or genuinely can't | Payment options or qualify out |
| "Need to think about it" | Not convinced | Identify remaining concern |
| "Talk to partner" | Don't want to decide alone | Include partner |
| "Other priorities" | Not urgent | Quantify cost of waiting |

### The Framework

**Step 1: Acknowledge**
```
"I hear you. It's not a small investment, and I wouldn't expect you to say yes without thinking it through."
```

**Step 2: Isolate**
```
"Let me ask - if the price were exactly where you wanted it, is this something you'd move forward with? Or is there something else you're not sure about?"
```

If another concern emerges, address that first.

**Step 3: Reframe Value**
```
"You mentioned you're missing [X] calls per week. At [$Y] per job, if we captured even [small number] of those monthly, that's [$Z] in revenue."

"The system pays for itself after [breakeven]. Everything after that is profit you weren't getting."

"Does that math make sense?"
```

**Step 4: Ask Again**
```
"Given all that - what would you like to do?"
```

### Specific Objections

**"Need to think about it"**
```
"That makes sense - this isn't a small decision. Can I ask what specifically you want to think about? If there's something I haven't addressed, I'd rather answer it now while we're both here."
```

**"Need to talk to partner"**
```
"Absolutely. When do you think you'll talk with them? Would it help if I put together a quick summary, or should we schedule another call with both of you?"
```

### What NOT to Do
- Never immediately offer a discount - signals you weren't serious about your price
- Never argue or get defensive
- Never say "I understand" and ignore the concern
- Never rush past the objection - it will come back

## SOP-010: Daily Campaign Monitoring

### Daily Checklist

| Metric | Target | Alert If |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Emails Sent | Consistent volume | < 80% of target |
| Bounce Rate | < 3% | > 5% |
| Reply Rate | Stable/trending up | Drops > 30% |
| Positive Reply Rate | > 1% | < 0.5% for 3 days |
| Unsubscribe Rate | < 1% | > 2% |
| Spam Complaints | Near 0 | Any complaints |

### Troubleshooting

**Reply Rate Dropping:**
- Subject line fatigue - Test new variations
- List quality decline - Verify sources
- Deliverability issues - Check sender reputation
- Copy staleness - Refresh personalization

**High Bounce Rate:**
- Outdated data - Re-verify emails
- Bad source - Identify and replace
- Catch-all domains - Filter out

### Benchmarks

**Performance Targets:**

| Metric | Poor | OK | Good | Excellent |
|--------|------|-----|------|-----------|
| Reply Rate | < 0.5% | 0.5-1% | 1-2% | > 2% |
| Positive Rate | < 0.3% | 0.3-0.7% | 0.7-1.5% | > 1.5% |
| Bounce | > 5% | 3-5% | 1-3% | < 1% |
| Booking Rate | < 50% | 50-65% | 65-80% | > 80% |
| Show Rate | < 60% | 60-75% | 75-85% | > 85% |

## Appendix: Quick Reference

### A.1 Stage Flow

**Primary Path:**
New Lead → Contacted → Engaged → Booked → Discovery → Closed Won

**Alternative Paths:**
- Engaged → Non-Booker → Booked OR Closed Lost
- Booked → No-Show → Booked OR Closed Lost
- Discovery → "Think About It" sequence → Closed Won OR Closed Lost

### A.2 Sequence Summary

| Sequence | Touches | Duration | Verticals |
|----------|---------|----------|-----------|
| Cold Email (SOP-002) | 3 | 7-10 days | HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical |
| Cold Email — Roofing (SOP-002R) | 4 | 7 days + phone escalation Days 8-14 | Roofing |
| Non-Booker | 4 | 12 days | All |
| No-Show | 3 | 48 hours | All |
| Verbal Commit | 3 | 3 days | All |
| Think About It | 3 | 7 days | All |
| Ghost | 2 | 10 days | All |

### A.3 Discovery Call Cheat Sheet

| Phase | Key Actions |
|-------|-------------|
| Open | Set agenda, confirm they know you'll discuss price |
| Discover | Size, volume, process, what happens to missed calls |
| Quantify | Calls missed x job value = $ lost |
| Diagnose | Reflect back, get agreement |
| Present | Map features to their problems |
| Close | State price, ask "Want to get started?", STOP TALKING |
