# No-show recovery: final set (section 2)

Edited 2026-09-23 from `drafts/N1.md` to `drafts/N8.md` against `writer_brief.md` ("The no-show sequence") and the bottom line of `research/no_show_recovery.md`. Every email goes as a reply in the lead's original cold-email thread, from the inbox they have been talking to. The subject lines below are only used when a lead has no thread. Any reply from the lead stops the sequence.

What a lead receives, all timed from the scheduled start S:
- First missed call: N1 (S+3 min), N2 (Lorenzo's own call, S+5 to S+8), N3 (about S+25), N4 (next business day), N5 (day 3 or 4), N6 (day 7), N7 (day 14), then the long-term nurture.
- Second missed call: N1, N2, the N3 second-no-show variant, N6 on day 3 or 4, then nurture.
- Third missed call: N1, then N8, then nurture.

Editor's call on N2: the voicemail and the hand-typed text point to the email and promise two times there; they name no times. N3 delivers the two times about 20 minutes later, so the pair reads as one promise kept rather than the same offer twice, Lorenzo types nothing time-specific on his phone, and {slots} appears in one place only.

Placeholders used: {first_name}, {company}, {time}, {call_link}, {booking_url}, {slots}. {niche_point} in N5 is filled with the campaign's line from the list under N5, not from lead data.

## Set check
Openers, in the order a lead could see them: "I'm on the call now, no rush." (N1), "Looks like we didn't connect today." (N3), "Today didn't line up, and no problem if now is a bad time." (N3 variant), "I can do {slots} if you still want to find 15 minutes." (N4), "One thing I was going to walk you through on our call:" (N5), "Quick one on the call: still want to chat, later, or not a fit?" (N6), "I'll leave this with you, no need to reply." (N7), "Seems like the timing isn't right at the moment, and that's fine." (N8). None repeats another, and none echoes the reminder picks (R1 "Here's the short version", R2 "Still good for tomorrow?", R3 "Morning.", R4 "Talk in an hour.").

Booking-link closers: "pick a different time here" (N3), "here's my calendar" (N3 variant), "everything else I have open is here" (N4), "take any other time here" (N5), "pick any time here" (N6), "the link still works" (N7). One ask per message; the asks get lighter from N3 (two times) to N7 (no ask beyond the link).

Hard rules: no em or en dashes, no exclamation marks, no emojis, no bullets, no mention of automation, no guilt, no counts of missed calls, no invented facts, only listed placeholders, every email 20 to 70 words with "Hey {first_name}," and "Lorenzo" on their own lines. The N2 text is 151 characters with a four-letter first name.

Claudeism pass (clauses whose only job was to restate the sentence before them; cut, not rewritten): "and I'll give you a ring" (N1), "so you can grab whatever fits your week" (N3 variant), "and it's yours" (N5), "so it starts from companies you have history with, not a cold list" (N5 recruiting line), "and I'll check back then" (N6), "and we'll pick it back up" (N8). Kept: "All good, things come up." (N3) and "and that's fine" (N8), because they carry the no-blame stance rather than restate a point.
