# N2: Lorenzo's own call right after a no-show, the voicemail he leaves and the text he sends after it
Timing: S+5 to S+8 minutes, after the N1 nudge got no answer; Lorenzo calls from his phone, leaves the voicemail if there is no answer, then texts by hand  Channel: voicemail + text (manual, not sent by the system)

Hey {first_name}, it's Lorenzo Villarreal about our {time} call. I'll keep the link open a few more minutes in case you can hop in. If not, no worries, I'll send new times in our email thread.

Text:
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo here, I'm keeping the call open a few more minutes if you can jump in: {call_link} If not, no worries, new times are coming by email.

Voicemail: unchanged.

Text:

## Version B
Voicemail (35 words, about 15 seconds):
Hi {first_name}, it's Lorenzo Villarreal, about our {time} call today. Totally fine if something came up. I'll email you two new times in the thread where we set this up, just reply with whichever works.

Text:
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo here, about our {time} call. No problem at all, two new times will be in your email shortly, same thread as before.

## Version C
Voicemail (34 words, about 15 seconds):
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo Villarreal here, about our call at {time}. No problem at all, things come up. I'm texting you two new times right now, and I'll put them in our email thread too.

Text:

## Pick
C because they are on their phone right then, and two real times are the easiest thing to answer from a text, one word back and it is booked.
A mostly repeats N1's "hop on now" three minutes later, and B only points them back to an email that has not arrived yet.

## Notes for Lorenzo (optional, max 3 short lines)
