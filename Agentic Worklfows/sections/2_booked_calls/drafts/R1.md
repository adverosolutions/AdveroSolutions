# R1: pre-read wrapper (subject, opening, cell-number ask) around the one-page offer note
Timing: 15 minutes after booking when the call is 0 to 2 days away; about 48 hours before the call when it is 3 or more days away  Channel: email

## Version A
Subject: before our call
Hey {first_name},

Here's the short version of what I'll walk through on {when}, so the call itself can be about {company} and not about me.

[the offer sections, unchanged]

Last thing: what's the best cell number for you? If either of us is running a few minutes late, we can text instead of guessing.

Lorenzo

## Version B
Subject: quick read before we talk
Hey {first_name},

Before we talk on {when}, here's the one-pager. Worth a skim so we can spend the 15 minutes on {company}.

[the offer sections, unchanged]

Can you reply with your best cell number? That way if one of us is running behind, a quick text sorts it out.

Lorenzo

## Version C
Subject: for our call
Hey {first_name},

Sending this ahead of {when} so you know what I'll cover and what I'll ask.

[the offer sections, unchanged]

What's the best cell to reach you on? Just in case either of us is running late.

Lorenzo

## Pick
A because the opening keeps Lorenzo's own line from today's note, reads right at 15 minutes or 48 hours out, and still works when {company} falls back to "your business".
The ask is a direct question with a plain, two-sided reason, so it invites a typed reply without sounding like a form.

## Notes for Lorenzo (optional, max 3 short lines)
Wrapper is about 50 words in A (B and C shorter), not counting the offer sections.
Today's prompt files use {when_ct}; the brief's placeholder is {when}, so the editor should align the files.
None of the versions say "tomorrow" or "just booked", so one template covers both timings.
