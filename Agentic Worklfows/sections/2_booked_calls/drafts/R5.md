# R5: Lorenzo's own call to a lead who has been quiet since booking, the voicemail he leaves and the text he sends after it
Timing: 4 hours before the call, only if the lead has not replied since booking; Lorenzo calls from his phone, leaves the voicemail if there is no answer, then texts by hand  Channel: voicemail + text (manual, not sent by the system)

## Version A
Voicemail (34 words, about 15 seconds):
Hey {first_name}, it's Lorenzo Villarreal. We've got 15 minutes on the books today at {time}. I'll text you right after this, and if the time doesn't work anymore, just reply with a better one.

Text:
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo here, just called. Link for {time} today: {call_link} If it doesn't work anymore, reply with a better time.

## Version A, no call link
Voicemail: unchanged.

Text:
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo here, just called. The link for {time} today is on your calendar invite, and if the time doesn't work anymore, reply with a better one.

## Version B
Voicemail (33 words, about 15 seconds):
Hi {first_name}, Lorenzo Villarreal here, about our call today at {time}. If that still works, no need to call back. If today got busy, text me a better time and I'll move it.

Text:
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo here, that was me calling. If today got away from you, text me a better time, otherwise see you at {time}: {call_link}

## Version B, no call link
Voicemail: unchanged.

Text:
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo here, that was me calling. If today got away from you, text me a better time, otherwise see you at {time}, the link is on your calendar invite.

## Version C
Voicemail (35 words, about 15 seconds):
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo Villarreal here. We set up a quick call over email for today at {time}. I'll text you the details. If something came up, just reply to that text with a better time.

Text:
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo here, we emailed about today's call at {time}. Here's the link: {call_link} If something came up, reply with a better time.

## Version C, no call link
Voicemail: unchanged.

Text:
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo here, we emailed about today's call at {time}. The link is on your calendar invite, and if something came up, reply with a better time.

## Pick
B because "that was me calling" ties the text to a missed call from a number they have not saved, and "no need to call back" gives a quiet lead who still plans to show up nothing to do.
Its text puts the easy out first and ends on the default, "see you at {time}", with the link last so it is one tap.

## Notes for Lorenzo (optional, max 3 short lines)
When you say {time} on the voicemail, use their local time if the task shows it ("three o'clock your time"); the text keeps CT.
With a four-letter first name and a standard Meet link, the B text is about 164 characters and the A text about 153. That is fine from your own phone.
If the voicemail box is full, send the text on its own. All three still make sense without the voicemail.
