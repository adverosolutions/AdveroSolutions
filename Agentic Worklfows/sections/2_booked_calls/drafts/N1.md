# N1: "I'm on" nudge so a lead who is running late can still join, or get a phone call instead
Timing: S+3 minutes, only if they have not joined  Channel: email

## Version A
Subject: I'm on
Hey {first_name},

I'm on the call now, no rush. Here's the link:
{call_link}

Or want me to just call you? Reply with a good number and I'll give you a ring.

Lorenzo

## Version A, no call link (only if the message shows {call_link})
Hey {first_name},

I'm on the call now, no rush. The link is on your calendar invite.

Or want me to just call you? Reply with a good number and I'll give you a ring.

Lorenzo

Lorenzo

## Pick
A because it is Lorenzo's own line from the spec ("I'm on... want me to just call you?") and reads like a text, 31 words.
"No rush" takes away any sense of being late, and the link sits alone on its line so it taps cleanly on a phone.

## Notes for Lorenzo (optional, max 3 short lines)
If they already gave a cell after R1, the call line still works (they just reply "call me"), and your N2 call at S+5 may beat their reply anyway.
No {time} or {when} on purpose: the call is now, and naming the time would point at the late start.
The "I'm on" / "I'm in the room" openers don't echo R2 to R4, which open on "still good", "today at" and "talk in an hour".
