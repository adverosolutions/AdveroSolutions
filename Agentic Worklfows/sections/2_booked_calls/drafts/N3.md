# N3: reschedule after a missed call, two new times or the booking link, plus a link-only variant for a second missed call
Timing: about S+25, once the call is clearly missed (second-no-show variant: same timing, when this is the second call they have missed)  Channel: email

## Version A
Subject: new time for our call
Hey {first_name},

Looks like we didn't connect today. All good, things come up.

Could we do {slots}? Just reply with the one that works, or pick a different time here: {booking_url}

Lorenzo

## Version A, second no-show
Subject: whenever works for you
Hey {first_name},

Today didn't line up, and no problem if now is a bad time.

Rather than me picking times, here's my calendar so you can grab one that fits your week: {booking_url}

Lorenzo

## Version B
Subject: another time
Hey {first_name},

Guessing something came up. Totally fine.

I can do {slots}. Reply with the one that works for you, or grab another time here: {booking_url}

Lorenzo

## Version B, second no-show
Subject: your pick
Hey {first_name},

No problem if now is a bad time. Weeks get away from everyone.

Whenever you want to pick this back up, choose a time that suits you here: {booking_url}

Lorenzo

## Version C
Subject: rescheduling our call
Hey {first_name},

Something must have come up, no worries at all.

Two times I can do: {slots}. Reply with whichever works best, or pick your own here: {booking_url}

Lorenzo

## Version C, second no-show
Subject: when it suits you
Hey {first_name},

We didn't catch each other today. No problem if now is a bad time.

Here's my calendar so you can pick whatever time suits you: {booking_url}

Lorenzo

## Pick
Main: A because "we didn't connect" makes the miss mutual, with no blame on the lead, and "things come up" is the brief's own stance.
"Could we do {slots}?" reads naturally once the times render, and the reply ask and the link are one sentence, so it is one ask.
Second no-show: A because "rather than me picking times" explains why no times are offered without counting the missed calls.
It shares no wording with A's main opener, so a lead who got both does not see the same email twice.

## Notes for Lorenzo (optional, max 3 short lines)
The second-no-show variant goes to someone who already got N3 once, so in every version it avoids the main email's opener and phrases.
"Today" assumes the S+25 send; if a send can slip to the next day, drop "today" and A still reads right.
None mention N2's voicemail, because you may not have called or left one.
