## {niche_point} lines (one per offer)
Cleaning, reactivation: I'd set up emails and texts to every account that cancelled, stopped renewing or never closed, and the ones ready to talk land on your calendar.

Bookkeeping, reactivation: I'd reach back out to your lost inquiries and former clients, timed around tax season, quarterly estimates and year-end cleanup.

Recruiting, reactivation: I'd set up follow-up to your dormant and near-miss client accounts, so it starts from companies you have history with, not a cold list.

Cleaning, outbound: I'd watch for new leases, office expansions, property-manager changes and new construction, then reach out under your name and put walkthroughs on your calendar.

Bookkeeping, outbound: I'd watch for new business formations, part-time bookkeeper job posts and second locations, then reach out to those businesses under your name.

Recruiting, outbound: I'd watch job boards for open roles in your specialties, then reach out to the hiring managers under your agency's name.

## Pick
A because "was going to walk you through on our call" gives a plain reason for writing on day 3 or 4 without any blame, and every line reads naturally after its colon.
"If that's worth 15 minutes to you" is the softest ask of the three, and "say which and it's yours" avoids N3's "reply with the one that works".

## Notes for Lorenzo (optional, max 3 short lines)
Each line is one sentence starting "I'd" (20 to 25 words), so every version stays under 70 words with {slots} rendered, and any line fits any version.
Lines stick to how it works (no price, no guarantee, no proof) to keep it one point; the proof list is all roofing, lending and consulting, so none of it is about these niches.
Cleaning reactivation says "your calendar" (the table says "the calendar"); switch to "the calendar" if you meant yours.
