# N6: one question, three easy answers (still want to chat, later, or not a fit)
Timing: day 7 after the missed call; also sent unchanged on day 3 or 4 after a second no-show  Channel: email

## Version A
Subject: still worth a chat?
Hey {first_name},

Straight question so I know where you're at: is a 15-minute call still worth it for {company}, better later on, or not a fit?

If you still want it, grab a time here: {booking_url}. If later, tell me roughly when. Any of the three is a good answer.

Lorenzo

## Version B
Subject: one question
Hey {first_name},

One question on our call: still want to chat, later, or not a fit?

A one-word answer is plenty. If it's later, just tell me roughly when and I'll check back then. If you still want to chat, pick any time here: {booking_url}

Lorenzo

## Version C
Subject: timing
Hey {first_name},

Timing is usually the whole thing, so I'll just ask. Should we still find 15 minutes, pick this up later (just say when), or call it not a fit for {company}?

If it's the first, any open time here works: {booking_url}

All three are fine answers.

Lorenzo

## Pick
B because it asks the three answers in the words the lead would type back, and says outright that one word is enough.
It names "our call" so it reads right as a thread reply or a new email, and never counts missed calls.

## Notes for Lorenzo (optional, max 3 short lines)

A "not a fit" reply should skip the long-term nurture, or at least go in with a flag.
