## Pick
A because it sounds most like Lorenzo texting, and "either answer is fine" makes it easy for a silent lead to finally reply.
B's reply-with-a-number can read like a survey, and a 7th email shouldn't feel automated.

## Per-offer lines (only if this email needs them)
None needed. All three versions work for all six offers.
