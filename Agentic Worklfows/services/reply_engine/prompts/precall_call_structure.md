# One-call close, 15 minutes (SOP-007)

| Phase | Time | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 1 to 2 min | Rapport, set the agenda, get buy-in that price is on this call |
| Discovery | 5 to 6 min | Situation, pain, quantify it in their own numbers |
| Diagnosis | 1 to 2 min | Reflect it back, get them to agree out loud |
| Solution | 3 to 4 min | The mechanism as the answer to what they just said |
| Close | 3 to 4 min | Price, ask for the decision, handle the objection, sign today |

## Opening
"Hey {first_name}, thanks for taking the time. Before we jump in, I want to make sure we use these 15 minutes well. I would like to ask a few questions about how {company} gets work and what happens to the ones that do not close. Then, if it sounds like I can help, I will show you exactly what I would do and what it costs. Sound good?"

Wait for the yes. That yes is buy-in on the structure and on talking about money.

## Discovery
- "Give me the quick overview. How big is the operation right now?"
- "Where does new work come from today?"
- "When someone asks for a quote or a proposal and then goes quiet, what happens next?"
- "Who is following up, and with what?"
- "Ballpark, how many of those go quiet in a month?"
- "What is one of those worth to you if it closes?"

Write the two numbers down. They are the close.

## Diagnosis
"Let me make sure I have this right. You are running {size}, work comes from {source}, and when a proposal goes quiet {what_happens}. At {value} each, {count} a month going quiet is {math} walking out the door. Does that sound about right?"

Get the agreement before you present anything.

## Solution
Say the mechanism for their offer, in one breath, then stop. Use the mechanism line in this brief verbatim. Then one temperature check: "Does that sound like it would work with how you run things?"

## Close
State the number, then stop talking.
- Reactivation: "There is nothing up front. I take a cut of what comes back, and nothing if nothing closes."
- Trigger or outbound: "It is a setup fee plus a monthly retainer, and the guarantee covers it."

Then: "Want to get started?" and say nothing until they answer.

Never offer to send information or follow up with pricing. The number is on the table. Ask for the decision.

## If they say yes
Payment link goes out within the hour, deposit terms as written in the proposal, onboarding form after that. The agreement is signed today, not "after I send something over".
