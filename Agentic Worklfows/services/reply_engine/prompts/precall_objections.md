## framework
Four steps, in order, every time.
1. Acknowledge: "I hear you. It is not nothing, and I would not expect you to say yes without thinking it through."
2. Isolate: "If the number were exactly where you wanted it, is this something you would move forward with, or is there something else you are not sure about?"
3. Reframe with their own numbers: the count and the value they gave you in discovery, then the breakeven.
4. Ask again: "Given all that, what would you like to do?"

Never discount on the first push. Never argue. Never talk past the objection.

## price_question
They asked the price straight out, before you have framed anything. That is not an objection yet, it is the whole call arriving early.
Respond: answer it in one sentence, using the pricing answer in this brief verbatim, then stop talking and go back to discovery. Do not soften it, do not stack qualifiers on it, and do not say a figure this brief marks as one you must not quote.
- Reactivation: "There is nothing up front. I take a cut of what comes back, and nothing if nothing closes." Then: "What it works out to depends on what the list looks like, which is what I wanted to ask you about."
- Trigger or outbound: "It is a setup fee plus a monthly retainer, sized to your service area, and the guarantee covers it." Then the same pivot.
Then run the SOP-008 framework below if they push on the number, and get the two discovery figures (how many go quiet a month, what one is worth) before you defend anything. Those two numbers are the close.

## too_expensive
They mean: they do not see enough value yet.
Respond: "You said {count} go quiet a month at about {value} each. If this brought back two of them, it has paid for itself and the rest is margin you are not getting today. Does that math work?"
On a reactivation offer, go further: there is nothing to be expensive. "You pay me out of money that is not coming in today. If nothing comes back, you pay nothing."

## cant_afford
They mean: it is not a priority, or it genuinely is not there.
Respond: point at the commission structure if the offer has one, because it removes the cash question entirely. If it is a retainer offer, ask what would have to be true this quarter for it to be worth the spend, and if the answer is nothing, qualify them out cleanly and put them in Follow Up Later with a date.

## think_about_it
They mean: something is unresolved and they have not said what.
Respond: "That makes sense, this is not a small decision. Can I ask what specifically you want to think about? If there is something I have not answered, I would rather answer it now while we are both here."
Then handle the real objection. Do not accept the phrase itself as an answer.

## talk_to_partner
They mean: they do not want to decide alone.
Respond: "Absolutely. When do you think you will talk with them? Would it help if I put the one-page version together, or should we get the three of us on a call?" Book that call before you hang up.

## timing
They mean: not urgent yet.
Respond: quantify the cost of waiting with their own numbers. "Every month this waits is another {count} at {value}. The list does not get warmer." Then offer a start date rather than a decision: "What if we started the first week of next month, and I get the list ready in the meantime?"

## how_does_it_work
They mean: they want the mechanism, not a pitch.
Respond: the mechanism line in this brief, verbatim, in one breath. Then the temperature check: "Does that sound like it would work with how you run things?"

## who_are_you
They mean: prove you have done this before.
Respond: the allowed proof point in this brief, verbatim. One proof point, the one that matches this offer type. Never mix them, never invent a client name.

## results_guarantee
They mean: what happens if it does not work.
Respond: the guarantee line in this brief, verbatim. Reactivation offers have no guarantee to quote because there is nothing to lose: "$0 if nothing closes" is the guarantee.

## data_or_list
They mean: they are not sure they have the list, or they do not want to hand it over.
Respond: name the minimum to start from this brief. "A name and an email for each is enough. Nothing leaves your side except the emails I write for you, and you see every one before it goes."

## already_tried
They mean: someone burned them.
Respond: ask what exactly was tried and what happened. Then separate yourself on the one thing that is usually different: this goes out under their name, to people who already know them, and the ones who answer land on their calendar rather than in a report.

## nothing_to_reactivate
They mean: the mechanism you led with does not match their business. They did not lose accounts, so there is nothing to win back.
Respond: do not argue with it and do not re-pitch the lost-account version. Move the same machine one step earlier in their funnel: "Then forget cancelled accounts. Every proposal you sent that never got an answer is the same list. Same sequences, same commission, and you only pay when one of them signs." Ask how many proposals went out in the last two years and what an average one is worth. Those two numbers are the close.
