# LinkedIn outreach copy kit

Written 2026-10-06. Lorenzo records and sends everything himself. Method from Jonatan's playbook (Maker School): blank connection request, a personal Loom about 1 minute long once they accept, three text follow-ups, a voice note months later. Lorenzo's voice rules win wherever Jonatan's style differs (no exclamation marks, no emoji in his own messages, no em dashes, sentence case).

Placeholders (fixed, filled per lead): `{first_name}`, `{role}`, `{company}`, `{what_they_do}` (3-8 words, e.g. "commercial cleaning in Fort Worth"). `{slot1}` and `{slot2}` appear only in the reply-with-times template, written in the lead's time zone with its label when known ("Thursday at 1pm ET").

Two variants, split tested in batches of about 100:
- **A, Reactivation**: the follow-up and win-back agent. Lead needs 60 or more lost or dormant accounts.
- **B, Reply and booking / outbound**: the reply agent plus the research agent.

A lead gets one variant from the connection to the voice note. Do not switch them mid-sequence.

## 1. Connection request

Sent blank, no note.

## 2. Loom scripts

Record with the lead's LinkedIn profile open on screen, camera bubble on. Download the Loom and attach the file in the DM so it shows as "Sent a video" (not a link). Aim for 60 to 75 seconds.

### Variant A, Reactivation (169 spoken words, each placeholder counted as one; a 5-word {what_they_do} makes it 173)

**[Greeting]**
Hey {first_name}, Lorenzo here. Thanks for taking the time to watch this.

**[Qualification]**
I see you're the {role} at {company}, doing {what_they_do}. Quick question. Do you have at least sixty past customers, lost proposals or old leads sitting around? If not, feel free to click off this video.

**[Dream outcome]**
If you do, I'd like to win some of those accounts back for {company} over the next six months.

**[How]**
I set up an AI agent that follows up with every one of them, under your name. When someone's ready, they get booked right onto your calendar.

**[Risk reversal]**
And there's nothing up front. I take a share of what comes back, and if nothing closes, you don't pay.

**[Proof]**
I built a similar system for a commercial roofer doing $150 million a year. I recovered about $6 million worth of contracts within six months.

**[Reminder + CTA]**
So if you want some of those old accounts back on your calendar, just reply with a thumbs up and I'll send over a couple of times.

**[Sign-off]**
Thanks, {first_name}. Talk soon.

## 6. Reply templates

Lorenzo sends each one himself. After a yes, he sends the calendar invite to the email they give him. Never a booking link.

**Thumbs up or "yes" (both variants)**

Hey {first_name}, sounds good. Would {slot1} or {slot2} work for a quick 15-minute call? Just reply with the one that works and the best email for the calendar invite.

**"What does it cost?"**

A:
Hey {first_name}, the short version is there's nothing up front. I take a share of what comes back, and if nothing closes, you don't pay. The rest is easier to cover on a quick call. Want me to send over a couple of times?

B:
Hey {first_name}, the short version is it comes with a written guarantee for your market. If the results fall short of that guarantee, you get your money back plus a steakhouse gift card. The rest is easier to cover on a quick call. Want me to send over a couple of times?

**"Send me more info"**

A:
Hey {first_name}, sure. I set up an AI agent that follows up with your past customers, lost proposals and old leads under your name, and books the ones who are ready onto your calendar. It's easier to walk you through it on a 15-minute call. Want me to send over a couple of times?

B:
Hey {first_name}, sure. I set up an AI agent that answers every reply the minute it lands, offers two times and books the call, plus a second one that reaches new accounts when they need you, under your name. It's easier to walk you through it on a 15-minute call. Want me to send over a couple of times?

**"What exactly is this?"**

A:
Hey {first_name}, fair question. I build AI agents for service businesses that close new work on a call, a walkthrough or a quote. The one in the video goes after every account that cancelled, stopped renewing, went quiet or never closed, and books the ready ones on your calendar. You don't pay anything up front. Want me to send over a couple of times to walk you through it?

B:
Hey {first_name}, fair question. I build AI agents for service businesses that close new work on a call, a walkthrough or a quote. There are two in the video. One answers every reply the minute it lands, offers two times and books the call, and the other reaches new accounts when they need you. Everything goes out under your name. Want me to send over a couple of times to walk you through it?

**"Not now" (both variants)**

Hey {first_name}, no problem at all. When would be a better time for me to check back in?

Then message them on the date they give. No more follow-ups before it.

**"Not interested" (both variants)**

Hey {first_name}, thanks for letting me know. Best of luck with {company}.

Then stop: no follow-ups, no voice note.

**"Someone else handles this" (both variants)**

Hey {first_name}, thanks for pointing me the right way. Would you mind introducing me to the person who handles this? An intro here on LinkedIn would be great, or I can reach out to them directly.

## 7. Tests to run

- **A vs B.** Alternate batches of about 100 accepted connections. Track per batch: replies, thumbs ups, calls booked, calls held. Call it after about 100 per variant.
- **US vs other English-speaking markets.** Jonatan saw about 10% replies in the US and much higher in the UK and Dubai. Run one batch per variant on UK, Canada, Australia and UAE service businesses next to a US batch. Times in `{slot1}` and `{slot2}` go in their local zone, and the usual 12:00-18:00 CT window falls outside working hours in the UK, UAE and Australia, so those leads need different CT times (Lorenzo's call).
