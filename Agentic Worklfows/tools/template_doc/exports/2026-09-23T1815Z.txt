Every email the system sends
Every email and text the system sends, in the order a lead meets them.
How to use it: Change any words you like. Keep the words in {curly brackets}: they are filled in for each lead. When you are done, tell Claude 'done'. Nothing goes live until you say so.
When you say 'done', everything in this doc counts as approved. Emails still only go out after you tell Claude 'go live'.
If a sentence appears in many emails (like the time-offer line in first replies), changing it once is enough; it changes everywhere. If you change two copies differently, Claude will ask.
Leave the small grey tags at the end of each heading as they are: they tell Claude which email is which. Grey lines marked note or section are only there to help you read.
Words in brackets: {first_name} their first name, {company} their company, {slots} two open times, {slot} one time, {booking_url} your booking link, {zoom_link} the call link, {loom_url} your Loom video, {original_subject} the subject of their email. The cold emails use Instantly's double brackets, like {{firstName}}.
If we don't have their first name, 'Hey {first_name},' becomes 'Hey there,'. If we don't have their company name, {company} becomes 'your business' ('your team' in the Day 2, 4, 5 and 30 follow-ups). In a referral reply with no name, {referral_name} becomes 'the right person' and {referral_first_name} becomes 'them'.
How times are written
Every time is Central (CT). The engine writes them like this:
{slots}: Tue 12:05pm or Thu 4:30pm CT (just Tue 12:05pm CT when only one time is free)
{slot}: Tue 12:05pm (the email adds CT after it)
{slot1} and {slot2}: Tue 12:05pm CT
{when_ct}: Tue, Jan 8 at 12:05pm CT, and in another timezone Tue, Jan 8 at 12:05pm CT (1:05pm ET)
{time_ct}: 12:05pm CT
{asked_time}: the time they asked for, in their own zone, like Mon 10:45am ET
{lead_local_time}: their own time, like 1:05pm ET (only added when they are not on Central)
{alt_slots}: like {slots}; when the times are in another week the dates are added, like Tue Jan 15 12:05pm or Thu Jan 17 4:30pm CT
Zones are written CT, ET, MT, PT, and MST for Arizona.
