# Runbook: daily follow-up cadence and booking (Blueprint S2, S3; brief A2)

## Purpose
Once Lorenzo's first reply to a positive lead has gone out (day 0), this system keeps the thread alive every business day with fresh calendar times until the lead books, replies, or asks to stop, and books the call the moment a reply names a workable time.

| Piece | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Cadence engine | `services/reply_engine/cadence.py` | `arm`, `due`, `build_touch` (E1..E10), `tick_lead`, `detect_manual_first_reply`, fresh-slot selection and release, stop rules, E1 veto window, nurture handoff |
| Touch copy | `services/reply_engine/prompts/cadence_e*.md`, `cadence_research.md` | One template per email; by default (`cadence.E2_RESEARCH_MODE = "fallback"`) E2's research line is the fixed fallback line, and no page is read and no model called. With `"llm"` the E2 research step runs (see "E2 research line" below) |
| E2 research step | `services/reply_engine/research.py`, `research_rules.py` | Reads the lead's own site, confirms identity by code, one `shared.llm` role `research` call, excerpt check, then the research rules; any failure is the fallback line |
| Booking | `services/reply_engine/booking.py` | `match_reply_to_slot`, `evaluate_reply` (verdict + counter-proposal), `book` (GHL appointment + Call Booked + Instantly status 2 + confirmation draft) |
| Hourly job | `jobs/cadence_tick.py` | Sweeps `lead:*` in the store, sends due touches (guarded), Job_Log row, one digest to Lorenzo |

The create enrols nobody: `conditions` ships with empty matchers |

After E10 the ladder is exhausted: the lead moves to GHL Follow Up Later and is enrolled in the 90-day nurture (`no_reply_after_sequence`, `runbooks/nurture.md`). A "not now" reply stops the ladder and enrols the lead as `not_now_reply`. No free times for a touch: the tick retries the next day, alerts on the 2nd miss (`cadence.NO_SLOTS_ALERT_MISSES`) and stops the ladder as `slots_unavailable` on the 5th (`cadence.NO_SLOTS_STOP_MISSES`).

Rule (2026-09-22): an engine email (first reply, counter-proposal, booking confirmation, cadence touch, show-rate or no-show message) is sent automatically only when its text is exactly a template Lorenzo approved with jobs/approve_templates.py and unchanged since, the template kill switch is off, and every send check passes (copy gate, opt-out marker, lead reply hold, 07:00-19:00 CT send window, recipient check, slot rules); anything else goes to Lorenzo as an approval card (replies) or is held (cadence, show-rate, no-show). So an email goes out on its own only once its template and its spliced line (the ids in the table above) are approved (`runbooks/template_approvals.md`). A day whose template is not approved, or whose text is not an auto-sendable form of it, is **held** (`held_template`), never a card: the touch is not sent, the hold is recorded on the lead (`cadence["template_hold"]`), reported once a day in the digest and counted in the auto-send FYI. A held E1 also drops its Veto ping, so a fix opens a fresh 30-minute window rather than sending behind an old ping. In live, E1 gets the full check (exact subject and body, the lead's values bound) before its Veto window opens, so a E1 the gate would refuse sends no "sends at" ping; after a refused check the window opens again only once something the check looks at has changed (the gate's rules version and catalogue included), or after a Resume, which drops the refusal. A gate-wide hold (`kill_switch`, `parity_failed`, `registry_error`) holds every ladder, never counts toward a stall and never stops a ladder: the day goes out late once it clears. Any other hold counts once per business day toward `cadence.TEMPLATE_HOLD_STALL_DAYS` (3), whatever the reasons and in any order (a gate-wide hold in between neither counts nor resets the count), and the next such hold after 3 counted days stops the ladder as `template_unapproved`, with one high-priority push ("Cadence stopped: template <id> not auto-sendable") worded for the reason that stopped it: ids and counts on ntfy, and the lead and a signed Resume link (`/cadence/resume`, confirm page then POST) in the push itself on a protected topic, else on the email leg only; with neither, the push says what to set and no link is recorded. While it stays stopped a fresh link is sent the same way every day, and at once when the held ids pass the gate, unless the lead is done with the ladder (opted out, blocklisted, booked, hostile or not now). After Resume the next tick runs the gate and every send check again. See `runbooks/template_approvals.md`, "Cadence holds, stalls and resume". The gate runs in shadow too, so the shadow preview is the live run.

Rules enforced in code: slots 12:00-18:00 CT Mon-Fri, at least 4 h out, within 5 business days, never a slot already offered to this lead or to any lead (reservations are keyed on the slot itself in the rolling `offered_slots` registry that `approval.py` also writes; `offered:<date>` is an audit trail only); one touch per lead per day; no weekends; sends only 07:00-19:00 CT and never before 10:00; stop on booking, on any inbound reply after arming (flags `needs_fresh_draft` for A1), on unsubscribe/hostile; `negative_notnow` moves the opportunity to Follow Up Later. Copy: no em dashes, no exclamation marks, never mentions AI or automation, signed "Lorenzo", same thread and inbox (`reply_to_uuid` = the lead's last inbound email id, `eaccount` = the original sending inbox).

## How to run (shadow)
`MODE=shadow` is the default. Every send is a `would_do` record in `.tmp/shadow_log.jsonl`; no HTTP write is made to Instantly or GHL.

```bash
cd "/Users/lorenzovillarreal/Desktop/AdveroSolutions/Agentic Worklfows"

# plan only: no store writes, no sends, no notifications
python3 -m jobs.cadence_tick --dry-run --bodies
python3 -m jobs.cadence_tick --dry-run --now 2026-09-22T10:05:00-05:00 --store /path/to/store.json

# shadow tick (what the hourly job does): persists cadence state, writes would_do records, sends Lorenzo a "[shadow]" digest
python3 -m jobs.cadence_tick
tail -5 .tmp/shadow_log.jsonl; tail -3 .tmp/job_log.csv

# static fallback subsequences: read-only diff against live Instantly (GET /subsequences only)
python3 changesets/instantly_subsequences/apply_subsequences.py --dry-run
```

How the ladder starts (day 0 = Lorenzo's own first reply, Lorenzo 2026-09-23): the engine never sends a first reply; it drafts one (two held times, no booking link) on a card and Lorenzo sends it himself from the Instantly thread. The hourly tick finds that send: for every unarmed lead with no `reply_sent` / `reply_sent_manually` on its history, a `thread_id` and inbox, a newest card answering a reply the lead sent within the last 14 days (`reply_too_old` otherwise, checked before any read) and still `pending`, `blocked`, `stale_slots` or `expired`, and a ladder that is not over for it (`cadence.terminal_reason`: booked, unsubscribed, hostile, not now or opted out), it makes one read-only `GET /emails` (`email_type=manual`, the inbox, the campaign; at most `cadence.FIRST_REPLY_MAX_READS` = 10 per tick, newest reply first) and looks for an email Lorenzo sent by hand (`ue_type` 3; a campaign step or scheduled send is not his reply) in the same thread after the reply the card answers (`cadence.first_reply_cutoff`: that reply's history entry, else the card's `reply_at`, and only when neither has a time `last_inbound_at`, which an auto-responder's answer to his reply moves) (`cadence.detect_manual_first_reply`). After the first Instantly read failure in a tick the remaining leads wait for the next tick. On a find it records what Mark sent records (`last_outbound_at` = the email's time, the card's two held times on `offered_slots`, a `reply_sent_manually` history entry with `source: "thread"` and the Instantly email id), takes the lead out of the 90-day nurture (`re_engaged`) if enrolled, and arms the ladder with day 0 = the send time, so E1 is the next business day at 10:00 CT (Friday 16:30 -> Monday). The write is under the lead row's lock and records nothing when a reply of ours is already on the row by then; `approval.arm_refusal` still applies (the lead wrote again or another card of theirs is open: recorded, not armed). The digest says "N ladders started after your reply" (counts only); an Instantly error is counted, makes the Job_Log row `errors` and is retried next tick. The other ways in are unchanged: an approved engine send (`approval.send_approved`) or the card's Mark sent (`approval.mark_sent_manually`) arm it at their own time. The first-contact card (section 4) has Mark sent and Veto, no Approve. Mark sent right after sending records the same thing, closes the card, and the tick then skips the lead. When the tick finds the send first, it closes the pending card after the row write (`approval.mark_answered_in_thread`: status `sent_manually`, marked by "thread"; its reason is on the lead's result as `card_close`); a `blocked` or `stale_slots` card stays as it is (`not_pending`). Mark sent on a card already answered in the thread closes it without a second event, and a hand-made Approve link on it is refused. Nothing else is needed. The ladder ignores a card whose reply is on the lead's history (`cadence.answered_card_ids`) when it checks for open cards. Booking is called from A1's reply handler when `booking.evaluate_reply(text, state.offered_slots, now, free_slots).bookable` is true: `booking.book(state, match.slot, ghl, instantly, now=now, lead_tz=match.lead_tz)` returns the confirmation `Draft`; otherwise `booking.counter_proposal_text(match, state)` is the draft. Either one is sent automatically only under the rule above (its template id is `booking.confirmation.zoom_link` or `booking.confirmation.no_zoom_link`, or a `booking.counter.*` id); otherwise it is an approval card. A counter to a time the lead asked for that only the slot rules refuse, and `booking.slot_already_taken`, are always cards.

Without a GHL token (today), free slots fall back to a 30-minute grid over the calendar's open hours (12:00-18:00 CT) and each touch is tagged `slots_source=default_grid`. In live mode the tick refuses to send default-grid times (`skipped_unverified_slots`); it only sends times read from the Discovery Call calendar.

Schedule (when live): every hour 07:00-19:00 CT on weekdays. Cron in UTC for CDT: `0 12-23 * * 1-5`; the 09:30 CT veto ping needs a half-hour run as well: `30 14 * * 1-5`.

## Rollback
- Unset `ALLOW_LIVE_WRITES` (or `MODE=shadow`) and restart the tick: every send returns to `would_do` immediately; no code change.
- Stop one lead: `cadence.record_veto()` (a veto stops the whole cadence for that lead, not just one day), or set `cadence.stopped_reason = "paused_by_lorenzo"` on its store record. Booking writes are individually listed as `live_write` rows in `.tmp/job_log.csv` with their payloads: cancel the GHL appointment by hand, move the opportunity back, `update_interest_status` back to 1.
- Subsequences: the pre-apply backup is in `changesets/instantly_subsequences/backups/`; delete the created subsequences in the Instantly UI (the library exposes no delete endpoint on purpose).

## E2 research line (P4)
E2 is "Hey {first_name}, {research_line} Fifteen minutes is enough for me to show you how this would run for {company}. ...". `{company}` in E2, E4 and E5 is the spoken name (`cadence.spoken_company`: no LLC / Inc. / Corp / Co. / Ltd / PLLC / LLP, no tagline after " - ", " | " or ":"); when nothing is left the email is held (`missing_lead_data`).

- **Off by default.** `cadence.E2_RESEARCH_MODE = "fallback"`: E2 uses the fixed line for the offer (`cadence.e2.research_fallback.<offer>`), reads no page and calls no model. Turning it on is a code change to `"llm"` (or `research_mode="llm"` on the tick), and it needs Lorenzo's OK because it bills. Even in `"llm"` mode the tick researches only when `cadence.e2.research_rules` passes the gate's id check (section 4's gate path landed, Lorenzo approved the frame plus `research_rules.RULES_TEXT`, kill switch off); otherwise nothing is read or paid and E2 goes with the fallback line.
- **What it reads** (no new network call in the tick path beyond the site itself): the lead's website from the record (`website`, else a `payload` / `custom_variables` dict on the state), else the lead's email domain unless it is a free-mail domain (`research.USE_EMAIL_DOMAIN`); the phone (`LeadState.phone`, filled from the lead's reply) and the city or metro when the state has one. Then read-only GETs of the home page plus at most 2 same-site pages (about / services / areas / locations / contact): http(s) on ports 80/443 only, public addresses only, redirects only inside the same registrable domain (at most 3), 6 s per request and 20 s for the whole read, 400 KB per page, User-Agent `AdveroResearch/1.0 (+https://theadvero.com; ...)`, no cookies, no environment proxy. With `LLM_FAKE` on nothing is fetched.
- **Identity by code:** the lead's phone (last 10 digits) on a page (`matched_on "phone"`), else the city/metro as a whole word (`"city"`), and the company's name on the site. Otherwise NONE, and the model is not called.
- **One model call** (`shared.llm` role `research`, `prompts/cadence_research.md`, JSON `{"line", "facts": [{"text", "source_url", "excerpt"}]}`, `max_tokens` 600). Then by code: every `source_url` is a fetched page and every `excerpt` is verbatim on it (whitespace and curly quotes normalised), then `research_rules.validate`. A pass gives parts `[cadence.e2.research_rules]`, `meta["research_line"]`, `meta["research_evidence"]` (facts + identity) and the research bindings (`company_spoken`, `company_record`, `website_domain`, `offer_branch`, `niche`, `city` when known) that the gate re-validates with.
- **When it falls back** (the fallback line and its part, exactly as in `"fallback"` mode; never an exception, never a hold for a research reason): missing lead data, the site unreadable, identity or name not confirmed, a model error, malformed JSON or NONE, an invented excerpt, any rule failure, the copy check refusing the line, the template gate refusing the researched touch, a cache read or write error. The reasons ride on `touch.meta["research_reasons"]`.
- **What it writes:** `research:<lead email>` in the engine store: the rules_sha, a hash of the inputs, the pages read, the model's line and evidence (or why there is none) and whether the model was called. A held or retried E2 reuses it (a cached line is re-validated first) and never pays twice; a record from other rules (a `RULES_VERSION` bump) or other inputs is ignored. Nothing is stored for missing lead data or an unreadable site. `--dry-run` reads the cache and writes no key.
- **Cost per lead** (`shared/llm.py`: `claude-sonnet-5`, $2 in / $10 out per million tokens): about 1,550 tokens of prompt plus up to 3 x 6,000 characters of page text, so at most about $0.018 per lead (6,150 in, 600 out), typically about $0.01; one call per lead per rules version, and none for a lead whose identity cannot be confirmed.

## The copy gate on the unattended path
E1 to E10 are the only path to a prospect with nobody reading first, so the gate on them is now the
**superset** of the two gates that exist, not a second implementation of one of them.

`cadence.copy_problems` calls `cadence.shared_problems`, which runs `draft.validate_draft` (the same
validator that gates the first reply Lorenzo approves by hand) and translates its sentences into the
cadence's short-code vocabulary. It then still runs every original cadence check on top: verbatim
money and percentage match against `config/offers.md`, leftover `{placeholder}`, and the blanket
refusal of the word "guarantee" in any touch. Two codes are new: `money_marked_to_confirm` and
`guarantee_from_another_offer` / `proof_from_another_offer`.

Two real holes closed: the cadence used to pass the `$2,500` retainer that `offers.md` marks
**TO CONFIRM**, and cleaning's `$24,000` guarantee quoted inside a bookkeeping thread. The cross-offer
half only arms when the touch knows whose offer row the lead is on, so `Touch` carries a
`campaign_key` (from `cadence.campaign_key_of`: an explicit key, else the campaign id's row in
`config/ids.json`, else a niche guess) and the two `booking.py` call sites pass it too.

**The gate fails closed.** If `services.reply_engine.draft` cannot be imported, or `validate_draft`
raises, `copy_problems` returns `copy_validator_unavailable` and the touch is blocked. A
`copy_rule:<sentence>` fallback means a rule `draft.py` grows later blocks the touch even before
anybody maps it to a short code here, so the two gates cannot drift apart silently.

**What this changes in practice:** all 54 shipped templates (6 campaigns x 9 touch days) still pass,
asserted by a test. But a hand-written or model-polished line that quotes a neighbouring offer's
number will now end the day at `blocked_copy` and push a card instead of going out. That is intended,
and it is also a new way for a ladder to stall: `BLOCKED_COPY_STALL_DAYS` stops the ladder entirely
after enough consecutive blocked days.

## Known gaps / open items
- **A booking made from a reply is held until the approval.** `booking.register_booking` writes `showrate_hold:<appt_id>` before arming the show-rate ladder, so no S4 touch and no no-show chase can reach a prospect before Lorenzo's approved reply goes out. `approval.send_approved` releases it; `approval.cancel_booking` tears it down. The GHL appointment itself is created `new` (not GHL's default `confirmed`) so no status-triggered GHL workflow or reminder fires before the approval (checklist 7b). A store error while reading that marker counts as held: the schedule keeps its `held` flag and nothing is sent from it that tick (`show_rate.read_hold`; `no_show._show_rate_held` answers held too). No-show recovery times are claimed through `approval.claim_slots`, all or nothing; a conflict re-picks from the same free-slot list and falls back to link-only copy. Tests: `services/reply_engine/tests/test_hold_status_and_hold_checks.py`. See `runbooks/show_rate_and_no_show.md`.
- **"next <weekday>" is never auto-booked** (past the 5-business-day horizon), so the lead is counter-proposed and waits for the "Book as asked" button. DECISIONS F4; pinned by tests on both sides.
- GHL free-slots response shape is handled for both `{date: {slots: [...]}}` and `{_dates_: {...}}`; verify with the first token-backed call.
- E2 research needs the lead's phone or city on the record to confirm identity. Today `LeadState` carries `phone` only when the lead's reply had one (classify) and no city/metro, so most leads get the fallback line even in `"llm"` mode (see "E2 research line").
- `LeadState` has no `reply_from` field yet (A1 owns `models.py`). The cadence keeps its own copy on `state.cadence['reply_from']` and `recipient_for()` prefers it, so a lead who replied from a shared or assistant mailbox is answered at the address that wrote. Until A1 persists `ReplyEvent.reply_to_address` onto the state, that copy has to be seeded when the cadence is armed.
- `services/reply_engine/app.py` calls `booking.evaluate_reply(...)` with no `subject=`, so booking's auto-responder guard runs on the body alone. It now does (signature and office-hours blocks are stripped before an availability window is parsed, and automated acknowledgements are recognised), but passing the subject through is still the stronger fix.
- An unconfirmed send marker from an EARLIER day is retried rather than escalated: a day-old POST that never got an answer is more likely lost than delivered. Same-day it is held and the digest says "confirm by hand in the Instantly inbox".
- Subsequence `conditions` is sent with empty matchers so the create enrols nobody. Whether Instantly reads an empty `crm_status` as "nobody" or as "everybody" is **not** verified against a live create: confirm 0 enrolled leads in the UI immediately after the first one.
