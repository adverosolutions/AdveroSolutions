## How to read this

- Replies from a person counts one reply per contact. Out of office and other automatic answers are counted on their own line and are never mixed into it.
- In the weekly table, interested, booked, held and closed are counted into the week by the date of the contact's last reply. The sending platform records the date a status last changed but not the date of each earlier step, so the table uses the reply date for every step. A contact whose status moves in a later week without a new reply will not move between weeks in the table.
- A cell reading "no source" means there was nothing to measure, not that the number was zero.
