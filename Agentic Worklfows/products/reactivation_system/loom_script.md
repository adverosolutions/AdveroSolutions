# Loom script: the reactivation system (3 minutes)

Client-facing. Record in one take. No price is spoken: `config/offers.md` carries the commission band and Lorenzo says it live, on the call, where he can hear the answer.

Screen: their own CRM list on screen where possible. If not, a generic list with the columns blurred.

---

**0:00 to 0:20. The number.**

Hi, this is Lorenzo. Before anything else, one number from your own system. You have got about {dormant_count} people in here who bought from you once and then went quiet. Not leads. Customers. You already paid to win every one of them.

**0:20 to 0:50. Why they are worth more than new leads.**

A cold list needs to be convinced you are real. This list already knows. They know what you charge, they know whether you turned up on time, and most of them did not leave for a reason, they just stopped having the problem for a while.

Most businesses never write to them again, because there is no obvious day to do it and nobody owns the job.

**0:50 to 1:40. What we build.**

Three things.

First, we clean the list. Your current customers come out, anyone in a dispute comes out, anyone you tell us to leave alone comes out. What is left gets checked so we are not sending to addresses that bounce.

Second, we write three notes in your voice, signed by you, from your address. Not a newsletter. A note from you to someone you used to work with, asking whether they still handle it the same way. We take your last few emails and match how you actually write.

Third, the timing. We do not send them on a schedule that suits us. Cleaning goes out at renewal and budget season. Bookkeeping goes out before the quarterly deadlines. Recruiting goes out when your clients start hiring.

**1:40 to 2:20. What you see.**

Replies land in your inbox, in your name. The ones who want to talk go on your calendar. Every week you get one page: how many went out, how many came back, how many booked, how many closed, and which specific people those were.

That last part matters, because it is also how we get paid.

**2:20 to 2:50. The risk question, answered before they ask.**

There is nothing up front. We take a share of what comes back, and only from the people on that list, and only if they sign. If the list does not produce, you have spent nothing and you still have a cleaned list and three emails you can send yourself.

**2:50 to 3:00. The ask.**

If you can pull an export of customers you have not worked with in the last year, I can tell you within a day whether this is worth doing. If the list is too small I will tell you that too.

Thanks for watching. Lorenzo.

---

## Recording notes

- Say the client's dormant count out loud. Guessing it is worse than not saying it.
- Never say the word "campaign" to a business owner. Say "three notes".
- Do not name the commission percentage on the video. It is a conversation, and on the video it sounds like a price list.
- Under three minutes. If it runs long, cut the middle of the second section, not the last thirty seconds.
