# Loom script: cold-email infrastructure (3 minutes)

Client-facing. No price is spoken. Screen: a domain list, then DNS records, then a warm-up health dashboard.

---

**0:00 to 0:25. The mistake, named fast.**

Hi, this is Lorenzo. If you are about to start sending cold email from the domain your invoices go out from, stop. That is the one move you cannot undo. Your quotes, your contracts and your reminders all live on that domain, and it takes about two weeks of cold sending to make them stop arriving.

**0:25 to 0:55. What gets built instead.**

Separate domains. Close enough to your brand that a reply looks right, entirely separate from the one your business runs on. Three inboxes on each, and each inbox sends twenty five a day. That is the ceiling, not the target.

So if you want three hundred a day, that is twelve inboxes on four domains. If you want a thousand, the number goes up accordingly. It is arithmetic, and I will do it with you before you buy anything.

**0:55 to 1:35. The part everyone skips. [screen: DNS records]**

These three records are what decide whether you land in an inbox or a spam folder. They tell the receiving side that you are allowed to send as that domain and that nothing was tampered with on the way.

Most setups get two of the three right and never check from outside. I check every domain from outside the platform before anything sends, and you get the results.

**1:35 to 2:15. The three weeks. [screen: warm-up health]**

Here is the part you need to hear before you buy, not after. A brand new domain that starts sending two hundred emails on day one looks exactly like a spammer, because that is what a spammer does.

So every inbox spends three weeks warming up. Small volumes, real conversations, building a history. Then it goes live. Three weeks, and I will give you the exact date on day two so it is in your diary and not a surprise.

**2:15 to 2:45. What you are left with.**

Domains you own, on your own card. Inboxes on your own account. And a written runbook with every record, every date, every setting and what to do when something starts bouncing.

That last part is deliberate. If I get hit by a bus, you can hand this to anyone and they can run it. You are not buying a dependency.

**2:45 to 3:00. The ask.**

Tell me how many emails a day you want to send and what your main domain is. I will come back with the domain list, the inbox count and the date you can start sending.

Thanks for watching. Lorenzo.

---

## Recording notes

- The three weeks is the most important thirty seconds. Do not rush it.
- Show a real warm-up health figure. 100 percent across twelve inboxes is the proof.
- "Their card, their domains" gets said out loud. It removes the objection before it forms.
- No price on the video.
